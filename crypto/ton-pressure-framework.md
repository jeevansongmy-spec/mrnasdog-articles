# GRAM Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Checked Nov 1 2026*

GRAM, the coin of The Open Network that was called Toncoin until June 2026, is inflationary and has no supply cap. Between Aug 3 and Nov 1 2026 about **101.39M GRAM** reached the market from block rewards, Believers Fund claims, Telegram's treasury and the Ethereum bridge, while only about **86,800 GRAM** left it, so net supply grew **+3.57%**. Other trackers show **+2.90%** for the same 90 days, and the coin page carries a warning note that explains why.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Gram supply is growing, and the next 90 days should look the same. Our count is **+3.57%** net over 90 days, or about **101.30M GRAM** on a circulating base of **2.84B GRAM**. Other trackers read **+2.90%**, a gap of **0.67 points**. The gap comes from rounding, not from coins: the price those trackers store is cut to the cent, which pushed their supply figure about 9.69M GRAM too high on Aug 3 2026 and about 11.35M GRAM too low on Nov 1 2026. So the coin page shows a warning note, and our figure stands. The label for Gram is plain: supply growing, projected to keep growing.

## Sell pressure: where new GRAM comes from

The biggest source is the network itself. Every block pays its validators freshly made GRAM: 1.7 GRAM for each block on the main chain and 1 GRAM for each block on the work chain. The reward is set per block, not per year, so when the April 2026 upgrade made blocks about six times faster, the yearly flow of new coins jumped with it. Blocks now arrive about every 0.4 seconds. Since collators went live on Aug 20 2026, block rewards have added about **572,000 GRAM a day**, or **51.52M GRAM** over 90 days. A plan to cut the reward to 0.35 and 0.2 GRAM was put to validators in April 2026, but it was never put in place, and the reward on the chain today is still 1.7 and 1.

The second source is the Believers Fund. In 2023 large early holders locked their coins in it for two years, and together with its reward pool it held about 1.32B GRAM. Since Oct 2025 a slice of about **36.59M GRAM** opens every 30 days, for 36 months. Three slices opened inside this window, on Aug 8, Sep 7 and Oct 7 2026, but holders only take out what they want, when they want. From Aug 3 to Nov 1 2026 they took out **19.57M GRAM**, and that is the number we count.

The third source is Telegram. Its treasury wallet sent **10M GRAM** to its Fragment payout wallet three times in the window, on Aug 8, Aug 29 and Sep 21 2026, so **30M GRAM** in all. Telegram has made sends like these every few weeks through 2026, and we expect about the same pace in the next 90 days.

The fourth row, long-term locked coins, is zero for now. About 1.08B GRAM in 180 early-miner wallets has been frozen since a validator vote in Feb 2023, and that freeze runs until Feb 21 2027, after this window. The fifth row is small: the bridge that backs wrapped GRAM on Ethereum let **295,060 GRAM** back out between Aug 3 and Oct 4 2026.

## Buy pressure: where new GRAM goes

Very little GRAM leaves the market. Nothing buys GRAM back on the network's behalf, so the buyback row is zero. Half of every fee is destroyed, but fees are tiny after a spring 2026 cut, so the burn removed only about **83,060 GRAM** in 90 days, less than 0.2% of what block rewards added.

Telegram did not move any GRAM back into its treasury wallet in the window; the last time it did was May 31 2026, so that row is zero too. Staked GRAM still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing, and the Believers Fund has not taken new deposits since Oct 2023. The last row is a curiosity: people sent **3,719 GRAM** to the zero address, which no one controls, so those coins are out of reach for good.

## Foundation and overhang

Three large piles sit outside the circulating count, and each one can add to supply later. The Believers Fund holds **1.25B GRAM**. About 367M of that has already opened and can be claimed at any time, and about 878M is still waiting for its monthly slices, which run to 2028. If holders start claiming faster, the second row grows quickly.

Telegram's treasury wallet holds **102.42M GRAM** after this window's sends. At 30M every 90 days, that pile would last about ten more months.

The frozen early-miner wallets hold **1.08B GRAM**. Most of them were never used, and their owners may no longer have the keys, but the freeze itself ends on Feb 21 2027. Any coins that move after that date would be new supply, so this is the pile to watch in early 2027. The Ethereum bridge holds another 9.30M GRAM that backs wrapped coins already trading there.

## How GRAM compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake chains pay validators in new coins, so new supply is normal. What sets Gram apart is how its reward is set. Ethereum pays validators according to how much is staked, and part of every fee is burned. Solana follows a yearly issuance rate that steps down on a published path. Gram pays a fixed amount per block, so its supply growth follows block speed. When blocks got about six times faster in April 2026, yearly issuance rose to match, and with about 1.31B GRAM staked, those rewards give stakers a very high yield, close to 17% a year.

Gram also leans less on its fee burn than many chains. Half of every fee is burned, but fees were cut on purpose to make sends almost free, so the burn barely moves the count. On top of the block rewards, Gram has two release valves that many Layer 1 chains no longer have: a large vesting fund that early holders can drain at their own pace, and a company treasury, Telegram's, that sells or pays out coins every few weeks.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 6 2026, Dec 6 2026 and Jan 5 2027:** three more Believers Fund slices of about 36.59M GRAM each open. If holders claim more than the 19.57M they took out in the last 90 days, the second row rises.

**Telegram's next payout:** its last 10M GRAM send was on Sep 21 2026. A new send would keep the third row on pace; a long pause would bring it down.

**Block speed:** since Oct 19 2026 the main chain has made about 2.5% fewer blocks, and fewer blocks mean fewer new coins. Any new vote to change the per-block reward would change the first row at once.

**Feb 21 2027:** the freeze on 1.08B GRAM in early-miner wallets ends, just after this window. Any vote to extend it, or any coins moving out after it, would change the long-term picture for Gram.

## Summary

Gram supply grew **+3.57%** in the 90 days to Nov 1 2026, about 101.30M GRAM net, and should grow about the same in the next 90 days.

Block rewards of about 572,000 GRAM a day are the biggest source, followed by 30M GRAM from Telegram's treasury and 19.57M GRAM claimed from the Believers Fund.

The fee burn takes out only about 83,060 GRAM in 90 days, so almost nothing offsets the new coins.

Large piles still wait outside the market: 1.25B GRAM in the Believers Fund, 102.42M in Telegram's treasury and 1.08B in frozen wallets whose freeze ends on Feb 21 2027.

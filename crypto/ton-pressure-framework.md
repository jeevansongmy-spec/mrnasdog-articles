# GRAM Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Checked Nov 10 2026*

GRAM is inflationary, and fast: there is no supply cap, and every block of The Open Network pays its validators in new coins. Between Aug 12 and Nov 10 2026 about **98.09M GRAM** reached the market, from block rewards, Telegram's own wallet and the Believers Fund locker, while the fee burn took out only **81,200 GRAM**. Net, circulating supply grew **+3.43%** in 90 days, and other trackers show **+3.72%** for the same stretch.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Gram, the coin that was called Toncoin until the Jun 15 2026 rename, added about **98.01M GRAM** to its circulating supply of **2.85B** in the last 90 days, a net rise of **+3.43%**. The next 90 days look almost the same at about **+3.42%**, because the block reward has not changed and Telegram and the Believers Fund are releasing coins at a steady pace. Other trackers read **+3.72%**, which is close enough that the coin page carries no warning note. We call this a growing supply that is projected to keep growing, one of the faster rates among large Layer 1 coins.

## Sell pressure: where new GRAM comes from

The biggest source is block rewards. Every masterchain block pays **1.7 GRAM** and every round of the main chain pays **1 GRAM**, split across however many pieces the chain is running in. When the Catchain 2.0 upgrade cut block time to about 0.4 seconds in April 2026, the pay per block stayed the same, so the yearly mint jumped. In the 90 days that came to **51.47M GRAM**, about 572,000 a day. A plan from April 2026 to cut the pay to 0.35 and 0.2 GRAM never went into force, and the network's settings showed no open proposal on Nov 10 2026.

The second source is the Believers Fund, a locker that people filled with about 1.32B GRAM in 2023. Since October 2025 it opens about **36.59M GRAM** every 30 days, until 2028. Opening a slice does not move coins on its own: each holder has to take their share out. Between Aug 12 and Nov 10 2026 holders took out **16.33M GRAM**, so we count that, and we expect about the same pace next.

The third source is Telegram's own wallet, whose coins are not counted as circulating. On Aug 29, Sep 21 and Nov 2 2026 it sent **10M GRAM** each time to Fragment, Telegram's marketplace for usernames, numbers and gifts. That is **30M GRAM** in 90 days, and Telegram has made sends like this in most months of 2026, so we expect a similar amount next.

Two rows are zero. Vesting by a team or investors does not exist for GRAM beyond the Believers Fund. The 1.08B GRAM in frozen early mining wallets stays locked until Feb 21 2027, just after the next 90 days. One small extra row covers the lock behind GRAM on Ethereum, which let out **294,620 GRAM**, almost all of it in August 2026, and has barely moved since.

## Buy pressure: where new GRAM goes

Gram has one built-in way to remove coins: half of every network fee is burned. Fees were cut about sixfold on May 1 2026 and are now tiny, so the burn removed only about **81,200 GRAM** in 90 days, less than 0.2% of what validators earned in the same time.

No programmatic buyback exists. No foundation or treasury bought GRAM back into a locked wallet in the window: the last time Telegram's wallet took coins in was May 31 2026, when **33.39M GRAM** came back from Fragment, and there is no schedule for the next time. Listed companies such as TON Strategy buy and stake GRAM, but those coins were already on the market, so the buying takes nothing out of the count. Staked GRAM still counts as circulating. The last tiny row is coins sent by mistake to the all-zero address, which sits on the frozen list: about **158 GRAM** in 90 days.

## Foundation and overhang

Three large pools sit outside the market today. The Believers Fund locker holds **1.25B GRAM**: roughly 403M of it is already open and waiting to be claimed, and about 842M more opens in 30-day slices until 2028. The frozen early mining wallets hold **1.08B GRAM** across 180 addresses; most have never been used, and their lock ends on Feb 21 2027. Telegram's wallet holds **92.42M GRAM** and has no published schedule.

Smaller pools are the lock behind GRAM on Ethereum with about **9.30M GRAM** and the old TON Foundation wallet with about **1.59M GRAM**, which sent nothing in the window. If any of these balances falls, the coins that leave will show up in the next check.

## How GRAM compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most large proof-of-stake chains pay validators a set share of supply each year, so their mint stays steady when the chain gets faster. Gram pays a fixed amount per block instead, which is why the move to sub-second blocks in 2026 multiplied the yearly mint several times over without any vote on inflation. That makes Gram's supply growth unusually sensitive to engineering changes.

The burn side is weak too. Chains with a large fee burn can cancel much of their new supply on busy days, while Gram's very low fees mean the burn hardly counts. And unlike chains whose unlocks are run by a team on a calendar, much of Gram's extra supply depends on choices by Telegram and by thousands of Believers Fund holders deciding when to claim.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Dec 6 2026, Jan 5 2027 and Feb 4 2027:** the next Believers Fund slices open, about 36.59M GRAM each. Watch whether holders claim faster than the 16.33M of the last 90 days.

**Telegram's wallet:** after the Nov 2 2026 send it holds 92.42M GRAM. Another 10M send to Fragment every few weeks would match the pace we expect; a bigger send would push supply growth up.

**The block reward:** any new vote to cut the pay per block would lower the biggest row at once. On Nov 10 2026 no such proposal was open.

**Feb 21 2027:** the freeze on 1.08B GRAM in early mining wallets ends, 13 days after the next 90 days. Most of these wallets have never been used, but any vote to extend the freeze or burn the coins before then would matter a lot.

**Telegram's built-in wallet:** Telegram began opening self-custody GRAM wallets to many more users in November 2026. It does not change supply, but more use would mean more fees and a larger burn.

## Summary

GRAM's circulating supply grew **+3.43%** between Aug 12 and Nov 10 2026, and the next 90 days look much the same at about **+3.42%**.

Block rewards of **51.47M GRAM** are the largest source, because the pay per block stayed the same when blocks became much faster in April 2026.

Telegram's wallet released **30M GRAM** and Believers Fund holders claimed **16.33M GRAM**, while the fee burn removed only about 81,200.

The big date to watch sits just past the window: the freeze on **1.08B GRAM** in early mining wallets ends on Feb 21 2027.

# GRAM Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads GRAM, the coin of The Open Network (TON) that was called Toncoin until Jun 15 2026, at **+3.64% net** over the last 90 days and **+3.67%** over the next 90. Three flows feed the float: **50.93M GRAM** of block rewards, **21.80M GRAM** claimed from the Believers Fund locker and **30.0M GRAM** sent out of Telegram's main wallet, against a fee burn of only **98.8K GRAM**. GRAM has no supply cap, and since blocks became about six times faster in April 2026 the block reward prints far more coins than it used to.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 4 to Oct 2 2026, **102.73M GRAM** entered the market and **102.6K GRAM** left it, for a net of **+3.64%** on a circulating supply of **2.82B GRAM**. For the next 90 days we project **103.53M GRAM** in and the same **102.6K GRAM** out, or **+3.67%**. The inflation monitor reads **+3.54%** for the same window, a gap of **0.10 percentage points**, well inside the 0.5-point line, so no warning chip is shown. GRAM is **inflationary by design on a half-locked float**: the chain prints new coins every 0.4 seconds while old locks slowly hand more coins to the market.

## Sell pressure: where new GRAM comes from

The biggest source is the block reward. TON pays **1.7 GRAM** for every masterchain block and **1.0 GRAM** for every basechain block. When the Catchain 2.0 upgrade cut block time from about 2.5 seconds to about 0.4 seconds in April 2026, nobody lowered those two numbers, so issuance jumped from roughly 90,000 GRAM a day to about 575,000 GRAM a day. A cut to 0.35 and 0.2 GRAM was discussed but never put in place, and no change is waiting in the network's config. Reading the whole network's supply at both ends of the window and adding back the burn gives **50.93M GRAM** of new coins. Extra block producers, called collators, went live on Aug 20 2026 and lifted the pace a little, so our next 90 days use that newer pace: **51.73M GRAM**.

The second source is vesting. In 2023 holders locked about 1.03B GRAM in the TON Believers Fund, which with its rewards now unlocks **1.32B GRAM** in 36 monthly slices of about **36.6M GRAM** each, from Nov 2025 to 2028. Owners are not forced to take their coins out, and most have not: only **21.80M GRAM** left the locker in these 90 days, while about 110M opened. We count what actually left, and we expect about the same pace next.

The third source is Telegram. Its main GRAM wallet sits outside the circulating count, and it sent **30.0M GRAM** to Telegram's Fragment payout wallet in three 10M transfers on Aug 8, Aug 29 and Sep 21 2026. Fragment pays creators and sellers inside Telegram, so these coins reach the market. The transfers come about every three to four weeks, so we expect about three more, **30.0M GRAM**, by the end of December. The fourth row, long locks, is zero for now: 1.08B GRAM in frozen early-miner wallets cannot move until Feb 21 2027, and no bankruptcy estate holds GRAM.

## Buy pressure: where new GRAM goes

Very little GRAM leaves the market. There is no buyback: no contract, foundation or treasury buys GRAM back, and the listed companies that hold GRAM bought it on the open market. The protocol burns half of every fee, but fees were cut about six times in May 2026 to make GRAM almost free to send inside Telegram, so the burn came to just **98,786 GRAM** in 90 days, about 1 GRAM burned for every 500 created. A further **3,832 GRAM** was sent to the zero address, which is on the frozen list and has no owner.

Telegram's main wallet sometimes pulls GRAM back from its own apps, which removes coins from the market; the last time was May 31 2026, with 33.4M GRAM, and nothing came in during this window, so that row is zero. About 658M GRAM is staked with validators, but staked coins still count as circulating, so staking takes nothing out of the float. Total buy pressure was **102.6K GRAM**, about 0.004% of supply.

## Foundation and overhang

About 46% of all GRAM sits outside the circulating count, in three places we track on-chain every day. The largest is the Believers Fund locker with **1.25B GRAM**; about 334M of that is already open and could be claimed any day, and the rest opens at about 36.6M a month. Next come the 180 frozen early-miner wallets with **1.08B GRAM**, blocked until Feb 21 2027; the four largest alone hold about 317M. The third is Telegram's main wallet with **102.42M GRAM**, which has been paying out about 10M GRAM every few weeks. An old TON Foundation wallet holds 1.59M GRAM and has not moved. If any of these balances falls between our checks, the outflow enters the sell side at the next check.

## How GRAM compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1s

Most proof-of-stake chains pay validators on a clock: so many coins per year or per epoch, no matter how many blocks are made. Ethereum, Solana and Cardano work that way, so making blocks faster does not print more coins. TON pays per block, and the reward did not change when blocks got six times faster, which is why GRAM's issuance runs near **4% of total supply a year**, well above Ethereum's roughly 0.8% net and closer to young, high-emission chains.

GRAM also differs from capped coins like Bitcoin, where new supply falls on a fixed halving schedule. GRAM has no cap and no halving: supply grows until validators vote to change the reward. And unlike chains with a big fee burn, such as Ethereum in busy months or BNB with its auto-burn, GRAM's burn is too small to matter, because fees were cut on purpose to suit Telegram payments.

Its locked supply is unusual too. Many young coins unlock team and investor tokens on a fixed calendar. GRAM's largest overhangs are holders' own coins: a locker people joined voluntarily, which they empty at their own pace, and early-miner wallets frozen by a validator vote. That makes the timing of new supply less certain than a normal vesting cliff, in both directions.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 7, Nov 6 and Dec 6 2026**: the next three Believers Fund slices open, about 36.6M GRAM each. If owners claim faster than the recent pace of about 7M a month, the vesting row grows.

**Telegram's payout transfers**: we expect about three more 10M GRAM sends to Fragment by the end of December; a pause or a bigger send changes the third sell row.

**Any validator vote on the block reward**: a cut like the 0.35 and 0.2 GRAM plan would shrink new supply by about 80%; none is pending today.

**The Gram Wallet rollout inside Telegram**: it may pull more GRAM into users' hands, and more fees, but with fees this low the burn will stay small.

**Feb 21 2027**, just after this window: the 1.08B GRAM in frozen early-miner wallets can move again.

## Summary

GRAM, the coin of The Open Network, is inflationary: **+3.64%** over the last 90 days and **+3.67%** projected for the next 90, against a monitor reading of **+3.54%**. Most new supply comes from a per-block reward that stayed the same when TON's blocks became six times faster, about **51M GRAM** a quarter, with the Believers Fund locker and Telegram's own wallet adding another **52M GRAM**. The fee burn is tiny, so nothing in the protocol offsets this. The key risk is the overhang: 2.43B GRAM sits in the locker, the frozen wallets and Telegram's wallet, and the frozen 1.08B GRAM can move after Feb 21 2027; the main upside is a validator vote to lower the block reward.

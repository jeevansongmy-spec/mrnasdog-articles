# GRAM Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Gram (GRAM), the native coin of The Open Network and the coin known as Toncoin until **Jun 15 2026**, is inflationary, and the reason is mechanical: The Open Network pays validators a fixed number of new Gram for every block, and when the Catchain 2.0 upgrade made blocks about six times faster, that per-block reward was never cut. Over the last 90 days validators received **50.95M GRAM**, early supporters claimed **20.95M GRAM** from the TON Believers Fund, and Telegram's treasury sent **40M GRAM** into the market, against a fee burn of only **84.4K GRAM**. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework therefore reads Gram at **+3.97% net** over the last 90 days and **+3.65%** over the next 90, against a supply-monitor reading of **+3.75%** — a gap of **0.22 percentage points**, which is agreement, not conflict. Gram has no supply cap, and **1,081.40M GRAM** of frozen early-mining coins stay locked until **Feb 21 2027**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Sep 25 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **Gram at +3.97% net**: **111.90M GRAM** reached the float against **0.09M GRAM** removed, on a circulating base of **2,813.15M GRAM**. The independent supply monitor reads the realised 90-day change at **+3.75%**. The gap is **0.22 percentage points**, inside the framework's half-point tolerance, so Gram ships with **no data-conflict flag**. The forward column reads **+3.65%** — slightly lower only because Telegram's treasury transfers are projected by date, three in the next quarter against four in the last; block minting itself runs a little faster forward. The label for Gram is **structurally inflationary by block reward**: a proof-of-stake Layer-1 whose issuance is set per block rather than per year, so every gain in speed became a gain in new supply.

## Sell pressure: where new GRAM comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **50.95M GRAM** and it is the largest line on the page. The Open Network's configuration pays **1.7 GRAM** for every masterchain block and **1.0 GRAM** for every basechain block, and that setting was read at every one of the **246** key blocks in the window and never changed. What did change, before the window, was speed: Catchain 2.0 went live on **Apr 9 2026** and took the masterchain from about 2.4 seconds per block to a measured **0.41 seconds**. The Gram block reward is counted per block, not per unit of time, and nothing in the protocol scaled it down, so the faster chain simply mints more — **18,977,367** masterchain blocks and **18,691,944** basechain blocks in 90 days. A cut to 0.35 and 0.2 GRAM per block was discussed around the upgrade, but it never reached the chain: no such proposal is live, and the reward setting never moved. The collator upgrade on **Aug 20 2026** lifted block output a little, to about **575,000 GRAM** a day, so the forward Sell #1 uses that post-upgrade pace, **51.77M GRAM**.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **20.95M GRAM**. The TON Believers Fund is an on-chain locker holding the **1,317.38M GRAM** that early supporters deposited plus its reward pool; since **Oct 12 2025** it opens one of 36 slices every 30 days, and each holder must claim. Three slices opened inside the window — Jul 9, Aug 8 and Sep 7 — worth about **109.8M GRAM** on paper, but only **20.95M GRAM** actually left the contract. The framework books what was released, not what was scheduled, and holds the same realised pace forward. Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **40M GRAM**: Telegram's treasury wallet sits outside the counted float, and it sent four transfers of **10M GRAM** each, on Jul 3, Aug 8, Aug 29 and Sep 21, to the wallet Telegram uses to pay its users. That is real new float every time it fires. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy supply, is **zero**: the 180 early-mining wallets validators froze in 2023 hold **1,081.40M GRAM** and cannot move until **Feb 21 2027**, and no bankruptcy estate holds Gram.

## Buy pressure: where new GRAM goes

Almost nowhere. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero** — no contract, treasury or listed company buys Gram back with revenue. Buy #2, the protocol fee burn, is **84.4K GRAM**: The Open Network destroys half of every transaction fee as it is paid, but fees were cut about six times in May 2026 under the MTONGA plan, so the Gram burn now runs near **940 GRAM** a day across the window and about **1,070** a day since the Aug 20 upgrade, which is the pace the forward column uses. The burn offsets under a fifth of one percent of what the block reward creates. Buy #3, foundation buying, is **zero**: the Telegram treasury received nothing this window. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is **zero**: about **655M GRAM** is staked by 378 validators and that stake is slashable, but staked Gram stays inside the circulating count, and no new lockup with a stated size began. One small extra line, Buy #5, records **3.84K GRAM** sent to the all-zero address, which is itself on the frozen list, so those coins left the float.

## Foundation and overhang

Three balances outside the counted float decide the Gram sell side, and together with a small flat remainder they account for the whole gap between total and circulating supply. The TON Believers Fund still holds **1,250.06M GRAM**; about a third of the fund's slices have opened, so a large part of that balance could already be claimed at any time, and the rest opens on the 30-day schedule to Oct 2028. The Telegram treasury wallet holds **102.42M GRAM** after this window's four transfers. The frozen early-mining wallets hold **1,081.40M GRAM** until Feb 21 2027. Also watched, though their coins already count as circulating: an older TON Foundation wallet at **1.59M GRAM** with no outflow, and two closed bridges holding **11.34M GRAM**. All of these are read on-chain at each rebuild. If any of those balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How GRAM compares to other proof-of-stake Layer-1 chains

Most proof-of-stake Layer-1 chains set issuance as a yearly rate or a curve over time, so a faster block time spreads the same annual reward across more blocks. Gram does the opposite: its reward is a fixed amount per block, so on The Open Network a speed upgrade is also a supply decision. When Catchain 2.0 cut block time by about six times, Gram issuance rose with it, and the network now mints about **7.5%** of circulating supply a year from blocks alone. Chains that tie rewards to time, or re-scale them when the interval changes, did not face that trade.

Against chains with a fee burn large enough to offset issuance, Gram sits at the other end. Its burn is real and runs every block, but The Open Network chose very low fees to serve Telegram-scale traffic, so the burn is a rounding error next to minting. And unlike chains whose genesis allocations are fully vested, Gram still carries two scheduled overhangs outside the float — the Believers Fund locker and the frozen mining wallets — plus a corporate treasury that pays out roughly monthly. The comparison is about mechanism, not price: the per-block reward, the claim-gated locker and the Telegram treasury are what move Gram supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, any validator vote on the block reward: a cut of the kind discussed after Catchain 2.0 would shrink Sell #1, the largest line, and none is pending today. Second, the TON Believers Fund slices on **Oct 7 2026**, **Nov 6 2026** and **Dec 6 2026**, about **36.6M GRAM** each; if holders start claiming their backlog instead of a trickle, Sell #2 could jump several times over. Third, the Telegram treasury: three **10M GRAM** transfers are booked forward, and one fewer would take the next-90-day reading below three percent. Fourth, the Gram Wallet rollout inside Telegram, which began on **Aug 31 2026**; more traffic means more fees and a larger burn, though at today's fee level it cannot offset minting. Beyond the window, the frozen wallets unlock on **Feb 21 2027**.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Gram at **+3.97% net** over the trailing 90 days and **+3.65%** over the next 90. The structural mechanism is a per-block reward that stayed at 1.7 and 1.0 GRAM while blocks became about six times faster, adding **50.95M GRAM** a quarter, joined by claims from the TON Believers Fund and transfers from Telegram's treasury, with a fee burn too small to matter. The key risk is size, not direction: a wave of Believers Fund claims or a larger Telegram payout would push the reading higher, and only a validator vote to cut the reward would bring it down. Gram has no supply cap, and the largest single release ahead is the **1,081.40M GRAM** in frozen early-mining wallets on Feb 21 2027.

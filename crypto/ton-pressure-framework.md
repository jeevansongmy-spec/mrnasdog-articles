# GRAM Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Checked Nov 20 2026*

GRAM supply is growing: between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026 the TON chain paid its validators **51.47M GRAM** in new coins, Telegram sent **40.00M GRAM** into the market and Believers Fund holders claimed **17.25M GRAM**, while the fee burn removed only **96.74K GRAM**. Net, the circulating supply rose **+3.79%** in 90 days, and the next 90 days look the same. GRAM has no supply cap; other trackers show **+4.16%** for the same window.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 20 2026, about **108.67M GRAM** joined the coins people can trade, a net rise of **+3.79%**, and the next 90 days project about the same **+3.78%**. Other trackers print **+4.16%**, a gap of **0.37 points**, and the coin page carries no warning note. GRAM is a clearly inflationary coin. Half of the new supply is validator pay that never stops, and the other half is coins that already exist being let out of a locker and a Telegram wallet. The burn is too small to matter.

## Sell pressure: where new GRAM comes from

The first source is validator pay. Every block on TON creates new GRAM for the validators: 1.7 GRAM for each block on the main chain and 1 GRAM for each block on the work chain where most activity happens. Since a speed upgrade in April 2026, blocks arrive about every 0.4 seconds, and the pay per block was not cut. From Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026 that came to **51.47M GRAM**, close to **572K GRAM** a day, or about 7.3% a year on the coins in circulation. Because the pay is per block, a faster chain means more new GRAM.

The second source is the TON Believers Fund. In 2023 holders locked about **1.03B GRAM** in this fund, and with its reward pool the locker held 1.32B. Vesting began on Oct 12 2025: one slice of **36.59M GRAM** opens every 30 days, 36 slices in all. Holders do not have to take their share right away, and most have not. Three slices opened in the window, yet only **17.25M GRAM** actually left the locker, so the Believers Fund row books what was claimed, not what opened.

The third source is Telegram. A wallet labelled Telegram sent **40.00M GRAM** to Fragment, the wallet that pays Telegram's ad and channel rewards, in four lots of 10M on Aug 29, Sep 21, Nov 2 and Nov 18 2026. Telegram's wallet sat outside the circulating count, so each lot added new coins to the market. The same wallet sent coins out in 9 of the 11 months of 2026, so the next 90 days assume the same pace.

Two rows are small or zero. The lock behind wrapped GRAM on Ethereum released **50.42K GRAM**, almost all of it in late August, and has been quiet since Oct 4 2026. The 180 frozen early mining wallets, holding **1.08B GRAM**, released nothing, because the freeze holds until Feb 21 2027.

## Buy pressure: where new GRAM goes

The only real buy-side flow is the fee burn. Half of every network fee is destroyed. Fees were cut about sixfold at the end of April 2026 and are now a tiny fraction of a cent per transfer, so the burn removed only **96.74K GRAM** from Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026, under 0.2% of what validators were paid. Cheap fees help people use the chain, but they leave the burn with almost no weight.

A second, tiny flow is coins sent to the all-zero address, which sits on the frozen list: about **144 GRAM** in the window. There is no buyback. No contract or treasury buys GRAM back, and no wallet tied to Telegram has taken GRAM back in since May 31 2026. Staking does not count either: staked GRAM still counts as circulating, so a bigger stake removes nothing. Listed treasury companies that hold GRAM buy coins that are already circulating, so their buying shows up in the price, not in the supply count.

## Foundation and overhang

Three large balances sit outside the circulating count. The biggest is the Believers Fund locker, with **1.24B GRAM**. About **402M** of that has already opened and could be claimed at any time, and the other 842M opens slice by slice until 2028. The second is the frozen list: **1.08B GRAM** in 180 early mining wallets that had never been used when validators froze them in 2023. Most of these wallets have never sent a coin, so nobody knows how much of it will ever move once the freeze ends. The third is the Telegram wallet, which still holds **82.42M GRAM**, about two more quarters at its current pace.

A smaller piece is the Ethereum bridge lock with **9.30M GRAM**, which backs wrapped GRAM and only moves when people bridge back. Together these balances are about 46% of all GRAM, which is why the unlocked share is only about 54%.

## How GRAM compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake chains pay validators by time or by the amount staked. Ethereum pays more as more ETH is staked and burns the base fee of every transaction, which takes back a real share of the new coins on busy days. Solana started near 8% a year and cuts its rate by 15% each year toward a floor of 1.5%, and it also burns half of the base fees. TON is different in two ways. Its pay is fixed per block, so the April 2026 speed upgrade that made blocks several times faster also made new supply several times bigger, and nothing in the rules steps it down over time. And its fees are so low that the burn barely registers.

GRAM also carries more coins waiting outside the market than most large chains. Ethereum has no locked batch left at all. GRAM has the Believers Fund slices, the Telegram wallet and the frozen list, and together they hold about 2.41B GRAM, not far below the 2.87B in circulation today.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Dec 6 2026**: the next Believers Fund slice of 36.59M GRAM opens. Watch how much of it is claimed in the following weeks; a rush of claims would push the Believers Fund row above its recent pace.

**Jan 5 2027** and **Feb 4 2027**: two more slices of the same size open. With about 402M already open and unclaimed, the real question is not the slices but whether holders who have waited start to take their coins.

**Feb 21 2027**: the freeze on the 180 early mining wallets ends, three days after this 90-day view closes. Those wallets hold 1.08B GRAM. If even a small part of it moves to exchanges, it would dwarf every other row on this page. Watch for any validator vote to extend the freeze or burn the coins before then.

Any time: Telegram's next 10M GRAM send to its payout wallet, and any validator vote to cut the block reward. A cut would be the single biggest change to GRAM's new supply, and none is open today.

## Summary

GRAM's circulating supply grew **+3.79%** between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026, and the next 90 days project about the same.

Validator pay of **51.47M GRAM** is about half of that growth, and Telegram's 40.00M and the Believers Fund's 17.25M make up the rest.

The fee burn removed only **96.74K GRAM**, and there is no buyback, so nothing on the buy side offsets the new coins.

The 1.08B GRAM frozen until Feb 21 2027 and the 402M Believers Fund GRAM that is open but unclaimed are the two biggest risks to watch.

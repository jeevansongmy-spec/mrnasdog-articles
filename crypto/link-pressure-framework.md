# LINK Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
Looking for the buy-or-sell answer? It is on the LINK coin page, with demand and price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/link](https://mrnasdog.com/research/link)

LINK supply is growing: up **+2.45%** in the 90 days from Aug 2 to Oct 31 2026, with about **+1.47%** expected in the next 90 days. Chainlink mints no new LINK at all, so the whole increase came from one release: **18.75M LINK** left the team's reserve wallets on Oct 9 2026, while nothing was burned. LINK is capped at **1B** coins, of which **766.85M** circulate, and other trackers show **+2.50%** for the same 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 31 2026, the circulating LINK supply rose by a net **+2.45%**, from about 748.10M to 766.85M LINK. Other trackers report **+2.50%** for the same 90 days, a gap of only **0.06 percentage points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days, Oct 31 2026 to Jan 29 2027, should bring about **11.25M LINK** more, or **+1.47%**, because Chainlink has released a smaller step every December. In one line, LINK is a fixed-supply oracle token whose float keeps growing as the team hands out its reserve, about **7% of all LINK a year**, with no burn to offset it.

## Sell pressure: where new LINK comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero**. All 1B LINK were created when the token launched in 2017, and the LINK token contract has no function that can make more: there is no mint, no owner and no upgrade switch in its code. Chainlink node operators and stakers are paid from coins that already exist, never from new ones.

Vesting unlocks are also **zero**. Chainlink has no investor or team vesting calendar today; the coins sold in the 2017 token sale have been free to trade for years. Everything still held back sits in Chainlink's own reserve wallets, which the next row covers.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are where all of LINK's new supply comes from: **18.75M LINK** in the last 90 days. Chainlink says its token release schedule is currently **7% of the total supply per year**, and it releases in steps. On the evening of Oct 9 2026, eight of its reserve wallets sent out 18.75M LINK in about two hours: **16.88M LINK** went through a short-lived wallet to Binance, and **1.88M LINK** went to a team-run multisig wallet. The steps over the last year were 18.75M on Oct 10 2025, 11.25M on Dec 19 2025, 19.0M on Apr 3 2026, 21.0M on Jun 19 2026 and 18.75M on Oct 9 2026. The last four add up to exactly **70M LINK**, which is 7% of the 1B supply. The December step was 11.25M on Dec 20 2024 and again on Dec 19 2025, so the next 90 days carry one expected step of **11.25M LINK**, likely around Dec 18 2026. If that step slips past Jan 29 2027, the next 90 days would add nothing.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **zero**. No court estate, frozen exchange balance or hacked pile of LINK is waiting to be paid back out.

## Buy pressure: where new LINK goes

Programmatic buyback books **zero**, even though the buying is real. The Chainlink Reserve turns revenue from Chainlink's services into LINK and keeps it in a timelocked contract. It bought **1.26M LINK** between Aug 2 and Oct 31 2026, including **427,577 LINK** in October, and now holds **6.48M LINK**. It has never sold, and Chainlink says it expects no withdrawals for years. The Reserve's coins are still counted as circulating, though, so its buying moves LINK between holders rather than taking it out of the count. The 1.26M LINK it bought equals only about **7%** of the 18.75M LINK released in the same 90 days.

Protocol fee burn is **zero**. LINK has no burn: fees go to node operators, stakers and the Reserve, and the LINK token contract has no burn function. Only a few stray coins sit in dead wallets.

Foundation buy is **zero**. The Chainlink team does not buy LINK on the market itself. The two US spot LINK funds buy coins that already circulate, so they take nothing out of the count.

New long-term lock is **zero**. About **40.88M LINK** sits in the community staking pool, which is full and did not change during the window, and about 1.65M LINK in the node operator pool. Staked LINK still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing from the float.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is Chainlink's own reserve: **233.15M LINK** across the wallets Chainlink lists as non-circulating, mostly in multisig wallets of 30M LINK each, plus wallets holding 29.4M, 28.75M, 22.0M and 3.0M. That is **23.3%** of all LINK, and at the current pace of about 70M a year it would take a little over three years to run out. Two more team-linked wallets already count as circulating: the team multisig that received 1.88M LINK on Oct 9 2026 now holds **10.32M LINK**, and the Chainlink Reserve holds **6.48M LINK**. All of these wallets are watched on-chain. If the reserve wallets' balance falls between checks, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check; if the Reserve or the team multisig ever sends LINK out, that outflow is followed the same way.

## How LINK compares to other oracle and data network tokens

LINK and PYTH are both fixed-supply oracle tokens with large held-back portions, but they release in very different shapes. Pyth has a 10B cap with about 78.75% unlocked, and most of the rest comes out in large yearly cliffs; the next one is set for May 19 2027, outside this window. Chainlink, with 76.7% unlocked, releases in smaller steps several times a year, so LINK's supply grows a little almost every quarter instead of in one big jump.

The Graph's GRT works differently again. GRT has no fixed cap: it targets about 3% new issuance a year to pay indexers and burns roughly 1% a year through query fees and other taxes. LINK has neither: no new issuance and no burn. All of LINK's supply growth comes from team decisions about the reserve, and all of its buying comes from the Reserve, which holds rather than burns.

That makes LINK's supply easy to measure but hard to schedule: the cap is fixed in code, yet the dates and sizes of each step are set by the team, not by a public calendar.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the expected December step: **11.25M LINK** was released on Dec 20 2024 and Dec 19 2025, so watch the reserve wallets around Dec 18 2026. A bigger step, or a second one before Jan 29 2027, would raise the next-90-day figure above +1.47%.

Second, the Chainlink Reserve's monthly additions. It added 427,577 LINK in October 2026; a faster pace would offset more of each release, but it would still not change the count while the Reserve's coins count as circulating.

Third, the team multisig holding 10.32M LINK. It received part of the Oct 9 2026 release and sent 500,000 LINK out on Oct 14 2026; larger moves to exchanges would add selling, though not new supply.

Fourth, the staking pools. The community pool has been full at about 40.88M LINK; a change in its size or in how rewards are paid would show up on this page.

## Summary

LINK supply grew **+2.45%** in the 90 days to Oct 31 2026, and about **+1.47%** more is expected by Jan 29 2027. Chainlink mints no new LINK and burns none; the growth comes from the team releasing its reserve, **18.75M LINK** on Oct 9 2026 and about 70M LINK a year in steps. The key risk is the **233.15M LINK** still in the reserve wallets, which the team releases on its own timing. The ceiling is hard: LINK can never exceed **1B** coins.

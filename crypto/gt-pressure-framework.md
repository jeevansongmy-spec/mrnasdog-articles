# GT Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking

GT, the coin of the Gate exchange and Gate Chain, is shrinking: circulating supply fell **1.80%** over the 90 days from Jul 27 to Oct 25 2026, and we expect about **2.07%** less over the next 90 days. Gate burned **1,987,321 GT** on Oct 4 2026, while Gate Chain paid its nodes **102,992 GT** of new coins, so the burn was almost 20 times larger than the new supply. GT has no vesting; the only coins outside the circulating count are **12.25M GT** in Gate's insurance fund.

      
## The verdict, in one paragraph

      
Over the last 90 days the GT supply moved by a net **−1,884,329 GT** against **104.69M GT** in circulation, a change of **−1.80%**. Other trackers show **−1.79%** for the same 90 days, a gap of just **0.01 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days we expect **−2.07%**: Gate Chain keeps paying about the same **103K GT**, and the next quarterly burn, due in early January 2027, should take about **2.27M GT** if Gate spends the same dollar amount as last quarter at today's price of **$9.85**. The label is simple: GT is deflationary, and the only thing that drives it is how much Gate chooses to burn.

      
## Sell pressure: where new GT comes from

      
Protocol inflation is the one real source of new GT. Gate Chain creates **0.4 GT** with every block and shares it among the nodes that make blocks and the people who stake with them. Between Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026 the chain made **257,480 blocks**, about one every 30 seconds, so it paid out **102,992 GT**, roughly **1,144 GT a day**. Since the chain started in 2020 this pay has added about **8.87M GT** in total, out of a budget of 30M set aside for it. About **40.04M GT** is staked on the chain today. No change to the block pay has been announced, so we expect about the same 103K GT in the next 90 days.

      
Vesting unlocks add nothing. Some unlock calendars still show **1.5M GT** on the 26th of every month, carried over from a plan written in 2019. But only **12.25M GT** sits outside the circulating count, all of it in one insurance fund, and that fund did not move in these 90 days. So those calendar dates did not bring a single new coin to the market.

      
Foundation and unscheduled unlocks booked zero too. Gate does move coins from its insurance fund into the wallet that pays for the burns: **2.57M GT** on Jul 4 2026, just after the Q2 burn, and a much larger move of about **15.18M GT** in January 2026, when Gate tidied up how GT is held across its chains. These top-ups have no fixed size or date, and the fund held the same **12,251,232 GT** on both Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026. No court case or bankruptcy estate holds GT either.

      
## Buy pressure: where new GT goes

      
The quarterly buyback-and-burn is the force that matters. Gate buys GT with part of its exchange profits and sends it to a burn address that nobody can spend from. On Oct 4 2026 it burned **1,987,321 GT** for the third quarter, worth over **$22.35M**, and that address now holds **191.93M GT**, about 64% of the 300M GT that first existed. The burn came out of Gate's own burn wallet, which counts as circulating, so the whole amount left the market. The last four burns came on Jan 8, Apr 26, Jul 4 and Oct 4 2026, so the Q4 2026 burn should fall in early January 2027, inside the next 90 days.

      
The size of each burn follows Gate's results in dollars, not a fixed number of coins. The last four burns ranged from **1.99M** to **2.57M GT**. If the next one spends the same $22.35M at today's price, it removes about **2.27M GT**; a weaker quarter for the exchange or a higher GT price would make it smaller.

      
The fee burn is real but tiny. Gate Chain destroys the base fee of every transaction, yet the chain is quiet, and well under 100 GT went this way in 90 days, too little to count. Fees on Gate Layer, the newer network built on top of Gate Chain, are kept by the network rather than burned. Gate's buying all feeds the burn, so there is no separate foundation buy, and the **40.04M GT** staked on Gate Chain still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing from the count.

      
## Foundation and overhang

      
The one pile to watch is Gate's GT insurance fund, holding **12,251,232 GT** on Oct 25 2026. It is the only GT not counted as circulating, and Gate has said it will settle with this fund from time to time in line with its burns. If Gate tops up the burn wallet to cover the Oct 4 burn, up to **1.99M GT** would join the market, and the next 90 days would end close to flat instead of **−2.07%**.

      
Gate's burn wallet held **10.63M GT** after the Oct 4 burn. It already counts as circulating, so the coins it holds add nothing new; it is simply where the next burn will most likely come from. The coins locked in the bridges between Ethereum, Gate Chain and Gate Layer back the GT on the other side and are not a separate pile.

      
## How GT compares to other exchange tokens

      
GT works much like BNB: both shrink because the exchange behind them burns coins every quarter, and both have a fee burn on their own chain that matters far less. The difference is that Gate Chain still pays a small amount of new GT to its nodes, about 0.1% of supply every 90 days, while BNB has no new coins at all. Against that, GT's burns are large for its size, close to 2% of supply each quarter.

      
OKB took another path: OKX burned most of its supply at once in 2025 and fixed what was left, so OKB no longer shrinks quarter by quarter. GT sits in between: no hard cap is left to reach, and its supply keeps falling only for as long as Gate keeps buying and burning. That makes the burn a promise from one company, not a rule in code, and it is the point to keep in mind when comparing GT with coins whose limits are written into the chain.

      
## What to watch in the next 90 days

      
Oct 26 2026 is the next date on the old monthly unlock calendar. We expect nothing to happen; if the insurance fund does fall that day, the coins would count as new supply.

      
Any day: an insurance fund top-up for the Oct 4 2026 burn. A move of up to **1.99M GT** from the fund to the burn wallet would cancel most of the next quarter's shrinking.

      
Early January 2027: the Q4 2026 burn. Our reading assumes about **2.27M GT**; the real size will follow Gate's fourth-quarter results and the GT price when it buys.

      
Through Jan 23 2027: Gate Chain's block pay. It has stayed at 0.4 GT per block, about 103K GT every 90 days, and about 21.1M GT of its 30M budget is left. Any change to that pay would show up here.

      
## Summary

      
GT supply fell **1.80%** over the 90 days to Oct 25 2026, and we expect about **2.07%** less over the next 90 days. Gate burned **1,987,321 GT** on Oct 4 2026, while Gate Chain added only **102,992 GT** of new block pay, and nothing vests. The main risk is that Gate refills its burn wallet from the **12.25M GT** insurance fund, which would add coins back to the market and cancel much of a burn. As long as the quarterly burns keep coming at about this size, GT keeps shrinking by close to 2% a quarter.

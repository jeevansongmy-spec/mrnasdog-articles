# GT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking

GT, the token of the Gate exchange, is shrinking. From Aug 24 to Nov 22 2026 Gate Chain paid out **102,985 GT** of new mining rewards, while Gate burned **1,987,321 GT** in its Q3 2026 buyback round, so the circulating supply fell about **1.80%**. With the Q4 2026 burn expected in early January 2027, GT is set to shrink about **3.06%** over the next 90 days; other trackers show **−1.78%** for the past 90 days, the same picture.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 22 2026, GT supply moved by **−1.80%**. Other market trackers put the same 90 days at **−1.78%**, a difference of only **0.02 points**, so the GT coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days look deeper still, at about **−3.06%**, because one more quarterly burn is due and GT now trades lower, so the same dollars buy and burn more coins. In one line: GT is an exchange token that is deflationary by a quarterly buyback-and-burn, with a thin stream of new coins from its own chain.

## Sell pressure: where new GT comes from

The only new GT comes from Gate Chain itself. Every block, roughly once every 30 seconds, the chain creates **0.4 GT** and pays it to its consensus nodes and the people who stake with them. From Aug 24 to Nov 22 2026 that added up to **102,985 GT**, about 0.1% of the circulating supply. Gate set aside a 30M GT mining pot for this when Gate Chain launched; about **8.9M GT** has been paid out so far and about **21.1M GT** is still to come, so this slow drip runs for many more years at today's pace.

Vesting unlocks add **0 GT**. An old 2019 plan still lists 1.5M GT a month for the community and the foundation, with the next dates on Nov 26 2026 and Dec 26 2026. But on-chain, the only GT held outside the market is the GT insurance fund, and it did not move at all between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026, so those calendar dates bring no new GT to market.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks also add **0 GT** this window. The insurance fund has released coins before, but only twice in the past year and with no published schedule, so nothing is booked for the next 90 days. Long-term locks and bankruptcy payouts add **0 GT**: no court or bankrupt firm holds GT, and the **52.4M GT** locked in the bridge to Gate Chain simply backs the GT on that chain one for one.

## Buy pressure: where new GT goes

The big force on GT is the buyback-and-burn. About once a quarter Gate buys GT back and sends it to a burn address that has never sent a single coin out. On Oct 4 2026, Gate's burn wallet sent **1,987,321 GT** there for Q3 2026, a round Gate valued at over **$22.35M**. That one round outweighed 90 days of mining pay about nineteen times over. Since GT launched, the burn address has collected **191.93M GT**, close to 64% of the original 300M.

The next round, for Q4 2026, is expected in early January 2027; last year's Q4 round landed on Jan 8 2026, and rounds since early 2024 have come between 54 and 128 days apart. GT has fallen to about $6.76, so if Gate spends the same dollars as in Q3, that round would burn about **3.31M GT**, more than any single round in the last two years. Recent rounds ranged from 1.99M to 2.57M GT, so a smaller budget would shrink this number.

The protocol fee burn books **0 GT**. Gate Layer, the newer network that uses GT for gas, collects its fees in fee wallets instead of destroying them; its base-fee wallet gained only about 60 GT in the window. Gate Chain's own fees are too small to show up in its supply. Foundation buying books **0 GT**, because every GT Gate buys back ends up in the quarterly burn. New long-term locks book **0 GT**: staked GT still counts as circulating, and no new long lock was set up.

## Foundation and overhang

The GT insurance fund is the one team-controlled pile outside the market. It holds **12.25M GT** and held exactly that amount from Aug 24 to Nov 22 2026. It is not idle forever: in January 2026 it sent **15.18M GT** to Gate's burn wallet, and on Jul 4 2026 it sent **2.57M GT** there about an hour after that quarter's burn, refilling the wallet by the exact amount it had just burned. If the fund refills the wallet again after the Q4 2026 burn, the coins it releases would cancel most of that burn, and supply would stay roughly flat for the quarter instead of falling.

Gate's burn wallet holds **10.63M GT**. Those coins already count as circulating, so they add nothing when they move; they only matter when they are burned. The bridge lock of 52.4M GT on Ethereum mirrors the GT on Gate Chain. We watch the insurance fund and the burn wallet on-chain: if the insurance fund's balance falls, the outflow enters Foundation and unscheduled unlocks on the GT coin page.

## How GT compares to other exchange tokens

Most big exchange tokens shrink through a burn paid for by the exchange, and GT belongs to that group. BNB burns an amount set each quarter plus part of its gas fees, so its supply falls on a set path. OKB burned most of its supply in one large step and now has a fixed total. LEO is bought back with a share of its parent company's income. GT sits closest to BNB: a quarterly burn funded by the exchange, about 2M to 2.6M GT a round lately, roughly 2% of the circulating supply each time.

Two things set GT apart. First, GT still has a small built-in mint: Gate Chain pays out 0.4 GT a block, so GT is not a pure fixed-supply coin, though the mint is tiny next to the burn. Second, GT has an insurance fund of 12.25M GT that can refill the burn wallet. When it does, part of a burn is paid from coins that were outside the market, and the net drop in circulating GT is smaller than the burn headline. Exchange tokens whose burn coins come only from open-market buying do not carry this catch.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The Q4 2026 GT burn, expected in early January 2027, decides most of the next 90 days; its size will show whether Gate's burn budget held up while GT fell from about $9.80 on Nov 12 2026 to $6.76 on Nov 22 2026.

The insurance fund's 12.25M GT balance after that burn: a refill of the burn wallet within hours of the burn, as on Jul 4 2026, would cancel most of the quarter's drop in circulating supply.

The old monthly unlock dates on Nov 26 2026, Dec 26 2026 and Jan 26 2027: they only matter if GT actually leaves the insurance fund on those days, which it has not done on any of the recent dates.

Gate Chain's mining pay of 0.4 GT a block, and any Gate Layer upgrade that starts destroying fees instead of collecting them, which would add a second, steady burn.

## Summary

GT, the Gate exchange token, shrank about **1.80%** in the 90 days to Nov 22 2026 and is projected to shrink about **3.06%** in the next 90 days. The driver is Gate's quarterly buyback-and-burn, which removed **1,987,321 GT** on Oct 4 2026, against only **102,985 GT** of new mining pay from Gate Chain. The main risk to that reading is the 12.25M GT insurance fund, which has refilled Gate's burn wallet after a burn before and could again. Of the original 300M GT, about **191.93M** has already been burned.

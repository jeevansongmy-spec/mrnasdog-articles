# DOGE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
Looking for the buy-or-sell answer? It is on the DOGE coin page, with demand and price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/doge](https://mrnasdog.com/research/doge)

Dogecoin's supply is growing, and it is set to keep growing at the same pace. Miners earn **10,000 new DOGE** in every block, there is no supply cap, and nothing in the network destroys or buys back coins. Between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026 miners received **1,228,620,000 DOGE**, nothing came back out, and supply rose **+0.78%**; other supply trackers show about **+0.82%** for the same stretch, close enough that no warning is needed.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

DOGE is a coin whose supply only goes one way. Over the 90 days to Nov 9 2026, new mining rewards added **1.23B DOGE** and no burn, buyback or lock removed any, so the net change was **+0.78%** of the roughly **156.68B DOGE** in circulation. Other supply trackers put the same 90 days at **+0.82%**, a difference of **0.03 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The label is simple: steady, predictable inflation of about **3.2% a year**, paid to miners, with no scheduled event in the next 90 days that would make it faster or slower.

## Sell pressure: where new DOGE comes from

**Protocol inflation** is the whole story. Every Dogecoin block pays its miner a fixed **10,000 DOGE**, a reward that has not changed since 2015 and has no halving. Between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026 miners found **122,862 blocks**, a little slower than the one-a-minute target, which made **1,228,620,000 new DOGE**, about **13.65M DOGE a day**. Because the reward is per block, the pace of block-making sets the pace of new supply; it held steady near 1,365 blocks a day for the whole stretch, so the next 90 days should bring about the same **1.23B DOGE**.

**Vesting unlocks** are zero. Dogecoin launched in December 2013 with no sale and no share for a team or investors, so there is no locked pile waiting for a release date.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks** are zero. The Dogecoin Foundation's developer fund pays **500,000 DOGE** to code contributors only when a new Dogecoin Core release comes out, and the last release was Dec 1 2024. The fund sent nothing out between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy** coins are zero. No court case or failed lender is holding DOGE to pay back to customers later.

## Buy pressure: where new DOGE goes

**Programmatic buyback** is zero. Dogecoin has no treasury or contract that buys coins back, and no income to pay for one.

**Protocol fee burn** is zero. Unlike Ethereum, Dogecoin does not destroy fees. Users paid about **814,236 DOGE** in fees between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026, and all of it went to miners. A well-known dead address that nobody can spend from holds about **1.85B DOGE**, but only **0.13 DOGE** landed there in the 90 days, and those coins have always been counted as circulating.

**Foundation buy** is zero. The Foundation does not buy DOGE on the market. Companies and funds trade coins that are already circulating, so their buying and selling does not change the count: CleanCore sold its **463M DOGE** in July 2026, and Bitwise closed its DOGE fund on Oct 14 2026.

**New long-term lock** is zero. Dogecoin has no staking, so there is nowhere for holders to lock coins away from the market.

## Foundation and overhang

Dogecoin has no team allocation and no investor unlocks, so the overhang is small. The one pile tied to the project is the Dogecoin Foundation's developer fund, a shared wallet that needs three of five signers. It holds **5,003,402 DOGE**, unchanged through the whole 90 days, and it would release **500,000 DOGE** on each future Dogecoin Core release. No release has a date, so nothing is counted for the next 90 days.

House of Doge, the Foundation's former business partner, reported holding no DOGE of its own on Jun 30 2026. Its exclusive deal with the Foundation ended on Oct 19 2026, its shares were suspended from Nasdaq on Nov 3 2026, and on Nov 5 2026 the 21Shares Dogecoin fund ended its support contract with the company. None of this moves new DOGE into the market.

## How DOGE compares to other proof-of-work chains

Most proof-of-work coins cut their miner rewards over time. Bitcoin halves its reward about every four years toward a fixed cap of 21M coins, and Litecoin, which shares its miners with Dogecoin, does the same toward 84M coins. Dogecoin chose the opposite path: a flat **10,000 DOGE** per block forever, with no cap.

The closest match is Monero, which also pays miners a small reward forever. The difference is size: Dogecoin adds about **5B DOGE a year**, roughly **3.2%** of today's supply. Because the yearly amount is fixed while the supply keeps growing, that percentage falls a little every year, but it stays well above Bitcoin's, which grew about 0.2% over the same 90 days.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Around Nov 16 2026:** House of Doge's next quarterly report is due. It will show whether the company bought or still holds any DOGE after its exclusive Foundation deal ended.

**Nov 18 2026:** the deadline for House of Doge to hand its Such App payment app to Dogecoin Labs. After that date either side may walk away if the deal has not closed.

**Dec 31 2026:** House of Doge must drop the Doge name from its company names under the deal that ended its partnership with the Foundation.

**No date yet:** the next Dogecoin Core release. When it ships, the developer fund pays out **500,000 DOGE** to contributors, less than one hour of mining rewards.

**Every day:** the block pace. Blocks have come about every 63 seconds, about 1,365 a day. A big change in mining power would push new supply a few percent above or below the **1.23B DOGE** expected.

## Summary

Dogecoin miners earn a fixed **10,000 DOGE** per block with no cap and no halving, which added **1.23B DOGE** between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026.

Nothing in Dogecoin burns, buys back or locks coins, so all of that new supply stays in the market, and DOGE supply grew **+0.78%** in 90 days.

There are no team or investor unlocks; the only project-linked pile is the Foundation's **5.0M DOGE** developer fund, which paid nothing out.

The next 90 days should look the same, about **1.23B** new DOGE and **+0.78%** supply growth.

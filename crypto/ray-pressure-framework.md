# RAY Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

RAY supply is **roughly steady**: the only new coins reaching the market are staking rewards from Raydium's mining reserve, **425,000 RAY** between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026, and no new RAY can ever be minted beyond the **555M** cap. Against that, nothing was taken out of the circulating count, because the **4.44M RAY** that fees bought back sit in a protocol wallet that is already counted as circulating, so supply grew about **+0.16%**, while other trackers show **+0.08%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 21 to Nov 19 2026, the circulating supply of RAY rose by **+0.16%**, and the next 90 days should look about the same, at **+0.16%**. Other trackers put the same 90 days at **+0.08%**, a difference of **0.08 points**; the two figures agree closely and the coin page carries no warning note. Raydium pays its stakers from a reserve set aside at launch, its team coins finished vesting in 2024, and the fee buyback keeps the coins it buys rather than burning them. RAY is a **fixed-cap coin with a slow reserve drip**, where the buyback changes who holds RAY but not how many coins circulate.

## Sell pressure: where new RAY comes from

**Protocol inflation added 425,000 RAY.** RAY has no mint: the mint authority was switched off, so every RAY that will ever exist already exists. New coins reach the market only when Raydium releases them from its reserves. In this window a release wallet topped up the staking reward pot three times, with **200,000 RAY** on Sep 10, **125,000 RAY** on Sep 28 and **100,000 RAY** on Oct 14 2026. Each top-up moved coins from a wallet outside the circulating count into a pot that pays stakers, so each one added to the count on the day it landed. The pot pays about **3.7% a year** on the **44.5M RAY** now staked, which works out to roughly the same 425,000 RAY over the next 90 days.

**Vesting unlocks added nothing.** The team and seed coins, about a quarter of all RAY, were locked for a year after the February 2021 launch and then released day by day until Feb 21 2024. Nothing has been on a vesting schedule since.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added nothing.** Raydium's treasury wallet still holds **282.66M RAY** that is not counted as circulating, and not one of its accounts sent out coins between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026. The mining reserve inside it last released coins in February 2024.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins added nothing.** No court, estate or failed firm is holding RAY for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new RAY goes

**The programmatic buyback removed nothing from the count.** Raydium sends **12%** of every trading fee to buy RAY on the open market, and between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026 that bought about **4.44M RAY**, almost two thirds of it in September. Raydium keeps those coins in a public protocol wallet that now holds **88.73M RAY**. The coins are not burned, and that wallet is part of the circulating count, so the buying takes coins off the market without lowering the number of RAY in circulation.

**The protocol fee burn is zero.** Raydium does not burn any of its fees. Holders destroyed about 284 RAY on their own over the 90 days, far too little to change the count.

**Foundation buying is zero.** Beyond the fee buyback there is no plan to buy RAY. The **4%** of pool fees that goes to the treasury is kept in USDC.

**New long-term locks are zero.** Staked RAY, about **44.5M** today, still counts as circulating, so staking takes nothing out, and no new lock of RAY was announced.

## Foundation and overhang

Raydium's treasury wallet is the big overhang. It holds **282.66M RAY**, more than the whole circulating supply of **269.96M**, split into **138.59M** for partners and the ecosystem, **123.32M** of mining reserve, **11.1M** for advisors, **7.3M** of team coins and **2.35M** from the community and seed pool. None of these accounts has sent out coins since February 2024, and Raydium has published no schedule for them.

Two more wallets are already part of the circulating count. The buyback wallet holds **88.73M RAY**, nearly a third of all circulating RAY, and the protocol multisig holds **8.87M RAY**. Selling from either would not raise the count, but it would put real coins back on the market. The release wallet that feeds stakers holds only **154,373 RAY**. We read these balances on-chain at every rebuild: if the buyback wallet or the multisig shrinks between rebuilds, the outflow is shown as a sell row at the next rebuild.

## How RAY compares to other DEX tokens

Most big exchange tokens now turn fees into buying, but they differ in what happens next. PancakeSwap and Uniswap burn the coins their fees remove, and Pump.fun burns what it buys, so their circulating counts fall. Jupiter, the other large Solana trading app, works like Raydium: it buys its coin and keeps it in a public wallet, so its count does not drop either.

RAY's supply side is quieter than most. There are no investor unlocks left, no inflation built into the token, and the only fresh coins are staking rewards near **0.6% a year** of the circulating supply. The open question is not new coins but old ones: the treasury's **282.66M RAY** and the **88.73M RAY** of kept buybacks are large next to the market, and both could move only by Raydium's own choice.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The next reserve release.** The release wallet that tops up the staking pot holds **154,373 RAY**, about one more month of top-ups at the autumn pace. Expect a fresh release from the **123.32M RAY** mining reserve, the first since February 2024, likely before the end of December 2026. It would not change the count by itself; the payouts to stakers would.

**Fees and the buyback pace.** Users paid about **$12.4M** in Raydium fees in the 30 days to Nov 19 2026, down from a peak of about **$46.3M** in the 30 days to Oct 5 2026. The buyback moves with fees, so a quieter market means less RAY bought.

**Stock tokens.** Tokens that track US shares have become Raydium's biggest fee source. Anthropic's share token began trading on Solana on Nov 19 2026, the day of its stock market debut, with a main trading pool on Raydium.

**The Q4 holder report.** Raydium's next quarterly report, due in January 2027, should show the full-quarter buyback and whether the stake and the reward rate changed.

## Summary

RAY's circulating supply grew about **+0.16%** between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026, and should grow about the same in the next 90 days.

All of that growth is staking rewards from the mining reserve, **425,000 RAY**, because no new RAY can be minted and every vesting schedule ended in 2024.

Raydium's fee buyback bought about **4.44M RAY** in the same 90 days, but it keeps the coins in a wallet that already counts as circulating, so the count does not shrink.

The real supply risk is the **282.66M RAY** still in Raydium's treasury and the **88.73M RAY** of kept buybacks, neither of which has a published plan.

# RAY Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

RAY supply is roughly steady: between Aug 2 and Oct 31 2026 stakers were sent **625,000 RAY** from the mining reserve, nothing was burned or locked away, and circulating supply grew **+0.23%**. RAY has a fixed cap of **555M** and no way to make new coins, so the only new supply is old reserve coins paid out as staking rewards. Raydium's fees bought about **4.69M RAY** in the same 90 days, but those coins are kept in a wallet that already counts as circulating; other supply trackers show **+0.34%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 31 2026, RAY's circulating supply rose by **+0.23%**, from staking rewards alone, and the next 90 days look about the same. Other supply trackers put the same period at **+0.34%**, a difference of only **0.10 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The label that fits is a capped DEX token with a thin reward trickle and a large fee buyback that is held, not burned.

## Sell pressure: where new RAY comes from

**Staking rewards are the only source of new RAY.** Every RAY was created at launch in February 2021, and the token's mint authority is switched off, so nobody can print more. What reaches the market instead are coins from the **123.32M RAY** mining reserve. The reserve fills a reward wallet, and that wallet tops up the RAY staking pool. Between Aug 2 and Oct 31 2026 it did so four times, on Aug 10, Sep 10, Sep 28 and Oct 14, for **625,000 RAY** in total. Stakers collected about **547,000 RAY** of that over the window, and the rest waits in the pool to be claimed. The project's own documents describe about 1.9M RAY a year of emissions, a little below this pace.

The reward wallet now holds only **154,373 RAY**. Keeping the same pace for another 90 days needs a fresh transfer from the mining reserve. That has happened seven times since December 2022, most recently **2.9M RAY** on Feb 25 2024, so we book the next 90 days at the same **625,000 RAY**.

**Vesting adds nothing.** Team and seed coins unlocked day by day from early 2022 and finished on Feb 21 2024, and no new vesting schedule has been announced since. **Unscheduled releases added nothing either.** Five launch wallets still hold RAY that does not count as circulating, and none of them moved a single coin between Aug 2 and Oct 31 2026; the details are in the overhang section below. **There is no bankruptcy estate** or court-run pile of RAY waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new RAY goes

**The buyback is real and large, but it does not shrink the count.** On every Raydium pool, 12% of the trading fee goes to buying RAY on the open market. In the 90 days to Oct 31 2026 that bought about **4.69M RAY**, roughly 7.5 times the new staking rewards. The bought coins are not burned. They are kept in a public buyback wallet that now holds **88.19M RAY**, about a third of everything in circulation. Because that wallet already counts as circulating supply, moving coins into it takes nothing out of the count, so the buyback books **0** in our supply total. It is still buying pressure on the market, just not a cut to supply.

**Raydium has no fee burn.** Since launch only about **2,673 RAY** has been destroyed, by users sending coins away by choice, out of 555M. **No foundation buying** outside the fee buyback was found. **Staking is not a lock** in this count: about **44.43M RAY** is staked, but staked RAY can be withdrawn and still counts as circulating, so it removes nothing.

## Foundation and overhang

RAY's biggest supply question is not the reward trickle but the coins that sit outside circulation. Five launch wallets, all controlled by one key, hold them: partnership and ecosystem **138.59M RAY**, the mining reserve **123.32M RAY**, advisors **11.10M RAY**, team **7.30M RAY**, and community and seed **2.35M RAY**. Together that is more than half of all RAY. None of these wallets has a published release schedule. The partnership wallet last sent coins in September 2023, the team and seed wallets in February 2024, and the advisor wallet has never sent any since 2021.

Two more wallets are controlled by the project but already count as circulating: the protocol's own multisig with **8.87M RAY**, and the buyback wallet with **88.19M RAY**. If either sells, the coins were already counted, but the market would still have to absorb them. No decision has been published on what happens to the bought-back RAY. We read every one of these balances at each rebuild. If any launch wallet's balance falls between rebuilds, that outflow enters the foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next one.

## How RAY compares to other DEX tokens

RAY sits in the small group of DEX tokens with a hard cap and no new minting at all. CAKE, by contrast, still mints new coins for its farms every block and relies on weekly buybacks that are burned to offset them. UNI's fee switch destroys UNI when fees are claimed, so its buyback really shrinks supply, while the treasury pays out a fixed quarterly budget. JUP burns the fees projects pay to get their tokens verified, in batches with no set date.

RAY's design differs on the one point that matters most for supply: its buyback is held, not burned. That makes RAY's buyback a steady buyer on the market but leaves the coins in a project wallet that could, in principle, be sold or handed out later. A burn-based buyback like UNI's or CAKE's closes that door; RAY's keeps it open, and with **88.19M RAY** already inside, the size of that wallet is now larger than all but the two largest launch reserves.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The next staking-pool top-up, likely in November 2026.** The reward wallet has 154,373 RAY left; a new transfer from the 123.32M mining reserve would be the first since Feb 25 2024.

**Any move by the five launch wallets.** A transfer out of the 138.59M partnership and ecosystem wallet would be the first since September 2023 and would enter the count in full.

**A decision on the bought-back RAY.** A vote to burn the 88.19M RAY would cut circulating supply by about a third; a vote to spend or hand it out would add selling pressure without changing the count.

**The buyback pace through Jan 29 2027.** It tracks trading fees, which jumped with tokenized-stock trading in September 2026 and cooled in late October 2026.

## Summary

RAY's supply is roughly steady: **+0.23%** in the 90 days to Oct 31 2026, all of it staking rewards paid from a reserve that was filled at launch, under a fixed cap of 555M with no way to mint more. Raydium's fees bought about **4.69M RAY** in the same period, roughly 7.5 times the new rewards, but the coins are held in a wallet that already counts as circulating, so they do not reduce supply. The real risk is the overhang: more than half of all RAY sits in launch wallets with no release schedule, and another **88.19M RAY** sits in the buyback wallet with no decided future.

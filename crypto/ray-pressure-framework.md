# RAY Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

RAY supply is **roughly steady**. No new RAY can ever be made, because the 555M cap was fully minted in 2021 and the right to mint was switched off, so the only new coins reaching the market are staking rewards paid out of a locked wallet: **425,000 RAY** between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026. Fee buybacks bought **4.52M RAY** in the same 90 days, but Raydium keeps those coins in a wallet that still counts as circulating, so the net change is **+0.16%**, and other supply trackers show about **+0.21%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 11 to Nov 9 2026, circulating RAY grew by **425,000** coins, or **+0.16%** of the **269.96M** RAY counted as circulating. We expect about the same, **+0.16%**, in the next 90 days, because the staking rewards are the only source of new RAY and they have been paid at a steady pace all year. Other supply trackers put the same 90 days at **+0.21%**. The two readings sit about 0.05 points apart, which is close enough that the RAY coin page carries no warning note. The label is mixed flows with supply roughly steady: a small, slow drip in, and nothing that takes RAY out of the count.

## Sell pressure: where new RAY comes from

Protocol inflation added **425,000 RAY**. Raydium funds its staking rewards from a release wallet that sits outside the circulating count. That wallet sent 200,000 RAY on Sep 10 2026, 125,000 RAY on Sep 28 2026 and 100,000 RAY on Oct 14 2026 into the staking reward pot, and from there the rewards go to the roughly **45.9M RAY** that holders have staked, at about 3.6% a year.

The release wallet now holds only **154,373 RAY**, so it will need a refill soon. Past refills came from the mining reserve, most recently 2.9M RAY on Feb 25 2024. A refill only moves coins between two locked wallets, so it adds nothing until the rewards are paid on to stakers.

Vesting unlocks added **0**. The team and seed round finished unlocking on Feb 21 2024, and no other schedule exists.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **0**. None of the six launch wallets sent a single RAY out between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins added **0**. No court case, hack estate or failed firm is waiting to pay out RAY.

## Buy pressure: where new RAY goes

The programmatic buyback is large but books **0**. Every Raydium pool sends 12% of its trading fee to buy RAY on the open market, and between Aug 11 and Nov 9 2026 that bought **4.52M RAY**, more than ten times the new staking rewards. Raydium does not burn these coins. It keeps them in a public buyback wallet, now about **88.44M RAY**, and that wallet is still counted as circulating, so the purchases take RAY off the market without changing the count.

The protocol fee burn is **0**. Raydium has no fee burn. A handful of holders destroyed about 166 RAY on their own between Sep 19 and Nov 9 2026, which is too small to show.

Foundation buying is **0**. The team does not buy RAY outside the fee buyback.

New long-term locks are **0**. Staked RAY still counts as circulating and can be withdrawn, so more staking does not shrink the count.

## Foundation and overhang

The big number to know is **282.66M RAY**, a little over half of all RAY, still held in the launch wallets and not counted as circulating. The partnership and ecosystem wallet holds **138.59M RAY**, the mining reserve **123.32M RAY**, the advisors wallet **11.1M RAY**, the team wallet **7.3M RAY** and the community and seed wallet **2.35M RAY**. None of them has sent RAY out since early 2024. Raydium has said these coins have no release schedule and no near-term plan to be released, and that any change will be announced in public.

Two more wallets are already inside the circulating count. The buyback wallet holds about **88.44M RAY**, close to a third of everything circulating, and the protocol wallet holds about **8.87M RAY**. Neither adds to supply today, but if either one starts selling, that RAY would reach the market, so we watch both every day.

## How RAY compares to other exchange tokens

Most exchange tokens that buy back with fees fall into two camps. Some burn what they buy, so every purchase shrinks the supply on the spot. Others keep the bought coins in a treasury, which takes them off the market but leaves them in the count and leaves a choice for later. RAY is firmly in the second camp, and its buyback wallet has grown into one of the biggest holders of the coin.

On new supply, RAY is quieter than most. Many younger exchange tokens still release investor and team coins every month. RAY finished vesting in 2024 and adds only staking rewards, around a quarter of a million to half a million RAY a quarter. The catch is the size of what is still locked: more than half of the 555M cap has not been released, and Raydium decides if and when that changes.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

By early December 2026, watch for the next staking top-up. The release wallet holds 154,373 RAY and the reward pot pays stakers about 6,000 RAY a day, so a refill from the mining reserve is likely before long. A larger refill than usual would be the first sign of a faster pace.

Every day, watch the buyback wallet. It took in about 241,700 RAY between Nov 2 and Nov 9 2026. It has never sold, and any RAY that left it would be new supply on the market.

Through December 2026, watch Raydium's fees. In the 30 days to Nov 9 2026 users paid about $12.6M, down from about $41.0M in the 30 days to Oct 9 2026, so the buyback is now buying far less each week than it did in early autumn.

By Feb 7 2027, the end of the next 90-day window, watch for any word on the locked launch wallets. Raydium has said any change to its non-circulating RAY will be announced in public. A plan to release, burn or lock those 282.66M RAY would change this picture more than anything else.

## Summary

RAY supply grew **+0.16%** in the 90 days to Nov 9 2026, all of it **425,000 RAY** of staking rewards from a locked wallet.

Raydium's fee buyback bought **4.52M RAY** in the same 90 days, but it keeps the coins in a wallet that still counts as circulating, so the buying does not shrink the count.

No RAY vests and no new RAY can ever be minted, but **282.66M RAY** still waits in launch wallets with no release schedule.

For the next 90 days we expect about the same **+0.16%**, with the next staking top-up and any change to the locked wallets as the two things to watch.

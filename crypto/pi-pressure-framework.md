Pi Network (PI) is the rare asset whose blockchain **mints nothing at all** and is still one of the most inflationary tokens the Pressure Framework tracks. The Pi ledger held exactly **100,000,000,000 PI** on both Apr 26 2026 and Jul 24 2026 — the entire cap was created at genesis — yet about **700M PI** of mined balances migrate onto Pi Mainnet every 90 days and another **387M PI** of Pioneer lockups expire on a published on-chain calendar. Against that, there is **no buyback and no burn**: the only offsets are roughly **491M PI** of newly written voluntary lockups and about **2.4M PI** of fees parked in the protocol fee pool. The framework nets that to about **+5.43%** supply growth over the next 90 days, against **10.94B PI** circulating; our supply monitor reads the trailing 90 days at **+6.38%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the window opening Jul 25 2026, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **PI at about +5.43% net** supply growth over the next 90 days — roughly **1,087M PI** of Mainnet migration and expiring lockups against about **493M PI** of new lockups and fee-pool accrual. The last 90 days read **+5.36%**, while our supply monitor reads the realized change at **+6.38%**, a gap of about **1.02 percentage points** that ships a **⚠ monitor-gap chip**. The deep walk on Jul 25 2026 tied that residual to timing rather than to a hidden flow: the Pi chain's lockup-expiry calendar was last rebuilt on Jun 15 2026, so short lockups written after that date are missing from the unlock row, and the migration sample sits in the cooling half of a window whose opening month pushed roughly 231M PI onto the float. Pi Network is **structurally inflationary on a pre-mined float that is still only a tenth of its cap** — the dilution is not created by the chain, it is released by it.

## Sell pressure: where new PI comes from

Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is the largest single force at about **700M PI** over 90 days, and it is worth being precise about what it is. Pi Network runs its own Stellar-consensus mainnet, and that ledger's coin count sat at exactly **100,000,000,000 PI** at sequence 26,367,593 on Apr 26 2026 and again at sequence 27,860,214 on Jul 24 2026. Nothing was minted. What the framework books as protocol inflation is the **Mainnet migration** pipe: Pi mined inside the mobile app becomes real, transferable on-chain PI once a Pioneer clears identity verification, and the on-chain migration ledger shows about **186.7M PI** moving across the 24 days to Jun 3 2026 — a run rate near **7.78M PI a day**, or roughly 700M over a 90-day window. Only **16.83B PI** of the 100B cap has migrated at all, so this pipe has years of fuel behind it.

Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — adds about **387M PI** over the next 90 days and ran near **380M PI** over the last. When a Pioneer migrates, much of the balance goes into a voluntary lockup, and those lockups sit on the Pi ledger as claimable balances with a hard expiry date, which makes the release calendar directly readable rather than a matter of trust. That calendar releases about **103.7M PI** in July 2026, **127.9M** in August, **132.6M** in September and **138.2M** in October, on the way to roughly **775.8M PI** across the second half of 2026. The monthly figure climbs almost every month, so Pi's vesting unlock row gets heavier as the window rolls forward, not lighter. Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is booked at **zero** because no dated Core Team market release fired inside the window, and Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero** because no bankruptcy estate or court-ordered distribution attaches to Pi Network.

## Buy pressure: where new PI goes

Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is **zero**, and on a page this heavy that absence is the loudest fact on it. Pioneers have spent 2026 publicly petitioning the Pi Core Team for a transparent buyback-and-burn, with proposals ranging as far as burning half the remaining supply; no such programme has been announced, so there is nothing to book. Buy #3 — Foundation buy — is likewise **zero**: Pi Network's reserves are pre-mined allocations, not PI purchased off the market, and no discretionary open-market buying has been observed or disclosed. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is a genuine but tiny **2.4M PI**: every Pi transaction pays a fee out of the sender's balance into a protocol fee pool the network never redistributes, and that pool grew from about **7.08M** to about **9.49M PI** across the window. Real, permanent, and about a fifth of one percent of the sell side.

The one offset that actually matters is Buy #4 — new long-term lock — at about **491M PI**. Pi's lockup feature pays a mining-rate bonus for committing balances, and freshly migrated Pi frequently lands straight into one, which means a large share of the migration in Sell #1 never reaches the market at all. The arithmetic is visible on-chain: the locked pool stood near **6,137M PI** on Jun 15 2026 and near **6,189M PI** on Jul 24 2026 even though roughly **161M PI** of lockups expired in between, so about **213M PI** of new lockups were written across 39 days — a run rate near 491M over 90 days. Pi's lock pool is growing rather than draining, and that is the only mechanism on this page pointing supply the other way.

## Foundation and overhang

Pi Network's team-controlled overhang is one of the largest in crypto by proportion. The **Core Team allocation is 20% of the 100B cap** and the ecosystem reserve another **10%**, neither carrying a published release schedule — enormous capacity with no announced release plan, so nothing is booked against them and both stay on permanent watch. Sitting alongside them is the **6,189M PI** of unexpired lockups on the Pi ledger, readable balance by balance, and the far larger un-migrated Pioneer reservoir: with only **16.83B PI** migrated against a 100B cap, roughly **83B PI** of mined and allocated supply has never touched the chain. The claimable-balance pool is re-read on-chain each rebuild; the Core Team and ecosystem allocations are re-walked roughly bi-weekly through official disclosure, because they have no on-chain address published as a unit. If any of these balances falls between refreshes through a real market release, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How PI compares to other pre-mined, migration-gated chains

Pi Network belongs to a small class of **fully pre-mined, distribution-gated networks** — chains where the whole supply exists from day one and the only variable is how fast it is handed out. That is the opposite of a halving-model chain like Bitcoin, where the cap is enforced by a mint schedule the market can compute years ahead, and it is also unlike an uncapped proof-of-stake L1, where issuance is a live protocol parameter voters can tune. On Pi, neither lever exists: the ledger's coin count is frozen at 100B and the release rate is set by how many Pioneers clear identity verification, migrate, and let their lockups run out. That makes Pi's inflation a **social and operational variable rather than a monetary one**, and it is why the framework reads dilution on a chain that literally never mints.

The comparison that hurts most is with exchange tokens and fee-burning L1s. An exchange token running a quarterly buy-and-burn converts revenue into permanent supply destruction and can read as clean deflation; a fee-burning L1 destroys a slice of every transaction. Pi Network does neither — the fee pool absorbs about **2.4M PI** a quarter against a sell side near **1,087M PI**, a ratio of roughly one to four hundred. The nearest structural analogue is a large-airdrop token in its multi-year distribution phase, where the float grows on a vesting calendar with no protocol-side buying, except that Pi's distribution runs for years longer and is denominated against a float that is still only about a tenth of the cap. Compared like for like on mechanism, Pi has the release schedule of a long vesting token and the buy side of a token with no treasury policy at all.

And watch **migration throughput**, running near 7.78M PI a day, since a fresh identity-verification wave would push Sell #1 up mechanically.

Pi Network is a fully pre-mined, migration-gated chain whose ledger held exactly 100,000,000,000 PI at both ends of the last 90 days and minted nothing, yet still put about 1,087M PI onto the tradable float through Mainnet migration and expiring Pioneer lockups. There is no buyback and no burn worth the name — about 2.4M PI of fee-pool accrual — so the only meaningful offset is roughly 491M PI of newly written voluntary lockups, leaving the framework at about +5.43% net supply growth over the next 90 days against 10.94B PI circulating. Our supply monitor reads +6.38% over the trailing 90 days, the extra coming from a lockup calendar snapshot that predates the window's newest locks and from a migration sample taken in its cooling half. The key risk is structural rather than dated: with roughly 83B PI still un-migrated behind a 10.94B float and no protocol-side buying anywhere in the design, PI is inflationary today and projected to keep growing, and the 100B cap is a ceiling on the total, not a brake on the release.

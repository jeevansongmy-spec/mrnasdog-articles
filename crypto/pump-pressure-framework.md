# PUMP Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

PUMP, the coin of Pump.fun, grew **+14.35%** over the 90 days to Nov 11 2026 because locked wallets released **83.38B PUMP**, mostly two September payouts to staff and early backers, while the Pump.fun buyback burned **17.58B PUMP**. No new PUMP can ever be made, so the supply can only fall from the 823.3B PUMP left today, and with only 1.88B PUMP due to unlock against about 21.63B PUMP of buyback burning, the next 90 days point to **−4.31%**. Other trackers show **+16.69%** for the same 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026, PUMP supply on the market grew by a net **65.79B PUMP**, or **+14.35%** of the 458.43B PUMP circulating today. Other trackers show **+16.69%**, a gap of **2.34** points. The gap is not a missing flow: those trackers count the same release and the same burn, but measure the rise against the 392.9B PUMP that circulated on Aug 13 2026, before the September payouts made the market bigger. The coins and the dates agree, so the PUMP coin page carries no warning note. Looking ahead, the Pump.fun buyback burn should outweigh the scheduled PUMP unlocks by more than ten to one, for a projected **−4.31%** over the next 90 days. The cite-able label: PUMP was inflationary last quarter on insider payouts and is deflationary ahead on the buyback burn.

## Sell pressure: where new PUMP comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. Pump.fun created all 1,000B PUMP once, on Jul 10 2025, and the power to create more was switched off for good, so no new PUMP is paid to anyone, ever. The supply has only gone down since: 823.3B PUMP is left after the buyback burns.

Vesting unlocks are the one source of new PUMP on the market: **83.38B PUMP** in the window. The main Pump.fun team wallet sent **20B PUMP** on Sep 8 2026 and **54B PUMP** on Sep 13 2026 to two payout wallets for staff and early backers. On Sep 14 2026 one payout wallet placed about 52.58B PUMP into 82 timed payouts that release one twelfth a month, so the PUMP for staff and backers from September 2026 through about August 2027 is already out of the locked wallet. A separate 30B PUMP allocation wallet, on a one-year cliff and then 625M PUMP a month, released **8.13B PUMP** on Aug 26 2026 to catch up the first 13 months, then 625M PUMP on Sep 12 and again on Oct 12 2026, for **9.38B PUMP** in total. In the next 90 days only that allocation wallet is due: 625M PUMP on each of Nov 12 2026, Dec 12 2026 and Jan 12 2027, or **1.88B PUMP**.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are booked at **0**. The main Pump.fun team wallet still holds 229.26B PUMP and pays out in lumps, last on Sep 13 2026, with no public date for the next one, so no PUMP is booked until it moves again. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is also **0**: no court case or bankrupt firm holds PUMP waiting to pay it out.

## Buy pressure: where new PUMP goes

The programmatic buyback is the one PUMP buyer that matters: **17.58B PUMP** destroyed in the window, about **$76.7M** worth. Since Apr 28 2026 half of Pump.fun's net revenue from its launch app, its PumpSwap market and its Terminal trading screen goes to buy-and-burn wallets that buy PUMP on the open market and burn it at once, under a contract Pump.fun set for one year. Because the PUMP is burned on arrival, the buyback leaves no stockpile behind. At the same spend and today's price, the burn would remove about **21.63B PUMP** in the next 90 days.

The protocol fee burn is **0** as a separate line: Pump.fun users pay fees in SOL, not PUMP, so every PUMP burn runs through the buyback above. A Foundation buy is **0**: Pump.fun keeps the other half of its revenue to run the business and has no public plan to buy PUMP with it, and outside companies that buy PUMP buy coins already on the market. A new long-term lock is also **0**: PUMP has no staking, and the timed payouts to staff and backers already count as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

Four wallets hold every PUMP that is not yet on the market, **364.89B PUMP** in all, and all four can be read on-chain at any time. The main Pump.fun team wallet holds **229.26B PUMP**, down from 303.26B PUMP on Aug 13 2026. It is the source of the PUMP for staff and early backers and has paid out in two recent lumps, 62.2B PUMP on Jun 25 2026 and 74B PUMP on Sep 8 and Sep 13 2026. Two wallets that have never sent a coin out since July 2025 hold **80B PUMP** and **35B PUMP**, with no public plan. The 30B allocation wallet holds **20.62B PUMP** and keeps releasing 625M PUMP a month until about August 2029.

Two payout wallets, holding 13.86B PUMP and 5.48B PUMP, and the timed payouts they fund are already counted as circulating, so their monthly payouts to staff and backers add nothing new to the PUMP count. If the main team wallet, either idle wallet or the allocation wallet sends more PUMP out than its schedule says between checks, that PUMP enters the unlock rows at the next check.

## How PUMP compares to other buyback-and-burn app tokens

PUMP sits in a small group of app tokens whose supply is fixed at launch and that use real revenue to take coins back. Most of that group buys coins and holds them in a treasury, which keeps the coins one decision away from the market. Pump.fun burns every PUMP it buys, so the PUMP buyback shrinks the supply for good, and with no way to make new PUMP, the burn is never offset by fresh issuance. That is a sharper shape than a chain coin such as SOL, which pays validators in new SOL every epoch and can only burn part of its fees against that.

The weak side of PUMP is the insider schedule. Like most coins that raised from investors and paid staff in coins, PUMP still has a large locked balance, 364.89B PUMP or about 44% of what is left, and the release of that balance runs to about 2029. Coins with a smaller locked share, or whose insider unlocks are already done, face less of this. PUMP's buyback is also tied to how much people use Pump.fun: a quiet month for new coins means less revenue and a smaller burn, while the unlock dates do not move.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

On Nov 12 2026, Dec 12 2026 and Jan 12 2027 the 30B allocation wallet is due to release 625M PUMP each time, 1.88B PUMP in all. Any new lump out of the main Pump.fun team wallet, which last paid out on Sep 13 2026 and holds 229.26B PUMP, would add straight to the unlock row. The size of the Pump.fun buyback depends on revenue from new coin launches, PumpSwap and Terminal, so a slow December would mean less PUMP burned. And the one-year buy-and-burn contract runs to about Apr 28 2027, just after this window: what Pump.fun says about renewing it is the biggest supply question for 2027.

## Summary

PUMP supply grew **+14.35%** in the 90 days to Nov 11 2026 as **83.38B PUMP** left locked wallets, mostly two September payouts to staff and early backers, against a **17.58B PUMP** buyback burn. No new PUMP can ever be made, and half of Pump.fun's net revenue keeps buying PUMP and burning it, so the next 90 days point to about **−4.31%**. The main risk is the 364.89B PUMP still in four locked wallets, above all the 229.26B PUMP in the main team wallet, which has no public release dates. The supply ceiling is fixed: 823.3B PUMP today, and only lower from here.

# PUMP Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

**PUMP** supply was growing over the last 90 days and is set to shrink over the next 90. From Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 the Pump.fun treasury and one insider wallet released **84.0B PUMP** into the market, while the revenue-funded buyback destroyed **16.92B PUMP**, a net rise of **+18.98%** of the **353.45B** PUMP in circulation. No new PUMP can ever be minted, and the total supply has already fallen from 1 trillion to **821.31B**; other trackers show **−9.42%** for the same 90 days because their circulating figure was cut by about 104B PUMP on Nov 21 2026.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 21 2026, PUMP supply in the market rose by **+18.98%**, and the next 90 days point to a fall of about **−8.26%**. Other trackers show **−9.42%** for the same window, a gap of **28.40 points**, so the PUMP coin page carries a warning note. The gap is not a supply flow: the circulating figure those trackers use rose by 67.2B PUMP from Aug 23 to Nov 20 2026, in step with the coins released and burned on-chain, then dropped by about 104B PUMP on Nov 21 2026 when more wallets were counted as locked, with no matching move of coins. The short label for PUMP today is a capped coin whose September treasury payout outran its buyback burn, with the burn now in front.

## Sell pressure: where new PUMP comes from

PUMP has no protocol inflation at all. The PUMP mint gave up its right to create coins at launch in July 2025, so the protocol inflation row is **0** and the supply can only fall from here.

Vesting unlocks added **10.0B PUMP**. One insider wallet that was still counted as locked released 8.13B PUMP on Aug 26 2026 to catch up on thirteen months, then 625M PUMP each on Sep 12, Oct 12 and Nov 13 2026. The same wallet is due to release 625M PUMP on Dec 12 2026, Jan 12 2027 and Feb 12 2027, which is the whole **1.88B PUMP** of new sell pressure booked for the next 90 days. The published Pump.fun schedule unlocks team and investor coins at 6.88B PUMP a month until July 2029, but the other investor wallets that release monthly were already counted as circulating, so their releases add nothing new.

The Pump.fun treasury released **74.0B PUMP**, the largest single source of new supply in the window. The treasury sent 20B PUMP on Sep 8 2026 and 54B PUMP on Sep 13 2026 to two payout wallets, and from Sep 14 2026 those payout wallets paid 61.81B PUMP to 142 wallets, which still hold about three quarters of it. The treasury did the same thing once before, with 62.2B PUMP on Jun 25 2026, ahead of the first team and investor unlock. Two batches are not a timetable, so the next 90 days book **0** here, and any new batch will be added the day it lands.

The long-term locked or bankruptcy row is **0**: no court case, bankrupt firm or trustee holds PUMP for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new PUMP goes

The PUMP buyback is the only buyer that takes coins away for good. Since Apr 28 2026, under a one-year contract, half of the net revenue from the Pump.fun bonding curve, PumpSwap and Terminal buys PUMP on the open market and burns it. From Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 about **$74.1M** went in and **16.92B PUMP** was destroyed, and the on-chain total supply fell to 821.31B PUMP. The buyback is set in dollars, not coins, so at the Nov 21 2026 price of about $0.0024 the same dollars would burn about **31.08B PUMP** over the next 90 days, close to twice the last window. If revenue keeps sliding, as it did from October into November 2026, the real figure will come in lower.

The protocol fee burn row is **0**. Pump.fun fees are paid in SOL, and every PUMP that is destroyed passes through the buyback, so it is counted once, above.

The foundation buy row is **0**. The Pump.fun team does not buy PUMP to hold. Listed companies such as Fitell, which bought 216.8M PUMP in early November 2026, buy coins that are already circulating, so the count does not change.

The new long-term lock row is **0**. PUMP has no staking lock or vote escrow that takes coins out of circulation.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest PUMP overhang is the Pump.fun treasury itself, which received all 1 trillion PUMP at launch and still holds **229.26B PUMP** on Nov 21 2026, close to two thirds of the coins in circulation. It has no public release dates, and its last outflow was the 74.0B PUMP batch in September 2026. The two payout wallets it fills hold another **19.34B PUMP**, already counted as circulating. Two allocation wallets that have not moved since launch week hold **80.0B** and **35.0B PUMP**, and the insider wallet behind the monthly releases holds **20.0B PUMP**. On top of that, about 104B PUMP in other wallets was counted as locked from Nov 21 2026. Every one of these balances is read on-chain at each rebuild. If the treasury, or any other wallet still counted as locked, sends coins out between rebuilds, those coins are added to the PUMP sell side at the next rebuild.

## How PUMP compares to other buyback-funded app tokens

PUMP belongs to the small group of app tokens whose supply is fixed and whose own revenue buys coins back. Its closest match is HYPE on Hyperliquid, which also routes trading revenue into open-market buybacks. The difference is in where the coins end up and how much of the supply is still held back. PUMP burns what it buys, so its total supply visibly falls, from 1 trillion at launch to 821.31B by Nov 21 2026. A buyback that only parks coins in a project wallet would leave the supply where it was and simply create a new overhang.

The weak side of PUMP is its unlock calendar. Team and investor coins unlock monthly until July 2029, and the Pump.fun treasury still holds 229.26B PUMP with no published timetable. Exchange tokens with a fixed burn schedule, or decentralised exchange tokens that switched on a fee buyback after their unlocks were finished, do not carry that kind of overhang. On PUMP, the burn and the unlocks are both big, so the 90-day reading swings with each treasury batch.

Against proof-of-stake chains such as Solana, PUMP looks very different: there is no staking reward and no new issuance at all, so every bit of new market supply is an old coin leaving a locked wallet, and every bit of shrink is a burn paid for by Pump.fun revenue.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The insider wallet's next three releases of 625M PUMP each are due on Dec 12 2026, Jan 12 2027 and Feb 12 2027, and together they are all the new PUMP sell pressure booked for the window.

The Pump.fun treasury batch is the big unknown: the last two came on Jun 25 2026 and Sep 8 to Sep 13 2026, about eleven weeks apart, so a third batch in December 2026 would fit the pattern even though no date has been published.

The buyback runs on revenue. The new prediction markets in the Pump.fun app since Nov 16 2026 and the perpetual futures added in late October 2026 are the products to watch for fresh fees.

The one-year buyback contract runs to Apr 28 2027, past this window; any vote or post about what follows it would change the PUMP reading for the spring of 2027.

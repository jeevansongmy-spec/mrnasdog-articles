# FF Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the Falcon Finance coin page for the short answer (should you buy FF?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/ff](https://mrnasdog.com/research/ff)

FF supply is growing fast: Falcon Finance made all **10B** FF at once and can never make more, but four locked pots released **481.11M FF** from Aug 28 to Nov 26 2026 and nothing was bought back or burned, so supply grew **+13.57%**. The next 90 days should add about **609.17M FF**, or **+17.18%**. Other trackers show **+15.56%** for the last 90 days only because they count from the smaller float of late August.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

FF is a fixed-supply coin whose supply still climbs every month. On Nov 26 2026 about **3.55B FF** were on the market out of 10B, so about **6.45B FF** still waits in the four locked pots. In the 90 days to Nov 26 2026 the pots released 481.11M FF, a net rise of **+13.57%**, and the next 90 days look bigger at **+17.18%** because the team and investor slices now arrive every month. Other trackers read +15.56% because they start from the late-August float of about 3.07B FF instead of today's 3.55B. Counted from late August, the releases above also come to about +15.7%, so the gap of about 2 points comes from where the count starts, not from any missing coins. The coin page carries no warning note. Label: supply growing, and projected to keep growing.

## Sell pressure: where new FF comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero**. The FF token created all 10B coins on Sep 19 2025 and has no function to create more, and the staking rewards paid to FF stakers come out of coins that already exist.

Vesting unlocks added **136.11M FF**. The team and the early investors waited a full year after launch, and since Sep 29 2026 they receive a slice every month for three years. The team pot released **55.56M FF** on Oct 1 2026 and again on Nov 2 2026, and the investor pot **12.5M FF** on the same two days. Three more slices of about 68.06M FF are due in the next 90 days, about **204.17M FF** in all.

Foundation and other unlocks added **345M FF**. The ecosystem pot paid out **75M FF** on Sep 2, Oct 1 and Nov 2 2026, and the Foundation pot **60M FF** on Oct 1 and Nov 2 2026. Falcon Finance does not publish a timetable for these two pots, but both have paid out every month since mid-2026, so the next 90 days should bring about **405M FF** more.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins are **zero**. No court or failed firm holds FF for a later payout. The lawsuit filed in October 2026 over FF said to have been sold early concerns coins already on the market, so it adds no supply.

## Buy pressure: where new FF goes

The programmatic buyback is **zero**. Falcon Finance earns income from its USDf dollar, but none of it is used to buy FF, and no buyback has been announced.

The fee burn is **zero**. FF has no burn, the total stayed at exactly 10B FF across the whole window, and the burn address holds no FF.

Foundation buying is **zero**. The Foundation releases FF to other wallets and has bought none back.

The new long-term lock is **zero**, even though a real lock exists. Holders voted on Sep 28 2026 to stake every team, investor and airdrop slice released between Sep 29 and Dec 29 2026 for three months before handing it over. About **274M FF** now sits in staking. Staked FF can be taken out after a three-day wait and still counts as circulating, so the vote slows selling without taking coins off the market.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the ecosystem pot, with **2.1B FF** still locked and 75M FF leaving it each month. The Foundation pot holds **2.04B FF** and releases 60M FF a month.

The team pot holds **1.89B FF** and the investor pot **425M FF**; they release about 55.56M FF and 12.5M FF a month until Sep 2029.

Coins already released can still move. The wallet that receives the ecosystem slices holds **898M FF** and has never sent any out. The Foundation passed its October and November 2026 slices on to other wallets within days. These coins already count as circulating, so moving them adds nothing to supply, but they are the coins that could reach the market fastest.

## How FF compares to other stablecoin-protocol tokens

Ethena, the maker of the USDe dollar, is the closest match. ENA also has a fixed supply that reaches the market through scheduled unlocks, and its float rose about **14.49%** in its last 90 days. The difference is the road ahead: Ethena's big pots have mostly opened, so its next 90 days look near **2%**, while FF's monthly release is only getting larger. Ethena holders have also voted for buybacks once USDe grows past a set size, and FF has no such plan.

Sky, the maker of USDS, sits at the other end. Sky spends part of its income buying SKY back, and its supply fell about **0.2%** in its last 90 days. FF has the opposite setup: no buyer, and about 203M new coins a month.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 29 2026**: the next monthly unlock of about **203.06M FF** across the ecosystem, Foundation, team and investor pots. So far the coins have landed a few days after the 29th.

**Dec 29 2026**: another 203.06M FF unlock, and the first handover under the Sep 28 2026 vote. The Sep 29 slice for the team and investors finishes its three months in staking and goes to its owners, who can then sell after a three-day wait.

**Jan 29 2027**: the third unlock in the window, again about 203.06M FF. The one after that, on Feb 28 2027, falls just outside it.

**Any month**: a first transfer out of the ecosystem receiving wallet, which has kept all 898M FF it was sent. A buyback plan from Falcon Finance would be the first real buyer of FF; none is on the table today.

## Summary

Falcon Finance can never mint more FF, but its four locked pots released 481.11M FF from Aug 28 to Nov 26 2026, a rise of +13.57%.

With the team and investor slices now monthly, the next 90 days should add about 609.17M FF, or +17.18%.

Nothing on the other side takes FF back: no buyback, no burn, and staking that still counts as circulating.

About 6.45B FF stays locked and keeps opening until Sep 2029, so steady monthly supply is the main fact to weigh for anyone holding FF.

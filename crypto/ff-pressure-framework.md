# FF Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the Falcon Finance coin page for the short answer (should you buy FF?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/ff](https://mrnasdog.com/research/ff)

FF supply is growing fast, and it will keep growing for years. No new FF can ever be made, since all **10B FF** were created at launch, but about **6.45B FF** still sit in four locked wallets that let coins out every month. Between Aug 18 and Nov 16 2026 **481.11M FF** came out against **0 FF** taken back, for net supply growth of **+13.57%**; other trackers show **+15.67%**, and the next 90 days point to about **+17.18%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 16 2026, the FF in circulation grew by **+13.57%**, from about 3.07B to **3.55B FF**. Other trackers show **+15.67%** for the same stretch, a difference of **2.10 points**. Both count the same 481M new coins; the trackers set them against the smaller supply of 90 days ago, so their percentage comes out higher. The coin page carries no warning note. Nothing in the window was created or destroyed; every new coin in the market came out of a locked wallet on schedule. The plain label for FF today is **a fixed-supply token with a heavy monthly unlock schedule**.

## Sell pressure: where new FF comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero**. The FF token on Ethereum has no mint function and no owner who could add one, and total supply read exactly **10B FF** at both ends of the window. People who stake FF are paid in coins that already exist, so staking rewards do not grow the supply. The copy of FF on BNB Chain is backed one for one by FF locked in the bridge on Ethereum.

Vesting unlocks added **136.11M FF**. Falcon Finance gave its team and its investors a one-year wait from the Sep 29 2025 launch, then three years of monthly unlocks. The wait ended on Sep 29 2026, and two payouts followed, on Oct 1 and Nov 2 2026: **55.56M FF** a month for the team and **12.5M FF** a month for investors. The next three payouts, due Nov 29 2026, Dec 29 2026 and Jan 29 2027, add about **204.17M FF**.

Foundation and ecosystem releases added **345M FF**, the largest share. The ecosystem wallet lets out **75M FF** a month and has done so since June 2026; the FF Foundation wallet lets out **60M FF** a month and started on Oct 1 2026. Three ecosystem payouts (Sep 2, Oct 1 and Nov 2 2026) and two Foundation payouts (Oct 1 and Nov 2 2026) fell inside the window. The official token page gives no schedule for these two pools, but both have paid out like clockwork each month; at the same pace the next 90 days bring about **405M FF** more.

Long-term locked coins and bankruptcy payouts add **zero**. No court estate holds FF. A lawsuit filed in London in October 2026 says a custodian sold discounted FF before its lock-up ended in spring 2026; whatever the court decides, those coins were already in the market and the case cannot add new ones.

## Buy pressure: where new FF goes

A programmatic buyback adds **zero**: Falcon Finance has no program that buys FF on the market, and its docs, its holder votes and three months of news show none. A protocol fee burn adds **zero**: no fee is used to destroy FF, the burn address held the same dust at both ends of the window, and total supply never moved. A Foundation buy adds **zero**: the FF Foundation is a seller on a fixed monthly schedule, not a buyer.

A new long-term lock also books **zero**, though one exists. A holder vote that closed on Sep 28 2026, with no votes against, requires that team, investor and airdrop coins unlocking between Sep 29 and Dec 29 2026 be staked for three months before they are handed over. All **136.11M FF** released to the team and investors so far went straight into staking within minutes. Staked FF is still counted as part of the circulating supply, so the lock delays selling but does not shrink the count.

## Foundation and overhang

Four locked wallets hold every FF that is not yet circulating. The ecosystem wallet holds **2.1B FF**, enough for 28 more monthly releases. The FF Foundation wallet holds **2.04B FF**, the team wallet **1.89B FF** and the investor wallet **425M FF**, each with 34 monthly releases left, running to 2029. Together that is about **203.06M FF** a month.

Coins that already left those wallets are counted as circulating, but some still sit with the project. The wallet that receives the ecosystem releases holds **898M FF** and has never sent a single coin on; the Foundation's payout wallet passed on its October and November coins within days. Moves out of these wallets add nothing to the count. We read the four locked wallets at every rebuild: if a balance falls between rebuilds, the outflow joins the unlock rows at the next one.

## How FF compares to other DeFi tokens with vesting schedules

Most DeFi tokens launched in the last two years follow the same shape as FF: a fixed cap, a small share at launch, then years of monthly unlocks for the team, investors and a treasury. What sets FF apart is the size of the monthly step compared with what is already out. At about **203M FF** a month against **3.55B FF** in circulation, each release adds close to 6% to the float, and only about **35%** of all FF is unlocked so far.

Older DeFi tokens that pay for buybacks from protocol fees, or burn part of their fees, can offset some of their unlocks. FF has no such link today: Falcon Finance earns on the USDf dollar and its reserves, but none of that flows back into FF. So FF's supply moves in one direction only, and the speed is set by the vesting calendar rather than by how much the protocol is used.

The one brake FF has that many peers lack is the holder vote that keeps fresh team and investor coins staked for three months. It shifts when those coins can be sold, but it ends with the Dec 29 2026 tranche, and from then on each release can reach the market directly.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 29 2026:** the next monthly release of about 203.06M FF is due; past payouts have landed one to five days after the scheduled date.

**Dec 29 2026:** the last tranche covered by the three-month staking vote unlocks, and the first staked team and investor coins, from the Sep 29 2026 tranche, become theirs to move.

**Any new holder vote:** a proposal to extend the staking rule, start a buyback or change the release pace would move this reading; none was open on Nov 16 2026.

**The ecosystem payout wallet:** its 898M FF has never moved; a first large transfer out would be worth reading closely.

## Summary

FF has a hard cap of **10B** coins and no way to mint more, yet its circulating supply grew **+13.57%** in the 90 days to Nov 16 2026 because four locked wallets release about **203M FF** every month. Nothing on the other side takes FF back: there is no buyback and no burn. A holder vote keeps new team and investor coins staked for three months, which delays their sale until early 2027 but does not change the count. With about **6.45B FF** still locked and releases running to 2029, supply is set to grow about **+17.18%** in the next 90 days.

## How FORM compares to other migrating ecosystem tokens

FORM is best compared with token migrations, not with continuously emitting proof-of-stake networks. A network reward creates additional economic units to compensate validators or stakers. A replacement contract changes the representation of an existing claim. The right comparison follows the old and new units together, applying the conversion ratio before calculating supply growth. This remains true when a project changes its name or product focus: a branding event does not establish an emission event.

FORM also differs from an immutable fixed-supply token. Its current minting configuration does not eliminate the owner’s ability to appoint a minter. The FORM contract has a **580M** ceiling, but that counter must be considered alongside outstanding predecessor claims and the rules governing conversion. A cap on one contract is not permission to ignore the others. Likewise, ecosystem revenue and token-holder removal are separate: a growing platform does not establish a FORM buyback unless a verifiable mechanism connects the revenue to purchases and an excluded destination.

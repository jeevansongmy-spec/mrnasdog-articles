# ONDO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

ONDO had a still quarter: from Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 not one new coin entered circulation, so the count of circulating ONDO moved by **0.00%**. That changes on **Jan 18 2027**, when the yearly unlock releases **1,710,223,118 ONDO**, about **35%** more than the **4.87B ONDO** circulating today. Nothing on the other side takes ONDO back: there is no buyback, no burn and no staking lock, and the supply is capped at **10B ONDO**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days ONDO's sell side was **0 ONDO** and its buy side was **0 ONDO**, a net change of **0.00%**. Other supply trackers show **−0.04%** for the same stretch, essentially flat as well, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days look very different: the Jan 18 2027 unlock alone lifts circulating ONDO by about **35.12%**, and no buyer inside the system offsets it. The label for ONDO this month is a calm quarter followed by one of the largest scheduled supply jumps of any coin we follow.

## Sell pressure: where new ONDO comes from

Protocol inflation is zero. ONDO pays no staking rewards and has no emission schedule; the total supply read **10B ONDO** on Aug 23 2026 and the same 10B ONDO on Nov 21 2026. The Ondo Foundation's main wallet still holds the right to create new ONDO, but that right has not been used since the first 10B ONDO were made in 2022, so we call this row checked, not closed for good.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story. Locked ONDO opens once a year on Jan 18, the date ONDO first became transferable in 2024. Nothing opened inside the last 90 days. The Jan 18 2027 batch is **1,710,223,118 ONDO**, and it falls inside the next 90 days, so it is counted in full. Roughly **792M** of it belongs to ecosystem growth, **660M** to protocol development and **258M** to early private-sale buyers. Last January the circulating count rose by the same 1.71B ONDO in a single day, which is how this unlock is expected to land again.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero. The Foundation's main wallet sent **150M ONDO** to a second Foundation wallet on Sep 23 2026, and that second wallet paid out **244M ONDO** to 11 wallets between Aug 27 and Oct 13 2026. Those coins had already opened in earlier years and were already part of the circulating count, so moving them added no new ONDO.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is zero. No court or failed firm holds ONDO for a later payout. The court fight over control of Ondo Finance after the founder's death in May 2026 is about the company, and no ONDO coins are part of it.

## Buy pressure: where new ONDO goes

Programmatic buyback is zero. Ondo's tokenized funds and tokenized stocks earn fees, but none of that money is used to buy ONDO. Holders have talked for months about a vote to send part of those fees to the coin; no such proposal has been put to a vote.

Protocol fee burn is zero. ONDO has no burn function at all, and the supply sat at exactly **10B ONDO** through the whole window.

Foundation buy is zero. The Ondo Foundation pays ONDO out to fund the ecosystem and has never announced a plan to buy it back, and no company or fund has announced a plan to take ONDO off the market.

New long-term lock is zero. There is no staking and no new lock, and nobody has proposed moving the Jan 18 2027 unlock to a later date.

## Foundation and overhang

The Ondo Foundation's main wallet holds **5.18B ONDO**. The Foundation says **4.42B ONDO** of that is locked, which leaves about **757M ONDO** that is already open and can be paid out at any time. Through 2026 it has moved that open share out in steps of 125M to 150M ONDO: on Apr 22, May 11, Jun 22, Aug 21 and Sep 23 2026.

The Foundation's payout wallet holds **36M ONDO** after sending out **244M ONDO** since late August 2026, in pieces of about 15M to 26M ONDO each. These payouts do not add new ONDO, but they do put coins into new hands that may sell.

After the Jan 18 2027 unlock, two more batches of the same size remain: **1.71B ONDO** on Jan 18 2028 and **1.71B ONDO** on Jan 18 2029. That is **5.13B ONDO** still locked today, more than all the ONDO circulating now.

## How ONDO compares to other real-world asset coins

Most coins tied to tokenized funds and stocks were built the same way as ONDO: a fixed supply set at launch, a large share held back for the team, investors and the ecosystem, and that share released on a timetable. What sets ONDO apart is the shape of the timetable. Many coins release locked supply a little each month, which spreads the selling out. ONDO releases it in one lump a year, so the supply sits still for eleven months and then jumps by more than a third in a day.

The second difference is the buy side. Some coins in decentralized finance use part of their fees to buy back their own coin or burn it. ONDO has none of that today. The business behind it is large, with over **$4B** in tokenized assets by Ondo's own count in early November 2026, but that business does not route any money into ONDO, so the coin has no built-in buyer to meet the unlock.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Jan 18 2027 — the yearly unlock.** 1,710,223,118 ONDO opens, about 35% of today's circulating supply. Watch the Foundation's main wallet in the days after: the move into circulation is a count change, but selling only happens once coins leave the wallets that hold them.

**Foundation payouts.** The main wallet last sent 150M ONDO on Sep 23 2026, and the payout wallet is down to 36M ONDO. A new transfer from the main wallet would put more already-open coins into new hands.

**A fee vote.** If Ondo's holders put forward a vote to send fees to buybacks, ONDO would get its first buyer. As of Nov 21 2026 no such vote has been proposed.

**The court case over control of Ondo Finance.** We found no ruling yet, as of Nov 21 2026, in the Delaware case brought by the late founder's mother. It does not touch the ONDO schedule, but a ruling could change who runs the company that builds Ondo's products.

## Summary

ONDO added no new coins to circulation between Aug 23 and Nov 21 2026, so its supply was flat at **4.87B ONDO**.

On Jan 18 2027 the yearly unlock adds **1.71B ONDO**, lifting circulating supply by about **35%** in the next 90 days.

ONDO has no buyback, no burn and no staking lock, so nothing inside the system absorbs that unlock.

Two more unlocks of the same size follow in January 2028 and January 2029, when all 10B ONDO will be open.

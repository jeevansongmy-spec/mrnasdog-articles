# ONDO Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

ONDO, the token of Ondo Finance, mints nothing — its total supply held at exactly **10B ONDO** across the window — yet the float still grew, because the Ondo Foundation keeps releasing coins it already holds. Two unannounced releases of **150M ONDO** on **Jun 22 2026** and **Aug 21 2026** passed through Foundation payout wallets, and **281.7M ONDO** reached outside addresses in 90 days against **zero** taken off the market. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads ONDO at **+5.79% net** over the last 90 days and **+4.03%** for the next 90, against a supply-monitor reading of **−0.15%** — a gap of **5.94 percentage points** that comes from how the monitor counts the Foundation's coins. The published ONDO unlock calendar is quiet until **Jan 18 2027**; the Foundation Safe behind the releases still holds **5.33B ONDO**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Sep 14 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **ONDO at +5.79% net**: **281.7M ONDO** left Ondo Foundation wallets for outside addresses, and nothing on the buy side removed a single coin. The independent supply monitor reads the same 90 days at **−0.15%**. The gap is **5.94 percentage points**, far beyond the framework's half-point tolerance, so ONDO ships with a **data-conflict flag** after a full deep walk. The walk found no mint, no burn and no hidden offset; it found that the monitor's counted float already includes **198.5M ONDO** of the Foundation Safe, so coins leaving that Safe cannot show up as new supply there. The framework keeps its reading. The forward column reads **+4.03%**, projected from the Foundation's own yearly release pace. The label for ONDO is **structurally inflationary through discretionary Foundation release**: a token with a fixed supply whose tradable float keeps rising between its calendar cliffs.

## Sell pressure: where new ONDO comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**. ONDO has no block reward and no emission curve: the ONDO contract read exactly **10B** at both ends of the window, and a full sweep of every ONDO transfer in those 90 days found none out of the zero address. That flat reading is a real measurement rather than a frozen number — the ONDO contract carries a working mint function, it writes the supply figure when used, and a test call from an outside address was refused with the contract's own "not authorized" message. The only holder of the minting role is the Ondo Foundation Safe, which is why this row stays watched rather than closed. Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is also **zero**: the published ONDO schedule releases coins in one cliff each January, the last on **Jan 18 2026** and the next on **Jan 18 2027**, about **1.94B ONDO**, which falls 36 days after the forward window closes.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, carries the whole ONDO sell side at **281.7M ONDO**. The Ondo Foundation Safe fell from **5,629.2M** to **5,329.2M ONDO** across the window, paying **150M** on Jun 22 2026 and **150M** on Aug 21 2026 into a Foundation payout wallet. That hop is internal, so it is not booked. The payout wallet and the smaller Foundation wallets under it then cut the coins into pieces of about **4M ONDO** and sent **280.9M** to exchange deposit addresses and **0.8M** to three other wallets; **49.7M** is still inside those payout wallets. The framework treated all 52 Foundation addresses as one group and booked only what left the group, and the transfer record closed against the wallets' balances with nothing left over. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy supply, is **zero**: no estate or trustee distributes ONDO, and the Delaware lawsuit filed on **Jul 24 2026** over control of Ondo Finance the company sets no release of coins.

## Buy pressure: where new ONDO goes

Nowhere — all four ONDO buy rows are **zero**. Buy #1, programmatic buyback: no ONDO buyback runs, no wallet collects ONDO from the market, no binding vote to start one has been held, and on-chain voting in the Ondo DAO is paused. Buy #2, protocol fee burn: the burn address held the same **7.59 ONDO** at both ends of the window, ONDO total supply did not fall, and the ONDO contract has no burn function, so both burn surfaces agree that nothing was destroyed. Buy #3, Foundation buy: the Ondo Foundation wallets only send ONDO out — in 90 days they took in **109 ONDO** from outside, test transfers and one small return, and bought nothing. Buy #4, new long-term lock: no lockup contract with a stated size appeared, and the Ondo Network that launched on **Jul 27 2026** did not add one. With a zero buy side, every ONDO the Foundation releases lands straight in the ONDO float.

## Foundation and overhang

The ONDO overhang is the Ondo Foundation Safe itself: **5,329.2M ONDO**, more than the entire **4,869.3M** counted float. The Foundation's own wallet disclosure, updated **Aug 26 2026**, counts **4,422.5M** of that Safe as locked, which leaves **906.7M ONDO** unlocked and held — enough for several more quarters at the current pace without touching a single calendar cliff. The Foundation payout wallets hold another **49.7M** already on its way out, an older payout Safe holds **0.37M**, and one further Safe tracked in earlier builds read **zero** at both ends. All of these are on-chain and are re-read on every refresh. Exchange custody wallets and unidentified large holders are excluded by rule; one unidentified holder rotated six wallets of **29.6M ONDO** each this window, which moved no supply. If the Foundation Safe or any of its payout wallets falls between refreshes and the ONDO leaves the group, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How ONDO compares to other tokens with cliff unlock schedules

ONDO sits in the large class of fixed-supply tokens whose float grows by unlocks rather than by minting, and the useful comparison is how those unlocks reach the market. A proof-of-stake Layer 1 issues new coins every block and hopes a fee burn offsets them; ONDO issues nothing, so every ONDO that enters the float was created at launch and released later. Against a token with a plain cliff schedule, where the unlock date is the supply event and a tracker can see it coming, ONDO is harder to read: its January cliffs unlock coins that stay inside Foundation custody, and the market-facing release then happens on the Foundation's own timing. Seven Foundation releases over the past year totalled **845M ONDO**, none of them on the calendar, and the longest stretch without Foundation coins reaching the market was **71 days**.

Against tokens that run a buyback or a burn, ONDO has no offset at all: there is no mechanism that takes ONDO out of the float, so the net reading equals the release. That is also why the ONDO forward reading depends on behaviour rather than on code. A token on a published emission curve can be projected to the unit; ONDO can only be projected from what the Ondo Foundation has actually done, which over the trailing year was **796.2M ONDO** reaching the market, or **196.3M** per 90 days. The trade-off for readers is visibility: the ONDO calendar says nothing until January 2027, while the chain shows the float growing every month.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the Ondo Foundation Safe at **5,329.2M ONDO**: the last release was **Aug 21 2026**, the previous gaps ran from 19 to 99 days, and another **150M** release inside the window would, together with the coins already in payout wallets, put the realised figure above the **196.3M** projection. Second, the **49.7M ONDO** still sitting in Foundation payout wallets, which on the pattern of this window reaches exchange deposit addresses within weeks. Third, the Foundation's wallet disclosure, updated **Aug 26 2026**: a change in the locked figure, or a new Foundation address, would change what the framework tracks. Fourth, the Delaware lawsuit over control of Ondo Finance, filed **Jul 24 2026**, and any Ondo DAO vote on a buyback, which would open the ONDO buy side. Beyond the window, the **Jan 18 2027** cliff of about **1.94B ONDO** is the next calendar event.

ONDO cannot be minted — Ondo Finance fixed the supply at **10 billion ONDO** and the on-chain total has never moved — and yet **300M ONDO** reached the market in the last 90 days, released from the project's own custody wallet on dates that appear on no published vesting calendar. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **+6.16% net** supply growth for ONDO over that window against an entirely **empty buy side**: no buyback, no burn, no fee share, no staking. The supply monitor reads **-0.04%**, a gap of **6.20 percentage points** that ships with a data-conflict flag.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Jul 27 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **ONDO at +6.16% net** on the realised view and **+6.16% net** on the forward view, driven entirely by discretionary custody release rather than by any protocol mint. The supply monitor reads the same window at **-0.04%**, essentially unchanged, so the gap is **6.20 percentage points** and this build ships a **monitor-gap flag**. The gap is explained rather than closed: the tracked circulating figure for ONDO is pinned to the modelled annual January-cliff calendar, so a release that happens between cliffs is invisible to it by construction, while the chain shows the Ondo custody wallet falling from **5,779,207,574 ONDO** to **5,479,207,574 ONDO** across the identical window. The label for Ondo Finance: **hard-capped, zero-mint, and inflationary on the active float by design**, because the token has a release engine and no absorption engine at all.

## Sell pressure: where new ONDO comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero** for ONDO, and it is genuinely zero. ONDO is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum with a fixed **10,000,000,000** supply, no block reward, no staking emission and no emission curve of any kind. An on-chain total-supply read at both ends of this window returned exactly **10,000,000,000 ONDO**, a delta of zero, so not a single new ONDO was created. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is zero as well: Ondo Finance has no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-supervised distribution of ONDO.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, carries the entire ONDO sell ledger at about **300M ONDO** for the window — and the striking part is that the published vesting calendar says the figure should be zero. Ondo's documented schedule releases tokens once a year on the January anniversary of the January 2024 launch, running to **Jan 18 2029**, and its next dated event is **Jan 18 2027** at **1,710,223,118 ONDO**. Nothing at all was scheduled between **Apr 28 2026** and **Jul 27 2026**. The framework's rule for a vesting allocation held in a readable on-chain escrow is that the realised outflow beats the calendar entitlement, because what matters is supply reaching the market, not supply vesting on paper. Reading the Ondo custody wallet at both window ends returns exactly **300,000,000 ONDO** out, in two transfers of **150M on May 11 2026** and **150M on Jun 22 2026**, both into a distribution wallet that forwarded **260.93M ONDO** onward to fourteen separate recipient wallets. One of those recipients received **26.06M ONDO** and sent the entire balance to an exchange deposit address on **Jul 17 2026**, which settles the question of whether these tokens are reaching the market.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, books at **zero** purely to avoid counting the same tokens twice, because the Ondo custody release is already carried in the vesting row above. The custody wallet's dated release history is what drives the forward projection: **100M** on **Oct 26 2025**, **100M** on **Jan 14 2026**, **125M** on **Apr 22 2026**, then **150M** and **150M** in the current window — a draining and accelerating escrow that shed **625M ONDO** over twelve months. Projected by date rather than by average, two firings of about **150M** fall inside the next 90 days, so the forward column carries **300M ONDO** as well.

## Buy pressure: where new ONDO goes

Every buy row on ONDO is **zero**, and that empty buy side is the structural weakness of the token. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is zero: Ondo Finance earns real management fees on OUSG and USDY, its tokenized US Treasury products, and on its tokenized equities, and not one cent of that revenue is routed into buying ONDO. A fee switch that would change this has been discussed in Ondo commentary for over a year and has never been put to a binding vote. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is zero: the ONDO burn address holds no tokens at all and the conventional dead address holds under **8 ONDO** in total since launch, while total supply has never moved off **10 billion**. Several secondary crypto-media pages published in July 2026 described a governance vote to burn **100M ONDO** closing **Jul 25 2026**; that claim does not survive contact with the Ondo DAO's own on-chain vote portal, which lists nine proposals with the most recent executed in **Jan 2024** and nothing since, and those same pages describe a one-billion total supply that ONDO does not have. The framework watches the claim rather than counting it.

Buy #3, Foundation buy, is zero, with no disclosed open-market accumulation by Ondo Finance or by the Ondo Foundation. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is zero as well, and this row is the clearest statement of ONDO's token design: ONDO has no native staking, no lock-to-earn escrow and no gas function. Holding ONDO grants a governance vote and nothing else, so there is no mechanism by which a holder can take ONDO off the market in exchange for a protocol reward. Ondo Chain, the institutional layer-1 the project has been building, is designed for validators to stake tokenized real-world assets rather than ONDO, so even a mainnet launch does not automatically create a Buy #4 entry.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang is the dominant fact about ONDO. Ten billion tokens exist, **4,869.33M** are counted as tradable, and the remaining **5,130.67M** is held outside the float — and almost all of it sits in one place. The Ondo custody wallet, a multisig the Ondo Foundation Group has publicly disclosed as holding its locked ONDO, held **5,479.21M ONDO** on **Jul 27 2026**, down from **5,779.21M** at the start of the window. That single wallet holds **54.79%** of everything that will ever exist, and more ONDO than the entire tradable float. Note the arithmetic: **5,479.21M** in the wallet against **5,130.67M** nominally locked means the custody wallet is already **348.5M** ahead of its own published calendar, the accumulated effect of releasing operationally instead of on cliff dates. The distribution wallet downstream of it holds a further **74.42M ONDO** and is refilled every few weeks; it is the pipe through which every release reaches the market, and the fourteen recipient wallets it feeds mostly empty within days. Behind all of it sit the published buckets — Ecosystem Growth at **52.11%** of supply, Protocol Development at **33.00%**, Private Sales at **12.90%** and the Community Access Sale at **1.99%** — with the core team on a five-year lock-up and investors on multi-year releases running to **Jan 18 2029**. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, that outflow enters the ONDO sell ledger at the next refresh.

## How ONDO compares to other real-world-asset tokens

Mechanically, ONDO belongs to the class of fixed-cap governance tokens attached to a revenue-generating off-chain business — the same shape as a tokenized-asset issuer or an institutional DeFi platform whose product is a yield-bearing instrument rather than the token itself. Against a hard-capped proof-of-work coin, ONDO looks superficially better: it has no mint at all, where a proof-of-work chain issues a block subsidy every ten minutes. The difference is that a proof-of-work coin's issuance is fully disclosed, dated and shrinking on a fixed halving schedule, while ONDO's dilution comes from a discretionary custody wallet whose release dates are published nowhere and are only observable after the transaction lands. Predictable issuance from a mint is easier to price than unpredictable release from an escrow.

The comparison that matters more is against exchange tokens and other fee-capturing tokens, because that is the design ONDO has chosen not to adopt. An exchange token with a quarterly buyback converts business revenue into permanent supply reduction, so growth in the underlying business mechanically shrinks the float. Ondo Finance runs a real business with real management fees on tokenized Treasuries, and routes none of it back to ONDO, so business growth and token supply are entirely disconnected. Compared to other real-world-asset tokens, Ondo's product traction is at the front of the sector; its token mechanics are not. Until a fee switch is actually voted and executed, ONDO is a governance claim on a business rather than a claim on its cash flows, and the Pressure Framework can only score what the token mechanically does.

The third structural analogue is any token still working through a multi-year unlock, and here ONDO's position is unusually heavy. Most tokens two and a half years past launch have released the majority of their supply. ONDO has released **48.69%**, with the release running to **Jan 18 2029** and the largest single dated event, **1,710,223,118 ONDO**, still ahead on **Jan 18 2027**. A token whose remaining locked supply exceeds its entire circulating float carries a dilution profile that no amount of product success resolves on its own.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The first watch item is the custody wallet itself: two releases of roughly **150M ONDO** are projected inside the forward window, and a third, or a materially larger tranche, would push the forward reading above **+6.16%**. The second is the reverse — a pause. The escrow released nothing between **Jan 14 2026** and **Apr 22 2026**, so a quiet quarter is entirely possible and would collapse the forward number toward zero. The third is the fee switch: any binding Ondo DAO vote to route management-fee revenue into an ONDO buyback or a staking reward would create the first non-zero buy row this token has ever had, and would be visible on the DAO's on-chain vote portal, which has recorded nothing since **Jan 2024**. The fourth is Ondo Chain mainnet, still unlaunched as of **Jul 27 2026**; if its final design gives ONDO a staking or gas role, Buy #4 stops being zero by design. The fifth is the **Jan 18 2027** cliff at **1,710,223,118 ONDO**, which sits outside this window but is the single largest dated supply event in the schedule.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Ondo Finance at **+6.16% net supply growth** over the 90 days to **Jul 27 2026** and the same **+6.16%** projected for the next 90 days — supply growing, and projected to keep growing. The structural mechanism is unusual and easy to misread: ONDO mints nothing and is capped at **10 billion** forever, so every unit of dilution comes from a discretionary release out of a custody wallet still holding **5,479.21M ONDO**, most recently **150M on May 11 2026** and **150M on Jun 22 2026**. The key risk is that this release is invisible to calendar-based supply trackers, which is exactly why the framework and the monitor disagree by **6.20 percentage points** on ONDO. The ceiling is real — **10 billion** and no more — but with an empty buy side, no burn, no buyback and no staking, that ceiling only tells you where the dilution stops, not when.

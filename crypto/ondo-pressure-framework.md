# ONDO Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Originally published at [https://mrnasdog.com/research/ondo/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/ondo/inflation)

Ondo Finance has never minted a single ONDO — the contract has returned exactly **10,000,000,000** at every reading for a year — and yet roughly **233.1M ONDO** reached the open market in the last 90 days, because the project's escrow pays out on dates that appear on no published calendar. Against that, the buy side of the ledger is **0** on all four rows: no buyback, no burn, no fee share, nothing to stake. The Pressure Framework reads net supply growth of **+4.79%** for the quarter and projects the same rate forward, while the monitor sees only **+0.14%** — a **4.64 percentage point** gap that exists because the counted float already includes **198.5M** coins still sitting inside that escrow.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to **Aug 25 2026** the ONDO ledger reads **233.1M** of sell pressure against **0** of buy pressure, which is **+4.79%** net supply growth on a circulating base of **4.87B ONDO**. Our inflation monitor reads **+0.14%** for the same window. The gap of **4.64 percentage points** is large enough to trigger a data-conflict flag on the overview card, and the deep walk explains it rather than dissolving it: the widely-published circulating figure for Ondo Finance is a step function that moved once on **Jan 18 2026** and has been frozen at the identical value on every one of the 219 days since. It counted the year's scheduled releases in advance, so coins physically leaving the escrow do not register as new supply anywhere upstream. Ondo Finance is **structurally inflationary on the active float** — a hard-capped token whose dilution comes entirely from custody, not from issuance.

## Sell pressure: where new ONDO comes from

**Protocol inflation is 0**, and it is a real zero. The ONDO contract on Ethereum was read at four separate points across the last year — today, 90 days back, 180 days back and a full 365 days back — and returned exactly **10,000,000,000** tokens every time. Ondo Finance has no block reward, no staking emission and no emission curve. One caveat belongs on the record, because the project's own documentation does not carry it: a mint function is still live in the deployed bytecode, and there is no on-chain evidence that the authority to call it was ever surrendered. ONDO's fixed supply is an operator choice, not something the code makes impossible, so this row is never treated as permanently settled.

**Vesting unlocks are the entire story, at 233.1M ONDO**, and the published calendar is the wrong place to look for them. On paper, the next vesting cliff for Ondo Finance is **Jan 18 2027**, when roughly **1.71B ONDO** of the Ecosystem Growth allocation comes due — comfortably outside this window and the next. In practice, the escrow ignores the calendar entirely. It released **150M ONDO** on **Jun 22 2026** and another **150M** on **Aug 21 2026**, both off-schedule. Reading every project-controlled wallet at both ends of the window, the group fell from **5,692.7M** to **5,459.6M** ONDO — a realised handover of about **233.1M** coins into third-party hands. That figure was then rebuilt a second time from the payout log alone, a completely different method, and landed on **233.1M** again. Walking the escrow's full transfer history back twelve months finds **seven** such releases totalling **845M ONDO**, at gaps of **19** to **99** days, with tranche sizes trending upward from 70M to a steady 150M. None of them appears on any vesting schedule Ondo Finance publishes.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book 0**, deliberately. Putting a number here would double-count the escrow drain already measured in the vesting row, so the row instead carries the watch list. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy is 0** as well: there is no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution paying creditors in ONDO. The litigation now surrounding Ondo Finance is a corporate control fight, not a creditor claim, so nothing feeds this row.

## Buy pressure: where new ONDO goes

Nowhere. All four buy rows read **0**, and each zero has a different structural reason. **Programmatic buyback is 0** because no buyback programme exists — Ondo Finance earns genuine management fees on the tokenized treasuries and tokenized stocks it issues, and its tokenized-stock product passed **$1B** in assets on **Aug 15 2026**, but not one dollar of that revenue reaches ONDO. Turning on a fee switch has never been put to a binding vote.

**Protocol fee burn is 0**, and unreachable rather than merely unused: the deployed ONDO token code carries no burn function at all. The dead address holds **7.59** coins of ancient dust that has not moved. A widely-repeated claim that a 100M burn was voted through has no executed proposal behind it — the governance portal shows no proposal since **Jan 2024** and is marked paused, and the contract still returns the full 10 billion, which settles it. **Foundation buy is 0**: across the entire year the escrow took in about **2** coins of dust and bought nothing. Money flows one direction through Ondo Finance custody. **New long-term lock is 0** because there is nothing to lock into — no staking contract, no vote-escrow, no lockup. Ondo Finance dropped its own planned blockchain in **Jul 2026** in favour of an execution layer that is explicitly not a chain, which removed the last plausible route by which ONDO might have become a staked or gas asset.

## Foundation and overhang

Three project-controlled wallets are tracked, and the first of them is the single most important number on this page. The main escrow holds **5,329.2M ONDO** — **53.3%** of the entire supply, and larger than the whole circulating float. It is refreshed against the chain daily. Downstream of it, a distribution wallet holds a further **130.0M ONDO**: coins that have already left escrow but have not yet been handed to anyone, which is exactly why they are counted as overhang rather than as sell pressure. A retired distribution wallet holds **0.4M** and has not moved all quarter.

Two things make this overhang unusual. The first is arithmetic: the counted float of **4,869.3M** plus the escrow's **5,329.2M** comes to **10,198.5M** against a fixed total of 10 billion, an overlap of **198.5M**. The independent unlock tracker reaches the same figure from the other side — it reports **5,130,669,353** ONDO still locked while the escrow actually holds **5,329,207,574**, a difference of **198,538,221** coins, the same number to the unit. That overlap is the reason an outflow from custody cannot register as new float upstream, and it shrinks by exactly the size of each release: it stood at **348.5M** before **Aug 21 2026** and at **198.5M** after. The second is governance. Ondo Finance founder Nathan Allman died on **May 26 2026**, and by **Aug 6 2026** his estate and the sitting chief executive were in open dispute over control of the company. The keys to a balance larger than the entire tradable float are the subject of that fight. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the Foundation row at the next refresh.

## How ONDO compares to other real-world-asset tokens

Against issuance-driven chains, ONDO looks pristine and the comparison is misleading. A proof-of-work coin dilutes by a published subsidy; a proof-of-stake chain dilutes by a published emission curve. In both cases the schedule is the mechanism, and you can read next quarter's dilution off the protocol. ONDO has none of that — the mechanism is a multisig, and the schedule is whatever the operator decides that week. A hard cap protects a holder only when the tokens inside it are already distributed. Ondo Finance is **48.69%** distributed, so the cap is doing very little work.

The sharper comparison is to exchange tokens and to the fee-capturing tokens in tokenization's own peer set. Those route a documented share of revenue into a buyback or a burn, so the sell side of the ledger meets a mechanical bid on the other side. Ondo Finance runs a genuinely successful business — regulatory clearance for US tokenized stocks landed **Jul 23 2026**, and the products are growing — but the token is a governance instrument standing next to that business rather than inside it. That is the structural distinction that matters: not that ONDO dilutes, but that it dilutes with a buy side that is **empty by design**, while its peers dilute against a bid.

It is also worth separating ONDO from tokens whose unlocks are cliff-shaped. A dated cliff is priced in advance because everyone can see it coming, and the market absorbs it in one move. Ondo Finance releases **150M** at a time on unannounced dates, which converts a priceable event into a continuous unpriced drip. For a framework that rewards predictability, that is the worse structure of the two, even where the annual totals would match.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The escrow balance is the whole watch list — it stood at **5,329.2M ONDO** on **Aug 25 2026**, and the observed gap of 42 to 60 days between 150M releases puts the next one somewhere around **Oct 2026**, with no announced date. The Delaware control litigation is the second item: whoever ends up holding the escrow keys sets the release pace, and a change of control could accelerate or freeze it. Third, watch for any binding governance vote on a fee switch or a buyback — the vote portal has been paused since **Jan 2024**, and a single executed proposal there would be the first non-zero entry the buy side of this ledger has ever carried. Fourth, the next dated cliff lands **Jan 18 2027** at roughly **1.71B ONDO**, outside this window but large enough that the market will begin pricing it well before the date. Fifth, watch the overlap itself: once the escrow drains past **5,130.7M**, releases will start registering as genuine new float upstream, and the monitor gap on this page will close on its own.

## Summary

The Pressure Framework reads ONDO at **+4.79%** net supply growth for the 90 days to **Aug 25 2026**, projected to continue at the same rate, which places Ondo Finance in the lowest inflation band. The structural mechanism is unusual and worth stating plainly: nothing is minted and nothing ever has been, but roughly **233.1M ONDO** per quarter walks out of a project-controlled escrow on dates nobody publishes, against a buy side that is **0** on all four rows. The key risk is that the escrow still holds **5,329.2M ONDO**, more than the entire circulating float, and control of it is being contested in court after the founder's death. The ceiling is real — **10 billion** tokens, never exceeded — but with less than half of them distributed, a hard cap is a statement about 2029, not about this quarter.

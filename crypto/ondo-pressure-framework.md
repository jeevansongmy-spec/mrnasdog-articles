# ONDO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

ONDO is the governance coin of Ondo Finance, a fixed pool of **10B** coins that opens on a yearly calendar instead of through mining or staking pay. From Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026 nothing was made, unlocked, bought back or burned, so supply moved **0.00%**, and other trackers show about the same, **+0.01%**. The next 90 days are very different: on **Jan 18 2027** about **1.71B ONDO** opens, which lifts the coins in circulation by about **+35.12%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

ONDO had a still quarter and faces a large step. Over the last 90 days the sell side and the buy side were both zero, so the net change was **0.00%** of the **4.87B ONDO** in circulation. Market data shows **+0.01%** for the same 90 days, practically the same reading, so the coin page carries no warning note. Looking forward, the Jan 18 2027 unlock of **1,710,223,118 ONDO** is the whole story: with nothing on the buy side, the next 90 days read **+35.12%**. The label for ONDO this month is a quiet past and a heavy, dated unlock ahead.

## Sell pressure: where new ONDO comes from

Protocol inflation is zero. ONDO has no mining and no staking pay, and total supply read **10B** at both ends of the window. The token contract still has a mint function, held only by Ondo's own multisig, but no ONDO has been made since the first 10B were created in 2022, and no plan to use it has been put forward.

Vesting is where every new ONDO comes from. Locked coins open once a year, on Jan 18. Nothing opened between Aug 13 and Nov 11 2026; the last step was on Jan 18 2026, when the coins in circulation jumped from about 3.16B to **4.87B**. The next step, about **1.71B ONDO** on Jan 18 2027, sits inside the next 90 days. Roughly 46% of it goes to the ecosystem fund, 39% to the Foundation and 15% to early investors. Some trackers quote a bigger figure near 1.94B for these yearly steps; the coins actually added to circulation last January matched the 1.71B size, and that is the number used here.

Foundation and other unscheduled releases add nothing this quarter. The Foundation moved **300M ONDO** out of its main multisig in two steps, on Aug 21 and Sep 23 2026, and a payout wallet spread almost all of it across 15 other wallets. Those coins came from the Foundation's unlocked share, which already counts as circulating, so they shifted owners without raising the count. No court case or failed firm is holding ONDO for a later payout either.

## Buy pressure: where new ONDO goes

Nothing on the buy side is running. Ondo Finance earns fees on its tokenized funds and stocks, but none of that money buys ONDO, and no proposal to change that has gone to a vote. Nothing burns ONDO either: the usual burn addresses held zero at both ends of the window and total supply never moved.

The Foundation pays ONDO out rather than buying it, and we found no public plan for it to buy any in the next 90 days. ONDO also has no staking or lock-up program for holders, so there is no pool that pulls coins out of circulation. Nothing took ONDO back in the last 90 days, and nothing is set to in the next 90.

## Foundation and overhang

The Ondo Foundation's main multisig holds **5.18B ONDO**. The Foundation says **4.42B** of that is still locked, which leaves about **757M** unlocked ONDO it can pay out at any time. That unlocked share was about 1.06B at the start of the window and fell by the 300M paid out in August and September.

The Foundation's payout wallet still holds about **36M ONDO**, the tail of the September transfer. Payouts from the multisig came in 150M steps in May, June, August and September 2026, and none since Sep 23. Coins from either wallet are already counted as circulating, so they would not add to supply, but they can still reach the market as selling.

Beyond the Foundation's multisig, about **708M** more locked ONDO is held elsewhere and opens on the same Jan 18 calendar. After Jan 18 2027 two equal steps remain, on Jan 18 2028 and Jan 18 2029.

## How ONDO compares to other real-world-asset and DeFi governance coins

ONDO works like a plain vesting coin. It has no emission, no fee share and no burn, so its supply only ever moves in steps, once a year. That makes the curve easy to read but lumpy: a whole year of new supply arrives on one day.

Chainlink's LINK also grows mostly through releases from team holdings, but those come several times a year in smaller slices, and LINK has a reserve that turns service fees into LINK purchases, so some buying meets the new supply. Pyth's PYTH opens in large blocks like ONDO, with one block still to come. Aave's AAVE, by contrast, already has about 15.4M of its 16M coins out, so little is left to unlock. Next to these, ONDO still has about half of its coins locked and no mechanism that takes coins back.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Jan 18 2027**: the yearly unlock of about 1.71B ONDO. Watch where the ecosystem and Foundation shares go after the date, since unlocked coins that stay in the multisig do not reach the market at once.

The Foundation's payouts: the last 150M step left the multisig on Sep 23 2026. A new step would not change the supply count, but it would be fresh selling that the payout wallet could pass on within days.

**Feb 9 2027**: the end of the next 90-day window. Without a change to the schedule, the count reads about +35% by then, and the next step after that is on Jan 18 2028.

Any vote on fees: a proposal to send part of Ondo's product fees to ONDO buybacks would be the first thing that takes ONDO back. None was on the table on Nov 11 2026.

## Summary

ONDO supply did not move from Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026: no new coins, no unlock, no buyback and no burn.

The Jan 18 2027 unlock adds about 1.71B ONDO, roughly 35% more coins in circulation within the next 90 days, with nothing on the buy side to offset it.

The Ondo Foundation holds about 757M unlocked ONDO plus 36M in its payout wallet, and moved 300M in August and September 2026 that were already counted as circulating.

Two more yearly steps of the same size follow on Jan 18 2028 and Jan 18 2029, after which all 10B ONDO are out.

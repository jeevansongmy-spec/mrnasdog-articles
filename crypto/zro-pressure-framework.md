# ZRO Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

LayerZero mints no new ZRO — the supply across all seven chains it lives on read exactly **1,000M ZRO** at both ends of the last 90 days — and yet the Pressure Framework reads ZRO at **+19.81%** over the trailing 90 days and **+19.81%** over the next 90. All of it comes from one mechanism: investor and core-team unlocks of about **23.3M ZRO** on the 20th of every month until **May 20 2027**. Sell pressure is **69.98M ZRO**, buy pressure is **0**, and the monitor reads **+39.91%** against a fixed ceiling of 1,000M ZRO.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **353.3M ZRO**, the framework books **69.98M ZRO** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days, a net of **+19.81%**, and projects **+19.81%** for the next 90 days on the same monthly calendar. The inflation monitor reads **+39.91%** for the same window, a gap of **20.11 percentage points**, which is over the framework's 0.5-point tolerance and ships with a monitor-gap warning on the overview page. The gap splits cleanly. The monitor's supply series did not move with each unlock; it stepped once, from **252.4M** to **353.3M ZRO** on **Jul 8 2026**, and stayed flat through the Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026 unlocks. **7.92 points** of the gap is the monitor dividing by the smaller supply of 90 days ago, and **12.19 points** is that single recount carrying about **30.8M ZRO** more than the three unlocks dated inside the window. The label for ZRO is **a fixed-supply token whose float is still unlocking fast**.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

It does not come from minting. ZRO has no block reward, no staking reward and no emission of any kind; all **1,000M ZRO** were created at the June 2024 launch. ZRO lives on Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Base, Optimism, Polygon and Avalanche, and a coin that crosses between them is burned on one chain and minted on the other. Read on all seven chains at both ends of the window, the total came to exactly 1,000M ZRO both times. So Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**. The Zero chain that LayerZero plans to launch in autumn 2026 will use ZRO for staking, so this row is watched rather than closed.

The whole ZRO supply story is Sell #2, vesting unlocks, at **69.98M ZRO**. After a one-year lock from launch, strategic partners and core contributors unlock in 24 monthly steps on the 20th, from **Jun 20 2025** to **May 20 2027**. The strategic-partner step was about 15.1M ZRO until the LayerZero Foundation bought 50M ZRO back from those partners in September 2025; the remaining partner allocation now releases about **12.7M ZRO** a month. Core contributors release about **10.6M ZRO** a month on the published calendar, and LayerZero says their individual terms are slower, so that share is a ceiling rather than a floor. Together that is about **23.3M ZRO** per step. Three steps fell in the window, on Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026, and three fall in the next one, on Oct 20, Nov 20 and Dec 20 2026. No ZRO is created by an unlock. The locked coins already sit in ordinary investor and custody wallets under a contract, so the unlock lifts a legal lock rather than moving coins out of a vesting contract — and that is exactly the moment ZRO becomes sellable float.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0**: the two largest LayerZero Foundation custody wallets did not move by a single coin across the window. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is also **0**. The **40M ZRO** once set aside for the FTX estate went back to strategic partners in a settlement and now unlocks inside the monthly steps above, not as a separate trustee payout.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0** in the ledger, and it needs explaining because a real buyback runs. Since April 2026, all Stargate bridge revenue buys ZRO on the open market each month, and **485,732 ZRO** was bought inside this window. Those coins are held in a LayerZero Foundation wallet, not burned: that wallet grew by exactly the published monthly purchases, to **2.4M ZRO**. The framework only counts a buyback when the coins leave the counted float, and that could not be shown for this wallet, so the purchases are recorded as a watched balance instead of a supply cut. At the current pace the buyback would remove well under 1% of float a quarter even if it were counted.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**. The LayerZero fee switch would add a protocol fee to each message and burn ZRO with it. Holders have voted on it four times, most recently on **Jun 20–27 2026**, and every result was off. The chain agrees: supply across the seven chains stayed at exactly 1,000M ZRO and the dead address did not move, so nothing was destroyed. The ATLAS exchange engine announced on **Aug 25 2026** plans to buy and burn ZRO with 75% of its net fees, but it runs on the Zero chain, which is not live. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0**: LayerZero Labs made a one-off open-market purchase in November 2025 and says later buys have no fixed timing. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**, because ZRO has no staking yet.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest ZRO overhang is the rest of the unlock calendar: about **186.6M ZRO** still to release in eight monthly steps from Oct 20 2026 to May 20 2027. The second is the LayerZero Foundation's re-locked reserve of **183M ZRO**, including the 50M it bought back from partners, which stays locked until the Zero chain launches; there is no launch date yet. The third is the Foundation's two largest custody wallets, holding **106.1M** and **69.5M ZRO**, unchanged across the window. Then come the buyback wallet at **2.4M ZRO**, which has never sent a coin out, and the old Stargate-to-ZRO swap pot at **13.4M ZRO**, which pays out slowly as STG holders convert. Each balance is read again from the chain at every rebuild. If any of these balances falls between refreshes by more than its schedule explains, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How ZRO compares to other unlock-driven tokens

ZRO belongs to the class of fixed-supply tokens that launched with most of their supply locked. On issuance alone it is stricter than any proof-of-work coin: Bitcoin still mints on every block at a slowing rate, while LayerZero mints nothing at all. It is also stricter than a staking chain paying a few percent a year in new coins. And yet ZRO reads **+19.81%** a quarter, far above either, because a hard cap limits total supply, not tradable float. What matters for ZRO is how fast locked coins become sellable, and right now that is about a fifth of the float every 90 days.

Against other venture-backed tokens with monthly unlocks, ZRO is typical in shape and heavy in size, because the float is still small next to the locked amount. What sets ZRO apart is that the unlocks are contract locks, not on-chain vesting, so there is no contract balance that drains as coins release. The one real offset is on paper: LayerZero has a fee-switch burn, a Stargate-funded buyback and a planned ATLAS burn. Exchange tokens with quarterly buyback-and-burn programmes can read negative. ZRO's burn has never switched on and its buyback holds what it buys, so the offset today is **0**.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the three monthly unlocks on **Oct 20 2026**, **Nov 20 2026** and **Dec 20 2026**, about 23.3M ZRO each; absent a change, the next reading lands near +19.81% again. Second, the Zero chain launch planned for autumn 2026: it would switch on ZRO staking and could start the ATLAS burn, and it is also the trigger that ends the lock on the Foundation's 183M ZRO reserve. Third, the next fee-switch referendum; the four so far ran in June and December, so another vote around **Dec 20 2026** is possible, and a yes would start burning ZRO for the first time. Fourth, the buyback wallet at 2.4M ZRO and the two Foundation custody wallets at 106.1M and 69.5M ZRO, which are the pots with a spender rather than a schedule.

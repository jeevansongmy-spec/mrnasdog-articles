# ZRO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

LayerZero's ZRO supply grew a net **+18.68%** between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026, and the next 90 days point to about **+17.38%**: supply growing, and projected to keep growing. Three monthly unlocks opened **69.84M ZRO** for early investors and the team, and Stargate swaps paid out **5.42M ZRO**, while the Stargate buyback took back only **530,212 ZRO**. ## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 23 2026, ZRO's circulating supply grew by a net **+18.68%**, and the next 90 days, to Feb 21 2027, project **+17.38%**. Other trackers show **+12.75%** for the same stretch, a gap of **5.94 points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. Their circulating figure moved only once, up 46.67M ZRO on Oct 6 2026, which is about two monthly unlocks; it has not yet counted the unlocks of Oct 20 and Nov 20 2026 or the Stargate swap payouts. ZRO is a coin that is **still in its unlock years**: a fixed batch reaches early backers every month until mid-2027, and nothing on the buy side comes close to it yet.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

Protocol inflation books **0**. Before Nov 23 2026 ZRO had no new coins at all. On that day staking opened for Zero, LayerZero's own chain, with rewards paid in new ZRO at a target of about 1% a year. Both the staked ZRO and its rewards stay locked until Mar 31 2027, so no new ZRO from staking can reach the market before Feb 21 2027.

Vesting unlocks are the big row: **69.84M ZRO**. On the 20th of each month about **23.28M ZRO** opens up, split between strategic partners (about 10.99M), core contributors (about 10.63M) and a bucket of coins LayerZero bought back from early backers before launch (about 1.67M). Releases landed on Sep 20, Oct 20 and Nov 20 2026, and the same three-release pattern repeats on Dec 20 2026, Jan 20 2027 and Feb 20 2027. The strategic partners' share is lower than it once was because the Foundation bought 50M ZRO from them in Sep 2025, cutting their monthly batch from 15M to about 12.7M.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book **0**. The Foundation's last 183M ZRO is locked until Zero's main network goes live, set for Nov 30 2026. When it opens it moves into the Foundation's own hands, not onto the market, so it adds nothing to the count unless the Foundation starts sending it out.

The long-term locked or bankruptcy row books **0**: no court, estate or failed firm holds ZRO for a later payout.

Stargate swap payouts are an extra sell row: **5.42M ZRO**. When LayerZero bought Stargate, holders of the old STG coin could swap each STG for 0.08634 ZRO from a pot the Foundation filled. Between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026 that pot paid out 5.42M ZRO to 1,188 wallets, most of it in the first half of October. Swaps close on Dec 15 2026, so the next 90 days carry only about **0.37M ZRO** more.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

The programmatic buyback takes back **530,212 ZRO**. All of Stargate's revenue buys ZRO on the open market once a month; the buys on Sep 4, Oct 6 and Nov 5 2026 cost about $846K. The coins are kept, not burned, in one LayerZero wallet that now holds **2.72M ZRO** and has never sent any out. Because the buyback is set in dollars, today's lower price stretches it: the same dollars buy about **717K ZRO** over the next 90 days.

The protocol fee burn books **0**. Holders have voted four times on turning on a fee that would buy and burn ZRO, and every vote left it off, the last in June 2026. Zero's trading engine, ATLAS, is meant to spend 75% of its fees after venue rebates on buying and burning ZRO from Nov 30 2026, but it has no trading record yet, so nothing is booked ahead of it.

The Foundation buy row books **0**. Its one large purchase, 50M ZRO from early investors in Sep 2025, has not been repeated, and those coins were locked again with the Foundation.

The new long-term lock row books **0**. Staked ZRO is locked until Mar 31 2027, but no staked amount has been reported yet and staked coins still count as circulating. Venues that want the best rebates on ATLAS must also stake ZRO, up to 1% of all ZRO for the top level, but no venue has reported an amount.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the Foundation's **183M ZRO** that stays locked until Zero goes live; the Foundation says it will use it slowly to grow the network and has no plan for another airdrop. Its Future Initiatives wallet holds **106.06M ZRO** and a second LayerZero wallet holds **29.0M ZRO**; neither moved between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026. The buyback wallet holds **2.72M ZRO** and the Stargate swap pot **8.10M ZRO**, which is no longer needed for swaps after Dec 15 2026. We read these balances at every rebuild: if any of them falls between checks, that outflow joins the sell side at the next check.

## How ZRO compares to other cross-chain and app tokens

ZRO is built like most tokens of its launch year: 1B coins made at launch, a large slice for early investors and the team, and a monthly release that runs about three years from launch. Chains such as Ethereum or Solana grow supply by paying validators; ZRO had no such payment at all until staking opened on Nov 23 2026, and even now its 1% target is small next to its unlocks. Its supply story is the unlock calendar, not the network.

Against tokens that turned their revenue into a buyback, such as Hyperliquid's HYPE or Jupiter's JUP, ZRO's buyback is small: about 0.1% of the circulating supply in 90 days, against unlocks well over a hundred times larger. It also keeps the coins rather than burning them, so they could come back one day. The ATLAS burn and a future fee switch are the two things that could make the buy side matter.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 30 2026:** Zero's main network and the ATLAS trading engine go live; the Foundation's 183M ZRO lock is tied to this day, and ATLAS fees start the buy-and-burn.

**Dec 15 2026:** STG to ZRO swaps close; the 8.10M ZRO left in the swap pot then stops paying out, and where it goes next is worth reading.

**Dec 20 2026, Jan 20 2027 and Feb 20 2027:** the next three monthly unlocks of about 23.28M ZRO each.

**Late December 2026:** the fee-switch vote has come round every six months since Dec 2024, so a fifth vote may be due; a yes would add a second buy-and-burn.

**Mar 31 2027, just past this window:** staked ZRO and its first rewards unlock, the first new ZRO ever to reach holders.

## Summary

ZRO's supply is growing fast, about **+18.68%** in the 90 days to Nov 23 2026 and about **+17.38%** projected for the next 90 days. Almost all of it is the monthly unlock of about 23.28M ZRO for early investors and the team, which runs until mid-2027. The Stargate buyback takes back roughly 530K to 717K ZRO a quarter and keeps the coins, far too little to offset the unlocks. The main risks to watch are the Foundation's 183M ZRO once Zero goes live, and the main hope is the ATLAS buy-and-burn starting on Nov 30 2026.

# ZRO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
Looking for the buy-or-sell answer? It is on the ZRO coin page, with demand and price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/zro](https://mrnasdog.com/research/zro)

LayerZero's **ZRO** is strongly inflationary on its circulating float: in the 90 days to Oct 16 2026, **74.84M ZRO** reached the market against a **506,608 ZRO** buyback, so circulating supply grew by about **18.58%**, and the next 90 days look the same at **18.63%**. No new ZRO has been minted since launch — the total stays at 1B — but only **399.98M** circulates, and the rest is released to the team, early investors and old Stargate holders month by month until May 2027.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Our ledger puts ZRO's 90-day net supply change at **+18.58%** of the **399.98M ZRO** float: **74.84M ZRO** of new circulating supply (three monthly team and investor releases of **23.33M** each, plus **4.86M ZRO** paid out to people swapping old STG) against a **506,608 ZRO** buyback. The monitor reads **+12.81%** for the same stretch, a gap of **5.77 percentage points**, well past our 0.5-point line, so the page carries a ⚠ monitor gap note. We walked five kinds of source and found why: the circulating figure the monitor reads only moves in jumps (flat near **353.3M** from Jul 8 to Oct 5 2026, then up **44.7M** in one day on Oct 6), while the published schedule releases coins every month. Our number stays. In one line: ZRO is a no-emission token that is **inflationary by unlock schedule**, not by mining or staking rewards.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. The ZRO contract created all 1,000,000,000 coins on the day it was deployed, and it has no function that prints more. ZRO lives on seven chains — Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Base, Optimism, Polygon and Avalanche — and moving it between them destroys coins on one side and recreates them on the other. Added up across all seven on Oct 16 2026, total supply reads exactly **1,000,000,000 ZRO**.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **69.98M ZRO** in the last 90 days. LayerZero's core contributors receive **10.63M ZRO** and its strategic partners (the early investors) **12.7M ZRO** on the 20th of every month, **23.33M** in total. The releases on Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026 fell inside our window, and the releases on Oct 20, Nov 20 and Dec 20 2026 fall inside the next one, so the forward figure is the same **69.98M**. LayerZero itself reports that investors still held about 64% of what had unlocked by May 31 2026, so not every released coin is sold — but a released coin can be sold at any time, and that is what this row counts. Eight releases remain, the last in May 2027, about **186.6M ZRO**.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**: none of the Foundation's known wallets moved a single ZRO in the window (more on them below). Long-term locks and bankruptcy are **0** too. The 40M ZRO that the Foundation once bought from the FTX estate went back to the strategic partners in the legal settlement, so it already sits inside their monthly releases.

The fifth source is new: STG-to-ZRO swaps, **4.86M ZRO**. When the LayerZero Foundation bought Stargate in 2025, it promised every STG holder **0.08634 ZRO** per STG and filled a swap contract with **56.98M ZRO**. In our window **56.33M STG** was swapped and destroyed, and exactly **4,863,418 ZRO** left the contract — most of it in October, when large exchanges converted their users' STG. We count it as new supply because those coins came out of a Foundation-funded contract, not from the market. Swaps close on Dec 15 2026, and at most about **8.43M ZRO** more can go out; we project the same **4.86M** for the next 90 days.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

The programmatic buyback took **506,608 ZRO** off the market. Since April 2026 all of Stargate's revenue buys ZRO once a month, and three buys landed in the window: **160,271 ZRO** on Aug 6, **183,904 ZRO** on Sep 4 and **162,433 ZRO** on Oct 6 2026, about **$677K** in all. The coins are not burned; they go to a Foundation wallet that now holds **2.54M ZRO** and has never sent any out. Because the buyback is set in dollars, the same spending at today's **$2.04** buys about **332K ZRO** in the next 90 days. That is less than 1% of what the monthly releases add.

The protocol fee burn is **0**. LayerZero has a fee switch that would add a fee to every cross-chain message and use it to buy and burn ZRO, but holders have voted on it four times, most recently Jun 20–27 2026, and it has stayed off each time. Foundation buys are **0**: LayerZero Labs bought $10M of ZRO once, in November 2025, with no plan to repeat it on a schedule. New long-term locks are **0**, because ZRO has no staking until the Zero chain launches.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the Foundation reserve: **183M ZRO**, including the 50M it bought back from investors in September 2025, locked until LayerZero's new Zero chain goes live. No launch date is set. On-chain, the Foundation's known wallets hold **106.06M ZRO** in a future-projects multisig, **69.55M ZRO** in a launch-distribution safe and **29.0M ZRO** in a third wallet; all three were read at both ends of the window and did not move. Add the **2.54M ZRO** of bought-back coins and the **8.67M ZRO** still waiting in the STG swap contract. On top of that, about **186.6M ZRO** of team and investor coins are still to be released on the monthly schedule. We read these wallets at every rebuild: if a Foundation balance falls between checks, the outflow goes into the Foundation row at the next check.

## How ZRO compares to other cross-chain tokens

ZRO belongs to the group of cross-chain messaging tokens that launched with most of their supply locked, as Wormhole's W also did. ZRO has a set total of 1B and no mining or staking rewards, so its supply growth comes almost entirely from insider releases. What stands out is the shape: ZRO releases the same amount every month on a fixed day, which makes its new supply easy to predict but steady and heavy — about **6%** of today's float each month until May 2027.

Tokens such as Axelar's AXL work the other way: they pay validators with newly minted coins, so supply keeps growing with no end date. ZRO has no such stream today; it will only start paying stakers when the Zero chain launches. Against buyback tokens, ZRO's buyback is real but small — it is funded by one product, Stargate, and spent about **$677K** in 90 days, against roughly **$143M** worth of coins released at today's price over the same stretch. If the fee switch ever passes, every LayerZero message would feed a burn, which would put ZRO closer to fee-burning tokens.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 20, Nov 20 and Dec 20 2026:** the next three monthly releases of **23.33M ZRO** each, to core contributors and strategic partners.

**Dec 15 2026:** STG-to-ZRO swaps close. Whatever is left in the swap contract (now **8.67M ZRO**) stops flowing to the market after that day.

**December 2026:** fee switch votes have come every six months since December 2024. A fifth vote that passes would start a burn funded by LayerZero message fees.

**Zero mainnet (no date):** the launch would unlock the Foundation's **183M ZRO** reserve and start staking. We will watch whether any of that reserve moves.

**Monthly buybacks:** the next Stargate-funded buy is due in early November; a larger Stargate revenue month means a larger buy.

## Summary

ZRO's total supply stays at 1B and it has no emission, yet its circulating supply grew about **18.58%** in the 90 days to Oct 16 2026 because **23.33M ZRO** of team and investor coins unlock every month and old Stargate holders swapped into **4.86M ZRO**. The Stargate-funded buyback took back **506,608 ZRO**, under 1% of that. The next 90 days look the same at **18.63%**, and the monthly releases run until May 2027, with the Foundation's 183M reserve unlocking whenever the Zero chain goes live. The main risk is steady insider supply; the main thing that could change the picture is a fee switch that burns ZRO.

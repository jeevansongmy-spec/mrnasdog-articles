# ZRO Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

LayerZero's **ZRO** supply is growing fast, and not because new tokens are minted. All 1B ZRO already exist; what grows is the part that can be traded. Monthly vesting unlocks released about **69.98M ZRO** to investors and staff over the 90 days to Sep 29 2026, against a buyback and burn side that removes **0**, for net growth of **+19.81%** of the **353.31M** circulating — and the same **+19.81%** is lined up for the next 90 days. The monitor reads **+40.52%**, because its count caught up with older unlocks in one jump on Jul 8 2026.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 1 to Sep 29 2026, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads ZRO's net supply change at **+19.81%**: **69.98M ZRO** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure, divided by **353.31M ZRO** circulating. The next 90 days carry the same three monthly unlock steps, so the forecast is also **+19.81%**. The inflation monitor reads **+40.52%** for the same window, a gap of **20.71 percentage points**, which is larger than our 0.5-point tolerance, so the page carries a ⚠ monitor-gap note. The gap is explained but not closed: the monitor's circulating count jumped about **103M ZRO** in a single day on Jul 8 2026 when it caught up with unlocks that happened before this window, and it has not yet added the Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026 unlocks. ZRO is an **unlock-driven float**: a fixed supply whose tradable share is still being released on a calendar.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. ZRO has no block reward and no staking reward, and every one of the 1B tokens was created in a single mint before the June 2024 launch. ZRO lives on seven chains as an omnichain token: moving it from one chain to another burns it on the first chain and mints it on the second. That makes each chain's own count move — the Ethereum count rose by about 130K ZRO across the window — but read together on Sep 29 2026 the seven chains add up to exactly **1,000,000,000 ZRO**. We count the token once, across all chains, and nothing new appeared.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **69.98M ZRO** in the window. After a one-year cliff, LayerZero's strategic partners and core contributors unlock on the 20th of every month from June 2025 to May 2027. LayerZero said on Jun 3 2026 that the strategic-partner step is now **12.7M ZRO** a month, down from 15M after the LayerZero Foundation bought back 50M investor tokens in September 2025. Core contributors hold 255M over 24 steps, up to **10.625M** a month, so each step releases about **23.3M ZRO**. Three steps fell inside the window — Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026. The one staff payout wallet we can read sent out 5.73M ZRO to 57 wallets in those three steps, less than the full staff step, because staff vest on their own start dates; we still count the full published schedule, the careful reading. About **186.6M ZRO** remain to unlock after Sep 20 2026.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**: no Foundation wallet sold or moved ZRO this window. Long-term locked or bankruptcy releases are also **0**. The 40M ZRO once bought back from the FTX estate were returned to strategic partners under a legal settlement, and they now unlock inside the normal monthly vesting schedule, so they are not counted twice.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

The programmatic buyback is real but books **0**. Since April 2026 all of Stargate's revenue buys ZRO on the open market each month. In this window **485,732 ZRO**, about $462K, arrived in the Foundation's buyback wallet: 141,557 on Jul 8, 160,271 on Aug 6 and 183,904 on Sep 4 2026. The wallet now holds **2.38M ZRO** and has never sold. But the tokens are held, not burned, in a Foundation wallet that the circulating count may already include, so we cannot show them leaving the market. Even if they were counted, the buyback would offset less than **1%** of the vesting unlocks.

The protocol fee burn is **0**. LayerZero's fee switch would add a fee to every cross-chain message and use it to buy and burn ZRO, but it has come out “off” in four holder votes in a row, most recently on Jun 20–27 2026 — support was high, but too few holders voted to meet the quorum. The dead address held the same 0.31 ZRO at both ends of the window, and the seven-chain total stayed at exactly 1B, so no burn happened on either surface. ATLAS, the trading engine LayerZero announced on Aug 25 2026, plans to send 75% of its net fees to buying and burning ZRO, but it runs on the Zero chain, which is not live. Foundation buying is **0** in the window — the last purchases were the 50M investor buyback in September 2025 and a $10M market purchase by LayerZero Labs in November 2025. A new long-term lock is also **0**: ZRO has no staking lock today.

## Foundation and overhang

The LayerZero Foundation and related wallets hold a large overhang that is not on any calendar. The main reserve wallet holds **106.06M ZRO**, a second Foundation safe holds **69.55M** and a third LayerZero wallet holds **29.0M**; all three read the same on Jul 1 and Sep 29 2026. The Foundation also says **183M ZRO** stay re-locked until its Zero chain launches, which it targets for autumn 2026 with no date. The swap contract that turns old Stargate STG into ZRO at a fixed 0.08634 ZRO per STG holds **12.80M ZRO**; it paid out 724,603 ZRO this window, almost all of it after an end date of Dec 15 2026 was set on Sep 15 2026. The buyback wallet holds 2.38M, and the staff payout wallet holds 20.73M for future steps. We read these wallets on the chain at every rebuild. If any of their balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How ZRO compares to other unlock-heavy infrastructure tokens

ZRO belongs to the class of fixed-supply infrastructure tokens whose float grows by vesting rather than by issuance. That is a different shape from an uncapped Layer 1 such as Ethereum, where validators are paid in new coins every block and a fee burn pushes back continuously. ZRO has no issuance at all, so in principle its supply story ends in May 2027, when the last investor and staff steps unlock — after that, only the Foundation reserves and the STG swap pool can add to the market.

The difference from buy-and-burn exchange tokens is the buy side. Tokens that burn a fixed share of revenue take coins out of the float every month; ZRO's Stargate buyback holds its coins in a Foundation wallet, and the protocol fee switch that would burn ZRO has never passed. And compared with tokens whose vesting is nearly done, ZRO's unlock step is heavy: about **6.6%** of its circulating count each month, because the float is still only about **35%** of total supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The next three vesting steps land on **Oct 20 2026**, **Nov 20 2026** and **Dec 20 2026**, about 23.3M ZRO each. The STG to ZRO swap closes on **Dec 15 2026**, and up to 12.80M ZRO could leave the swap contract before then. If the fee switch follows the pattern of the last two years, a fifth holder vote would run in late December 2026; a yes vote would start the first protocol burn. A Zero mainnet date would end the Foundation's 183M re-lock and bring ATLAS, and its buy-and-burn, closer. Finally, the monitor's circulating count should jump again when it catches up with the Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026 unlocks.

## Summary

ZRO is a fixed 1B-token supply whose tradable float is growing by **+19.81%** per 90 days as LayerZero investor and staff tokens unlock at about 23.3M a month, with the same pace set for the next 90 days. The Stargate buyback is real — 485,732 ZRO this window — but it is held by the Foundation and is far smaller than the unlocks, and no burn is live. The key risk is supply: 186.6M ZRO still to unlock through May 2027, plus Foundation reserves and a re-lock that ends when the Zero chain launches. The ceiling is the 1B created at launch: across all seven chains, total supply has held at exactly that number.

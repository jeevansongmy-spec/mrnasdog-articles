# ZRO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
Looking for the buy-or-sell answer? It is on the ZRO coin page, with demand and price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/zro](https://mrnasdog.com/research/zro)

ZRO supply grew a net **+18.65%** in the 90 days from Aug 15 to Nov 13 2026, and the next 90 days point to about **+17.56%**: supply growing, and projected to keep growing. No new ZRO is printed and the total stays at **1B**, but monthly releases for early investors and the LayerZero team added **69.98M ZRO** and STG swaps paid out **5.17M ZRO**, against a Stargate buyback of **530,212 ZRO**. Other trackers show **+13.12%** because their count has not caught up with the October release.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 15 to Nov 13 2026 the ZRO in circulation rose by a net **+18.65%**, and the next 90 days come to about **+17.56%**, because three more monthly releases are already on the calendar. Other trackers show **+13.12%**, a difference of **5.54 percentage points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. Their circulating figure sat near 353.3M ZRO until Oct 5 2026, jumped in one recount on Oct 6 2026, and has not yet added the 23.33M ZRO released on Oct 20 2026 or the ZRO paid out for STG. In one line: LayerZero is a capped token in the middle of a heavy release calendar, and its buyback is far too small to soak that up.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

Protocol inflation adds nothing. All **1B ZRO** were created in one step when the token launched in June 2024, and the token contract has no reward printing. ZRO moves between Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Base and other chains by being removed on one side and recreated on the other, so the total across all chains still adds up to 1B.

Vesting releases are the big one. On the 20th of every month about **23.33M ZRO** opens up: **12.7M** for strategic partners, the early investors, and about **10.63M** for the core contributors who build LayerZero. Releases on Aug 20, Sep 20 and Oct 20 2026 added **69.98M ZRO** in the window, and Nov 20 2026, Dec 20 2026 and Jan 20 2027 will add the same again. The investor share used to be 15M a month, until the LayerZero Foundation bought 50M ZRO back from investors in September 2025. The calendar ends in June 2027.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks booked zero. The LayerZero Foundation's large wallets did not send out a single coin in the window, and no plan to release more has been published.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins booked zero as well. The 40M ZRO that once sat with FTX and Alameda went back to LayerZero, and its monthly slice of about 1.67M ZRO goes to LayerZero itself, not to the market.

STG swaps are the fifth source. When the LayerZero Foundation bought Stargate in 2025, it promised STG holders **0.08634 ZRO** for every STG. The swap contract paid out **5.17M ZRO** between Aug 15 and Nov 13 2026, about 3.35M of it in early October, when Binance, KuCoin and other exchanges swapped their users' STG in one go. Swaps stop on Dec 15 2026, so only about **0.8M ZRO** more is expected at the recent pace.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

The programmatic buyback is the only buyer that books a number. All of Stargate's revenue buys ZRO on the open market once a month. Buys on Sep 4, Oct 6 and Nov 5 2026 took in **530,212 ZRO** for about $845K. The coins sit in a public LayerZero wallet, which now holds about 2.72M ZRO, and they are not burned. At today's price the same dollars buy about **527,954 ZRO** in the next 90 days.

The protocol fee burn is zero today. ZRO holders have voted four times, most recently from Jun 20 to Jun 27 2026, on turning on a LayerZero fee that would buy and burn ZRO, and each time it stayed off. The ATLAS trading engine plans to spend 75% of its fees, after rebates to trading venues, on buying and burning ZRO once it goes live, but it has no trading volume yet, so we book nothing until it does.

A Foundation buy is zero. LayerZero Labs bought $10M of ZRO on the market once, in November 2025, and has set no repeat date. A new long-term lock is also zero: staking for the Zero chain and for ATLAS venues has not started, and staked ZRO would still count as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the release calendar itself: eight more monthly releases from Nov 20 2026 to Jun 20 2027, at about 23.33M ZRO each, or roughly **186.6M ZRO**. Next comes the LayerZero Foundation. It says **183M ZRO** of its own coins, including the 50M bought back from investors, stay locked until its new chain, Zero, goes live, set for Nov 30 2026. Nothing has been said about what happens to them after that. Its three large wallets hold about **106.06M**, **69.55M** and **29.0M ZRO** and did not move in the window. The buyback wallet holds about 2.72M ZRO, and the STG swap contract about 8.35M ZRO that is left over for swaps until Dec 15 2026. If any of these balances falls, the coins that leave will show up on the coin page at the next check.

## How ZRO compares to other interoperability tokens

Most tokens of cross-chain projects share ZRO's shape: a fixed total, a big slice for investors and the team, and a release calendar that drives supply for years after launch. What sets LayerZero apart is that two kinds of buying sit on top of that calendar. One is already running and small, the monthly Stargate buyback; the other is planned and still untested, the ATLAS burn. Many peers have only the calendar and nothing on the buy side.

LayerZero is also turning into a chain of its own. On Zero, ZRO pays the fees and secures the network through staking, which makes it look more like the coin of a proof-of-stake chain than a pure project token. For now none of that changes the count: no staking reward has been announced, so no new ZRO is printed, and the release calendar still does almost all of the work.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Nov 20 2026: the next monthly release of about **23.33M ZRO**, followed by Dec 20 2026 and Jan 20 2027. Each one is close to 6% of the ZRO in circulation today.

Nov 30 2026: Zero and the ATLAS trading engine are set to go live with more than a dozen trading venues. Two things to watch that day: whether ATLAS draws real trading, since 75% of its fees after rebates go to buying and burning ZRO, and whether the Foundation says anything about the 183M ZRO kept locked until Zero goes live.

Early December 2026 and early January 2027: the next Stargate buybacks, each so far between about 160K and 185K ZRO.

Dec 15 2026: STG swaps into ZRO close for good. About 8.35M ZRO is still in the swap contract; a last-minute rush could pay out more than the 0.8M we expect.

December 2026: if the fee-switch vote keeps its six-month rhythm, a fifth vote could come around then. None has been announced.

## Summary

ZRO supply grew a net **+18.65%** from Aug 15 to Nov 13 2026 and should grow about **+17.56%** in the next 90 days.

Almost all of that comes from monthly releases of about 23.33M ZRO to early investors and the LayerZero team, which run until June 2027, plus the last STG swaps before Dec 15 2026.

The Stargate buyback took back 530,212 ZRO in the window, less than 1% of what came out, and the ATLAS burn has not started.

Other trackers show +13.12% only because their count has not yet caught up with the Oct 20 2026 release and the STG payouts; the coin page carries a warning note for that.

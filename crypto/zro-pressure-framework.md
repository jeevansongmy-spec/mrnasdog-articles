# ZRO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 7 2026 to Oct 5 2026, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads LayerZero's net supply change at **+11.02%**: **69.98M ZRO** of sell pressure against **0 ZRO** of buy pressure, measured on a float of **634.91M ZRO**. The inflation monitor reads **+39.96%** for roughly the same window, a gap of **28.94 percentage points**, which is above the 0.5-point line, so the coin page carries a ⚠ monitor gap chip. The gap does not come from new coins. The circulating count the monitor reads jumped by about 101M ZRO in a single day on Jul 8 2026 and then stayed near 353M ZRO, missing the Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026 unlocks; on Oct 5 2026 that count was raised to 634.9M ZRO, which now includes every unlock to date. Our label for ZRO: a no-mint token with a heavy, dated unlock calendar and no sink.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0 ZRO**. LayerZero has no block reward, no staking pay and no emission programme. All 1,000,000,000 ZRO were created once in 2024 and now live on seven EVM chains through burn-and-mint bridging; adding up the token's supply on Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Base, Optimism, Polygon and Avalanche on Oct 5 2026 gives 1B ZRO. Bridging moves ZRO from one chain to another and changes each chain's count, never the total.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **69.98M ZRO** in the last 90 days and **69.98M ZRO** in the next 90. Strategic partners and core contributors sat behind a one-year cliff after the June 2024 launch and now unlock on the 20th of every month until May 2027. LayerZero says the Foundation's purchase of 50M ZRO from investors in September 2025 cut the gross monthly investor step from 15M to **12.7M ZRO**. The team's 255M ZRO allocation vests on individual terms that LayerZero calls slower, so we count it at most at **10.63M ZRO** a month. Together that is about **23.3M ZRO** per unlock: Jul 20, Aug 20 and Sep 20 2026 inside the last window, and Oct 20, Nov 20 and Dec 20 2026 inside the next. These locks are contractual: investor and team tokens have sat in custody wallets since the 2024 distribution, so the published calendar, not a readable escrow, decides when ZRO joins the float.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0 ZRO**. The LayerZero Foundation released nothing in the window; its largest wallets, holding 106.06M ZRO and 69.55M ZRO, did not move. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is also **0 ZRO**: there is no estate paying ZRO out, and the 40M ZRO once tied to FTX and Alameda Research went back to strategic partners in a settlement and now vests inside their monthly step.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

The programmatic buyback books **0 ZRO**, even though it is real. Since April 2026, all of Stargate's revenue buys ZRO on the open market each month, and **485,732 ZRO** reached the LayerZero buyback wallet in this window, taking it to **2.38M ZRO**. Those coins are held, not burned, and the wallet already counts as part of the circulating float: the coins still outside the float are explained almost exactly by the Foundation's re-locked reserve and the unvested calendar. A purchase into a wallet inside the float takes nothing off the market.

The protocol fee burn is **0 ZRO**. LayerZero's rules include a fee on every cross-chain message that would be converted to ZRO and burned, but ZRO holders have voted the fee switch off four times, most recently between Jun 20 and Jun 27 2026. The burn address gained only 0.0002 ZRO in the window. A Foundation buy is **0 ZRO**: the last ones were the 50M ZRO repurchase from investors in September 2025 and a $10M market purchase by LayerZero Labs in November 2025. A new long-term lock is **0 ZRO**: nothing new was locked in the window, and the Foundation's re-lock covers coins that were already outside the float.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the vesting calendar itself: about **186.6M ZRO** still to unlock for investors and the team over eight monthly steps from Oct 20 2026 to May 20 2027, checked by hand every two weeks. Next is the Foundation's re-locked reserve of **183M ZRO**, including the 50M ZRO bought from investors, which stays locked until LayerZero's own Zero chain launches; LayerZero has said fall 2026 but has not given a date, and we watch its announcements every two weeks. The Foundation's two largest wallets, at **106.06M ZRO** and **69.55M ZRO**, are read on-chain every day.

Two smaller piles sit inside the float and are read on-chain every day: the Stargate buyback wallet at **2.38M ZRO**, and the STG-to-ZRO swap pool at **11.72M ZRO**, which pays STG holders 0.08634 ZRO per STG and paid out **1.81M ZRO** in the window. Exchanges are converting their users' STG in early October 2026, and the exchange swap window closes on Dec 15 2026. If the balance of any of these Foundation-controlled piles falls between our checks, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How ZRO compares to other cross-chain protocol tokens

Most proof-of-stake Layer 1 coins grow through issuance: validators are paid in new coins every epoch, and the rate is a policy choice. ZRO has no issuance at all, so its supply growth comes only from a vesting calendar with a known end. That makes ZRO closer to other capped infrastructure and DeFi tokens launched with a one-year cliff and two years of monthly unlocks than to an uncapped chain. The catch is size: about 23.3M ZRO a month is roughly 3.7% of today's float, far more than most mature coins add in a year.

On the buy side, ZRO differs from tokens with a live fee burn. A chain that burns part of every fee removes coins as it is used; LayerZero has the same design written into its rules but keeps it switched off by vote, so message volume does not reduce supply today. Its one active buyer, the Stargate revenue buyback, is closer to a treasury purchase than to a burn: the coins are bought and held by the Foundation, so they could later return to the market. Exchange tokens that buy and burn on a fixed schedule take coins out for good; ZRO's buyback does not.

The end point is the cleanest part of the comparison. Uncapped chains have no last unlock. ZRO's investor and team calendar ends on May 20 2027, after which the only large remaining overhang is the Foundation's re-locked 183M ZRO, whose release depends on the Zero launch rather than a date.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The next three unlocks fall on **Oct 20 2026**, **Nov 20 2026** and **Dec 20 2026**, each about 23.3M ZRO; an early investor wallet sent 8M ZRO to an institutional custody desk on Oct 3 2026, a sign some holders are preparing to sell or lend.

The Zero mainnet launch is the biggest swing factor: if it lands inside the window, it ends the lock on the Foundation's 183M ZRO, and any release of those coins would move straight into Sell #3.

A fifth fee-switch vote is due around late December 2026 on the six-month cycle; a yes would start a burn tied to message volume and give ZRO its first buy-side row.

The STG-to-ZRO conversion closes on exchanges on **Dec 15 2026**; the payouts come from a pool already inside the float, so they add no new supply, but a fast drain of the 11.72M ZRO pool would show where those coins go next.

The ceiling is clear: the investor and team calendar finishes on May 20 2027, and the total has stayed at 1B ZRO across all seven chains.

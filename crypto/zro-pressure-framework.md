# ZRO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

**ZRO is inflationary on its circulating float**, even though no new ZRO has been minted since launch. LayerZero created all **1 billion ZRO** at launch in June 2024, and the float grows because locked investor and team coins unlock on the 20th of every month. Between Jul 28 and Oct 26 2026, **74.90M ZRO** came into the market against a **506,608 ZRO** buyback, a net rise of **+18.60%**; other trackers show **+13.35%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the LayerZero float grew by a net **+18.60%**, and the next 90 days point to about **+18.62%**, because three more monthly unlocks are already on the calendar. Other trackers show **+13.35%** for the same 90 days, a gap of **5.25 percentage points**, so the ZRO coin page carries a warning note. The circulating figure those trackers use does not move on each unlock day: it sat near **353.3M ZRO** until Oct 5 2026 and then rose **45.6M ZRO** in one day on Oct 6 2026, and it has not yet added the Oct 20 2026 release or the ZRO paid out for STG swaps. The cite-able label for ZRO today: a **fixed-supply token that is inflationary on the active float by its unlock calendar**.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero**. ZRO has no block reward and no staking reward, the token contract has no mint function beyond the bridge that moves ZRO between chains, and no new ZRO has been minted since the single launch mint.

Vesting unlocks are the main source of new ZRO: **69.98M ZRO** in the window. Strategic partners, meaning investors and advisors, had a one-year lock and then receive monthly releases over two years; core contributors at LayerZero Labs follow the same shape. Each release is about **12.7M ZRO** to strategic partners and **10.63M ZRO** to core contributors, so **23.33M ZRO** unlocks on the 20th. The strategic partner step used to be larger; it fell after the LayerZero Foundation bought 50M ZRO back from partners in September 2025. Three releases landed on Aug 20, Sep 20 and Oct 20 2026, and three more are set for Nov 20 2026, Dec 20 2026 and Jan 20 2027, so the next 90 days also add **69.98M ZRO**. About **163M ZRO** is left on this calendar, which runs to May 2027.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **zero** this window. The LayerZero Foundation did not move any ZRO out of its large wallets, so no unscheduled release is booked.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **zero**. The 40M ZRO that LayerZero Labs once bought back from FTX and Alameda Research were returned to strategic partners in the legal settlement, so they now unlock with the monthly partner calendar rather than as a separate estate sale.

One extra source adds supply: STG-to-ZRO conversions, **4.92M ZRO** in the window. Since the LayerZero Foundation bought the Stargate bridge, holders of the old STG coin can swap 1 STG for 0.08634 ZRO through a contract the Foundation funded. Most of the 4.92M ZRO left that contract in October 2026, when large exchanges moved their STG users over. The swap contract still holds **8.61M ZRO**, and swaps close on Dec 15 2026, so at the recent pace the next 90 days add about the same amount again.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

The programmatic buyback took back **506,608 ZRO**. Since April 2026 all Stargate revenue buys ZRO on the open market once a month. In this window the buys landed on Aug 6, Sep 4 and Oct 6 2026, for about **$677K** in total. The ZRO goes to a LayerZero Foundation wallet that now holds **2.54M ZRO**; it is kept there, not burned. Because the buyback is set in dollars, the same revenue at today's price buys about **415K ZRO** over the next 90 days.

The protocol fee burn is **zero**. LayerZero has a fee switch that would charge a fee on each cross-chain message and use it to buy and burn ZRO, but ZRO holders have voted on it four times, most recently Jun 20 to 27 2026, and every vote missed its quorum, so the fee stays off. The ATLAS exchange engine is due to send 75% of its fees after venue rebates to buying and burning ZRO from Nov 30 2026, but it has no trading yet, so nothing is booked.

Foundation buying is **zero**. LayerZero Labs last bought ZRO on the open market in November 2025, $10M in one go, and says future buys follow its profits with no fixed timing, so nothing is projected for the next 90 days.

New long-term locks are **zero**. Staking on the Zero network and the ZRO that trading venues stake on ATLAS for better rebates both start with the Nov 30 2026 launch, and staked ZRO still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the **183M ZRO** the LayerZero Foundation keeps re-locked until the Zero mainnet launches. That pile is the rest of the 383M ZRO community allocation plus the 50M ZRO bought back from strategic partners, and the Foundation says it will use these coins slowly to grow the ecosystem, with no plan for another airdrop. The Foundation's three largest wallets hold **106.06M**, **69.55M** and **29.0M ZRO**, and none of them moved between Jul 28 and Oct 26 2026. The buyback wallet holds **2.54M ZRO**, the STG swap contract holds **8.61M ZRO**, and about **163M ZRO** of the investor and team calendar is still locked. If any Foundation wallet's balance falls, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks line at the next check.

## How ZRO compares to other interoperability tokens

ZRO sits with the fixed-supply interoperability tokens. Like Chainlink's LINK, ZRO has no emission at all: every coin exists from day one, and the float grows only when locked or reserve coins are released. That puts ZRO in a different group from interoperability chains such as Axelar, whose AXL pays validators in newly issued coins every block, so its supply rises even with no unlocks left.

Among unlock-driven tokens, the shape of the calendar matters. ZRO releases in even monthly steps of about 23.33M ZRO, which spreads the selling, while Wormhole's W releases mostly in large yearly cliffs that land on one day. A monthly calendar keeps the net rate high but steady; a cliff calendar is quiet for most of the year and then jumps.

On the buy side, ZRO is one of the few bridge tokens with a live buyback paid from real revenue, the Stargate income, but it holds the coins in a Foundation wallet rather than burning them, and the buyback is under 1% of what the calendar releases. The fee switch and the ATLAS burn are designs that could change that balance; neither is burning ZRO today.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 20 2026, Dec 20 2026 and Jan 20 2027:** the next three monthly investor and team unlocks, about 23.33M ZRO each, which is what keeps the next 90 days near +18.62%.

**Nov 30 2026:** the Zero mainnet and the ATLAS exchange engine are set to launch. That date starts the ATLAS buy-and-burn and ZRO staking, and it also ends the re-lock on the Foundation's 183M ZRO; any move of those coins would show up as a Foundation release.

**Dec 15 2026:** STG-to-ZRO swaps close. Up to 8.61M ZRO is left in the swap contract, and whatever is not claimed by then stops adding to supply.

**December 2026:** the fee switch referendum runs every six months, so the fifth vote is due around December 2026, with no date announced yet. A yes vote with quorum would start a message fee that buys and burns ZRO.

## Summary

LayerZero's ZRO has a fixed supply of 1 billion and no minting, but its circulating float is growing fast: about **+18.60%** in the last 90 days and about **+18.62%** in the next 90. The cause is the monthly unlock of about 23.33M ZRO to investors and the team, plus STG swap payouts, against a Stargate-funded buyback that takes back only about 507K ZRO per quarter and keeps it rather than burning it. The main risk is the Nov 30 2026 Zero launch, which ends the lock on 183M Foundation ZRO at the same time as it starts the first ZRO burn. The calendar has a hard end: the last investor and team unlock is due in May 2027, after which only Foundation releases can add to supply.

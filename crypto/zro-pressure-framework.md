# ZRO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
Looking for the buy-or-sell answer? It is on the ZRO coin page, with demand and price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/zro](https://mrnasdog.com/research/zro)

ZRO is **inflationary** for now: the cap is fixed at **1B ZRO** and no new coins are ever made, but early partners and staff get a release on the 20th of every month. Between Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026 those releases freed **69.98M ZRO** and STG swaps paid out **5.08M ZRO**, while the Stargate buyback took back only **506,608 ZRO**, so supply grew **+18.64%**, with **+17.57%** next. Other trackers show **+13.39%**, so the coin page carries a warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 4 2026, ZRO's circulating supply rose a net **+18.64%**, measured against the **399.98M ZRO** that circulate today. Next 90 days, the same three monthly releases come round again, so the figure stays close at **+17.57%**. Other trackers show **+13.39%**, a gap of **5.25 points**. That gap does not come from hidden coins. The circulating figure those trackers use sat near 353.3M ZRO until the end of September, jumped 45.62M ZRO in one recount on Oct 6 2026, and has not yet added the Oct 20 2026 release or the STG swap payouts. The coin page carries a warning note, and our figure stands. Label: **supply growing, projected to keep growing**, until the releases end in May 2027.

## Sell pressure: where new ZRO comes from

**Protocol inflation is zero.** LayerZero made all 1B ZRO once, on launch day, Jun 20 2024. ZRO lives on several chains, and when it moves across, the coins burned on one chain are made again on the other, so the total never grows.

**Vesting releases are the big one: 69.98M ZRO in 90 days.** Strategic partners, the early investors, get about **12.7M ZRO** a month, down from 15M after the Foundation bought 50M of their coins back in September 2025. Core contributors, the people who built LayerZero, get about **10.6M ZRO** a month. Together that is close to **23.3M ZRO** on each of Aug 20, Sep 20 and Oct 20 2026. The same amount lands on Nov 20 and Dec 20 2026 and on Jan 20 2027. These coins sit with the holders' own custodians, so a release does not mean a sale. LayerZero said in June 2026 that investors still held 63.8% of everything unlocked to them so far. Still, each release is coins their owners are now free to sell.

**Foundation and unscheduled releases: zero.** The LayerZero Foundation's three main wallets did not move between Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins: zero.** The 40M ZRO once owed to the FTX and Alameda estate went back to strategic partners in a settlement. Those coins now pay out inside the partners' monthly releases, so nothing extra waits in a court.

**STG-to-ZRO swaps: 5.08M ZRO.** When LayerZero bought Stargate in 2025, it promised holders of the old STG token **0.08634 ZRO** for each STG, paid from a Foundation pool. Most of the 5.08M paid out in the window went in the first week of October 2026, when big exchanges swapped their users' STG in one go. Swaps close on Dec 15 2026. At the slower pace seen since mid-October, about **0.70M ZRO** more goes out before then.

## Buy pressure: where new ZRO goes

**Programmatic buyback: 506,608 ZRO.** Stargate, the bridge the LayerZero Foundation owns, has sent all its revenue to buying ZRO since April 2026. The buys land once a month. Purchases on Aug 6, Sep 4 and Oct 6 2026 added 506,608 ZRO, worth about **$677K**, to a Foundation wallet that has never sold a coin. Those dollars are fixed by revenue, not by coin count, so at today's price of about $1.77 the next 90 days buy back closer to **382K ZRO**. The buyback cancels well under 1% of what the releases add.

**Protocol fee burn: zero.** Holders have voted four times on a fee switch that would buy and burn ZRO, the latest in June 2026, and it is still off. Nothing burns ZRO today.

**Foundation buy: zero.** LayerZero Labs bought $10M of ZRO in November 2025, but nothing like it happened between Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026, and there is no schedule for another.

**New long-term lock: zero.** Staking for the new Zero chain has not started, and staked ZRO would still count as circulating anyway.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest pile is the Foundation's **183M ZRO**, which it re-locked until its Zero chain goes live. LayerZero has set Zero's launch for Nov 30 2026, which lifts that lock inside the next 90 days. The Foundation says it will spend these coins slowly to grow the network and plans no new airdrop. They add no supply until coins leave its wallets.

The three main Foundation wallets on Ethereum hold **106.06M**, **69.55M** and **29.0M ZRO**, and all three read exactly the same on Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026. The buyback wallet holds **2.54M ZRO** and the STG swap pool holds **8.45M ZRO**. After the Nov 20 2026 release, six more monthly releases of about 23.3M ZRO each remain for partners and staff, about **140M ZRO** in all, ending in May 2027.

## How ZRO compares to other cross-chain messaging tokens

Cross-chain tokens come in three shapes. Some, like Axelar's AXL, pay their validators in newly made coins every block, so supply grows for as long as the network runs. Others, like Wormhole's W, have a fixed cap and feed locked coins out to early backers over several years, the same shape as ZRO. Chainlink's LINK also has a fixed cap and slowly releases coins it holds back, while its reserve buys LINK with service revenue.

ZRO sits in the fixed-cap group, with no new coins ever made, and it already runs a buyback funded by real revenue. Its weak spot is the pace of releases: for one more half year, monthly releases to partners and staff dwarf the buyback. Once the last release lands in May 2027, ZRO's new supply drops to near zero, and the buyback, plus any ATLAS burn, starts to matter.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 20 2026, Dec 20 2026 and Jan 20 2027:** the three monthly releases, about 23.3M ZRO each. Watch how much reaches exchanges.

**Nov 30 2026:** Zero mainnet and the ATLAS trading engine go live. ATLAS plans to spend 75% of its fees, after rebates to trading venues, on buying and burning ZRO. It also lifts the lock on the Foundation's 183M ZRO, so watch the Foundation wallets for any outflow.

**Dec 15 2026:** STG-to-ZRO swaps close for good. Any last rush adds to the 0.70M ZRO we expect; whatever stays unclaimed in the 8.45M ZRO pool stays with the Foundation.

**Around December 2026:** the fee switch has gone to a vote every six months, so a fifth vote may come. If it passes, protocol fees would buy and burn ZRO.

## Summary

ZRO's supply grew **+18.64%** in the 90 days to Nov 4 2026, and the next 90 days point to **+17.57%**. The growth comes from monthly releases to early partners and staff, 69.98M ZRO in the window, plus 5.08M ZRO paid out for STG swaps. The Stargate buyback, 506,608 ZRO, takes back only a small slice. With releases ending in May 2027 and the Zero chain and ATLAS starting on Nov 30 2026, ZRO's supply story should look very different by the second half of 2027.

Chiliz Chain minted **159.7M CHZ** over the last 90 days and the project's own two allocation wallets released a further **108.6M CHZ** of standing reserve into the market, against just **5.49M CHZ** of buyback-and-burn and fee burn. The framework reads **+2.49% net** on a **10.57B** circulating base; the supply monitor reads **+2.26%**, a gap of **0.22 percentage points**. CHZ is an **uncapped layer-1 whose issuance is genuinely easing** — the per-block mint was cut **16.5%** on **Jun 17 2026** — but the burn that is meant to offset it is roughly two percent of the mint.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window from **Apr 25 2026** to **Jul 24 2026**, the framework reads **Chiliz at +2.49% net inflation**: **268.28M CHZ** of sell pressure against **5.49M CHZ** of buy pressure. The supply monitor reads **+2.26%**, leaving a gap of **0.22 percentage points** — inside tolerance, so no monitor-gap chip ships on the CHZ page. Forward, the reading improves sharply to **+1.30%**, because the annual issuance step-down has already landed and the reserve release was a one-off. Chiliz is best labelled a **decaying-emission layer-1 with a token burn too small to matter yet**: the protocol is doing the right thing to its own issuance curve, while the offsetting mechanism is still an order of magnitude short.

## Sell pressure: where new CHZ comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, booked **159.7M CHZ**, and that figure is a measurement rather than a model. Chiliz Chain publishes its own supply counters, and reading them at both ends of the window shows cumulative issuance moving from **1,525.88M** to **1,685.53M CHZ**. The chain mints a fixed amount of CHZ in every block and resets that amount once a year on a published decay curve that starts at **8.80%** in year one and eases toward a **1.88%** floor by year fourteen. The annual reset landed inside this window: at block 34,977,246 on **Jun 17 2026** the per-block mint dropped from **66.28 CHZ** to **55.33 CHZ**, a **16.5%** cut that puts Chiliz on its year-three rate of **5.53%** a year. Because the change landed mid-window, the forward figure is re-based on the post-cut rate rather than the blended average, giving **143.2M CHZ** for the next 90 days. Sixty-five percent of every block goes to validators and delegators on Chiliz Chain; the remaining thirty-five percent goes to project pools, which is where the second sell row starts.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **0**, and permanently so. The genesis allocation of **8,888,888,888 CHZ** — token sale, pre-sale, userbase reserve, marketing, seed investors, team and advisors — finished vesting in **2022**. There is no cliff, no tranche and no forward unlock calendar for CHZ; the circulating figure and the total supply are now the same number to within a rounding error. Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is the row that carries this window, at **108.6M CHZ**. Chiliz publishes the two addresses that receive the non-validator share of each block: a community vault covering liquidity and restaking, and an ecosystem and operational wallet. Those two wallets held a standing balance of **112.5M CHZ** at the window open and hold **3.9M** today. Twelve months of balance samples show that reserve sitting untouched at 90M to 115M through 2025 and into April 2026, then emptying in early **May 2026**. The coins did not move between project vaults — they went through a relay address that holds nothing into a high-turnover distribution wallet, so they left project custody. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**: Chiliz has no bankruptcy estate, no trustee schedule and no lockup contract, and staked CHZ unbonds in two days after a governance change, which makes it liquid rather than locked.

## Buy pressure: where new CHZ goes

Buy #1, programmatic buyback, booked **4.66M CHZ**. Under the project's 2030 roadmap, ten percent of monthly fan-token revenue buys CHZ on the open market and sends it to a burn address, and that address is readable, so the row is a count rather than an estimate. It held **20.11M CHZ** at the window open and **24.77M** at the close, which is exactly one firing: **4,660,013.33 CHZ** destroyed on **May 26 2026**. The project's own burn report for that month states the identical figure. Lifetime, the programme has burned **24.77M CHZ** across three transfers, on **Mar 9 2026**, **Apr 7 2026** and **May 26 2026**. Nothing has been burned since. The June and July executions had not reached the chain as of **Jul 25 2026**, so the forward figure holds at the amount actually realised rather than assuming three more monthly firings — a distinction that matters, because assuming the schedule holds would have tripled this row.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, booked **0.83M CHZ**. Chiliz Chain implements an EIP-1559 base fee that is destroyed rather than paid to validators, and the chain's own burn counter moved from **5.23M** to **6.06M CHZ** across the window. That is real destruction, and it is also about half a percent of what the chain minted in the same period. Fan-token activity around the summer tournament lifted it through May and June and it has eased since. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0**: there is no separate treasury accumulation programme, and every CHZ the project buys through the revenue programme is burned rather than held. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**: staking on Chiliz Chain holds a large share of supply but the unbonding period is two days, so it takes nothing off the tradable float, and no new lockup was announced in the window.

## Foundation and overhang

Chiliz has exactly two identified team-controlled overhangs, and both are published by the project itself. The first is the community vault covering liquidity pools and restaking rewards, which took **53.1M CHZ** into the window and holds **2.0M** now. The second is the ecosystem and operational distribution wallet, which took **59.4M CHZ** into the window and holds **1.9M** now. Between them they released **108.6M CHZ** net, which is the single largest supply event on the CHZ ledger this quarter and larger than two thirds of the mint itself. Both wallets are read on-chain at each refresh.

There is no third overhang. The buyback executor is a pass-through with a zero balance and three lifetime transactions, all of them burns, so no CHZ accumulates there. There is no DAO treasury separate from those two wallets, no bankruptcy estate, and no unscheduled genesis allocation, because the genesis schedule closed in 2022. The practical consequence is that the forward reading is much cleaner than the trailing one: the standing reserve is spent, so both wallets now function as pass-throughs for the current block mint, which Sell #1 already counts in full. If either wallet's balance falls again between refreshes, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh — but with **3.9M CHZ** left across the pair, the ceiling on the next such event is small until the pools rebuild.

## How CHZ compares to other uncapped proof-of-stake layer-1s

Chiliz belongs to the family of uncapped proof-of-stake layer-1s that fund validators from continuous issuance and try to claw some of it back with a fee burn — the same structural family as Ethereum after EIP-1559, BNB Chain, Avalanche and Tezos. What distinguishes Chiliz inside that family is that its issuance curve is **pre-committed and decaying** rather than adaptive. Ethereum's issuance floats with the amount staked; Tezos recalculates its rate each cycle to chase a staking target. Chiliz fixed the whole schedule in advance — **8.80%**, then **7.20%**, then **5.53%**, easing to **1.88%** from year fourteen — and steps it once a year, which is why the mint visibly dropped **16.5%** on a single date rather than drifting. For a supply metric, pre-commitment is worth something: the next four quarters of CHZ issuance are already knowable to the block.

The comparison that goes against Chiliz is the burn. On a busy Ethereum quarter the base-fee burn can exceed issuance outright and turn net supply negative. On BNB Chain a quarterly auto-burn removes a fixed, large share of supply on a published schedule. On Chiliz the base-fee burn is **0.83M CHZ** against **159.7M** minted, and the revenue buyback adds **4.66M** — together about **2%** of issuance. The mechanism exists, it is real and it is verifiable on-chain, but it is not yet the counterweight the marketing implies. That is a usage problem rather than a design problem: the buyback scales with fan-token revenue, so it grows only if Socios activity grows.

The third contrast is custody. A capped proof-of-work chain has no treasury and therefore no discretionary supply. Chiliz routes thirty-five percent of every block into project-controlled wallets, which means a share of issuance passes through a decision-maker before it reaches the market. This quarter that mattered enormously — the reserve those wallets had accumulated over a year came out in a few weeks and doubled the effective sell side. An investor reading only the headline issuance rate would have seen **5.53%** a year and missed it entirely.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, whether the buyback-and-burn resumes. The last burn was **May 26 2026** and two monthly executions are now outstanding; a catch-up burn covering the tournament quarter would be the single most positive supply event available to CHZ, and it should appear at the burn address before it appears in a report. Second, the two allocation wallets. They hold **3.9M CHZ** between them, so the near-term downside is capped, but a rebuild followed by another release is the pattern this window just demonstrated. Third, fan-token revenue after the tournament. The buyback is ten percent of it, so a post-tournament drop feeds straight into a smaller burn. Fourth, the next issuance step, which is not due until **Jun 17 2027** — nothing in this window changes the mint rate, so **55.33 CHZ** per block is the number to hold constant. Fifth, governance: Chiliz has amended its inflation schedule by hard fork before, and the last supply-affecting proposal was in **Feb 2026**, so a new one would be the only thing that could move Sell #1 off its published path.

## Summary

Chiliz is an uncapped sports-and-fan-token layer-1 whose issuance is on a published, decaying schedule, and the framework reads it at **+2.49% net** over 90 days — **159.7M CHZ** minted plus a **108.6M CHZ** one-off release from the project's two allocation wallets, against **5.49M CHZ** of buyback and fee burn. The supply monitor agrees at **+2.26%**. Forward the reading eases to **+1.30%**, because the per-block mint was cut **16.5%** on **Jun 17 2026** and the reserve that drove the rest is now spent. The structural mechanism is sound and unusually predictable; the key risk is that the offsetting burn is roughly two percent of issuance and has not fired since **May 26 2026**, so CHZ has no realistic path to flat supply until fan-token revenue is many times larger.

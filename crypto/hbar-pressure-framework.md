# HBAR Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

HBAR, the gas coin of the Hedera network, is never minted and never burned — all **50B HBAR** were created at launch, so HBAR supply grows only when the Hedera Governing Council moves coins out of its reserve accounts. Over the last 90 days the Council released **458.33M HBAR**: **441.67M** in Council releases, four of them large lumps, plus a **16.66M** quarterly coin-plan payout. Nothing on the buy side removed a single HBAR, so the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads HBAR at **+1.05% net** against a supply-monitor reading of **+1.19%** — a gap of **0.15 percentage points**, which is agreement. The Council still holds **6.17B HBAR** in reserve, and the pace it chooses is the whole of the HBAR supply story.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Sep 13 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **HBAR at +1.05% net**: **458.33M HBAR** reached wallets outside the Hedera Council's control and **zero** HBAR was bought back, burned or locked. The independent supply monitor reads the realised 90-day change at **+1.19%**. The gap is **0.15 percentage points**, inside the framework's half-point tolerance, so HBAR ships with **no data-conflict flag**. The forward column reads **+0.51%**, lower than the trailing window, because the trailing window happened to catch two 150M transfers on a single day, while the forward figure carries only a dated coin-plan payout and the Council's average operations release across its last four quarters. The label for HBAR is **inflationary by discretionary reserve release**: a fixed-genesis chain whose float grows on the Council's timetable, not on a protocol emission curve.

## Sell pressure: where new HBAR comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**. Hedera has no block reward and no mint; every HBAR was issued at launch into Council treasury accounts. HBAR staking rewards are real, but they are paid out of a staking reward account that was funded in advance and is topped up by network fees, and that account fell from **187.38M HBAR** to **139.81M HBAR** across the window — the rewards spend HBAR that already exists rather than creating new HBAR. The 50B HBAR total is Council policy rather than a line of code: changing it would need a unanimous vote of the Hedera Governing Council, so the framework tracks it rather than calling it permanent.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **16.66M HBAR**. The coin plan that pays founders, executives and staff still releases HBAR once a quarter from a Council account. The last payment left on **Jun 30 2026**, when **16.64M HBAR** was split across about a dozen wallets, followed by a small top-up on **Aug 28 2026**. The early HBAR investor sales under purchase agreements have finished paying out, leaving no investor vesting cliff of any size.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **441.67M HBAR** and is the largest line on the HBAR page. Every one of the **548** accounts that hold unreleased HBAR was read at both ends of the window, and every transfer touching them was pulled; the transfers match each account's balance change exactly, and the combined HBAR reserve fell from **6.63B** to **6.17B**. The Council moved **100M HBAR** out on **Jun 15 2026**, two separate **150M HBAR** transfers on **Jul 2 2026** and **40M HBAR** on **Aug 15 2026**, plus quarterly board pay; moves between the Council's own accounts were netted out, and no release of any size has left since mid-August. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy supply, is **zero**: no estate or trustee holds HBAR, and every unreleased coin sits in the Council reserve counted above.

## Buy pressure: where new HBAR goes

Nowhere, and that is the other half of the HBAR story. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero**: neither Hedera network fees nor Council funds buy HBAR on the market. Buy #2, the protocol fee burn, is **zero** because Hedera does not burn fees at all. The network charges roughly **5,100 HBAR** a day and pays it out to node operators, the staking reward account and a fee account, so every HBAR spent on gas stays in circulation. Buy #3, foundation buying, is **zero**: across all 548 reserve accounts only **2,425 HBAR** flowed back in over ninety days, small returns rather than purchases. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is **zero**: about **11.44B HBAR** is staked to Hedera nodes, but HBAR staking has no bonding period, no unstaking delay and no slashing, so a staked HBAR can be sold the same minute and none of it is held off the market.

## Foundation and overhang

The HBAR overhang is the Council reserve itself: **6.17B HBAR** across 548 accounts, about **14%** of circulating HBAR. It is on-chain and readable, so it is re-read on every refresh rather than taken from a report. The Council publishes a quarterly treasury report, and its guidance for this quarter still lists roughly **3.52B HBAR** for the ecosystem development program that has not moved. Guidance of that size has been published before and has mostly not been drawn — earlier quarters forecast several billion HBAR and released a few hundred million — so the framework treats that line as capacity and does not book it. A staff-pay relay account and the network fee account sit outside the reserve list and were checked too; both passed coins straight through and hold no reserve. If the Council reserve balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How HBAR compares to other foundation-released layer-1 chains

HBAR belongs to the group of layer-1 chains that issued their whole supply at genesis and hand it out over years, rather than minting new coins every block. Against an uncapped proof-of-stake layer-1, the difference is where HBAR supply growth comes from: a staking chain adds new coins every epoch on a published curve that anyone can project, while HBAR adds none from the protocol and instead grows by release decisions. That makes HBAR supply less predictable quarter to quarter — the trailing window here contains two 150M transfers on one day — but it also means the growth can slow or stop without a protocol upgrade.

Against burn-driven chains, HBAR has nothing pushing the other way. A chain with a fee burn or a funded buyback offsets part of its new supply; Hedera redistributes every fee, and its fee economy is small — about **5,100 HBAR** a day, roughly **0.004%** of the HBAR market capitalisation a year — so even a burn would barely register. Against other foundation-release chains, the distinguishing feature is transparency of the reserve: all unreleased HBAR sits in a published list of accounts that anyone can read, so the framework measures realised release directly rather than trusting an unlock calendar.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the next quarterly coin-plan payout, due around **Oct 1 2026** and booked at **16.66M HBAR**. Second, the Council operations release: the forward column carries the four-quarter average of **206.19M HBAR**, and a quarter above roughly **420M HBAR** would push the forward net above one percent. Third, the undrawn **3.52B HBAR** of ecosystem guidance — if even part of it moves before quarter end on **Sep 30 2026** or in the next report, it would dwarf every other line. Fourth, the Council's next treasury report and meeting minutes, which set fourth-quarter guidance. Fifth, the staking reward account at **139.81M HBAR**: it is shrinking, and a top-up from the Council reserve would count as a new release.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads HBAR at **+1.05% net** over the trailing 90 days and **+0.51%** over the next 90, with **458.33M HBAR** released and nothing removed. The structural mechanism is a fixed-genesis layer-1 with no mint, no fee burn and no staking lock, whose HBAR float grows only as the Hedera Governing Council releases reserve coins. The key risk is that the release pace is a Council decision, not a protocol rule, and published guidance for ecosystem grants runs far above what has actually moved. The ceiling is the **6.17B HBAR** still held in Council reserve accounts — the most HBAR supply can ever grow from here under the current 50B total.

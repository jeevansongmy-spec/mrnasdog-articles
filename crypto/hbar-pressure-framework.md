# HBAR Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

HBAR supply is growing. Hedera never makes new coins, because all **50B HBAR** were created at launch, but the Hedera Council keeps moving coins out of its reserve and into the market, and the total can never pass 50B. From Aug 3 to Nov 1 2026 the Council released **536.06M HBAR**, nothing was burned or bought back, and circulating supply rose **+1.21%**, with about **+1.26%** expected in the next 90 days. Other trackers show **+1.22%**, the same picture.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 1 2026, HBAR in circulation grew from about **43.79B** to **44.33B**, a net rise of **+1.21%**. Other supply trackers put the same window at **+1.22%**, a gap of less than two hundredths of a point, so the HBAR coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days we expect about **556.39M HBAR** to come out, or **+1.26%**. Our label is plain: supply growing, and projected to keep growing. The growth does not come from a code rule. It comes from decisions by the Council, which can speed up or slow down from one quarter to the next.

## Sell pressure: where new HBAR comes from

**1. Protocol inflation — 0.** Hedera has no block reward and no mint, so no new HBAR is ever created. Stakers are paid from a reward pot that already counts as circulating; its balance fell about **46M HBAR** in the window, but that only moves coins between holders.

**2. Vesting unlocks — 16.06M.** Hedera staff are paid coin grants under the 2018 Coin Plan, and the payouts go out at the end of each quarter. The Sep 30 2026 payout sent **16.06M HBAR** to staff wallets, the same amount the Council reported for that quarter. The Council's plan for the last quarter of 2026 lists about **36M HBAR** for the next payout, near Dec 31 2026, plus **0.39M** under old purchase agreements.

**3. Foundation + unscheduled unlocks — 520.00M.** This is the big one. The Council released **150M HBAR** on Oct 1 2026, which its plan describes as operating reserves, and another **300M HBAR** on Oct 23 2026. One account received **40M** on Aug 15 and **28M** on Oct 15 2026, and small monthly payouts added about **2M**. Together that is **520.00M HBAR**. Over the past year, releases like these came in every quarter, sometimes 300M at a time, so we expect roughly the same amount in the next 90 days.

**4. Long-term locked or bankruptcy — 0.** No court estate or bankruptcy pile of HBAR is waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new HBAR goes

**1. Programmatic buyback — 0.** Hedera has no buyback. Nothing uses network income to buy HBAR off the market.

**2. Protocol fee burn — 0.** Hedera burns no fees. Every fee is paid to node operators, the staking reward pot and a network account, and all of those coins stay in circulation.

**3. Foundation buy — 0.** The Council and the groups it funds sell or spend HBAR; they do not buy it back. Spot HBAR funds buy coins that already circulate, so they take nothing out of the count.

**4. New long-term lock — 0.** Staking on Hedera leaves the coins in the owner's wallet, free to move at any time, so staked HBAR still counts as circulating. Nothing is locked away.

## Foundation and overhang

The one large holder that matters is the Hedera Council reserve itself. On Nov 1 2026 it still held **5.67B HBAR**, about 11% of all HBAR, spread across a set of reserve accounts. Every coin the reserve lets go adds to circulating supply, and there is no fixed release schedule beyond the Council's one-quarter plan. That plan, with data as of Oct 6 2026, lists up to **3.8B HBAR** for ecosystem funding in the last quarter of 2026. Plans of that size have been printed for four quarters in a row, while the amounts that really went out were far smaller, between about 186M and 527M HBAR a quarter. So we do not count the 3.8B, but it is the number to watch: if the Council really sent it out, HBAR supply would jump by nearly 9% in one quarter.

The staking reward pot is the second pool to watch. It held about **128.54M HBAR** on Nov 1 2026 and pays out about half a million HBAR a day. Those coins already circulate, but if the pot ran low and the Council topped it up from the reserve, that top-up would count as new supply.

## How HBAR compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake chains grow their supply by paying validators in freshly made coins, and many claw some of it back with a fee burn. Hedera works differently. Its supply is capped at 50B and already fully created, so the growth comes from one place only: a governing body choosing to release coins it holds in reserve. That makes HBAR's supply path less like a chain's emission curve and more like a treasury drawing down its savings.

The upside is a hard ceiling. Once the reserve is empty, circulating supply stops growing for good, and Hedera reached about 89% released on Nov 1 2026. The downside is that the pace is a choice, not a fixed rule. A chain with fixed validator pay lets holders plan a year ahead; on Hedera, one quarter can bring 13M HBAR and another 4B. And because Hedera burns no fees at all, nothing on the other side of the ledger softens those releases.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Around Dec 31 2026 — staff coin payout.** The Council's plan puts about **36M HBAR** into the quarter-end Coin Plan payout, more than twice the Sep 30 2026 amount.

**Early January 2027 — the next Council table.** The Council publishes actual releases for the last quarter of 2026 and a new plan for the first quarter of 2027. The test is whether the 3.8B ecosystem line finally goes out or slips again.

**Any large reserve move.** Releases of 300M HBAR landed on Dec 20 2025, Mar 5, Jul 3 and Oct 23 2026, about three to four months apart. Another one before the end of January 2027 would keep supply on the path we expect; a quiet quarter would bring the next 90 days in lower.

**Nov 4 2026 — test network upgrade.** Hedera upgrades its test network to version 0.79 after the main network moved to 0.78.2 on Oct 28 2026. Neither upgrade changes how many HBAR exist.

## Summary

HBAR supply grew **+1.21%** in the 90 days to Nov 1 2026, all of it from Hedera Council releases, and we expect about **+1.26%** in the next 90 days.

Hedera mints nothing, burns nothing and buys nothing back, so the Council's choices are the whole story of HBAR supply.

The Council reserve still holds **5.67B HBAR**, and its own plan lists up to 3.8B for the last quarter of 2026, far more than it has really released in any recent quarter.

Other trackers agree with our reading within two hundredths of a point, so the HBAR coin page shows no warning.

DoubleZero has never minted a single 2Z — **10B** were created at the October 2025 launch and the Solana mint has issued nothing since — yet the Pressure Framework reads **+1.19%** net supply to market over the last 90 days, because **41.6M 2Z** left DoubleZero treasury wallets and **40.5M** of it was traced onto an exchange. Our supply monitor reads **−0.01%**, a gap of **1.20 percentage points**, and that gap is the story: DoubleZero's own tokenomics disclosure classifies unmoved Foundation 2Z as already circulating, so a treasury release moves the float without moving the count. Ahead of all of it sits **Oct 2 2026**, when the one-year vesting cliff frees roughly **1.63B 2Z** — about **47%** of everything trading — under a lockup that no on-chain contract actually enforces.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 19 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **2Z at +1.19% net**. Sell pressure is **41.6M 2Z**, buy pressure is **0.259M 2Z**, and the circulating base is **3.47B 2Z**. Our supply monitor reads **−0.01%** — a gap of **1.20 percentage points**, well past the half-point tolerance, so a data-conflict chip ships on the overview page. The deep walk located the cause rather than guessing at it. DoubleZero's tokenomics disclosure of **Sep 26 2025** states plainly that Foundation and Ecosystem tokens are unlocked from day one and that trackers disagree over whether unmoved Foundation holdings count as circulating; the wider market counts them as circulating, so when the DoubleZero treasury sends 2Z to an exchange the classified supply does not change at all, while the tradable float genuinely does. The framework is measuring the flow; the monitor is measuring the classification. 2Z is best characterised as **a zero-issuance token with an entirely discretionary float** — nothing is minted, but almost everything that matters is a decision rather than a rule.

## Sell pressure: where new 2Z comes from

Not from issuance. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**. The entire 2Z supply — **10,000,000,000** tokens — was minted at the DoubleZero launch on Oct 2 2025, and the Solana mint's live supply now reads **9,998,158,871**, which is **1,841,129** tokens BELOW where it started. We walked the complete transaction history of the account that holds mint authority over 2Z — 291 transactions stretching back to September 2024, inner instructions included — and it contains no mint instruction on this token at any point in its life. But the wording here is deliberate: that authority is a live multisig, and it has never been renounced. The freeze authority was given up in **August 2025**; the mint authority was not. Zero-so-far and zero-forever are different claims about 2Z, and only the first one is evidenced.

The cliff lives in Sell #2, vesting unlocks. Across the trailing 90 days it is **zero**, and the proof is arithmetic rather than impression: DoubleZero's classified circulating figure is the launch-unlocked total minus everything ever burned, and it declined every week of the window without a single upward step. A vesting release would have produced one. DoubleZero's tokenomics disclosure puts **6.5B 2Z** on a "Standard Lockup" across Jump Crypto, Malbec Labs, Institutions, Team, Contributors and Builders, described as a gradual unlock over four years from launch with nothing released in year one. The one-year mark is **Oct 2 2026** and the first tranche is a quarter of that pool: **1,625M 2Z**. Three independent angles agree — the figure derived from DoubleZero's own allocation table, an unlock aggregator publishing **1.62B** across six rounds for that exact date, and a calendar aggregator publishing **1.66B**, which is the same cliff plus the 30M validator remainder.

One finding on that lockup deserves its own sentence, because it inverts the usual reading. There is no escrow. Every large 2Z allocation account we read this session is an ordinary SPL token account — initialized, no delegate, no close authority — and the mint's freeze authority is renounced, so nothing on the Solana chain would prevent any of those wallets from transferring today. The DoubleZero lockup is contractual, not cryptographic. That means the framework's "released beats scheduled" escrow rule has nothing to read against, so the published vesting schedule governs the forward window; it also means the Oct 2 2026 date is a promise being kept, not a vault opening.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **41.6M 2Z** — and it very nearly hid. The wallet the tokens moved through holds nothing today and held nothing on May 21 2026, yet **41,614,752.69 2Z** passed straight through it during the window: in from the DoubleZero treasury (30.0M), two genesis distribution wallets (4.0M and 6.0M) and a launch-era distributor (1.61M), and out again the same day on **May 21**, **May 22**, **Jun 8**, **Jul 17**, **Aug 5** and **Aug 12 2026** — that last date alone in four separate 5.0M tranches. Following it two hops further, **40.5M** landed in an exchange wallet inside the same window. On the funding side, the DoubleZero treasury's own balance fell **55.0M**, of which the 30.0M went down this path and 25.0M went to an unlabelled wallet on Jul 17 2026 where it still sits untouched — a resting position, so it is tracked as overhang rather than booked. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero**: DoubleZero launched in October 2025 as a going concern.

## Buy pressure: where new 2Z goes

Almost nowhere, and the reason is structural rather than accidental. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero**. DoubleZero does run a buyback: protocol revenue converts to 2Z on the open market, a tenth is burned and nine tenths is paid out to the fiber contributors who supply the network. Read as supply, that mechanism nets out — the buy removes 2Z from the float and the contributor payout returns it — so the only part that changes supply is the tenth that is destroyed, and that tenth is already inside the measured burn row below. Booking it twice would inflate the buy side with tokens that never left circulation. Size makes the point moot in any case: the 5% validator seat fee that funded the DoubleZero buyback at scale was removed at **epoch 939** in **April 2026**, that revenue line has read zero every single day since May 2026, and the subscription product replacing it booked about **$30K** across the entire 90-day window. This is the pivot the whole coin turns on — an announced buyback is not a realised flow.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is the one buy row that is not empty, at **0.259M 2Z**, and this quarter it is measured rather than estimated. Because DoubleZero's circulating figure is the launch-unlocked total minus cumulative burning, its change across the window IS the burn: **3,469,835,660** on **May 21 2026** falling to **3,469,576,371** on **Aug 19 2026**, so **259,289 2Z** were destroyed. Integrity burning is real — it is how DoubleZero discourages contributors from manufacturing traffic to inflate their reward share — but at this rate it is roughly **0.0075%** of the float per quarter, and it ran at about half the token's lifetime average, which fits a burn fed by revenue that collapsed after the April 2026 fee change. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **zero**: no DoubleZero entity has disclosed an open-market purchase, and every identified team wallet was a net sender this quarter. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero**: staking 2Z is described in the token disclosure as a future capability.

## Foundation and overhang

The team-controlled overhang is the dominant feature of 2Z's supply, and it is unusually legible. First, the launch lockups: **6.5B 2Z** across the Jump Crypto, Malbec Labs, Institutions, Team, Contributors and Builders buckets, held in ordinary wallets under contract, with the first release dated **Oct 2 2026** and the schedule running to 2029. Second, the DoubleZero treasury wallet that funds distributions, holding **1.24B 2Z** after sending 55.0M out this quarter. Third, a genesis wallet holding **1.75B 2Z** that has exactly one transaction in its entire lifetime. Fourth, the **25.0M** that left the treasury on **Jul 17 2026** for an unlabelled wallet and has sat still ever since. Fifth, the pass-through relay and the operations wallet behind it, which hold small residuals but carried the whole quarter's flow. Sixth, the validator remainder of about **30M 2Z**, contractually free since around **May 2026** and still unmoved.

All six are readable on-chain and re-read on every refresh, and the trigger condition is the same for each: if any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh. That is precisely how this quarter's **41.6M 2Z** was found — and it is worth noting that reading balances alone would have missed it entirely, because the wallet that carried the flow was empty at both ends of the window. Only the transfer log showed it.

## How 2Z compares to other DePIN and cliff-vesting tokens

Against an uncapped continuous-emission Layer-1 such as Solana, the chain 2Z is issued on, DoubleZero looks inert. Solana mints new SOL every epoch on a published, decaying inflation curve, so its supply grows smoothly and a holder can price that dilution to the day. DoubleZero mints nothing; its supply has contracted slightly since launch. The dilution risk is not in the issuance schedule — it is in the vesting calendar and in treasury discretion, and both arrive in lumps rather than in a drip.

Against other DePIN networks, the contrast is in where contributor pay comes from. Helium and Filecoin mint new tokens to pay their hardware providers, so their supply expands as the network grows. DoubleZero chose the opposite structure: contributors are paid from 2Z bought on the open market with real revenue, with a slice burned — a design that makes usage deflationary rather than inflationary. That is the more elegant model, and it is exactly why removing the validator seat fee in April 2026 matters so much. The DoubleZero buy engine is not broken; it is unfunded, waiting on subscription revenue that is currently running about two orders of magnitude below what the old fee produced.

And against the exchange tokens and Layer-2s that hold their unvested supply inside audited lock contracts, DoubleZero is the softer case. A token whose lockup is enforced in code cannot release early even if everyone involved wanted it to; a token whose lockup is contractual can. Nothing suggests DoubleZero intends to — the wallets have been static for the better part of two years — but the framework grades structure, not intention, and 2Z's structure places **6.53B** tokens behind a promise rather than behind a program.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 2 2026** is the date that decides the quarter: the one-year cliff frees roughly **1.63B 2Z**, about **47%** of the current float, in a single day. Watch whether the monthly drip that should follow it — approximately **135M 2Z** a month as the remaining 75% vests over three years — is given a stated monthly date; no source we could read this session gives one, so it is deliberately excluded from the forward ledger and would push the reading higher if confirmed. Watch the DoubleZero treasury wallet's distribution rhythm, which accelerated sharply to 20.0M in a single day on **Aug 12 2026** after months of 5.0M tranches. Watch the **25.0M** resting in the unlabelled wallet since **Jul 17 2026** — the moment it moves, it becomes sell pressure. And watch DoubleZero Edge subscription revenue: the buy side stays empty until it scales far enough for the ten-percent burn to register against a 3.47B float.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads DoubleZero (2Z) at **+1.19%** net supply to market over the 90 days to **Aug 19 2026**, and projects **+48.03%** for the next 90 days. The structural mechanism is unusual and worth stating precisely: 2Z has no protocol inflation at all — the Solana mint has never issued a coin beyond the 10B genesis supply, and the token has burned **1,841,129** 2Z net over its life — so every unit of pressure comes from distribution rather than issuance, and 2Z's supply risk is entirely a matter of who chooses to move what and when. The key risk is the **Oct 2 2026** unlock of roughly **1.63B 2Z** landing against a buyback that lost its funding in April 2026, into a float of **3.47B**. The ceiling is genuine — 10B 2Z is the maximum that will ever exist and the supply has only ever fallen — but the floor is discretionary: **6.53B** of it is held back by contract, not by code.

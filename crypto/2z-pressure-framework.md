DoubleZero is the rare token whose supply has genuinely stood still. **10B 2Z** were minted at launch, not one coin has been added since, and a locked bucket of **6.53B** has not moved on-chain in ten months. The last 90 days show only **21.6M 2Z** of team-wallet distribution reaching the market, a net of **+0.61%** against a monitor reading of **+0.14%** — a **0.47 percentage point** gap, inside tolerance. What makes 2Z a supply story is a single date: on **Oct 2 2026** the one-year cliff frees roughly **1.63B 2Z** at once, about **47%** of everything trading, and that date now sits inside the forward window.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 11 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **2Z at +0.61% net**. Sell pressure is **21.6M 2Z**, buy pressure is **0.5M 2Z**, and the circulating base is **3.47B 2Z**. Our supply monitor reads **+0.14%**, a gap of **0.47 percentage points** — below the half-point threshold, so no data-conflict chip ships, and this is the first clean end-check the coin has produced. The direction of that small residual is expected: the tokens the framework watches leaving DoubleZero's own wallets were already treated as circulating by the wider market, and DoubleZero's tokenomics disclosure itself notes that unmoved but unlocked Foundation supply is counted differently by different trackers. The forward read is a different animal: **+47.25%**, driven entirely by the Oct 2 2026 unlock. 2Z is best characterised as **structurally frozen with a single dated cliff**.

## Sell pressure: where new 2Z comes from

Not from issuance. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and unusually so: the entire 2Z supply — **10,000,000,000** tokens — was minted at the October 2025 launch, and the chain's live supply counter now reads **9,998,183,721**, which is **1.82M** tokens *below* where it started. DoubleZero's inflation design bounds new minting by how much has already been burned for integrity, so issuance cannot outrun destruction — and to date it has not started at all. The mint authority is a multisig, and no mint instruction appears in its history across the window.

The cliff lives in Sell #2, vesting unlocks. Across the trailing 90 days it is **zero** — a scan of the sixty largest 2Z accounts finds the launch lockups completely static, with the single largest, holding **1.75B 2Z**, showing one transaction in its entire lifetime. DoubleZero's tokenomics disclosure puts **6.5B 2Z** on a "Standard Lockup" — Jump Crypto, Malbec Labs, Institutions, Team, Contributors and Builders — a gradual unlock over four years from launch with nothing released in year one. The one-year mark is **Oct 2 2026**, and the first tranche is a quarter of that pool. That quantum was derived rather than copied: the six locked allocations sum to **6,500M**, and 25% of that is **1,625.0M**. Public unlock trackers publish **1.66B** for the same date, within **2.2%**, so the derived number ships. Because the lockups are contractual and held in ordinary multisigs rather than a readable on-chain escrow, there is no released-versus-scheduled divergence to measure: the published schedule governs the forward window.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **21.6M 2Z** — the only supply that actually moved. Three team multisigs fed a single distribution wallet, which forwarded 2Z to one exchange wallet in five dated payments: **May 21**, **May 22**, **Jun 8**, **Jul 17** and **Aug 5 2026**. The funding side agrees within **7%**, and the recent rhythm is a clean **5.0M** a month, so **15.0M** is projected forward. Crucially, a separate **25.0M** that left the Foundation multisig on Jul 17 2026 was *not* counted: it moved to another team wallet, where it still sits unmoved, so it is an internal transfer rather than market supply. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero**: DoubleZero launched in October 2025 as a going concern, with no estate, trustee schedule or court-ordered 2Z distribution anywhere in the picture.

## Buy pressure: where new 2Z goes

Almost nowhere, and that is the sharpest fact on the page. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero** — but it was not always. DoubleZero ran a genuine buyback-and-distribute: a **5%** cut of validator block rewards, worth roughly **$3.5M** annualised across more than 450 connected validators, was converted to 2Z on the open market, with a tenth burned and the rest paid to fiber contributors. That fee was removed in **April 2026**, before this window opened. Its replacement is a subscription product selling low-latency market data over the same fiber, and at the last public read it was generating about **$205K** annualised — under 1M 2Z of market buying a quarter. A single figure that small does not clear the framework's cross-checking bar, so the row ships at zero with the mechanism disclosed.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is the one buy row that is not empty — at **0.5M 2Z**. Integrity burning is real: it is how DoubleZero discourages contributors from manufacturing traffic to inflate their reward share, and it shows up directly in the supply counter, which has fallen **1.82M 2Z** below the genesis mint across the token's ten-month life. That works out at roughly **0.5M** destroyed every 90 days — a running rate rather than a measured quarter, because the chain publishes a cumulative total — and about **0.05%** of the float. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **zero**: no DoubleZero entity has disclosed an open-market purchase, and the identified team wallets are net senders. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero**: staking 2Z is a future capability in the token disclosure, and none of the largest accounts is a lock contract taking deposits. With the buy side amounting to half a million tokens a quarter, nothing meaningful offsets the Oct 2 2026 cliff.

## Foundation and overhang

The team-controlled overhang is the dominant feature of 2Z's supply, and it sits in four readable pools. The launch lockups: **6.5B 2Z** across the Jump Crypto, Malbec Labs, Institutions, Team, Contributors and Builders buckets, all on the four-year Standard Lockup, all untouched on-chain, first release **Oct 2 2026** and the schedule running to 2029. The Foundation and Ecosystem treasury: the main multisig still holds **1.26B 2Z** from an allocation that is unlocked from day one, and it is the wallet funding the monthly distributions counted in Sell #3. A second team wallet holding the **25.0M 2Z** it received on **Jul 17 2026** and has not moved since. And the validator remainder of about **30M 2Z**, contractually unlocked around **May 2026** and still unmoved.

All four are re-read on every refresh, and the trigger is the same for each: if a balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3. That is precisely how the **21.6M 2Z** in this build was found — the Foundation multisig sent **35.0M** out during the window, and the framework counted only the **21.6M** that actually reached an exchange rather than the portion that moved between team wallets.

## How 2Z compares to other DePIN and cliff-vesting tokens

Against an uncapped continuous-emission Layer-1 such as Solana, on whose chain 2Z is issued, DoubleZero looks almost inert. Solana mints new SOL every epoch on a published, decaying curve, so a holder can price that dilution to the day. DoubleZero mints nothing at all. The dilution risk is not in the issuance schedule — it is in the vesting calendar, and vesting risk arrives in lumps rather than in a drip.

Against other DePIN networks, the contrast is in where the buy side comes from. Helium and Filecoin both mint new tokens to pay their hardware contributors, so their supply expands as the network grows. DoubleZero chose the opposite structure: contributors are paid from tokens bought on the open market with real fee revenue, with a slice burned — usage is genuinely deflationary rather than inflationary. That is the more elegant design, and it is why the removal of the validator fee in April 2026 matters so much. The buy engine is not broken; it is unfunded.

The closest structural analogue is any young token with a twelve-month insider cliff. What makes 2Z an unusually pure example is the ratio: **1.63B 2Z** against a float of **3.47B**, a single day that can add roughly half again to everything currently trading, into a market where no buyback, no burn and no staking lock is absorbing anything. A fixed 10B cap describes the ceiling, not the direction, and 2Z's direction over the next 90 days is up.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the cliff itself: **Oct 2 2026**, roughly **1.63B 2Z** becoming transferable. The number to watch is not the unlock but the movement — these are contractual lockups in ordinary multisigs, so the tokens become free without any on-chain event and the real signal is whether those wallets start sending. Second, the monthly team distribution: **5.0M 2Z** in each of the last two firings, with the next ones due in early September, October and November 2026; a step up in size moves Sell #3 directly. Third, the subscription revenue: every dollar becomes a 2Z market buy with a tenth burned, so the first quarter it is large enough to measure is the first quarter Buy #1 leaves zero. Fourth, 2Z staking: a live lock contract taking deposits would be the first genuine Buy #4 this asset has ever had. Fifth, any change to the vesting calendar itself — no governance action affecting the schedule has been announced.

On **Oct 2 2026** roughly **1.63B 2Z** — about **47%** of the current float — unlocks in a single day, while the buyback that would absorb it lost its funding in April 2026 and its replacement earns roughly **$205K** a year.

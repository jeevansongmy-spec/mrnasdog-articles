# GRASS Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Grass mints nothing. The entire **1B** GRASS supply was created at launch on Solana, the token still reads **999.99M** and it has only ever fallen, by dust — yet the tradable float keeps growing, because vesting hands existing GRASS to the people who were promised it. Over the 90 days to **Aug 18 2026** the MrNasdog Pressure Framework measures **45.4M GRASS** actually leaving team custody: **32.05M** from the two monthly vesting rails and one ad-hoc **13.33M** multisig transfer on **Jun 10 2026**. The buy ledger is **zero** on all four rows — no buyback, no burn, no new lock — for a net of **+6.93%** on a **654.6M** circulating supply, and **+4.90%** projected for the next 90 days. That is materially below the **+11.50%** our supply monitor reads, and the reason is the single most important fact about Grass vesting: a large part of what unlocks on paper never leaves its vault.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 18 2026** the framework reads **GRASS at +6.93% net**, built from Sell #2 vesting unlocks of **32.05M** and a Sell #3 foundation transfer of **13.33M**, against a buy ledger of **zero**. Our supply monitor reads **+11.50%**, so the gap is **4.57 percentage points** and a **⚠ monitor gap chip ships** on the GRASS overview. The deep walk found the cause and it is mechanical, not an error on either side: the three GRASS lock vaults released exactly **1,948,333.333** each on **May 28 2026**, **Jun 28 2026** and **Jul 28 2026**, and the Grass multisig funded its payout wallet with **4,838,295.42** on **May 25 2026**, **Jun 23 2026** and **Jul 27 2026** — about **10.68M** a month of real custody change, against roughly **22.45M** a month of entitlement booked upstream. The framework's label for GRASS is precise and unusual: **a fixed-supply token that inflates by release, not by minting** — and the release running at roughly half the published calendar is the whole story.

## Sell pressure: where new GRASS comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0** for Grass and it is 0 for a structural reason: Grass is not a chain with block rewards, it is a DePIN network that pays people for unused bandwidth and resells the resulting web data to AI buyers. The GRASS mint on Solana has never issued a token beyond the launch supply — the on-chain read is **999,993,121.924** against a **1B** ceiling, and it drifts down, not up. The Grass Foundation confirmed the direction of travel in its **Jul 7 2026** holder call: Stage 2 participant rewards are funded by network revenue, paid in dollars, with zero net new emissions. One caveat keeps this row honest rather than permanent — the GRASS mint authority has **not** been renounced, so the capacity to mint still exists even though it has never been used.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is where all the routine GRASS pressure lives, and it runs on two separate rails. The first is three lock vaults, each funded with **70,000,000 GRASS** by the Grass multisig in 2024, each paying out exactly **1,948,333.333** on the 28th of every month; across the window that is three firings and **17.535M GRASS** released. Each vault still holds **13,638,333.335**, which is precisely seven more monthly payments, ending **Feb 28 2027**. The second rail runs through the Grass multisig itself, which funds a payout wallet with exactly **4,838,295.42** every month before that wallet fans the GRASS out to roughly 150 individual beneficiaries and drains back to a resting balance of about **115,000**. That resting balance is a trap worth naming: read the payout wallet at both ends of the window and it looks untouched, when in fact it carried **14.515M GRASS** to holders. Both rails together give Sell #2 of **32.05M** for the last 90 days and the same **32.05M** for the next 90, since the firing pattern is unchanged.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, carries **13.33M** — a single transfer of **13,333,332.333 GRASS** that left the Grass multisig on **Jun 10 2026** and landed in a wallet with three signatures in its entire life, which has not moved the GRASS since. It sits on neither vesting rail, so booking it does not double-count the calendar. For the next 90 days this row reads **0**: the ad-hoc grants out of the Grass multisig have been sporadic in size — **1.8M** on **Feb 26 2026**, about **6.08M** on **Apr 23 2026**, **13.33M** on **Jun 10 2026** — with no published schedule to project from. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0** and always has been: Grass launched in 2024 with its whole supply created at once, so there is no estate, no trustee and no distressed block queued behind the vesting calendar.

## Buy pressure: where new GRASS goes

Every buy row for GRASS reads **0**, and that is the harshest fact on this page. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0** because no buyback contract exists — the Grass Foundation's **Jul 7 2026** holder call laid out revenue, staking and the Stage 2 reward mechanism without committing to any GRASS purchase programme, and no GRASS arrived in any identified project wallet across the window. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**: Grass has no burn mechanism, and lifetime destruction on the GRASS mint amounts to about **6,878** tokens of dust, none of it inside this window. Destruction was checked in both directions — by event and by the mint's own supply reading — and the answer is the same.

Buy #3, foundation buy, is **0** for the plainest possible reason: the Grass multisig has only ever paid out. Its balance fell from **287,387,620.556** at the start of the window to **259,539,401.963** at the end, and it took in nothing but a single unit of dust. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0** as well. Grass does have staking, and stakers do receive a share of network revenue, but the exit is seven days rather than a lock, and staked GRASS stays inside the circulating count — so it removes nothing from the tradable float. The result is a ledger with a real, measurable sell side and an absolutely empty buy side, which is what pins GRASS in the framework's bottom inflation band.

## Foundation and overhang

The dominant GRASS overhang is the Grass multisig itself, the single largest GRASS balance on Solana at **259,539,401.963** as of **Aug 18 2026**, held by the same key that still carries the mint authority. It is refreshed on every rebuild from the chain. Behind it sit the three lock vaults, holding **13,638,333.335** each, or **40.9M GRASS** together, on a fully known schedule that ends **Feb 28 2027**. Third is the wallet that received the **13.33M** ad-hoc transfer on **Jun 10 2026** and has held it untouched ever since; it is a single-signer wallet with no lock, so it can move at any moment. There is no separate DAO treasury, no bankruptcy residual, and no buyback accumulation wallet, because there is no buyback. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How GRASS compares to other DePIN networks

GRASS belongs to the fixed-supply DePIN class, and it is the cleanest example of why a hard cap is not the same thing as low inflation. Networks like Helium or Filecoin issue new tokens to reward providers, so their sell pressure is genuine minting and shows up as protocol inflation; Grass took the opposite route, creating its whole **1B** at launch and paying participants from revenue instead. That makes Sell #1 permanently quiet and pushes every unit of pressure into Sell #2, where the mechanism is release rather than issuance. For a holder the distinction is academic — **32.05M GRASS** a quarter arriving from a vault spends exactly like **32.05M** freshly minted — but for a framework it matters, because a release schedule has an end date and an emission curve usually does not.

The second comparison is against exchange tokens and revenue-generating protocols that run buybacks. Grass earns real money from AI data buyers, and it has chosen to hand that revenue to stakers and participants in dollars rather than convert it into GRASS bids. Structurally that is the difference between a token whose buy row is a mechanism and a token whose buy row is empty: GRASS revenue growth improves the business without ever touching the ledger the framework measures. Until some portion of that revenue is routed into on-market GRASS purchase, Grass will read as one-directional supply no matter how well the network performs.

The third comparison is the most useful one for reading GRASS specifically, and it is against every token whose unlock calendar is quoted by trackers rather than measured on chain. The published Grass calendar and the realised GRASS flow have diverged by roughly a factor of two through this window — about **22.45M** a month booked upstream against **10.68M** a month of actual custody change. Coins where the vesting sits in a readable escrow, as GRASS does in its three lock vaults, allow that divergence to be measured; coins where it does not, do not. That is exactly why the framework reads the vault and not the calendar.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The three GRASS lock vaults fire again on **Aug 28 2026**, **Sep 28 2026** and **Oct 28 2026**, releasing **5.845M GRASS** each time; any change in that exact per-vault figure of **1,948,333.333** would be the first sign the schedule has been amended. The Grass multisig should fund its payout wallet with another **4,838,295.42** in each of August, September and October; a missed month, or a larger one, moves Sell #2 directly. A second ad-hoc multisig transfer of the kind seen on **Jun 10 2026** would move Sell #3 off zero and lift the next-90-day reading immediately. On the buy side, the single item that would change the verdict is any announcement converting Grass network revenue into on-market GRASS purchase — the Foundation's next holder call is the surface to watch for it. Finally, the **13.33M GRASS** parked since **Jun 10 2026** in a single-signer wallet is the nearest live risk: if it moves, it is already counted, but where it lands will say what it was for.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Grass (GRASS) at **+6.93%** net supply growth over the 90 days to **Aug 18 2026** and **+4.90%** projected for the next 90, on a circulating supply of **654.6M** out of a fixed **1B**. Nothing is minted — the mechanism is release, not issuance — and every unit of pressure comes from vesting: **32.05M GRASS** a quarter from three lock vaults and a monthly multisig payout, plus one **13.33M** ad-hoc transfer on **Jun 10 2026**. The key risk is that the buy ledger is empty on all four rows, so a profitable network with real AI revenue still produces no bid for its own token, and the key constraint is the vault schedule itself: **40.9M GRASS** remaining, seven monthly payments, ending **Feb 28 2027**. The framework ships **+6.93%** rather than the monitor's **+11.50%** because it counts GRASS that has actually left custody, not GRASS that has merely become eligible to.

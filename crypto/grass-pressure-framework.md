# GRASS Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

*Originally published at [https://mrnasdog.com/research/grass/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/grass/inflation)*

Grass mints nothing. All **1,000,000,000 GRASS** were created at launch on the Solana blockchain, and the on-chain total has only ever fallen — by **6,896 GRASS** of dust in the token's entire life. Every unit of GRASS inflation is therefore a release: over the 90 days to **Aug 28 2026**, **39.54M GRASS** left team custody, **26.21M** of it through two dated monthly rails and **13.33M** through a single ad-hoc transfer on **Jun 10 2026**. Against that, the buy side is **zero** — Grass runs no buyback, burns nothing, and pays its network participants in dollars. Net supply pressure is **+6.04%** of the **654.55M** circulating GRASS, with **+4.90%** projected for the next 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the window ending **Aug 28 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads GRASS at **+6.04% net**, with the forward view at **+4.90%**: a sell side of **39.54M GRASS** against a buy side of **0**. Our own supply monitor reads **+7.39%** for the same trailing window, a gap of **1.35 percentage points** — outside the framework's **0.5-point** tolerance, so a **⚠ monitor gap** chip ships on the overview. Roughly **0.51** of those points is arithmetic convention rather than disagreement: the monitor divides its **45.07M** move by the supply it saw 90 days earlier, and the same move on today's float reads **6.89%**. The remaining **0.84** points, about **5.5M GRASS**, is the real story — the wider market count books the published entitlement while the chain releases less. GRASS is best labelled **a pure release schedule with no offsetting bid**: no issuance, no burn, no buyback, and dilution that arrives on named dates from named wallets.

## Sell pressure: where new GRASS comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and for GRASS that is a fact about how the token was built, not a quiet quarter. Grass is a DePIN network — it pays households for unused residential bandwidth and resells the resulting web data to AI buyers — and its token is an SPL token on Solana with no block reward and no emission curve at all. The chain read is unambiguous: total supply stands at **999,993,103.89 GRASS** against a ceiling of **1,000,000,000**, so the number has only ever moved down. One honest qualifier belongs here: the mint authority on the GRASS mint has **not** been renounced, so the cap is enforced by policy rather than by code, which is why this row is re-checked at every rebuild instead of being retired. The other thing that could have created GRASS did not: the Grass Foundation confirmed at its **Jul 7 2026** holder call that Stage 2 participant rewards — about **3M dollars**, claimable from **Jul 22 2026** — are paid in **USDC** out of revenue, with zero net new emissions. The much-discussed **170M GRASS** Season 2 airdrop is therefore not a supply event.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **26.21M GRASS**, and it was read as released rather than scheduled. Grass runs two separate rails and this build walked both transaction by transaction. The first is three multisig lock vaults, each paying exactly **1,948,333.333 GRASS** on the 28th of the month; the firings observed on chain were **May 28**, **Jun 28** and **Jul 28 2026**, of which the last two fall inside this window, so the vault rail contributed **11.69M**. Each vault now holds **13,638,333.335 GRASS**, which divides into exactly **seven** more payments — the vault stream ends on **Feb 28 2027**, not in October as the investor calendar implies. The second rail is invisible if you only read balances: the Grass treasury multisig funds a payout wallet with exactly **4,838,295.42 GRASS** a month, and that wallet fans the tokens out to roughly 150 beneficiaries before returning to a resting balance of **115,000**. It fired on **Jun 23**, **Jul 27** and **Aug 24 2026**, adding **14.51M**. A limit worth stating: the payout wallet still held the **Aug 24** tranche at the moment the window closed, so the amount actually delivered to beneficiaries inside the window is **9.68M** rather than **14.51M**; the escrow-outflow figure is shipped so both rails are measured the same way.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **13.33M GRASS**, and it is the row that a calendar-driven reading would miss entirely. On **Jun 10 2026** a single transfer of **13,333,332 GRASS** left the Grass treasury multisig for a wallet that had been created two days earlier, has a single signer, holds no other token, and has never sent a GRASS since. It is on neither vesting rail, so counting it adds no double-count. The forward value for this row is **zero**: ad-hoc grants out of the Grass treasury are sporadic and their size varies several-fold between firings, and the framework does not project a run rate from a pattern that has no dates and no fixed quantum. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero** and always has been — Grass launched in 2024 with its whole supply created at once, so there is no estate, no trustee distribution and no expiring lockup contract anywhere in the token's history.

## Buy pressure: where new GRASS goes

All four buy rows read **zero**, and that is the single most important sentence on this page. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, does not exist: no buyback contract has been deployed, no purchase wallet has ever been named, and the Grass treasury multisig received no GRASS from the open market across the whole window. A news summary of the **Jul 7 2026** holder call asserted a cumulative buyback worth about **5%** of second and third quarter revenue, moving from hand-run to automatic execution — but the Foundation's own written recap of that same call contains no buyback commitment, no quantum and no destination, and the chain shows no purchased GRASS arriving anywhere identifiable. The one inflow the treasury did receive, **1.8M GRASS** on **Aug 21 2026**, came from another wallet the same multisig key already controls and was executed by the multisig itself, so it is an internal reclaim rather than a bid.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **zero** and was verified two ways, because a burn is often not a supply-reducing call. The supply surface shows a lifetime destruction of **6,896 GRASS** — dust, and the on-chain total moved by only **18 GRASS** in the ten days before this rebuild. The dead-address surface was read separately: the two unspendable Solana addresses people use as burn sinks hold **0.000000001 GRASS** each and did not move. Grass has no fee burn and no destruction mechanism of any kind. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **zero** for the reason that makes GRASS unusual: the business is genuinely profitable — roughly **17M dollars** of revenue in the first half of 2026 against a projected **65M to 75M dollars** for the full year — but the **Jul 7 2026** governance decision on capturing that revenue ended in dollar distributions to participants, so none of the money becomes demand for the token. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero** as well: GRASS staking has no minimum term, a **7-day** unstaking cooldown, and neither burns nor mints anything, so it moves no supply off the market.

## Foundation and overhang

Behind the **654.55M** circulating GRASS sit four identified pools. The largest by far is the Grass treasury multisig, holding **256.50M GRASS** and owned by the same key that still holds the mint authority — an unusual concentration, because the treasury key and the mint key are one key. It drains on the monthly payout rail already counted in Sell #2, and occasionally through the ad-hoc grants counted in Sell #3. Second are the three lock vaults, holding **13,638,333.335 GRASS** each for a combined **40.92M**, fully scheduled at seven more payments apiece and exhausted on **Feb 28 2027**. Third are the three payout hubs that receive each vault firing and pass it on within days, holding **63.34M** between them as working balance. Fourth is the single-signer wallet that received the **Jun 10 2026** transfer and still holds all **13.33M** of it, unlocked, one signature away from the market at any moment.

Those four total roughly **374M GRASS**, which is more than the **345.44M** the market count treats as non-circulating — so part of what this analysis calls identified overhang is already inside the circulating denominator, most likely the payout hubs. Every one of these balances is re-read on each rebuild, and the rule is the same for all of them: if any of these balances falls between refreshes beyond its scheduled release, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How GRASS compares to other DePIN tokens

The instructive comparison is with DePIN networks that pay their contributors in their own token. A bandwidth or wireless network that mints rewards every epoch has continuous, usage-linked emission: supply grows with adoption, and the token is simultaneously the product's currency and its cost of goods sold. Grass deliberately went the other way in 2026 — Stage 2 rewards were paid in USDC out of revenue rather than in GRASS out of an incentive allocation — which means the network can grow without the token diluting. It is a genuinely better structure for holders on the emission side, and this page books Sell #1 at zero because of it. The cost is on the demand side: a token that is not paid to contributors and not bought back by the treasury has no mechanical reason for anyone to hold it, which is exactly the criticism the community raised when the USDC decision landed.

Against capped tokens with a revenue-funded buyback, GRASS is the mirror image. Both have a hard supply ceiling and neither mints; the difference is that a buyback token converts revenue into a standing bid while GRASS converts revenue into dollar payouts, so one shrinks its float while the other simply stops growing it. And against uncapped continuous-emission chains, GRASS looks better on a long horizon and worse on a short one: its ceiling is real at **1,000,000,000 GRASS**, but with only **65.5%** of that in circulation, the remaining **345.44M** is an unlock calendar rather than a mining curve, and unlock calendars front-load their dilution. The framework's reading of GRASS is therefore not a judgement on the business, which is profitable and growing — it is a reading of a token whose supply mechanics are all release and no absorption.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the three lock-vault firings dated **Aug 28 2026**, **Sep 28 2026** and **Oct 28 2026**: each releases **5.85M GRASS** across the three vaults, and each vault balance should fall by exactly **1,948,333.333 GRASS** per firing if the pattern holds. Second, the monthly treasury payout funding of **4,838,295.42 GRASS**, whose date has drifted between the 23rd and the 27th over the last four months — three more are expected inside the window. Third, whether another ad-hoc transfer follows the **Jun 10 2026** one: those grants are the only unpredictable line in this ledger, and a second one of similar size would add nearly two percentage points to the forward reading on its own. Fourth, the end of the early-investor calendar in late **Oct 2026** — the published schedule completes the investor allocation then, while the vaults on chain run to **Feb 28 2027**, and watching which of the two the float actually follows is the cleanest test of the released-versus-scheduled gap this page describes. Fifth, any move by the Grass Foundation to convert revenue into a GRASS bid; the **Jul 7 2026** vote settled on dollars, but a buyback with a named wallet would be the single change that flips this ledger.

## Summary

Grass adds about **+6.04%** to its circulating GRASS every 90 days without minting a single token, because its entire supply already exists and is being released on a schedule. Three lock vaults pay **1,948,333.333 GRASS** each on the 28th of the month, the treasury funds a payout wallet with **4,838,295.42 GRASS** a month, and one further **13.33M** left on **Jun 10 2026** on neither rail — **39.54M** in total, against a buy side of exactly nothing. The key risk is that structure rather than any single event: Grass is a profitable business that pays its participants and its holders in dollars, so revenue growth does not become token demand, and there is no burn or buyback to absorb the release. The ceiling is real — **1,000,000,000 GRASS**, with **345.44M** still to come — but the mint authority has not been renounced, so that ceiling rests on policy rather than on code.

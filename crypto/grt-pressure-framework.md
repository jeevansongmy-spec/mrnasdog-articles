# GRT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

GRT supply is growing. Between Aug 19 and Nov 17 2026 The Graph created **77.36M GRT** in new coins and the Graph Foundation's lock released **38.75M GRT**, while burns removed only **59,625 GRT**. Net supply rose **+1.06%** in 90 days, and the next 90 days look the same. GRT has no supply cap. Other trackers print **+0.005%**, so the coin page carries a warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

GRT is inflationary, and steadily so. Over the 90 days to Nov 17 2026 the circulating supply of The Graph's token grew by about **116.07M GRT**, a net rise of **+1.06%**, and the next 90 days project the same **+1.06%**. Other trackers show **+0.005%**, a gap of **1.06 points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. The reason is simple: the circulating figure those trackers use has sat near **10.94B GRT** since Aug 19 2026, while on-chain the network kept creating coins every block and the Foundation kept claiming its monthly slices. Our label for GRT this quarter is **supply growing**, with almost nothing on the other side of the scale.

## Sell pressure: where new GRT comes from

**Protocol inflation: about 77.37M GRT per 90 days.** The Graph creates **120.73 GRT** for every Ethereum block, roughly **869K GRT a day**, and the rate did not change in the window. The coins are created on Arbitrum, where the network now runs. Most of them pay the indexers who store and serve blockchain data to apps, and the delegators who back those indexers.

Two changes this autumn altered where the new GRT goes, not how much is made. From Sep 1 2026 a fifth of every block's GRT, **24.146 GRT**, goes to an Innovation Allocation the Graph Foundation runs to build and maintain the network; it received **13.06M GRT** in the window. From Aug 25 2026 a new eligibility check stops paying indexers who fail to serve enough real queries. Their share is still created, but it is parked in a holding contract, which took in **4.70M GRT**. Indexers who passed received **59.60M GRT**. Together that is **77.36M GRT** in the window, and the pace since the Sep 1 split works out to **77.37M GRT** per 90 days.

**Vesting unlocks: 38.75M GRT.** The Graph Foundation's share of the original supply sits in a 10-year lock that opens **12.92M GRT** a month from Dec 2020 to Dec 2030. The Foundation claimed slices on Aug 25, Sep 23 and Oct 26 2026, **38.75M GRT** in all. Three more open in the next 90 days: one has been ready since Nov 17 2026, then Dec 17 2026 and Jan 17 2027.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** The building fund and the holding contract were counted when their coins were created, so spending them later adds nothing new. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court or bankrupt firm holds GRT for a later payout, and the early team and investor shares have finished vesting.

## Buy pressure: where new GRT goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** No contract or treasury buys GRT back, and neither the Foundation nor Edge & Node has announced a plan to.

**Protocol fee burn: 59,625 GRT.** The Graph burns two things. A slice of what apps pay for data is destroyed, and 1% of every GRT placed as signal on a subgraph is burned on the way in. Between Aug 19 and Nov 17 2026 the curation burn destroyed **31,659 GRT** and the payment burn **27,707 GRT**; another **259 GRT** was sent to dead addresses. The total cancels under **0.1%** of the new GRT. The burn is small because paid use is small: apps paid only about **$8.6K** in query fees in the 30 days to Nov 17 2026, against about **$61K** a year earlier.

**Foundation buy: 0.** The Foundation spends GRT and has no plan to buy it on the market. **New long-term lock: 0.** GRT staked by indexers and delegators still counts as circulating, so more staking takes nothing out.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the Graph Foundation's own lock, which still holds **645.83M GRT**. That balance is about **5.9%** of today's circulating supply, and it reaches the market at **12.92M GRT** a month until Dec 17 2030. The Foundation's wallet that receives each slice holds only about **167K GRT**, because it passes the coins on soon after they arrive.

Two smaller pots sit with the Foundation and the protocol. The Innovation Allocation holds **1.22M GRT** after paying out **12.02M GRT** since September. The holding contract for withheld indexer pay holds **4.72M GRT**, and the Foundation says no decision has been made on how it will be used. Both were counted when the coins were created, so they would add no new supply if spent, but they are GRT that could still be sold.

## How GRT compares to other data-network tokens

GRT works like a payroll token. The Graph pays its indexers by creating new coins on a fixed per-block schedule, and the only offsets are small burns tied to usage. Filecoin (FIL) is built the same way: storage providers are paid in newly created coins and part of the network's fees is burned. The difference is scale. GRT's burn covers well under one coin in a thousand created, because query fees are tiny next to the reward budget.

Chainlink (LINK) sits at the other end. LINK has no per-block issuance at all; its supply grows only when coins leave reserves held outside the circulating count. GRT has both kinds of pressure at once, steady issuance and a dated monthly unlock, which is why its supply grows faster than either part alone suggests. Unlike many newer infrastructure tokens, GRT has no large cliff left: the Foundation's lock opens in even slices, so there is no single day when a big block of coins arrives.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 17 2026: a bigger curation burn.** A governance vote raised the burn on signal from 1% to 3%, with the change set to go live on Nov 17 2026. It is meant to stop indexers from moving signal around to steer rewards to themselves. If deposits hold up, the curation part of the burn roughly triples, still a sliver of new supply.

**Nov 17 2026: stricter rules for big indexers.** From this date an indexer must serve one subgraph for every 4M GRT of stake, with a floor of 10. Indexers that fall short lose their rewards to the holding contract. This changes who is paid, not how much GRT is created.

**Dec 1 to Dec 15 2026: Base traffic moves to the network.** Subgraphs for the Base chain must be published to The Graph Network by Dec 1 2026, and all Base traffic should run there by Dec 15 2026. More paid queries would raise fees and the payment burn, which has fallen far this year.

**Dec 17 2026 and Jan 17 2027: Foundation slices.** Each opens **12.92M GRT**, on top of the slice ready since Nov 17 2026. The next one after that falls on Feb 16 2027, just outside this window.

## Summary

GRT supply grew **+1.06%** between Aug 19 and Nov 17 2026, and the next 90 days project the same **+1.06%**. The Graph created **77.36M GRT** for indexers, its Foundation building fund and a holding contract, and the Foundation's lock released **38.75M GRT**. Burns removed only **59,625 GRT**, because apps pay very little in query fees today. The Foundation's lock still holds **645.83M GRT** and opens **12.92M GRT** a month until Dec 2030, so this pressure is set to continue.

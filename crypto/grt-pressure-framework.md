# GRT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

GRT is inflationary. Between Aug 29 and Nov 27 2026 The Graph minted **76.07M** new GRT and the Graph Foundation's lock released **38.75M** more, while the network's two small taxes burned only **70,295** GRT, so supply grew about **+1.05%** in 90 days. GRT has no supply cap, and the next 90 days look much the same at about **+1.06%**, while other trackers show **0.00%** because the supply figure they use has not moved since August.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

GRT supply grew by **+1.05%** over the 90 days to Nov 27 2026, and the same mix of new coins points to about **+1.06%** over the 90 days to Feb 25 2027. Other trackers show close to **0.00%**, a gap of about **1.05** points, so the coin page carries a warning note. The reason is simple: the circulating figure those trackers use has stood still at 10.94B GRT since August 2026, while the chain kept minting GRT every block and the Foundation's lock kept opening every month. Our label for GRT this quarter is steady growth: about one percent more GRT every three months, with almost nothing taken back.

## Sell pressure: where new GRT comes from

**Protocol inflation: 76.07M GRT.** The Graph mints **120.73** GRT for every Ethereum block, about 317M a year, and that rate did not change in the window. From Aug 29 to Nov 27 2026 indexers were paid **55.20M** GRT for serving data. Since Sep 1 2026 one fifth of every block goes to the Foundation's Innovation Allocation, which received **14.81M** GRT. Another **6.06M** GRT was minted into a holding pot instead of going to indexers that failed the new service test. Because the Foundation's share now arrives every day rather than whenever indexers collect, the next 90 days should bring about **77.23M** GRT.

**Vesting unlocks: 38.75M GRT.** The Graph Foundation was given 2B GRT at launch, and 1.55B of it sits in a lock that opens **12.92M** GRT about once a month until Dec 17 2030. The Foundation claimed three slices in the window, on Sep 23, Oct 26 and Nov 19 2026. Three more slices open on Dec 17 2026, Jan 17 2027 and Feb 16 2027, another **38.75M** GRT.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** The Foundation's other GRT was already counted when it was minted or released, so spending it adds nothing new to the market count.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court case or failed company is holding GRT for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new GRT goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** The Graph has no buyback, and no contract buys GRT on the network's behalf.

**Protocol fee burn: 70,295 GRT.** Curators who back a subgraph lose a slice of every deposit, and 1% of every query fee is burned. In the window that came to **45,751** GRT from curation, **24,284** GRT from query fees and 259 GRT sent to dead addresses. On Nov 18 2026 the curation tax went from 1% to 3% to stop indexers from moving their own signal around for extra rewards, so the next 90 days should burn about **148.86K** GRT. Even doubled, the burn takes back about one GRT for every 780 that arrive.

**Foundation buy: 0.** The Graph Foundation sells GRT to pay for its work and has announced no plan to buy any back.

**New long-term lock: 0.** Staked and delegated GRT stays in the market count, and so does the Foundation's new liquid staking token, so neither takes GRT out of circulation.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the Graph Foundation's lock, which still holds **632.92M** GRT. That GRT is not in the market count yet, and it comes out at **12.92M** a month on a fixed schedule, so every slice is already booked as a vesting unlock rather than a surprise.

Two smaller pots sit with the Foundation outside the lock. The Innovation Allocation holds **2.97M** GRT and pays out to Foundation projects. The pot of rewards held back from indexers holds **6.11M** GRT, and the Foundation says no decision has been made on how it will be used. Both pots were counted when they were minted, so spending them would not add to supply, though selling them would still add sellers.

## How GRT compares to other data and compute networks

The Graph pays its workers the way many data and compute networks do: new coins on a fixed schedule, with a small burn on the side. Livepeer also pays its node operators in new tokens, but moves its inflation rate up or down depending on how much of the supply is staked, while The Graph keeps a flat amount per block. Filecoin pays storage miners in new coins that unlock slowly over the following months, and burns part of its network fees.

Render tries the opposite approach: users pay for work by burning tokens, and new tokens are minted to pay the workers. The Graph's burn is tiny next to its minting, because people pay only a few thousand dollars a month in query fees while the network mints millions of GRT. Until paid use grows a lot, GRT will keep growing at around four percent a year.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Dec 8 2026: a stricter service test.** Large indexers will need to serve one subgraph for every 3M GRT of stake, up from one for every 4M. The total minted does not change, but more rewards could land in the held-back pot.

**Dec 17 2026, Jan 17 2027 and Feb 16 2027: Foundation slices.** Each opens **12.92M** GRT. The Foundation has claimed each slice a few days after it opened, so the Feb 16 2027 slice could be claimed just after this window ends.

**The higher curation tax.** The 3% curation tax has run only since Nov 18 2026. If subgraph deposits fall after the change, the burn will grow less than the expected **148.86K** GRT.

**The held-back pot.** The Foundation has not said what it will do with the **6.11M** GRT of held-back rewards. Burning it would be the first sizeable burn in years; handing it out would change nothing in the count.

## Summary

GRT supply grew **+1.05%** in the 90 days to Nov 27 2026, and the next 90 days point to about **+1.06%**.

The Graph mints **120.73** GRT per Ethereum block for indexers and the Foundation, and the Foundation's lock adds **12.92M** GRT a month until Dec 17 2030.

Curation and query-fee taxes burned only **70,295** GRT in the window, and the new 3% curation tax should still keep the burn under **150K** GRT a quarter.

Other trackers show GRT flat because the supply figure they use has not moved since August 2026, so the coin page carries a warning note.

JTO is one of the few tokens in this catalog that literally cannot be minted again — the Jito governance token's mint authority on Solana is empty, a one-way door — and it still reads inflationary, because the December 2023 launch allocation is not finished handing itself out. Over the last 90 days the JTO vesting stream released **33.46M JTO** to contributors and investors, against **1.44M JTO** of governance-mandated open-market buyback, for a net **+6.21%** of circulating supply reaching the market. The inflation monitor reads **+7.88%** over the same window, a gap of **1.67** percentage points. The next 90 days read **+6.32%**, and the structural turn is close: the JTO vesting calendar empties around **Dec 2026**, after which nothing is scheduled to sell.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The JTO ledger is lopsided and honest about it. Sell pressure over the trailing 90 days is **33.46M JTO**, all of it vesting. Buy pressure is **1.44M JTO**, all of it the Jito DAO's buyback mandate. That nets to **+6.21%** of circulating supply going to market over 90 days, against the inflation monitor's **+7.88%** for the same window — a gap of **1.67** percentage points, over the framework's half-point tolerance, so this page ships with a warning chip. The gap is not new issuance: the JTO mint on Solana holds **986,522,959** tokens with no mint authority, read directly this session. It is the difference between what the published three-year vest schedule accounts for and what the classified float actually released, **37.65M**, with the excess being discretionary Jito DAO and Jito Foundation treasury deployment that this framework books at zero until a dated release is observed. The cite-able label for JTO in August 2026: **a fixed-supply token still working through its launch overhang, with the buy side committed but not yet delivered**.

## Sell pressure: where new JTO comes from

Nowhere, in the strict sense. Protocol inflation for JTO is zero and permanently so: the Jito governance token is a plain Solana SPL token whose mint authority and freeze authority both read null on chain, which cannot be reversed. There is no JTO staking emission, no block reward, no rebase. Jito the protocol earns real money — MEV tips, Block Engine fees, BAM fees, and from July 2026 the JTX trading terminal — but every one of those streams is denominated in SOL or JitoSOL and paid to stakers and the DAO. Not one of them creates a JTO.

What does reach the market is the launch allocation, and it is the whole sell side. Jito's core contributors hold **245M JTO** and its investors **162.1M JTO**, a combined **407.1M**, on a three-year vest with a one-year cliff running from the **Dec 7 2023** genesis. One third came free at the **Dec 7 2024** cliff and the remaining **271.4M JTO** streams out daily until the schedule ends around **Dec 2026** — **371,781 JTO** a day, or **33.46M** across a 90-day window. This build treats that as a stream rather than as monthly cliffs, and the chain-side evidence is decisive: the classified JTO float rose smoothly all quarter with no step on the 7th of June, July or August, and its total 90-day growth of **37.65M** is smaller than the three monthly tranches of **16.96M** that unlock aggregators publish. The monthly calendar is a bucketing artifact; the daily stream is what the JTO supply actually does.

The other two sell rows are zero. Foundation and unscheduled unlocks is zero on value but not on scope: **471.21M JTO** sits outside the tradable float, including **209.56M JTO** in the Jito DAO treasury wallet and the Jito Foundation's undeployed 25% strategic allocation. Neither readable wallet fell inside the window — the DAO treasury actually took JTO in — and nothing moved on a dated, quantified release, so the row stays at zero and the balances are watched. Long-term locked or bankruptcy is zero because JTO launched after the estates that overhang older assets were formed; no trustee holds JTO.

## Buy pressure: where new JTO goes

The Jito DAO is contractually buying JTO right now. A governance vote passed in **May 2026** obliges the cryptoeconomics subDAO to deploy its own treasury reserves on open-market JTO purchases matching **100%** of protocol revenue, epoch by epoch, across the whole third quarter of 2026. Last published quarterly protocol revenue was **1.28M dollars**, so the 61 days of that mandate falling inside this window come to about **849,000 dollars**, and at the quarter's measured average JTO price of **0.5886** dollars that is **1.44M JTO**. This is not a hypothetical mechanism — an earlier subDAO report logs **3.2M dollars** of JTO buybacks accumulating **2.15M JTO** — but the per-epoch execution dashboard for this quarter has not been published, so the figure is the amount the vote requires rather than a confirmed fill, and the obligation stops on **Sep 30 2026**.

The protocol fee burn is the row everyone expects to carry this page, and it is zero — a measured zero, not an unknown. A **Jul 13 2026** vote committed **100%** of the Jito DAO's revenue share from the JTX trading terminal, itself **80%** of all JTX platform fees, to open-market JTO buybacks with every acquired token permanently burned, running through **Q4 2027**. JTX opened phased access on **Jul 14 2026**. The burn was checked from both sides this session, because a burn is often a transfer rather than a supply-reducing call: JTO's on-chain supply reads **986,522,959**, exactly where the project's own June quarter-end cumulative burn total of **13.48M** leaves it, and Solana's unspendable address holds **0.04 JTO**. Nothing has been burned since June, and no JTX revenue figure exists yet to size what will be. Foundation buy is zero because the purchasing runs through the subDAO already counted above, and new long-term lock is zero because no new JTO lockup contract with an announced quantum was deployed.

## Foundation and overhang

Four team-controlled JTO overhangs are tracked, and three of them were read directly on Solana this session. The Jito DAO treasury, governed on Realms by JTO holders, holds **209.56M JTO** — and it moved the right way, taking in about **87,000 JTO** since its previous read rather than sending any out. The Jito Foundation's own multisig reads **0 JTO**; the Foundation's **25%** strategic allocation is held elsewhere and releases at its discretion rather than on a calendar. The Jito governance stake pool holds **21.17M JTO**, which is custody rather than a lock — it is withdrawable at will, which is exactly why it does not count as buy pressure. The fourth is the cryptoeconomics subDAO multisig, the destination for every JTO the buyback acquires; its address has not been published, so it is opaque and monitored through official disclosure. Together with the unvested remainder of the launch allocation these account for the **471.21M JTO** gap between the **986.52M** that exists and the **515.32M** that trades. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How JTO compares to other revenue-sharing governance tokens

The natural comparison class for JTO is exchange and infrastructure tokens that route protocol revenue into buybacks or burns. Against an exchange token running a quarterly auto-burn, JTO looks structurally similar in intent and completely different in delivery: the auto-burn chains destroy a fixed, published quantum every quarter regardless of sentiment, and their supply curve bends down on a schedule you can read a year ahead. Jito's buy side is revenue-linked instead of quantum-linked, so it scales with how much the network actually earns — and Jito's protocol revenue fell **45%** in the second quarter of 2026. A revenue-linked burn is more honest and more volatile.

Against uncapped continuous-emission layer-1s, JTO is in a far better structural position than its **+6.21%** reading suggests. An emitting chain's sell side never ends; it is a permanent tax on holders that only governance can cut. JTO's sell side is a fixed, finite pile with a date on it. Every token the JTO vesting stream releases is one that will never be released again, and the mint authority being null means no successor emission can be introduced without deploying a different token entirely. This page reads inflationary today and structurally cannot read inflationary from vesting after the calendar empties.

Against fee-burn chains, the difference is that a base-fee burn destroys supply from ordinary usage with no discretion involved, while JTO's burn depends on a new consumer product finding volume. The Jito DAO has bound itself for a year, which removes the discretion risk, but it cannot bind the revenue.

First, **Sep 30 2026**: the subDAO's par-buyback obligation expires and the BAM subsidy programme hits its hard cutoff, returning **100%** of Jito protocol revenue to the DAO — how the DAO routes it in the fourth quarter is a pending vote and is the single biggest swing factor on this page. Second, the first published Rev Splitter figures for JTX fees collected and JTO bought and burned, promised to the Jito DAO on a per-epoch basis; the first non-zero burn would be the first movement in JTO's on-chain supply since June. Third, the end of the JTO vesting calendar around **Dec 2026**, which removes the entire sell side of this ledger in one step. Fourth, JIP-39, passed to the forum on **Aug 26 2026**, which moves 25% of JitoSOL delegation to a reference validator and winds the BAM subsidy to zero by mid-2027 — it moves no JTO directly but it restores revenue to JTO value accrual. Fifth, the Jito DAO treasury and Jito Foundation balances, which are the only way this page's Sell #3 stops being zero.

## Summary

JTO cannot be minted — the Jito governance token's Solana mint authority is null and **13.48M JTO** of the **1B** genesis has already been destroyed — and yet the framework reads **+6.21%** over the last 90 days and **+6.32%** over the next, because the launch allocation is still paying out at **33.46M JTO** a quarter. The buy side is committed on paper and thin in practice: **1.44M JTO** of mandated buyback that expires **Sep 30 2026**, and a buy-and-burn programme that has burned nothing since the terminal launched in July. The key risk is that the burn stays theoretical while the vesting stream keeps running; the key upside is that the vesting stream has a hard end around **Dec 2026** and the supply ceiling behind it is permanent. There is no scenario in which more JTO is created — only scenarios in which the existing **986.52M** is distributed faster or destroyed faster.

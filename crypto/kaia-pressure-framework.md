# KAIA Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

KAIA supply is growing, and it will keep growing: between Aug 20 and Nov 18 2026 the Kaia chain created **74,609,184 KAIA** at a fixed **9.6 KAIA** per block, while burns removed **2,599,703 KAIA**, so net supply rose **+1.11%** in 90 days, with about **+1.04%** expected over the next 90. KAIA has no supply cap and no vesting; new coins come only from the block mint, and the only ways out are a monthly burn of unearned rewards and a small fee burn. Other trackers read **+1.12%** for the same window, so the two readings agree.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

In the 90 days to Nov 18 2026, KAIA's supply grew by about **72.01M KAIA**, a net rise of **+1.11%** on **6.47B KAIA** in circulation. Other trackers show **+1.12%**, a difference of **0.01 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days look a little lower at about **+1.04%**, because three monthly reward burns fall inside that window instead of one. Kaia is a steady-mint chain: inflationary by design, with burns that slow the growth but do not stop it.

## Sell pressure: where new KAIA comes from

The block mint is the only source of new KAIA. Every block, about once a second, Kaia creates **9.6 KAIA**, which came to **74,609,184 KAIA** between Aug 20 and Nov 18 2026. Stakers and validators receive 40% of each block's new coins, the Kaia Ecosystem Fund 25%, the Kaia Infrastructure Fund 25%, and the last 10% goes to a reward pool for people who use KAIA on the chain. Every one of those wallets already counts as circulating, so the full mint reaches the market count on the day it is made. The next 90 days should bring the same **74.61M KAIA**, since the mint per block has not changed and no vote to change it has been published.

Vesting unlocks add **0**: KAIA has no vesting schedule, and nearly every existing coin is already counted as circulating. Foundation and unscheduled unlocks also add **0**. The two Foundation funds moved **60M KAIA** from their main wallets to their working wallets, 20M on Sep 2 and 40M on Nov 11 2026, and spent part of it on projects, but all of those coins were already in circulation, so the spending changes who holds KAIA, not how much exists. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**: no court, estate or bankrupt firm holds KAIA for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new KAIA goes

The largest sink is the burn of unearned rewards. Since July 2026, the 10% of each block that used to go to the block proposer feeds a Contribution Reward pool with a budget of **2.5M KAIA** a month, paid to people who stake KAIA and also deposit stablecoins in chosen apps on Kaia. Only about 3% of each month's budget has been earned so far, and the rest is burned two months later. The first burn, **2,463,579 KAIA**, went out on Oct 21 2026. Three more fall inside the next 90 days: **2,441,567 KAIA** around Nov 20 2026, **2,431,005 KAIA** around Dec 20 2026, and about **2.43M KAIA** around Jan 19 2027, for about **7.30M KAIA** in all.

The fee burn is small. Most of the gas fee in each block is destroyed, which removed **136,124 KAIA** between Aug 20 and Nov 18 2026, less than 0.2% of the new coins in the same days. The coming permissionless upgrade may change how much of each fee is burned. Kaia runs no buyback, so the programmatic buyback row is **0**; using fees to buy KAIA is only a long-term idea in the 2026 tokenomics plan. The Foundation buy row is **0**, because the Foundation spends KAIA rather than buying it. The new long-term lock row is **0** as well: staked KAIA still counts as circulating, and the move to new staking contracts that began on Nov 17 2026 shifts coins without locking new ones away.

## Foundation and overhang

Five Foundation-controlled wallets hold about **254.5M KAIA**, close to 3.9% of the circulating supply. The Kaia Ecosystem Fund's main wallet holds **149.33M KAIA** and its working wallet **25.86M KAIA**. The Kaia Infrastructure Fund's main wallet holds **48.34M KAIA** and its working wallet **23.81M KAIA**. The Contribution Reward pool holds **7.15M KAIA**, most of it already set for the next burns. Each main wallet gains 2.4 KAIA every block and sends larger lots to its working wallet every few months after a public notice; spending is approved in monthly use-of-funds votes. All five wallets already count as circulating, so none of them is a hidden unlock. We read every balance on-chain at each rebuild; if any of these wallets falls between two checks, that outflow will show up in the Foundation row at the next check.

## How KAIA compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

KAIA sits with the uncapped proof-of-stake chains that mint a fixed amount per block. Its mint does not fall on a schedule, so new supply stays near **300M KAIA** a year while the supply grows, and the yearly rate slips only slowly, from about 4.6% today. Solana takes another path: its inflation rate steps down every year toward a long-term floor. Cosmos Hub moves its rate up or down with the share of coins staked. Kaia changes its mint only by a Governance Council vote, as it did when Klaytn cut the block reward from 9.6 to 6.4 in late 2022, and when the move to Kaia in 2024 set it back to 9.6.

On the burn side, Kaia looks more like Ethereum than like BNB Chain. Ethereum and Kaia both burn part of every fee, but Kaia's chain is cheap to use, so its fee burn removes less than 0.2% of what it mints. BNB Chain adds a scheduled burn on top of its fee burn. Kaia's own extra sink is the monthly burn of unearned rewards, which is set by how many people take part rather than by a fixed schedule; if more users earn the rewards, less is burned. Half of each block's new coins also go to two Foundation funds instead of to stakers.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 20 2026:** the second monthly reward burn of **2,441,567 KAIA** is due at the end of the fourth reward month.

**Nov 26 2026:** stakers can start withdrawing from the old staking contracts and restaking in the new ones; coins that stay unstaked are still circulating, so this changes who sells, not how much KAIA exists.

**Dec 20 2026:** the third reward burn of **2,431,005 KAIA** is due, and the staking move ends for reward purposes.

**Jan 19 2027:** the fourth reward burn, about **2.43M KAIA**, is due; its exact size depends on how much of the October to November budget was earned.

**Main network date not set:** the permissionless upgrade ran on Kaia's test network on Nov 18 2026. Its release notes leave the 9.6 KAIA mint as it is but change the fee burn rules, and a separate review of the mint itself is planned with no date.

## Summary

KAIA supply grew **+1.11%** in the 90 days to Nov 18 2026 and should grow about **+1.04%** in the next 90 days. Kaia mints a fixed **9.6 KAIA** per block with no cap, about **74.61M KAIA** every 90 days, and 60% of that goes to Foundation funds and a reward pool that already count as circulating. The monthly burn of unearned rewards, about **2.43M KAIA** each month, is the main brake, and it shrinks if more people earn those rewards. Only a Governance Council vote to cut the mint would change the picture in a big way.

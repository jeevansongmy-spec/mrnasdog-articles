# JST Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking

<!-- main-page -->
This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about JUST, see [mrnasdog.com/research/jst](https://mrnasdog.com/research/jst).
JST is deflationary. No new JST is made, and on Oct 16 2026 JUST bought back and burned **170,723,144 JST**, so the circulating supply fell **2.13%** in the last 90 days. The next 90 days point to about **−1.25%**, from one smaller burn of about **100.61M JST** expected in mid-January 2027.

Sell pressure was **0 JST** and buy pressure **170.72M JST**, set against **8.02B JST** in circulation. Other trackers show **−2.12%** for the same 90 days, in line with our figure.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 5 to Nov 3 2026 not a single new JST was created, and **170.72M JST** left the market for good when JUST sent them to the dead address on Oct 16 2026. That gives a net change of **−2.13%** in 90 days. Other trackers show **−2.12%**, so the two readings agree and the coin page carries no warning note. Looking ahead, the burns go on but get smaller, because the old reserve money that paid for the big early rounds is now spent. The label for JST this month is simple: **supply shrinking, projected to keep shrinking**.

## Sell pressure: where new JST comes from

Protocol inflation is **0 JST**. JST pays no block reward, no staking reward and no lending reward in fresh coins. The full **9.9B JST** was minted in one go in April 2020, and the token has not minted a single coin since. The contract still has an owner key that could mint, so we keep watching it, but it has never been used after launch.

Vesting unlocks are **0 JST**. The team, partner, seed and ecosystem shares finished unlocking in 2023, so no locked tranche is waiting to reach the market.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0 JST**. The JUST Treasury and the big protocol wallets all hold coins that already count as circulating, so any move out of them adds no new supply. The next section lists them.

Long-term locks and bankruptcy piles are **0 JST**. No court estate or long lock of JST is waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new JST goes

The buyback and burn took out **170.72M JST**. On Oct 16 2026 the JUST Treasury sent **170,723,144 JST** to the dead address, a TRON address nobody controls, and the move is public on-chain. JUST paid **$19.62M** for those coins: about $9.28M of JustLend income from the third quarter and about $10.34M that was the last slice of an old revenue reserve. It was the fifth round since the program started in October 2025, and the five rounds together have burned about **1.88B JST**, close to a fifth of the first 9.9B.

The way JST burns are paid for changed on Oct 23 2026. JUST DAO now runs the program instead of the JustLend DAO, the old reserve is gone, and new rounds are paid from ongoing income: JustLend lending and staking, the GasFree payment service, and USDD income above $10M once it gets there. On top of that, JUST set aside a **$20.34M** surplus to be spent in four parts from the fourth quarter of 2026, and it keeps at least **$50M** in reserve that is never used for buybacks. Third-quarter income plus one part of the surplus comes to about **100.61M JST** at today's price for the next round. GasFree income could add to it, but no quarterly figure is out yet, so we leave it out.

Protocol fee burns are **0 JST** for this window. The last one was on Jul 17 2026, when **106.66M JST** paid as old stablecoin fees were destroyed in one go, and nothing says it will happen again soon.

Foundation buying outside the burn is **0 JST**, because every JST JUST buys back is burned and counted once above. New long-term locks are also **0 JST**: JST locked for voting or lent out in the lending market still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The JUST Treasury wallet holds **500.00M JST**. It is the wallet that pays each burn. On Aug 16 2026 it sent 200M JST out and got the same 200M back on Aug 24, and on Oct 16 it received the bought-back coins and passed **170.72M JST** to the dead address minutes later. Its balance has not moved since.

The JST voting contract holds **700.02M JST** that holders have locked to vote, and the JST market in JustLend holds **91.99M JST** that people have lent out. Both are user coins that can leave at any time, and both already count as circulating.

The last overhang is the owner key on the token contract, which could mint new JST. It has minted once, at launch in April 2020, and never again. A mint would show up in the very next check.

## How JST compares to other DeFi buyback-and-burn tokens

Most DeFi tokens still pay out new coins to users or insiders, so any buyback has to beat that flow first. JST has no such flow: all of it was created in 2020 and vesting is over, so every coin bought back and burned is a straight cut to supply. That is why a burn of about $20M a quarter can move JST by about two percent.

JST is close to BNB in shape: a fixed starting supply and a burn that runs on a quarterly rhythm. It differs from SUN, the TRON swap token, which burns smaller amounts in many frequent rounds. The weak spot for JST is the funding. With the old reserve gone, the size of each round now rests on what JustLend, GasFree and USDD earn, plus the four surplus parts, so the burn rises and falls with that income.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Mid-January 2027 is the main date. The five past rounds fired between 14 and 19 days after each quarter ended, which points to about Jan 14 to Jan 19 2027 for the sixth. We expect about **100.61M JST**, though the real size depends on fourth-quarter income and the JST price on the day.

The first part of the **$20.34M** surplus is due from the fourth quarter of 2026. If JUST puts in more than a quarter of it, the January burn will be bigger than our figure.

GasFree income joins the buyback from the fourth quarter of 2026, and the fourth-quarter report, likely late in January 2027, should show the first numbers. USDD income only counts once it passes $10M, which has not happened yet.

We also watch the **500.00M JST** in the JUST Treasury wallet and the owner mint key. A large outflow from the treasury or any new mint would change this page at the next check.

## Summary

JST supply fell **2.13%** in the 90 days to Nov 3 2026, because JUST burned **170.72M JST** on Oct 16 2026 and no new JST was made.

All of JST was minted in 2020 and vesting ended in 2023, so the only flow left is the buyback and burn, paid from JUST's business income.

Since Oct 23 2026 the burns are paid from ongoing income and a $20.34M surplus in four parts, so the next round, expected in mid-January 2027, should be about **100.61M JST**, a cut of about **1.25%**.

The JUST Treasury holds **500.00M JST** and the contract owner can still mint, so those two stay on our watch list.

# SYRUP Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

SYRUP, the token of the Maple Finance lending platform, was flat over the last 90 days: **0 SYRUP** of new supply reached the market and **0 SYRUP** left it, for a net of **0.00%**, with the same **0.00%** projected for the next 90 days. The three-year SYRUP issuance stopped on **Oct 1 2026**, but **23.2M SYRUP** earned before that date has not been minted yet, and the Maple buyback of **2.90M SYRUP** stays in a wallet that still counts as circulating. The monitor reads **+0.37%**, close enough that no warning is needed.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

On our ledger SYRUP supply moved **0.00%** over the 90 days from Jul 11 to Oct 9 2026, and we project **0.00%** for the next 90 days. The small rise the monitor sees comes from how the circulating count was re-drawn in August 2026, not from new coins: total SYRUP supply read exactly **1,244,677,492.51** at both ends of the window. In one line, SYRUP is a quiet supply with one loaded spring: the unminted issuance.

## Sell pressure: where new SYRUP comes from

Protocol inflation booked **0 SYRUP**. Since the MPL to SYRUP switch, Maple Finance has run a three-year issuance of roughly 5% a year, paid to the Maple treasury through an on-chain issuance contract. The contract works in windows: about **52.32M SYRUP a year** from Jan 1 2026, then a rate of zero from Oct 1 2026, which ends the issuance. New SYRUP only exists once the treasury claims it, and the last claim was on Apr 22 2026, when **28.55M SYRUP** was minted. Nothing was claimed in this window, so the total supply did not move. What remains is **23.2M SYRUP** already earned and ready to mint, about **1.99%** of the circulating supply. Only the governor wallet can claim it, and past claims came anywhere from one week to six months apart, so there is no date we can book.

Vesting unlocks booked **0 SYRUP**. The original team and investor allocations are fully unlocked, and the reward contract that pays community rewards over time, holding about **17.17M SYRUP**, already counts as circulating, so its payouts move coins inside the float rather than into it.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks booked **0 SYRUP**. Only one wallet sits outside the circulating count, a reserve of **77.36M SYRUP** that held the same balance at both ends of the window. The DAO safe did send **8.0M SYRUP** to an operations wallet in August, but both wallets already count as circulating, so that move adds nothing new.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy booked **0 SYRUP**. There is no estate or trustee. Staking rewards ended in November 2025, and holders keep leaving the staking contract, which fell from **210.20M** to **152.50M SYRUP** this window, but staked SYRUP was always part of the circulating count.

## Buy pressure: where new SYRUP goes

The programmatic buyback booked **0 SYRUP**, even though it is real. A vote that closed on Jul 17 2026 set the Maple buyback by revenue: 10% of a month's revenue under $1.5M, 20% between $1.5M and $2.0M, and 30% above $2.0M, bought by the 15th of the next month, for six months. In this window **2.90M SYRUP** arrived in three lots: 852,841 on Aug 5, 676,290 on Sep 8 and 1,371,166 on Oct 8 2026. The coins are held in a Maple wallet that counts as circulating, and **2.1M SYRUP** was later passed to a trading desk, so the buyback supports demand without taking coins out of the float.

The protocol fee burn booked **0 SYRUP**. Borrowers pay Maple in dollars, nothing is destroyed, and no SYRUP went to a dead address. The foundation buy row is also **0 SYRUP**: no purchase outside the monthly buyback was announced or seen on chain. New long-term lock is **0 SYRUP** too, because staking pays nothing now and staked coins count as circulating anyway.

## Foundation and overhang

The SYRUP overhang has two parts. The first is the unminted issuance, **23.2M SYRUP**, which the treasury can claim at any time; it was minted to the treasury in the past and then forwarded to the DAO safe. The second is the reserve wallet of **77.36M SYRUP**, the only balance outside the circulating count, which has only ever received coins and last did so on Dec 26 2025. Inside the float, Maple also controls the DAO safe (**23.09M**), a reserve safe (**48.62M**, unmoved) and the buyback wallet (**1.60M**). We read every one of these balances on chain each rebuild. If the reserve wallet's balance falls, or the treasury mints the pending issuance between refreshes, that outflow enters Sell #3 or Sell #1 at the next refresh.

## How SYRUP compares to other DeFi lending tokens

Most DeFi lending tokens grow their supply in one of two ways: a steady stream of reward tokens paid to users, or vesting unlocks for early teams and backers. SYRUP now has neither running. Its team and investor allocations are fully unlocked, and its issuance, which went to the treasury rather than to users, stopped on Oct 1 2026. That puts SYRUP closer to a fixed-supply token today, with the one caveat that the governor can still mint the earned **23.2M SYRUP** and could, by vote, schedule new issuance windows.

On the buy side, Maple follows the pattern of lending platforms that turn revenue into token buybacks, such as the Aave buyback, rather than the burn model some exchange tokens use. The difference is where the coins end up. A buyback that burns coins or locks them away shrinks the float; the Maple buyback keeps its SYRUP in a wallet counted as circulating, and some of it has gone back to market makers. So for supply, the Maple buyback is a steady buyer but not a shrinking force, and its size is modest: **2.90M SYRUP** in 90 days is about **0.25%** of the circulating supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the treasury claim: if the governor claims the **23.2M SYRUP** of earned issuance, total supply rises by about 1.9% in one block, and how much of it reaches the market depends on where the coins go next. Second, the monthly buybacks due around **Oct 15**, **Nov 15** and **Dec 15 2026**, and whether those coins stay in the Maple wallet or move to trading desks. Third, the Q3 ecosystem call on **Oct 13 2026**, where Maple may update its buyback plan. Fourth, the end of the six-month buyback rule in early 2027, which needs a new vote to continue. Fifth, any new vote that schedules fresh issuance windows.

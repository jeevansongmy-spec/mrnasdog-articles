# MON Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

MON supply is growing, and the big step is still ahead. Over the last 90 days **454.9M MON** entered the market — **177.2M** of block rewards that reached stakers outside the Monad Foundation and **277.7M** paid out from Foundation wallets — against a fee burn of **33.5M**, a net **+3.56%** of the **11.83B MON** float. On **Nov 24 2026**, the first anniversary of Monad mainnet, about **16.61B** locked team, investor and Category Labs coins open, so the next 90 days read **+152.04%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads MON at **+3.56%** net over the last 90 days and **+152.04%** over the next 90. The monitor reads **+0.02%** for the same trailing window, a gap of **3.55 percentage points** — well past the 0.5-point line, so a ⚠ monitor-gap note ships. The reason is plain: the circulating count the monitor divides by is a fixed number, about 11.83B MON since Apr 14 2026, so it never sees new coins arrive. We counted them on-chain, block by block. MON is a young proof-of-stake Layer 1 in its first-year lockup: mildly inflationary today, and facing an unlock larger than its whole float.

## Sell pressure: where new MON comes from

**Protocol inflation** comes from block rewards. Monad mints new MON in every block and hands it to the validator that made the block and its stakers: **25 MON a block** until the MIP-12 upgrade on **Jul 23 2026**, then **18 MON a block** as blocks sped up from 400 to 300 milliseconds. The cut kept yearly issuance near 2B MON. Across the window that made **467.9M** new MON. The Monad Foundation stakes about 11.08B MON with validators and earned **290.7M** of those rewards, which it kept; the other **177.2M** reached outside stakers. At the new rate the next 90 days mint about **462.3M**, and about **183.7M** of that reaches the market.

**Vesting unlocks** were zero in the window: every team, investor and Category Labs coin has been locked since the Nov 24 2025 launch, and locked coins cannot even be staked. That changes on **Nov 24 2026**. By Monad’s own terms, released team coins reach about 10.7% of the initial supply at the first anniversary (**10.70B**), and investors and Category Labs unlock a quarter of their holdings at the cliff (**4.92B** and **0.99B**) before monthly releases of 1/48 each. The first of those monthly releases, about **0.94B**, lands on **Dec 24 2026**, still inside the next 90 days. Together that is **17.55B MON** — larger than today’s float.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks** added **277.7M**. The Monad Foundation stewards the ecosystem pool, which was fully unlocked at launch but sits outside the circulating count. Its main wallet moved 250M MON to a payout wallet on Jul 17 2026, and its payout wallets sent a net 277.7M MON to grantees and partners over the window, in batches every few days. It has paid out like this for months, so we project the same amount forward. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy** is zero: no estate or court schedule holds MON.

## Buy pressure: where new MON goes

**The protocol fee burn** is the only buyer. Every Monad fee has a base part, at least 100 gwei per unit of gas, and that part is destroyed. On Monad you pay for the gas limit you set, not only the gas you use, which makes the burn larger than on most chains of this size. The burn removed **33.5M MON** in 90 days, about 1.4 MON a block, plus 21,441 MON sent to dead addresses. Use is rising — about 404,000 MON a day since the July upgrade and more in September — so the next 90 days project **36.4M**.

**Programmatic buyback** is zero. Category Labs, the company building Monad, has said it may buy up to $80M of MON on the market by the end of 2026, but no purchase has been disclosed. **Foundation buy** is zero too: in August 2026 the Foundation offered to buy locked MON from early investors for up to $60M, nearly all declined, and anything it bought stays locked on the same schedule — those coins never touched the market. **New long-term lock** is zero: 16.12B MON is staked, but staked coins stay in the float and can be withdrawn after a short wait.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang around MON is large. The Monad Foundation’s main wallet holds **17.14B MON**; its delegation wallets stake **11.08B** with validators and hold another 2.60B in rewards and spare coins; its payout wallets are refilled from the main wallet. The locked insider bucket — about **50.63B MON** of team, investor and Category Labs coins — starts releasing on Nov 24 2026 and runs monthly until late 2029. Some locked investor MON now belongs to the Foundation after the August 2026 program, size not disclosed. We read these wallets on-chain at every rebuild. If any of their balances falls between checks, the coins that leave go into the Foundation row at the next check.

## How MON compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1s

Most proof-of-stake Layer 1s share MON’s basic shape: steady block rewards for stakers, a fee burn that takes back a small slice, and no supply cap. Monad’s issuance is modest — about 2% of the initial supply a year — and its burn is unusually large for its age because fees are charged on the gas limit. Where MON differs is the float. Only about 12% of all coins circulate, while mature chains have most of their supply already trading, so any release looks huge next to the float.

The closest analogues are other recent launches with one-year cliffs. On those chains the first anniversary usually moves more coins than a whole year of staking rewards, and what matters is how much of the unlocked supply is sold rather than staked or held. MON’s case is sharper than most: the Nov 24 2026 release alone is about 1.4 times the circulating supply, while the Foundation keeps paying out from a reserve larger than the float.

Against chains with a fixed cap or a halving, MON has neither. Supply only falls if the fee burn outgrows issuance, which at today’s use would take roughly a thirteen-fold jump in fees paid.

Any new upgrade that changes the 18 MON block reward or the 150M block gas limit; none is scheduled today.

MON supply grew **+3.56%** over the last 90 days, as block rewards reaching outside stakers and Monad Foundation payouts outran a 33.5M MON fee burn. The next 90 days read **+152.04%**, because about 17.55B locked team, investor and Category Labs coins release on Nov 24 and Dec 24 2026 — more than today’s whole float.

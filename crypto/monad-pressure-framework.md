# MON Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

MON supply is growing, and the next 90 days bring the biggest jump since Monad launched. From Aug 19 to Nov 17 2026, **172.4M** new MON from block rewards reached stakers outside the Monad Foundation and Foundation wallets paid out **265.9M** MON, while the fee burn destroyed **42.8M** MON, for a net rise of **+3.34%**. On Nov 24 2026 the first-year lock on team and investor MON ends, about **17.35B** MON unlocks by Feb 15 2027, and the next-90-day reading is about **+150%**; other trackers show **+0.01%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 17 2026, MON supply on the market grew by **+3.34%**: about **438.3M** MON came in and **42.8M** MON was burned, against **11.83B** MON in circulation. Other trackers show **+0.01%** for the same stretch, a gap of **3.33** percentage points, so the coin page carries a warning note. Their circulating figure has sat near 11.82B MON since May 2026 and does not add new or paid-out coins, while on-chain the Monad network kept creating MON with every block. For the next 90 days the Monad reading is about **+150%**, because the Nov 24 2026 unlock alone is larger than the whole circulating supply. The label for MON today: a young chain with a small float and a very large unlock wall.

## Sell pressure: where new MON comes from

Protocol inflation is the first source. Every Monad block creates **18 MON** for the validator that made it and the people staking with that validator. Monad cut this from 25 MON to 18 MON on Jul 23 2026, when blocks became faster, so the yearly amount stayed near 2% of the first 100B MON. Between Aug 19 and Nov 17 2026 the network made 25.68M blocks and created **462.2M** MON. The Monad Foundation stakes about **15.8B** MON with validators, so **289.8M** of the new MON went to its own wallets and stayed off the market. The other **172.4M** MON went to validators and outside stakers, and that is what the Monad ledger books.

Vesting unlocks added nothing in the last 90 days, because all team and investor MON stayed locked for the first year after the Nov 24 2025 launch. That changes inside the next window. On Nov 24 2026 about **15.63B** MON unlocks: **4.93B** for investors, a quarter of their 19.7B MON, and about **10.7B** for the team. After that, investor MON unlocks in equal monthly parts and team MON is released over three more years, about **863M** MON on Dec 24 2026 and the same on Jan 24 2027. Together that is **17.35B** MON in the next 90 days. The 3.95B MON set aside for Category Labs staff unlocks into the company's own treasury, so it is not counted until it is paid out.

Foundation payouts are the third source. The Monad Foundation holds the ecosystem allocation, which was unlocked from day one and has no release dates. Its payout wallets sent **265.9M** MON to outside wallets between Aug 19 and Nov 17 2026, in sends of 15M to 30M MON every week or two, and the same pace is booked for the next 90 days. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is zero: no court or bankrupt firm holds MON for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new MON goes

The Monad fee burn is the only steady buyer. The base part of every transaction fee is destroyed, and Monad charges that fee on the gas a user reserves, not only the gas used. From Aug 19 to Nov 17 2026 the burn took **42.8M** MON out of supply, close to 475K MON a day, a little faster in the last month. That cancels about a quarter of the new MON from rewards that reached the market, and the same pace is booked forward.

There is no programmatic buyback. Category Labs, the company that builds Monad, said it may buy up to **$80M** of MON on the open market by the end of 2026, but it has not said it bought any, so the foundation-buy row stays at zero. In August 2026 the Monad Foundation offered to buy locked MON from early investors for up to $60M, and almost all of them said no; any MON it bought stays locked on the same schedule, so nothing left the market. No new long-term lock exists either: staked MON can be withdrawn, so staking takes nothing off the market for good.

## Foundation and overhang

The Monad Foundation controls the largest pool of MON outside the market. Its main ecosystem wallet holds about **9.91B** MON after it sent 7.23B MON to other Foundation wallets on Oct 23 2026. One of those, a new wallet, received **3.5B** MON that day and has not moved since. Twenty Foundation wallets stake **15.8B** MON across about two hundred validators and hold another **1.82B** MON beside it, and the payout wallets hold about **146M** MON. None of this has a published release date, and Foundation staking rewards keep adding to it.

The locked team, investor and Category Labs MON is the second overhang, about 50.65B MON in all, and its dates are known: the first big part opens on Nov 24 2026 and the last in late 2029. Each Foundation wallet is read on-chain at every rebuild; if a Foundation balance falls between rebuilds, the MON that left enters the Foundation row at the next rebuild.

## How MON compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Monad pays a fixed amount per block, like many proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains, but its yearly issuance of about 2% of the starting supply is low next to Solana, which started near 8% a year. Like Ethereum, Monad burns the base fee, yet Monad's fees are tiny, so its burn covers only about a quarter of the new MON reaching the market, far from the near-balance Ethereum sees on busy days.

What sets MON apart is the float. Only about 11.7% of all MON is unlocked, much like the young Layer 1 chains that launched with small floats, such as Sui and Aptos. On those chains the first big insider unlock was the moment supply on the market jumped. MON reaches that moment on Nov 24 2026, and the Monad unlock is unusually large: more MON opens in one day than all the MON trading today.

A large foundation treasury is common on newer chains, but the Monad Foundation's share is large too: about 31B MON when its staked coins are included. Chains with a big foundation treasury depend on how fast that treasury is spent, and for MON that pace, about 265.9M MON in 90 days, is now a larger source of new market supply than block rewards.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Nov 24 2026 is the first-year unlock: about 15.63B MON opens for the team and investors, and it is the biggest single supply event in MON's life so far. Watch how much of it moves to exchanges in the following days.

Dec 24 2026 and Jan 24 2027 bring the first monthly unlocks, about 863M MON each, which set the steady pace for the next three years.

Category Labs' plan to buy up to $80M of MON runs until the end of 2026. Any disclosed purchase, and where the bought MON goes, would change the buy side.

The Monad Foundation's payout wallets send MON out every week or two. A faster pace, or the 3.5B MON wallet filled on Oct 23 2026 starting to move, would raise the Foundation row.

## Summary

MON supply on the market grew **+3.34%** in the 90 days to Nov 17 2026, from block rewards that reached outside stakers and from Monad Foundation payouts, with a small fee burn against them. The next 90 days are very different: about **17.35B** MON of team and investor coins unlocks, starting on Nov 24 2026, about **+150%** of today's circulating supply. The key risk is selling from newly unlocked insiders into a small float, while the burn removes only about 475K MON a day. MON has no supply cap, and most of its 100.68B coins are still locked or held by the Foundation.

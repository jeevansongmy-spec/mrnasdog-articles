# MON Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

MON supply grew fast in the last 90 days, and most of the growth came on one day. On **Nov 24 2026**, Monad's first birthday, the one-year lock on team and investor coins ended and **15.61B MON** joined the market. Around it, Monad keeps creating **18 MON** with every block for stakers, and the Monad Foundation keeps paying out grants. Between Aug 29 and Nov 27 2026 new MON added up to **16.05B** against a fee burn of **44.06M**, a net rise of **+61.98%**; other trackers show **+118.16%**. MON has no supply cap.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Counted on the chain and against the schedule the project published, MON supply on the market grew **+61.98%** in the 90 days to Nov 27 2026, and the next 90 days add about **+11.44%** more as monthly unlocks begin. Other trackers show **+118.16%**, a gap of **56.18 points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. Their supply figure stood still near **11.83B MON** all quarter and then jumped **13.98B MON** on Nov 27 2026, less than the unlock itself, and it never counted the steady staking pay or grants. The label that fits Monad today is simple: an insider-unlock chain, inflationary by schedule until late 2029.

## Sell pressure: where new MON comes from

The first source is the block reward. Every Monad block creates **18 MON**, a rate set on Jul 23 2026 when blocks got faster and the reward was cut from 25 MON, so the yearly total stayed about the same. From Aug 29 to Nov 27 2026 the chain made 25.68M blocks and **462.18M** new MON. The Monad Foundation owns about 72% of all staked MON, and its share of the rewards goes back into Foundation wallets rather than onto the market. What reached validators and other stakers was **178.63M MON**, and about **176.97M** more is expected in the next 90 days.

The second source is vesting, and it dwarfs everything else this quarter. At launch on Nov 24 2025 about 50.6B MON, half of the first 100B, was locked for the team, early investors and Category Labs, the company that builds Monad. The lock ended on Nov 24 2026. That day the team received about **10.7B MON** and the investors a quarter of their 19.65B, or **4.91B MON**, for a total of **15.61B MON**. From here investors receive **409.32M MON** on the 24th of each month and team MON opens a little every day, about **2.57B MON** in the next 90 days.

The third source is the Monad Foundation spending its ecosystem fund. Its payout wallets send 15M to 30M MON every week or two to builders, incentive programs and service providers, and they paid out **255.66M MON** in this window. The fourth row, long-term locked or bankruptcy coins, is **0**: no court or failed firm is sitting on MON for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new MON goes

Monad has no programmatic buyback, so that row is **0**; no contract uses fees to buy MON back. The only built-in sink is the fee burn. The base part of every Monad fee is destroyed, and Monad charges for the full gas a transaction asks for, not just the gas it uses. From Aug 29 to Nov 27 2026 the burn removed about **44.06M MON**, close to a tenth of the new MON the blocks created and well under 1% of the unlock.

The Foundation-buy row is **0**. Category Labs said it may buy up to $80M of MON on the open market until Dec 31 2026, but no purchase has been shown, so nothing is booked. The Foundation's offer to buy locked MON from early investors closed on Aug 18 2026, before this window, and almost everyone said no; any MON it did buy stays locked on the original schedule. The new-lock row is **0** too: about 22.44B MON is staked, but staked MON can be withdrawn, so it still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The Monad Foundation holds the largest pile of MON that is not yet on the market. Its main ecosystem wallet holds **9.91B MON** after it moved 7.23B on Oct 23 2026: 3.5B into a new holding wallet that still has **3.38B MON**, and the rest into the 20 wallets it uses to stake, which now have **16.09B MON** staked. Its payout wallets keep a working balance of about 234M MON. None of these coins counts as circulating until the Foundation spends it.

Category Labs also became a large holder on Nov 24 2026, when **997M MON** of its 3.99B treasury opened for future staff pay, with 83.09M more each month. Behind them sit about 31B locked team and investor MON that will open every month until late 2029. If any Foundation or company wallet balance falls between our checks, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next check.

## How MON compares to other new Layer 1 chains

MON follows the pattern of most venture-funded Layer 1 coins launched in the last few years: a small float at launch, a large insider share under a one-year cliff, then three years of monthly releases. Aptos, for example, kept its investor and team coins under a one-year lock with monthly releases after it, and other young chains have gone through the same first-year cliff, and each saw the coins on the market jump far more than any staking reward could. What sets MON apart is the size of the first step. The Nov 24 2026 unlock alone was larger than all the MON that traded before it.

On the issuance side MON is moderate. The block reward works out to roughly 1.9B MON a year, under 2% of supply, which is lower than many proof-of-stake chains that pay 5% to 7% a year. Like Ethereum, Monad burns the base fee, but Monad traffic is cheap, so the burn takes back only about a tenth of the reward. Unlike Ethereum or Bitcoin, MON's supply story for the next three years is driven by vesting, not by mining or staking.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The first monthly investor unlock of **409.32M MON** lands on **Dec 24 2026**, followed by the same amount on **Jan 24 2027** and **Feb 24 2027**. Category Labs' up-to-$80M buyback window closes on **Dec 31 2026**; a disclosed purchase would be the first real buyer on the books. Watch how much of the newly unlocked MON moves into staking: the stake has already risen to 22.44B MON, and every coin staked by insiders also dilutes the Foundation's share of the rewards. Watch the Foundation's holding wallet with 3.38B MON, which has not spent anything since Oct 23 2026 apart from one 125M MON payment on Nov 20 2026. And watch for the Foundation's next quarterly holder report, which should cover the cliff and any MON the Foundation sold.

## Summary

MON is in its heaviest supply phase. The first-year unlock on Nov 24 2026 added **15.61B MON** for the team and investors, and with staking pay and Foundation grants net supply grew **+61.98%** in 90 days, against a fee burn of only **44.06M MON**. Monthly unlocks of about 0.86B MON for the team and investors keep supply growing by about **+11.44%** in the next 90 days and continue to late 2029. MON has no supply cap, and the Monad Foundation and Category Labs still control more than 30B MON outside the market.

# MON Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Monad (MON) supply is growing, and the next 90 days are far bigger than the last 90. Between Jul 31 and Oct 29 2026, **178.72M MON** of new block rewards reached holders outside the Monad Foundation and the Foundation's payout wallets sent out **254.83M MON**, while the base-fee burn destroyed **36.94M MON**: a net **+3.35%** of the 11.83B MON that trade today, against **+0.002%** on other trackers. Monad has no supply cap, and on **Nov 24 2026** the one-year lockup ends for about **15.63B MON** of team and investor coins, which takes the next-90-day reading to about **+149.88%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

MON supply grew **+3.35%** over the 90 days to Oct 29 2026, and the next 90 days project to **+149.88%** because team and early-investor coins start to unlock on Nov 24 2026. Other trackers put the last 90 days at **+0.002%**, a gap of about **3.35 percentage points**. That gap is not a disagreement about new MON: the circulating figure those trackers use has stayed near 11.83B MON since Apr 14 2026, so it cannot see block rewards or Foundation payouts. Because the gap stays open, the coin page carries a warning note next to its supply numbers. In one line: Monad is a young chain with steady inflation from staking pay and Foundation grants today, and an unlock wall arriving inside the next 90 days.

## Sell pressure: where new MON comes from

Protocol inflation comes first. Every Monad block mints a fixed reward for the stakers of the validator that made it. Since the Jul 23 2026 upgrade that reward is 18 MON per block, down from 25 MON, and the same upgrade cut the block time from about 400 to about 300 milliseconds, so the amount paid per day barely changed. The chain made 25.70M blocks between Jul 31 and Oct 29 2026, which minted 462.54M MON in all. But about 61% of that was earned by stake the Monad Foundation lends to validators, and the Foundation's own rules say those rewards go back into its ecosystem pool. The part that reached other holders was **178.72M MON**. The Foundation added a lot of stake on Oct 23 2026, so the outside share drops to about **154.45M MON** over the next 90 days.

Vesting unlocks are the big event. No MON unlocked in the last 90 days: all team, investor and Category Labs treasury coins have been locked since the Monad mainnet launch on Nov 24 2025, and locked MON cannot even be staked. That changes on **Nov 24 2026**, the one-year anniversary, when about **10.70B MON** of team tokens and **4.93B MON** for early investors are released, about **15.63B MON** in all. Investor coins then release in equal monthly parts over three more years, and team coins over the same three years, so about **863M MON** follows on Dec 24 2026 and again on Jan 24 2027. Together that is **17.35B MON** in the next 90 days, more than all the MON that trades today.

Foundation payouts are the second steady source. The Monad Foundation's four payout wallets sent **254.83M MON** to 45 outside wallets in the 90 days, in every month of the window, mostly to ecosystem projects. We expect about the same over the next 90 days, because the payouts have run month after month and the wallets were topped up again on Oct 23 2026. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply books zero: no court or estate holds MON waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new MON goes

The protocol fee burn is the only removal that counts. The base part of every Monad transaction fee is destroyed, and Monad charges fees on the gas a transaction asks for, not the gas it ends up using. Over the 90 days that burned about **36.94M MON**, roughly 410,000 MON a day, with the base fee at or near its 100-gwei floor almost all the time. That is about **8%** of all new block rewards. We carry the same burn forward, since the floor ties it to how much gas people use.

There is no programmatic buyback: no contract buys MON back on a fixed rule. The Foundation buy row is also zero. Category Labs, the company that builds the Monad software, said it may buy up to $80M of MON on the open market by the end of 2026, at its own choice, but it has not reported any purchase, so nothing is counted. In August 2026 the Monad Foundation also offered to buy locked MON from some early investors; most said no, and the coins it did buy keep their original unlock dates. New long-term locks book zero as well: about **20.42B MON** is staked, but staked MON can be withdrawn after a short wait, so staking does not take coins out of supply.

## Foundation and overhang

The Monad Foundation controls the largest pile of MON outside the market. Its main ecosystem wallet holds **9.91B MON**, down from 17.14B at the start of the window because on Oct 23 2026 it moved 3.57B to its validator-stake wallets, 160M to a payout wallet and **3.50B MON** to a new wallet that has not sent anything since. The Foundation also has **15.85B MON** staked with validators, and its payout wallets hold **177.24M MON** ready to pay out. None of this has a release schedule.

Two more piles follow a schedule. The Category Labs treasury, about **3.95B MON** for future staff pay, unlocks a quarter on Nov 24 2026 and the rest monthly, into the company's own hands rather than the market, so we track it here instead of counting it as an unlock. The rest of the locked team and investor block unlocks monthly until the fourth anniversary in late 2029. We re-read the Foundation wallets at every rebuild: if any of them falls between checks, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check, and if Category Labs reports buying MON, its holdings join this list.

## How MON compares to other new proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Monad's design looks like the high-speed proof-of-stake chains that launched with most of their coins locked. Like Aptos, Monad pays stakers a steady stream of newly minted coins with no supply cap; Sui, by contrast, pays stakers from a fixed supply of 10B SUI. In all three, most of the supply sat with the team, investors and a foundation on day one. The difference is the shape of the first unlock: Monad held every insider coin back for a full year and then releases a quarter of the investor allocation, plus about 40% of the team allocation, on a single day.

Against Ethereum, MON looks different on both sides of the ledger. Ethereum has no locked insider coins left, and its staking pay depends on how much ETH is staked. Monad's reward is a fixed amount per block, so yearly issuance stays near 1.88B MON at today's block pace whatever the stake, and a foundation that holds most of the stake keeps most of that pay. Both chains burn the base fee, but on Monad the burn covers under a tenth of new rewards, because fees are tiny and the base fee rarely rises above its floor.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 24 2026**: the one-year cliff, about **15.63B MON** for the team and early investors, the largest single supply event in Monad's history so far, plus the first quarter of the Category Labs treasury.

**Dec 24 2026** and **Jan 24 2027**: the first two monthly releases, about **863M MON** each.

**Dec 31 2026**: the end of the window in which Category Labs said it may buy back up to $80M of MON; any reported purchase would enter the Foundation buy row.

The Monad Foundation's wallets: the new 3.50B MON wallet filled on Oct 23 2026, the payout wallets' monthly pace, and any change to its validator stake, which moves how much of the 18 MON block reward reaches the market.

Monad (MON) supply grew **+3.35%** in the 90 days to Oct 29 2026: holders outside the Monad Foundation received **178.72M MON** of new block rewards, the Foundation paid out **254.83M MON**, and the base-fee burn removed **36.94M MON**. The next 90 days are far larger, because the one-year lockup ends on Nov 24 2026 and about **17.35B MON** of team and investor coins unlock by Jan 24 2027, more than the 11.83B MON that trade today. Monad has no supply cap, the burn covers under a tenth of new rewards, and no buyback has been reported. The main risk for holders is how much of the unlocked supply reaches the market.

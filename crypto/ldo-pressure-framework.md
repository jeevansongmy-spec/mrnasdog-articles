# LDO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply was shrinking · trend softening

Lido DAO's LDO token took supply off the market over the last 90 days: the Lido DAO treasury bought and kept **6.79M LDO** while **0 LDO** was added, a net change of **−0.82%** of the **829.57M** LDO in circulation. The next 90 days flip the sign. On **Jan 1 2027** a one-year lock on **48.93M LDO** ends in a single step, against about **5.92M LDO** of planned buybacks, so LDO supply is projected to grow about **+5.19%**. LDO has a fixed 1,000,000,000 supply and no emission; every change in its float comes from the DAO treasury and that one lock.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the net change in LDO supply was **−0.82%**, and over the next 90 days it is projected at **+5.19%**. Our inflation monitor read **−1.56%** for its own 90-day window, a gap of **0.75** percentage points. We walked that gap down and it closes on timing alone: the monitor's window starts a few days earlier than ours and so includes a **6.47M LDO** buyback delivery that reached the treasury on Jul 8 2026, one day before our window opens. Counted over the same days, the on-chain treasury balance gives the same answer as the monitor, so no warning chip is shown. LDO is a **fixed-supply governance token whose float is set by its own DAO treasury**: shrinking while the treasury buys, and growing when a lock it controls opens.

## Sell pressure: where new LDO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0 LDO**. LDO is a governance token with no staking reward, no block reward and no emission schedule; total supply read exactly 1,000,000,000 LDO at both ends of the window. That is a policy, not a hard cap: the Lido DAO controls the token through its token manager and can mint or burn by vote, and it used that power once in December 2025, when it burned and re-issued hacked coins with total supply left unchanged.

Vesting unlocks were **0 LDO** in the last 90 days, but they are the whole story of the next 90. In December 2025 a large LDO holder lost control of a wallet to a seed-phrase thief. The Lido DAO froze the coins, then on Jan 1 2026 handed **48.93M LDO** back across ten new wallets under a one-year lock with no partial release. That lock ends on **Jan 1 2027**, inside the next 90-day window, and all **48.93M LDO** becomes free to move on the same day — about **5.9%** of today's circulating LDO. The DAO can still cancel the lock by vote, but nothing on the forum asks it to. The original 2020 team and investor vesting finished years ago.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks were **0 LDO**. The Lido DAO treasury sent out no LDO in the window; its last outflow was on Mar 2 2026, after paying out **7.69M LDO** over the previous year, mostly to the contributor reward plan. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0 LDO**: no estate or trustee holds LDO.

## Buy pressure: where new LDO goes

The programmatic buyback took **6,354 LDO**. Lido's automatic buyback went live on Aug 14 2026 and sends half of yearly staking income above **$40M** into LDO for the treasury, capped at $50,000 a day and $10M a year. Its only fill was a one-stETH test order on Aug 13 2026. Staking income has stayed under the line since launch and its running budget is negative, so we project **0 LDO** for the next 90 days unless ETH rises well above its current level.

There is no protocol fee burn: Lido staking fees reach the treasury in stETH, the dead address held the same 5.25 LDO at both ends, and total supply did not fall. The foundation buy is the big row: **6.73M LDO**. In spring 2026 the Lido DAO approved spending up to 10,000 stETH to buy LDO in 1,000-stETH rounds run by its Growth Committee, with every coin bought returned to the treasury. Two deliveries landed in our window, **2.15M LDO** on Aug 25 and **4.58M LDO** on Sep 25 2026, and the on-chain treasury inflows match the committee's published reports to the cent. Round four, 1,000 stETH, was drawn on Oct 2 2026 and may run to Nov 24 2026 under a price limit; at today's LDO-to-ETH price it buys about **5.92M LDO**. About 7,000 stETH of the budget remains after it.

New long-term locks are **0 LDO**: LDO has no staking or lock that removes coins from the float. One extra row adds **56,513 LDO**, an unvested contributor grant that was cancelled on Jul 31 2026 and sent back to the treasury.

## Foundation and overhang

The Lido DAO treasury is the main overhang. It holds **121.50M LDO**, up from 114.70M at the start of the window because every buyback lands there, and none of it counts as circulating. Its balance is read on-chain every day. A contingent market-making loan of up to **7.5M LDO** from the treasury was approved on Sep 21 2026 to protect exchange listings; it is not switched on, and if it is, those coins would enter the float. The ten re-locked wallets hold the **48.93M LDO** described above and are watched the same way. The contributor reward multisig holds about 4.0M LDO, but those coins already count as circulating. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How LDO compares to other DeFi governance tokens

LDO sits in the class of fixed-supply DeFi governance tokens whose DAO treasury buys the token with protocol income. The closest mechanism peers are lending and liquid-staking tokens that run buybacks from fees; the difference is that most of them buy on a fixed schedule or a set share of revenue, while LDO's two buyback routes are slower by design. The automatic route only fires when staking income clears a fixed cost line, and the stETH program buys only below a published LDO-to-ETH price.

LDO also keeps what it buys rather than burning it. A burn takes coins out for good; a treasury buyback takes them out of the float only as long as the treasury holds them, which is why the market-making loan and future contributor payouts are listed as overhangs. Compared with tokens that still have years of team and investor unlocks, LDO's original vesting ended long ago; its one remaining cliff is the 48.93M LDO hack-recovery lock, a single dated event rather than a monthly drip.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 24 2026**: the end of buyback round four. If the LDO-to-ETH price stays above the round's limit, fewer than 5.92M LDO will be bought, and the next round's terms will show the pace for the rest of the 7,000 stETH budget.

**Jan 1 2027**: the one-year lock on 48.93M LDO ends. Any Lido DAO vote to cancel or extend it before then would change the next-90-day number more than anything else.

The market-making loan: a switch-on would move up to 7.5M LDO from the treasury into exchange books. The automatic buyback: a forum proposal from Oct 7 2026 asks to tie its trigger line to the yearly budget, which would let it start buying sooner. The contributor reward plan: any new treasury top-up would show as a Sell #3 outflow.

# XDC Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

XDC supply barely moved in the 90 days to Nov 14 2026, up about **+0.09%**, but the next 90 days should bring about **+4.31%**, because the yearly release for the founders and the ecosystem fund is due on Feb 5 2027. XDC Network pays its validators a fixed **5,000 XDC** of new coins every epoch, which came to **18.22M XDC** in the window, while nothing is burned or bought back; other trackers read **+0.01%** for the same 90 days. There is no supply cap, and only about half of the 38.07B XDC in existence is unlocked today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 16 to Nov 14 2026 the XDC supply rose by a net **+0.09%**. Other trackers show **+0.01%** for the same window, a difference of **less than a tenth of a percentage point**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days look very different: the validator pay keeps adding about 18.22M XDC, and a release of about **841.18M XDC** lands on Feb 5 2027, which together come to about **+4.31%** of today's circulating supply. In one line: XDC is a quiet chain most of the year, with one large supply step each February.

## Sell pressure: where new XDC comes from

Protocol inflation added **18,215,000 XDC** in the 90 days. XDC Network runs 108 validators, and at the end of every epoch, about every 36 minutes, the chain creates exactly 5,000 new XDC for them, with 10% of it going to the network's foundation wallet. There were 3,643 epochs between Aug 16 and Nov 14 2026. The reward has no halving and no end date, and the planned change to how nodes are paid has no date on the main network yet, so the next 90 days repeat the same 18.22M XDC.

Vesting unlocks were **zero** in the last 90 days, and they are the big item in the next 90. Once a year a batch opens for the founders and the ecosystem fund. The last one, on Feb 5 2026, freed **841.18M XDC**: 441.18M for the founders, advisors and team, and 400M for the ecosystem fund. The next one is due on Feb 5 2027 at about the same size, so the full **841.18M XDC** is booked in the next 90 days. No lock contract holds these coins; the release happens on the schedule, and the coins sit in the project's own wallets.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **zero**. The project's ecosystem payout wallet sent out 300M XDC between Aug 16 and Nov 14 2026 to pay node runners and partners, and on Oct 29 2026 the founders and team reserve moved 1B XDC into three new wallets. The payouts use coins that were already counted as circulating, and the 1B moved from one locked reserve to new wallets that have not spent a single coin, so neither adds new supply.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **zero**. No court case or failed firm holds XDC waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new XDC goes

The programmatic buyback is **zero**. No contract or treasury buys XDC back; the project spends XDC to grow the network.

The protocol fee burn is **zero**. Since the Jan 30 2026 upgrade every XDC transaction carries a base fee, as on Ethereum, but on XDC Network the whole fee, base fee included, is paid to the validator that made the block. Nothing is destroyed, and the burn address held the same tiny balance at both ends of the window.

Foundation buying is **zero**. Neither the foundation nor the founders buy XDC back; their wallets only send coins out.

New long-term locks are **zero**. Each validator stakes 10M XDC, and the staking pool grew from about 3.36B to 3.71B XDC in the window, but staked XDC still counts as circulating, so a bigger stake does not take coins out of the count.

## Foundation and overhang

Most of the XDC that is not yet unlocked sits in three reserves funded when the network launched. The founders and team reserve holds **12.45B XDC**, down from 13.45B XDC after it sent 1B XDC on Oct 29 2026 to three new wallets, which hold 450M, 450M and 100M XDC and have not moved since. Two more launch reserves hold **3.61B XDC** and **1.23B XDC**, and neither moved in the 90 days.

Two wallets are already part of the circulating supply. The ecosystem payout wallet holds **575.6M XDC** and sends 20M to 50M XDC every week or two to pay node runners and partners. The foundation wallet collects its 10% of every epoch's reward and passes it on, so it is close to empty most of the time. If one of the three reserves sends coins that reach the market between two checks, that outflow joins the foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check.

## How XDC compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

XDC sits between two common designs. Like Ethereum, it has no cap and pays validators in new coins forever. Unlike Ethereum, it pays a fixed amount per epoch no matter how much is staked, and it burns none of its fees, so there is nothing to offset the new supply. At about 18.22M XDC every 90 days, that pay is small next to the circulating supply, well under half a percent a year.

Where XDC differs most from a mature chain such as Ethereum is the locked half of its supply. Only about 52% of the 38.07B XDC is unlocked, and the rest comes out on a yearly schedule set at launch, more like a young network with team and investor vesting than a chain whose coins are all already out. That makes XDC's supply growth lumpy: almost flat for eleven months, then one step of about 841M XDC each February.

Chains that burn part of every fee, such as Ethereum and BNB Chain, can offset new coins when the network is busy. XDC has no such brake today: busier use pays validators more, but it never removes XDC from supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Feb 5 2027: the yearly release for the founders and the ecosystem fund, about 841.18M XDC, which alone accounts for nearly all of the projected +4.31%.

January 2027: the team targets this month for paying protector and observer nodes straight from the chain instead of from the ecosystem wallet, but no date is set on the main network yet; if it ships, the pay would come from new coins instead of reserves already counted.

Any date in the window: the three new wallets that took 1B XDC from the founders and team reserve on Oct 29 2026. If those coins start moving to exchanges, the foundation and unscheduled unlocks row turns on.

Every week or two: the ecosystem payout wallet, which has been sending 20M to 50M XDC at a time to fund node runners and partners and held 575.6M XDC on Nov 14 2026.

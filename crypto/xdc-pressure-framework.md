# XDC Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

XDC supply is roughly steady: the network made **18.25M** new XDC in the 90 days to Nov 4 2026, burned nothing and bought nothing back, so supply grew about **+0.09%**, and the next 90 days look the same. New coins come from one fixed source, **5,000 XDC** every epoch paid to the nodes that run the chain, and there is no supply cap. Other trackers show about **−0.01%** for the same period, close to our reading.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 6 to Nov 4 2026, XDC's net new supply came to **+0.09%** of the **19.95B** XDC that circulate. Other trackers put the same 90 days at **−0.01%**, a difference of about **0.10 points**, and the coin page carries no warning note. Every bit of that growth is the epoch reward, which does not change from one quarter to the next, so the next 90 days should add about the same **18.25M** XDC. The short label: a quiet chain with a fixed, small mint and large reserves held off the market.

## Sell pressure: where new XDC comes from

Protocol inflation is the only live source. XDC runs on delegated proof of stake with **108** masternodes, and at the end of every epoch of 900 rounds the network creates exactly **5,000 XDC**: 90% goes to the owners of the masternodes that signed, and 10% goes to a network reward wallet. There were **3,649** epochs between Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026, so the network made **18,245,000 XDC**, about 203,000 a day. The reward does not halve, and it is paid per epoch, so when rounds run slow the epochs simply take longer and fewer XDC are made each day.

Vesting unlocks booked zero. XDC has no on-chain vesting contract; its team and ecosystem allocations sit in reserve wallets, and the project lists one yearly release for them. The last one came on Feb 5 2026 and the next is listed for **Feb 5 2027**, three days after the coming 90-day window closes.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks also booked zero. The biggest event in the window was on Oct 29 2026, when the founders and team reserve sent **1B XDC** to three brand-new wallets, in amounts of 450M, 450M and 100M. None of those wallets has spent a coin, so nothing reached the market. The project's payout wallet also sent 260M XDC to its spending wallets, and they paid node runners **55.71M XDC** in monthly rewards: 66,666 XDC to each standby node and 22,500 XDC extra to each masternode. Those coins already counted as circulating before they moved, so they add nothing to the total.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is zero. No court estate, bankruptcy pile or long lock of XDC is waiting to be released.

## Buy pressure: where new XDC goes

Nothing buys XDC back for the network, so the programmatic buyback row is zero. The fee burn is zero too. XDC turned on a base fee in January 2026, and some summaries describe it as burned, but on the chain the whole fee, base fee included, is paid to the owner of the node that made the block. That owner's balance rises by exactly the fees paid, so no coins disappear.

The foundation buy row is zero: the foundation does not buy XDC on the market, and its venture arm invests in companies such as the payments firm ScallopX rather than in the coin. The new long-term lock row is zero as well. Node stakes grew by **380.2M XDC** to **3.69B** as more standby nodes joined, but staked XDC can be withdrawn and still counts as circulating, so the growing stake removes nothing from supply.

## Foundation and overhang

Almost half of all XDC sits outside the market. The founders and team reserve holds **12.45B XDC** after the Oct 29 2026 transfer, and the three wallets that received that transfer hold **1B XDC** between them. Two older reserves, set up at the network's launch, hold **3.61B** and **1.23B XDC** and have not moved in this window. Together these hold about 18.28B XDC.

A second group of wallets pays the network's bills. The project's payout wallet holds **615.6M XDC**, down from 875.6M at the start of the window, and it tops up the spending wallets that pay node rewards each month. The network reward wallet that collects 10% of every epoch's mint is swept out every few weeks and held almost nothing on Nov 4 2026. If any reserve's balance falls and the coins reach outside hands, that outflow enters the foundation row at the next rebuild.

## How XDC compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

XDC's mint is a flat amount per epoch rather than a share of the stake. That is a different shape from Ethereum, where validator pay grows with the amount staked and a fee burn takes part of it back. On XDC, adding more stake does not raise the mint, and with no burn, nothing pulls the other way. The result is a small, very steady increase of under 0.1% a quarter.

The bigger difference is custody. Many proof-of-stake chains hold team and ecosystem coins in on-chain vesting contracts that release on a timer anyone can read. XDC holds them in ordinary reserve wallets, with a yearly release that the project announces rather than code that enforces. That makes the reserves the real swing factor for XDC supply: the mint is tiny next to an 18.28B XDC overhang that moves only when the project decides to move it.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the three wallets that received **1B XDC** on Oct 29 2026: if they send coins to exchanges or outside buyers, that becomes new circulating supply. Second, the monthly node payments, likely around Dec 1 2026, Jan 1 2027 and Feb 1 2027, each about 19M XDC from the project's spending wallets.

Third, the XDC 3.0 reward change, now targeted for January 2027, which would pay masternodes, protector nodes and observer nodes a fixed amount each from the protocol instead of the shared 5,000 XDC per epoch; no mainnet date has been set, and it could raise or lower the mint. Fourth, the yearly team and ecosystem release listed for **Feb 5 2027**, just after this window, which will show up in the next rebuild.

## Summary

XDC Network adds a fixed 5,000 XDC per epoch, which came to 18.25M XDC, or about +0.09%, in the 90 days to Nov 4 2026, with nothing burned and nothing bought back. Supply is uncapped but grows slowly, and the next 90 days should look the same. The real risk is the 18.28B XDC held in project reserves, including 1B XDC moved to new wallets on Oct 29 2026, which can reach the market whenever the project chooses. A reward change planned for January 2027 and the next yearly release on Feb 5 2027 are the two dates most likely to change this reading.

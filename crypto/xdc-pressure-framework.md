# XDC Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

XDC had a quiet 90 days and faces a heavy next 90. From Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026 the XDC Network created about **17.91M XDC** for its masternodes, a fixed 5,000 XDC every epoch with no cap, and burned nothing, so supply grew about **+0.09%**. The yearly founders and ecosystem release of about **841.18M XDC** is due around Feb 5 2027, which lifts the next 90 days to about **+4.31%**; other trackers show **+0.01%** for the last 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Net supply grew **+0.09%** in the last 90 days and should grow about **+4.31%** in the next 90. Other trackers show **+0.01%** for the same 90 days, a small difference of **0.08 points**, and the coin page carries no warning note. The label is “mixed last 90 days, projected to grow”: the steady part, the masternode reward, is small at under a tenth of a percent a quarter, while the yearly release is big and lands in one step. Nothing on the other side takes XDC away, because XDC has no burn and no buyback. So the whole XDC story for the next three months comes down to one date in early February 2027.

## Sell pressure: where new XDC comes from

**Protocol inflation** came to **17.91M XDC**. The XDC Network pays a flat 5,000 XDC at the end of every epoch, and an epoch is 900 rounds of block making, about 36 minutes at today’s pace. From Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026 there were **3,581** epochs, close to 199K new XDC a day. Nine tenths goes to the masternodes that signed blocks in that epoch and the last tenth, 500 XDC an epoch, goes to a network reward wallet. The reward has no halving and no cap on the main network, and the same pace carries into the next 90 days.

**Vesting unlocks** were zero in the last 90 days and are about **841.18M XDC** in the next 90. Once a year a block of the team’s locked coins is counted as released: 441.18M XDC for the founders and team and 400M XDC for ecosystem work. The last one came on Feb 5 2026 and the next is due around Feb 5 2027, though one tracker puts it on Jan 1 2027; both dates fall inside the next 90 days. No contract holds these coins. They sit in ordinary team wallets, so the release is a promise kept by the team, not a lock that opens by itself.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks** booked zero. On Oct 29 2026 the team’s largest reserve wallet sent **1B XDC** to three new team wallets, 450M, 450M and 100M, and none of it has moved since. A separate payout wallet sent 300M XDC in the window, mostly to set up new nodes. Those coins either stayed with the team or were already counted as circulating, so they add no new XDC. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy** is also zero: no court or bankrupt firm holds XDC for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new XDC goes

**Programmatic buyback** is zero: no contract or treasury buys XDC back from the market.

**Protocol fee burn** is zero. A base fee arrived with the Jan 30 2026 upgrade, but the XDC Network still hands the whole fee, base fee included, to the masternode that made the block. Fees are tiny in any case, about **32K XDC** in base fees over the 90 days, so even a burn would barely show.

**Foundation buy** is zero: neither the team nor the XDC Foundation has a plan to buy XDC on the market. **New long-term lock** is zero too. Running a masternode locks 10M XDC, and the node contract grew from **3.46B** to **3.82B XDC** in the window, the same weeks MoneyGram, Taurus and Amber joined as node runners. Node stakes still count as circulating, so a bigger stake takes nothing out of the count.

## Foundation and overhang

The team’s launch reserves hold about **17.28B XDC**, close to the 18.12B XDC that does not count as circulating. The largest reserve wallet holds **12.45B XDC**, after the 1B it sent out on Oct 29 2026. Two older launch reserves hold **3.61B** and **1.23B XDC** and have not moved in years. The three new wallets from Oct 29 hold **1B XDC** between them, untouched. A payout wallet that funds nodes and partners holds **525.6M XDC** after sending 300M in the window. Any of these could sell at any time, and none of them is held back by a contract, so they are the coins to watch on the explorer.

## How XDC compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

XDC sits at the gentle end of steady issuance. Its masternode reward works out to well under half a percent a year, far below the several percent that many proof-of-stake chains pay to keep validators staked. Where XDC differs is the shape of its unlocks: most chains that still have team coins locked release them in monthly or quarterly slices, while XDC releases one large block a year. That makes XDC’s supply look flat for most of the year and then jump in one step.

XDC also has no fee burn at all, unlike chains that destroy part of every fee. With fees this small a burn would not change much, but it means nothing offsets the yearly release. And the masternode set is fixed at 108 block makers, with firms such as MoneyGram, Taurus, Amber and DSRV joining as node runners this autumn, so new stake grows the locked node contract rather than taking coins out of circulation.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Jan 1 2027 and Feb 5 2027:** the yearly founders and ecosystem release of about **841.18M XDC**. One tracker dates it Jan 1, the last one came on Feb 5, and either way it is the single largest supply event of the quarter.

**The 1B XDC moved on Oct 29 2026:** the three new team wallets still hold **450M**, **450M** and **100M XDC**. If those coins start moving to exchanges, that is new selling the yearly count does not show.

**The XDC 3.0 reward change:** a new payout model that pays masternodes, protector nodes and observer nodes separately, and pays them by how many blocks they really signed, has been running on the test network since late October 2026. It has no date on the main network yet; when it gets one, the 5,000 XDC per epoch figure can change.

**New node runners:** each new masternode locks 10M XDC. More firms joining after MoneyGram, Taurus and Amber would grow the node contract again, though staked coins still count as circulating.

## Summary

XDC’s supply grew only **+0.09%** from Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026, from a flat masternode reward of 5,000 XDC per epoch with nothing burned. The next 90 days look very different at about **+4.31%**, because the yearly release of about **841.18M XDC** for the founders, team and ecosystem is due around Feb 5 2027. The team still holds about **17.28B XDC** in launch reserves, and its biggest wallet sent 1B XDC to new team wallets on Oct 29 2026. XDC has no burn and no buyback, so nothing in the system offsets these releases.

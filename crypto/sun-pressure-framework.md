# SUN Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

<!-- main-page -->
This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about SUN, see [mrnasdog.com/research/sun](https://mrnasdog.com/research/sun).

SUN supply is roughly steady and edging down: from Aug 29 to Nov 27 2026 no new SUN was created, and the SUN.io fee buyback burned **7.23M SUN**, so the circulating count fell by about **0.04%**. SUN has a hard ceiling of **19.90B** coins with no way to mint more, and the only thing that moves the count is the quarterly buyback burn, paid for by trading fees on SunSwap, SunPump and SunX. Other trackers show **-0.08%** for the same 90 days, close enough that the coin page carries no warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 27 2026, SUN supply moved by **-0.04%**: nothing was added and **7,232,977 SUN** was burned out of about **19.21B** circulating. Other trackers read **-0.08%** for the same stretch, a difference of **0.04 points**, so no warning note is shown on the SUN coin page. The next 90 days should look much the same, about **-0.04%**, with one more burn of around **8.51M SUN** expected in late January 2027. The cite-able label for SUN today: a fixed-supply DeFi token with a small, steady fee burn.

## Sell pressure: where new SUN comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0.** The SUN token contract on TRON has only the standard functions for moving and approving coins. It has no mint function, no owner and no upgrade path, so the total of **19,900,730,000 SUN** set at the May 2021 redenomination can never rise.

**Vesting unlocks: 0.** SUN had no team, investor or private-sale allocation. It was handed out through liquidity mining, governance mining, an airdrop and the SUN DAO share, and that distribution ran from June 2021 to June 2026. It ended before this window, and every SUN that is not burned already counts as circulating, so no locked batch is waiting to open.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** There is no reserve that sits outside the circulating count. Large holders do exist, and some of them moved SUN during the window, but those coins were already counted, so a move from one SUN wallet to another adds nothing new to the market count.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court case or failed company is holding SUN for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new SUN goes

**Programmatic buyback: 7.23M SUN in the last 90 days.** The SUN.io buyback takes 0.05% of every SunSwap V2 trade, one sixth of the fees from the busiest SunSwap V3 pool (added on May 2 2026), all of SunPump's protocol revenue and half of SunX's net revenue. That money buys SUN about once a month, and once a quarter the pile is sent to a dead wallet on TRON that no one can spend from. The round-52 burn on Oct 27 2026 sent **7,232,977 SUN** there, covering trading from Jul 25 to Oct 25 2026. The SUN.io buyback has now burned **685.78M SUN** since December 2021, about 3.4% of all SUN ever made.

For the next 90 days we expect one more SUN burn, for trading from Oct 25 2026 to Jan 25 2027. The fee money behind the last round would buy about **8.51M SUN** at today's lower price, and the pending-burn wallet already holds **2.18M SUN** for it from the October purchases.

**Protocol fee burn: 0.** SUN.io has no separate fee burn; the fee shares above are the money behind the buyback, so they are counted once. No other SUN reached the dead wallet between Aug 29 and Nov 27 2026.

**Foundation buy: 0.** No team or foundation buys SUN outside the fee buyback, and none has said it plans to.

**New long-term lock: 0.** About **459.46M SUN** is locked as veSUN to vote on SUN.io and boost mining rewards, but locked SUN still counts as circulating, so the veSUN lock takes nothing out of the count.

## Foundation and overhang

SUN.io runs one wallet that matters for supply: the pending-burn wallet, which collects the SUN bought with fees between burns. It held **2.18M SUN** on Nov 27 2026, all of it bought on Oct 30 2026, and it empties into the dead wallet at each quarterly burn. If the pending-burn wallet's balance ever falls without a matching burn, that outflow moves into the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check.

Beyond SUN.io's own wallet, eight large wallets with no public owner hold about **15.36B SUN**, roughly four in every five circulating SUN. Most of them did not move at all in the window; one received 441.31M SUN in November 2026 from another large holder. Because all of these coins already count as circulating, a sale from any of them would change who holds SUN, not how much SUN exists. It is still the biggest thing a SUN holder should know: the float is very concentrated.

## How SUN compares to other DeFi exchange tokens

Among exchange-platform tokens, SUN sits with the fixed-supply, fee-buyback group. Like CAKE after its supply cap and like JST on the same TRON network, SUN uses platform revenue to buy its own coin and burn it, but unlike CAKE there is no ongoing emission for farming left to offset, because the SUN distribution finished in June 2026. That makes the SUN burn a pure reduction, even if a small one.

Compared with governance tokens that still pay emissions to liquidity providers, such as CRV, SUN prints nothing, so its supply cannot drift upward between burns. Compared with tokens whose burn is large against their supply, the SUN burn is modest: about 0.04% of circulating SUN per quarter, because only a slice of SunSwap fees and part of SunX revenue goes to it, and SunPump's contribution has not grown since late 2025.

The real difference between SUN and most DeFi tokens is ownership, not mechanism: about four fifths of circulating SUN sits in eight wallets with no public owner. The supply count is clean and shrinking, but how the float behaves depends on a handful of holders.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Late January 2027:** the round-53 SUN buyback burn for trading from Oct 25 2026 to Jan 25 2027; we expect about **8.51M SUN**, and a smaller or larger round moves the next reading directly.

**Monthly SUN purchases into the pending-burn wallet** at the end of November and December 2026: their size shows whether SunSwap and SunX fee income is holding up before the burn.

**The eight large SUN wallets:** any sizeable transfer to an exchange would not add SUN, but it would show a major holder getting ready to sell.

**SUN DAO votes on fee shares:** a change to how much of SunSwap, SunPump or SunX revenue goes to the buyback would change the burn size for every quarter after it.

## Summary

SUN supply is roughly steady and slowly shrinking: no SUN was created in the 90 days to Nov 27 2026, and the SUN.io fee buyback burned **7.23M SUN**, a net change of **-0.04%**. SUN has a hard cap of **19.90B** with no mint function, and its distribution finished in June 2026, so the quarterly burn is the only thing that moves the count. The next SUN burn, expected in late January 2027, should remove about **8.51M SUN**. The main risk is not new supply but concentration: eight wallets with no public owner hold about 15.36B SUN.

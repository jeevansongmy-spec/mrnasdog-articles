# SUN Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

<!-- main-page -->
This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about SUN, see [mrnasdog.com/research/sun](https://mrnasdog.com/research/sun).

SUN supply is roughly steady and edging down: no new SUN can be created, and a buyback paid for by SUN.io's trading products destroys a few million coins every quarter. Between Jul 31 and Oct 29 2026 nothing new came in and **7,232,977 SUN** were burned, so the circulating count of **19.21B** fell by **0.04%**. Other trackers show **−0.04%** as well, and the next burn, about **6.41M SUN**, is due around Jan 25 2027.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 29 2026, SUN's circulating supply changed by **−0.04%**: zero new coins against **7.23M SUN** destroyed in one quarterly burn on Oct 27 2026. Other trackers read **−0.04%** for the same 90 days, a difference of less than **0.01** percentage points, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days we expect about the same: no new supply, and one burn of roughly **6.41M SUN**, a fall of about **0.03%**. The one-line label for SUN is a fixed-supply token with a slow, revenue-funded burn.

## Sell pressure: where new SUN comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero**, and it cannot change. The SUN token contract on TRON contains only the standard functions for moving and approving coins; it has no mint function, no owner and no way to be upgraded. All **19.90B SUN** were created once, at the May 2021 switch that turned every old SUN into 1,000 new ones, and the total has stayed exactly there ever since.

Vesting unlocks are **zero**. SUN.io says the coin never had a team share, an investor round or a private sale. Its coins went out through mining programs and a SUN DAO pool of **47.16%** that was released over five years, from July 2021 to June 2026. That release finished before this window began, and every SUN that has not been burned already counts as circulating.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **zero**. There is no treasury held outside the circulating count, so when project or DAO wallets move SUN, the coins were already counted. The only coins that sit outside the count are the ones already burned. Long-term locks and bankruptcy releases are also **zero**: no court case, estate or locked pile of SUN waits to be released.

## Buy pressure: where new SUN goes

The programmatic buyback is the only flow on SUN's chart, at **7,232,977 SUN** in this window. SUN.io sets aside income from four products: 0.05% of every SunSwap V2 trade, one sixth of the fees from SunSwap V3's main TRX and USDT pool since May 2 2026, all of the protocol income from SunPump, and half of the net income from the SunX trading platform. That money buys SUN on the market, the coins wait in a burn wallet, and once a quarter they are sent to a black-hole address on TRON where no one can ever move them. Round 52, covering Jul 25 to Oct 25 2026, was burned on Oct 27 2026, and it brought the total ever destroyed to **685.78M SUN**, about **3.4%** of everything created.

The burn is getting smaller. Round 50 in April destroyed 18.84M SUN, round 51 in July destroyed 9.03M, and round 52 destroyed 7.23M. The income collected over the last 90 days buys about **6.41M SUN** at today's price, and that is what we book for the next burn.

The protocol fee burn is **zero** as a separate row, because the trading fee share meant for SUN is first used to buy SUN; it is already inside the buyback and is not counted twice. Foundation buying is **zero**: no company or foundation buys SUN for itself. New long-term locks are also **zero**. Holders can lock SUN for 26 weeks up to four years to vote and share in fees, and about **475.57M SUN** sits in that vault, but locked SUN still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

SUN has no team or foundation overhang in the usual sense, because nothing sits outside the circulating count except burned coins. The burn wallet that collects bought-back SUN between burns held **0 SUN** after the Oct 27 2026 burn, and fills again month by month until the next one. If that wallet ever sends SUN anywhere other than the black hole, those coins would show up as supply on the coin page at the next check.

Ownership is still very concentrated. The eight largest wallets hold about **14.92B SUN**, roughly three quarters of everything in circulation, and none has a public owner. Across the whole window they sent only **1.2M SUN**. Because these coins already count as circulating, a sale from them would add no new supply, but it would add real selling, so we read their balances at every check.

## How SUN compares to other DeFi exchange tokens

Most exchange and lending tokens still pay new coins to liquidity providers or stakers and then burn part of their fees back, so their net supply depends on which side is bigger in a given month. SUN sits in the smaller group whose supply can only go down: the mint path is gone for good, the distribution has finished, and the one remaining flow is a buyback that removes coins. In that sense SUN behaves more like a fixed-cap coin with a slow burn than like a farm token.

The trade-off is size. A burn of a few million SUN a quarter removes only a few hundredths of a percent of supply, so the burn alone cannot move the coin much. Tokens that send most of their fees to buybacks, and whose fee income is large next to their value, shrink many times faster. For SUN, the size of each burn rises and falls with trading on SunSwap and SunX, so the burn tells you as much about how busy SUN.io's products are as it does about supply.

Next to TRX, the coin of the chain it lives on, SUN is the simpler case: TRX supply moves with network fees and new block rewards, while SUN has no rewards at all, only the burn.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Round 53 of the buyback should be burned around **Jan 25 2027**, covering Oct 25 2026 to Jan 25 2027; we expect about **6.41M SUN** at today's price, and a smaller round would confirm that trading income is still cooling.

The monthly top-ups to the burn wallet, which arrived around the end of August and September in this window, are the earliest sign of how big round 53 will be; a quiet November or December would mean a smaller burn.

SunPump's share has not grown in the last two rounds, so new income now comes mostly from SunSwap and SunX; a revival of SunPump launches would be the quickest way for the burn to grow again.

Any SUN DAO vote that changes how much income goes to the buyback, or adds a new product to it, would change the next burn directly.

A large transfer out of one of the eight biggest wallets to an exchange would not change supply, but it would be the first sign of heavy selling.

## Summary

SUN has a fixed supply of 19.90B coins that no contract can add to, and its circulating count of 19.21B only goes down. In the 90 days to Oct 29 2026 a revenue-funded buyback burned 7,232,977 SUN, cutting supply by about 0.04%, and other trackers agree. The next quarterly burn, about 6.41M SUN, is due around Jan 25 2027, and the burn has been shrinking for three rounds as trading income cools. The main risk is not new supply but concentration: eight wallets hold about 14.92B SUN, and any sale from them would land on the market even though it adds no new coins.

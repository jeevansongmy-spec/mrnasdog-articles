## Sell pressure: where new JASMY comes from

The JASMY token on Ethereum created all 50B coins in a single step when it was deployed on Dec 26 2019, and its code has only the standard transfer and approval functions: there is no mint function, no owner and no upgrade path, so no one at Jasmy can add coins. This is a property of the token contract itself, which is why this row carries a permanent tag on the JASMY page.

Vesting unlocks are **0 JASMY**. The investor and incentive schedules for JASMY started in late 2021 and ended in 2023, and unlock trackers list no future JASMY unlocks. About **98.89%** of all JASMY is already counted as circulating.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0 JASMY** for the window. The only JASMY outside the circulating count is the original issuer wallet, which held **555.0M JASMY** on Jul 22 2026 and the same amount on Oct 20 2026. Its last outflow was **50M JASMY** on Jan 16 2025, and the one before that was 95M on Sep 27 2024, sent to an exchange deposit address. There is no published schedule for the rest, and nothing left the wallet in the past year, so nothing is projected for the next 90 days. A large smart wallet did send **93.5M JASMY** to an exchange on Sep 29 2026, but those coins were already counted as circulating, so the move added no new supply.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0 JASMY**. No bankruptcy estate, court trustee or long-term lock holds JasmyCoin. The exchanges that delisted JASMY in Korea in September 2026, and the Japanese exchange closing JASMY by Oct 28 2026, are giving coins back to their own users; those coins were in the float before and stay in it after.

## Foundation and overhang

JasmyCoin has one tracked overhang: the original issuer wallet, which received all 50B JASMY at launch and still holds **555.0M JASMY**, about **1.11%** of total supply. It is exactly the part of JASMY that is not counted as circulating. Its history is sporadic: large distributions at launch, a run of weekly tranches of 40M to 700M JASMY from June to November 2023, then 95M in September 2024 and 50M in January 2025, almost all of it sent to the same exchange deposit address. We read this wallet on-chain at every rebuild.

Other big JASMY wallets — exchange hot wallets, the four largest holders at about 3.5B to 3.6B each, and the smart wallet that moved 93.5M on Sep 29 2026 — are already inside the circulating count, so a sale from them shifts coins between holders without adding supply. If the issuer wallet's balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How JASMY compares to other IoT and data-network tokens

Most tokens tied to IoT and data networks pay for their network with new coins. Helium (HNT) mints new HNT on a schedule to reward hotspots and burns HNT when people buy data credits, so its supply depends on the race between emission and burn. Filecoin (FIL) pays storage miners with new FIL and still releases coins from vesting. Both carry steady sell pressure from emission, and both can only offset it with real usage that burns or locks coins.

JASMY sits at the other end. Like a fully minted fixed-supply token, it has no emission, no mining and no vesting left, so its supply cannot grow from the protocol at all. Unlike Bitcoin, which has a fixed cap but still pays miners new coins until the cap is reached, every JASMY that will ever exist is already in someone's wallet. The trade-off is that JASMY also has no burn and no buyback: nothing removes coins either, so the supply reading stays at zero in both directions.

That makes the JASMY supply question simple: the only possible new sell pressure is the 555.0M issuer wallet, which is about 1.11% of supply, and it has been still since January 2025. Price then depends on demand, not on new coins.

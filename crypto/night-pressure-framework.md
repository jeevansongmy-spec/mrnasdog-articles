# NIGHT Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Midnight cannot make another NIGHT — the Cardano ledger records exactly **one** mint of **24,000,000,000 NIGHT** on **Nov 25 2025** and **zero** burns since — and the float still grows every quarter, because the Glacier Drop airdrop is thawing. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Midnight at **+6.85% net** over the trailing 90 days and **+6.85%** forward: **1,136.9M NIGHT** of thaw against a buy side that is **zero** on all four rows, on a circulating base of **16,607.4M NIGHT**. The supply monitor reads the same window at **−0.01%**, because the figure it divides by already counts the frozen tokens.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the framework reads NIGHT at **+6.85% net**: **1,136.9M NIGHT** released by the Glacier Drop thaw, nothing at all bought back, burned or newly locked, against **16,607.4M NIGHT** of circulating supply. The forward quarter reads the same **+6.85%**, because the thaw runs unchanged until **Dec 4 2026**. The supply monitor reads **−0.01%** — a gap of **6.85 percentage points**, far outside the half-point tolerance, so this build ships a **monitor-gap flag**. The reconciliation is arithmetic, not mystery. The tracked circulating figure for Midnight is **16,607,399,400.63**, and that number is exactly the Midnight Foundation's **8,400.0M** plus Midnight TGE Ltd's **3,660.0M** plus the entire claimed Glacier Drop and Scavenger Mine pool of **4,547.4M**. It is a sum of allocation buckets fixed at listing, not a live measurement — so the whole claimed pool was booked as circulating on day one, including the half of it that had not thawed and the **1,307.4M** still sitting in the redemption escrow when we read it. NIGHT is **fixed in supply and inflationary in float**: the coin count never changes, and the tradable share of it grows a quarter of the airdrop every 90 days.

## Sell pressure: where new NIGHT comes from

Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero**, and this build measured it rather than assuming it. Midnight does not mint NIGHT to pay block producers. It pays them out of a pre-funded Reserve of **6,000,000,000.87 NIGHT**, and that Reserve is locked until block production becomes permissionless. Midnight mainnet is still a federated set of **13** node operators, with the Midnight Foundation running **2** of them, so the Reserve has never opened. Its Cardano script address read **6,000,000,000.873988 NIGHT** this session — an exact match to the genesis allocation down to the non-divisible **873,988** STAR remainder left over from the Scavenger Mine. An exact match to genesis is a both-ends read: nothing has left and nothing has returned since the token was created.

Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — carries the entire ledger, and it is the Glacier Drop thaw. Every successful Glacier Drop and Scavenger Mine claim unfreezes in four equal **25%** tranches spaced **90** days apart, and each claim was assigned its own random start day inside the first 90-day window running from **Dec 10 2025** to **Mar 9 2026**. Randomising the start per claim removes the cliff: across the whole pool the release is a near-straight line, and any 90-day slice frees exactly a quarter of the **4,547.4M NIGHT** claimed pool, which is **1,136.9M NIGHT**. Midnight's own tokenomics paper corroborates the rate from the other direction, recording the pool as roughly **41.67%** unlocked in May 2026 — five of twelve monthly steps, the same quarter-per-quarter pace. The thaw ends **Dec 4 2026**, followed by a 90-day grace window on the redemption portal.

One honest limit belongs here. The framework prefers a released figure to a scheduled one whenever the escrow can be read, and Midnight's escrow is only half readable. The first redemption moves a claim's whole balance out of the redemption contract at once, sending the thawed share to the claimant and pushing the unthawed remainder into a separate per-claim lock script — so the escrow balance, **1,307.4M NIGHT** when read, understates what is still frozen, and the lock side has no historical balance to difference against. With no fully readable escrow the published schedule stands, and the chain says the schedule is an **upper bound**: holders must return and take each step, and many have not. Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is **zero** because no entity wallet was seen releasing NIGHT in the window, and Sell #4 is **zero** because Midnight is a going concern with no bankruptcy estate.

## Buy pressure: where new NIGHT goes

The buy side of Midnight is empty, and unusually, that is the design rather than an oversight. Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is **zero**: no buyback is defined, funded, announced or governed anywhere in Midnight's tokenomics, and the supply mechanism points one way only, from the Reserve out to block producers once that door opens. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is **zero** for a design reason worth stating plainly. Midnight is a two-resource chain: NIGHT is the transferable token, and DUST is a shielded, non-transferable, decaying resource that holding NIGHT generates and that pays for transactions. Because fees are settled in DUST, NIGHT is never spent on a transaction and never destroyed by using the network. The ledger confirms it: the Cardano asset records one mint and a burn count of **zero**, with the total still reading **24,000,000,000 NIGHT**. Buy #3 — Foundation buy — is **zero**; neither entity bought NIGHT on the open market. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is **zero**, and measured: the Reserve, the on-chain Treasury and the Lost-and-Found pool all read their exact opening balances of **6,000.0M**, **1,200.0M** and **192.6M NIGHT**.

## Foundation and overhang

Midnight discloses its own concentration, and it is the largest single fact about this token after the thaw. The Midnight Foundation holds **8,400,000,000 NIGHT**, or **35.00%** of total supply, and Midnight TGE Ltd holds **3,660,000,000 NIGHT**, or **15.25%** — together **50.25%** of every NIGHT that will ever exist. Both allocations were unlocked at the start of the redemption period on **Dec 10 2025** and carry no protocol lockup at all. They move by multisig under internal policies whose signer structure is not published, and the only commitment to disclose material transfers is described as a present intention rather than an obligation. Alongside them sit three protocol-locked pools read this session at their exact genesis balances: the Reserve at **6,000,000,000.87 NIGHT**, which opens only when block production becomes permissionless; the on-chain Treasury at **1,200,000,000 NIGHT**, locked until on-chain governance is enabled; and the Lost-and-Found pool at **192,600,598 NIGHT**, which still has no launch date. A further **209.6M NIGHT** sits in attacker-controlled wallets that seven exchanges have frozen after the Jul 20 2026 bridge exploit. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How NIGHT compares to other fixed-supply chains

NIGHT looks like Bitcoin on the supply line and behaves nothing like it on the float line. A hard-capped proof-of-work chain has a cap and a live emission: coins are still being created, slowly, on a halving schedule, so its inflation is small, positive and mechanical. Midnight has a cap and **no live emission at all** — the entire **24,000,000,000 NIGHT** was created in a single transaction and the block-reward Reserve has never paid a unit. On a coin-count basis Midnight is the tighter asset of the two. What the coin count hides is that Midnight distributed **100%** of supply by airdrop rather than by mining, so the question is not how many coins exist but how many are loose, and that number is still climbing.

Against fee-burning smart-contract chains the contrast is sharper still. A chain that burns a base fee has a buy row that scales with usage, so growth in activity mechanically offsets issuance. Midnight deliberately routes fees through DUST instead, which protects holders from spending their NIGHT but also means **no amount of network usage will ever remove a single NIGHT**. There is no burn, no buyback and no staking lock, so the buy side is pinned at zero by design and only the sell side can move. Against other large airdrop tokens, Midnight's randomised per-claim thaw is genuinely better design — it replaces a single cliff that dumps a quarter of the pool in one day with a smooth quarterly drip — but smoother is not smaller. Over the four thaw periods the same **4,547.4M NIGHT** reaches the market either way, and the framework reads the drip at roughly **6.85%** of circulating per quarter.

And watch the Midnight Foundation and Midnight TGE Ltd wallets, because a **50.25%** holding with no lockup is the largest thing this page cannot forecast.

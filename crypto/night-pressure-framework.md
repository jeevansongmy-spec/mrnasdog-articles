# NIGHT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

NIGHT supply did not grow at all in the last 90 days, because every one of the **24B** NIGHT was created at launch in late 2025 and no more can ever be made. From Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026 the count of circulating coins stayed at **16.6B**: **0** new NIGHT came in and **0** NIGHT was burned or bought back, so the net change is **0.00%**. Other trackers show **+0.02%**, close enough that the coin page carries no warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Midnight is a privacy chain that works next to Cardano, and NIGHT is its coin. The full supply of **24B** NIGHT was minted in one go on Cardano, and that record shows one mint and no burns. About **16.6B** of those coins count as circulating, which includes the airdrop coins still thawing and the Midnight Foundation's share. The rest, about **7.4B**, sits in three pools that have not paid out: a **6B** reserve for future block rewards, and about **1.39B** more for the treasury and for people who missed the airdrop. Over the last 90 days the net change was **0.00%**, and with no dated payout in sight the next 90 days read the same. Other trackers show **+0.02%** for the same stretch, a difference of **0.02** points, so there is no warning note. The label is simple: a fully minted coin with steady supply for now, where the real change will come the day block rewards start.

## Sell pressure: where new NIGHT comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0.** No new NIGHT can be created, and the block rewards that will one day come out of the **6B** reserve have not started. Today a small set of approved companies runs Midnight's nodes, and Midnight says plainly that these operators earn no block rewards.

**Vesting unlocks: 0.** The airdrop coins from the Glacier Drop and the Scavenger Mine thaw in four equal parts, 90 days apart, and the last part thaws between Sep 6 and Dec 4 2026. Roughly **1.1B** NIGHT thawed in this window. Those coins have been counted as circulating since launch, so the thaw does not raise the count, even though it hands more coins to people who can now sell them.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** The Midnight Foundation's **8.4B** NIGHT and the **3.66B** launch allocation handed out when NIGHT started trading are both inside the circulating count already, so any sale from them moves coins without adding new ones. The treasury and the Lost-and-Found pool, which sit outside the count, did not release anything.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court or bankrupt firm is holding NIGHT for a later payout. The roughly **515M** NIGHT stolen from the Wanchain bridge on Jul 21 2026 were already circulating before the theft, and the theft happened before this window.

**Unbacked bridge copies: 0.** About **526.5M** NIGHT copies trade on BNB Chain, but since the theft only **11.8M** NIGHT are left behind them in the bridge. No new copies were made in the window, and their count slipped by **4,708**.

## Buy pressure: where new NIGHT goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** No contract or treasury buys NIGHT back, and none of the network's fees are set aside to do it.

**Protocol fee burn: 0.** People pay for Midnight transactions in DUST, a resource that holding NIGHT slowly produces and that cannot be traded. Because fees never touch NIGHT itself, more use of the network does not burn any coins, and not one NIGHT has been burned since launch.

**Foundation buy: 0.** The Midnight Foundation has announced no plan to buy NIGHT, and exchange or fund buying only shifts coins that are already circulating.

**New long-term lock: 0.** No new lock removed NIGHT from the count. Holding NIGHT to make DUST, or moving it over to the Midnight network itself, leaves the coins counted as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The Midnight Foundation holds the largest single share, **8.4B** NIGHT by its launch plan, and has said it has no near-term plan to sell. Because those coins already count as circulating, a Foundation sale would not change the supply figure, but it would add coins to the market.

The three pools outside the count are the true overhang. The **6B** reserve is meant to pay block producers over many years on a slowly falling curve, starting once outside stake pool operators can produce blocks. The treasury is meant to fund the ecosystem once on-chain voting exists, and the Lost-and-Found pool is for people who were eligible for the airdrop but never claimed. On-chain those two sit in wallets holding **1.2B** and **192.6M** NIGHT. None of the three has moved since Mar 13 2026.

One more pool matters for traders rather than for the count: airdrop coins that are not yet redeemed. About **1.01B** NIGHT still sat in the airdrop claim contract on Nov 23 2026. Those coins stay claimable until about early March 2027, and Midnight has not said where any coins left unclaimed after that go.

## How NIGHT compares to other privacy Layer 1 chains

Most privacy coins grow their supply every day. Zcash pays miners new coins in every block under a halving schedule, and Monero adds a small fixed amount of new coins forever, so both keep a steady flow of fresh supply. NIGHT is different: nothing is mined and nothing is printed, and the only future source of new circulating coins is the reserve that already exists.

The closest model is Cardano itself, which also pays its stake pools from a reserve that shrinks a little over time. The big difference today is that NIGHT's reserve has not opened, so NIGHT shows a flat line where most proof of stake chains show steady growth. NIGHT also sits apart in how fees work: chains that burn fees can shrink when busy, while NIGHT gets no burn from use because fees are paid in DUST.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Dec 4 2026:** the last airdrop thaw ends. After that, no scheduled release of claimed airdrop coins remains, which takes away one steady source of selling.

**Q4 2026 to Q1 2027:** Midnight's roadmap lists block production rewards for Q4 2026 and opening block production to Cardano stake pool operators, plus NIGHT staking, for Q1 2027. A draft proposal, still open on Nov 22 2026 and not yet approved, would pay out about **504M** NIGHT in the first year, a little over **3%** of today's circulating coins. The first day the reserve pays out is the day NIGHT supply starts to grow, and no day has been named yet.

**Around early March 2027:** the claim window for thawed airdrop coins closes, 90 days after Dec 4 2026. What happens to coins nobody claimed has not been announced.

**Any date:** the opening of Lost-and-Found claims, and any treasury spending, would each move coins from outside the count into it.

## Summary

NIGHT supply was flat from Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026, with **0** new coins and **0** coins burned, for a net change of **0.00%**.

All **24B** NIGHT were minted at launch, about **16.6B** count as circulating, and the last airdrop thaw ending Dec 4 2026 was already part of that count.

The real supply change ahead is the **6B** NIGHT reserve, which starts paying block producers once Midnight opens block production, an event set for Q4 2026 to Q1 2027 with no day named.

Midnight's fees are paid in DUST, so busier use of the network will not burn NIGHT or offset that future payout.

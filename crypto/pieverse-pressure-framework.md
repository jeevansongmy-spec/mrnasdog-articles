# PIEVERSE Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

## The verdict, in one paragraph

In the 90 days to Oct 28 2026, Pieverse shows **29.75M PIEVERSE** of new supply reaching the market and **0** taken out, a net of **+10.83%** of circulating supply. Other trackers show about **0%** for the same 90 days, a gap of **10.83 percentage points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. The reason is plain: the circulating figure those trackers use has stayed at 274.75M PIEVERSE every day since Jun 15 2026, while on-chain the team-run wallets fell by exactly the 29.75M they paid out. Our +10.83% stands. For the next 90 days we expect about **71.25M PIEVERSE** of new supply and no buyer on the other side, or **+25.93%**. Pieverse is an **unlock-driven token**: the supply cap is fixed, but about 70% of it still waits in team hands and is now being let out month by month.

## Sell pressure: where new PIEVERSE comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. PIEVERSE has no mining and no staking emission; exactly 1B coins existed on both Jul 30 and Oct 28 2026, split between Ethereum and BNB Chain, and the bridge only moves coins from one chain to the other. The token owner still holds a mint path, so this row stays under watch rather than closed for good.

Vesting unlocks added **21.25M PIEVERSE**. Two team-run community wallets did the paying: one sent 5M on Aug 17, 5M on Sep 18 and 5M on Oct 20 2026, and the other sent 6.25M on Aug 17. The published calendar allowed more than that, so we count what actually left, not what was allowed. The bigger change comes next. The 12-month lock on team and investor coins ends on **Nov 14 2026**; the team then vests its 200M over 24 months and the investors their 150M over 18 months, which is about **16.67M PIEVERSE** on each of Nov 14 2026, Dec 14 2026 and Jan 14 2027. Add the community pace of 21.25M and the next 90 days hold about 71.25M.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **8.5M PIEVERSE**. The team's operations wallet sent that amount to a fresh wallet on Aug 17 2026. This wallet pays out at no set time, with gaps of weeks or months between payments, so nothing is booked for the next 90 days. The 100M foundation reserve has sent nothing at all, even though its paper vesting started in May 2026.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**. There is no court case, estate or frozen pile of PIEVERSE waiting to be released.

## Buy pressure: where new PIEVERSE goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**, because Pieverse has no buyback; no contract and no treasury buys PIEVERSE back. The protocol fee burn is also **0**: the burn address holds only dust and total supply stayed at 1B on both chains through the window. Foundation buying is **0**, since the foundation and the team only pay coins out and we found no purchase by either.

New long-term locks are **0** as well, though one flow is worth knowing about. Pieverse staking went live in August 2026 with 90-, 180- and 365-day terms that pay points rather than coins, and about **10.72M PIEVERSE** now sits in the Ethereum staking contract. Staked coins still count as circulating, so they do not shrink the count, and they come back to their owners when each term ends.

## Foundation and overhang

Nine multisig wallets run by the same three keyholders held **699.65M PIEVERSE** on Ethereum on Oct 28 2026, plus **4M** on BNB Chain. The biggest is a **166M** wallet that has never moved, most likely community coins by its size. Two wallets of **150M** each have never moved either; by size they match the investor allocation and most of the team allocation, and a **50M** wallet completes the team share. The **100M** foundation reserve is untouched. The two community payout wallets now hold **30M** and **27.5M**, a further idle wallet holds **20M**, and the operations wallet holds **6.15M** on Ethereum and **4M** on BNB Chain.

We read every one of these wallets again at each rebuild. If any of their balances falls between rebuilds, that outflow enters the foundation and unscheduled row at the next one. Coins that already left them, such as the 8.5M, 5M and 5M still sitting untouched in the wallets that received them in August, September and October, already count as circulating.

## How PIEVERSE compares to other young unlock-driven tokens

PIEVERSE belongs to the large group of tokens launched with a fixed cap and a small float, where the market price is set on a minority of the supply while the rest unlocks on a calendar. Against a coin like Ethereum, which prints new coins for validators and burns part of its fees, PIEVERSE has neither printing nor burning; all of its supply change comes from coins leaving team-run wallets. That makes the timing of those wallets, not any network rule, the whole story.

Compared with exchange tokens or fee-sharing tokens that run regular buybacks, PIEVERSE has nothing pulling coins back out of the market, so every unlock lands on the market with no offset. Compared with older tokens whose insider vesting has finished, PIEVERSE sits near the start of its heaviest stretch: the team and investor monthly payments begin in November 2026 and the last scheduled release falls in November 2028. Only about 27.5% of the cap is counted as circulating today, so each month of unlocks is large next to the float.

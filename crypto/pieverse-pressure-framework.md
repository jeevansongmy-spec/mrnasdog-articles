# PIEVERSE Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads PIEVERSE as inflationary: **29.75M PIEVERSE** left the Pieverse project safes in the last 90 days, a net **+10.83%** of the **274.75M** circulating, with no buyback and no burn to offset it. The supply is capped at **1B PIEVERSE**, so the ceiling is fixed — but about **728.65M** of it still sits in project safes waiting to be released.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the trailing 90 days (Jul 1 to Sep 29 2026) Pieverse released **29.75M PIEVERSE** from its vesting safes and removed nothing, so the framework reads **+10.83% net**. Over the next 90 days it projects **54.58M PIEVERSE** released and **+19.87% net**. The inflation monitor reads **+0.01%**, a gap of **10.82 percentage points**, which is far past the 0.5-point line and triggers the ⚠ chip on the coin page. The reason is found and dated: the circulating count the monitor reads has been stuck at **274.75M PIEVERSE** since **Jun 14 2026**, so it cannot see any release after that day, while the safe balances on-chain fell by exactly the 29.75M we count. PIEVERSE is an unlock-driven token: its supply is capped, nothing new is minted for rewards, and every percent of growth comes from coins leaving the team's own safes.

## Sell pressure: where new PIEVERSE comes from

**Protocol inflation is 0.** PIEVERSE has no block reward and pays no staking reward in tokens — the staking program that opened on Aug 25 2026 pays points, not new coins. The token lives on Ethereum and BNB Chain, and the supply on the two chains added up to exactly **1,000,000,000 PIEVERSE** on both Jul 1 and Sep 29 2026; about 0.10M moved from BNB Chain to Ethereum through the bridge, which changes where coins sit, not how many exist. The contract owner can still mint for the bridge, so the cap is a project rule rather than something the code makes impossible.

**Vesting unlocks: 21.25M PIEVERSE last 90 days, 54.58M next.** At launch on Nov 14 2025 the whole Ethereum supply was split into ten safes and two wallets, one per allocation. Two of those safes pay the community pools each month: they sent out **5M** on Jul 15, **11.25M** on Aug 17 and **5M** on Sep 18 2026. Most of it was handed out to many wallets within one to three days, in lots of about 1M, which is what community rewards and campaign payouts look like on-chain. The schedule allowed about 16.7M a month for the community and foundation pools together; much less actually left, because the foundation reserve has not drawn a single coin since launch. For the next 90 days the framework keeps the two community safes at the same pace (**21.25M**) and adds the scheduled investor and team releases: **8.33M** for investors and **8.33M** for the team on each of **Nov 14** and **Dec 14 2026**, **33.33M PIEVERSE** in all.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 8.50M PIEVERSE last 90 days, 0 next.** A project operations safe sent **8.5M PIEVERSE** to a single wallet on **Aug 17 2026**. This safe pays out when the project needs it — past sends ranged from 0.3M to 30M, months apart — so there is no pattern steady enough to project, and the next 90 days book 0 from it. The 100M foundation reserve and the other idle safes did not move this window. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy is 0**: Pieverse has no estate, trustee or court-run distribution.

## Buy pressure: where new PIEVERSE goes

**Programmatic buyback is 0.** Pieverse has not announced a buyback, and no wallet or contract buys PIEVERSE off the market. **Protocol fee burn is 0.** Fees on the Pieverse payment network are not destroyed, and the burn addresses on both chains held nothing but dust at both ends of the window. **Foundation buy is 0**: no treasury purchase was announced or seen on-chain.

**New long-term lock books 0, but it is real.** PIEVERSE staking opened on **Aug 25 2026** with 90, 180 and 365-day locks, and the staking contract now holds **10.87M PIEVERSE** across Ethereum and BNB Chain, with no withdrawals on Ethereum so far. Two deposits on the first day made up 10.4M of it — one of them came straight out of an exchange. These coins were already counted as circulating, so locking them does not shrink the count. What it does is keep them off the market until the first 90-day locks end, around Nov 23 2026.

## Foundation and overhang

The PIEVERSE overhang is large and fully visible. Project safes hold **728.65M PIEVERSE** — almost three quarters of the 1B cap: **150M** for investors and **200M** for the team and advisors (both locked until Nov 14 2026), a **166M** community reserve and a **100M** foundation reserve that have never moved, **62.5M** in the two safes that make the monthly payouts, **10.15M** in the operations safe (4M of it on BNB Chain), and **40M** in two safes that were idle this window. Outside the safes, three large wallets from earlier handouts hold 33M, 30M and 19.5M PIEVERSE and did not move. We read every one of these balances directly on-chain at each rebuild; if any of them falls between rebuilds, the outflow enters the sell side at the next refresh.

## How PIEVERSE compares to other unlock-driven tokens

PIEVERSE belongs to the class of capped tokens whose supply grows only through vesting. That separates it from uncapped proof-of-stake Layer 1s such as Ethereum, where validators are paid in new coins every block and the growth never ends, and from halving coins such as Bitcoin, where new supply falls on a fixed timetable. For PIEVERSE the total is fixed at 1B; what grows is the share of that billion that is free to trade — about 27% today.

Among unlock-driven tokens, Pieverse sits early in its schedule. Tokens like Starknet or Arbitrum release insider allocations on a monthly calendar after a first-year cliff, and their float grows by a steady slice of the remaining locked supply each month. PIEVERSE has just reached that stage: its community pools are already paying out monthly, and the investor and team cliff on Nov 14 2026 roughly doubles the scheduled monthly release. Because only about 27% of the supply circulates, each release is large next to the float — the same 16.67M would matter far less for a token with most of its supply already out.

What PIEVERSE lacks is a counterweight. Tokens that route fees into a buyback or a burn — Hyperliquid and BNB are the best-known cases — can absorb part of their unlocks. Pieverse has no such mechanism today, so every released coin adds to supply one for one. Its staking program locks coins for up to a year but does not remove them from the float.

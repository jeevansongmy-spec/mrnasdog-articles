## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the circulating supply of ARB grew by **+6.00%**, with sell pressure of **419.69M ARB** and buy pressure of **0**. Other trackers show **+4.79%** for the same 90 days, a gap of **1.21 points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. The reason is simple and dated: those trackers have not yet counted the 100M ARB that left the Arbitrum DAO treasury on Nov 20 2026, two days before this check. Their figure did rise in three monthly steps on Sep 18, Oct 17 and Nov 17 2026, and those steps match the team, investor and Foundation unlocks in the ARB ledger. For the next 90 days the ARB ledger expects **321.06M ARB** of new supply and nothing taken out, or **+4.59%**. ARB is an unlock-driven coin: inflationary by schedule, with no burn and no buyback to push back.

## Sell pressure: where new ARB comes from

Protocol inflation added **0 ARB**. The ARB token contract held its total supply at about 9.99999B ARB at both ends of the window and created no new coins. The Arbitrum DAO can vote to create up to 2% more ARB a year, but it has never used that power and no such vote is on the table.

Vesting unlocks added **277.94M ARB**. The team, future team, advisors and early investors hold 4.447B ARB in total, locked for four years from Mar 16 2023. A quarter unlocked on Mar 16 2024, and the rest unlocks in 36 equal monthly steps of about **92.65M ARB**. Three steps fell in this window, on Sep 16, Oct 16 and Nov 16 2026, and three more fall in the next 90 days, on Dec 16 2026, Jan 16 2027 and Feb 16 2027. About 370.6M ARB of this group is still locked, and the last step lands on Mar 16 2027.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **43.70M ARB**. The Arbitrum Foundation received a 700M ARB budget that unlocks second by second over four years, from Apr 17 2023 to Apr 17 2027. Its vesting wallet paid **43.70M ARB** into the Foundation's main wallet during the window, and the same schedule frees about **43.12M ARB** in the next 90 days.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins added **0 ARB**. No court or bankrupt firm holds ARB. On Oct 29 2026 the Arbitrum Security Council froze **74.56M ARB** that had been stolen in the Binance hack. Those ARB coins were already circulating before the theft, so the freeze, and any later return to Binance, adds no new ARB to the market count.

DAO treasury grants added **98.06M ARB**. After delegates voted to back the USDG stablecoin, the Arbitrum DAO treasury sent **100M ARB** to its DRIP rewards program on Nov 20 2026, while a past program sent **1.94M ARB** of unused coins back. Treasury grants come only by vote and have no fixed calendar; the last two were 230M ARB to the Foundation in June 2026 and this 100M in November. No new ARB grant from the treasury is up for a vote now, so the next 90 days book 0 here.

## Buy pressure: where new ARB goes

Programmatic buyback took out **0 ARB**. Arbitrum's network fees are paid in ETH and flow to the DAO treasury, and no rule turns that money into ARB purchases.

Protocol fee burn took out **0 ARB**. ARB is not the gas coin of Arbitrum, so there is no fee burn at all; only about 7 ARB was sent to a dead address during the window, far too little to count.

Foundation buy took out **0 ARB**. Offchain Labs, the company that builds Arbitrum, said in March 2025 that it would buy ARB over time and last reported buying in December 2025. Those ARB coins were already circulating, so the buying moves coins between holders without shrinking the market supply.

New long-term lock took out **0 ARB**. Arbitrum has no ARB staking lock. Holders delegate their voting power, but the ARB stays in their own wallets and stays in the circulating count.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the Arbitrum DAO treasury, which holds about **2.46B ARB** after the Nov 20 2026 grant. Those coins sit outside the market until delegates vote to spend them, and the treasury balance is read on-chain at every rebuild. Next comes the locked team and investor bucket, about **370.6M ARB**, which drains by 92.65M a month until Mar 16 2027. The Arbitrum Foundation's vesting wallet holds about **80.98M ARB** that unlocks by Apr 17 2027.

Three more wallets matter but already count as circulating, so spending from them adds nothing new: the Foundation's main wallet with about **329.02M ARB**, the DRIP rewards wallet with about **152.14M ARB**, and the frozen Binance-hack address with **74.56M ARB**. If the DAO treasury or the Foundation vesting wallet shrinks between checks, that outflow enters the ARB sell ledger at the next rebuild.

## How ARB compares to other Ethereum Layer 2 chains

Arbitrum, like most Ethereum Layer 2 chains, charges gas in ETH, not in its own coin. That one design choice shapes ARB's supply story: users never spend or burn ARB to use Arbitrum, so heavy network use does not remove ARB from the market. Ethereum itself burns part of every fee in ETH, and exchange coins such as BNB burn coins on a schedule; ARB has neither.

ARB is instead a governance coin with a fixed cap and a large unlock calendar, the same broad shape as other Layer 2 voting coins launched with big team, investor and treasury buckets. Its supply grows in steps rather than through a steady block reward, and those steps stop on a known date: the team and investor calendar ends on Mar 16 2027 and the Foundation vest ends on Apr 17 2027. After that, new ARB reaches the market only when the DAO votes to spend its treasury or, in theory, votes to create new coins.

The DAO earns its income in ETH from sequencer fees, priority-fee auctions and chain licences, and it keeps that income rather than buying ARB. So unlike tokens that send fees into buybacks, ARB holders carry the full weight of each unlock with no built-in buyer on the other side.

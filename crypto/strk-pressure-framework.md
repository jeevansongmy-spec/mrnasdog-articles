# STRK Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads STRK, the token of the Starknet layer two, as **strongly inflationary**: about **718.7M STRK** reached the market in the last 90 days against a buy side of **zero**, a net **+9.78%** of the 7.35B circulating supply, with about **+8.55%** expected over the next 90 days. Most of it is the monthly unlock of up to **127M STRK** for early contributors and investors, which runs until Mar 15 2027, plus releases from a team reserve wallet and a small staking mint. Starknet has no fee burn and no buyback, so nothing takes STRK back off the market.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the trailing 90 days (Jul 1 to Sep 29 2026) the Starknet ledger shows **718.7M STRK** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure, a net **+9.78%** of circulating supply. For the next 90 days (to Dec 28 2026) the framework books **628.2M STRK**, or **+8.55%**. The inflation monitor reads **+12.79%** for the same trailing window, a gap of **3.01 percentage points**, which is above the 0.5-point line and so carries a monitor-gap warning on the coin page. We walked every source class to explain it: the monitor's count steps up on fixed calendar days and runs about **152.5M STRK** ahead of every outflow we can find on-chain, while it does not count the **37.7M STRK** newly minted for stakers at all. STRK is a token in heavy unlock season: supply grows on a calendar, and nothing on the buy side answers it.

## Sell pressure: where new STRK comes from

**Protocol inflation.** Starknet pays stakers in newly minted STRK. The token contract on Ethereum minted **37.7M STRK** in the window, in 29 equal batches of 1.3M, about one every three days, and its total supply rose from 10.13B to 10.17B by exactly that amount. The mint follows a curve tied to how much STRK is staked: stake rose from about **1.42B** to **1.59B STRK** during the window, and the curve at those levels gives almost the same figure. With no change to the curve scheduled, the next 90 days book the same 37.7M.

**Vesting unlocks.** The largest row. Early contributors and investors, who together received about 38% of the original 10B STRK, unlock up to **127M STRK** on the 15th of every month from Apr 15 2025 to Mar 15 2027. Three unlocks fell inside the last 90 days, on Jul 15, Aug 15 and Sep 15 2026, for **381M STRK**, and three more fall in the next 90, on Oct 15, Nov 15 and Dec 15 2026, for another **381M**. About 776M STRK is still locked after the Sep 15 unlock. One group of older lock contracts paid **61.8M STRK** to its owners in the window, but those coins are part of the same schedule and are not counted twice.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks.** A team reserve wallet sent out **300M STRK** in the window: 100M on Jul 28 2026 and 200M on Sep 10 2026, part of which was bridged onto Starknet. The same wallet has released **849.6M STRK** over the past year in seven transfers between 69.6M and 200M, with at least one in every quarter. That steady rhythm is why the framework books about **209.5M STRK** for the next 90 days, even though no dates are published. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy** is zero: there is no estate, no trustee and no court distribution of STRK.

## Buy pressure: where new STRK goes

All four buy rows are **zero**. There is no **programmatic buyback**: no contract, programme or treasury buys STRK on the market. There is no **protocol fee burn**: since Sep 2025 all Starknet fees are paid in STRK, but they go to the network operator, and burning part of the base fee is described only as coming later, with no date. We checked this both ways: the Ethereum and Starknet burn addresses held the same tiny balances at both ends of the window, and total supply rose by exactly the staking mints. There is no **Foundation buy**: the team side only sends STRK out. And there is no **new long-term lock**: staking grew, but staked STRK can leave after a 7-day wait and stays inside the circulating count, so a larger stake removes nothing from the float. It does, however, raise the staking mint.

## Foundation and overhang

Four team-side balances could still reach the market. First, the reserve wallet that released 300M this window still holds **950M STRK**; it is read on-chain every day. Second, a multisig safe that once held a quarter of all STRK now holds **1.0B STRK**; it sent 1.5B to the reserve wallet in Apr 2026 and has not moved since. Third, a smaller safe holds **83.6M STRK** and did not move in the window. Fourth, the locked contributor and investor pool of about **776M STRK** opens on the monthly schedule already booked above; within it, older lock contracts still hold 415.8M. Together these balances match the part of supply the market does not yet count as circulating. None of them buys STRK back. If any of these balances falls between our checks, the outflow enters the foundation row at the next check.

## How STRK compares to other Ethereum layer-two tokens

STRK sits in the class of Ethereum layer-two tokens that launched with a fixed genesis supply and a multi-year unlock calendar for insiders. Arbitrum's ARB and Optimism's OP share that shape: a large team and investor allocation that opens month by month, and fees that are not burned. For all three, the unlock calendar, not network use, sets the pace of new supply while it lasts. What sets STRK apart is that it adds a staking mint on top. Starknet is the layer two where the token secures the network through staking, and stakers are paid in new STRK, so its total supply is already about 170M above the original 10B and keeps rising.

Against layer-one chains that burn fees, the contrast is the empty buy side. A chain that burns part of every fee can offset some of its issuance when it is busy; Starknet fees cost users very little and none are destroyed, so every new STRK stays. Against capped proof-of-work coins, the difference is the calendar: a capped coin's new supply shrinks on a fixed schedule, while STRK's biggest source of new supply runs at full size until Mar 15 2027 and then stops, leaving the staking mint and any team releases as the ongoing flow.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the three monthly unlocks of up to **127M STRK** each on **Oct 15 2026**, **Nov 15 2026** and **Dec 15 2026**, which make up most of the next-90-day figure. Second, the team reserve wallet holding **950M STRK**: its releases have come every one to three months, and a quiet quarter would cut the forecast by up to 209.5M. Third, the older lock contracts reach the end of their four-year release around **Nov 29 2026**, after which their remaining 415.8M can be claimed in full. Fourth, the fee burn Starknet has said is coming: if it goes live, the protocol fee burn row would stop being zero. Fifth, the next staking upgrade and any change to the minting curve, which would move the 37.7M staking mint.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads STRK at **+9.78% net** over the trailing 90 days and about **+8.55%** over the next 90: **381M STRK** from the monthly contributor and investor unlock, **300M** from a team reserve wallet and **37.7M** minted for stakers, against no fee burn and no buyback. The structural driver is an insider unlock calendar that runs until Mar 15 2027, with a staking mint that grows as more STRK is staked. The key risk is the team side: 950M STRK in the reserve wallet and 1.0B in a safe that has not moved could add to supply on no published schedule. Starknet's original 10B supply is no longer a ceiling, because the staking mint keeps adding new STRK.

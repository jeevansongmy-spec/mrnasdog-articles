# STRK Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about Starknet, see [mrnasdog.com/research/strk](https://mrnasdog.com/research/strk).

**STRK is inflationary.** Starknet's coin started with **10B** units and has no hard cap, because staking rewards are paid in newly made STRK. In the 90 days from Aug 7 to Nov 5 2026, about **853.5M STRK** joined the circulating supply and nothing was taken out, so supply grew about **11.14%**. Other trackers show about **+12.45%** for the same coins.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Starknet adds a lot of new STRK and removes none. Over the last 90 days, **381M STRK** came from the monthly unlock for investors and the early team, **433.5M** came out of reserve wallets tied to the Starknet Foundation, and **39.0M** was minted as staking rewards. The buy side was empty: fees are not burned and nothing buys STRK back. That puts net growth at about **+11.14%** for the last 90 days and about **+9.23%** for the next 90. Other trackers read about **+12.45%**, and they count the same roughly 850M new coins, so the coin page carries no warning note. Our label: **supply growing, and projected to keep growing** until the unlock calendar ends in March 2027.

## Sell pressure: where new STRK comes from

**Staking rewards.** Starknet pays the people who stake STRK with freshly made coins. Between Aug 7 and Nov 5 2026 the token contract on Ethereum minted **39.0M STRK**, in 30 batches of exactly 1.3M, about one every three days. The reward rate follows a curve that grows with the share of STRK staked. With about **1.76B STRK** staked today, that curve points to roughly 42M a quarter, close to what was actually minted. We expect about the same 39.0M in the next 90 days.

**The monthly unlock.** Coins held by investors and early team members are locked and released on the 15th of each month, up to **127M STRK** at a time. Three of these fell inside the window, on Aug 15, Sep 15 and Oct 15 2026, for **381M**. The next three land on Nov 15 and Dec 15 2026 and Jan 15 2027, another 381M. After that only two remain, in February and on Mar 15 2027, and about **635M STRK** is still locked in total.

**Foundation reserve releases.** This is the biggest row this quarter. The Starknet Foundation holds about half of all STRK for grants, community programs and reserves, and those coins only count as circulating once they leave its wallets. Reserve wallets that trace back to the Foundation's share sent out **200M STRK** on Sep 10, **150M** on Oct 17 and **83.5M** on Oct 27 2026, which makes **433.5M** in the window. The circulating count rose by the same amount over the quarter. There is no public schedule for these releases, but they have come about every five or six weeks: ten in the past year, worth about **1.17B STRK**. At that pace about **287M** more could come out in the next 90 days.

**Long-term locks and estates.** Zero. No court case, bankruptcy estate or long lock of STRK is waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new STRK goes

**Buyback: zero.** No contract, program or vote buys STRK back on the network's behalf.

**Fee burn: zero.** Since September 2025 every fee on Starknet is paid in STRK, but those fees go to the company that runs the network's block producers, and none are destroyed. A burn tied to fees has been discussed since 2025 and still has no launch date. Nothing on-chain was burned in the window apart from dust.

**Foundation buying: zero.** Neither the Foundation nor StarkWare buys STRK. Both hand coins out.

**New long-term locks: zero.** About **1.76B STRK** is staked, but stakers can leave after a 7-day wait, so staked coins still count as circulating and do not shrink the supply.

## Foundation and overhang

Two reserve wallets on Ethereum still hold large balances that have not reached the market. One reserve safe holds about **1.00B STRK** and has not moved since it sent 1.5B onward in April 2026. One reserve wallet holds **800M STRK** and is the one that made the Sep 10 and Oct 17 2026 releases. Together that is about **1.80B STRK**, almost a quarter of today's circulating supply, and none of it has a public release date.

On top of that, about **635M STRK** of investor and team coins is still locked and will unlock by Mar 15 2027. StarkWare, the company behind Starknet, was given 1.076B STRK for its own work and does not publish its current balance. If the reserve wallets keep up last year's pace, the Foundation's 1.80B would last roughly another year and a half.

## How STRK compares to other Ethereum Layer 2 chains

Most Ethereum Layer 2 tokens are governance coins with a long unlock calendar and no staking pay. Arbitrum's ARB is the closest match: it also started with 10B coins, it also releases insider coins every month until March 2027, and it mints nothing for staking. STRK shares that monthly calendar but adds two things ARB lacks. Its holders can stake the coin and are paid in new STRK, so the supply has no ceiling. And its fees are paid in STRK, which gives the coin real use on the network, even though none of that fee money comes back to holders as a burn or buyback today.

The result is a heavier supply load than most of its peers. Monthly unlocks plus Foundation releases add new coins every few weeks, while nothing built into the network takes any back.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 15 2026:** the next monthly unlock of up to 127M STRK for investors and the early team. The same happens on **Dec 15 2026** and **Jan 15 2027**.

**Foundation reserve wallets:** the last releases came on Sep 10, Oct 17 and Oct 27 2026. At the past year's rhythm, one or two more are likely before early February 2027, and the 1.00B and 800M balances will show it.

**The Layer 1 plan:** a non-binding community vote that closed on Oct 22 2026 backed research into turning Starknet into its own base chain. A real move would need a new vote. If it ever came with rewards for block producers, that could add a new source of fresh STRK.

**A fee burn:** a burn or other way of returning fees to holders has been promised for 2026 and has not shipped. It would be the first buy-side row for STRK.

## Summary

STRK's supply grew about **11.14%** in the 90 days to Nov 5 2026, with 853.5M new circulating coins and nothing removed.

The new coins came from three places: 381M from the monthly investor and team unlock, 433.5M from Starknet Foundation reserve wallets and 39.0M from staking rewards.

For the next 90 days we expect about **9.23%** more, with three more monthly unlocks and Foundation releases at about last year's pace.

The Foundation's reserve wallets still hold about 1.80B STRK, and no fee burn or buyback exists yet to offset any of it.

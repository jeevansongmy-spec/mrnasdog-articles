# QNT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

QNT supply was roughly steady over the 90 days to Nov 21 2026: it grew about **+0.08%**, and the next 90 days look flat. No new QNT has been created since June 2018, so the only change came from a Quant team wallet that released **12,002 QNT** into circulation in October 2026, against just **15.02 QNT** lost by mistake. Other trackers show about **+0.04%**, and every coin sits inside a total of **14.61M** QNT.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 the circulating supply of Quant's QNT grew by **+0.08%**. Other trackers put the same 90 days at **+0.04%**, a gap of **0.05 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days, to Feb 19 2027, nothing is scheduled to add QNT, and the small, steady loss of coins sent to the wrong address leaves the count at about **0.00%**. QNT is a quiet, fixed-supply coin whose only real supply risk is the company's own small store of coins.

## Sell pressure: where new QNT comes from

Network rewards add **0 QNT**. QNT is a token on Ethereum with no staking pay and no mining, and the last new QNT were created on Jun 25 2018, when the token sale handed out its coins. One fact holders should know: the 2018 sale contract still has an owner key that could, in theory, create more QNT. That key has not been used since 2018, and Quant has never said it plans to use it.

Vesting unlocks add **0 QNT**. Staff coins from the sale were locked for 12 months after it closed, so every vesting batch finished long ago and none is scheduled.

Company releases added **12,002 QNT**. Two Quant team wallets are left out of the circulating count. One of them, which held **42,378 QNT** on Aug 23 2026, sent **2,001 QNT** to a new wallet on Oct 6 2026 and another **10,001 QNT** to the same wallet on Oct 10 2026. The new wallet still holds all 12,002 QNT, but because it sits outside the company store, those coins now count as circulating. The release came about two weeks after QNT's price had roughly quadrupled on news of a large US bank deal. Nothing tells us another release is planned, so the next 90 days book **0 QNT** here.

Locked or bankrupt holdings add **0 QNT**. No court, failed firm or long lock holds QNT for a later payout. Several large wallets that had sat still for years moved coins in late September 2026, including a wallet that press reports tie to Quant's founder, which sent **25,777 QNT** to new wallets. Those coins were already circulating, so moving them changes nothing.

## Buy pressure: where new QNT goes

Buybacks remove **0 QNT**. Quant sells software licences to banks and firms, and that money stays with the company; none of it is used to buy QNT back from the market.

A fee burn removes **0 QNT**. QNT has no burn built in. Its one burn happened on Sep 14 2018, when **9.55M** unsold QNT were sent to the token's own address, where no one can ever move them.

Company buying removes **0 QNT**. Quant does not buy QNT on the market, and the bank network deal announced on Sep 24 2026 did not say that any bank must buy, hold or pay in QNT.

New locks remove **0 QNT**. There is no staking for holders yet. Staking for people who run nodes on Quant's Fusion network has been discussed since that network went live on Jun 2 2026, but it has no start date.

Coins lost by mistake removed **15.02 QNT**. People keep sending QNT to the token's own address, the same place the 2018 burn went, and coins sent there can never come out. That happened 24 times between Aug 23 and Nov 21 2026, and we expect about the same over the next 90 days.

## Foundation and overhang

Quant has no foundation or DAO treasury; its supply overhang is the company's own store of coins, and it is small. Team Wallet 1 holds **25,938.84 QNT** and did not move in the 90 days. The second team wallet holds **30,376 QNT** after its October release. Together that is about **56.3K QNT**, under 0.4% of all QNT. The wallet that received the October coins holds **12,002 QNT** and has not sold any yet, but those coins already count as circulating.

The bigger unknown is the 2018 sale contract. Its owner key still has room, on paper, to create about **27.5M** more QNT, nearly twice today's supply. It has sat unused for more than seven years. We read both team wallets and the sale contract at every rebuild: if either team wallet's balance falls between checks, or the sale contract creates a single coin, the change goes into the sell side at the next check.

## How QNT compares to other interoperability tokens

Most coins that link blockchains together still add supply. Polkadot's DOT is minted around the clock to pay stakers, which added about 13.78M DOT over the same 90 days, and Cosmos Hub's ATOM pays stakers with new coins and has no cap at all. QNT has neither: it has no staking pay and no mint in use, so its supply only moves when a holder outside the count lets coins in.

The closer match is Chainlink's LINK, where every coin already exists and the team releases coins from a reserve it holds back. The difference is size. LINK's reserve still holds more than 200M LINK, and one release on Oct 9 2026 was 18.75M LINK. Quant's company store is about 56.3K QNT, so even if it were all released at once, QNT supply would grow by under 0.4%. XRP works in a similar way on a far larger scale, with Ripple opening a 1B XRP escrow every month.

The flip side is that QNT also lacks anything that takes coins away. There is no burn tied to use and no buyback, so busier use of Quant's software does not shrink the supply. QNT's supply is steady because almost nothing happens to it, not because buying offsets selling.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The two team wallets**, through Feb 19 2027: they hold about 56.3K QNT, and a repeat of the Oct 6 and Oct 10 2026 transfers would be the only likely way new QNT reaches the market.

**The 2018 sale contract**: its last successful use was on Jun 25 2018, and any new call from its owner key would be the first sign of new QNT in more than seven years.

**Fusion staking**: Fusion went live on Jun 2 2026, but staking has no date yet. If it opens with a lock, that would be the first thing to take QNT out of circulation on purpose.

**The bank network**: The Clearing House picked Quant on Sep 24 2026 for a tokenized deposit network due in the first half of 2027. Watch whether its rules ever ask banks to pay or hold QNT; so far they do not.

**Coins sent by mistake**: about 15 QNT a quarter are lost this way, so this count should stay tiny unless a large holder makes an error.

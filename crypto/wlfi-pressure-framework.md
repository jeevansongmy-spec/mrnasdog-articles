# WLFI Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

WLFI supply reaching the market is roughly steady: the Pressure Framework reads World Liberty Financial's WLFI at **+0.01%** over the last 90 days and **+0.01%** for the next 90, against a monitor reading of **−0.22%**. Sell pressure is **3.64M WLFI**, all of it early buyers claiming tokens that unlocked on Sep 1 2025, and buy pressure is **0**. The constraint is the lock itself: **46,104.6M WLFI** sits in the unlock contract, and the insider share does not start to unlock until **May 6 2028**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **31,777.8M WLFI**, the framework books **3.64M WLFI** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days, a net of **+0.01%**, and projects the same **+0.01%** for the next 90 days. The monitor reads **−0.22%** for the same window, a gap of **0.24 percentage points**, which is inside the framework's tolerance, so the overview carries no warning. The monitor's supply estimate moves a few tenths of a percent from day to day around the published float, while the chain shows that float moving by only the claims. An earlier reading of this page, **+4.69%**, counted payouts from a project Safe that was already part of the tradable float; those payouts moved tokens from one tradable wallet to another and did not add supply. The label for WLFI is **a large locked supply that is not yet moving**.

## Sell pressure: where new WLFI comes from

It does not come from minting. Protocol inflation is **0**: WLFI is an Ethereum token with no block reward and no staking emission, and no WLFI was created in the window. The count of WLFI in existence fell from **96,742.7M** to **96,722.7M**, and the only change was a single burn. The WLFI contract can be upgraded and its owner can move balances between wallets, so the zero is watched rather than called permanent.

Vesting unlocks are the whole sell side, at **3.64M WLFI**. Buyers in the 2024 and 2025 token sales received 20% of their tokens on **Sep 1 2025**, and some of them are still collecting. Claims out of the WLFI unlock contract rose by **3.64M WLFI** across these 90 days, and those tokens are what entered the tradable float. The next 90 days are projected at the same pace, because only **11.19M WLFI** is claimable today. The May 6 2026 governance vote moved the rest of the early-buyer tokens onto a two-year cliff and a two-year unlock, and the founders, team and advisors onto a two-year cliff and a three-year unlock. Nothing on either schedule releases before **May 6 2028**.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**. The main World Liberty Financial multisig, which also owns the WLFI contract, held **12,620.9M WLFI** at both ends of the window and made no transfer at all. A second project Safe paid out **998.1M WLFI**, but its whole balance is already counted in the circulating figure, so those payouts add nothing new to it. Long-term locked or bankruptcy is **0**: there is no estate, trustee or court-run distribution attached to WLFI.

## Buy pressure: where new WLFI goes

Nowhere this window. Programmatic buyback is **0**. WLFI holders voted in September 2025 to spend all fees from the project's own liquidity positions on buying WLFI and burning it. The buyback-and-burn ran in the autumn of 2025 and has not burned anything since; holders asked in public in March 2026 why it stopped, with no answer on the forum. In this window no buyback burn happened on Ethereum, on BNB Chain or on Solana. The bridged copies on the two other chains add up to the balance locked in the Ethereum bridge pool, to within a few thousand WLFI, so nothing was burned there either.

Protocol fee burn is **0**: WLFI has no fee burn built into the token, and the dead address held **0.47 WLFI** at both ends. Foundation buy is **0**, since the multisig received no WLFI. New long-term lock is **0**: holders moved **215.6M WLFI** into the unlock contract, but those tokens could not be traded before. One wallet was frozen on Sep 1 2026, and it held no WLFI. The one burn in the window, **20M WLFI** on **Sep 7 2026**, was an insider joining the new vesting plan, which destroys 10% of the insider's allocation. Those tokens were locked, so the burn takes **0** off the market.

## Foundation and overhang

The WLFI overhang is large and mostly locked. The biggest item is the unlock contract at **46,104.6M WLFI**. Almost all of it waits for the May 6 2028 cliff, and only **11.19M** can be claimed today. The second is the main multisig at **12,620.9M WLFI**, which is not on any schedule. It last released tokens on **Apr 7 2026**, in a run of releases from February to April 2026 that added about **5.1B WLFI** to the float. The third is **6,219.4M WLFI** in wallets from the original sales that have not signed up to unlock and cannot move their tokens. The fourth is the project Safe at **7,565.7M WLFI**, which is already counted as tradable but pays out on a steady schedule. All four balances are read on chain at every rebuild. If the multisig, the unlock contract or the unsigned wallets fall between refreshes by more than the claims account for, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How WLFI compares to other governance tokens with large locked supply

WLFI belongs to the class of governance tokens where most of the supply was issued at launch and then locked, rather than minted over time. That puts it next to recently launched tokens working through an investor and team vest, not next to proof-of-stake chains that pay validators with new coins. The difference is the timing: most such tokens unlock monthly from their first year, while WLFI's insiders gave up 10% of their tokens to accept a single two-year cliff. The result is a very quiet float until May 2028 and a large change after it.

Against exchange tokens and DeFi tokens that run buybacks funded by revenue, WLFI has the mechanism on paper and not in the ledger. A voted buyback-and-burn that does not fire reads the same as no buyback, so WLFI's buy side is empty while peers with active burns can read negative. And unlike tokens with a hard minting cap written into the code, WLFI's contract can be upgraded by its owner, so its supply limit depends on the project as well as the code.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the voting rewards plan proposed on Sep 14 2026 to start on **Oct 1 2026**: it would pay stakers about 2% a year from project sources, and if those rewards come out of the multisig they become sell pressure. Second, the multisig itself at **12,620.9M WLFI**: any transfer out of it enters the float. Third, the buyback-and-burn: a restart would give WLFI its first buy pressure since 2025. Fourth, more insiders joining the vesting plan, each of which burns 10% of locked tokens and moves the rest to the May 2028 schedule without touching the float. Fifth, claims from the **11.19M WLFI** backlog, which set the pace of the only sell row through **Dec 22 2026**.

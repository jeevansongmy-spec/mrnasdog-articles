# MNT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

MNT supply is **roughly steady**: between Aug 23 and Nov 21 2026 the circulating count rose about **+0.29%**, and nothing is scheduled to add to it in the next 90 days. No new MNT is minted. The only real source of new circulating coins is the Mantle treasury, which holds about **2.91B MNT** and released **10M MNT** on Nov 10 2026, against **291,000 MNT** a team wallet sent back on Oct 1 2026.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 21 2026, MNT's circulating supply grew by a net **+0.29%**, and other trackers show **+0.29%** as well, so the two readings agree to within **0.01** of a percentage point and the coin page carries no warning note. The total supply of **6.22B MNT** did not change by a single coin. The whole move came from one treasury release, slightly offset by one return. The next 90 days project at **0.00%**, because the treasury publishes no release dates. The label that fits MNT is a fixed-supply token with a large treasury that lets coins out in bursts.

## Sell pressure: where new MNT comes from

Protocol inflation added **0** MNT. The token contract on Ethereum can mint new coins only once a year and only up to a limit its owner sets, at most 2% of supply. That limit sits at zero today, and a test call to mint even one coin is refused by the contract itself. Lifting it would need a visible change on-chain first, so a mint could not arrive unannounced.

Vesting unlocks added **0** MNT. MNT took over from the older BIT token one for one in 2023, and the old lockups were removed before that switch, so no team or investor batch is waiting for a date.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **10M MNT**. On Nov 10 2026 a treasury wallet sent its whole balance of 10M MNT to a wallet outside the treasury, which now holds about **34.38M MNT**. That move is what lifted the circulating count. It was the third release this year, after 10M MNT on Mar 3 2026 and 24.35M MNT on Apr 28 2026, and the gaps between them were uneven, so we do not project another one into the next 90 days.

Long-term locks and bankruptcy payouts added **0** MNT. No court case, failed firm or lock contract holds MNT for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new MNT goes

Programmatic buybacks removed **0** MNT. Mantle does not buy back its token. A Mantle delegate opened a forum discussion on Sep 18 2026 about using part of the network's revenue to buy and burn MNT, and asked again on Nov 19 2026 under the Halo Prime proposal, but no plan has gone to a vote.

The fee burn removed **0** MNT. Gas on Mantle is paid in MNT, yet the fees collect in the network's fee wallets and are later withdrawn rather than destroyed. Fees are also small: users paid under $9,000 in the last 30 days.

Foundation buying removed **0** MNT. A team wallet took in about 10.77M MNT from an exchange on Aug 31 2026, but that wallet already counts as circulating, so the move changes nothing.

New long-term locks removed **0** MNT. Ideas to burn part of the treasury were discussed early in 2026 and then archived.

One extra flow took coins out: on Oct 1 2026 a team wallet sent **291,000 MNT** back into a treasury wallet on the Mantle network. It was a single move with no plan behind it to repeat.

## Foundation and overhang

The Mantle treasury is the one overhang that matters. Its listed wallets hold about **2.91B MNT**, close to **47%** of all MNT, and almost all of it, about 2.90B, sits in one main wallet that has not moved a coin in the window. None of it is on a release schedule. The current budget, MIP-35, passed on Nov 11 2026 and allows Mantle's core team to spend up to **150M MNT** and $40M in stablecoins between Jul 1 2026 and Jun 30 2027, with most of the MNT set aside for builder programmes. That is a ceiling, and the team said actual spending will likely stay well below it.

A second group of team wallets already counts as circulating: the core budget wallets, a payout wallet, the wallet that took in coins from an exchange and the wallet that received the Nov 10 2026 release, about 61M MNT together on Ethereum. Selling from them would add selling pressure but no new circulating coins. If the treasury's balance falls, the coins that left are counted as a new release at the next check.

## How MNT compares to other Ethereum Layer 2 chains

Most Layer 2 tokens still pay out a vesting calendar. Arbitrum's ARB, for example, releases about 92.65M ARB to its team and investors every month into early 2027, so its supply grows on known dates. MNT has no such calendar. Its supply is fixed at 6.22B, and new circulating coins appear only when the treasury decides to spend them.

That makes MNT closer to a treasury-run token than to an emission token. The risk is not a steady drip but the size of the reserve: the treasury holds nearly as many MNT as the whole circulating float. Ethereum itself burns part of every fee, while MNT burns none, so MNT has no built-in sink to offset any release.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, any outflow from the main treasury wallet, which has held about 2.90B MNT without moving through Nov 21 2026; a release from it would show up here at the next check. Second, the revenue-buyback discussion opened on Sep 18 2026: if it reaches a vote, MNT would gain its first buyer. Third, the revised MIP-36 vote on Halo Prime, which opened on Nov 19 2026 and closes on Nov 26 2026; its thread drew new buyback questions, and any answer that sends revenue to MNT would matter more than the vote itself. Fourth, spending under the MIP-35 budget, which runs to Jun 30 2027 and can draw up to 150M MNT. Fifth, any change to the token's mint limit, which stands at zero today.

## Summary

MNT supply is roughly steady: the circulating count rose about **+0.29%** in the 90 days to Nov 21 2026, all of it from a **10M MNT** treasury release on Nov 10 2026, and nothing is scheduled for the next 90 days. No MNT is minted, no MNT vests and no MNT is burned or bought back. The main risk is the treasury's **2.91B MNT**, which can be spent without a timetable, up to a budget of **150M MNT** through Jun 30 2027. The hard ceiling is the fixed total of **6.22B MNT**, which can only grow if the token's owner first raises a mint limit that is zero today.

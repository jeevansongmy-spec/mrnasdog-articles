# MNT Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

MNT supply is roughly steady. In the 90 days to Oct 2 2026, Mantle created **0 new MNT** and the only coins that left the market were **291,000 MNT** sent back into the Mantle Treasury, so circulating supply eased by **0.01%**; the next 90 days project **0.00%**. The monitor reads **+0.01%**. MNT has no block rewards, no vesting and no burn: what can move it is the **2.92B MNT** treasury, which held still.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 4 2026 to Oct 2 2026, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework measures MNT net supply at **−0.01%**: **0 MNT** of sell pressure against **291,000 MNT** of buy pressure, on a circulating supply of **3.30B MNT**. The inflation monitor reads **+0.01%** for the same stretch, a gap of **+0.01 percentage points**, well inside the half-point line, so no warning chip is shown. For the next 90 days the projection is **0.00%**, because the one buy was a single return with no schedule behind it. In one line: MNT is a quiet token whose float only changes when the Mantle Treasury decides to spend or to take coins back.

## Sell pressure: where new MNT comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. Mantle does not pay validators or sequencers in new MNT, and the MNT token on Ethereum read a total supply of **6,219,316,794.89 MNT** at both ends of the window. The token contract does keep a mint function, but only its owner can call it and the mint limit is set to zero. To create new MNT, the owner would first have to raise that limit, and the contract caps it at 2% of supply a year. Nothing like that happened in this window, so no new MNT was made.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. MNT came from the 2023 swap of the old BitDAO token, BIT, and the unlock schedule ended that year. The old swap contract is halted, so no stream of converted coins is still arriving.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**. The Mantle Treasury is the only large holder that sits outside the circulating count, and its main safe on Ethereum held exactly **2,900,022,409 MNT** at the start and the end of the window. Its last outflow was on Oct 16 2024. The treasury pays for work through yearly budgets, but the budget wallets already count as circulating, so the 3.02M MNT the Ethereum budget wallet spent in this window was a move inside the market, not new supply.

Long-term locks and bankruptcy releases are **0**. No estate, trustee or court process holds MNT, and no locked pile is due to open.

## Buy pressure: where new MNT goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**. Mantle has no buyback. On Sep 18 2026 a community member opened a discussion asking the DAO to spend part of Mantle's revenue on MNT buybacks, but it has not become a vote.

Protocol fee burn is **0**. Gas on the Mantle network is paid in MNT, but the fees are kept, not destroyed. About 47,000 MNT gathered in the network's fee wallets during the window, and those coins still count as circulating. The dead addresses on both chains gained less than 2 MNT.

Foundation buy is **0**. The treasury did not buy MNT on the open market. Besides its MNT, it holds bitcoin, ether and dollars, which it lends and invests rather than using to buy MNT.

New long-term lock is **0**. MNT that is staked or sitting in bridges still counts as circulating, so it does not reduce the float.

The one buy-side entry is **291,000 MNT** returned to the treasury. On Oct 1 2026 a wallet run by the Mantle team sent those coins back into one of the treasury's wallets on the Mantle network, which moved them out of the circulating count. It was a single return with no schedule, so it adds nothing to the next 90 days.

## Foundation and overhang

The Mantle Treasury is the overhang that matters. Mantle's own list of treasury wallets adds up to **2,917,313,412 MNT**, exactly the gap between total and circulating supply. Most of it sits in one Ethereum safe (2.90B MNT), with 10M MNT in a second Ethereum safe and about 7.29M MNT in wallets on the Mantle network. None of it has a release date: spending needs a governance vote, and the last yearly budget allowed up to 200M MNT. Inside the market, two budget wallets hold 9.17M and 12.77M MNT for running costs, and a separate team-run wallet holds about 24.38M MNT; their spending does not change the circulating count. We read every one of these wallets on the chain each time we check. If the treasury's balance falls between checks, the coins that leave are counted as new sell pressure at the next check.

One more lever exists: the token owner could raise the mint limit, at most to 2% of supply a year (about 124M MNT). It is set to zero today, and we watch it.

## How MNT compares to other Ethereum layer-2 tokens

Most layer-2 tokens launched with team and investor allocations that unlock on a monthly calendar for years, so their float grows on a fixed date whether demand is there or not. MNT has none of that. Its supply was fixed at the 2023 swap, nothing vests, and the network pays its operators from fees rather than new coins. That makes MNT one of the few layer-2 tokens whose 90-day supply change can sit at zero.

The trade-off is concentration. Close to half of all MNT, 2.92B of 6.22B, sits in a treasury that a vote can spend. A vesting calendar tells you the date in advance; a treasury tells you only when a budget passes. Compared with exchange-linked coins that run a scheduled burn, MNT also has no built-in buyer: fees are kept, not burned, so the float does not shrink by itself.

In short, MNT trades the steady drip of a vesting token for one large, idle pile. Supply is flat while the treasury waits, and it moves in steps when the treasury acts.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

A fourth budget vote: the third budget cycle ran from July 2025 to June 2026 and its three-month extension ended around Sep 30 2026, so a new budget proposal is due. A new budget that draws MNT from the main treasury safe would show up here as sell pressure.

The buyback discussion opened on Sep 18 2026: if it becomes a formal vote with a set share of revenue, a real buy-side row would appear for the first time.

The mint limit on the MNT contract, now zero. Any rise would be the first chance of new MNT since launch.

The treasury's main safe of 2,900,022,409 MNT. It has not sent out a coin since Oct 16 2024; the first move out of it would be the biggest supply event for MNT.

## Summary

MNT supply is roughly steady: **−0.01%** over the 90 days to Oct 2 2026 and **0.00%** projected next, against a monitor reading of **+0.01%**. Mantle makes no new MNT, has no vesting left and burns no fees; the only change was 291,000 MNT returned to the treasury. The key risk is the **2.92B MNT** Mantle Treasury, which a governance vote can release, plus a mint that is switched off but not removed. Until the treasury spends, the MNT float has no built-in source of new coins.

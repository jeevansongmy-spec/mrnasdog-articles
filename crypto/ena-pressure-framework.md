# ENA Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · A hard cap that still dilutes

Ethena's ENA can never be minted. All **15,000,000,000 ENA** were created at the March 2024 launch, and an on-chain read of the token contract returned exactly **15,000,000,000** both today and at the block roughly 90 days earlier — flat, to the decimal. And yet ENA is one of the most diluting supplies the MrNasdog Pressure Framework tracks. A single vesting step of about **267.2M ENA** fires on the 5th of every month, covering core contributors, investors, the foundation and ecosystem distribution. Three of those steps landed inside the trailing 90 days, for a sell total of about **801.6M ENA**, and three more land in the next 90. The buy ledger is **empty**. The framework reads **+8.16% net** for both the trailing and the forward window, against an independent monitor read of **+8.71%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 11 2026**, the framework reads **ENA at +8.16% net** — a sell side of about **801.6M ENA** against a buy side of **zero** — and the forward window to **Nov 9 2026** reads the identical **+8.16%**, because the unlock calendar is fixed and fires three more times inside it. Our own supply monitor reads **+8.71%** over the trailing window, a raw gap of **0.553 percentage points**. That gap does not ship as a data-conflict flag, because a deep walk closed it arithmetically rather than by assumption: both sides carry the *same* **801,562,500 ENA** numerator, and the only difference is the base each divides by. Measured against the float as it stood 90 days ago — **9,026.6M ENA** — that identical numerator reads **+8.880%**, leaving a residual of just **0.171 percentage points** against the monitor, comfortably inside tolerance. The framework divides by today's larger float of **9,828.1M** by definition. Two readings, one mechanism, one quantum. Ethena is **structurally inflationary on its active float**: nothing is created, but a large locked share is handed to the market on a fixed monthly schedule with nothing pushing back, until the calendar empties in **April 2028**.

## Sell pressure: where new ENA comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and it is worth being precise about why, because "zero inflation" here does not mean "flat supply". Ethena minted its entire supply once, at the March 2024 token generation event. There is no block reward, no staking emission and no reward mint. The protocol earns its revenue in dollars, from the collateral and funding behind the USDe synthetic dollar — never in freshly issued ENA. The on-chain total confirms it at both ends of the window, and the null address and the dead-burn address hold no ENA whatsoever. Every unit of measured inflation in this ledger is locked allocation reclassifying into the tradable float, not a new token coming into existence.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is about **515.6M ENA** and it is the backbone of the ledger. Core contributors hold **30%** of supply and investors **25%**, both on identical published terms: a one-year **25%** cliff followed by **36 months** of linear monthly vesting. That arithmetic produces a fixed monthly tranche of about **171.9M ENA** — roughly **93.8M** to core contributors and **78.1M** to investors — released on the 5th of each month. Three of those dates fell inside the trailing window: **Jun 5 2026**, **Jul 5 2026** and **Aug 5 2026**. Three more fall inside the forward one: **Sep 5 2026**, **Oct 5 2026** and **Nov 5 2026**. The framework counts each firing by its date rather than averaging a trailing rate, which is exactly why the trailing and forward columns match to the decimal. It is also where a step-schedule read goes wrong most easily: a 90-day window that sits a day inside either edge catches only two of the three steps and under-reports the ledger by a third.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is about **285.9M ENA**, and it is the part most unlock trackers under-report. The foundation's **15%** allocation and the ecosystem-development allocation release on the very same monthly date, adding roughly **95.3M ENA** per step on top of the contributor and investor tranche — a reported foundation slice of about **40.6M** plus roughly **54.7M** of ecosystem distribution. That figure is derived from the schedule itself, never back-derived from a supply delta: of the **5,171.9M ENA** still locked, the contributor and investor calendar accounts for **3,265.6M** across its **19** remaining tranches, leaving **1,906.3M** to release over the **20** months to the documented April 2028 end. Combined, the monthly step reaching the market is about **267.2M ENA**. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero**: no estate, trustee schedule or court-ordered distribution touches ENA.

## Buy pressure: where new ENA goes

Nowhere — which is the uncomfortable half of the story. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero**. Ethena is one of the most profitable protocols in crypto; the USDe business has generated fees at a scale most base chains never see. None of that revenue currently buys ENA on the open market. The long-discussed fee switch, which would route a bracket of gross protocol fees to staked ENA, **has not been executed** — the binding vote was still weeks away as of early August 2026, and the governance forum carried no fee-switch, buyback or burn item at all between May and August 2026. Even once it is live, the design matters more than the headline: a revenue share denominated in protocol revenue rather than in ENA removes no float. Until an ENA repurchase leg actually executes on-chain, the framework books **zero**.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **zero**. ENA is a governance token on Ethereum, not a gas asset, so there is no base-fee burn of the kind a settlement layer runs — and the on-chain evidence is unambiguous, since total supply has never moved off **15 billion** and the burn addresses are empty. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **zero**: two large treasury buyback rounds ran during 2025, but both sit outside the trailing window and nothing has fired since — last firing **Sep 2025**, with no public schedule for the next 90 days. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero**: staking ENA into sENA is liquid and reversible, so it does not take supply off the market, and no new lockup contract was deployed inside the window.

## Foundation and overhang

Three team-controlled overhangs are tracked. The largest is the **unreleased vesting balance**: **5,171.9M ENA**, about **34.5%** of maximum supply, still outside the float and scheduled to arrive at roughly **267.2M** a month until **April 2028**. The second is the **foundation treasury**, the **15%** allocation feeding about **40.6M ENA** into each monthly step; it is opaque as a wallet set and is walked from public disclosure rather than read from a public vesting contract.

The third is the one most readers miss. A listed corporate treasury vehicle, built specifically to hold ENA, has carried roughly **3,029M ENA** since it began trading in **June 2026** — about **31% of the float the market counts as circulating** — locked, and subject to a foundation veto on any sale. That position was assembled before this window and no fresh purchase was disclosed inside it, so nothing is booked as flow; the balance is surfaced as an overhang and watched. But it cuts both ways, and the plain reading matters: because those tokens are counted as circulating yet cannot freely trade, the *effective* float behind ENA's price is materially smaller than the headline **9,828.1M**, which makes each monthly unlock heavier in practice than **+8.16%** implies. It also concentrates a very large decision in one corporate treasury. The standing trigger applies to all three overhangs — if any balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh rather than being absorbed silently.

## How ENA compares to other vesting-schedule tokens

Ethena belongs to the class of **fixed-cap governance tokens whose entire supply was minted at launch and released on a multi-year cliff-and-linear schedule** — the same structural family as most 2024-vintage venture-backed launches. Set it against a token where the mint is renounced and every unit has been liquid since day one and the contrast is total: both have a hard cap, neither can create a coin, but one reads a genuine **0.00%** while ENA reads **+8.16%**, purely from the unlock calendar. A hard cap is not the same thing as a flat supply. ENA is the clearest example of that distinction in the catalog, and it is why **65.52% unlocked** — **9.83B** of a fixed **15B** — is the single most important number on the page.

Against an uncapped, continuously-issuing base chain, ENA is worse on the trailing arithmetic and better on the horizon. A proof-of-stake chain issuing **4%** to **6%** a year mints forever, but it usually pairs that with a fee burn and with staking that locks a large share of float. Ethena has neither offset today, so its dilution runs at roughly four times a typical chain's annualised rate — but it is finite, and it stops. The closer comparison is an exchange token where a share of business revenue is routed into a recurring buyback or burn and the ledger reads deflationary despite a large treasury. Ethena has the revenue to do exactly that; its fee base *is* the argument for the fee switch. Until a repurchase leg actually executes, the framework can only score the mechanism that exists.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the three unlock dates that define the forward reading — **Sep 5 2026**, **Oct 5 2026** and **Nov 5 2026**, each releasing about **267.2M ENA**. If a step lands materially larger or smaller than the calendar implies, the schedule has changed and the ledger is re-derived. Second, the fee-switch vote: an executed switch routing protocol fees into **open-market ENA purchases** would put the first real number in Buy #1 and is the single most consequential change available to this reading — while a revenue share paid in stablecoins would leave the ledger untouched. Read the implementation, not the headline. Third, the corporate treasury position: a disclosed new purchase would be a real float removal, a disclosed sale enters Sell #3. Fourth, the foundation's monthly governance updates, where a change to the foundation or ecosystem release schedule would surface first. Fifth, the burn addresses and the on-chain total, checked every rebuild — if ENA ever leaves **15 billion**, both Sell #1 and Buy #2 change character.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Ethena at **+8.16% net** over the next 90 days, from a sell ledger of about **801.6M ENA** and a buy ledger of **zero**. The mechanism is a **fixed 15 billion supply that cannot be minted but is still barely two-thirds released** — **9.83B** circulating, **65.52%** unlocked — handing the market a fixed **~267.2M ENA** step on the 5th of every month across core contributors, investors, the foundation and ecosystem distribution. Our supply monitor reads **+8.71%**; the deep walk closed the difference on base convention, with the same numerator reading **+8.880%** on the 90-day-ago float and a residual of **0.171pp**, so no data-conflict flag ships. The key risk is that this dilution meets no offset at all — no burn, no executed fee switch, no live buyback — while roughly **3,029M ENA** of the counted float sits locked in a listed treasury vehicle, making the tradable float smaller and each step heavier than the headline suggests. The ceiling is real and dated: **5,171.9M ENA** remain to be released, and the calendar empties in **April 2028**, after which Ethena's supply is genuinely fixed.

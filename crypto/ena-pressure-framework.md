# ENA Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Ethena mints no ENA at all — all **15,000,000,000 ENA** were created at the March 2024 launch — yet ENA is still one of the most dilutive tokens the MrNasdog Pressure Framework tracks. About **534.4M ENA** reached the market over the trailing 90 days and **801.6M** is projected for the next 90, all of it the monthly vesting release of about **267.19M** on the 5th, against a buy side of effectively **zero**. That puts the framework at **+5.59%** net for the trailing window and **+8.38%** forward, with **5.44B ENA** still waiting on a calendar that runs to April 2028. Our supply monitor reads **+5.94%**, a **0.35pp** gap that stays within tolerance, so no data-conflict flag ships.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window opening **Aug 4 2026**, the framework reads **ENA at +8.38% net** forward, up from the **+5.59%** it measured over the trailing 90 days — not because the pace of dilution changed, but because the trailing window happened to catch two monthly releases while the forward window catches three. Ethena releases locked allocation in one step of about **267.19M ENA** on the 5th of each month, and where the 90-day boundary falls decides whether two or three of those steps land inside it. Our supply monitor reads the trailing window at **+5.94%**, a gap of just **0.35 percentage points** — inside the framework's half-point tolerance — with the small residual explained by the monitor dividing the same unlocks by the float as it stood 90 days ago rather than today's larger float. There is no buyback, burn or lock pushing back. ENA is best characterised as **a fixed-supply token that is structurally inflationary on its active float until its vesting schedule empties in April 2028**.

## Sell pressure: where new ENA comes from

Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero** on Ethena. All **15,000,000,000 ENA** were minted at the March 2024 token generation event, and the token has no block reward, no staking emission and no reward mint. Ethena earns real revenue — among the highest in decentralised finance at its peak — but it earns it in dollars and stablecoins from the delta-neutral collateral behind the USDe synthetic dollar, never in ENA. Nothing on the protocol adds supply; the float grows only because locked allocation is reclassified into it.

Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — is the entire ENA ledger. The published float steps by about **267.19M ENA** on the 5th of each month and is flat in between. Two of those steps landed inside the trailing window, on **Jun 5 2026** and **Jul 5 2026**, for about **534.4M ENA**, and three land in the next, on **Aug 5 2026**, **Sep 5 2026** and **Oct 5 2026**, for about **801.6M**. The composition follows Ethena's published terms: core contributors at **30%** and investors at **25%** sit on a one-year **25%** cliff followed by 36 months of linear monthly vesting, which is **171.88M ENA** a month — the tranche the market usually quotes — and the foundation at **15%** and the ecosystem and airdrop pool at **30%** release roughly **95.31M** more on the same date. A second angle confirms the quantum: this session's monitor shows circulating rising **535.6M** over the trailing 90 days, a two-step window, which matches two releases of **267.19M** almost exactly. And the **5.44B ENA** still unreleased divided by that monthly rate lands the schedule in **April 2028**, the end date the vesting trackers publish.

Sell #3 — foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is **zero** this window, and the flatness of the float between the monthly steps settles it: no ENA arrived off the calendar, so everything that reached the market is already inside Sell #2. That does not make the overhang small — it is carried as a set of tracked balances instead. Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero** permanently: there is no bankruptcy estate, trustee schedule or court-ordered distribution holding ENA.

## Buy pressure: where new ENA goes

Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is **zero**, and this is the finding most likely to surprise a reader who followed the fee-switch story. The Ethena fee switch is live: a share of protocol revenue now flows to holders of staked ENA rather than only to sUSDe holders. But it does not buy ENA — the yield is paid in stablecoins, not repurchased tokens, so it bids for nothing on this ledger. Separately, a Nasdaq-listed treasury company has built an ENA position of roughly **3.03B ENA**, about **20%** of supply, and continues to accumulate. That looks like open-market buying, but it is not a bookable buyback here: most of its stock was acquired at a discount directly from the foundation — locked ENA moving into a corporate treasury rather than tokens being lifted off the market — and what it buys it holds, so it still counts as circulating. The large 2025 repurchase programme, roughly **$890M** across two tranches, completed before this window opened and cannot be re-counted.

Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is **zero** because Ethena has no burn address anywhere in its fee path and ENA is not a gas token on any network it lives on. Supply is fixed and only ever moves from locked to circulating, never down. Buy #3 — foundation buy — is **zero** as a realised figure: a 2026 governance idea for ENA repurchases and burns exists and moved the price when it was floated, but the framework books executed flow, not announcements, and no dated executed quantum falls inside this window. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is **zero**: staking ENA into sENA exists, but staked tokens stay liquid behind a short cooldown and the vault has trended to net outflow, so there is no fresh lock large enough to offset the unlocks.

## Foundation and overhang

The team-controlled overhang on Ethena is the largest single fact about ENA, and it is unusually easy to size because the supply is fixed. **5.44B ENA** — about **36%** of everything that will ever exist — has not yet reached the float, and unlike a discretionary treasury it is on a calendar: it leaves at about **267.19M** a month, on the 5th, without a vote, until the schedule empties around **April 2028** — roughly twenty more releases from today. Behind that sit the **15%** foundation allocation and the **30%** ecosystem and airdrop pool that feed each monthly step. And already inside the float but concentrated in one hand is the Nasdaq-listed treasury company's roughly **3.03B ENA**, about **20%** of supply, acquired largely from the foundation and held. All of these are read every rebuild. If any of these balances falls between refreshes and the ENA reaches the market off-schedule, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh rather than being absorbed silently into the vesting row.

## How ENA compares to other fixed-supply governance tokens

ENA belongs to the fixed-supply, fully-minted governance-token class — the same structural family as the large layer-2 and DeFi governance tokens — and that class shares one property that separates it cleanly from a proof-of-work chain: the ceiling is absolute and the dilution has an end date. A halving chain mints genuinely new coins forever on a schedule nobody controls; Ethena will never create a fifteen-billion-and-first ENA. What it shares with its own class is the weakness that matters right now — the token is not the fee asset. Ethena's revenue builds a treasury and pays sUSDe and sENA holders without removing a single ENA from supply.

Against an exchange token running a quarterly buy-and-burn, the contrast is entirely about the plumbing rather than the profits. Both models generate real cash flow; one converts it into token demand every quarter and prints a shrinking supply, the other routes it to stablecoin holders and to a staking vault that receives no ENA. Ethena's fee switch looked like it would close that gap, and on the revenue side it does — sENA stakers now earn a real yield. But the framework measures tokens, not dollars, and a yield paid in something other than ENA does not bid for ENA. Until a repurchase leg actually deposits tokens or a burn address appears, the buy column stays structurally near zero.

Against its own class on timing, ENA is at the worst point of the curve rather than the best. A governance token whose vesting is two-thirds complete is releasing a shrinking share of a growing float; ENA is releasing about **267.19M** a month against a float of **9,560.9M**, roughly **2.8%** of the tradable supply arriving every month, and it does not taper — the calendar runs flat and then stops. The honest reading is that ENA's inflation profile is fully knowable and fully unpleasant for another twenty releases, after which the vesting row goes to zero permanently and the whole question becomes what the buy side does by then.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Watch the three monthly vesting releases on **Aug 5 2026**, **Sep 5 2026** and **Oct 5 2026**, at about **267.19M ENA** each — they are the whole sell ledger, and the only thing that could change them is a governance decision to re-cut the schedule, which has never happened. Watch whether the fee switch ever starts buying ENA rather than paying stablecoin yield; the day a repurchase leg begins depositing real tokens is the day Buy #1 moves off zero for the first time. Watch the Nasdaq-listed treasury company's reporting, because a shift from discounted foundation blocks to genuine open-market accumulation — or a sale of its **3.03B ENA** — would move real float. Watch the governance forum for the repurchase-and-burn proposal to produce a dated executed amount rather than a commitment. And watch USDe supply and protocol revenue, because the fee switch only pays out after its reserve conditions are met — a revenue squeeze would suspend the one mechanism that could ever make ENA less dilutive.

## Summary

Ethena is a fixed-supply, zero-mint, zero-burn governance token whose float still grows faster than almost anything the framework tracks, because **5.44B ENA** from its 2024 launch is still unreleased and leaves on a fixed calendar at about **267.19M** a month. About **534.4M ENA** reached the market over the trailing 90 days and **801.6M** is projected forward — two monthly releases then three — against a buy side of effectively **zero**, leaving the framework at **+5.59%** net trailing and **+8.38%** forward. Our supply monitor reads **+5.94%**, a **0.35-point** gap that stays within tolerance. The key risk is not the schedule, which is finite and ends in **April 2028**, but the buy side: Ethena's fee switch is live and paying, yet it pays in stablecoins, so the protocol's revenue is not buying back a single ENA.

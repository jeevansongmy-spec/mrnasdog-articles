# ENA Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

**ENA supply is growing fast, and the next 90 days are the heaviest yet.** Ethena mints no new ENA, but its unlock calendar released **801.6M ENA** in the last 90 days — **+7.94%** of the **10.10B** circulating — and about **1.97B ENA** comes free in the next 90, or **+19.55%**, because every remaining investor coin unlocks on **Oct 5 2026**. Nothing is bought back or burned yet: the ENA buyback voted in on Sep 2 2026 waits until USDe supply reaches **$7.5B**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Sep 29 2026, ENA supply grew **+7.94%**, and the ledger projects **+19.55%** for the next 90 days. The monitor reads **+8.61%** for the same stretch, a gap of **0.67 percentage points**, which is above the 0.5-point line, so the Ethena page carries a ⚠ monitor gap chip. The gap is not a missing flow: the monitor's supply figure rose **799.8M ENA** and our calendar adds **801.6M**. The difference is the base — the monitor divides by the 9,294.4M ENA circulating 90 days ago, we divide by today's 10,095.3M. Our reading stands. In one line: **ENA is a fixed-supply token that is still unlocking — every new coin on the market comes from the vesting calendar, not from minting**.

## Sell pressure: where new ENA comes from

**Protocol inflation is zero.** The ENA contract on Ethereum held exactly **15B ENA** at both ends of the window. The contract does let its owner mint up to 10% more once a year, so the 15B cap is a policy, not a law of the code — but that mint has never been used since ENA launched in March 2024, and Ethena pays no staking rewards in new ENA.

**Vesting unlocks are the whole story: 801.6M ENA in 90 days.** Ethena's calendar frees **267.19M ENA** a month — 93.75M for the team, 78.13M for investors and 95.31M from the Foundation and ecosystem funds — and three of those monthly rounds fell inside the window, in early July, August and September 2026. There is no on-chain lock contract behind the calendar: the coins wait in custody wallets, so the published schedule is the measure. The next 90 days are much bigger. On Aug 27 2026 the Ethena Foundation and its lead investors agreed to end the monthly investor steps and release everything that was left in one go on **Oct 5 2026** — about **1.41B ENA**. Add the regular team, Foundation and ecosystem steps in October, November and December, and about **1.97B ENA** unlocks by late December. Ethena also said it stops all ENA rewards tied to USDe from Sep 30 2026, but the ecosystem coins still come free on the calendar, so we keep them in the count.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add nothing.** No coins left a locked balance outside the calendar this window. The large holders that did move ENA — including a listed company's treasury — hold coins that are already circulating, so their moves do not add new supply. **Long-term locks and bankruptcy add nothing** either: no estate or court schedule holds ENA.

## Buy pressure: where new ENA goes

**The programmatic buyback is zero — for now.** On Sep 2 2026 ENA holders passed the fee switch with no votes against. Once USDe supply averages **$7.5B** over 14 days, 5% of Ethena's gross revenue is taken, rising to 10% at $10B, 15% at $15B and 20% at $20B, and 95% of what the Foundation receives buys ENA. USDe sits near **$4.2B**, so the switch has not fired and not one ENA has been bought. It is also not yet decided whether bought ENA would be burned, held or locked.

**The protocol fee burn is zero.** Ethena earns its revenue in dollars from the USDe trade, not in ENA, and burns no ENA: total supply stayed at 15B and the dead address held the same 2.6 ENA at both ends. **The Foundation buy is zero** too. In August 2026 the Foundation bought locked coins privately from seed investors who had been selling since the October 2025 peak — but those coins were never circulating, so the purchase takes nothing off the market. **New long-term locks are zero**: about **1.19B ENA** sits in the staking vault and more is being staked, but staked ENA can be withdrawn and is still counted as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

About **4.90B ENA** is still locked on the calendar: **1.69B** for the team, released at 93.75M a month until March 2028; **1.41B** for investors, all of it on Oct 5 2026; and **1.81B** for the Foundation and ecosystem funds, released at about 95.31M a month. We re-check this calendar every two weeks.

Three holders sit on top of that. The Ethena Foundation will receive the investor coins it bought back in August on Oct 5 2026 — it has not said how many. StablecoinX, a Nasdaq-listed company built around Ethena, holds about **3.03B ENA** in three wallets; its lock-up ends on Oct 5 2026, but any sale still needs the Foundation's written consent, with five business days' notice and a right for the Foundation to buy first. And a project custody wallet holds about **1.26B ENA**. We read the wallets on-chain. If any of these balances falls between our checks, the outflow goes into the Foundation and unscheduled-unlock row at the next check.

## How ENA compares to other DeFi governance tokens

Most DeFi governance tokens fall into two groups. The first has a fixed supply that is fully or almost fully unlocked and a buyback already paid from real revenue, so supply shrinks or holds steady. The second is still vesting, and its circulating supply grows every month as team and investor coins come free. **ENA today is firmly in the second group**: no minting and no burn, but a calendar that adds **+7.94%** in one quarter and about **+19.55%** in the next.

What sets ENA apart is how the investor tail ends. Most vesting tokens let investor coins trickle out month by month until the last step. Ethena chose to pull the remaining 18 monthly steps into **one day**, after buying out the investors who were selling — a sharp one-time rise in supply in exchange for ending the monthly overhang. And unlike layer-1 coins that pay validators in new tokens, ENA's supply growth has a known end: the team steps finish in March 2028 and the Foundation steps in April 2028.

On the buy side, ENA has a buyback on paper that is tied to the size of its stablecoin, not to the token price or to a fixed budget. That makes it easy to check — USDe supply is public — but it also means ENA holders get no buying at all while USDe stays below $7.5B. Tokens whose buybacks already run have a buyer every day; ENA does not yet.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 5 2026:** about 1.41B investor ENA unlocks at once, together with the monthly team, Foundation and ecosystem step — about **1.60B ENA** in one day. The same day StablecoinX's lock-up ends; watch whether the Foundation lets it sell any of its 3.03B ENA.

**Nov 5 2026 and Dec 5 2026:** the regular monthly steps, about **189.1M ENA** each, now without any investor part.

**USDe supply:** the buyback switches on only after a 14-day average above $7.5B, up from about $4.2B. Ethena's new backing with tokenized stocks, announced on Sep 25 2026, is the main hope for growth. If the switch fires, the buyback row turns on.

**The ecosystem fund:** ENA rewards for USDe end on Sep 30 2026. If the ecosystem coins stop coming free on the calendar as well, the next-90-day figure drops by about half a point for each month skipped.

ENA supply grew **+7.94%** in the 90 days to Sep 29 2026 and is projected to grow about **+19.55%** in the next 90 — supply growing, projected to keep growing. Ethena mints no new ENA; all of the growth comes from its vesting calendar, which releases about 1.41B investor coins in one step on Oct 5 2026.

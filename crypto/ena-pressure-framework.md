# ENA Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads ENA as a fast-growing supply: Ethena's vesting calendar released **801.56M ENA** in the last 90 days, a net **+7.94%** of the **10.10B** ENA in circulation, and the next 90 days bring about **1.97B ENA**, or **+19.55%**, because every remaining investor coin unlocks at once on **Oct 5 2026**. Nothing on the buy side offsets it yet: there is no fee burn, and the ENA buyback that holders approved in September 2026 only starts once USDe supply reaches **$7.5B**. The monitor reads **+8.66%**, a **0.72pp** gap that comes from dividing the same unlocks by an older, smaller base.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the ENA ledger books **801.56M ENA** of new supply against **0** removed, a net change of **+7.94%**. The forward 90 days book **1,973.44M ENA** against **0**, a net **+19.55%**. The inflation monitor reads **+8.66%** for the same past window, so the gap is **+0.72pp**, above the 0.5pp line, and a ⚠ chip ships with the reason spelled out: the monitor saw its supply figure rise 804.5M ENA while our calendar adds 801.6M, so both see the same three monthly unlocks, and the monitor divides them by the 9.29B ENA that circulated on Jul 4 2026 while we divide by today's 10.10B. ENA is an unlock-driven token: no coin is printed by the protocol, and the whole supply story is a 15B pool that was minted at launch and is still being released on a calendar.

## Sell pressure: where new ENA comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. ENA pays no block rewards and no staking emissions. The ENA token contract does keep a mint function that lets its owner add up to 10% of supply once a year, but it has never been used: exactly **15B ENA** existed at both ends of the window, and the contract still records its launch day, Mar 5 2024, as the last mint. Because that switch exists, the row is checked on every rebuild rather than marked permanent.

Vesting unlocks are the whole sell side: **801.56M ENA** over the last 90 days. Ethena's published calendar releases about **267.19M ENA** a month: 93.75M to the team, 78.13M to early investors and about 95.31M to the Ethena Foundation and the ecosystem pool. Three of those monthly rounds landed in the window, on Jul 5, Aug 5 and Sep 5 2026. The next 90 days are far heavier. On Aug 27 2026 the Ethena Foundation folded every remaining monthly investor tranche into one release on **Oct 5 2026**: **1.41B ENA**, about 14% of the coins now circulating. Add the team, Foundation and ecosystem rounds on Oct 5, Nov 5 and Dec 5 2026 and the forward column reaches **1.97B ENA**. Ethena also stopped paying ENA incentives to USDe holders on Sep 30 2026, which may slow how fast ecosystem coins actually reach the market, but the calendar itself has not changed, so the framework keeps the ecosystem rounds in.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0** as a separate row, because every locked ENA coin sits on the same dated calendar and is already counted under vesting. Long-term locked or bankruptcy is **0**: there is no estate or trustee paying out ENA. The one long lock in the story, StablecoinX's 48-month lock-up, covers coins that are already counted as circulating, so its end on Oct 5 2026 adds nothing to the count.

## Buy pressure: where new ENA goes

Programmatic buyback is **0** today. In September 2026 ENA holders approved a fee switch: once USDe supply averages **$7.5B** over 14 days, 5% of protocol revenue goes to ENA buybacks, rising step by step to 20% at $20B of USDe. USDe supply is about **$4.90B**, up from $4.45B at the start of the window, so it would need to grow by more than half before the first ENA is bought. Ethena has not said whether bought ENA will be burned, held or locked.

Protocol fee burn is **0**: Ethena burns no ENA, and the dead address held the same 2.63 ENA through the window. Foundation buy is **0**: the Ethena Foundation did buy ENA in August 2026, but it bought investor coins that were still locked, so no traded ENA left the market. New long-term lock is **0**: staked ENA in sENA grew by 136.2M to about 1.17B, but stakers can leave after a cooldown and staked ENA still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the locked pool itself: **4.90B ENA**, made of 1.69B for the team, 1.41B for investors and about 1.81B for the Ethena Foundation and ecosystem. After Oct 5 2026 the investor part is gone, and the rest keeps unlocking month by month into 2028. Part of the Oct 5 investor release goes to the Ethena Foundation itself, because it bought locked coins from early investors that had been selling; it has not disclosed how many coins, at what price or into which wallet, so that pile is tracked as opaque.

The second overhang is StablecoinX, a listed company controlled by Ethena's operating arm, which holds about **3.03B ENA** across three wallets that were funded on Jul 8 and Jul 9 2026 and have not moved since. Those coins already count as circulating, so they add nothing to the ledger, but its lock-up ends on Oct 5 2026; any sale still needs the Foundation's written consent and five business days' notice, during which the Foundation can buy the coins itself. The framework reads these wallets on every rebuild: if either the Foundation pile or the StablecoinX wallets start to fall between refreshes, that outflow is written into the Foundation row at the next refresh.

## How ENA compares to other DeFi governance tokens

ENA sits in the class of DeFi governance tokens with a fixed minted supply and a long insider calendar. As with most DeFi governance tokens, the total was created at launch and the question is only how fast it is released. What sets ENA apart this quarter is the shape of the calendar: most tokens of this type thin out gradually, while Ethena chose to pull 18 months of investor unlocks into a single day. That removes the monthly overhang for good but makes the next 90 days one of the heaviest unlock windows among large tokens.

On the buy side ENA is behind DeFi protocols that already turn trading or lending revenue into regular token buybacks, where fee flows take real coins off the market every week. ENA has the vote but not the trigger: its buyback is tied to USDe size rather than to a fixed date, so it depends on the stablecoin growing by more than half. Compared with chains that pay validators in new coins, ENA has no issuance at all, so once the calendar ends in 2028 its supply would stop growing unless the owner uses the mint function.

USDe supply: the buyback starts only at a 14-day average of **$7.5B**, against about $4.90B today. Also watch for any disclosure of how much ENA the Ethena Foundation bought from investors, and whether bought ENA will be burned, held or locked.

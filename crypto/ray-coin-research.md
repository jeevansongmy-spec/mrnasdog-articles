### When is the next RAY token unlock?

No dated Raydium (RAY) unlock is scheduled in the next 90 days (checked Nov 19 2026). New RAY still arrives: about 425K RAY in 90 days.

### Does Raydium burn or buy back RAY?

Not in a way that removes RAY today: nothing takes RAY off the market over the next 90 days (checked Nov 19 2026). 12% of every trading fee buys RAY: 4.44M in 90 days. Raydium keeps the coins in a wallet already counted as circulating, so nothing leaves the count, and no fees are burned.

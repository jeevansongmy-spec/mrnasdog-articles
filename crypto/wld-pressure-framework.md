# WLD Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

World's WLD supply is growing and is projected to keep growing: the Pressure Framework reads WLD at **+4.15%** over the last 90 days and **+3.42%** over the next 90, against a monitor reading of **+5.70%**. No new WLD can be minted — all **10,000M WLD** exist already and the token's code blocks any mint before **Jul 24 2038** — so every point comes from existing coins reaching the market: **134.4M WLD** of Tools for Humanity team and investor unlocks plus **17.1M WLD** paid out of World Foundation wallets, for **151.5M WLD** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure. The large community unlock that most trackers count goes into foundation custody, not the market.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **3,647.0M WLD**, the framework books **151.5M WLD** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days — a net of **+4.15%** — and projects **+3.42%** for the next 90 days now that the team and investor unlock pace has stepped down. The inflation monitor reads **+5.70%** for the same window, a gap of **1.55 percentage points**, which is over the framework's tolerance and ships with a monitor-gap warning on the overview. About **0.31 points** of that gap is base convention, because the monitor divides by the supply of 90 days ago. The other **1.24 points** is supply growth the monitor recorded that no on-chain release out of foundation wallets or the team schedule can account for; the monitor also briefly counted the tokens World sold in **Jul 2026** and then removed them again. The label for WLD is **a fixed-supply token whose team unlock still feeds the market**.

## Sell pressure: where new WLD comes from

It does not come from minting. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**. The WLD token on Ethereum read exactly **10,000M WLD** of total supply at both ends of the window. Its one-time launch mint is marked as done, no minter address is set, and the earliest date on which any new WLD could be minted is written into the contract as a fixed value: **Jul 24 2038**. After that date, World governance may switch on a capped mint of up to about **1.5%** a year. Until then, WLD supply cannot grow, and the World Chain and Optimism versions of WLD are bridge copies of the same coins, not extra ones.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **134.4M WLD**, and it is the bulk of the reading. Tools for Humanity team members and investors hold about a quarter of all WLD, and their tokens unlock a little every day with no cliffs. The pace was about **1.9M WLD** a day until **Jul 24 2026**, when a shorter two-year lockup covering part of those tokens ended; since then it is about **1.3M WLD** a day, which runs until **Jul 2028** with about **872M WLD** still to unlock. These tokens count as circulating on that schedule, whether or not a holder has moved them yet, so the schedule is the number.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **4.46M WLD**: one World Foundation wallet paid out **4.4M WLD** on **Aug 10 2026**, with no schedule behind it, so nothing is projected forward. A further row, Sell #5, covers grants to verified humans: World ID holders claim WLD from foundation wallets on World Chain, and those wallets fell **12.6M WLD** across the window, net of coins moved in from the old network. That pace roughly halved after community emission stepped down on **Jul 24 2026**, so the next 90 days carry about **7.7M WLD**. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0** — WLD has no estate, trustee or court-ordered distribution.

What is missing from that list matters as much. The World community allocation unlocks about **1.6M WLD** a day, and a **674.6M WLD** release landed on **Aug 11 2026**. None of it reached the market: every community unlock flows into the World Foundation's cold wallet, which sits outside the counted float. It becomes sell pressure only when the foundation spends it.

## Buy pressure: where new WLD goes

Nowhere, this window. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**: the World Foundation funds itself by selling WLD, not by buying it back. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0** as well. Gas on World Chain is paid in ETH, the WLD token has no burn function, total supply read **10,000M WLD** at both ends, and the dead address on all three networks moved by only a few hundred WLD of user dust — so both burn surfaces agree that nothing was destroyed.

Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0**; the only coins flowing into foundation wallets from outside were a small migration inflow from the old network, already netted in the grants row. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**. The **Jul 24 2026** strategic sale placed **44.4M WLD** in one-year lock contracts, but those coins came straight out of foundation custody and were never tradable, so locking them removed nothing from the market.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang on WLD is very large, and almost all of it is discretionary. The World Foundation cold wallet holds **1,397.9M WLD**, fed by the community unlock. Three community vesting contracts still hold **3,500M WLD**: the 2026–2029 tranche of **1,750M WLD**, of which **97.3M WLD** is already unlocked and ready to claim, and two later tranches of **875M WLD** each that start in **Jul 2029** and **Jul 2032**. A custody wallet funded only by the foundation holds **163.7M WLD**, and grant and operating wallets on World Chain hold about **91.4M WLD**.

The newest item is the foundation's own sales. World raised **$52.5M** on **Jul 24 2026** by selling **194.4M WLD** at about **$0.27** to strategic buyers, with a one-year lockup, and delivered another **39.4M WLD** to buyers on **Sep 7 2026** that is also held outside the float — **233.7M WLD** that can reach the market from about **Jul 2027**. All of these balances are read on-chain at every rebuild. If any of them falls between refreshes by more than the grants and schedule explain, that outflow enters the Foundation row at the next refresh.

## How WLD compares to other fixed-supply tokens with a long unlock

WLD belongs to the class of tokens that were fully minted at launch and then released on a long calendar. That is a different promise from a halving chain like Bitcoin, which keeps minting on every block at a falling rate. World mints nothing at all until **2038**, so on the pure issuance axis WLD is stricter than any proof-of-work coin and stricter than every staking chain that pays validators with new coins.

And yet WLD reads **+4.15%** a quarter, far above a mature capped chain. A fixed total caps supply; it does not cap the tradable float. WLD looks much more like a recently launched venture-backed token working through a four-year team and investor vest, with one twist: the biggest pool, the community allocation, is not on a calendar to the market at all. It sits with a foundation that sells when it needs funding — **$65M** of sales in **Mar 2026** and **$52.5M** in **Jul 2026**. That makes WLD's float less predictable than a pure vesting token.

Compared with exchange tokens that run buybacks and burns, WLD has no offset at all. Those tokens can read flat or negative because a share of revenue removes coins; WLD has no revenue-linked removal, so the unlock is the whole story.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the team and investor unlock, which runs at about **1.3M WLD** a day through **Dec 22 2026** and drives nearly all of the **+3.42%** forward reading. Second, the World Foundation cold wallet at **1,397.9M WLD**: any large transfer out of it to buyers without a lockup lands in the Foundation row. Third, the **97.3M WLD** already claimable from the 2026–2029 vesting tranche, which moves into foundation custody when claimed and matters only if it is then sold. Fourth, the grant wallets on World Chain after the **Sep 17 2026**launch of World's money app, in case grant payouts speed up again. Fifth, any further close of the strategic sale, and whether its coins arrive locked like the first two.

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads World's WLD at **+4.15%** over the trailing 90 days and **+3.42%** projected forward: supply growing, projected to keep growing.

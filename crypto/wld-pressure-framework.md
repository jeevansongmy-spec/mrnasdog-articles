# WLD Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
The World coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/wld](https://mrnasdog.com/research/wld). Below: supply, line by line.

World's WLD supply is growing because locked coins keep reaching the market: up **+5.62%** in the 90 days from Aug 4 to Nov 2 2026, and on course for about **+3.21%** in the next 90 days. Team and investor unlocks added **117M WLD**, the end of a six-month lock on March 2026 sale coins and one Foundation transfer added **90.1M WLD**, and airdrops added **3.06M WLD**, while nothing buys WLD back or burns it. No new WLD can be minted before Jul 24 2038, so the **10B** made at launch is the ceiling for now.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 2 2026, the WLD that people can trade grew by **210.16M WLD**, or **+5.62%** of the **3.74B WLD** now circulating. Other trackers show **+4.48%** for the same period, a gap of **1.14 points**. Almost all of that gap comes from one day: on Sep 14 2026 their supply figure dropped by **64.2M WLD** with no matching move on chain. Once that day is set aside the two readings agree, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days the count falls to about **120.06M WLD**, or **+3.21%**, because no sale lock ends before July 2027. The label that fits WLD is a fixed-supply token that inflates its float through unlocks.

## Sell pressure: where new WLD comes from

Protocol inflation is zero. All **10B WLD** were created when World launched in July 2023, the total still reads exactly 10B, and the token's code blocks any minting until Jul 24 2038, after which a mint would be capped at 1.5% a year.

Vesting unlocks are the steady source. Coins held by the Tools for Humanity team and its early investors unlock a little every day, and since Jul 24 2026 the pace has been about **1.3M WLD** a day, down from 1.9M before that date. Over 90 days that is **117M WLD**, with no single large unlock day, and the schedule runs on until mid-2028.

Foundation and unscheduled releases added **90.1M WLD**. The World Foundation sold WLD to four buyers in March 2026, and part of that sale was locked for six months. When the lock ended, one buyer wallet moved **37.72M WLD** on Sep 16 2026 and another moved **42.38M WLD** on Sep 22 2026. Separately, the Foundation sent **10M WLD** to an outside custody wallet on Sep 11 2026. Nothing like this is scheduled for the next 90 days, so this row books zero going forward.

Long-term locked coins add nothing yet. About **399M WLD** sold to funds between Jul 24 and Oct 28 2026 carry a one-year lock and cannot be sold before Jul 24 2027. No court estate or bankruptcy pile of WLD exists.

User airdrops are the last and smallest source. People who verified their World ID before Jun 1 2026 still claim a monthly WLD airdrop until their 12-month cycle ends, and those claims paid out **3.06M WLD** in the window. The pace keeps slowing, because World stopped creating new airdrop cycles on Jun 1 2026.

## Buy pressure: where new WLD goes

WLD has no buyback. The World Foundation sells WLD to fund its work and does not buy any back.

WLD has no fee burn either. Fees on World Chain are paid in ETH, and the WLD total stayed at exactly 10B for the whole window.

No Foundation buying takes coins out of the market. A listed company, Eightco, reported about **302M WLD** on Aug 20 2026, but it buys coins that already circulate, so the count does not change.

New long-term locks remove nothing from circulation. The one-year locks on the 2026 sale coins hold WLD that came straight from the Foundation, not from the market, so buy pressure for WLD stays at zero.

## Foundation and overhang

The World Foundation controls most of the WLD that is not yet circulating. Its main cold wallet holds about **1.18B WLD**. Three community unlock contracts hold another **3.5B WLD** and pay the Foundation about 1.6M WLD a day; of that, **162.6M WLD** can be claimed right now. Two working wallets hold **67.2M WLD** between them, and the airdrop wallets on World Chain hold **51.1M WLD**. None of these coins count as circulating today, and moving them between Foundation wallets changes nothing.

The team and investor coins still locked come to roughly **820M WLD** at the published pace, and the **399M WLD** of locked sale coins open between Jul 24 and Oct 22 2027. We count none of it until it moves. If the Foundation's cold wallet or working wallets fall between checks because coins went to outside buyers, that outflow is added to Foundation releases at the next check.

## How WLD compares to other fixed-supply Layer 2 chains

WLD sits in the same family as other tokens tied to Ethereum Layer 2 networks that minted their whole supply up front and release it on a schedule. Like those tokens, WLD has no mining or staking reward that creates new coins, so all of its supply growth is coins moving from locked or Foundation hands into the market. The difference is shape: many unlock-driven tokens release large amounts on one day each month, while WLD's team and investor coins drip out every day with no cliff.

WLD also differs from chains whose own coin pays for gas. On networks such as Ethereum, a fee burn takes part of the new supply back. World Chain charges its fees in ETH, so heavy use of World App and World ID does not burn a single WLD, and nothing on the network buys WLD back.

The biggest structural difference is how much stays in one set of hands. About **63%** of all WLD is still outside circulation, most of it with the World Foundation, which decides when community coins are sold, granted or locked. That makes Foundation sales, not a fixed schedule, the main swing factor in WLD's supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Team and investor unlocks continue at about 1.3M WLD a day through Jan 31 2027, adding roughly 117M WLD over the next 90 days.

The Foundation can claim the 162.6M WLD already released by its community unlock contract at any time; a claim alone adds nothing, but a sale of those coins after Nov 2 2026 would add to Foundation releases.

Monthly airdrop claims keep shrinking as 12-month cycles end, and the last cycles started before Jun 1 2026 finish by May 31 2027.

A US spot WLD fund filed on Jul 20 2026 is still waiting to start trading; if it opens, its buying would come from coins that already circulate.

The first locked sale coins open on Jul 24 2027, after this window, and that date is the next large step in WLD's supply.

The main risk is the World Foundation, which holds billions of WLD and about 399M WLD of locked sale coins that open from Jul 24 2027, while no new WLD can be minted before Jul 24 2038.

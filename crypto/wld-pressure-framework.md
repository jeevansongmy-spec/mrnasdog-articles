# WLD Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
The World coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/wld](https://mrnasdog.com/research/wld). Below: supply, line by line.

WLD supply grew about **+5.59%** in the 90 days from Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026, and it should grow about **+3.19%** in the next 90 days: supply growing, projected to keep growing. No new WLD can be minted before Jul 24 2038, so all of the growth comes from coins that already exist leaving their locks: **117M WLD** of investor and team unlocks, **90.1M WLD** of World Foundation releases and **2.72M WLD** of user grants. Nothing came back out, because WLD has no buyback and no burn. Other trackers show **+4.45%** for the same 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Across Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026, **209.81M WLD** joined the roughly **3.75B WLD** that circulate, and none left, for a net of **+5.59%**. For the next 90 days, to Feb 9 2027, the investor and team unlock plus the grants add about **119.7M WLD**, or **+3.19%**, because the one-off Foundation releases of September 2026 have no follow-up on any calendar. Other trackers read **+4.45%**, a difference of **1.15 points**. Their circulating figure for WLD dropped by about 64M WLD on Sep 14 2026 without any WLD moving on-chain that day, which is why their 90-day number runs lower; that one step accounts for the difference, so the WLD coin page carries no warning note. The label for WLD today: a fixed-supply token that still dilutes holders by unlocks, with nothing on the other side of the ledger.

## Sell pressure: where new WLD comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. All 10B WLD were minted at launch in July 2023, and the WLD contract on Ethereum refuses any new mint until Jul 24 2038. After that date the contract allows at most about 1.5% a year, and only if World governance chooses to switch it on.

Vesting unlocks are the largest flow: **117M WLD** in 90 days. Tools for Humanity's investors and team receive about 1.3M WLD every day, down from 1.9M a day before Jul 24 2026, when the shorter part of their schedule finished. These coins are free to sell as soon as they unlock, and the stream runs, a little smaller each year, until around July 2028. The next 90 days should bring the same **117M WLD**.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **90.1M WLD**. In March 2026 the World Foundation sold about $65M of WLD to four buyers, and about $25M of that carried a six-month lock. Those coins, about **80.1M WLD** in two wallets, moved on Sep 16 and Sep 22 2026 as the lock ended. The Foundation also sent **10M WLD** to a custody wallet on Sep 11 2026. No similar release is scheduled for the next 90 days, so this row books zero going forward.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins are **0**: no court case or failed company holds WLD waiting to be paid out. User grants, booked as the fifth sell row, added **2.72M WLD**. Verified World ID holders can still claim a small monthly grant, now under 1 WLD a claim, and the pace keeps slowing because World is moving away from token rewards in what it calls Phase 3.

## Buy pressure: where new WLD goes

Every buy row on the WLD ledger is **0**. There is no programmatic buyback: neither the World Foundation nor Tools for Humanity buys WLD back. Listed companies that keep WLD in their treasury buy coins that already circulate, so their buying moves WLD from one holder to another and takes nothing out of the count.

There is no protocol fee burn either. Transactions on World Chain pay their gas in ETH, so heavy use of the World App and World Chain destroys no WLD. The Foundation is a seller, not a buyer: it sells WLD to pay for Orbs, research and operations, and it bought none back between Aug 13 and Nov 11 2026. No circulating WLD was locked away for the long term; the new locks of 2026 sit on coins the Foundation sold straight from its own reserve, which were never circulating in the first place.

## Foundation and overhang

The World Foundation controls most of the WLD that does not yet circulate. Its three unlock contracts on Ethereum hold **3.5B WLD**: 1.75B WLD unlocking to the Foundation from Jul 24 2026 to Jul 24 2029, then two batches of 875M WLD that start unlocking in 2029 and 2032. The Foundation's main wallet holds about **1.18B WLD**, down from 1.40B WLD on Aug 13 2026 after it handed 214.7M WLD to buyers and to its own operating wallets on Sep 28 2026. A second Foundation wallet holds **61.8M WLD**.

Buyers from the Foundation's 2026 sales hold another large block outside the float. About 205M WLD delivered in September and October 2026 has not moved since it arrived, part of it inside a lock contract that opens on Oct 22 2027, and the coins from the $52.5M sale of July 2026 are locked until July 2027. Tools for Humanity's investors and team still have several hundred million WLD to unlock at about 1.3M WLD a day. We read these wallets at every rebuild; if the Foundation's balance falls between checks, the coins that leave it enter the Foundation row at the next check.

## How WLD compares to other fixed-supply network tokens

WLD belongs to the large group of tokens with a fixed supply that was created in full on day one and then released on a schedule. For tokens like this, the real question is not how many coins exist but how fast the locked ones reach the market. WLD's answer is fast: with only about 37.5% of the 10B supply circulating, the investor and team stream alone adds about 3.1% to the float every 90 days, and the Foundation's own sales add more in bursts.

Compared with Bitcoin, which has a hard cap, no team allocation and new coins only from mining, WLD has no mining at all but a much bigger locked pile waiting to be released. Compared with Ethereum, which mints new coins for validators but burns part of every fee, WLD mints nothing today yet has no burn to offset its unlocks. Compared with other fixed-supply network tokens that pay investors and teams in large monthly batches, WLD drips its unlocks out every day, which spreads the selling out but never pauses it.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the investor and team unlock of about 1.3M WLD a day keeps running through Feb 9 2027; any change to that pace would come from World itself, as the Jul 24 2026 cut did. Second, the Foundation's main wallet of about 1.18B WLD: on Sep 28 2026 it sent out 214.7M WLD in one day, and the next large outflow would show up there first.

Third, the 1.75B WLD unlock contract that has been unlocking to the Foundation since Jul 24 2026 had not paid out any WLD by Nov 11 2026; a claim moves those coins into Foundation wallets, closer to the market. Fourth, any new Foundation sale: the Foundation sold WLD in March and July 2026 and handed more coins to buyers in September and October, and a sale without a lock would add to the float at once. Fifth, the monthly grant size, which World sets and has cut almost every month during 2026.

## Summary

WLD supply grew **+5.59%** in the 90 days to Nov 11 2026 and should grow about **+3.19%** in the next 90 days, with no WLD burned or bought back to offset it. World cannot mint new WLD before Jul 24 2038, so the growth comes from **117M WLD** of investor and team unlocks, **90.1M WLD** of World Foundation releases and **2.72M WLD** of user grants. The main risk is the size of the locked pile: the World Foundation alone controls about 4.7B WLD in its unlock contracts and wallets, more than the 3.75B WLD that circulate today. The ceiling stays fixed at 10B WLD until 2038, but holders should expect steady dilution until the investor and team schedule ends around July 2028.

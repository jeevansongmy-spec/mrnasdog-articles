# WLD Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
The World coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/wld](https://mrnasdog.com/research/wld). Below: supply, line by line.

**WLD is inflationary on its circulating float**, even though the World token contract cannot mint a single new coin before 2038. In the 90 days to Oct 14 2026, **338.1M WLD** entered the market — **134.7M** from team and investor vesting unlocks and **203.4M** released by the World Foundation and its token company — while nothing was bought back or burned. That is **+8.87%** of the **3.81B** WLD in circulation, with **+8.73%** projected for the next 90 days; the inflation monitor reads **+7.80%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework puts WLD at **+8.87% net over the last 90 days** and **+8.73% over the next 90**. The inflation monitor reads **+7.80%**, a gap of **1.07 percentage points**. That is above our 0.5-point line, so we walked every source class again. The walk found the reason: the monitor's starting point, Jul 11 2026, still uses a supply figure 64.9M WLD higher than World's own, later-corrected count for that day, because its upstream supply series was stepped down on Sep 14 2026 without rewriting older days. On the corrected series the two readings agree, so no warning chip is shown. In one line: **WLD is a fixed-cap token that still inflates its float by about 9% a quarter, through unlocks and Foundation releases, with no buyback or burn to absorb them**.

## Sell pressure: where new WLD comes from

**Protocol inflation is 0.** All 10B WLD were minted when World launched on Jul 24 2023. The WLD contract only allows new minting through a single function that is time-locked until Jul 24 2038, the minter role is empty, and the one-time launch mint is already used. We called that mint function on Oct 14 2026 and it failed, as the code says it should. After 2038 the contract caps any new WLD at about 1.5% a year, and only if governance turns it on.

**Vesting unlocks added 134.7M WLD.** Team and investor coins from Tools for Humanity unlock a little every day, with no single-day cliff. World cut that pace on Jul 24 2026, as it announced in April: about 1.88M WLD a day before, about 1.46M a day since. Across the window that is **60.8M** for the team and **73.8M** for investors. The same schedule gives **129.3M WLD** for the next 90 days, and close to 1B team and investor WLD are still locked until July 2028.

**Foundation and unscheduled releases added 203.4M WLD**, the largest row. The World Foundation and its token company, World Assets, hold the 7.5B community allocation and decide when it goes out. The biggest single move came on Sep 30 2026, when 105M WLD left a World Assets wallet for an outside wallet that the supply count treats as circulating. About 115M more, mostly coins from earlier Foundation sales, became free between Sep 12 and Sep 26 2026, and about 11M went to users and Orb operators as grants and rewards. These releases are not on a published calendar, but they have happened every quarter for a year (from about 95M to 345M a quarter), so we project the same 203.4M for the next 90 days. The user grant part is shrinking: World stopped opening new monthly airdrop cycles on Jun 1 2026.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy is 0.** No court estate or trustee holds WLD. About 358M WLD that the Foundation sold in July and September 2026 sits under one-year locks with the buyers. Those coins are not counted as circulating yet, and their locks end from July 2027, outside the next 90 days.

## Buy pressure: where new WLD goes

**Every buy row is 0.** There is no programmatic buyback: the Foundation sells WLD to fund the network and has announced no plan to buy it back. There is no fee burn: WLD has no burn function, total supply read exactly 10B at both ends of the window, and the dead address on World Chain gained only 175 WLD that users sent there by hand. There was no Foundation buy: the Foundation and World Assets only sold or paid out WLD in this window. And there is no new long-term lock that removes coins from the float. WLD has no staking, and the one-year locks on the Foundation's sale lots cover coins that were never counted as circulating, so they take nothing out. Listed companies that build WLD treasuries buy coins that are already in the market; that is demand, not a change in supply.

## Foundation and overhang

The WLD overhang is large and almost all of it sits with the Foundation side. Three community unlock contracts on Ethereum still hold **3.5B WLD** (1.75B, 875M and 875M), releasing into the Foundation's hands at about 1.6M a day until July 2029 and more slowly after that until 2038; a fourth contract emptied its last 674.6M WLD into World Assets on Aug 11 2026. The World Assets main wallet holds about **1.19B WLD**, up from 941M at the start of the window. Grant, reward and payout wallets hold about **175M WLD** more, and close to **1B** team and investor WLD remain locked off-chain. We read the Foundation wallets on the chain at every rebuild. If any of these balances falls between refreshes and the coins land in a wallet counted as circulating, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How WLD compares to other fixed-supply tokens with big unlocks

WLD sits in an unusual spot. Like Bitcoin it has a hard cap set in code, with zero minting for its first 15 years, so "inflation" here never means new coins. But unlike Bitcoin, most WLD already exists and is simply not circulating yet: 3.81B of 10B, or about 38%. For WLD the question is how fast the other 62% leaves locked contracts and Foundation wallets, and that pace is set by a schedule plus the Foundation's own choices.

That makes WLD closer to young venture-backed tokens with long unlock tails than to proof-of-work coins. The difference is size and length: World's community share unlocks over 15 years, team and investor coins over five, and the Foundation must keep selling WLD to pay for Orbs and operations. Tokens that pair unlocks with a fee burn or a buyback can absorb part of the new float; WLD has neither today. World has said World ID fees could one day fund buying, but no such mechanism is live.

Against inflationary layer-1 chains that pay stakers in new coins, WLD's roughly 9% a quarter is far higher, but its cause is different: it is a release of existing supply, which slows over time by design (the community pace halves every few years), not a permanent issuance rate.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Foundation sales.** World Assets sold WLD in March, July and September 2026. The next sale, and whether the buyers take locks, decides most of the Sell #3 row.

**The World Assets main wallet.** It held about 1.19B WLD on Oct 14 2026; a falling balance is the earliest sign of new releases.

**The unlock pace.** Team and investor unlocks run at about 1.44M WLD a day on the schedule through Jan 12 2027, adding about 129.3M WLD.

**A spot WLD fund in the US.** A fund manager filed in July 2026 to list a spot WLD fund on Nasdaq; its fee and start date were still blank. Approval would be new demand, not new supply.

## Summary

WLD cannot be minted before Jul 24 2038, yet its circulating supply grew **8.87%** in the 90 days to Oct 14 2026, and we project **8.73%** for the next 90. The growth comes from **134.7M WLD** of team and investor unlocks and **203.4M WLD** released by the World Foundation, mostly through sales, with no buyback or burn on the other side. The key risk is the size of what is still held back — 3.5B WLD in unlock contracts, about 1.19B with World Assets and close to 1B team and investor coins — because the Foundation, not a fixed calendar, decides most of the pace. The ceiling is the 10B cap; the float is only 38% of the way there.

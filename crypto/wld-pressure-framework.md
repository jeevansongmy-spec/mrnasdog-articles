# WLD Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The World coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/wld](https://mrnasdog.com/research/wld). Below: supply, line by line.

World cannot mint a single new WLD before **Jul 24 2038**, yet the number of WLD in circulation still rose fast. From Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 about **239.5M WLD** entered circulation and nothing left it, a rise of **+6.31%**. About **119.4M WLD** is set for the next 90 days, or **+3.15%**, and other trackers show **+5.10%** for the last 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

WLD supply is growing and is set to keep growing. Over the 90 days to Nov 21 2026 the coins in circulation rose by **+6.31%**, and the next 90 days should add about **+3.15%**, about half as much, because no Foundation release is scheduled. Other trackers show **+5.10%** for the same 90 days, a gap of about **1.21 points**. Most of that gap comes from one day, Sep 14 2026, when the market figure dropped 62.3M WLD without any WLD moving on-chain, so the coin page carries no warning note. The label is simple: growing, with unlocks that are scheduled to run until July 2028 and a Foundation that still holds billions of WLD.

## Sell pressure: where new WLD comes from

**1. Protocol inflation — 0.** All 10B WLD were created at launch in July 2023. The token contract has no minter, and it blocks any new WLD until Jul 24 2038. After that date holders could vote to add at most 1.5% a year.

**2. Vesting unlocks — about 117M WLD.** Coins held by investors and the team at Tools for Humanity, the company that built World, unlock a little every day. Since Jul 24 2026 that pace has been about **1.3M WLD a day**, down from about 1.9M before. Over 90 days that is **117M WLD**, and the same pace runs into 2027, with the last of these coins unlocking around July 2028.

**3. Foundation and unscheduled releases — about 120.1M WLD.** The World Foundation sold WLD in March 2026, and part of that sale was locked for six months. Those buyers moved **80.1M WLD** once the lock ended, on Sep 16 and Sep 22 2026, and part of it went straight to an exchange. The Foundation also sent **40M WLD** to a custody wallet on Sep 11 and Nov 13 2026. The Foundation's sales have no schedule, so none is counted for the next 90 days.

**4. Long-term locked or bankruptcy — 0.** No bankrupt firm or court holds WLD. The buyers of the Foundation's later sales are still locked, and none of their coins can trade before Feb 19 2027.

**5. User grants — about 2.41M WLD.** People who verified with the Orb before Jun 1 2026 still claim small monthly grants in World App. The main grant wallet paid out **2.41M WLD** in the 90 days. New sign-ups no longer get grants, so this flow keeps shrinking.

## Buy pressure: where new WLD goes

**1. Programmatic buyback — 0.** Nothing buys WLD back. No contract, fee or treasury plan takes WLD out of the market.

**2. Protocol fee burn — 0.** No WLD is burned. Fees on World Chain are paid in ETH, and the total stayed at exactly **10B WLD** from Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026.

**3. Foundation buy — 0.** The World Foundation sold WLD in March, July and September 2026 and bought none. Eightco, a listed company that keeps WLD as its treasury, held about **302M WLD** on Sep 30 2026, but it buys coins that were already circulating, so its buying does not change the count.

**4. New long-term lock — 0.** The new buyer locks of September and October 2026 hold coins that came straight from the Foundation and were never circulating, so they remove nothing.

## Foundation and overhang

The World Foundation is the largest holder by far. Its main wallet holds about **1.18B WLD**, and four community unlock contracts hold another **3.5B WLD** for it. One of those contracts, with 1.75B WLD, has been releasing coins to the Foundation day by day since Jul 24 2026, about 1.6M WLD a day, but the Foundation has not drawn on it yet: about **193M WLD** is ready to draw. Two smaller Foundation wallets hold about **31.8M WLD** and **5.5M WLD**.

None of these coins count as circulating until the Foundation hands them out, through sales, grants, payments to Orb operators or custody moves. That is why the Foundation row can jump in a single week, as it did in September 2026.

Buyers of the Foundation's own sales are the second overhang. The July 24 2026 sale, which raised **$52.5M**, is locked until about Jul 24 2027. A **40.7M WLD** lot is locked until Oct 22 2027. Another **164.1M WLD** went to buyers on Sep 7 and Sep 28 2026 with no public end date; none of it has moved. The investor and team coins still locked, close to **778M WLD**, unlock on the schedule above.

## How WLD compares to other Layer 2 chains

World Chain is a Layer 2 network built on Ethereum, and WLD works much like the tokens of other Layer 2 chains: a fixed 10B supply created at launch, with most of it held back and released over years. The difference is in the timing and in who decides.

Many Layer 2 tokens release team and investor coins in large monthly batches, so their supply jumps on known dates. WLD releases them in a small drip every day, so there is no single unlock day to watch. Some Layer 2 tokens also let holders vote to add new coins each year. WLD cannot add any before 2038.

The bigger difference is the community share. Three quarters of all WLD, **7.5B**, belongs to the World community, and the World Foundation decides when those coins reach the market. That makes WLD's supply more discretionary than most: the schedule caps how fast the Foundation can draw coins, but the Foundation chooses when to sell or hand them out. Fees also differ. Like most Layer 2 chains, World Chain charges fees in ETH, so heavy use does not burn or lock any WLD.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Daily unlocks, every day to Feb 19 2027.** About 1.3M WLD a day from investors and the team, close to 117M WLD over the 90 days. This is the one flow that is certain.

**A new Foundation sale.** The Foundation sold WLD on Mar 20, Jul 24 and Sep 28 2026, about two to four months apart, with no schedule. Another sale before Feb 19 2027 would add to supply only if its coins can trade at once; locked sales add nothing until the lock ends.

**The 1.75B WLD community contract.** It has been releasing coins to the Foundation since Jul 24 2026. A large draw would not change circulating supply by itself, but it would show the Foundation preparing to spend.

**The custody wallet.** The Foundation topped it up on Sep 11 and Nov 13 2026. Further top-ups would count as new supply.

**Grayscale's WLD fund.** Grayscale filed on Jul 20 2026 to list a WLD fund on Nasdaq. It has no approval date. A launch would add buyers, not supply.

## Summary

WLD supply grew **+6.31%** from Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026, with about **239.5M WLD** entering circulation and none leaving it.

The World token contract cannot mint new WLD before Jul 24 2038, so all of that growth came from investor and team unlocks, World Foundation releases and user grants.

About **119.4M WLD**, or **+3.15%**, is set to enter circulation over the next 90 days, almost all from the investor and team unlocks, and more if the World Foundation sells again.

Nothing buys back or burns WLD, so new WLD has no built-in buyer.

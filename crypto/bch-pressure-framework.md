# BCH Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

*Originally published at [mrnasdog.com/research/bch/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/bch/inflation).*

Bitcoin Cash supply is roughly steady: miners earned **40,600 BCH** of new coins between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026, nothing was burned or bought back, and the count grew a net **+0.20%**, against **+0.12%** on other trackers. The only source of new BCH is the block reward, a fixed **3.125 BCH** for every block, and the total can never pass **21M**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 19 2026 the Bitcoin Cash supply grew a net **+0.20%**, and the next 90 days should add about the same **+0.20%**. Other supply trackers show **+0.12%** for the same stretch, a difference of **0.08** of a percentage point. That is small enough that the coin page carries no warning note. All of the change is the block reward paid to miners; there is no unlock, no burn and no buyback on either side. In one line: Bitcoin Cash is a **quiet, fixed-schedule mined coin**, adding new coins at a slow and known pace.

## Sell pressure: where new BCH comes from

Protocol inflation is the whole story. Each block pays the miner who found it **3.125 BCH**, a reward that has held since the April 2024 halving. From Aug 21 to Nov 19 2026 miners found **12,992 blocks**, slightly more than the 12,960 a perfect ten-minute pace would give, which added **40,600 BCH**, or about **451 BCH** a day. Miners usually sell a large share of these coins to pay for power and machines, so this is real selling that the market has to absorb. The Bitcoin Cash difficulty rule steers blocks back toward one every ten minutes, so the next 90 days should add close to the same **40,600 BCH**.

Vesting unlocks are zero. Bitcoin Cash split from Bitcoin in August 2017 and copied every holder's balance at the moment of the split. There was no token sale, no team share and no investor share, so no locked batch of BCH is waiting to be released.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero. No foundation, company or development fund holds a BCH reserve, and the block reward goes to miners only, with no slice set aside for developers. There is no public evidence of any new release in the window, and the row stays monitored.

Long-term locked and bankruptcy holdings are zero as well. The Mt. Gox estate is still paying back creditors of the exchange that collapsed in 2014, and its deadline was moved to **Oct 31 2027**. The estate has not said how much BCH it still holds. Those coins were mined long ago and already count as circulating, so paying them out does not add a single new BCH, even if creditors later sell them.

## Buy pressure: where new BCH goes

Programmatic buyback is zero. No contract, treasury or company buys BCH back for the network, and transaction fees, about **26 BCH** over the whole window, go to miners rather than to a buyback.

The protocol fee burn is zero. Bitcoin Cash has no burn built into the network. Between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026 the on-chain supply rose by exactly the miners' new coins, and the best-known burn address received nothing, so no BCH was destroyed in the window.

Foundation buying is zero, because there is no foundation. The largest steady buyer is a listed company, mF International, which held **678,922 BCH** on Oct 15 2026 and is closing its old trading business to focus on its Bitcoin Cash treasury and its own mining. Its purchases come out of coins that were already circulating, so they take nothing off the count, even though they can matter a lot for price.

New long-term locks are zero. Bitcoin Cash is secured by mining, not staking, so no coins have to be locked to run the network, and no lock-up plan has been announced.

## Foundation and overhang

Bitcoin Cash has no team-controlled overhang. With no premine, no sale and no foundation, there is no project wallet holding a reserve that could be sold on a schedule. Nearly every mined BCH already counts as circulating: the gap between the circulating count and the total is about **3 BCH**, a single block.

The one large holder worth naming is the Mt. Gox estate. Its BCH balance is not published, and its payout deadline is **Oct 31 2027**, outside the next 90 days. The estate is checked at every rebuild of this page. If the estate's balance falls between refreshes, the outflow is reviewed for the sell side at the next refresh; because those coins already circulate, a payout is expected to stay a move between holders rather than new supply.

## How BCH compares to other proof-of-work chains

Bitcoin Cash shares Bitcoin's money rules exactly: the same **21M** cap, the same halving every 210,000 blocks and the same **3.125** coin reward since April 2024. That means BCH and BTC add new coins at nearly the same yearly rate, a little under **1%**. The difference is who pays for security: BCH fees are small, so its miners rely almost fully on the block reward, and each halving cuts their income harder than it does on Bitcoin.

Litecoin follows the same halving model with a larger **84M** cap and faster blocks, so it also has a known end point and a shrinking reward. Dogecoin is the opposite case: it pays a flat reward with no cap at all, so new coins never stop. Next to those, Bitcoin Cash sits in the hard-cap group, where supply growth only ever falls.

Compared with most proof-of-stake chains, BCH has no staking yield, no fee burn and no foundation treasury. There are no unlock cliffs to watch and no burn that could flip the count to shrinking. The whole supply picture fits in one number, the block reward.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the node software release for the **May 15 2027** network upgrade. The Faster Blocks plan would cut the time between blocks from ten minutes to one and the reward per block to a tenth, so the coins added per hour stay the same; the release will show whether it is in.

Second, the planned conversion of Grayscale's Bitcoin Cash trust into a listed fund, still pending in November 2026. A listed fund buys real BCH, but from coins that already circulate.

Third, the next holdings update from mF International after its **678,922 BCH** count of Oct 15 2026, and any Mt. Gox movements ahead of its **Oct 31 2027** deadline.

Fourth, the next halving at block **1,050,000**, expected around April 2028. It is outside this window, but every month brings it closer, and it will cut new supply in half.

## Summary

Bitcoin Cash added **40,600 BCH** in the 90 days to Nov 19 2026, all of it paid to miners as block rewards, for a net supply change of **+0.20%**.

Bitcoin Cash has no unlocks, no foundation reserve, no burn and no buyback, so nothing else moves the count in either direction.

The next 90 days should add about the same **+0.20%**, and supply growth only falls from here, halving again around April 2028 on the way to a **21M** cap.

# TAO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Checked Nov 1 2026*

Bittensor's supply is growing fast for a capped coin: up **+4.17%** in the 90 days from Aug 3 to Nov 1 2026, and on course for about **+2.71%** in the next 90 days. The network paid out **323,705 TAO** of block pay and made a one-time **172,001 TAO** for root stakers on Sep 15 2026, while fees and registration costs removed **8,136 TAO**; TAO has a hard cap of 21M, no vesting and no buyback. Other trackers show **+21.70%** for the same period because their supply figure stood still for months and then caught up with the chain in two jumps, so the coin page carries a warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

TAO is inflationary today. Net new supply over the last 90 days was **+4.17%** of the **11.68M TAO** in circulation, and about **+2.71%** is expected over the next 90 days, once the one-time root top-up drops out. Other trackers report **+21.70%**, about **17.5 points** higher, because the supply figure they use sat near 9.6M TAO until Sep 14 2026, then jumped 1.75M TAO on Sep 15 2026 and 0.32M TAO on Oct 27 2026. Those jumps were recounts of coins made long before, not new TAO, so the coin page carries a warning note and the on-chain figure stands. The label for TAO is **supply growing, projected to keep growing**.

## Sell pressure: where new TAO comes from

**Protocol inflation.** Every block, about every 12 seconds, Bittensor makes **0.5 new TAO** and sends it to the AI subnets, where it is shared between miners, validators, subnet owners and stakers. From Aug 3 to Nov 1 2026 the chain produced 647,410 blocks, which came to **323,705 TAO**, close to 3,600 TAO a day. The same pace is expected for the next 90 days. The block pay halves only when total supply reaches 15.75M TAO, and Bittensor is about 4M TAO short of that line, so no halving falls in the next 90 days.

**Vesting unlocks.** Zero. Bittensor had no presale, no team share and no investor share, so no locked tranche exists to unlock. Every TAO in circulation was earned by running the network.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks.** Zero. The Opentensor Foundation holds no reserve set aside at launch; it earns TAO as a validator like any other. Listed companies that hold TAO bought coins already in circulation, so when they sell, no new supply appears.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy.** Zero. No court estate, bankruptcy pile or escrow of TAO is waiting to be paid out.

**One-off root stake top-up.** On Sep 15 2026 a network upgrade made **172,001 TAO** in a single block and paid them into the root staking pool. For a long time, rewards had been credited to root stakers without real TAO behind them, so the last stakers trying to leave could not withdraw. The upgrade filled that hole once and is built never to run again, so it adds nothing to the next 90 days. It is the reason the last 90 days read **+4.17%** while the next 90 days read only **+2.71%**.

## Buy pressure: where new TAO goes

**Programmatic buyback.** Zero. Nothing buys TAO back on the network's behalf. Subnet owners who buy back their own subnet tokens pay with TAO that stays in circulation.

**Protocol fee burn.** Bittensor takes transaction fees and the cost of registering on a subnet out of supply for good. From Aug 3 to Nov 1 2026 that removed **8,136 TAO**, about **5,842 TAO** of it from subnet registrations. An upgrade on Sep 18 2026 cut transaction fees in half, so about **7,528 TAO** is expected over the next 90 days. Either way, this burn takes back only about 2% of what block pay creates.

**Foundation buy.** Zero. No foundation or treasury buys TAO back. Funds and listed companies that buy TAO take coins that already circulate, so they remove nothing from the count.

**New long-term lock.** Zero. About **7.47M TAO**, close to two thirds of all TAO, sits staked in subnet pools. Staked TAO can be withdrawn and still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing from supply.

## Foundation and overhang

TAO has no team-controlled overhang. Bittensor launched in 2021 with every coin earned through the network, and no foundation, team or investor wallet holds coins outside circulation. All 11.68M TAO issued so far count as circulating. The root staking pool that received the Sep 15 2026 top-up, the subnet pools and the treasuries of listed TAO companies hold coins inside that count, so moves between them add no new supply. The only supply that has not reached the market yet is the TAO still to be made under the 21M cap, about 9.3M TAO, which arrives block by block through the schedule above.

## How TAO compares to other halving-schedule chains

Bittensor copies the shape of Bitcoin's money: a 21M cap, a fixed reward per block and halvings that come when a supply line is crossed. Bitcoin is much further along that curve. Bitcoin pays 3.125 BTC a block after four halvings and its supply grows about **0.19%** in 90 days, while Bittensor has had only one halving and still adds about **2.7%** every 90 days from block pay alone.

Two things set TAO apart. First, Bittensor ties its halvings to total supply, not to a block count, and the TAO it takes back through fees and registrations lowers that total, so recycling pushes the next halving slightly further out. Second, Bittensor is run by frequent network upgrades rather than by miners voting with hash power, and one of those upgrades minted the one-time 172,001 TAO top-up in September 2026. On a chain like Bitcoin or Litecoin, a mint outside the block reward cannot happen without a hard fork.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Subnet pay splits.** An upgrade released on Oct 19 2026 lets each subnet choose how its pay is split between its owner, validators and miners. It changes who receives the 0.5 TAO a block, not how much TAO is made, so supply growth should stay near **+2.71%** for the 90 days to Jan 30 2027.

**Free registration by work.** Since Oct 7 2026 subnet owners can let new miners join by solving a computing puzzle instead of paying a registration fee. Subnet registrations removed about 5,842 TAO in the last 90 days. If many subnets switch, the burn could shrink and net supply growth would edge up slightly.

**Other trackers catching up.** The +21.70% shown elsewhere should fall back toward the on-chain figure as the stale starting point leaves the 90-day count, mostly by around Dec 14 2026 and fully by around Jan 25 2027.

**Any new one-off mint.** The Sep 15 2026 top-up showed that a network upgrade can create TAO outside block pay. No such upgrade is announced for the next 90 days, and the next halving, at 15.75M TAO, is years away.

## Summary

Bittensor's supply grew **+4.17%** over the 90 days to Nov 1 2026 and should grow about **+2.71%** over the next 90 days. Block pay created **323,705 TAO** and a one-time fix minted **172,001 TAO** for root stakers on Sep 15 2026, while fees and registrations removed only **8,136 TAO**. TAO has a 21M cap, no vesting, no buyback and no team overhang, so block pay is the whole story until the next halving. Other trackers show a much higher figure because of two recounts in their supply number, not because of new TAO.

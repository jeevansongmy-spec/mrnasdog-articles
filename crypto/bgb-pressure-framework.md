# BGB Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

Over the 90 days to Nov 22 2026, the BGB that people can trade changed by **0.00%**, and the next 90 days to Feb 20 2027 should read the same **0.00%**. The label that fits BGB today: a capped exchange coin whose burns shrink a locked reserve, not the float.

## Sell pressure: where new BGB comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. BGB is not a reward coin: nobody is paid in freshly made BGB, and the token contract on Ethereum holds exactly 2B coins with no function that could add more. Coins on the Morph chain are a mirror of coins locked on Ethereum, about **22.72M BGB** on each side, so the bridge adds nothing either.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. The only scheduled release left is the Morph Foundation treasury's allowance of up to 2% of 220M BGB a month, about **4.4M BGB**. In more than fourteen months since the treasury received its coins on Sep 4 2025, every coin that left it went to the burn address, and nothing went out to be spent or sold between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**. The treasury still holds **207.88M BGB** outside the circulating count, but it released none of it to the market in the window.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**. No court or failed firm holds BGB for a later payout. The Sep 24 2026 Bitget hack took other coins from the exchange's wallets; it did not create or release any BGB.

## Buy pressure: where new BGB goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**. No contract buys BGB on the open market. Before September 2025 Bitget paid for its burns itself; since it handed BGB's roadmap to the Morph Foundation, the quarterly burn is drawn from the treasury instead.

The fee burn books **0** for circulating supply, even though coins were destroyed. On Oct 19 2026 the Morph Foundation sent **3,044,901 BGB** to the burn address for July to September, a size set from about $7,930 of fees on the Morph chain multiplied by a large booster. The burned coins came straight out of the locked treasury, so total supply fell to about **907.88M BGB** while the coins in circulation stayed at about **699.99M BGB**.

Foundation buying is **0**. Neither Bitget nor the Morph Foundation has announced a plan to buy BGB. Wallets linked to Bitget gathered about **329M BGB** into one address between Oct 21 and Nov 18 2026, but those coins were already circulating, so the move changes nothing.

New long-term locks are **0**. BGB staking on Morph, open since Apr 14 2026, pays 5% a year for a 90-day lock and takes at most 2M BGB, and staked coins still count as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

BGB has one team-controlled overhang: the Morph Foundation treasury, a multi-signature wallet that held **207,883,944 BGB** on Nov 22 2026, equal to about 30% of the circulating supply. It began with 220M BGB on Sep 4 2025 and has given up 12,116,056 BGB since, all of it burned in four quarterly rounds on Jan 23, Apr 9, Jul 14 and Oct 19 2026. If the treasury's balance falls between checks and the coins go anywhere other than the burn address, that outflow enters the sell side at the next check, as an unlock if it follows the 2% monthly allowance and as an unscheduled release if it does not.

## How BGB compares to other exchange-token chains

Exchange tokens usually shrink by buying coins back and burning them, which takes coins out of circulation. BNB is the best-known case: its quarterly burn removes coins that were trading, with a long-term goal of 100M BNB. BGB chose a different path. After a one-time burn of 800M BGB in December 2024 and a further 220M in September 2025, its remaining burns are paid from a reserve that the market never counted, so they lower total supply without touching the float.

OKB sits at the other end: its supply was cut to a fixed amount and it no longer burns at all, so its float moves only when a holder sells. BGB is closer to OKB in practice today, a flat float with no new coins, but with one difference that matters: a 207.88M BGB reserve that could be released at up to 4.4M BGB a month if the Morph Foundation ever chose to use its allowance.

Against uncapped proof-of-stake coins, which add new coins every day to pay validators, BGB has no issuance at all. Its supply risk is not inflation but a single reserve that, so far, has only ever been burned.

**Morph Tachyon:** the Morph team says its fast trading chain is launching soon, with no date yet. More activity on Morph raises the fees that size each burn, but the booster decides most of the amount.

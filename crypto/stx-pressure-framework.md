# STX Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Originally published at [mrnasdog.com/research/stx/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/stx/inflation). MrNasDog Pressure Framework · Inflation Analysis.*

This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about Stacks, see [mrnasdog.com/research/stx](https://mrnasdog.com/research/stx).

Stacks' supply is growing at a steady clip: up **+1.27%** in the 90 days from Aug 7 to Nov 5 2026, and on course for about **+1.27%** in the next 90 days. Two built-in mints did it, **13.14M STX** to miners and **10.72M STX** to the Stacks Endowment, while nothing burned or bought back any STX, and STX has no supply cap. Other trackers show **+1.29%** for the same period, so the two readings agree.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 5 2026, STX supply rose by a net **+1.27%**, from about 1.857B to 1.880B STX, an increase of **23.86M STX**. Other trackers report **+1.29%** for the same 90 days, a gap of only **0.02 percentage points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days should look the same, because both mints run at fixed rates that do not change before mid-2027. In one line, STX today is a steadily inflating coin with two sources of new supply and nothing that takes coins back out.

## Sell pressure: where new STX comes from

The first source is the miner reward. Stacks miners bid in bitcoin for the right to make Stacks blocks, and the winner of each Bitcoin block earns **1,000 new STX**. The Jul 30 2026 upgrade put the reward back at that level after it had been halved to 500 STX in April 2026, and it removed the planned cuts. From Aug 7 to Nov 5 2026 miners received **13,135,577 STX**, about 146,000 a day. When a Bitcoin block passes with no Stacks miner, its reward rolls into the next one, so the total tracks the number of Bitcoin blocks.

The second source is the Endowment mint. Under the growth plan Stacks holders approved in 2025, every time a new miner takes over, **1,140 new STX** go straight into the Stacks Endowment's mint contract. There were 9,405 such handovers in the window, so the Endowment received **10,721,700 STX**. That rate is set to rise to 1,705 STX around July 2027, outside the next 90 days.

Vesting adds nothing new. No team or investor schedule is still unlocking, and the Endowment's 100M STX locked tranche, which frees about 4.17M STX each month, was created in 2025 and already counts as circulating. There is no court estate or bankruptcy pile of STX waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new STX goes

Nothing removes STX from supply. Stacks has no buyback, and transaction fees are paid to miners rather than destroyed. Between Aug 7 and Nov 5 2026 only about **3 STX** reached burn addresses, too small to count. The 2025 growth plan only allows a future burn if the community votes for one, and no such vote is planned.

The Endowment sells STX to fund its work rather than buying any back. Locking is growing fast: about **493.40M STX** is locked for stacking and Bitcoin staking this cycle, and 513.91M STX is set for the next one. Those coins still count as circulating, though, so the growing lock keeps them off the market for a while without shrinking the count.

## Foundation and overhang

The Stacks Endowment is the big holder to watch. Its mint contract holds **67.12M STX**, of which **29.72M STX** is ready to claim now, and it made no claim in the 90 days. Its receiving wallet holds **6.86M STX** after sending 39.08M STX to its working wallet on Sep 24 2026, and that working wallet holds **2.45M STX** after a run of payouts in late October and early November 2026. All of these coins already count as circulating, so selling them adds no new supply, but it does add coins to the market, so their balances are worth checking each month.

## How STX compares to other Bitcoin-linked networks

Bitcoin itself is the clearest contrast. Its supply grows only through a halving block reward toward a hard cap of 21M, and it rose about **0.18%** in the same 90 days. Stacks borrows Bitcoin's security, since miners spend bitcoin to win Stacks blocks, but STX follows its own rules: no cap, a miner reward that was halved in April 2026 and restored by vote in July 2026, and a second mint for its treasury.

Many Bitcoin layers use bridged bitcoin as their fuel and add no new coin at all. Stacks instead pays its miners and its treasury in fresh STX, which is why its supply growth is several times Bitcoin's. What Stacks offers in return is a way to earn bitcoin: Bitcoin staking pays holders in BTC, and taking part needs STX locked beside the bitcoin.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Bond 4 around Dec 3 2026.** Each Bitcoin staking bond needs STX worth about 5% of the bitcoin staked. Bond 3 sold out on Nov 5 2026, and a bigger Bond 4 would lock more STX, though it creates none.

**Stacks 4.1.** A draft upgrade plan published on Nov 4 2026 brings a new smart-contract version and a new deposit address for miners. It does not touch the miner reward or the Endowment mint, and no date is set yet.

**The Endowment's monthly slice, late November 2026.** Another 4.17M STX of the locked tranche becomes claimable. It is already counted, but a claim followed by sales would put coins on the market.

**The next staking design, 2027.** A research plan published on Nov 4 2026 would sell staking capacity at open auction, and its proposal is set to review the 1,000 STX miner reward. Any change would need a community vote.

## Summary

STX supply grew **+1.27%** in the 90 days to Nov 5 2026, and the next 90 days look the same. Miners received 13.14M new STX and the Stacks Endowment 10.72M STX, while nothing burned or bought any back. Stacks has no supply cap, and the Endowment's mint rate is set to rise around July 2027. A growing share of STX is locked for Bitcoin staking, which keeps those coins off the market without removing them from the count.

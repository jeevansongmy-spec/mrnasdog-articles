# STX Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

STX supply is growing at a steady clip and nothing takes coins back out. Between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026 Stacks paid its miners **13.12M STX** and sent **10.70M STX** to the Stacks Endowment, while no STX was burned or bought back, so supply grew **+1.26%** in 90 days, and the next 90 days look the same. Other trackers read **+1.24%** for the same stretch.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

STX added **23.82M STX** of new supply in 90 days on a base of about **1.89B STX**, a net rise of **+1.26%**. Other trackers show **+1.24%**, a gap of only **0.02 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days should add about the same **23.82M STX**, since both of the Stacks mints run block by block with no change due before late July 2027. Over a full year that pace works out to roughly **5%** more STX. The label that fits: inflationary by design, with two built-in mints and no burn to offset them.

## Sell pressure: where new STX comes from

The first and largest source is the miner reward. Stacks miners compete by spending Bitcoin, and each Bitcoin block pays the winner **1,000 new STX**. When a Bitcoin block passes with no Stacks miner, that block's reward rolls into the next winner's payout, so the rate tracks Bitcoin blocks rather than Stacks miners. Bitcoin produced **13,217 blocks** between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026, a little faster than its usual pace, and Stacks miners received **13.12M STX**.

The 1,000 STX reward is new again. Stacks cut it to 500 STX in April 2026 under an older halving plan, then the Bitcoin Staking upgrade that went live on Jul 29 2026 put it back to 1,000 STX and removed every later step-down. The upgrade calls the 1,000 STX rate a starting rate, to be looked at again in a future upgrade with no date yet.

Vesting unlocks add **0**. No investor or team allocation is still waiting on a schedule that creates new STX.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks also add **0**. The Stacks Endowment moved a lot of STX in the window, but every one of those coins was counted as supply on the day it was made, so moving it changes nothing. The next section lists what the Endowment holds.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy holdings add **0**. No court or bankrupt firm is holding STX for a later payout.

The second mint is the Endowment mint. Since a 2025 community vote created the Stacks Endowment, each new Stacks miner tenure also makes STX for it. The amount is **1,140 STX** per tenure at present, and there were **9,386 tenures** between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026, adding exactly **10.70M STX**. Unlike the miner reward, a Bitcoin block without a Stacks miner makes nothing here. The rate steps up to 1,705 STX around late July 2027, then down to 1,305 and 1,155 STX, and the Endowment mint ends in 2030.

## Buy pressure: where new STX goes

The programmatic buyback is **0**. No contract or treasury buys STX back on the market.

The protocol fee burn is **0**. Stacks pays transaction fees to miners instead of destroying them. The few coins sitting at burn addresses moved by about 3 STX in 90 days and still count as supply.

The Foundation buy is **0**. The Stacks Endowment pays STX out to builders and programs and does not buy it back. The 2025 vote that created the Endowment says a burn can be put to the community only once the Endowment holds more than $1 billion in liquid assets, and no such proposal exists.

The new long-term lock is **0**. About **522.98M STX** is staked this cycle, some of it paired with Bitcoin in the new staking bonds, but staked STX still counts as circulating, so a bigger stake takes nothing out of the supply.

## Foundation and overhang

The main overhang is the Stacks Endowment's own contract, which holds **69.33M STX**. Of that, **37.5M STX** is still locked and frees up in slices of 4.17M STX each month, with the next three around Nov 27 2026, Dec 27 2026 and Jan 26 2027. The rest can be claimed at any time. Between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026 the Endowment's wallets passed about **45.58M STX** on to programs and grantees, and its three spending wallets now hold about **1.39M STX**.

All of these coins are already part of the 1.89B STX supply, so none of them is new pressure in the numbers above. If the Endowment's balances fall between rebuilds, the outflow shows up in the Foundation row at the next refresh.

## How STX compares to other Bitcoin layer chains

Bitcoin itself has a hard cap and halves its reward every four years, which kept Bitcoin supply growth to about 0.22% over the same 90 days. Stacks follows Bitcoin's blocks but not Bitcoin's scarcity: STX is paid per Bitcoin block, yet the Bitcoin Staking upgrade removed the halvings, so STX now grows about six times faster than Bitcoin with no cap in sight until a future upgrade sets one.

Most Bitcoin layers and sidechains use Bitcoin itself, or a wrapped copy of it, as their main coin, so they add no new coins at all. Stacks is different because STX pays the miners and funds the network's own treasury. That gives Stacks a budget for growth, but every coin of that budget is new supply that holders have to absorb.

Proof-of-stake chains such as Ethereum pay validators in new coins too, but they burn part of every fee, which pulls some of the new supply back. Stacks has no fee burn and no buyback, so the full 23.82M STX of new supply in each 90 days stays in the market.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The Stacks 4.1 vote closes around Dec 1 2026, and if it passes, the upgrade goes live around Dec 9 2026. It brings a new version of the Stacks contract language and a new Bitcoin deposit address for miners, and it does not change the 1,000 STX miner reward or the Endowment mint.

Bond 4, the next Bitcoin staking bond, is set to start around Dec 5 2026 with room for 1,050 BTC. Each staked Bitcoin must be paired with STX worth 5% of its value, so more STX gets staked, though staked STX still counts as supply.

The Endowment's next locked slices of 4.17M STX free up around Nov 27 2026, Dec 27 2026 and Jan 26 2027. They add nothing to supply, but large transfers from the Endowment to trading venues would be worth watching.

The next staking upgrade will look again at the 1,000 STX miner reward, and there is no date for it yet. Any cut would slow STX supply growth, so the proposal itself is the signal to watch.

## Summary

Stacks made **23.82M** new STX between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026, a net supply rise of **+1.26%** in 90 days. Two mints drive it: 1,000 STX to miners for each Bitcoin block and 1,140 STX to the Stacks Endowment for each miner tenure, with no burn or buyback on the other side. The key risk is that STX supply now grows about 5% a year with no cap, and the Endowment still holds 69.33M STX it can pay out. The only thing that would change the pace is a future upgrade that lowers the miner reward.

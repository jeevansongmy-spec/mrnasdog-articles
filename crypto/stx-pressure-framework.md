# STX Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Originally published at [mrnasdog.com/research/stx/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/stx/inflation). MrNasDog Pressure Framework · Inflation Analysis.*

This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about Stacks, see [mrnasdog.com/research/stx](https://mrnasdog.com/research/stx).

**STX** supply is growing, and it should keep growing at the same pace. Stacks creates new STX in two ways at once: miners earn about 1,000 STX for every Bitcoin block, and the chain mints 1,140 STX more at the start of each mining turn for the Stacks Endowment. Between Aug 16 and Nov 14 2026 that came to **12.96M STX** for miners and **10.55M STX** for the Endowment, with **0** burned or bought back, so net supply rose **+1.25%**. Other supply trackers read **+1.25%** as well, and Stacks has no supply cap.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 16 to Nov 14 2026, STX supply grew **+1.25%**: about **23.51M** new STX on a circulating base of **1.88B**. Other supply trackers show **+1.25%** for the same period, a gap of under **0.01** percentage points, so the Stacks coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days should look the same, about **+1.25%**, because both mints run at fixed rates that do not change before late July 2027. Stacks is inflationary by design: two steady mints, and nothing on the other side of the ledger.

## Sell pressure: where new STX comes from

**Protocol inflation, 12.96M STX.** Stacks miners spend Bitcoin to win the right to make Stacks blocks, and they are paid in brand-new STX. The Stacks 4.0 upgrade of Jul 29 2026 put this coinbase back to 1,000 STX per Bitcoin block, after a cut to 500 STX in April 2026, and it removed the old plan to keep cutting it. Over 13,060 Bitcoin blocks in the window the miners received **12.96M STX**, close to 1,000 per block, because a Bitcoin block with no Stacks winner passes its reward to a later miner. The 1,000 STX rate is called temporary: the next big Stacks upgrade, PoX-6, has promised to review it, with no date yet.

**Vesting unlocks, 0.** The early investor and team STX finished vesting years ago. The one live schedule is a 100M STX batch held in the Stacks Endowment's mint contract, which opens in 24 slices of about 4.17M STX. Those coins were created in 2025 and already count as circulating, so each slice only changes who can move them, not how many STX exist.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, 0.** The Stacks Endowment did move a lot of STX: its main wallet sent out **45.58M STX** in the window, 39.08M of it on Sep 24 2026 and 6.5M on Nov 5 2026, onward to the wallets that pay grants, staking programs and partners. All of those coins already counted as circulating, so the payouts add nothing to the count, even though they can reach the market.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy, 0.** No court case or failed company holds STX that it will release later.

**Endowment mint, 10.55M STX.** Since the community approved SIP-031 in 2025, the Stacks chain mints extra STX straight into the Stacks Endowment's contract at the start of every mining turn. The amount stepped up from 475 to 1,140 STX per turn in late July 2026, and over the 9,253 turns between Aug 16 and Nov 14 2026 it added **10.55M STX**. It rises again to 1,705 STX per turn around late July 2027, then falls back in steps until the mint ends in 2030. This second mint is now almost as large as the miners' pay.

## Buy pressure: where new STX goes

**Programmatic buyback, 0.** No contract or treasury buys STX back. The Endowment's plan says later mints could be burned if STX does very well, but only after a community vote, and no vote has been proposed.

**Protocol fee burn, 0.** Transaction fees on Stacks go to the miners, so no STX is burned when people use the chain. The Bitcoin that miners spend is Bitcoin, not STX. The two burn addresses on Stacks gained only about 3 STX in 90 days.

**Foundation buy, 0.** Neither the Stacks Foundation nor the Stacks Endowment buys STX on the market. The Endowment's extra rewards are paid in Bitcoin, about 1 BTC a month from Sep 10 to Dec 10 2026.

**New long-term lock, 0.** About **513.91M STX** is locked for stacking this cycle, and every Bitcoin staking bond also needs STX worth 5% of the Bitcoin placed. Locked STX still counts as circulating, so a bigger lock does not take STX out of the count, even if it keeps those coins off the market for a while.

## Foundation and overhang

The Stacks Endowment is the one team-controlled holder that matters. Its mint contract holds about **68.07M STX**, of which about **30.57M** can be claimed now and the rest opens in monthly slices; it grows by 1,140 STX every mining turn and paid nothing out during the window. The Endowment's main wallet, which received the earlier claims, now holds about **0.36M STX** after its two big transfers, and its two working wallets hold about **0.85M** and **0.58M STX**. We check these wallets at every rebuild. If any of their balances falls and the coins leave for the market, the outflow is recorded under Foundation and unscheduled unlocks at the next refresh.

## How STX compares to other Bitcoin-linked chains

Bitcoin itself has a hard cap of 21M coins and halves its block reward every four years, so its supply growth keeps shrinking. Stacks borrowed that shape at first, but the Stacks 4.0 upgrade dropped its halving-style cuts, and STX has no maximum supply. Stacks now behaves more like an uncapped proof-of-stake Layer 1, where new coins pay for security year after year, except that STX miners pay for blocks with Bitcoin and STX holders who lock their coins earn Bitcoin, not new STX.

What sets STX apart from most chains is the second mint. Many networks fund a foundation or treasury once, at launch; Stacks voted in 2025 to keep minting for its Endowment for five years. That makes STX inflation higher than the miner pay alone suggests: about **1.25%** every 90 days, and about 5.4% over the next year once the Endowment step-up in July 2027 is counted, against roughly 0.2% every 90 days for Bitcoin. Unlike Ethereum, Stacks has no fee burn to offset any of it.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Around Nov 17 2026**, the community vote on the Stacks 4.1 upgrade is planned to open. As proposed, it changes smart contracts and sBTC deposits, not the STX mint.

**Around Nov 28 2026, Dec 29 2026 and Jan 28 2027**, the next three Endowment slices of about 4.17M STX each become claimable. They add nothing to the count, but a large claim followed by transfers to exchanges would be the first sign of Endowment selling.

**Around Dec 3 2026**, the next Bitcoin staking bond is expected to open. Each bond locks STX alongside the Bitcoin, which matters for selling pressure even though it does not change the supply count.

**Around Dec 9 2026**, Stacks 4.1 is expected to go live at Bitcoin block 979,500 if the vote passes. Any new proposal for PoX-6, which has promised to review the 1,000 STX miner reward, is the item that could change the STX inflation rate itself.

## Summary

STX supply grew **+1.25%** in the 90 days to Nov 14 2026 and is set to grow about the same in the next 90 days. Stacks mints new STX twice: **12.96M STX** went to miners at about 1,000 per Bitcoin block, and **10.55M STX** went to the Stacks Endowment at 1,140 per mining turn. No STX is burned or bought back, and locked STX still counts as circulating. The key risk is the Endowment's holdings of about 68M STX reaching the market, and with no supply cap, the ceiling is set only by future votes on the mint.

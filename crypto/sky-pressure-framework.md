# SKY Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to shrink

**Main page:** the full SKY coin page — signal, price drivers and the buy/sell questions → [mrnasdog.com/research/sky](https://mrnasdog.com/research/sky)

SKY supply shrank a little over the last 90 days and is set to shrink faster in the next 90: between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026 the Sky treasury paid stakers **113.72M SKY**, while protocol buybacks took **160.36M SKY** off the market, so the SKY counted as circulating fell **0.20%**. No new SKY is printed; stakers are paid with coins the protocol bought back, and an extra buyback that began on Nov 19 2026, after SKY's sharp drop, lifts the next 90 days to about **−0.81%**. Other trackers show **−0.21%** for the same window.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 22 2026, SKY's counted supply went down by **0.20%**. Other trackers show **−0.21%**, a gap of only **0.01** of a point, so the two readings agree and the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days point lower, to about **−0.81%**, because the buyback is set in dollars and SKY now costs far less, so each dollar buys more coins. Label: a stablecoin protocol's coin that shrinks through profit-funded buybacks.

## Sell pressure: where new SKY comes from

Protocol inflation is the only open tap, and it is not new printing. The Sky treasury paid **113.72M SKY** to its staking reward pool between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026, about **1.26M SKY** a day. Every one of those coins was first bought back on the market, then held in the treasury, so for the circulating count they simply come back out. The payout is reset once a month from what the buyback bought the month before. The plan now open for a vote pays **104.90M SKY** over the next 90 days.

Vesting unlocks are zero. SKY has no team or investor vesting left. The one open door from the past is the MKR upgrade: an MKR holder can still swap one MKR for 24,000 SKY, less a 5% fee. In the window about **5,170 MKR** were upgraded, and the SKY for them, about **118.5M**, came from a ready-made reserve of **2.02B SKY** that already counts as circulating, so the upgrade adds nothing. About 82,660 MKR are still to come.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero. No foundation or team wallet holds SKY outside the circulating count; the only such wallet is the protocol treasury, and what it pays out is already in the first row.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is zero. No court or failed firm holds SKY for a later payout. Staked SKY used to back loans can be sold if a loan fails, but those coins are already counted.

## Buy pressure: where new SKY goes

The programmatic buyback is the big one. Every 45 minutes or so, Sky spends part of its USDS income buying SKY on the market. Between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026 it spent **10.11M USDS** on **160.36M SKY**, an average of about 6.3 cents a coin, and sent all of it to the treasury, where it no longer counts as circulating. On Nov 19 2026 Sky's Core Council added a one-off buyback of **750,000 USDS** a day for seven days, **5.25M USDS** in all, on top of the usual pace, and said every coin from it will be burned. At today's price of about 4.2 cents, the rest of that extra run plus the usual pace adds up to about **294.09M SKY** over the next 90 days.

The fee burn books zero here, even though coins really were destroyed. The treasury burned **2.86M SKY** on Sep 13 and **7.37M SKY** on Oct 12 2026, and total supply fell by exactly **10.23M**. Those coins had already left the market when they were bought back, so burning them takes nothing more out of circulation.

Foundation buys are zero. No foundation buys SKY to hold it; every buyback is run by the protocol itself.

New long-term locks are zero. About **8.44B SKY** is staked for rewards, but staked SKY still counts as circulating, so staking more takes nothing out.

## Foundation and overhang

The one holder that matters is the Sky treasury. It held **76.79M SKY** on Nov 22 2026, up from **40.38M** on Aug 24, because buybacks came in faster than stakers were paid. These coins wait to be paid to stakers or burned; a **7.77M SKY** burn is in the vote now open, and the coins from the extra buyback are meant to be burned as well. We read the treasury balance at every rebuild. If the treasury pays out more than it takes in between rebuilds, the extra enters the sell side at the next one. The MKR upgrade reserve of **2.02B SKY** is not team money; it belongs to MKR holders who have not upgraded yet.

## How SKY compares to other DeFi buyback tokens

Many DeFi tokens now buy themselves back with protocol income, but they handle the bought coins in different ways. UNI's fee switch, live since December 2025, buys UNI and burns every coin, so each buy shrinks supply for good. SKY does something in between: most bought coins go back out to stakers as rewards, and a smaller slice is burned. That makes SKY's net result depend on two dials, how much is bought and how much is paid out, rather than on a burn alone.

SKY also differs from coins that pay stakers by printing new tokens, as most proof-of-stake chains do. Those chains grow their supply every day by design. SKY's staker pay is capped by what the protocol earned and bought the month before, so the payout can never run ahead of the buyback for long.

The flip side is that SKY's buyback is set in dollars, not in coins. When the price falls, the same dollars buy more SKY, so the supply shrinks faster in a slump. When income falls, as it can if USDS shrinks, the buyback shrinks with it.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The extra buyback is due to end on **Nov 26 2026**, after which the regular pace returns; Sky has said it will set the new pace through the same process, and a faster one was already planned before the extra run.

The executive vote first set for Nov 19 2026 and moved to Nov 20 2026 carries the October settlement, a **7.77M SKY** burn from the treasury and a new staker payout of **104.90M SKY** over 90 days; it can take effect once it is cast, from about Nov 23 2026.

USDS shrank sharply after more than $2 billion was swapped out on Nov 19 2026. Sky's income, and so the buyback, follows how much USDS is out there, so the December and January monthly settlements will show whether the buyback holds up.

A community proposal from Nov 19 2026 asks to move more rewards into buybacks until SKY trades above 5 cents. It is not adopted; if it passes, the buy side grows again.

## Summary

SKY is the coin of Sky, the protocol behind the USDS stablecoin, and no new SKY is printed. Over the 90 days to Nov 22 2026, buybacks took **160.36M SKY** off the market while the treasury paid stakers **113.72M SKY**, so the circulating count fell **0.20%**. With an extra buyback running and SKY near 4 cents, the next 90 days point to about **−0.81%**. The main risk to that reading is lower protocol income after USDS shrank in November, since the buyback is paid from that income.

# MORPHO Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · A capped coin whose float still grows

Morpho's MORPHO token is capped at **1,000,000,000** and was fully minted at deployment, so no coin has been created or destroyed since — yet the tradable float still grew. Over the 90 days to **Aug 11 2026**, **22.5M MORPHO** reached the market: **20.3M** distributed out of the Morpho team treasury safe, **1.0M** of vested MORPHO converting into transferable form, and **1.2M** of governance rewards claimed. Buy pressure was **zero** — Morpho runs no buyback, no burn and no staking lock, and its lending fee switch has never been activated. On a circulating base of **656.2M MORPHO** the Pressure Framework reads **+3.43%** net against our supply monitor's **+3.99%**, a gap of **0.56 percentage points** that ships a monitor-gap flag. MORPHO is a **capped token that is still inflationary on its active float**, because the supply comes out of treasuries rather than out of a mint.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 11 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **MORPHO at +3.43% net**: sell pressure of **22.5M MORPHO** against buy pressure of exactly **zero**, on a circulating base of **656.2M MORPHO**. Our supply monitor reads **+3.99%** for the same window, a gap of **0.56 percentage points** — just outside the half-point tolerance, so a monitor-gap flag ships with this page. The gap is a released-versus-scheduled divergence, and reading the contracts settles it. Morpho's legacy MORPHO token is not transferable; a coin only becomes tradable when it is exchanged for the wrapped token held by the migration contract at **0x9D03bb2092270648d7480049d0E58d2FcF0E5123**. That contract still holds **391.4M MORPHO**, which means only **608.6M** of the 1B supply is transferable at all — while the monitor's upstream classifier reports **656.2M** circulating because it follows the published unlock calendar rather than the escrow. The calendar entitles roughly **27M** for this window; the escrows actually released **22.5M**. The framework books the realised figure. MORPHO is best labelled **persistently inflationary on the active float, with a capped supply and no offsetting mechanism whatsoever**.

## Sell pressure: where new MORPHO comes from

Nowhere, in the ordinary sense — and that is the point of MORPHO. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**: the token supply is capped at **1,000,000,000** and every unit was minted at deployment in **Jun 2022**. Reading the MORPHO contract on Ethereum at both ends of this window returns the identical total supply, so there is no issuance, no emission schedule and no halving to model. Every figure below is already-minted MORPHO moving from a team-controlled wallet into someone else's hands. That makes Morpho the mirror image of an emitting chain: the ceiling is fixed and known, and the only question is how fast the locked share of that ceiling is released.

The largest release is Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, at **20.3M MORPHO** over 90 days. The Morpho team treasury safe at **0x6ABfd6139c7C3CC270ee2Ce132E309F59cAaF6a2** fell from **111.7M** to **91.4M** in transferable MORPHO across the window, paying out to third-party wallets in lumpy batches with no published per-period release plan — roughly **3.5M** by late May, another **9.3M** by mid June, **6.5M** more by mid July, then almost nothing since. That safe still holds **105.6M MORPHO**.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is only **1.0M**, and the reason is the escrow. Morpho's founder allocation of **152M** vests linearly to **May 17 2028** and Strategic Partners Cohort 3's **67M** vests linearly to **Nov 21 2027**, a paper entitlement near **27M** for this window — but only **1.0M** actually left the migration contract and became transferable. Vested-on-paper is not the same as tradable, and the framework books what moved.

Sell #5, reward distribution, adds **1.2M**: governance pays MORPHO incentives to lenders and borrowers, and the combined balance of the governance and reward wallets on Ethereum and Base fell by that much net. This is also where the prior read was wrong and has been corrected. A **6.0M** transfer inside the window looked like a distribution and was previously booked as protocol inflation; it was not a distribution at all. It moved from the governance multisig to the Morpho-controlled rewards multisig — team to team — and never reached the market. MORPHO cannot be minted in any case, so that row is **zero by mechanism**, not by measurement. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero**: Morpho has no estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution.

## Buy pressure: where new MORPHO goes

It goes nowhere, and this is the cleanest fact on the page: every buy row for MORPHO is **zero**. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is zero because Morpho runs no buyback and has no revenue stream that could fund one — the Morpho Blue fee switch, which governance may set as high as **25%** of the interest borrowers pay, has never been activated, so the protocol collects nothing for the token. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is zero for the same reason and one more: MORPHO has no burn function at all, and the on-chain total supply has never moved off its **1B** cap. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is zero — there is no public evidence of release in window, and the team wallets are net distributors of MORPHO rather than buyers of it.

Buy #4, new long-term lock, is zero, and this is the mechanical gap that separates MORPHO from most governance tokens of comparable size. There is no staking contract, no vote-escrow lock and no revenue share for holding MORPHO, so nothing removes coins from the tradable float once they arrive. The one item worth watching sits as a tracked extra, Buy #5: a large asset manager signed a cooperation agreement with the Morpho Association in **Feb 2026** giving it the option to acquire up to **90M MORPHO** — **9%** of total supply — over **48 months** through open-market, over-the-counter and negotiated purchases, with transfer restrictions attached. No executed amount has ever been disclosed, so the row ships at **zero** and is re-checked at every refresh. If it ever fires visibly, it is the single mechanism that could put a real number on Morpho's buy side.

## Foundation and overhang

Morpho's overhang is unusually large and unusually concentrated. The governance treasury at **0xcBa28b38103307Ec8dA98377ffF9816C164f9AFa** controls about **304.0M MORPHO** across Ethereum and Base — the biggest single holding in the token — and almost none of it is in tradable form: **290.4M** of it is still legacy MORPHO, which cannot be transferred until it is wrapped, and that legacy balance was identical at both ends of this window. It has no release schedule; it moves only when governance votes. Next is the team treasury safe at **0x6ABfd6139c7C3CC270ee2Ce132E309F59cAaF6a2**, holding **105.6M MORPHO** and actively draining at the pace described above, again with no published per-period plan. Third is the un-migrated remainder sitting behind the migration contract: **391.4M MORPHO** that no one can trade yet, of which the governance treasury accounts for roughly three quarters and founder, strategic-partner and contributor allocations for the rest. Fourth are the reward wallets, holding about **20.5M** earmarked for lender and borrower incentives.

The rule that governs all four is the same. If the team treasury safe's balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3; if the governance treasury or the reward wallets fall, the outflow enters Sell #5; and if the migration contract's balance falls, that release enters Sell #2 as newly transferable MORPHO. Every one of these pools is read directly on-chain at each rebuild, so none of them can move quietly. The honest summary is that roughly **34%** of the total supply — the **343.8M** the classifier counts as not yet circulating — remains under identified team or governance control, and the entire supply story for the next two years is the pace at which that share is let out.

## How MORPHO compares to other capped DeFi governance tokens

The right comparison class for MORPHO is the capped, fully-minted DeFi governance token — the lending and exchange protocols whose supply ceiling was fixed at genesis and whose only supply dynamic is unlock pacing. Against an emitting chain or a vote-escrow exchange token, MORPHO looks conservative: there is no mint, no emission curve, no halving, and the ceiling can never rise. Against its own class, though, MORPHO sits at the inflationary end, and the reason is mechanism rather than marketing. Capped tokens diverge on three choices: whether a fee switch is live, whether that revenue funds a buyback or a burn, and whether holders can lock the token in exchange for something. MORPHO answers no to all three.

Contrast that with a capped exchange token that routes real revenue into a buyback and burn: that token's float shrinks while its unlocks run, and the two effects partly cancel. Or contrast it with a vote-escrow governance token, where a large share of supply is voluntarily locked for years and net locking offsets much of what unlocks. MORPHO has neither cushion, which is why a relatively modest **22.5M** of quarterly release translates into a full **+3.43%** net reading — nothing at all sits on the other side of the ledger. The compensating strength is the ceiling itself: MORPHO's inflation is finite and its end date is visible. Founder vesting completes **May 17 2028** and Strategic Partners Cohort 3 completes **Nov 21 2027**, after which the only discretionary supply left is whatever governance chooses to release from its own treasury. An uncapped emitter never reaches that point.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the team treasury safe's drawdown pace. It released **20.3M MORPHO** across the full window but only **0.9M** in the last 30 days, which is why the forward reading is re-based to **11.0M** and the projected net drops to **+2.01%** — a resumption of the earlier batch size would push it straight back up. Second, the migration contract: it holds **391.4M MORPHO** that is untradable today, and a wave of wrapping by vested founders or strategic partners would convert paper entitlement into real float far faster than the **1.0M** per quarter observed so far. Third, any governance vote to activate the Morpho Blue fee switch — that is the single change that could create a buy row where none exists, and it is the most consequential item on Morpho's governance agenda. Fourth, the **90M** strategic accumulation agreement signed in **Feb 2026**: any disclosed execution would be the first genuine buy-side figure MORPHO has ever carried. Fifth, the reward-rate schedule, which governance has been trimming through successive reductions and now adjusts under an optimistic framework — further cuts would shrink the **1.2M** reward leg.

## Summary

MORPHO is a capped, fully-minted governance token that is nonetheless inflationary on its tradable float, because its supply reaches the market from treasuries rather than from a mint. Over the 90 days to **Aug 11 2026**, **22.5M MORPHO** was released — **20.3M** from the team treasury, **1.0M** of vesting converting to transferable form and **1.2M** of rewards claimed — against **zero** buy pressure, for a net of **+3.43%** on a **656.2M** circulating base, projected to ease to **+2.01%** as the front-loaded treasury deployment decays. Our supply monitor reads **+3.99%**, a **0.56-point** gap that reflects a classifier following the published unlock calendar while the escrows released less than the calendar entitled. The key risk is not a hidden mint, because there cannot be one; it is that **343.8M MORPHO** — about a third of the ceiling — is still held by identified team and governance wallets with no published release plan and nothing on the buy side to absorb it.

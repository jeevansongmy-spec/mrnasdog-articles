# MORPHO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

MORPHO supply is growing fast without a single new coin being made. Over the 90 days to Oct 3 2026, about **49.32M MORPHO** reached the market: **27.0M** from founder and strategic-partner vesting and **22.3M** paid out of Morpho-run wallets, led by the Morpho Association. Nothing was bought back or burned, so the net is **+7.05%** of the **699.96M** circulating MORPHO, and the same pace is projected for the next 90 days. The total stays capped at 1B MORPHO by policy, and about 30% of it is still outside the market.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads MORPHO at **+7.05%** net supply growth over the last 90 days and **+7.05%** for the next 90. The inflation monitor reads **+7.76%**, a gap of **0.71 points**. Most of that gap is arithmetic: the monitor divides by the supply of 90 days ago, which was smaller, while we divide by today's supply. Our same 49.32M MORPHO on the older base would read +7.59%, leaving only **0.16 points** — about 1.06M MORPHO — unexplained. That is inside our tolerance, so no warning chip ships. The label for MORPHO today: **a fixed-cap governance token whose supply grows by unlocks and treasury payouts**.

## Sell pressure: where new MORPHO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. MORPHO has no staking rewards and no block rewards, and the total supply read exactly the same on both the current MORPHO token and its older legacy version at the start and the end of the window — just under 1B each. One honest caveat: the Morpho DAO's multisig owns both MORPHO contracts and can still call a mint. The project's own token page sets 1B MORPHO as the maximum supply, and that mint path has not been used, but the cap is a promise kept by governance rather than a lock in the code.

Vesting unlocks are **27.0M MORPHO** per 90 days. Two locked groups open a little every day. The founders hold 152M MORPHO, 15.2% of the supply, on a two-year linear vest that began after a one-year lockup from the day MORPHO became transferable (Nov 21 2024), so it runs from Nov 21 2025 to Nov 21 2027: **18.74M** per 90 days. The third cohort of strategic partners holds 67M MORPHO on the same two-year line to Nov 21 2027: **8.26M** per 90 days. About **124.2M MORPHO** is still locked between them. No on-chain vesting contract holds these coins, so the published schedule is the measure.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **22.3M MORPHO**. This is the realised outflow of the wallets Morpho runs, read on-chain at both ends of the window. The Morpho Association, which received a **150M MORPHO** grant from the DAO in April 2026 under MIP 131 for its 2026–2030 program, paid out **19.03M**, every coin traced to a transfer. Two contributor grant Safes paid out **1.10M**, and the DAO's reward wallets sent a net **2.20M** to lenders and borrowers. The Association has paid out every month since the grant, so the next 90 days repeat this pace. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**: no estate or court case holds MORPHO.

## Buy pressure: where new MORPHO goes

There is no buy side today. The programmatic buyback is **0**: Morpho's fee switch is off, so the interest borrowers pay goes to lenders and vault curators, and no part of it buys MORPHO. The protocol fee burn is **0**: both total supply and the burn address held still across the window. A foundation buy is **0**: the Association only sent coins out this window, and the project says it has never bought its own token. A new long-term lock is **0**: MORPHO has no staking or lockup program that takes coins off the market. All 49.32M MORPHO of new supply therefore meets ordinary market demand with no protocol buyer on the other side.

## Foundation and overhang

Four Morpho-controlled piles could still reach the market. The largest is the Morpho DAO treasury, a 5-of-9 multisig holding about **304.0M MORPHO** (291.0M on Ethereum and 13.0M on Base). It did not move in the window; it pays out only after a governance vote, and its April grant to the Association is now the main source of payouts. The Morpho Association holds **91.44M MORPHO** and spends it month by month. Two contributor grant Safes hold about **53.16M**, and the DAO's reward wallets hold about **7.89M** waiting for users to claim. On top of that, about **124.2M** founder and partner MORPHO is still locked and opening a little each day. We read the Morpho wallets on-chain every rebuild: if any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How MORPHO compares to other DeFi lending tokens

Among lending-protocol tokens, MORPHO sits at the heavy end for new supply. Like Aave's AAVE and Compound's COMP, it has a fixed total, so its growth comes from coins that already exist leaving locks and treasuries rather than from new minting. What sets MORPHO apart is timing: it became transferable only in November 2024, so its founder and partner lockups are still in their main release years, and its treasury is large — about 30% of all MORPHO sits with the DAO alone.

The other difference is the buy side. Aave now runs an automatic AAVE buyback paid from protocol revenue, which gives that token a steady buyer. Morpho keeps its fee switch off and pays nothing to MORPHO holders, so every unlock and every treasury payout lands on the market with no protocol offset. That makes MORPHO a pure supply story for now: the protocol can grow quickly while the token keeps absorbing new coins.

Against tokens that print to pay stakers, MORPHO's path is easier to see ahead: the vesting ends on Nov 21 2027, after which the only new supply would be whatever the DAO and the Association choose to spend from their treasuries.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the Association's monthly payouts: it sent **9.52M MORPHO** in two transfers on Sep 16 2026 alone, and a quieter or busier quarter moves Sell #3 more than anything else. Second, Nov 21 2026 marks one year of the founder and partner linear vest — the release keeps running at about 0.30M MORPHO a day until Nov 21 2027. Third, any DAO vote to move coins out of its 304.0M treasury, such as a new grant or a larger rewards budget; MIP 134, passed on Sep 18 2026, adds a reward budget of about $50,000 a month for activity on Arc. Fourth, any proposal to turn on the fee switch, which would create the first buyer of MORPHO and is the one change that could cut the net figure.

## Summary

MORPHO is in the Supply growing, projected to keep growing band: about **+7.05%** net supply in 90 days, with no new coins made. Founder and partner vesting adds **27.0M MORPHO** per quarter until Nov 21 2027, and Morpho-run wallets — mostly the Morpho Association spending its April grant — added **22.3M** more. There is no buyback, burn or lock to offset it, because the fee switch is off. The key risk is the treasury overhang of about 304.0M MORPHO at the DAO and 91.44M at the Association; the ceiling is the 1B MORPHO total, which the DAO could in theory change.

# MORPHO Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads MORPHO as **inflationary**: circulating supply grew about **+7.03%** over the last 90 days and is projected to grow about the same over the next 90. No new MORPHO is minted and none is burned; the growth comes from **26.97M** of founder and strategic-partner unlocks plus **22.21M** paid out of Morpho-run wallets, with no buyback to offset it. The hard ceiling is the **1B** cap, of which **699.63M** is circulating today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Sep 29 2026, **49.18M MORPHO** entered the market against zero removed, a net of **+7.03%** of the **699.63M** circulating supply, and the forward reading for the 90 days to Dec 28 2026 is the same **+7.03%**. The inflation monitor reads **+7.77%** for the same window, a gap of **0.74 percentage points**. That is above our 0.5-point line, so the page carries a ⚠ monitor-gap note: most of the monitor's rise came in one step on Aug 31 2026 that no Ethereum transfer explains, while our reading books the Morpho unlock schedule and the Morpho-run wallets directly. In one line: MORPHO is a **fixed-cap governance token inflating on the active float through unlocks and treasury spending**, not through minting.

## Sell pressure: where new MORPHO comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**. MORPHO total supply read exactly the same at both ends of the window on the live token and on the legacy MORPHO token it replaced, and the supply figure sits in normal contract storage, so the flat reading is a real measurement. The MORPHO contract does keep a mint function, callable only by the Morpho DAO, and no vote to use it exists; the 1B cap is a governance commitment rather than a limit in the code.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **26.97M MORPHO** per 90 days. Two allocations are still vesting on the schedule the Morpho docs publish. The founders hold **152M**, 15.2% of supply, relocked into a two-year linear vest that finishes by May 17 2028 at the latest. The third cohort of strategic partners holds **67M**, 6.7% of supply, on a two-year linear vest that finishes on Nov 21 2027. Together they release about **300,000 MORPHO a day**, with roughly **162M** still locked. The first two strategic-partner cohorts and the early contributors finished vesting by Dec 2025. No on-chain vesting contract holds the locked MORPHO, so the published schedule is the measure.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **22.21M MORPHO**, read from the balances of every Morpho-controlled wallet on Ethereum and Base at both ends of the window. The Morpho Association alone paid out **19.09M**, matched transfer by transfer. Two other safes controlled by the same eight signers, including the Morpho Labs wallet, sent **0.92M**, and the Morpho DAO reward wallets paid users **2.20M** in vault and market incentives. The Association has paid out in every one of the last twelve months, so the forward reading holds the same 22.21M. Sell #4, long-term locks or bankruptcy, is **zero**: Morpho has no estate, no trustee and no long lock unwinding.

## Buy pressure: where new MORPHO goes

Every Morpho buy row is **zero**. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is zero because interest paid by Morpho borrowers goes to lenders and vault curators; the protocol fee switch exists, capped at 25% of borrower interest, but it is off, and Morpho's founders have said they would rather reinvest than buy MORPHO back. Buy #2, fee burn, is zero: total supply did not fall and the burn address did not grow. Buy #3, foundation buying, is zero because neither the Morpho DAO nor the Morpho Association took in any MORPHO this window. Apollo agreed in Feb 2026 to acquire up to 90M MORPHO over four years, but no tranche date or size has been disclosed, and any MORPHO the Association sells to Apollo leaves a Morpho wallet and is already counted in Sell #3. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is zero because MORPHO has no staking or lock program.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest team-held MORPHO overhang is the Morpho DAO treasury, **304.0M MORPHO** across Ethereum and Base, which did not move this window; it moves only by governance vote. The Morpho Association holds **91.49M** after spending from the **150M** grant the DAO sent it on Apr 30 2026 for its 2026 to 2030 program; a second safe run by the same signers holds **41.90M** and the Morpho Labs wallet **11.45M**. The DAO reward wallets hold about **7.9M** waiting to be claimed by users. On top of those balances sit the roughly **162M** founder and partner MORPHO still vesting. We re-read every one of these wallets on each refresh. Exchange custody wallets and unlabelled large holders are left out because those coins belong to depositors or to no identified group. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How MORPHO compares to other DeFi governance tokens

MORPHO belongs to the class of fixed-cap DeFi governance tokens whose supply was minted in full at launch and released over time, rather than chain coins that pay validators in new issuance. For tokens in this class the inflation question is never about a protocol emission rate; it is about how fast locked allocations and treasuries reach the market. On that measure MORPHO sits at the heavy end right now, because two separate streams run at once: a linear founder and partner vest of about 27M MORPHO a quarter, and a Morpho Association that is actively spending a large DAO grant.

The contrast with fee-sharing lending and exchange tokens is the buy side. Lending tokens that route part of borrower interest into buybacks or burns can offset unlocks; Morpho has chosen not to, keeping its fee switch off and paying interest through to lenders and curators. Against governance tokens whose treasuries sit idle, the difference is that the Morpho DAO treasury is largely untouched while the Association, funded from it, pays out monthly. MORPHO's supply ceiling is firm in practice at 1B, but the path to that ceiling runs through the vesting calendar until 2028 and through Association spending until 2030.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the founder and strategic-partner vest, which keeps releasing about 300,000 MORPHO a day through Dec 28 2026 and beyond, until Nov 21 2027 for the partners and May 17 2028 at the latest for the founders. Second, the size of Morpho Association payouts: they ran at **19.09M** this window but were far larger in Apr and May 2026, right after the 150M grant arrived. Third, any Apollo tranche under the up-to-90M agreement, which would show up as an Association outflow. Fourth, new Morpho DAO incentive votes such as the Arc program approved in Sep 2026 at **$50,000 a month**, and extra vault rewards that become claimable from Oct 1 2026. Fifth, any proposal to turn on the fee switch, which would be the first mechanism able to buy MORPHO back.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads MORPHO at **+7.03% net** over the trailing 90 days and **+7.03%** over the next 90: **26.97M** of founder and partner vesting plus **22.21M** paid out of Morpho-run wallets, against no buyback and no burn. The structural mechanism is release, not issuance: MORPHO minted its full 1B supply at launch and now reaches the market through a vesting calendar and a funded Morpho Association. The key risk is the size of that treasury, more than **450M MORPHO** across the DAO, Association-run and reward wallets plus about 162M still vesting. MORPHO's supply cannot pass 1B unless the Morpho DAO votes to mint.

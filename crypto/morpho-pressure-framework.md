# MORPHO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

MORPHO is inflationary, and not because of minting. No new MORPHO was created in the 90 days to Oct 24 2026, yet **49.65M MORPHO** reached the market: **34.79M** from the monthly unlocks of founders and strategic partners, and **14.86M** paid out by the Morpho Association from its grant. Nothing buys back or burns MORPHO, so the net is **+7.06%** of the **702.92M** circulating supply, and the next 90 days look the same. Market trackers read **+7.60%** for the same window. Supply is capped at 1B MORPHO, but about 196M are still locked and open every month until April 2028.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 26 to Oct 24 2026, MORPHO supply in the market grew by **+7.06%**, and we project **+7.06%** again for the 90 days to Jan 22 2027. Market trackers show **+7.60%** for the same window, **0.54 points** higher. Both see about 49.7M MORPHO arriving, so the two readings agree and no warning flag is shown on the coin page. MORPHO is a governance token with no mint in use and no buyback, so the only question is how fast locked and treasury coins reach the market. Today the answer is about 16.5M MORPHO a month. In one line: **an unlock-driven token with no buyer built in**.

## Sell pressure: where new MORPHO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. Total MORPHO supply read 999,999,999.8 at both ends of the window, and nothing was minted. The token contract does keep a mint function that only the Morpho DAO can use, so supply is capped by governance, not by code. MORPHO rewards for lenders and borrowers come out of coins the DAO already holds; those coins already count as circulating, so paying them out adds nothing new. The DAO reward wallet paid out about 4.16M MORPHO in the window this way.

Vesting unlocks are the biggest source: **34.79M MORPHO**. Founders hold 15.2% of supply and the third group of strategic partners holds 6.7%. Both relocked their coins and now release them in equal monthly steps of about **11.60M MORPHO** on the 21st of each month. The Aug 21, Sep 21 and Oct 21 2026 unlocks fall inside the window. The Nov 21 and Dec 21 2026 and Jan 21 2027 unlocks fall inside the next one, so the forecast repeats the same 34.79M. About 196.39M MORPHO is still locked this way, with the last unlock in April 2028.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add **14.86M MORPHO**. The Morpho Association, the non-profit that runs most of the work around the Morpho lending network, received a 150M MORPHO grant from the DAO on Apr 30 2026 to fund a program through 2030. Its wallet paid out 14,861,161 MORPHO between Jul 26 and Oct 24 2026, including 9.52M on Sep 16 2026 and 1.66M on Sep 28 2026. There is no published payout calendar, so we project the same 14.86M for the next 90 days. Long-term locks and bankruptcy releases are **0**: no court-held MORPHO exists and no long lock is due to open.

## Buy pressure: where new MORPHO goes

The buy side of MORPHO is empty. There is no programmatic buyback (**0**): borrowers on Morpho pay interest to lenders and to the vault managers who pick the markets, and the protocol fee that could send a cut to the DAO has never been switched on. There is no fee burn (**0**): the burn addresses held nothing at either end and total supply did not fall.

There is no foundation buy (**0**). The Association and the DAO pay MORPHO out; they do not buy it back. The largest outside buyer on record is Apollo, which agreed in February 2026 to acquire up to 90M MORPHO over four years, on the market or over the counter. Those purchases move coins that already circulate, so they do not take anything out of the count, even though they help absorb new supply. There is no new long-term lock (**0**) either: MORPHO has no staking and no vote-escrow, so nothing pulls coins out of the market for long periods.

## Foundation and overhang

Three pools matter for MORPHO's future supply. The first is the monthly unlock queue: about **196.39M MORPHO** still owed to founders and strategic partners, opening by about 11.60M a month until April 2028. It is a fixed schedule, checked by a web walk every two weeks.

The second is the Morpho Association wallet, which held **90.97M MORPHO** on Oct 24 2026 across Ethereum and Base. It pays out every month without a calendar, and we read its balance on-chain every day. The third is the Morpho DAO treasury, about **304M MORPHO** across Ethereum and Base, which did not move in the window. The DAO treasury already counts as circulating, so its payments add new supply only when they go to the Association. Its last big decisions were the 150M Association grant in April 2026 and a 6M top-up of the reward wallet in June 2026. Smaller team and contributor wallets hold about 41.6M, 11.4M, 9.5M, 9.0M and 4.0M MORPHO and all count as circulating.

If the Association wallet or the DAO treasury falls between our checks, that outflow enters the foundation row at the next refresh, and the same is true for any unlock that arrives earlier or larger than the schedule.

## How MORPHO compares to other DeFi lending governance tokens

Among DeFi lending tokens, MORPHO sits at the heavy-supply end. Aave runs a weekly buyback of AAVE paid for by protocol revenue, so part of its supply growth has a buyer built in. Maple uses part of its revenue to buy back SYRUP. MORPHO has neither: the Morpho lending network earns large interest flows for lenders and vault managers, but none of it reaches MORPHO holders while the protocol fee stays off.

The bigger difference is the unlock calendar. Older lending tokens finished their team and investor vesting years ago, so their new supply comes from emissions or treasury spending. MORPHO only became transferable on Nov 21 2024, and founders and partners relocked their coins into a schedule that runs to 2028. That puts MORPHO closer to young Layer 1 and Layer 2 tokens than to mature DeFi tokens: a hard 1B cap, but a float that grows every month for the next eighteen months. About 70% of MORPHO circulates today; the rest is the locked queue and the Association's grant.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The Nov 21 2026 unlock, about **11.60M MORPHO** for founders and partners, is the next dated step, followed by the same amount on Dec 21 2026 and Jan 21 2027.

The Morpho Association wallet is the second line: a month with payouts well above 5M MORPHO would push the foundation row above our forecast, and a quiet month would pull it below.

A governance vote to switch on the protocol fee or to fund a buyback would be the first real buyer of MORPHO built into the system; none is on the forum or the vote page as of Oct 24 2026.

Any new DAO grant or reward top-up, such as the Arc incentives the DAO approved on Sep 18 2026 at about $50,000 a month, should be checked for where the coins land: coins sent to the Association add to supply, while rewards paid straight from the DAO do not.

## Summary

MORPHO supply grows about **+7.06%** every 90 days with no minting at all: about **34.79M MORPHO** of monthly founder and partner unlocks plus about **14.86M** paid out by the Morpho Association. Nothing buys back or burns MORPHO, because the protocol fee has never been switched on. The key risk is that this pace holds until the unlocks end in April 2028, while about 196M locked MORPHO and 91M in the Association wallet still wait to reach the market. The ceiling is the 1B cap, which the DAO could only lift by voting to use the token's mint function.

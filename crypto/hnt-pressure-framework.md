# HNT Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

**HNT** is clearly inflationary right now: circulating Helium supply grew about **+8.26%** in the 90 days to Oct 25 2026 and is on course for about **+9.63%** in the next 90. The cause is a growth fund HNT holders voted for in July 2026, which has minted **196,000 HNT** a day for Nova Labs since Aug 6 2026, on top of the halving-schedule network rewards. Against **18.32M HNT** of new supply in the window, HNT burned for network data removed **1.95M**; other trackers read **+9.03%** for the same period.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 27 to Oct 25 2026, Helium created **18.32M HNT** and burned **1.95M HNT**, so net HNT supply grew by **+8.26%** of the **198.05M HNT** in circulation. Other trackers show **+9.03%** for the same window, a gap of **0.77 percentage points**. Both readings see the same flow of about 16.4M new HNT, so the gap is explained and the HNT coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days the ledger points to **19.62M HNT** created and about **549.63K** burned, or **+9.63%**. In one line: Helium is a burn-and-mint network whose voted growth fund now prints far more HNT than its data burn destroys.

## Sell pressure: where new HNT comes from

The first source of new HNT is protocol inflation: the network pays Helium hotspots, veHNT stakers and the IoT network in fresh HNT every day. The halving schedule sets **20,548 HNT** a day until Aug 1 2027, and up to **1,644 HNT** a day more is re-minted from coins burned earlier. In August 2026 the network also minted extra top-ups so Mobile hotspot owners reached a minimum dollar pay per gigabyte. Together these came to **2.44M HNT** between Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026, of which **469K** were the August top-ups.

Since Sep 1 2026 the network-reward pace has been about **21,980 HNT** a day, which gives about **1.98M HNT** for the next 90 days. That newer pace follows two changes inside the window: the largest carrier offload deal, which carried close to half of Helium Mobile traffic, was switched off on Aug 31 2026, and HIP-150, approved on Sep 2 2026, changed how the hotspot top-up is paid.

The second and much larger source is the **growth fund mint** created by HIP-149. HNT holders approved it in July 2026: about **141M** extra HNT over 36 months to fund Nova Labs' carrier expansion, engineering and operations. Since the first mint on Aug 6 2026 the network has created **196,000 HNT** every day, **193,550** into a Nova Labs vault and **2,450** to the five elected members of the Advisory Council. That is **15.88M HNT** in the 81 days of the window, and the flat rate gives **17.64M HNT** in the next 90 days. On its own, this mint is about nine times the size of the halving-schedule rewards.

Vesting unlocks add **0**. HNT was never pre-minted for a team or investors; every coin was created as a network reward, and all of it already counts as circulating. Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add **0** too, because every Nova Labs and treasury wallet is already inside the circulating count, as the next section shows. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**: there is no estate or court-held pile of HNT waiting to be released.

## Buy pressure: where new HNT goes

Helium has one built-in way to remove HNT: anyone who pays for network data burns HNT to create Data Credits, and the burned HNT is gone for good. In the 90 days to Oct 25 2026 this protocol fee burn destroyed **1.95M HNT**, most of it before Aug 31 2026, when the biggest carrier offload deal ended. Since Sep 1 2026 burns have run at only about **6,100 HNT** a day, so the next 90 days point to about **549.63K HNT**. Nova Labs' main burn wallet stopped burning from Sep 23 and started again on Oct 18 2026, at roughly 6,000 to 16,000 HNT a day since.

A programmatic buyback adds **0**. Helium Mobile did buy HNT on the market every day from late 2025, but that buyback was stopped on Jan 2 2026 and has not been restarted. A foundation buy is also **0**: neither Nova Labs nor the Helium Foundation buys HNT on the market, and the growth fund is newly minted rather than bought. New long-term locks add **0**, because HNT locked as veHNT for voting power is still counted as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

Every team-controlled HNT balance sits inside the circulating count, so none of it is new supply when it moves. It is still worth watching, because these wallets decide how fast freshly minted HNT reaches the market. The largest flow runs through the Nova Labs growth fund vault, which held **967,750 HNT** on Oct 25 2026 after **14.71M HNT** had already been moved out since Aug 6 2026, usually every two to eight days. HIP-149 asks Nova Labs to prefer over-the-counter sales with lockups over open-market selling and to report those sales to the Advisory Council.

Nova Labs also holds an operating wallet with **1.43M HNT** and a data-credit burn wallet with **83,396 HNT**. The MOBILE and IoT sub-DAO treasuries, which pay out HNT when old MOBILE and IOT tokens are swapped, hold **5.28M** and **2.25M HNT**. The Advisory Council account receives **2,450 HNT** a day and passes it to its members. These balances are re-read on-chain at every refresh, and if any of them falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Foundation and unscheduled unlocks at the next refresh.

## How HNT compares to other burn-and-mint DePIN networks

Helium belongs to the burn-and-mint family of decentralized physical infrastructure networks, the same broad design Render uses: users burn the token to pay for a real service, while new tokens are minted on a fixed schedule to reward the people who supply the hardware or work. In that design the burn only wins when real usage grows faster than the emission schedule. HNT spent the first half of 2026 close to that point, with supply drifting slightly down as data burns ran ahead of halving-schedule rewards.

The HIP-149 growth fund changes that comparison. It is a second mint stream outside the halving schedule, paid to the operating company rather than to hardware owners, and it is sized at about nine times Helium's own network rewards during its flat first year. Most burn-and-mint peers keep a single emission curve; Helium now runs two, and the second one sets the direction of HNT supply until it tapers away by mid-2029.

Compared with proof-of-stake chains that pay validators a few percent a year, HNT's current pace of roughly **9%** a quarter is far heavier. The difference is time-limited by design: the growth fund has hardcoded end dates, the HIP-20 schedule halves again on Aug 1 2027, and HNT holders can vote to cut, pause or stop the growth fund through the Advisory Council.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the growth fund mint of **196,000 HNT** continues every day through the whole next 90 days; only a community vote raised through the Advisory Council could reduce or pause it, and none is open as of Oct 25 2026.

Second, watch the size of the data burn after Nova Labs' burn wallet restarted on Oct 18 2026: if it holds near its recent pace of 10,000 HNT a day or more, the burn will come in above the **549.63K** estimate.

Third, watch new carrier offload deals after the Aug 31 2026 switch-off, since carrier traffic is what drives most Data Credit burns on Helium Mobile.

Fourth, Nova Labs owes the Advisory Council a first recommendation on tying its interests to HNT holders within six months of the first mint, so by about Feb 6 2027, just after this window.

Fifth, further out, the flat growth fund window ends around Jul 31 2027 and the halving schedule cuts network rewards in half on Aug 1 2027; both fall outside the next 90 days.

## Summary

HNT is inflationary: Helium supply grew **+8.26%** in the 90 days to Oct 25 2026 and is projected to grow about **+9.63%** in the next 90 days. The driver is the HIP-149 growth fund, which mints **196,000 HNT** a day for Nova Labs, about nine times the network's own halving-schedule rewards, while the HNT data burn removed only **1.95M** and has slowed since the largest carrier deal ended. The key risk is that the growth fund's coins reach the market faster than real network usage burns HNT. The ceiling is set in code: the growth fund totals about 141M HNT and tapers to zero by mid-2029, unless HNT holders vote to cut it sooner.

# TIA Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

TIA supply is growing, and it should keep growing over the next 90 days. Two things add coins: locked batches that open a little every day, and new TIA created for stakers, with no supply cap. From Aug 27 to Nov 25 2026 the unlocks added **26.83M TIA** and staking pay added **6.53M TIA**, while only **500 TIA** was destroyed, so supply grew **+3.36%**, close to the **+3.48%** other trackers show.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Celestia added about **33.36M TIA** to its circulating supply in the 90 days to Nov 25 2026 and removed almost none of it, so supply grew **+3.36%** on about **994.2M TIA** in circulation. Other trackers show **+3.48%** for the same stretch, just **0.13 points** higher, so the coin page carries no warning note. The pace is slowing: the early team's batch finished on Oct 30 2026 and the yearly staking rate stepped down on Oct 31 2026, so the next 90 days should add about **22.83M TIA**, or **+2.30%**. The label is simple: a growing supply, with growth cooling but still well above zero.

## Sell pressure: where new TIA comes from

The largest source is vesting. Celestia launched on Oct 31 2023 with 1 billion TIA, and most of the insider shares were locked and then opened a little each day. In this window those locked batches released **26.83M TIA**.

The early team's share, about 176.37M TIA in all, released **10.31M TIA** between Aug 27 and Oct 30 2026, at roughly 161,000 TIA a day, and then it was done. Nothing of it is left to unlock.

The Celestia Foundation's research and ecosystem share, about 267.94M TIA, released **16.52M TIA**, close to 183,524 TIA a day. This stream runs until Oct 30 2027, so it adds about the same **16.52M TIA** in the next 90 days and is now the single biggest source of new TIA. The coins sit with the Foundation rather than in a locked account on the chain, so the published calendar is what counts them.

The second source is staking pay. Every block the network creates new TIA for stakers and puts 2% of it in a shared community fund. That came to **6.53M TIA** in the window. On Oct 31 2026 the yearly rate fell from about 2.32% to **2.17%** of total supply, exactly as scheduled, so the next 90 days should bring about **6.31M TIA**. The rate drops by about a fifteenth each year until it settles at 1.5%, which on today's schedule happens early in the next decade.

The seed and Series A and B investors finished unlocking on Oct 30 2025, so they add nothing new. No bankrupt firm or court holds TIA for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new TIA goes

Very little TIA leaves the supply. The only coins destroyed in the window were **500 TIA** on Oct 6 2026, when a governance proposal was withdrawn and half of its deposit was burned under the chain's rules. That only happens when a proposal is pulled or vetoed, and none is open, so the next 90 days book zero.

Celestia burns no fees today. What rollups pay to post their data, including the new Fibre lane that went live in October 2026, goes to validators and stakers. Nothing buys TIA back on a schedule, and the Foundation announced no purchase in the window. Its one buyback, about 43.45M TIA taken from an early investor in July 2025, was handed on to new holders and has finished unlocking.

Staking takes nothing out either. About **466.17M TIA** is staked, close to half of the circulating supply, but staked coins still count as circulating and staking rewards can be sold as soon as they arrive.

## Foundation and overhang

Two pools sit outside the circulating supply. The first is about **125.94M TIA** set aside at launch for future public programs. It is unlocked but not counted as circulating, and it can only be spent after a public vote. No such vote is open, and none of it moved into circulation in the window.

The second is the Foundation's research and ecosystem share still on its calendar, **62.21M TIA** on Nov 25 2026. It is not a surprise risk: it is the same stream counted above, opening at about 183,524 TIA a day until Oct 30 2027. Together the two pools come to about **188.16M TIA**, the whole gap between the 1.18B TIA that exist and the 994.2M that circulate.

The shared community fund holds about **3.66M TIA**, up from 3.52M TIA on Aug 27 2026 with no spending. Those coins already count as circulating, so a grant from it would add nothing new.

## How TIA compares to other proof-of-stake chains

By mechanism, Celestia sits between two common designs. Like most proof-of-stake chains it pays stakers in new coins with no cap, but its rate is fixed in the code and falls on a set date each year, rather than moving with how much of the supply is staked, as on the Cosmos Hub.

Unlike Ethereum, it has no fee burn to offset that new supply, so every new coin stays. Celestia sells space for other chains' data, and those fees are still small next to the value of the network, so even a burn would take back little for now.

What really sets TIA apart today is the unlock calendar. Many chains that launched in the same years are past their insider unlocks by now. Celestia still has a Foundation share opening every day for another eleven months, and that stream adds roughly two and a half times as much TIA as staking pay does.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Every day to Feb 23 2027:** the Foundation's share keeps opening at about 183,524 TIA a day, about **16.52M TIA** over the 90 days. Watch whether those coins stay with the Foundation or reach exchanges.

**The blob fee plan (shared Sep 22 2026):** a set of proposals would add a base fee for posting data and could burn part of it. The first step is a public vote on a small feasibility budget, and as of Nov 25 2026 no vote has been filed. A fee burn would need its own later vote.

**The fee-burning idea (posted Nov 7 2026):** a separate forum post asks for blob fee burning and a staking requirement for rollups. It is a discussion, not a proposal with a vote date.

**Faster blocks (draft dated Oct 30 2026):** a planned upgrade would let blocks come as often as every 0.8 seconds. Staking pay is set by the clock, not by the block count, so this does not change how much TIA is created.

**Oct 30 and Oct 31 2027:** the Foundation's stream ends, and the yearly staking rate steps down again to about **2.02%**. After that, new TIA will come almost only from staking pay.

## Summary

TIA supply grew **+3.36%** from Aug 27 to Nov 25 2026, as locked batches released **26.83M TIA** and stakers were paid **6.53M TIA**.

Only **500 TIA** was destroyed in that time, and Celestia has no fee burn or buyback, so almost every new TIA stays in circulation.

With the early team's batch finished and the staking rate lower since Oct 31 2026, the next 90 days should add about **+2.30%**, mostly from the Foundation's share.

That Foundation stream ends on Oct 30 2027; after it, TIA supply grows only by staking pay of about 2% a year, falling toward 1.5%.

# TIA Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Celestia (TIA) supply is growing fast: **+3.82%** over the 90 days from Jul 29 to Oct 27 2026, and about **+2.36%** over the next 90 days. Two unlock streams that run every day released **31.01M TIA** and stakers were paid **6.62M TIA** of new coins, while only **500 TIA** was destroyed. TIA has no supply cap and no fee burn; the unlock streams are what make it grow this fast, and the larger of them runs until Oct 30 2027.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 27 2026, Celestia added **37.63M TIA** to its circulating supply and removed **500 TIA**, a net rise of **+3.82%** on a circulating supply of **986.31M TIA**. Market-data trackers show **+3.92%** for the same 90 days, **0.11 points** away from our figure, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days should bring **23.32M TIA**, or **+2.36%**, because the early-team stream ends on Oct 30 2026 and the staking rate steps down a day later. The label that fits TIA today: **inflationary by unlock schedule, with no burn to offset it**.

## Sell pressure: where new TIA comes from

The first source is staking pay. Every Celestia block mints new TIA and hands it to validators and the people who stake with them. The yearly rate is fixed in the chain's code at **2.32%** of supply until Oct 31 2026, and the amount minted depends on time passed, not on how many blocks are made. Between Jul 29 and Oct 27 2026 that came to **6.62M TIA**, about **73,550 TIA** a day. On Oct 31 2026 the rate takes its yearly step down to **2.17%**, so the next 90 days should bring about **6.32M TIA**. Each year it falls a little more, until it settles at 1.5%.

The second and biggest source is vesting. Two allocations from the 2023 launch still unlock a little every day. The early team's allocation released **14.50M TIA** in these 90 days, about 161,000 a day, and that stream ends on Oct 30 2026, leaving only **0.48M TIA** for the next window. The research and ecosystem pool, run by the Celestia Foundation, released **16.52M TIA**, about 183,500 a day, and keeps going at that pace until Oct 30 2027. Together the two streams released **31.01M TIA** last window and should release about **17.00M TIA** next. The early investors' allocations finished unlocking on Oct 30 2025, so they add nothing now.

The Foundation row books zero. A reserve set aside for future initiatives, **125.94M TIA**, is still kept out of the circulating count, but no vote, plan or sale has been announced for it, so nothing is booked. There is no bankruptcy estate or court-held pile of TIA, so the last sell row is zero too.

## Buy pressure: where new TIA goes

Very little leaves. No contract or treasury buys TIA back for the network, so the buyback row is zero. Celestia does not burn fees either: what rollups pay to post their data goes to validators and stakers, and the new Fibre service that went live on Oct 20 2026 pays validators the same way. The Foundation's last purchase was **43.4M TIA** bought back from an early investor in July 2025, and it has bought nothing since, so the Foundation row is zero.

Staking does not remove coins either, because staked TIA still counts as circulating. In fact staking shrank: staked TIA fell from **506.27M** to **464.42M** during the window, and **47.31M TIA** is now on its way out of staking. The only real removal came on Oct 6 2026, when a governance proposal was withdrawn and half of its 1,000 TIA deposit, **500 TIA**, was destroyed. That kind of burn is rare, so the next 90 days book none.

## Foundation and overhang

Three piles sit outside the circulating count today, and together they explain the whole gap between the **1.18B TIA** that exist and the **986.31M** that circulate. The largest is the future-initiatives reserve of **125.94M TIA**, with no release date. Next is the research and ecosystem pool, with **67.54M TIA** still on its calendar; it unlocks every day until Oct 30 2027 and is already in the vesting row. Last is the early-team stream, with only **0.48M TIA** left before it ends on Oct 30 2026.

Other team-linked wallets already count as circulating. A large operating wallet held since launch went from **20.45M** to **19.03M TIA** in the window, and the on-chain community pool grew from **3.48M** to **3.61M TIA** with no spending. When these wallets pay out, the coins only change hands inside the float, so they add no new supply. We re-check the piles at every rebuild; if the future-initiatives reserve starts to shrink between checks, the outflow enters the Foundation row at the next one.

## How TIA compares to other proof-of-stake data chains

Most proof-of-stake chains grow through staking pay alone. TIA's staking pay is now modest at **2.32%** a year and falling on a fixed schedule toward 1.5%, lower than many Cosmos-built chains whose rate floats with how much is staked. What sets TIA apart is the unlock calendar: three years after launch it is still releasing about **344,000 TIA** a day from team and Foundation allocations, which is more than four times what it pays stakers per day.

Ethereum, the main rival for rollup data, works the other way. It has no unlocks, and it destroys the base fee of every transaction, including the blob fees rollups pay, so heavy use can slow or even reverse its supply growth. Celestia sends all its fees to validators instead, so more data posted on Celestia raises staking income but does not cut supply. A community plan published on Sep 22 2026 asks to study a partial fee burn and paid capacity, but it is only a forum draft with no vote and no code.

The comparison changes after Oct 30 2027. Once the ecosystem stream ends, TIA's supply growth should fall to just its staking pay, about 2% a year, unless the future-initiatives reserve is released or the fee model changes.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 30 2026:** the early-team unlock stream ends, which removes about 161,000 TIA a day of new supply. **Oct 31 2026:** the yearly staking-rate step from 2.32% to 2.17% takes effect in the chain's code, cutting new coins by about 1.26M TIA a year. **Nov 5 2026 (estimated):** the Forma chain stops, and the TIA left on it is moved to Eden, a newer chain that uses TIA for gas; that moves coins, it does not create any. **Next weeks:** whether the 47.31M TIA now leaving staking gets sold, and whether Fibre, live since Oct 20 2026, brings paying users. **Ongoing:** any vote to release the 125.94M TIA reserve or to switch on a fee burn, which would change this reading.

## Summary

Celestia's TIA supply grew **+3.82%** over the 90 days to Oct 27 2026 and should grow about **+2.36%** over the next 90 days. Most of the growth comes from two unlock streams, **31.01M TIA** in the window, plus **6.62M TIA** of staking pay; TIA has no fee burn and no buyback, and only 500 TIA was destroyed. The key risk is the Foundation's ecosystem stream, which keeps adding about 183,500 TIA a day until Oct 30 2027, and a 125.94M TIA reserve with no release plan. TIA has no supply cap, but once the streams end its growth drops to its staking pay, set to fall toward 1.5% a year.

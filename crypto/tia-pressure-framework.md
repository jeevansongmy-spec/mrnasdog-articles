# TIA Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

TIA supply is growing and is projected to keep growing: the Pressure Framework reads Celestia at **+3.87%** over the last 90 days and **+2.97%** over the next 90. Most of it is not the mint. Two launch unlock streams moved **31.01M TIA** into the float, while Celestia's staking mint added **6.62M TIA** at **2.32%** a year. Buy pressure is **0**: TIA has no buyback and no fee burn, and the monitor reads **+4.06%** for the same window.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **972.1M TIA**, the framework books **37.63M TIA** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days, a net of **+3.87%**, and projects **28.92M TIA**, or **+2.97%**, for the next 90 days. The inflation monitor reads **+4.06%** for the same window, a gap of **0.19 percentage points**. That is inside the framework's 0.5-point tolerance, so the overview ships with no warning. Most of the small difference is base convention: the monitor divides by the supply of 90 days ago, which was smaller. The label for TIA is a **staking chain whose launch unlocks still outweigh its mint**, and the unlocks are now winding down.

## Sell pressure: where new TIA comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **6.62M TIA**. Celestia mints new TIA in every block and pays it to stakers, with **2%** sent to the community pool. The mint is paid by time, not by block count, so the move to 3-second blocks inside this window changed nothing. The chain's own mint settings read an inflation rate of **2.32%** and yearly provisions of **26.87M TIA** at both ends of the window. Total TIA rose from **1,171.4M** to **1,178.1M**, which matches the mint to about **1.3 TIA** of rounding across **2.6M blocks**. The rate sits on a fixed curve set by the Celestia upgrade of November 2025: it started that era at **2.67%**, drops **6.7%** each year, and stops at a **1.5%** floor. The next step lands on **Oct 31 2026**, cutting the rate to **2.17%**, so the forward mint reads **6.44M TIA**.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is the bigger number at **31.01M TIA**. Two Celestia launch allocations unlock in a straight line every day. The initial core contributors, **176.4M TIA** in total, release about **161,000 TIA** a day until **Oct 30 2026**. R&D and ecosystem, **267.9M TIA** in total, release about **184,000 TIA** a day until **Oct 30 2027**. The seed and Series A and B investors finished unlocking in October 2025, so they add nothing now. Because the core-contributor stream stops about a month into the next window, forward vesting falls to **22.48M TIA**. Only part of this is locked in accounts on chain: those accounts released **12.03M TIA**in the window, most of it core-contributor coins. The R&D and ecosystem coins are held by the Celestia Foundation instead, and they join the counted float as the published schedule releases them. No TIA is created by an unlock. These coins already existed; they simply join the tradable float.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0**. The only pool outside the float without a date is the public allocation for future initiatives, and no governance vote spent any of it in this window. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is also **0**: TIA has no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-ordered payout.

## Buy pressure: where new TIA goes

Nowhere, this window. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**. The Celestia Foundation's one repurchase, **43.45M TIA** bought from an early investor in July 2025, was a private deal outside this window, and those coins were passed on to new holders on a set schedule. A forum plan dated **Sep 22 2026** to let paid data services replace part of the mint is still a proposal, with no vote and no code.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**, and it is checked two ways. Celestia fees go to stakers, not to a burn, and total TIA rose by exactly the minted amount. Any burn would have pulled supply below the mint, and it did not. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0**: the Foundation bought no TIA on the market; its delegation program stakes coins it already holds. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**. Staked TIA actually fell from **503.2M** to **474.3M**, and staked coins count as float here anyway.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest pool outside the float is the public allocation for future initiatives, about **125.9M TIA**. It has no release date and can only be spent through Celestia governance, so it is watched at every rebuild. The second item is what is still locked on the two unlock schedules, about **79.7M TIA**: **73.8M** of R&D and ecosystem and **5.96M** of core contributors. That part is not a decision anyone makes; it is already on a calendar. Third is the community pool, which held **3.57M TIA** at the end of the window, up from **3.43M**, growing only from its share of the mint. It already counts as float, so spending it would move coins inside the market, not add to it.

Those three pools fully explain the gap between total TIA and the counted float, to the coin. If the future-initiatives pool or either locked schedule releases more than the calendar allows between refreshes, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How TIA compares to other proof-of-stake chains

On the mint alone, TIA now sits at the low end of proof-of-stake chains. A **2.32%** rate falling toward a **1.5%** floor is well below the **5%** to **10%** that many staking chains built on the same software still pay. Celestia got there by cutting twice, in July 2025 and November 2025. What it does not have is a burn. Ethereum and exchange tokens with regular buybacks remove coins as usage grows, and their readings can turn negative. TIA has no such exit, so its reading can only fall as far as the mint.

The real story is closer to a young token working through its team and investor vest. For now the unlocks are nearly five times the mint. That changes on a known date. After **Oct 30 2026** only the R&D and ecosystem stream is left, at roughly **16.5M TIA** a quarter, and after **Oct 30 2027** nothing is left but the mint. Unlike a cliff-based vest, TIA releases smoothly every day, so there is no single unlock day to trade around.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, **Oct 30 2026**, when the core-contributor unlock stream ends after releasing its last **5.96M TIA**. Second, **Oct 31 2026**, when the yearly mint step cuts the rate from **2.32%** to **2.17%**. Third, the Sustainable Blob Economy package from **Sep 22 2026**: any vote that ties issuance to revenue or adds a burn would change the buy side for the first time. Fourth, the next network upgrade, now in testing; the code as it stands does not change the mint, and any version that does would show up in the chain's mint settings. Fifth, the **125.9M TIA** future-initiatives pool, which only a governance vote can move.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads TIA at **+3.87%** over the trailing 90 days and **+2.97%** projected forward: supply growing, projected to keep growing. The main driver is launch unlocks, **31.01M TIA** a quarter, with a **2.32%** staking mint adding **6.62M** on top, and nothing being bought back or burned. The key risk is that TIA has no way to remove supply, so every new coin stays. The easing is on a calendar: one unlock stream ends **Oct 30 2026**, the last one on **Oct 30 2027**, and the mint keeps falling toward a **1.5%** floor.

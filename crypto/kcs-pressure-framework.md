# KCS Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

*Checked Nov 4 2026*

**TL;DR.** KCS supply did not move in the 90 days to Nov 4 2026: net **0.00%**, and the next 90 days look the same. KuCoin Token makes no new coins, its monthly buy-and-burn has been silent since **Dec 31 2025**, and the 2022 lock wallet still holds **47.5M KCS** it has not touched since Mar 31 2022.

Sell pressure was **0 KCS** and buy pressure was **0 KCS**. Other trackers show **+1.83%**, because they counted one more 2.5M KCS slice of the lock plan as unlocked on Oct 2 2026, so the coin page carries a warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

KCS is **flat**. Over the 90 days from Aug 6 to Nov 4 2026 no KCS was created, none left a lock, and none was burned, so the net change is **0.00%**, with **0.00%** expected for the 90 days to Feb 2 2027. Other trackers read **+1.83%**, a gap of **1.83 points**. That difference is a paper count, not coins on the market: a 2.5M KCS slice of the 2022 lock plan was marked as unlocked on Oct 2 2026 while the coins stayed in the same wallet. The KCS coin page carries a warning note that explains it. Label: **mixed flows, supply roughly steady**, with a large team-held pile that is the real thing to watch.

## Sell pressure: where new KCS comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0.** KCS has a hard limit of 200M coins, and nothing in the current chain rules makes new ones. Validators on KuCoin Community Chain earn about **0.059 KCS** a block, but that pay comes from a reward pool that KuCoin refills with coins that already exist. The pool simply passes them along, so the count does not rise. The older Ethereum copy of KCS also held at **165.64M** from Aug 6 to Nov 4 2026.

**Vesting unlocks: 0.** In 2022 the founders and early backers re-locked **90M KCS**. Of that, 20M was burned, 20M went to a holder reward pool, and **50M** was set to free up in 20 quarterly slices of **2.5M KCS**, ending with the last quarter of 2026. On paper, the 19th slice opened on Oct 2 2026. In practice the lock wallet held **47.5M KCS** on both Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026, and its last outgoing transfer was on Mar 31 2022, when it sent the first slice. Since then, 18 slices have been counted as free while every one of those coins stayed put, so no new KCS reached the market from this plan.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** The lock wallet and the holder reward pool could both send coins at any time, but neither sent a single KCS in the window. The size of these wallets is covered in the overhang section below.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court estate or bankruptcy pile of KCS is waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new KCS goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** For years KuCoin used part of its profit to buy KCS and burn it every month. The last burn was the 66th, **20,240 KCS**, on Dec 31 2025. In the ten months since, no burn has been sent and none has been announced, even though KuCoin's own token pages still describe the program. Until a burn actually happens again, this row stays at zero.

**Protocol fee burn: 0.** Gas paid on KuCoin Community Chain goes to the validator reward pool, not to a burn. The burn address took in just **0.018 KCS** of stray coins from Aug 6 to Nov 4 2026.

**Foundation buy: 0.** Neither KuCoin nor the KCS Management Foundation bought KCS back beyond the dormant burn.

**New long-term lock: 0.** About **55.5M KCS** sits in the chain's staking contract, but staked KCS still counts as circulating, so staking takes nothing out of the count.

## Foundation and overhang

KCS has **142.16M** coins in total and **139.66M** counted as circulating, so **98.2%** is already treated as unlocked. That headline hides how much is still in team hands. The 2022 lock wallet holds **47.5M KCS**, about a third of everything circulating, and only the final 2.5M of it is still marked as locked. The holder reward pool, funded from the same wallet on Mar 31 2022, holds **19.9M KCS**.

Together that is **67.4M KCS** under team control that has sat still for more than four years. None of it is selling now, so it adds nothing to the 90-day count. If either wallet starts sending coins, those moves will show up here as new sell pressure, and they would dwarf anything else on this page.

## How KCS compares to other exchange tokens

Exchange tokens live or die on one lever: how much of the exchange's money is turned into burns. BNB still runs a quarterly burn on a published plan, so its supply keeps falling on a known timetable. OKB took the other route: OKX burned down to a fixed 21M count and ended its buyback in August 2025, so its supply simply stays put.

KCS now sits closest to OKB in practice, flat, with no new coins and no burns, but without an announcement that the burn has ended. Its own twist is the lock plan: many KCS coins are counted as free on paper while they still sit in a team wallet. That makes KCS's quiet supply less settled than OKB's, because the biggest change could come from that wallet rather than from the exchange's profits.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Early January 2027: the last paper slice.** The final 2.5M KCS of the 2022 plan is due to be counted as unlocked around Jan 2 2027, the same rhythm as the Jul 2 and Oct 2 2026 slices. Trackers will likely show another jump of about 1.8%. On-chain, nothing changes unless the lock wallet moves.

**Any move from the 47.5M lock wallet.** The first outgoing transfer since Mar 31 2022 would be the biggest supply event KCS has had in years, and it would go straight into the sell side.

**Whether the monthly burn comes back.** A 67th burn notice would put buy pressure back on the page. Each month without one makes the Dec 31 2025 burn look more like the last.

**KuCoin's user base.** KuCoin ended service for users in Russia and Belarus on Oct 29 2026. Fewer users means less trading profit, which is the money any future burn would come from.

## Summary

KCS supply was flat in the 90 days to Nov 4 2026, at **0.00%**, and is set to stay flat through Feb 2 2027.

KuCoin Token makes no new coins, and KuCoin's monthly buy-and-burn has been silent since the **20,240 KCS** burn on Dec 31 2025.

Other trackers show **+1.83%** because a 2.5M KCS slice of the 2022 lock plan was counted as unlocked on Oct 2 2026, while the lock wallet's **47.5M KCS** has not moved since Mar 31 2022.

The real risk for KCS supply is that team-held pile of **67.4M KCS**, not the paper unlocks or the missing burns.

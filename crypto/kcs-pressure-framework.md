# KCS Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads KuCoin Token (KCS) as mildly inflationary: **+1.82%** net over the last 90 days and **+1.82%** over the next 90. The whole move comes from one scheduled source, a 2022 plan that releases **2.5M KCS** from a locked pool at the start of every quarter, while the KuCoin buyback-and-burn that used to offset it has not fired since **Dec 31 2025**. Only **5.0M KCS** is still locked, so the unlocks end after two more slices, around **Oct 1 2026** and **Jan 1 2027**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the trailing 90 days, from **Jul 1 2026** to **Sep 29 2026**, the KCS ledger shows **2.5M KCS** of new tradable supply and **zero** removed, against **137.16M KCS** circulating: a net of **+1.82%**. The next 90 days read the same, **+1.82%**, because the next quarterly slice is due inside that window and no burn is scheduled. Our inflation monitor reads **+1.82%** as well, a gap of **0.00 percentage points**, far inside the half-point tolerance, so no warning chip is shown. The label for KCS today: an exchange token whose supply grows by schedule while its buyback sits idle.

## Sell pressure: where new KCS comes from

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, carries the whole sell side: **2.5M KCS**. When the KCS whitepaper was published on **Mar 29 2022**, the founders and early backers of KuCoin put their 90M locked KCS on a new plan. 20M KCS was burned, 20M KCS went to a pool for holder and contributor rewards, and the remaining **50M KCS** — 18M for early investors and 32M gifted to the KCS Management Foundation — was set to be released in equal quarterly slices over five years. That is 20 slices of **2.5M KCS**. KuCoin publishes the KCS circulating and total supply in its own notices, and the steps match the plan exactly: each quarter circulating supply rises by 2.5M KCS while total supply stays flat. In 2026 the slices landed on **Jan 2**, **Apr 2** and **Jul 1**; the July slice took circulating supply from **134.66M** to **137.16M KCS**, and it falls inside this window.

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**. KCS is the gas coin of KuCoin Community Chain, and blocks on that chain carry no reward: validators earn only the fees users pay, in KCS that already exists. The published KCS total supply did not rise at any point in the window. Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **zero**: none of the tracked team wallets released KCS in the past year. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy supply, is **zero**: no estate or court schedule holds KCS, and the only locked coins are the 5.0M on the quarterly plan.

## Buy pressure: where new KCS goes

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is **zero**, and this is the biggest change in the KCS story. KuCoin describes a monthly buyback-and-burn funded from exchange income, and through 2025 it ran every month, removing between **17.8K** and **83.7K KCS** a time. The 66th burn, **20,240 KCS** on **Dec 31 2025**, is the last one: no KCS burn has been announced in 2026, and the burn address on KuCoin Community Chain has received nothing but stray dust since. That burn address holds **26.78M KCS** in total. With no firing for nine months, the framework books nothing for the next 90 days either; if KuCoin restarts the monthly burn, Buy #1 returns at the next refresh.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **zero**: KuCoin Community Chain burns no fees, and the **0.018 KCS** that reached the burn address this window came from a stray send. Buy #3, foundation buying, is **zero**, with no purchase announced. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is **zero**: about **55.41M KCS** is staked with the chain's validators and more is staked on the exchange, but staked KCS still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing from the float.

## Foundation and overhang

The one overhang that matters for the KCS count is the **5.0M KCS** still locked under the 2022 plan, the gap between total supply of **142.16M** and circulating supply of **137.16M**. It is due in two slices, the first around **Oct 1 2026**. On KuCoin Community Chain the old lock wallet still holds **47.5M KCS** and has not moved since **Mar 31 2022**; KuCoin counts only 5.0M of it as locked, so the rest is already inside the float and a sale from it would add nothing new. Three more team-linked wallets are tracked: a holder and contributor incentive pool with **19.9M KCS**, unchanged since Dec 2024, and two early wallets with **1.6M** and **0.9M KCS** that have never moved. The burn address is tracked too, at **26.78M KCS**. These balances are re-read on every refresh; if any of them falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How KCS compares to other exchange tokens

Exchange tokens share one promise: the exchange uses part of its income to take its own token off the market. KCS and BNB both tie a burn to the business and both aim for a long-run supply of 100M — for KCS, from a 200M start. The difference is in who sets the burn. A burn written into fixed code fires on a timetable whatever the managers decide, while the KCS burn is a policy KuCoin runs by hand. That design is why the KCS burn could stop for nine months with no rule broken, and why the KCS supply reading now turns on a company decision rather than on code.

The second difference is the unlock tail. Many exchange tokens have no locked supply left, so their float can only shrink or stay flat. KCS still has a scheduled release running, and with the burn idle that release sets the direction: **2.5M KCS** a quarter is about **1.8%** of the float. Unlike proof-of-stake chains that pay validators in new coins, KCS mints nothing, so once the last slice lands around **Jan 1 2027** the KCS float can only hold steady or shrink.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the 19th of 20 quarterly slices, **2.5M KCS** due around **Oct 1 2026**, which the forward reading already counts. Second, any sign that KuCoin restarts the monthly buyback-and-burn: a burn notice or a new transfer to the burn address would bring Buy #1 back and cut the net. Third, the final slice around **Jan 1 2027**, just outside this window, after which the KCS lock is empty. Fourth, the old lock wallet with **47.5M KCS** and the **19.9M KCS** incentive pool, which have not moved in years. Fifth, KCS utility changes on the exchange, such as token sales for KCS holders, which change why people hold KCS but not how many exist.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads KuCoin Token (KCS) at **+1.82% net** over the trailing 90 days and **+1.82%** over the next 90: a scheduled quarterly unlock of **2.5M KCS** with nothing on the buy side, because KuCoin's monthly buyback-and-burn has not fired since **Dec 31 2025**. KCS has no block reward, so the unlock plan is the only source of new tradable supply, and only **5.0M KCS** of it is left. The key risk for the reading is the burn: it depends on a KuCoin decision, and it can return or stay silent. After the last slice around Jan 1 2027, KCS supply has no scheduled growth at all.

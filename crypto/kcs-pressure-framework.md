# KCS Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

KCS supply did not move from Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026: **0 KCS** came out and **0 KCS** was burned, for a net change of **0.00%**, and the next 90 days look the same. The KuCoin Community Chain pays its block makers from coins KuCoin already owns, KuCoin's monthly buy-and-burn has been quiet since Dec 31 2025, and the wallet behind the 2022 lock plan still holds **47.5M KCS**. Other trackers show **+1.79%**, so the coin page carries a warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

KCS is in a quiet spell. Between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026 no new KCS was made, no locked KCS left its wallet, and KuCoin burned none, so the net change is **0.00%** on about **139.7M KCS** counted as circulating. The next 90 days, to Feb 21 2027, also come out at **0.00%** unless something changes. Other trackers show **+1.79%**, a gap of **1.79 points**. That gap comes from one date: on Oct 1 2026 the market's supply figure rose by 2.5M KCS, because one more quarterly slice of the 2022 lock plan was counted as unlocked, while the coins themselves stayed where they were. For that reason the coin page carries a warning note. Our label is mixed flows, supply roughly steady: nothing is being added, and nothing is being taken away either.

## Sell pressure: where new KCS comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0 KCS.** The KuCoin Community Chain has no block reward. The nodes that make blocks are paid from a reward pot, and KuCoin refills that pot every day with KCS it already holds, about **1,600 to 3,500 KCS** a day in late November 2026. Coins move from one wallet to another, but none is created.

**Vesting unlocks: 0 KCS.** In March 2022 the team re-locked 90M KCS. Of that, 20M was burned, 20M went to a reward pool for holders and builders, and 50M was set to be freed in 20 slices of **2.5M KCS**, one per quarter. The nineteenth slice was counted as unlocked on Oct 1 2026, and the twentieth, the last, is due around Jan 1 2027. Yet the lock wallet still held **47.5M KCS** on both Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026, and it has sent nothing since Mar 31 2022. Coins that are free on paper but never leave the wallet have not been sold, so nothing reached the market in the last 90 days, and nothing is expected to in the next 90.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0 KCS.** None of the wallets tied to the 2022 plan moved in the window. They are listed by name in the overhang section below.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0 KCS.** No court, trustee or failed firm is holding KCS for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new KCS goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0 KCS.** For years KuCoin bought KCS with part of its profit and burned it each month, aiming to shrink supply to 100M. The last burn was the sixty-sixth: **20,240 KCS**, worth about 244,475 USDT, on Dec 31 2025. The burn address on the KuCoin Community Chain has not grown since, KuCoin has posted no burn notice in 2026, and total supply still reads **142.2M KCS**, the same figure KuCoin gave after that last burn. KuCoin's own price page still says 10% of net profit goes to buying back and burning, but nothing has been burned for almost 11 months and no date is given, so the next 90 days also show zero.

**Protocol fee burn: 0 KCS.** The chain does not burn its fees; they go to the nodes that make blocks.

**Foundation buy: 0 KCS.** Neither KuCoin nor the KCS Management Foundation has a public plan to buy KCS and hold it off the market.

**New long-term lock: 0 KCS.** Staked KCS still counts as circulating and can be withdrawn, so staking removes nothing from supply.

## Foundation and overhang

Four wallets from the 2022 plan hold the coins that could reach the market one day. The lock wallet holds **47.5M KCS**, the part of the 50M plan that never left. The holder and builder reward pool holds **19.9M KCS**. Two wallets that received the first slice on Mar 31 2022 hold **1.6M KCS** (the KCS Management Foundation's share) and **0.9M KCS** (the early investors' share). Together that is close to **69.9M KCS**, about half of the circulating count, and none of it moved between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026. Most of it is already counted as circulating, so the risk is not a jump in the supply figure but real selling if these wallets ever start to send. A move from any of them would show up on the coin page.

## How KCS compares to other exchange tokens

Most large exchange tokens keep their supply in check with a regular burn paid for by the exchange's earnings, so holders can watch coins leave every month or quarter. KCS used to work the same way with its monthly burn, but in 2026 it has had none. Today it behaves more like a token with a fixed supply: no new coins come in and none go out.

The other thing that sets KCS apart is the gap between paper and wallet. Many tokens release coins from a lock and those coins quickly reach exchanges. With KCS, the lock plan is almost complete on paper, yet the wallet that holds the coins has not moved in more than four years. That makes the supply count look larger than the coins that have really changed hands.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Around Jan 1 2027: the last paper slice.** The twentieth and final 2.5M KCS slice of the 2022 plan is due. If it follows the past slices, the market's supply figure will rise by 2.5M KCS while the lock wallet stays at **47.5M KCS**. If the wallet does send coins, that would be the first real release since 2022.

**Any burn notice from KuCoin.** A sixty-seventh burn would be the first since Dec 31 2025 and would put the buy side back to work.

**The lock wallet and the reward pool.** Together they hold about **67.4M KCS**. Any outflow from either would be the first real new selling of KCS in years.

**KuCoin's business.** KuCoin left Russia and Belarus on Oct 29 2026 and paused euro bank deposits from Nov 18 2026. A smaller business means less profit to fund any restart of the burn.

## Summary

KCS supply was flat from Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026: no new coins, no burns, and a net change of **0.00%**.

KuCoin's monthly buy-and-burn last fired on Dec 31 2025, when it removed **20,240 KCS**.

The 2022 lock plan is almost done on paper, but its wallet still holds **47.5M KCS** and has sent nothing since Mar 31 2022.

Other trackers show **+1.79%** because they count each paper slice as new supply, so the coin page carries a warning note.

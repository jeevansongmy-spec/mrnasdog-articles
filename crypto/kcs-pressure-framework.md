# KCS Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

KuCoin Token (KCS) supply was flat over the 90 days to Oct 25 2026: **0.00%** net, and flat again for the next 90 days. KuCoin Community Chain creates no new KCS, the monthly buyback and burn has not fired since **Dec 31 2025**, and the wallet holding the last **47.5M KCS** of the 2022 team lock has not sent a single coin since Mar 31 2022. Other trackers show **+1.92%**, because **2.5M KCS** of that wallet was counted as unlocked on Oct 1 2026 without moving.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Between Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026, **0 KCS** entered the market and **0 KCS** was destroyed, so KuCoin Token supply moved **0.00%**, and the same flat reading is expected for the next 90 days. Other trackers read **+1.92%** for the same stretch, a difference of **1.92 points**. That difference comes from one dated change in the count, not from coins moving: on Oct 1 2026 one more **2.5M KCS** slice of the 2022 lock plan was counted as circulating while the lock wallet kept every coin. The coin page carries a warning note that explains this. KCS today is a frozen exchange token: no new coins, no burn, and one large wallet that never moves.

## Sell pressure: where new KCS comes from

**Protocol inflation is zero.** KCS is the gas coin of KuCoin Community Chain, and a block on that chain creates nothing. Validators are paid from a reward pool that KuCoin refills with coins that already exist. About **153K KCS** reached validators this way between Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026, but every one of those coins was already counted as circulating, so the total did not change.

**Vesting unlocks are zero.** In 2022 KuCoin's founders and early backers re-locked their coins and set **50M KCS** to unlock in 20 quarterly slices of **2.5M**, finishing at the turn of 2026. The first slice left the lock wallet on Mar 31 2022. None of the other slices ever did. The slice counted as unlocked on Oct 1 2026 stayed in the wallet, which held **47.5M KCS** on both Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026. Coins that are unlocked on paper but never leave the wallet cannot be sold, so this row books nothing.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero.** None of the team-side wallets sent KCS during the window; they are listed one by one in the overhang section below.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is zero.** No court case, bankruptcy estate or seized pile of KCS waits to be paid out. KuCoin's 2025 settlement with US authorities was paid in money, not in KCS.

## Buy pressure: where new KCS goes

**The programmatic buyback is zero for now.** For years KuCoin bought KCS on the market each month, using part of its revenue, and sent it to a dead address on its chain. That address now holds **26.78M KCS**. The last burn was **20,240 KCS** on Dec 31 2025. Since then the dead address has received only dust, and KuCoin has announced no burn in 2026, although its own token page still describes the burn as monthly. With no burn in ten months and no new schedule, the next 90 days book zero too.

**The protocol fee burn is zero.** Gas paid on KuCoin Community Chain is not destroyed. It goes into the same pool that pays validators, so using the chain does not shrink supply.

**Foundation buying is zero.** Neither KuCoin nor the KCS Management Foundation buys KCS to hold in a treasury. The burn was the only buyer, and it is paused.

**New long-term locks are zero.** About **55.46M KCS** is staked with validators on KuCoin Community Chain, and stakers took out about **534K KCS** during the window. Staked KCS is still counted as circulating, so staking neither adds to nor takes away from the count.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the 2022 lock wallet, which KuCoin named as the home of the locked team and backer coins back in 2020. It holds **47.5M KCS**, about a third of everything in circulation, and its last outgoing transfer was on Mar 31 2022. Almost all of it is already counted as circulating, so if it ever moves, the coins will hit the market without the count rising first.

Three more wallets sit on the team side. A holder-reward pool funded from the lock in 2022 holds **19.9M KCS** and has barely moved since. An early KuCoin wallet holds **12.48M KCS**; its last transfer, 8M KCS to another wallet, was on Mar 18 2025. Two wallets that received the first slice of the plan hold **1.6M** and **0.9M KCS** and have never sent anything. Every one of these balances was the same on Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026. We read them again at each rebuild: if any of them falls between checks, the outflow enters the foundation row at the next check.

## How KCS compares to other exchange-token chains

Most big exchange tokens follow the same idea: a fixed supply created at launch, cut over time by burns paid for with exchange revenue. KCS started at **200M** and aims to end at 100M; about 142.16M exist today. Where the burn keeps running, an exchange token shrinks every quarter. KCS has had no such shrink in 2026, so for now it behaves like a fixed-supply coin with nothing taken away.

The other difference is the chain itself. Some exchange chains destroy part of every gas fee, so busy days remove coins without any decision from the exchange. KuCoin Community Chain does not; gas goes to validators. That leaves KCS supply fully in KuCoin's hands: it shrinks only when KuCoin chooses to burn, and it grows on the market only when the lock wallet chooses to send.

Compared with tokens that still have years of team vesting ahead, the KCS plan is almost finished on paper. Its weak point is that the paper and the wallet tell different stories: the plan says nearly all of the 47.5M KCS is unlocked, while on-chain none of it has moved.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The last slice, around Jan 1 2027.** The 2022 plan ends at the turn of 2026, when the final **2.5M KCS** should be counted as unlocked. Other trackers will likely show another jump then; on our reading it changes nothing unless coins leave the lock wallet.

**The lock wallet.** Any transfer out of the 47.5M KCS wallet would be the first since Mar 31 2022, and the biggest supply event KCS could have.

**A burn in late October, November or December 2026.** KuCoin used to burn in the last week of each month. A burn in any of those weeks would mean the buyback has restarted and would put KCS back on a shrinking path.

**Staking.** Watch the 55.46M KCS staked on KuCoin Community Chain. It does not change the count, but a fast drop would put more coins in reach of sellers.

## Summary

KuCoin Token supply was flat over the 90 days to Oct 25 2026, at **0.00%**, and should stay flat for the next 90 days. KuCoin Community Chain mints no KCS and burns no fees, and KuCoin's monthly buyback and burn has been silent since Dec 31 2025. The main risk is the 2022 lock wallet with **47.5M KCS**, counted almost entirely as unlocked yet untouched since Mar 31 2022. With no new coins being created, supply stays at the 142.16M KCS that exist today unless the chain's rules change, and only a restarted burn can move it down toward the 100M target.

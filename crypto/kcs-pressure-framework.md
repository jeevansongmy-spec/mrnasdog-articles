# KCS Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

**KCS** supply is flat: between Aug 15 and Nov 13 2026 no new KCS was made, none was burned and no locked KCS reached the market, so the net change is **0.00%**, with **0.00%** expected for the next 90 days too. KuCoin Token has no block reward, the KuCoin buy-and-burn has been silent since the **20,240 KCS** burn of Dec 31 2025, and the 2022 lock wallet still holds **47.5M KCS**. Other trackers show **+1.82%**, because they counted another **2.5M KCS** as unlocked on Oct 1 2026 even though none of those coins moved.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 13 2026, the KCS ledger books **0 KCS** of sell pressure and **0 KCS** of buy pressure against **139.66M KCS** in circulation, a net change of **0.00%**. Other supply trackers show **+1.82%** for the same window, a gap of **1.82 points**, and the KCS coin page carries a warning note that explains it: on Oct 1 2026 one more quarterly slice of the 2022 KuCoin Token lock plan was counted as unlocked, but the lock wallet did not send a single KCS. The next 90 days look the same, with the last paper slice due around Jan 1 2027 and no burn on the calendar. KCS is a **frozen-supply exchange token**: nothing is printed, nothing is being destroyed, and a large locked pile sits still.

## Sell pressure: where new KCS comes from

**Protocol inflation is 0 KCS.** KuCoin Community Chain, the KCS home chain, pays no block reward. Validators are paid from a reward pool that moves about 0.059 KCS to the staking contract every block, and KuCoin refills that pool each day with roughly 1,200 to 4,000 KCS it already owns. Those coins change hands; none is created.

**Vesting unlocks are 0 KCS.** The KCS whitepaper of March 2022 re-locked 90M founder and angel coins: 20M were burned, 20M went to a holder and contributor reward pool, and 50M were set to be released in 20 quarterly slices of **2.5M KCS** from 2022. On paper, slice 19 was counted on Oct 1 2026 and slice 20 is due around Jan 1 2027. On-chain, only the first slice ever left: the lock wallet sent 2.5M KCS to two wallets on Mar 31 2022 and has not sent anything since. It held **47.5M KCS** on Aug 15 2026 and still holds 47.5M KCS on Nov 13 2026. Coins that stay in the lock are not supply on the market, so the KCS ledger books nothing here.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are 0 KCS.** None of the watched team wallets sent coins in the window. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy is 0 KCS**: no court case or bankrupt firm is holding KCS to pay out.

## Buy pressure: where new KCS goes

**Programmatic buyback is 0 KCS.** For years KuCoin bought KCS with part of its profit and sent it to a dead address on KuCoin Community Chain, once a month. The 66th burn, **20,240 KCS** on Dec 31 2025, is the last one. The dead address gained only 0.018 KCS of dust between Aug 15 and Nov 13 2026, the KCS total supply has stayed at about **142.16M** since that burn, and no burn notice has been published for any month of 2026. KuCoin still describes the burn as a 10%-of-profit program, so it could come back, but there is no date for it.

**Protocol fee burn is 0 KCS.** KuCoin Community Chain does not burn any part of its gas fees; they go to validators, and most blocks today carry no transactions at all. **Foundation buy is 0 KCS**: no foundation buying program exists, and KuCoin's reward-pool top-ups and the KCS holder bonus use coins that already circulate. **New long-term lock is 0 KCS**: about 55.5M KCS is staked with validators, but staked KCS still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest KCS overhang is the **2022 lock wallet with 47.5M KCS**, about a third of the circulating figure. Most of it is already counted as unlocked on paper, so if that wallet ever starts sending, the coins reach the market with little warning. Next is the **holder and contributor reward pool with 19.9M KCS**, which has been quiet since 2022, and two wallets from the first 2022 slice holding **1.6M KCS** and **0.9M KCS**, which have never sent a coin. KuCoin's burn wallet holds about 1.9K KCS, too small to matter. All of these balances were read at both ends of the window and none moved. We re-read them at every rebuild; any coins that leave them will show up as new sell pressure on the KCS page.

## How KCS compares to other exchange tokens

Exchange tokens usually earn their supply story through a burn paid from exchange profit. BNB runs a quarterly auto-burn sized by published rules and also burns part of its gas fees, so its supply shrinks on a schedule. Other exchange coins buy back on a monthly or quarterly rhythm and publish the amount. KCS used to sit in the same group, with a burn every month since 2019, but its burn has now been silent for more than ten months, which leaves KCS as one of the few exchange tokens with no live supply-reducing mechanism at all.

KCS also differs from chain coins that pay validators in new coins. Its validators are paid in KCS that KuCoin already owns, so there is no built-in inflation; the supply can only fall through a burn or rise through the lock wallet. That makes the KCS reading a question about one company's decisions rather than about code: when KuCoin burns, the ledger turns deflationary, and if the lock wallet ever moves, it turns inflationary in a single step.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Around Jan 1 2027**, the 20th and last 2.5M KCS slice of the 2022 lock plan falls due. If it follows the earlier slices, it will be counted as unlocked while the coins stay in the lock wallet, and other trackers will show a jump that the KCS ledger does not.

**Any KuCoin burn notice.** A 67th burn, the first since Dec 31 2025, would put a buy row back on the KCS page, sized by the coins that reach the dead address.

**The lock wallet itself.** Any transfer out of the 47.5M KCS wallet, the first since Mar 31 2022, would be the largest supply event KCS can have.

**KuCoin's business news.** KuCoin stopped serving Russia and Belarus on Oct 29 2026, blocked transfers to and from MEXC on Nov 10 2026, and will pause euro bank deposits from Nov 18 2026. Less profit means less money for any future burn.

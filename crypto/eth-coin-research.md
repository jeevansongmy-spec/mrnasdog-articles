## Ethereum supply questions, answered

### Is Ethereum (ETH) inflationary or deflationary?

Ethereum (ETH) is inflationary right now: its supply changes +0.21% over the next 90 days and +0.86% over the next year (checked Oct 30 2026). More ETH is added than removed.

### What is the ETH inflation rate in 2026?

About +0.86% a year: that is how much Ethereum (ETH) supply changes over the next 12 months, with +0.21% in the next 90 days (checked Oct 30 2026). That puts ETH #66 of 100 coins we track for new supply (#1 = the most new supply).

### When is the next ETH token unlock?

No dated Ethereum (ETH) unlock is scheduled in the next 90 days (checked Oct 30 2026). New ETH still arrives: about 265K ETH in 90 days.

### Does Ethereum burn or buy back ETH?

Yes. In our count, about 6.04K ETH is expected to leave the market over the next 90 days through burns, buybacks or locks (checked Oct 30 2026). How it works: The fee burn is the only removal: 6,045 ETH in 90 days, about 1,600 of it in four hours on the night of the Oct 29 2026 Binance hack. It still cancels only 2.3% of new ETH.

### Where does new ETH supply come from?

How it works: Validator pay is the only new ETH: 265K in 90 days, edging up as the stake grows (44.09M staked, 1.22M more waiting to join). Foundation sales move coins already counted, so they add nothing.

### How much ETH is in circulation?

About 122M ETH is in circulation (checked Oct 30 2026). Over the next 90 days that changes +0.21%, and over a year +0.86%.

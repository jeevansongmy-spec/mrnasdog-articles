## Ethereum supply questions, answered

### Is Ethereum (ETH) inflationary or deflationary?

Ethereum (ETH) is inflationary right now: its supply changes +0.21% over the next 90 days and +0.86% over the next year (checked Nov 16 2026). More ETH is added than removed.

### What is the ETH inflation rate in 2026?

About +0.86% a year: that is how much Ethereum (ETH) supply changes over the next 12 months, with +0.21% in the next 90 days (checked Nov 16 2026). That puts ETH #65 of 100 coins we track for new supply (#1 = the most new supply).

### When is the next ETH token unlock?

No dated Ethereum (ETH) unlock is scheduled in the next 90 days (checked Nov 16 2026). New ETH still arrives: about 267K ETH in 90 days.

### Does Ethereum burn or buy back ETH?

Yes. In our count, about 6.61K ETH is expected to leave the market over the next 90 days through burns, buybacks or locks (checked Nov 16 2026). How it works: The fee burn is the only removal: 6,606 ETH in 90 days, about 1,756 of it in under seven hours on the night of the Oct 29 2026 Binance hack. It cancels only about 2.5% of new ETH.

### Where does new ETH supply come from?

In our count, about 267K new ETH reaches the market over the next 90 days (checked Nov 16 2026). How it works: Validator pay is the only new ETH: about 267K in 90 days, edging up as the stake grows (44.7M staked, about 910K more waiting to join). Foundation sales use coins already counted, so they add nothing.

### How much ETH is in circulation?

About 122M ETH is in circulation (checked Nov 16 2026). Over the next 90 days that changes +0.21%, and over a year +0.86%.

### Does ETH have more new supply than BTC, BNB or XRP?

Over the next 90 days (checked Nov 16 2026): ETH +0.21%, BTC +0.20%, BNB −1.27%, XRP +1.42%. ETH sits in the middle of these 4 coins.

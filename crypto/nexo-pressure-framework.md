## Sell pressure: where new NEXO comes from

Protocol inflation is **0 NEXO**, and this one is fixed for good. NEXO is a simple token on Ethereum, and its code sets the 1B supply only once, at launch. There is no mint function, no staking reward paid in new NEXO and no block reward, because NEXO is not a chain. Nexo's own token paper for European rules says the same: the token has no supply adjustment of any kind.

Vesting unlocks are **0 NEXO**. The NEXO contract itself held the release calendar for the team (112.5M over four years), the advisers (52.5M), the community (60M) and a 250M lending reserve. Every one of those calendars started in April 2018, and the longest, the team's, finished in 2022. No vesting date falls inside the window or the next 90 days.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0 NEXO**, even though the pile behind this row is big. Nexo can still withdraw about **353.85M NEXO** left in the four old allocation slots, and none of it moved between Aug 7 and Nov 5 2026. Because every NEXO already counts as circulating, a release from those slots, or a sale from any Nexo wallet, would move coins inside the float and add no new supply.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0 NEXO**. No court estate, failed fund or bankruptcy pile of NEXO is waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new NEXO goes

The programmatic buyback is **0 NEXO**. Nexo has bought NEXO back on the open market three times, with **$12M** in December 2020, **$100M** in November 2021 and **$50M** in August 2022, and it kept those coins in its buyback reserve rather than burning them. That reserve held **114.80M NEXO** on both Aug 7 and Nov 5 2026, and no new round has been announced. A new round would also book zero here, because the reserve counts as circulating.

The protocol fee burn is **0 NEXO**. NEXO has no burn of any kind. The dead address held 10.71 NEXO at both ends of the window, and total supply never dropped below 1B.

Foundation buying is **0 NEXO**. When Nexo pays savings interest, card cashback or referral rewards in NEXO, it pays them from coins it already holds, so those payouts move coins inside the float and take nothing out of it.

New long-term locks are **0 NEXO**. Clients keep NEXO in their Nexo accounts to reach loyalty tiers and better rates, but those coins still count as circulating, so holding them removes nothing.

None of these balances is new supply, because all 1B NEXO already count as circulating. Every one of these wallets is read again at each check. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How NEXO compares to other company-issued platform tokens

Most tokens tied to a crypto company fall into two groups. Exchange tokens such as BNB, OKB and BGB pair a fixed or capped supply with a regular burn, so their supply shrinks each quarter or each block. NEXO is in the other group: a fixed supply with no burn at all. Its past buybacks took coins off the market for a while, but the coins went into a company reserve and were never destroyed, so the supply count never fell.

Against uncapped chain tokens that pay stakers in new coins, NEXO has the cleanest supply line on the board: nothing is printed, nothing unlocks and nothing burns. The risk is of a different kind. Where a chain token's holders are spread across thousands of stakers, about 84% of NEXO sits with one company, so the real question for NEXO supply is not how many coins exist but how many Nexo chooses to sell, pay out or keep.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, any new buyback round from the Nexo board. The last one was announced on Aug 30 2022, and a new one would show up as a rising buyback reserve.

Second, any withdrawal from the four old allocation slots, which have held **353.85M NEXO** without moving through Nov 5 2026. A large withdrawal would not change the supply count, but it would put more NEXO within easy reach of the market.

Third, outflows from the corporate treasury and the two platform wallets after the Oct 22 2026 listing on a large US exchange, which gave NEXO a new place to trade against US dollars and stablecoins.

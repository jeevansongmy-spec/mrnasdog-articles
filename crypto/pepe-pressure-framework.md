# PEPE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

PEPE supply is roughly steady, and it can only ever go down. Every one of the **420.69T** PEPE was created on launch day in April 2023, the Pepe contract has no way to create more, and nobody holds the keys to change it. From Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 **0** new PEPE appeared, while about **2.54B** PEPE left for good through mistakes and burns, a net change of **−0.0006%**; other trackers show **−0.078%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026, PEPE supply changed by **−0.0006%**, and for the next 90 days we count **0.00%**, because nothing is scheduled to add or remove PEPE. Other trackers show **−0.078%** for the same 90 days, a difference of only **0.08 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. That small gap does not come from any PEPE being created or destroyed. The label that fits PEPE best is simple: a fixed-supply meme coin that slowly leaks coins it can never get back.

## Sell pressure: where new PEPE comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**, and it stays 0 for good. The Pepe token contract on Ethereum made all 420.69T PEPE once, when it was deployed on Apr 14 2023, and it holds no other way to create coins. The owner role was given up, so even the settings the team once had, a blocklist and a limit on early buyers, can no longer be used.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. PEPE never sold coins to investors and never set up a vesting plan. At launch 93.1% of the supply went into a trading pool whose ownership tokens were burned, and the other 6.9% went to a team wallet that could move from the first day.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**. PEPE has no foundation. The one team wallet still holding coins kept about **2.12T** PEPE from Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 without sending any out. Those coins already count as circulating, so even a sale from that wallet would put no new PEPE into the count.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**. No court case, failed company or lock contract is holding PEPE for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new PEPE goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**. PEPE has no income, no treasury plan and no contract that buys coins back. The protocol fee burn is also **0**: PEPE charges no transfer tax, and because the contract can no longer be changed, one can never be added. Foundation buying is **0**, since no team or company has said it will buy PEPE, and new long-term locks are **0**, since PEPE has no staking.

The real buy side of PEPE comes from two kinds of accident and choice. The bigger one is coins stuck in the token contract: people keep sending PEPE to the Pepe token's own address, usually by pasting the wrong address, and the contract has no way to send it back. Between Aug 23 and Nov 21 2026, 47 such transfers trapped **2.54B** PEPE, including **1.41B** PEPE in a single transfer on Aug 24 2026. About **41.63B** PEPE now sits in that address forever.

The smaller one is holder burns. In the same 90 days holders destroyed **5.33M** PEPE, mostly by sending it to dead addresses that nobody controls; one wallet alone burned about **4.95M** PEPE between Oct 18 and Oct 20 2026. Neither the trapped coins nor the burns follow a plan, so the next 90 days count **0** for both, and any new accident will show up at the next check.

## Foundation and overhang

PEPE's only team-controlled overhang is one multi-signature wallet that needs three signatures to move coins. It traces back to the original 6.9% team share. In August 2023 about 16T PEPE left the first team wallet and went to exchanges, which the remaining founder later called theft by former team members. In October 2023 that founder burned **6.9T** PEPE, and in December 2023 most of what was left moved into the current wallet. That wallet holds about **2.12T** PEPE, about 0.5% of the supply. It sent nothing out between Aug 23 and Nov 21 2026; its last payment out was **42B** PEPE on Dec 22 2025. A filing for a planned PEPE fund says the remaining team coins pay for running costs and promotion.

We read this wallet's balance at every check. If it falls between checks, the outflow will be named on the page under foundation unlocks at the next check. Because the wallet's coins already count as circulating, an outflow would be a warning about selling, not new supply.

## How PEPE compares to other meme coins

PEPE sits at the strictest end of the meme coin group. Like Shiba Inu and Bonk, PEPE was created in one go with a huge round supply, but PEPE never had a team unlock, an ecosystem fund or a staking reward that could feed new coins into the market. Dogecoin is the opposite design: Dogecoin miners receive about 5 billion new DOGE every year with no end date, so Dogecoin supply grows by design while PEPE supply cannot grow at all.

On the burn side, PEPE has no built-in burn. Shiba Inu holders run community burns, and some newer meme coins charge a tax on every trade and burn part of it. PEPE has neither, so its supply only falls when holders burn coins by choice or lose them by mistake. Those leaks are tiny: **2.54B** PEPE in 90 days is less than one-thousandth of one percent of the supply.

That makes PEPE a clean case for supply: what you see is all there is. The flip side is that nothing in the coin itself pulls coins off the market, so the price depends almost entirely on how many people want to hold PEPE.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The team wallet balance is the main watch line. It held about **2.12T** PEPE on Nov 21 2026; any outflow would be the first since Dec 22 2025, and we would show it on the page at the next check.

The planned spot PEPE fund is the second. Its filing was updated on Oct 2 2026 and names a US exchange for listing, with no approval date yet. A fund would buy PEPE that already circulates, so it would change demand, not supply.

Large burns are the third. The biggest burn this quarter was about **4.95M** PEPE in October 2026; a burn in the billions would show up as a clear drop in supply at the next check.

Mistaken transfers into the token address are the fourth. They added **2.54B** PEPE in the 90 days to Nov 21 2026, and a single large slip, like the **1.41B** PEPE one on Aug 24 2026, can double the quarter's total in one day.

## Summary

PEPE supply is roughly steady, with a net change of **−0.0006%** from Aug 23 to Nov 21 2026 and **0.00%** counted for the next 90 days. All **420.69T** PEPE were created at launch, the Pepe contract cannot create more, and the owner role was given up, so PEPE has no inflation and never will. The only supply that leaves is lost to mistakes and burns, about **2.54B** PEPE in 90 days, and the only team overhang is one wallet holding about **2.12T** PEPE that did not move in the window. For PEPE the supply story is settled; the risk sits on the demand side.

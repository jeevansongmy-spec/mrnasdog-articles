# PEPE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

PEPE supply was flat in the 90 days from Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026, a net change of about **−0.0006%**, and it should stay flat in the next 90 days: mixed flows, with supply roughly steady. Pepe can never make a new coin, so **0 PEPE** was added, while about **2.53B PEPE** got locked for good in the token's own address by mistake and holders burned another **5.34M PEPE**. The hard cap is the **420.69T PEPE** made on launch day, and other trackers show **−0.01%** for the same window.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 13 to Nov 11 2026 the PEPE supply moved by a net **−0.0006%**, which rounds to **0.00%**, and the next 90 days come to **0.00%** because no coin is due to be made, unlocked or burned on any schedule. Other trackers show **−0.01%** for the same 90 days, a difference of under **0.01 percentage points**, so the two readings agree and the coin page carries no warning note. In one line: Pepe is a fixed-supply meme coin where nothing new ever reaches the market, and the only coins that leave it are lost or burned by holders themselves.

## Sell pressure: where new PEPE comes from

Protocol inflation is **0 PEPE**, and it will stay zero. The Pepe token contract on Ethereum has no mint function, its owner gave up control in 2023, and the code cannot be upgraded, so the 420.69T PEPE created on launch day in April 2023 is the most that will ever exist. Unlike a chain that pays validators or miners, Pepe has nobody to pay, so there is no stream of new coins at all.

Vesting unlocks are **0 PEPE**. Pepe launched with no investors and no locked team share: most of the supply went straight into a trading pool, and the rest went to a team wallet that could move from the first day. There is no unlock calendar for PEPE because nothing was ever locked.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0 PEPE**. The team wallet holds about **2.12T PEPE** and did not move once between Aug 13 and Nov 11 2026. Those coins already count as circulating, so even a sale would only move PEPE from one holder to another, not add new supply. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is also **0 PEPE**: no court case, bankrupt firm or hacker is holding PEPE that sits outside the count.

## Buy pressure: where new PEPE goes

Programmatic buyback is **0 PEPE**. Pepe has no business and no income, so there is no money to buy coins back with. Protocol fee burn is also **0 PEPE**: transfers carry no tax or fee, and since the contract is ownerless and fixed, one can never be switched on. Foundation buying is **0 PEPE**, and a new long-term lock is **0 PEPE** because PEPE has no staking and no lock contract.

The real buy side comes from holders. The largest item is PEPE sent to the token's own address by mistake: the Pepe contract has no way to send coins out of that address, so whatever lands there is locked forever. In the 90 days, 51 such transfers locked about **2.53B PEPE**, including a single send of 1.41B PEPE on Aug 24 2026, and the pile has grown to about 41.58B PEPE. Holder burns added about **5.34M PEPE**: 5.14M sent to the dead address, 194K to another address no one controls, and 2.1K destroyed with the burn function. Together that is worth roughly $12K, against a market value near $2.02B.

Neither flow is planned. Different holders make these mistakes and burns at their own choice, and the size swings a lot from one quarter to the next, so the ledger counts **0 PEPE** of them for the next 90 days. If they keep happening, they will show up at the next check as a small extra fall in supply.

## Foundation and overhang

Pepe has no foundation, no DAO and no treasury program. The one team-controlled pile is the team wallet, a multi-signature safe that holds about **2.12T PEPE**, roughly 0.5% of all PEPE. It is what is left of the 6.9% listing share set aside at launch: in August 2023 former team members sent about 16T PEPE from the old wallet to exchanges, the remaining team burned 6.9T PEPE in October 2023, and the rest moved into this safe in December 2023. Since then the safe has paid out small amounts for costs and campaigns, the last one 42B PEPE on Dec 22 2025, and it did not move at all in this window.

We read the team wallet's balance on-chain at every check. If the Pepe team wallet's balance falls between checks, the outflow enters the foundation and unscheduled unlocks line at the next check, and because those coins already count as circulating, it would show as a sale by the team rather than as new supply.

## How PEPE compares to other meme coins

Among meme coins, Pepe sits at the strict end. Dogecoin is the opposite design: Dogecoin miners are paid 10,000 new DOGE in every block with no cap, about 5B DOGE a year, so Dogecoin supply grows every single day. Pepe has no miners and no validators to pay, which is why PEPE inflation is zero by design rather than by choice.

Shiba Inu is closer to Pepe: a fixed supply, no mint, and a large share of coins sitting in burn addresses that holders and fans keep adding to. Pepe has a burn pile too, but a much smaller one, about 6.92T PEPE at the dead address, most of it from the team's single burn in October 2023. Meme coins with a transfer tax or a fee burn, by contrast, take coins away with every trade; Pepe has no tax and cannot add one.

The real risk for a fixed-supply meme coin is not new coins but who holds the old ones. With nothing to unlock and nothing to mint, PEPE supply pressure comes only from large holders and the team wallet choosing to sell coins that already circulate.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The Canary spot PEPE ETF: Canary Capital amended its filing on Oct 2 2026 and has no decision date yet. A fund would buy PEPE that already circulates, so it would not change the supply count, but it would change who holds the coins.

The team wallet: about 2.12T PEPE, with its last payout on Dec 22 2025. Any move from it will show at the next check.

Large holders: wallets linked to Justin Sun took in about 11.85T PEPE from an exchange on Oct 17 and Oct 26 2026. Those coins were already circulating, so they add nothing to supply, but a sale of that size would add to selling pressure.

The locked pile at the token's own address: about 41.58B PEPE on Nov 11 2026. Every mistaken send makes PEPE a little scarcer, and the next check will count what arrived after Nov 11 2026.

PEPE supply was flat over the 90 days to Nov 11 2026, at about **−0.0006%**, and should stay flat over the next 90 days. Pepe is a fixed-supply meme coin on Ethereum with a hard cap of **420.69T PEPE**, no mint, no vesting, no tax and no buyback. The only coins leaving the market are about **2.53B PEPE** locked by mistake in the token's own address and **5.34M PEPE** burned by holders. The main supply risk is not new coins but the team wallet's 2.12T PEPE and a few very large holders, whose coins already circulate and could be sold at any time.

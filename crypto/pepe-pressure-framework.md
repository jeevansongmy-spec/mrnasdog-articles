# PEPE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

Pepe's supply is flat: from Aug 4 to Nov 2 2026 no new PEPE was made, and holders sent about **2.58B PEPE** where it can never be spent again, a change of **-0.0006%**. All **420.69T** PEPE were made on launch day in April 2023, the contract cannot make more, and there is no vesting, no buyback and no fee burn. Other trackers show **-0.05%** for the same 90 days, close to our figure, so the coin page carries no warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 2 2026, PEPE supply moved by a net **-0.0006%**, which rounds to zero. New PEPE added: **0**. PEPE taken out for good by holders: **about 2.58B**, against a supply of **420.69T**. For the next 90 days we expect **0.00%**, because nothing is scheduled to add coins and the burns are unplanned. Other trackers read **-0.05%**, close to our figure, so no warning is shown. The label is simple: **mixed flows, supply roughly steady**. For PEPE that means the supply side will not push the price either way; whatever moves PEPE comes from the people buying and selling it.

## Sell pressure: where new PEPE comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0.** The PEPE contract created all 420.69T coins once, when it was deployed on Apr 14 2023. The code has no mint function, and the owner key was handed over to nobody, so no person or vote can add a mint later. This is a fixed supply in the strict sense: it can only go down.

**Vesting unlocks: 0.** PEPE never had investors, a presale or a vesting plan. On launch day the deployer put **93.1%** of the coins into a trading pool and sent the other **6.9%** to a team wallet for exchange listings. Nothing is locked, so nothing is waiting to open.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** The last team wallet still holds about **2.12T PEPE**. It sent nothing between Aug 4 and Nov 2 2026, and its last payout was on Dec 22 2025. Those coins already count as circulating, so even a sale would not add new supply; it would only be selling that buyers have to absorb.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court estate or bankrupt firm holds a PEPE pile waiting to be paid out. The hacker who broke into a Binance wallet on Oct 29 2026 sold about **7.77T PEPE** on Oct 30 2026. That was heavy selling, but those coins were already in circulation, so the supply count did not change.

## Buy pressure: where new PEPE goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** There is no company, treasury or fee income behind PEPE, so nothing buys coins back on a schedule.

**Protocol fee burn: 0.** Transfers carry no tax and no fee, and the contract can no longer be changed, so PEPE will never have a built-in burn.

**Foundation buy: 0.** No team or foundation buys PEPE. Big holders do: wallets linked to Justin Sun held about **2.82%** of supply on Oct 27 2026. Their coins came from coins already circulating, so they take nothing out of the count.

**New long-term lock: 0.** PEPE has no staking and no lock-up program, so no coins are set aside for a fixed time.

**Holder burns: about 2.58B.** Some holders send PEPE to places it can never leave. From Aug 4 to Nov 2 2026, 33 wallets sent about **2.56B PEPE** to the token's own address, which has no way to move coins out, and **17.78M** more went to the dead address. The single biggest send was **1.41B PEPE** on Aug 24 2026. These sends are unplanned, so we count none for the next 90 days. Over the last 12 months they added up to about **5.49B PEPE**, roughly one coin in every 77,000.

## Foundation and overhang

PEPE has one team-controlled wallet left. It holds about **2.12T PEPE**, roughly **0.5%** of supply. It is what remains of the **29.03T** team share from launch day: in August 2023 about **16.05T** of it was sent to exchanges by former team members, and on Oct 23 2023 the remaining team burned **6.9T** to the dead address. Since then this wallet has paid out only small amounts, the last on Dec 22 2025. Its coins are already counted as circulating, so a sale would not raise the supply number, but it would be selling the market has to absorb. We read its balance at every rebuild.

The biggest wallets on the PEPE chart belong to exchanges, which hold coins for their users. They are not team coins and are not counted as overhang.

## How PEPE compares to other meme coins

Meme coins split into two kinds of supply. Dogecoin is a mined coin: miners receive **10,000 DOGE** with every block, forever, so its supply grows a little every day and holders always face new coins being sold. PEPE is the opposite. Every coin was made on day one, and the only thing that can change the count is a holder throwing coins away.

Other fixed-supply meme tokens, such as Shiba Inu, depend on holders and community projects sending coins to dead addresses. PEPE works the same way, but without any organized burn program, so its burns are small and random. That makes PEPE's supply about as quiet as a coin's supply can be: no unlock dates, no mining rewards, no team schedule. The trade-off is that PEPE has no built-in buyer either. Demand has to come entirely from people who want to hold it.

**The planned US PEPE fund.** A fund manager updated its filing for a spot PEPE fund on Oct 2 2026, and it is still waiting for approval. If it launches, it would buy coins that already circulate, so the supply count would not change, but new buyers would arrive.

**The team wallet.** The last team wallet holds about **2.12T PEPE** and last paid out on Dec 22 2025. A large move from it would be the clearest new selling to watch.

**The Binance hacker's wallets.** After the Oct 29 2026 break-in, about **7.77T PEPE** was sold on Oct 30 2026. Any further sales from the same wallets would be selling, not new supply.

**Wallets linked to Justin Sun.** They held about **2.82%** of supply on Oct 27 2026. A big holder moving coins to exchanges is worth watching, even though it never changes the supply count.

## Summary

PEPE's supply is fixed at the **420.69T** coins made on launch day in April 2023, and the contract cannot make more. From Aug 4 to Nov 2 2026 holders burned about **2.58B PEPE**, so supply moved by **-0.0006%**, which is flat. The only team wallet left holds about **2.12T PEPE**, already counted as circulating, and it did not move in the window. For PEPE, the supply side is quiet, and price depends on how much people want to hold it.

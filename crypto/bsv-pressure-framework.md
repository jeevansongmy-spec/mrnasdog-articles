# BSV Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

**Bitcoin SV (BSV)** grew its supply by about **0.20%** in the 90 days to Nov 26 2026, and the same pace is expected for the next 90 days. Every new BSV comes from mining: miners found **12,920** blocks at **3.125 BSV** each, which made **40,375 BSV**, while **0 BSV** was burned or bought back. BSV has a fixed cap of **21M**, **20.12M** are already mined, and the outside supply tracker reads **+0.23%** for the same 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 28 to Nov 26 2026 the Bitcoin SV supply grew by **+0.20%**, all of it from the block reward, and the next 90 days to Feb 24 2027 should add the same **+0.20%**. The supply tracker we check against reads **+0.23%** over the same 90 days, a gap of only **0.03** percentage points, so the BSV coin page carries no warning note. Nothing on the buy side takes BSV back out of the market: there is no fee burn, no buyback and no staking lock. BSV is a quiet, mining-only chain where supply grows a little every day and shrinks never.

## Sell pressure: where new BSV comes from

**Protocol inflation** is the only source of new BSV. Each block pays its miner a subsidy of **3.125 BSV** plus the fees inside it. Between Aug 28 and Nov 26 2026 miners found **12,920** blocks, about **143.6** a day against a target of 144, so the BSV block reward created **40,375 BSV**, roughly **449 BSV** a day. Because the reward is paid per block, a slightly slow chain makes slightly fewer coins than the round target of **40,500 BSV**. The next 90 days should look the same: the next BSV halving, which cuts the reward to **1.5625 BSV**, comes at block 1,050,000, around April 2028.

**Vesting unlocks** are zero. Bitcoin SV split from Bitcoin Cash on Nov 15 2018 and copied every existing balance one to one. There was no token sale, no team share and no investor allocation, so no BSV vesting cliff exists and none can fall inside the window.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks** are zero. No team, foundation or fund was ever handed a block of BSV. Every coin in existence was mined, and all **20.12M** mined so far already count as circulating, so there is no reserve outside the market that could be released.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy** coins are zero as new supply, but two large piles are worth naming. A 2020 report put nearly **202,000 BSV** in the Mt. Gox estate, whose payout plan leaves BSV out, so those coins were due to be sold or may already be gone; the estate now has until Oct 31 2027 to finish paying creditors. Separately, Chinese prosecutors are seeking about **72,746 BSV** held in the UK in the Qian Zhimin fraud case; a court in Tianjin accepted the request on Sep 28 2026. Both piles were mined years ago and are already part of the circulating supply, so a sale would move BSV between owners without creating any.

## Buy pressure: where new BSV goes

**Programmatic buyback** is zero. Bitcoin SV has no company, treasury or smart contract that buys BSV back for the network.

**Protocol fee burn** is zero. Every BSV transaction fee goes to the miner who includes it, so the protocol burns nothing. A few people still send coins to well-known addresses that nobody can spend from, but between Aug 28 and Nov 26 2026 that came to less than one hundredth of a BSV, far too little to count.

**Foundation buy** is zero. The BSV Association looks after the node software and has announced no plan to buy BSV, and no other foundation or treasury has either.

**New long-term lock** is zero. BSV has no staking and no lock-up contract. Miners can freeze coins named in a court order through the network's recovery tool, but no such freeze was found in this window, and a frozen BSV would still exist and still count.

## Foundation and overhang

Bitcoin SV has no team-controlled overhang. There is no foundation treasury, no locked team wallet, no buyback wallet and no DAO treasury, because none was ever created. The piles that could reach the market belong to courts and estates: the Mt. Gox estate, last reported with nearly **202,000 BSV** in 2020, and the roughly **72,746 BSV** sought in the Qian Zhimin case. Neither has a public wallet we can read, so both are followed through court notices and news every two weeks. They already count as circulating, so a sale would be a large market sale rather than new supply. If either pile is sold or paid out between our checks, the sale goes into the BSV ledger at the next check.

## How BSV compares to other halving-model proof-of-work chains

Bitcoin SV keeps Bitcoin's money rules almost exactly: a **21M** cap, a reward that halves every 210,000 blocks, and a 10-minute block target. Bitcoin and Bitcoin Cash share the same schedule and the same **3.125** coins per block today, so all three grow at about the same rate; Bitcoin's own supply grew about **0.22%** over the same 90 days. None of the three burns fees, none has a buyback, and none has a team allocation. Against these peers, BSV's supply story is the same; what differs is everything around it.

The main difference is who pays the miners. BSV is built for very large blocks and very cheap transactions, so fees are tiny: the block that closed our window carried about **0.017 BSV** in fees against a **3.125 BSV** subsidy. Miners therefore live almost entirely on newly made coins, and many of them must sell those coins to cover power bills. That makes the **40,375 BSV** per quarter a steady seller in the market, even though it is only about **0.20%** of supply.

Compared with proof-of-stake chains such as Ethereum, which pay validators with uncapped new coins and burn part of every fee, BSV has a hard ceiling and no burn. Its inflation can only fall over time, step by step at each halving, and it can never turn negative.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The block pace.** If miners leave or join, blocks come slower or faster for a while, and the count of new BSV moves with it before the difficulty adjusts; the window closes on Feb 24 2027.

**The Qian Zhimin case.** The Tianjin court posted its notice on Oct 13 2026 with a six-month waiting period, so the hearing on the roughly **72,746 BSV** should not come before about Apr 13 2027; any earlier news on how the coins would be sold matters for the market, not for supply.

**The Mt. Gox estate.** The payout deadline moved to Oct 31 2027 on Oct 16 2026. Any notice that the estate is selling or has sold its BSV would show where that old pile went.

**Court-ordered freezes.** The BSV recovery tool lets miners freeze or move coins named in a court order. A first real use would not change the supply, but it would change who holds the coins.

**Teranode.** The BSV Association has said a public test network for its new node software is coming, after an internal test mined a block of about **505GB** on Nov 4 2026. It changes how many transactions fit in a block, not how many BSV a block pays.

## Summary

Bitcoin SV's supply grew **+0.20%** in the 90 days to Nov 26 2026, and the next 90 days should add the same. All **40,375** new BSV came from the **3.125 BSV** block reward, and nothing was burned, bought back or locked away. The main supply risk is not new coins but old ones: large court and estate piles, such as the Mt. Gox estate's BSV and the coins in the Qian Zhimin case, could be sold into a thin market. The **21M** cap and the next halving, around April 2028, mean BSV inflation can only shrink from here.

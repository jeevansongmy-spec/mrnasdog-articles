# SHIB Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

<!-- main-page -->
Looking for the buy-or-sell answer? It is on the SHIB coin page, with demand and price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/shib](https://mrnasdog.com/research/shib)

SHIB supply is roughly steady and can only go down: the token has **no way to mint** and no locked coins left to release. From Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026 **0 SHIB** was created, while holders burned **710.43M SHIB** and sent another **764.88M** to the token's own address, where it can never move again. That is a net change of about **−0.0003%**, against **+0.08%** on other trackers.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 20 2026, SHIB's circulating supply fell by about **0.0003%**, or **1.48B SHIB** out of **589.24T**. Other trackers show **+0.08%** for the same stretch, a gap of **0.08 percentage points**, which is small enough that the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days should look the same, because nothing new can be minted and the burns come from many holders sending coins away week after week. The label that fits SHIB is a **fixed-supply meme coin with a slow, voluntary burn**.

## Sell pressure: where new SHIB comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**, and that is permanent. The SHIB token on Ethereum holds twelve basic functions, such as transfer, approve and burn, and none of them can create coins. There is no owner who could add one, and the code cannot call out to another contract, so no upgrade can sneak a mint in later.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. SHIB launched in August 2020 with one quadrillion coins: half went into a trading pool and half to Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin, who burned most of his share in 2021. No team, investor or adviser slice was ever set aside, so there is no unlock calendar to follow.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**. The wallet that launched the token still holds **50.50M SHIB** and did not move between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026. There is no published team treasury that pays people in SHIB.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy coins are **0**. A US government wallet from the FTX case holds **54.90B SHIB** and did not move in the window. The hacker behind the Oct 29 2026 Binance breach sold about **499.9B SHIB** around Oct 30 2026, but those coins were already circulating before the theft, so the sale moved them between owners without adding any.

## Buy pressure: where new SHIB goes

A programmatic buyback is **0**. No contract or team fund buys SHIB back on the market.

The protocol fee burn is **0** in this window. Shibarium, the project's own network, was designed to turn part of its fees into SHIB and burn them on Ethereum. That route has been stuck since the 2025 attack on the Shibarium bridge, and in late October 2026 the burn portal still held only 371 of the 1,000 BONE it needs before it fires. None of it reached the burn wallet between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026.

A foundation buy is **0**: no team or foundation buys SHIB. A new long-term lock is also **0**, because staked SHIB and SHIB moved to other chains still count as circulating.

Burns to dead wallets took out **710.43M SHIB**. Holders, exchanges and a few apps sent 706.07M SHIB to two well-known dead wallets, and the token's own burn button destroyed another 4.36M. The biggest single burn was **297.57M SHIB** from one holder on Oct 5 2026. A Robinhood wallet sent coins to the dead wallet 73 times, including 88.6M on Nov 15 2026, and two small launch and fee apps added about 54M between them.

Coins stuck in the token's own address took out another **764.88M SHIB**. People sent SHIB to the token contract itself in 53 transfers, mostly by mistake, and the contract has no way to send anything back out. One Coinbase wallet accounts for **233.80M** of it. These coins are as gone as a burn, and the circulating count already leaves them out.

## Foundation and overhang

SHIB has no foundation treasury holding a large unreleased pile, which is rare for a coin this size. The closest things to an overhang are three wallets. The launch wallet holds **50.50M SHIB** and has not moved. The US government's FTX-case wallet holds **54.90B SHIB**; it is already part of the circulating supply, so a sale would push the price but would not add coins. A third balance of about **780.75M SHIB** sits outside the circulating count with no known owner, and it has not moved either.

We re-read all three balances on the chain at every rebuild. If the launch wallet or the unknown balance starts to shrink, those coins enter the foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check.

## How SHIB compares to other meme coins

Dogecoin is the meme coin SHIB is most often set against, and on supply they work in opposite ways. Dogecoin pays miners a fixed **10,000 DOGE** per block forever, roughly 5B new coins a year, with no cap and no burn, so its supply grows every day. SHIB has no miners and no reward, so its supply can only shrink.

Pepe sits closer to SHIB: a fixed supply, no mint and no team unlocks. The difference is that SHIB holders keep sending coins to dead wallets, so SHIB shrinks a little each week while Pepe stays flat. The catch is scale. With 589.24T SHIB in circulation, even 1.48B leaving in 90 days barely moves the total, and at this pace a one percent cut would take about a thousand years.

Compared with chains that burn part of every fee, SHIB's burn is voluntary. Nothing in the token forces a burn when people trade. The one built-in link, the Shibarium fee burn, is the part that is currently switched off.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, whether the Shibarium burn portal fires again before Feb 18 2027; no restart date has been given, and the portal stood at 371 of 1,000 BONE in late October 2026.

Second, the US government's **54.90B SHIB** wallet, which did not move between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026; any sale would land on the market but not add new SHIB.

Third, large one-off burns like the **297.57M SHIB** sent on Oct 5 2026, which can swing a 90-day total on their own.

Fourth, Shibarium use itself, which fell to only a few hundred transactions a day in November 2026. Without traffic there are no fees to burn even when the bridge reopens.

## Summary

SHIB cannot mint new coins and has no unlocks, so its supply can only fall. From Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026 holders burned **710.43M SHIB** and another **764.88M** got stuck in the token's own address, a net change of about **−0.0003%**. The main supply risk is not new coins but large existing holders, such as a **54.90B SHIB** US government wallet, selling coins that already circulate. The burn is real but tiny next to 589.24T SHIB, and the Shibarium fee burn that was meant to speed it up has been paused since the 2025 bridge attack.

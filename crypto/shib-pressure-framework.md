## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to **Aug 14 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Shiba Inu at **−0.00%** net supply pressure: **zero** new SHIB against **3.61 billion SHIB** destroyed. It projects the same **−0.00%** forward, because there is no mechanism on either side capable of moving a 589-trillion float by a measurable amount in one quarter. Our supply monitor reads **+0.12%** over the same window, so the gap is **0.12 percentage points** — inside the framework's **0.5 percentage point** tolerance and consistent with the ordinary drift of a classified circulating figure on a token this large. The arithmetic that decides this page is worth stating plainly: **3,612,508,668** divided by **589,239,598,794,288** is **0.0006%**. Shiba Inu is a **fixed-supply memecoin with a symbolic burn** — the burn is real, permanent and auditable, and it is also a rounding error.

## Sell pressure: where new SHIB comes from

Nowhere, and unlike most zeros in this framework it is provable in a single read. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0** because the SHIB token contract contains no function that can create a coin. Scanning the deployed bytecode for the standard selectors this session returned nothing for mint, nothing for owner, nothing for transferOwnership or renounceOwnership, and nothing for any minter role; a call to the owner function reverts because no such function exists. The only supply-changing entrypoint is a public burn. Shiba Inu is also not a blockchain and pays no block reward: Shibarium, the Shiba Inu layer-2, uses BONE for gas, and its fee revenue is spent buying and destroying SHIB rather than issuing it.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **0** because no allocation ever existed to unlock. The entire genesis of **1,000,000,000,000,000 SHIB** was placed on day one in 2020 — half into a public Uniswap trading pool, half sent to Vitalik Buterin, who destroyed **410.24 trillion** of it in May 2021 and donated the remainder. There was no team tranche, no investor round and no cliff calendar, which is why no unlock tracker publishes a Shiba Inu schedule at all. Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0** on observed flow, and the next section walks what is watched. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**: no estate or trustee is liquidating SHIB on a schedule. A large custodial SHIB balance changed corporate owner in July 2026, but that is one exchange's customer coins moving house — a custody transfer, not supply reaching the market.

## Buy pressure: where new SHIB goes

Buy #2, the burn, is the only non-zero row on the page and it removed **3,612,508,668 SHIB** over the window. It is measured directly rather than reported: the two graveyard addresses that receive every Shiba Inu burn held **410,433,193,712,247** and **49,216,303,464 SHIB** at the start of the window and **410,436,650,254,003** and **49,370,418,958** at the end, and the contract's own supply figure fell by a further **1,851,418** through direct burn calls. Two things go into that total: Shibarium fee revenue routed through the burn portal, and voluntary burns by exchanges and trading apps. The composition matters, because one trading application sent roughly **3.02 billion** of the quarter's total in early **Aug 2026**; strip that one event out and the ordinary Shiba Inu run rate is nearer **110 million** a month. The twelve-month on-chain read gives **4,673,344,715 SHIB** burned, so this single quarter carried about **77%** of the year — the forward projection is generous to the buy side and still moves the headline by less than a basis point.

The other three buy rows are empty by design. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**: nothing in the Shiba Inu ecosystem commits a share of revenue to buying SHIB back off the open market on a defined schedule, which is exactly the difference between Shiba Inu and an exchange token. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **0** because there is no treasury accumulating SHIB — coins routed into a burn reach a graveyard address the same day, so no balance builds that could later be sold back. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**: staking SHIB in the ecosystem's own pool is an existing feature rather than a new lockup, staked coins remain classified as tradable, and no new lock contract was deployed inside this window.

## Foundation and overhang

Shiba Inu has almost no team-controlled overhang, which is unusual enough to state precisely. Three positions are enumerated and watched. The largest is an unlabelled off-market bucket of **256,718,049,505 SHIB** — the gap between the **589.50 trillion** total supply and the **589.24 trillion** classified as circulating. It carries no published address and no release calendar, and it is unchanged to the token from the previous refresh, so it is capacity without a pattern; even if it emptied entirely in one quarter it would add **0.044%** to the reading. The other two are dust: the founder-donation wallet at **0xb8f2…fa08** holds **50,503,748 SHIB** and the India Covid-Crypto Relief Fund wallet at **0x68a9…dff1** holds **2,250,000 SHIB**, both byte-identical at the two ends of the window. There is no Shiba Inu foundation treasury, no DAO wallet holding SHIB reserves, no buyback accumulation destination and no bankruptcy residual.

Everything larger on the holder chart is deliberately excluded. The biggest balances after the graveyard addresses belong to exchanges holding customer coins, which are depositor property rather than team supply, and to unidentified whales, who cannot be read as a coordinated unit. All three watched positions are re-read on every rebuild. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh rather than being smoothed into a forecast.

## How SHIB compares to other memecoins

Shiba Inu sits in the fixed-supply memecoin class, where the whole supply was created once and the only variable afterwards is how much gets destroyed. Inside that class the honest comparison is not burn-versus-no-burn but burn-versus-float. A memecoin that destroys a few billion tokens a quarter against a few hundred billion in circulation is genuinely deflationary; Shiba Inu destroys a similar absolute number against **589.24 trillion**, so the same mechanism produces a thousandth of the effect. That is the single most misread fact about SHIB: the cumulative **410.5 trillion** burned headline is true, and **410.24 trillion** of it happened in one transfer in May 2021, before Shibarium existed.

Against a fee-burning blockchain the contrast is about what meters the burn. A base-fee burn scales with blockspace demand and can outrun issuance outright, which is what makes a busy settlement layer deflationary. Shibarium's burn is metered by activity on a layer-2 whose transaction counts run in the thousands per day, so the burn scales with a much smaller base and lands on a much larger float. And against a capped proof-of-work chain the difference is direction of travel: a halving-model coin still issues, just more slowly, while Shiba Inu cannot issue at all. Shiba Inu wins that comparison outright on the sell side and then wins nothing on the buy side, which is precisely why it scores as flat rather than as shrinking.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Watch whether the early-**Aug 2026** burn spike repeats or reverts: a single trading application produced roughly **3.02 billion** of this window's **3.61 billion**, and the daily burn had already collapsed to a few million tokens by **Aug 7 2026**. Watch the Shib Alpha Layer beta and the pending Shibarium upgrade, both still undated as of **Aug 14 2026** — a settlement-layer launch is the only thing that could plausibly raise the burn base by an order of magnitude. Watch Shiba Inu governance for any proposal that introduces a revenue-funded buyback, which would be the first non-zero Buy #1 in this coin's history. Watch the two dust wallets named above for any movement. And watch for any attempt to redeploy or wrap SHIB under a contract that does have a mint entrypoint; the current contract's immutability is the load-bearing fact on this page.

## Summary

Shiba Inu is a token that cannot grow and does not meaningfully shrink. Its contract has no mint entrypoint and no owner, its **1 quadrillion** coins were all created in 2020, and **410.5 trillion** of them are already in graveyard addresses, leaving **589.24 trillion SHIB** alive. The only live mechanism is the burn, which removed **3,612,508,668 SHIB** in the 90 days to **Aug 14 2026** — **0.0006%** of supply — giving a framework reading of **−0.00%** now and **−0.00%** projected against a monitor reading of **+0.12%**. The key risk is not dilution, which the contract makes impossible, but expectation: the burn is marketed as a deflation engine and measures as a rounding error, and closing that gap would take a burn roughly a thousand times larger than the current pace. The ceiling is fixed forever; the floor is not moving.

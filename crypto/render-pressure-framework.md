# RENDER Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

RENDER supply is roughly steady and edging up: between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026 the Render Network minted **1.48M RENDER** in its monthly batches and the Foundation's reserve let out **185,736** more, while paid jobs burned **655,677 RENDER**, so net supply grew **+0.19%** in 90 days and should grow about **+0.08%** in the next 90. RENDER runs on burn-and-mint: a fixed, shrinking monthly mint pays the network, and every job burns coins worth its dollar price, under a hard limit of **644.17M** RENDER. Other trackers show **+7.14%** for the same window because their supply count changed method, not because of new RENDER.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

On-chain, RENDER supply rose **+0.19%** from Aug 21 to Nov 19 2026, about **1.06M RENDER** net on a float of **556.41M**. Other trackers show **+7.14%** for the same 90 days, a difference of **6.95** percentage points, so the coin page carries a warning note. That gap is not new coins: the supply count those trackers use stood near 518.8M RENDER until Oct 30 2026, then jumped 12.9M on Oct 31 and 22.0M on Nov 12 2026 as it was recounted twice, the second time to count the coins on Solana and the old RNDR still on Ethereum together. For the next 90 days the reading falls to about **+0.08%**, because the monthly mint shrinks on Dec 1 2026 and the dollar-priced burn buys more coins at today's lower price. Label: a usage-burned token whose fixed mint still runs slightly ahead.

## Sell pressure: where new RENDER comes from

**Protocol inflation: 1.48M RENDER.** Every 30 days the Render emission program on Solana mints one batch of new RENDER for node operator rewards, artist and AI grants and the Foundation's running costs. Three batches of **492,132 RENDER** landed on Aug 23, Sep 23 and Oct 26 2026, the Year 3 rate that adds up to 5.9M a year. The schedule written into the program drops each batch to **380,284 RENDER** from Dec 1 2026, and the batch for each 30-day period is set by the rate in force when the period ends. The period ending Nov 22 2026 still pays 492,132; the periods ending Dec 22 2026 and Jan 21 2027 pay 380,284 each, so the next 90 days bring about **1.25M RENDER**.

**Vesting unlocks: 0.** RENDER has no team or investor unlock calendar. Every new coin comes from the monthly batch.

**Foundation and unscheduled releases: 185,736 RENDER.** Until October, most of each batch went into a Foundation emission reserve that is not counted as circulating. That reserve took in 864,264 RENDER in the window and paid out 1.05M (250,000 on Aug 27 and 800,000 on Oct 12 2026), so 185,736 RENDER net joined the market. Since Oct 26 2026 each new batch goes straight to a spending wallet instead, and the reserve still holds **2.20M RENDER**. Its payouts come every one to three months with no published dates, so none is booked for the next 90 days.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court case, bankrupt firm or long lock holds RENDER that could be paid out at once. Old RNDR on Ethereum turns into RENDER one for one from a pool, so the upgrade itself adds nothing.

**Polygon upgrade refills: 55,000 RENDER.** Holders of the old Polygon version of RNDR swap it for RENDER from a small pool, which the Foundation refilled five times between Sep 1 and Oct 22 2026. The RNDR behind those Polygon coins is still counted on Ethereum, so each refill raises the count. Refills are irregular, so none is booked ahead.

## Buy pressure: where new RENDER goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** No contract buys RENDER to hold it. When a job is paid in dollars, those dollars buy RENDER on the market and the coins are burned on the spot, so that buying is counted once, in the burn.

**Protocol fee burn: 655,677 RENDER.** Every render or compute job is priced in dollars, and that value of RENDER is destroyed. From Aug 21 to Nov 19 2026 the two burn wallets destroyed **651,895 RENDER**, worth about **$1.10M**. The pace jumped in the autumn: about 31,000 RENDER in the last days of August, 141,000 in September and **363,662** in October, with **81,940 RENDER** burned on Oct 26 2026 alone. A new channel opened on Nov 6 2026: once a day it burns job payments and mints about 95% of them back to a payout account, a net burn of 3,782 RENDER so far. Because jobs are priced in dollars, the same dollar flow at today's price of $1.38 burns about **802,000 RENDER** in the next 90 days.

**Foundation buy: 0.** The Render Network Foundation has no buyback program. Funds and traders who buy RENDER take coins that are already circulating, which removes nothing from the count.

**New long-term lock: 0.** RENDER has no staking lock, and coins waiting in the burn wallet still count as circulating until they are burned.

## Foundation and overhang

The emission reserve is the one Foundation balance kept outside the circulating count: **2.20M RENDER**, unchanged since Oct 12 2026. If its balance falls, the outflow is booked as a Foundation release at the next check. The upgrade pool holds **3.86M RENDER**, but it only pays out against old RNDR that is locked on Ethereum at the same moment, so it adds nothing.

Several Foundation wallets are already counted as circulating, so their sales would add no new coins but could still reach the market. The largest is the OTOY vault with **81.90M RENDER**, about 15% of the float, which has not moved since Jul 16 2026. The developer vault holds 802,124 RENDER, the operations wallet 767,776, and the multisig that funds the dollar burn holds 398,120 RENDER and 378,665 USDC. The burn wallet itself holds 524,687 RENDER and 141,596 USDC waiting to be burned.

## How RENDER compares to other decentralized compute tokens

RENDER follows the burn-and-mint model that Helium made known: users burn the token to pay for work, and a separate schedule mints new tokens to reward the people who supply the hardware. The difference from a plain fee token is that the burn grows with real usage in dollars, while the mint is fixed. When usage rises or the price falls, the burn takes out more coins; when usage fades, the fixed mint wins.

Compared with compute tokens that pay providers only from fresh emissions, with no burn tied to sales, RENDER gives back a large share of what it mints: about 38% over these 90 days, and more than 60% on the next 90-day reading. Compared with networks that still have large team and investor unlocks ahead, RENDER has none; its new coins come only from a mint that steps down each year until it stops on Dec 1 2028, under a hard limit of 644.17M.

The main structural difference is the large OTOY vault already inside the circulating count. Unlike a vesting schedule, it has no release dates, so the risk it carries is sale pressure, not new supply.

## Summary

RENDER supply grew **+0.19%** between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026 and is set to grow about **+0.08%** in the next 90 days, a mixed and roughly steady reading. The Render Network mints a fixed, shrinking batch of RENDER every month while every paid job burns RENDER worth its dollar price, and in these 90 days the burn took back more than a third of what was minted.

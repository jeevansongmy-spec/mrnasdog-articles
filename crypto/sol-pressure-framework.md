# SOL Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Originally published at [mrnasdog.com/research/sol/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/sol/inflation).*

<!-- main-page -->
This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about Solana, see [mrnasdog.com/research/sol](https://mrnasdog.com/research/sol).

The free supply of SOL grew about **+1.49%** in the 90 days from Aug 10 to Nov 8 2026, and it should grow about **+1.14%** in the next 90 days: supply growing, and projected to keep growing. Solana pays stakers in brand-new SOL at **3.565%** a year with no cap on the total, and old stake accounts with lock dates keep opening every month. In the window **9.01M SOL** reached the free supply, while burns and new locks took back only **181K SOL**; other trackers show **+1.56%** for the same days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

SOL is inflationary, and the selling side is far bigger than the buying side. Net, about **8.83M SOL** joined the **591.09M SOL** that can trade today, a rise of **+1.49%** in 90 days. Other trackers show **+1.56%** over the same stretch, only **0.07** points away, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days the dated lock endings are smaller, which brings the expected rise down to **+1.14%**. Our label: supply growing, projected to keep growing.

## Sell pressure: where new SOL comes from

Protocol inflation is the largest source. Solana mints new SOL for stakers at the start of every epoch, which now comes about once a day, and between Aug 10 and Nov 8 2026 the network paid out **5,446,909 SOL**. Of that, **4,958,086 SOL** landed in free wallets; the rest went to locked stake accounts and only counts when their locks end. The yearly rate started at 8%, falls by 15% each year and stops falling at 1.5%; today it is **3.565%**. Faster blocks this autumn did not raise the pay, because the network adjusted the reward per block each time the block time dropped. For the next 90 days we expect about **4.67M SOL** to reach free wallets.

Vesting unlocks are the second source. Old stake accounts with a written lock date join the free supply on the day the lock ends, and in the window that added about **3.36M SOL**. The pattern is regular: about **0.64M SOL** opens on the 7th of every month until January 2028, and about **204K SOL** opens on the 11th of every month until September 2027. The lock dates already on-chain release **2,270,145 SOL** between Nov 8 2026 and Feb 6 2027, with the biggest days on Dec 7 2026 and Jan 7 2027.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **696,990 SOL**. The largest piece was a **587,871 SOL** stake account that a reserve wallet handed to a new owner on Aug 26 2026; smaller releases followed on Sep 25, Oct 4 and Nov 3 2026. These moves follow no schedule, so the next 90 days count none of them. Long-term locked or bankruptcy holdings add nothing: no court case or failed firm is holding SOL outside the free supply, and coins bought in old estate sales sit inside the dated locks above.

## Buy pressure: where new SOL goes

Solana has no programmatic buyback. A new trading app from Solana Labs, launched on Nov 5 2026, says it will use future fees to buy SOL and burn it, but it charges no fees yet, so it removes nothing today.

The protocol fee burn is the main buyer. Half of every base fee is destroyed, and from Aug 10 to Nov 8 2026 fees burned about **77,715 SOL**. Before the Alpenglow upgrade on Oct 21 2026, about half of that came from validators paying to vote. Votes no longer pay fees; instead each validator burns **0.8 SOL** every epoch to keep its seat, which took **9,601 SOL** in the 18 epochs since. Together the burn removed **87,316 SOL**, under 2% of what stakers were paid, and about **82.3K SOL** is expected next.

There is no foundation buy: no foundation buys SOL back, and spot funds or companies that hold SOL buy coins that are already free. New long-term locks did take some coins away. Between Aug 17 and Nov 7 2026, **97** new stake accounts with lock dates years away pulled **93,877 SOL** out of free wallets, and new lots appear most weeks.

## Foundation and overhang

The main reserve key still controls about **27.32M SOL**. It is part of **29.48M SOL** sitting in old stake accounts whose lock dates have passed but which stay on the network's locked list, so none of it counts as free today. When the reserve hands an account to a new owner, as it did on Aug 26 2026, those coins join the free supply at once. On Oct 8 2026 the reserve also moved **500,000 SOL** into a new account locked until Sep 14 2028, so that grant stays locked.

Another **16.67M SOL** sits in stake accounts that are locked only by a date. The biggest group, about **8.98M SOL** under one lock-keeper, opens month by month until January 2028; the rest open on their own dates, mostly in 2027. In all, **46.16M SOL** is outside the free supply today, about **7.2%** of all **637.25M SOL**.

## How SOL compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Solana pays stakers on a falling schedule: it began at 8% a year, drops by 15% each year and settles at 1.5%. Most large proof-of-stake chains also pay stakers in new coins, but they differ in what they burn. Ethereum destroys the whole base fee of every transaction, so busy days can cancel much of its new pay; Solana burns only half of its base fee and none of the priority fees users add to jump the line, so its burn stays far below its pay.

The other difference is the lock schedule. Chains that launched more recently often still have team and investor unlocks ahead; Solana's early sale coins are long free, but stake accounts with written lock dates, which appear to include the locked SOL a failed exchange's estate sold in 2024, still open every month into 2028. That makes Solana's supply growth a mix of steady staking pay and a slow, known stream of lock endings, rather than one big cliff.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 9 2026:** the network resumes switching on new features from its latest software release. Validators approved a faster fall in staking pay on Aug 28 2026, from 15% a year to 30% a year, but no switch-on date has been named. If it starts, the pay falls gradually, with no sudden drop.

**Dec 7 2026 and Jan 7 2027:** the two largest monthly lock endings in the window, **642,582 SOL** and **639,575 SOL**. Smaller lots open on Nov 11, Nov 28, Dec 11, Dec 20 2026 and Jan 11 2027.

**Reserve hand-offs, any date:** the reserve released coins on Aug 26, Sep 25, Oct 4 and Nov 3 2026 with no notice. Another hand-off of a large stake account would add straight to the free supply.

**The new burns:** the validator seat ticket now burns about 534 SOL each epoch, and the Solana Labs trading app plans to burn SOL once it starts charging fees. Neither is large next to about 57,000 SOL of new pay each day.

## Summary

SOL's free supply rose **+1.49%** from Aug 10 to Nov 8 2026 and is expected to rise about **+1.14%** in the next 90 days. Staking pay of **3.565%** a year is the largest source, and monthly lock endings plus irregular reserve releases add a steady second stream. Solana burns half of each base fee and a small validator seat ticket, which together removed only **87,316 SOL** in the window. Other trackers show nearly the same result, so the coin page carries no warning note.

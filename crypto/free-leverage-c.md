Two ways to get leverage on Bitcoin. Only one can wipe us out.

## The old way: 3x on Bitcoin

We buy Bitcoin at **$16,000** with **3x**.

A line sits under us, near **$10,700**. **Below it, we are liquidated** — the
money is gone.

Bitcoin dips, touches it, and we are out. Then Bitcoin goes to **$120,000**.

![Bitcoin dips below the liquidation line soon after we buy with 3x, we are liquidated, and Bitcoin then climbs to 120,000 dollars without us](charts/liquidation.svg)

*Right about Bitcoin. Out before it paid.*

Two more costs while we hold: interest on the loan every day, and a fee to the
shorts every few hours, because almost everyone is long.

## The new way: buy Strategy

**Strategy** (MSTR) hoards Bitcoin, so its price follows Bitcoin — and moves
more in both directions.

It is not real leverage. But its history behaves like it.

When Bitcoin was **$16,000**, Strategy was **$16**. When Bitcoin reached
**$120,000**, Strategy was **$394**.

Bitcoin up about **7 times**. Strategy up about **25 times**.

![From November 2022 to August 2025 Bitcoin rose about 7 times while Strategy stock rose about 25 times](charts/proof.svg)

*Same first day, same last day. About 3 times Bitcoin.*

No borrow fee. No fee to the shorts. And **no liquidation line at all** — it is
a stock. Bitcoin could fall to $4,000 and the shares are still ours.

## Side by side

| | 3x on Bitcoin | Buying Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Fee to borrow | Every day | **None** |
| Fee to the shorts | Every few hours | **None** |
| Can we be liquidated? | Yes — money gone | **Never** |
| Moves more than Bitcoin? | Exactly 3x | About 3x, from its history |
| Paid to hold? | No | Possible — preferred shares pay 8–10% |

**Ethereum** has **Bitmine** (BMNR). **Solana** has **Forward Industries**
(FWDI). Most large coins have one now.

So when we are confident about a coin, this kind of stock can replace leverage
on the coin.

Not risk-free — the stock swings harder, and sometimes the coin rises while the
stock does not. But next to borrowing against our coins or using DeFi leverage,
the risk is far smaller, for one reason:

**Nobody can close our position. Only we can.**

Let's keep researching, and let's keep improving.

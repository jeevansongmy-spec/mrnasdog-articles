# FLOKI Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

<!-- main-page -->
The Floki coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/floki](https://mrnasdog.com/research/floki). Below: supply, line by line.

FLOKI supply is roughly steady and edging down: no new FLOKI can be made, and burns removed **3.90B FLOKI** between Aug 10 and Nov 8 2026, a net change of **-0.04%** over 90 days. Almost all of that burn is the penalty paid by holders who left the Floki staking program early; a small fee-funded buyback adds **38.05M FLOKI**. Sell pressure from new coins is **0**, and other trackers read about **-0.02%**, so the two readings agree.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 10 to Nov 8 2026, FLOKI supply fell by **0.04%**, from burns of **3,903,602,313 FLOKI** against zero new coins. Other trackers show **-0.02%** for the same stretch, a difference of about **0.03 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days look the same, about **-0.04%**, if early staking exits keep their pace. The cite-able label for FLOKI today is a fixed-supply meme coin with a slow, penalty-driven burn: it cannot inflate, and it shrinks only when stakers pay to leave early.

## Sell pressure: where new FLOKI comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. The FLOKI token code fixes **10T** coins on Ethereum and another **10T** on BNB Chain, the two copies are linked one for one through exchanges, there is no mint function in the code on either chain, and the owner key now sits at the burn address. Nobody is paid in new FLOKI for staking or for running anything.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. FLOKI launched in July 2021 without a team or investor allocation on a release schedule, so there is no locked batch waiting for a date.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**, even though the team was a seller. The Floki treasury on Ethereum sent **44.26B FLOKI** to a market maker in three batches, on Aug 27, Sep 28 and Oct 30 2026, and a second team safe moved **50B FLOKI** over to the BNB Chain treasury. Every one of those coins already counted as circulating, so they add nothing new to the supply, even if they end up on the market.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**. No court case or failed firm holds FLOKI, and the roughly **1.69T FLOKI** in the staking program already counts as circulating, so a stake running out releases nothing new.

## Buy pressure: where new FLOKI goes

The programmatic buyback removed **38.05M FLOKI**. A share of the fees from Floki's own products buys FLOKI on Uniswap and PancakeSwap and sends it straight to the burn address. It fired on four days between Aug 21 and Sep 27 2026 and spent about **$960**; the same dollars at today's lower price would burn about **45.21M FLOKI** over the next 90 days. One of those fee sources, the Floki trading bot, has been offline since Nov 5 2026.

The protocol fee burn is **0**. FLOKI charges a **0.3%** tax on every buy and sell, but that tax funds the treasury and is not burned.

Foundation buying is **0**: the team bought no FLOKI back in the window, and its treasury sold instead. New long-term locks are **0** as well, because fresh stakes of 3 to 48 months still count as circulating.

The staking exit burn is the big one, at **3.87B FLOKI**. Anyone who leaves the Floki staking program before the chosen term ends pays a penalty of up to **20%**, and that penalty goes to the burn address. Early exits burned **1.75B FLOKI** on Ethereum and **2.12B FLOKI** on BNB Chain, and one holder leaving on Sep 20 2026 burned **797M FLOKI** alone. A few holders also sent about **15.45K FLOKI** to the burn address by hand.

## Foundation and overhang

The Floki team holds about **207.43B FLOKI**, a little over 2% of supply, in wallets anyone can watch. The largest is a second Ethereum safe with **114.65B FLOKI**. The BNB Chain treasury holds **74.69B FLOKI**, the wallets set aside for the Floki exchange-traded product hold **16.31B FLOKI**, the Ethereum treasury is down to **1.92B FLOKI** after its sales, and the FlokiFi wallet holds **78M FLOKI**.

None of these wallets has a public release plan, and all of them already count as circulating, so their balances never enter the supply count as new coins. What they show is selling capacity: the Ethereum treasury has paid a market maker roughly once a month since August, and we read every one of these balances again at each rebuild.

## How FLOKI compares to other meme coins

Among meme coins, FLOKI sits in the fixed-supply group. Dogecoin is the opposite case: miners receive brand-new DOGE in every block with no cap, so its supply rises a little every year whatever holders do. FLOKI has no miners and no validators to pay, so its count can only stay flat or fall.

Shiba Inu and Pepe also have a fixed supply. Shiba Inu leans on burns that holders and its own network send to a dead address, while Pepe has no built-in burn at all. FLOKI is unusual in that its main burn is written into its staking contract: it bites only when a staker breaks a lock, so the pace depends on how many people leave early, not on trading volume.

FLOKI also keeps a trading tax that pays the team, which most large meme coins do not have. That tax does not create coins, but it funds a treasury that can sell into the market, which is why the overhang section matters more for FLOKI than the supply count does.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the Floki trading bot, offline since Nov 5 2026 while the team looks into an exploit. Its fees fund part of the buyback, so a long outage shrinks an already small burn.

Second, the treasury's next payment to a market maker. Payments landed on Aug 27, Sep 28 and Oct 30 2026; another in late November or December 2026 would not change the supply count, but it is FLOKI that can be sold.

Third, early exits from staking. One large exit on Sep 20 2026 burned **797M FLOKI** in a single day, so the next 90 days could land above or below the **3.87B FLOKI** of the last 90.

Fourth, the Floki DAO. Holders have voted on burns before, but there has been no vote since March 2026; a new burn vote before Feb 6 2027 would be the one thing that could move the reading sharply.

## Summary

FLOKI cannot inflate: the token code fixes its supply and holds no mint function, and supply now stands at **9.64T FLOKI**. Between Aug 10 and Nov 8 2026 burns removed **3.90B FLOKI**, mostly penalties paid by stakers who left early, for a net change of **-0.04%**, and the next 90 days look about the same. The main risk is not new coins but team selling, with about **207.43B FLOKI** in team wallets and a monthly payment pattern to a market maker. The verdict is mixed flows with supply roughly steady, edging down slowly.

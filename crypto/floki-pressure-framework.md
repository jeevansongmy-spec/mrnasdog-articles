# FLOKI Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

Mixed flows · supply roughly steady: FLOKI recorded **0 new circulating tokens** against **4.54B FLOKI burned**, leaving **−0.05% net supply change** over the 90 days ending Sep 19 2026. A similar **−0.05%** is projected through Dec 18 2026, while the independent supply monitor reads **−0.08%**. Most destruction comes from early-staking-exit penalties; neither treasury payments nor unstaking creates additional FLOKI.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The measured **−0.05%** supply change and the monitor’s **−0.08%** reading differ by approximately **0.03 percentage points**, below the warning threshold, so the overview carries no monitor-gap flag. About **9.64T FLOKI** remains circulating across Ethereum and BNB Chain. Burning a few billion sounds substantial until it is compared with that circulating base: the reduction is small enough for the headline verdict to remain roughly steady. The next-quarter estimate repeats the observed ongoing burn activity and does not assume a discretionary treasury burn or a successful new product launch. FLOKI is a fixed-issuance token with modest ongoing destruction.

## Sell pressure: where new FLOKI comes from

Protocol inflation contributes **0 FLOKI** because neither live token contract contains an ongoing mint path. Each chain began with **10T** units, and staking distributes the separate TOKEN asset rather than newly created FLOKI. Vesting unlocks contribute **0** because no verified FLOKI release calendar supplies an upcoming cliff; TOKEN’s own distribution schedule cannot be copied into FLOKI’s ledger.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks also contribute **0 new circulating FLOKI**. The treasury can spend its holdings, but those holdings are already included in circulation. Counting a treasury payment as issuance would count the same tokens twice. This does not make treasury spending harmless: a recipient can sell existing coins, just as any other holder can. It means that a supply-change measure should not call that sale new supply.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy releases contribute **0** for the same reason where staking is concerned. The staking contracts hold approximately **1,699.08B FLOKI**, already inside the circulating count. Returning those tokens to users changes who can transact with them, not how many circulating tokens exist. No separately evidenced estate distribution adds a supply release to this window.

## Buy pressure: where new FLOKI goes

Programmatic buybacks removed **9.91M FLOKI** during the measured quarter. FlokiFi’s eligible fees fund purchases that send FLOKI to the burn address, with **25%** allocated to buying and burning and the remainder going to its treasury. Four verified fee-processing transactions account for the measured amount. The next-quarter estimate is also **9.91M**, conditional on comparable activity; a fee percentage does not guarantee a fixed number of tokens when usage and execution prices change.

Protocol fee burn is **0** in the canonical second row: the **0.3%** FLOKI trading tax funds treasury operations, while ordinary network gas is paid in ETH or BNB. Early-staking-exit burns are shown separately and account for **4.534B FLOKI**. Leaving before the selected term ends destroys **5% to 20%** of the withdrawn stake, depending on that term. Repeating the observed early-exit pace supplies the ongoing estimate, rather than assuming that every staker will leave early.

Foundation buys contribute **0**, with no verified discretionary purchase-and-removal commitment. New long-term locks contribute **0** because staking and the ETP allocation remain within circulation. Other holder burns removed **12.89K FLOKI** historically, but no recurring commitment supports projecting them. Together, the measured removals total **4.54B**, and the continuing mechanisms project approximately **4.54B** next quarter.

## Foundation and overhang

The published treasuries hold **36.41B FLOKI on Ethereum** and **74.69B on BNB Chain**. A second Ethereum Safe, verified to share the treasury’s signer set, holds **114.65B**. FlokiFi’s BNB Chain treasury holds **78.46M**, while its Ethereum counterpart holds **0 FLOKI**. The community-approved ETP allocation holds **16.31B** across both chains, and the two trading-tax handlers together hold approximately **0.437M**. The checked fee-purchase routes send their acquired FLOKI directly to the burn destination.

These balances belong on a daily on-chain watch list, with product and governance commitments reviewed every two weeks. They are already circulating, so a balance decrease alone does not enter Sell #3 as additional supply. A confirmed change that moves previously excluded tokens into circulation would enter that row at the next refresh; a verified burn from a counted balance would enter the appropriate buy row. The ETP allocation is especially worth watching because its eventual division between liquidity support and destruction remains conditional on execution.

## How FLOKI compares to other fixed-supply tokens

FLOKI and JASMY both lack an ongoing mint path, but their current supply outcomes differ. JASMY’s measured reserve and burn balances are unchanged, leaving its supply ledger flat. FLOKI has actual destruction, chiefly from staking penalties, alongside a much smaller fee-funded buy-and-burn. A cap describes the ceiling; these separate mechanisms determine whether the amount circulating actually declines.

Ethereum provides a different comparison. It issues ETH to validators and burns transaction base fees, so its net outcome depends on two active forces. FLOKI does not issue its own consensus reward, and Ethereum gas charged for a FLOKI transfer does not burn FLOKI. The token’s own destruction must be traced separately.

Revenue purchases and exit penalties also carry different economic meanings. The first depends on people paying to use a service. The second depends on people leaving a lock early. Both remove tokens when completed, but neither proves sustained demand for holding FLOKI, and neither alone predicts its market price.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Through **Dec 18 2026**, watch whether FlokiFi continues routing a quarter of eligible fees into completed purchases and burns.

Watch the two staking contracts for a change in early-exit volume or penalty terms, which would alter the largest removal estimate.

Watch for the replacement trading product’s confirmed fee split and launch terms: the **Sep 7 2026** community update left that percentage unsettled, so the retired bot’s old split is not projected.

Watch the ETP allocation for an executed liquidity deployment or burn; no fixed launch date supplies a scheduled supply event in this forecast.

## Summary

FLOKI’s verdict is **mixed flows · supply roughly steady**, with **−0.05%** measured and approximately **−0.05%** projected over 90 days. No new tokens are issued; **4.54B** was destroyed, overwhelmingly through early-staking-exit penalties. The main uncertainties are future exit activity, product-fee revenue and discretionary treasury decisions. Its original **20T** across the two contracts cannot expand through an ongoing mint function, but a fixed ceiling does not prevent existing holders from selling.

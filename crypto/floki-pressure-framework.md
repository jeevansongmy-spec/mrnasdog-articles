# FLOKI Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

<!-- main-page -->
The Floki coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/floki](https://mrnasdog.com/research/floki). Below: supply, line by line.

**FLOKI** does not inflate: no new FLOKI was made between Aug 20 and Nov 18 2026, and none can be, because both FLOKI contracts were created with a fixed 10 trillion coins and their owner keys were handed to the burn address. In those 90 days Floki burned **3.38B FLOKI**, almost all of it fees paid by holders who left the staking program early, so the supply shrank by **0.04%**, and other trackers show **-0.07%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 20 to Nov 18 2026 the FLOKI supply fell by **0.04%**, from burns alone, and the next 90 days should look about the same, at **-0.04%**. Other trackers show **-0.07%** for the same stretch, a difference of **0.03 points**, small enough that the FLOKI coin page carries no warning note. The burn is real but slow: at this pace it would take decades to remove even a tenth of the **9.64T** FLOKI in circulation. The label that fits FLOKI is a fixed-supply meme coin with a slow burn.

## Sell pressure: where new FLOKI comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. FLOKI exists twice, once on Ethereum and once on BNB Chain, and each FLOKI contract was created with exactly 10 trillion coins. Neither contract has a function that adds coins, and the owner key of both now sits with the burn address, so no one can change the FLOKI code to add one. The 10 trillion figure on each chain read the same at both ends of the window.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. FLOKI launched in July 2021 as a community meme coin, with no investor round and no team vesting schedule, so there is no locked batch of FLOKI waiting to be released.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**, even though the Floki treasury was busy. The Floki treasury on Ethereum sent **44.26B FLOKI** to a market maker in three batches, on Aug 27, Sep 28 and Oct 30 2026. Those FLOKI coins were already counted as circulating before they moved, so the sales put more FLOKI on the market but did not create any new FLOKI.

Long-term locks and bankruptcy payouts are **0**. No court or bankrupt firm holds FLOKI for a later payout, and FLOKI coins in the staking program are already counted, so a stake that ends adds nothing new.

## Buy pressure: where new FLOKI goes

Every FLOKI that left the supply in the window went to the burn address, and three kinds of burn add up to the whole **3.38B FLOKI**. The largest by far is the staking exit burn: holders who leave the Floki staking program before their lock ends pay a fee of 5% to 20% in FLOKI, and that fee is burned. Early exits burned **3.34B FLOKI** across both chains from Aug 20 to Nov 18 2026, with a burn landing every few days.

The programmatic buyback burned **36.83M FLOKI**, worth about $930. Part of the fees from Floki's own products buys FLOKI on the market and sends it straight to the burn address, in batches; this window saw batches on Aug 31, Sep 21 and Sep 27 2026. The Floki Trading Bot, one of those fee sources, has been offline since an exploit on Nov 5 2026, so this small buyback may shrink further.

Holder burns took out another **1.82M FLOKI**, from people who sent FLOKI to the burn address themselves or routed a swap there.

Three buy rows are **0**. The 0.3% fee on every FLOKI buy and sell goes to the Floki treasury rather than to a burn. The Floki treasury sold FLOKI in this window and has no plan to buy any back. And the roughly **1.69T FLOKI** sitting in the staking program still counts as circulating, so staking itself takes nothing out of the supply.

## Foundation and overhang

Floki team wallets held about **207.65B FLOKI** at the end of the window, roughly 2% of the supply, all of it already counted as circulating. The largest is a second team Safe on Ethereum with **114.65B FLOKI**. The Floki treasury on BNB Chain holds **74.69B FLOKI**, and the Floki treasury on Ethereum is down to **1.92B FLOKI** after the three market-maker batches emptied most of it. The wallet that backs the FLOKI exchange-traded product holds **16.31B FLOKI**, and a FlokiFi wallet holds **78.46M FLOKI**.

We re-read each of these FLOKI wallets every time the page is rebuilt. If any of them falls between checks, the outflow is recorded on the coin page at the next check. Because these FLOKI coins are already circulating, a sale from them adds selling on the market but never adds new supply.

## How FLOKI compares to other fixed-supply meme coins

Against Dogecoin, FLOKI sits on the opposite side of the supply question. Dogecoin pays miners 10,000 new DOGE in every block with no end date, so the DOGE supply rises by several billion coins a year by design. FLOKI has no miners or validators to pay, and its supply can only go down.

Against other fixed-supply meme coins, the difference is the burn. Many meme tokens on Ethereum were minted once, handed their owner keys away, and have no built-in way to take coins out, so their supply stays flat forever. Shiba Inu is closer to FLOKI: its supply is fixed and part of it is burned, mostly when holders choose to send coins to a burn address. FLOKI's burn is tied to its own products instead, above all the penalty on early staking exits, which means the burn rises when stakers lose patience and fades when they stay.

What FLOKI shares with most meme coins is that the real selling pressure comes from coins that already exist. For FLOKI that is the treasury: the 44.26B FLOKI it moved to a market maker in 90 days was more than ten times the 3.38B FLOKI burned.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the Floki treasury batches: the Ethereum treasury sent FLOKI to a market maker in late August, late September and late October 2026, so a late-November or late-December 2026 transfer from the BNB Chain treasury or the second team Safe would show the selling continues.

Second, the Floki Trading Bot. It went offline after an exploit on Nov 5 2026, and Floki's monthly update on Nov 2 2026 said a new version could go live sooner than expected; its return would restart one source of the FLOKI buyback.

Third, the staking program. Floki said on Nov 2 2026 that the current program ends in 2027. Holders who leave early before then keep feeding the burn, and the size of those exits decides most of FLOKI's burn in the next 90 days.

Fourth, any new vote of the Floki community. None has been posted since March 2026, and a vote to burn part of the treasury, as Floki did in 2024, would be the one event that could move FLOKI's supply by a large step.

## Summary

FLOKI has a fixed supply of 10 trillion coins on each of its two chains, and no new FLOKI can ever be made. From Aug 20 to Nov 18 2026 the FLOKI supply shrank by **0.04%** as Floki burned **3.38B FLOKI**, mostly fees from holders who left staking early. The bigger force on the market was the Floki treasury, which sent **44.26B FLOKI** to a market maker in the same 90 days. FLOKI's supply will not rise, but at the current pace it will only fall very slowly.

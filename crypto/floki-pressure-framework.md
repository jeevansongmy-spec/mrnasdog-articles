FLOKI is a token that literally cannot be minted and is still, on the Pressure Framework's reading, a net seller. The Floki contract on Ethereum and the Floki contract on BNB Chain each return a **10,000,000,000,000** supply that is compiled into the code rather than stored, and ownership of both has been handed to the burn address, so no mint function exists and none can be added. Yet over the 90 days to **Aug 20 2026** the Floki DAO treasury paid **47.22B FLOKI** to a trading desk in three dated tranches and the Floki staking pool released another **22.65B FLOKI** back to holders, against just **4.12B FLOKI** destroyed — and every coin of that burn was an early-unstake penalty, not the revenue buy-and-burn the project advertises. The framework reads **+0.68%** net supply pressure on the **9,645,605,426,314 FLOKI** in circulation, and **+0.78%** forward. Our supply monitor reads **−0.10%**, a gap of **0.78 percentage points**, because both flows move coins that the counted supply already includes.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to **Aug 20 2026** the framework reads FLOKI at **+0.68%** net supply pressure: **69.87B FLOKI** reaching the market on the sell side against **4.12B FLOKI** destroyed on the buy side. Forward it projects **+0.78%**, because the treasury tranches are dollar-sized rather than coin-sized and a lower FLOKI price buys the same payment with more coins. Our supply monitor reads **−0.10%** for the same window, so the gap is **0.78 percentage points** and the overview carries a data-conflict flag. That gap is mechanical, not a dispute about facts: FLOKI's circulating figure is the **20 trillion** ever created on the two chains minus the **10.35 trillion** resting in the two burn wallets, and both the Floki DAO treasury and the Floki staking pool sit inside that count, so paying out of one or unlocking from the other moves coins to market without moving the counted number. The monitor can only see the **4.12B FLOKI** that was burned. FLOKI is best labelled a **hard-capped token that inflates from the inside**: the ceiling is unreachable, but the float is not fixed.

## Sell pressure: where new FLOKI comes from

None of it is new. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**, and this is one of the few tokens where that zero is provable from the source rather than inferred from a flat chart. The Floki contract on Ethereum, **0xcf0c122c6b73ff809c693db761e7baebe62b6a2e**, declares totalSupply as a **pure** function returning ten trillion — a compiled constant with no storage slot behind it — and the file contains no mint function, no burn function and no internal issuance path at all. It is not a proxy, so there is no implementation to swap. The two owner-gated setters that do exist are unreachable, because owner on both the Ethereum contract and the BNB Chain contract **0xfb5b838b6cfeedc2873ab27866079ac55363d37e** reads as the dead address. Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **0** for a simpler reason: FLOKI was a fair launch with no investor round, no team cliff and no release calendar, and the unlock trackers carry no schedule for FLOKI because none was ever written.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, carries **47.22B FLOKI** and is the real story. The Floki whitepaper publishes the DAO treasury addresses, and the Ethereum one, **0x2b9d5c7f2EAD1A221d771Fb6bb5E35Df04D60AB0**, fell from **93.40B FLOKI** to **46.18B FLOKI** across the window in exactly three transfers: **11.24B** on **May 27 2026**, **21.56B** on **Jun 25 2026** and **14.42B** on **Jul 27 2026**, all to the same high-throughput trading desk. Priced on their own firing dates those three payments are **$326,753**, **$496,743** and **$323,679** — dollar-denominated operating spend, which is why the forward column is projected in dollars and lands at **56.54B FLOKI** rather than repeating the coin count.

A fourth transfer looked exactly like a fourth tranche and is not one. On **Aug 14 2026** a second Floki team safe sent precisely **50,000,000,000 FLOKI** through a relay wallet into an exchange. Three days later the BNB Chain Floki treasury received **250,000,005,000 FLOKI** from that same exchange and returned **200,000,005,000 FLOKI** through the identical relay wallet — a net gain of **50,000,000,000 FLOKI**, matching the Ethereum outflow to the single unit. That is FLOKI moving between its two chains, which the whitepaper describes as a one-to-one swap, not FLOKI being sold. Booking it would have doubled the row. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**: there is no estate and no trustee winding FLOKI into this market. Sell #5 is the Floki staking pool, which released **22.65B FLOKI** net back to holders — a real three-to-forty-eight-month lock draining, which is float arriving at the market just as surely as a treasury payment.

## Buy pressure: where new FLOKI goes

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is the only live buy row at **4.12B FLOKI**, and reading it correctly required sweeping every transfer into both Floki burn wallets rather than trusting a headline. There were **25** such transfers on Ethereum and **188** on BNB Chain, and effectively all of them came from one address on each chain: the Floki staking contract **0xb8D2471E35eE033Db509e0456c8eFc4135f4EE43**. Every Ethereum burn was triggered by an unstake call from an ordinary holder wallet. This is the early-exit penalty the Floki whitepaper documents — forfeit **5%** of the stake at three months, rising to **20%** at forty-eight — burned on the way out. It is a real burn and it is counted, but it is holders quitting, not the protocol buying.

Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**, and that is a measurement, not an oversight. Floki documents three revenue-funded buy-and-burns — a quarter of FlokiFi Locker service fees, **1%** of prepaid-card top-up fees, and half of Floki Trading Bot fees — and not one coin from any of them reached either burn wallet in these 90 days. The burn is real; the revenue burn is not currently firing. It is also worth naming the trap: a separate token called FlokiBurn ranks for FLOKI burn searches and advertises an automatic **5%** sell-tax burn. That is a different token entirely; the Floki contract has no burn function of any kind. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **0** — the Floki team wallets only sent FLOKI out this window. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0** because the one lock FLOKI has shrank rather than grew, which is precisely why that flow sits on the sell side instead.

## Foundation and overhang

Four team-controlled pools sit behind the FLOKI float and all four are watched on-chain. The published Ethereum Floki DAO treasury holds **46.18B FLOKI** after its three tranches. The published BNB Chain Floki treasury, **0x17e98a24f992BB7bcd62d6722d714A3C74814B94**, holds **74.69B FLOKI**, up from **20.06B FLOKI** because of the chain migration described above. A second team safe, **0x04b604712A92D8E206b7c9D1A74d2Aa78e8CDDa5**, is not in the published list but is driven by the same signer key that deployed the Floki staking contract and holds **114.65B FLOKI**. The FlokiFi multisig on BNB Chain holds a residual **78.46M FLOKI**. Together that is roughly **235.6B FLOKI**, about **2.4%** of circulating supply, sitting one signature away from the market. One inbound transfer of **4.63B FLOKI** on **Jul 20 2026** from an unidentified holder is disclosed but not booked as a buy, because nothing evidences it as a purchase. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How FLOKI compares to other fixed-supply memecoins

Against a halving-model chain, FLOKI is the cleaner instrument on paper. Bitcoin and its hard-capped descendants still issue new coins every block and only approach their cap asymptotically; FLOKI reached its cap at deployment and has issued nothing since. Where a halving chain's inflation is a protocol parameter you can forecast a decade out, FLOKI's protocol contribution to inflation is permanently and provably zero. The comparison that actually matters is with the other big fixed-supply memecoins, and there FLOKI splits the difference. Shiba Inu and Dogecoin sit at the two extremes — one fixed with a burn culture, one uncapped with a fixed annual issuance — and FLOKI is fixed like the first while carrying something neither has: a funded DAO treasury that spends in the open market.

That treasury is the structural difference and it cuts both ways. Exchange tokens with quarterly buybacks convert revenue into supply removal, so their float shrinks when the business works. FLOKI converts its treasury into operating spend, so its float grows when the business needs funding — and the payments are dollar-sized, which means a weaker FLOKI price mechanically increases the coin count leaving the treasury. Meanwhile the Floki staking program, which the project has always framed as its deflation engine, is currently running in reverse: the lock is draining faster than the penalty destroys, so on net it adds float. A fee-burning chain can flip deflationary in a busy month. FLOKI can too, but only if the FlokiFi Locker, the prepaid card and the Floki Trading Bot actually route their promised share to the burn wallets, which they did not do once in this window.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the next Floki DAO treasury tranche, expected in late **Aug 2026** on the observed monthly beat and worth roughly **18.8B FLOKI** at current prices — if it lands, the forward reading holds; if the beat breaks, Sell #3 falls hard. Second, whether any transfer into either burn wallet comes from something other than the Floki staking contract, which would be the first evidence in this window that the FlokiFi and Trading Bot buy-and-burns are live. Third, the direction of the Floki staking pool: it holds **992.31B FLOKI** on Ethereum and **742.28B FLOKI** on BNB Chain, and a return to net staking would flip **22.65B FLOKI** of sell pressure into a buy row. Fourth, the **114.65B FLOKI** in the unpublished team safe, which has now moved twice. Fifth, the **0.30%** buy and sell tax, permanently fixed at that rate because both tax handlers have renounced ownership — the project's stated goal of removing it entirely would require a redeployment, not a vote.

## Summary

FLOKI has the strongest possible answer to the mint question — a supply figure compiled into two contracts as a constant, ownership renounced to the burn address on both, and **10.35 trillion** of the **20 trillion** ever created already destroyed. It still reads as a net seller at **+0.68%** over 90 days and **+0.78%** forward, because the pressure comes from inside the counted supply rather than from issuance: **47.22B FLOKI** of dollar-denominated DAO treasury spending and **22.65B FLOKI** of staking lock draining, against **4.12B FLOKI** of early-exit penalty burn. The key risk is that the one mechanism marketed as making FLOKI deflationary — revenue buy-and-burn — contributed nothing measurable this window, while the treasury has **235.6B FLOKI** left to spend. The ceiling is genuinely permanent; the floor under the float is not.

**TL;DR.** Floki is a token that cannot inflate and is inflating anyway. FLOKI was issued once on Ethereum and once on BNB Chain — **10 trillion** on each, **20 trillion** ever created — and ownership of both contracts was handed to the burn address, so no mint exists for anyone to call. Yet over the 90 days to **Aug 11 2026** the Floki DAO treasury pushed **47.2B FLOKI** into the market across three dated tranches while the revenue buy-and-burn destroyed only **3.74B FLOKI** — a net of **+0.45%**, against our supply monitor at **−0.15%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 11 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **FLOKI at +0.45% net**. Sell pressure totals **47.2B FLOKI**, buy pressure totals **3.74B FLOKI**, against a circulating base of **9.65T FLOKI**. Our supply monitor reads the same window at **−0.15%**, a gap of **0.61 percentage points**, so a monitor-gap chip ships on the overview page. The reason is structural rather than a disagreement about facts: the counted float for FLOKI is simply the 20 trillion ever created minus whatever sits in the burn address, so the monitor can only ever move when coins are burned — a treasury wallet is already inside that float, and paying out of it cannot register there at all. The forward reading is **+0.48%**, slightly worse, because the burn engine lost one of its three fee sources inside the window while the treasury spending did not change. FLOKI is best characterised as a **hard-fixed supply with a soft, discretionary float**.

## Sell pressure: where new FLOKI comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and it is zero in the strongest sense available anywhere in this coverage. Both FLOKI contracts were read directly at both ends of the window and both returned a total supply of exactly **10,000,000,000,000** — unchanged on Ethereum, unchanged on BNB Chain. More importantly, the owner of each contract is the burn address itself, meaning the deployer keys are gone: there is no privileged function, no governance path and no upgrade route that can create a single new FLOKI. There is no block reward, no staking emission and no rebase. This row is not merely off; it is structurally unable to turn on.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **zero**. Floki launched openly in 2021 with no venture round and no investor cap table to unwind, and the one small team allocation completed a four-year release in 2025 — a full year before this window opened. No unlock calendar exists for FLOKI anywhere, which removes the supply-cliff risk that dominates most tokens of this size. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is likewise **zero**: Floki is a going concern, there is no estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution touching the token.

That leaves Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, at **47.2B FLOKI** — the entire sell side of this page. The Floki DAO treasury on Ethereum fell from **93.4B** to **46.2B** over the window, and the outflow log resolves that fall to the unit across three transfers: **11.2B on May 27 2026**, **21.6B on Jun 25 2026** and **14.4B on Jul 27 2026**. Each tranche was forwarded within the same block through a pass-through address into a market-facing desk wallet that was observed trading against exchange hot wallets as recently as **Aug 10 2026**, so these are not internal reshuffles — the coins left project custody and reached buyers. The tranches are sized in dollars rather than tokens, which is why the FLOKI quantum moves around while the monthly rhythm does not.

## Buy pressure: where new FLOKI goes

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is **3.74B FLOKI** and it is genuine destruction rather than accumulation. Floki routes a share of ecosystem product fees — a quarter of the locker fees, a slice of the prepaid-card fees, and until recently half of the trading-bot fees — into open-market purchases of FLOKI that are then sent to the burn address. Because the destination is the burn address, the framework can measure the programme at its endpoint instead of trusting a dashboard: the burn balance rose by **2.87B** on Ethereum and **872.8M** on BNB Chain over the window, **3.74B FLOKI** together. That is the whole buy side of the FLOKI ledger.

The forward number is deliberately lower, and for a dated reason. The **Floki Trading Bot** was retired inside this window — flagged in the **Jun 5 2026** monthly community call and confirmed discontinued in the **Aug 3 2026** one, on security and infrastructure grounds — and the burn rate steps down exactly there. Sampling the burn address through the window gives **445.5M** in the 30 days to **Jun 12 2026**, **1.94B** in the 30 days to **Jul 12 2026**, then **200.2M** in the week to **Aug 4 2026** and just **94.7M** in the week to **Aug 11 2026**. Projecting the trailing blend forward would forecast an engine that no longer exists, so the next-90-day column is re-based on the fully post-retirement week at **1.22B FLOKI**. A replacement product with a similar fee-and-burn design has been promised but has no shipping date, so nothing is credited for it.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **zero**, which is a distinction worth drawing precisely. The **0.3% tax** on FLOKI buys and sells at decentralised venues is often described as a burn; it is not. That tax funds the treasury, and neither contract can reduce its own supply, so every FLOKI ever destroyed had to be bought on the open market first — which is the Buy #1 mechanism above. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **zero**: the treasury only receives tax and pays out, and no project-controlled wallet was seen accumulating FLOKI. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero** as well; the staking programme is an existing product rather than new supply removal, and it ran the other way this window.

## Foundation and overhang

Four pools are tracked. The **Ethereum DAO treasury** holds **46.2B FLOKI** after the three tranches, on no published release plan — at the pace of the last three months that is roughly nine months of spending left. Its **BNB Chain counterpart** holds **24.7B FLOKI** and grew by **4.63B** over the window purely on tax income, having paid nothing out; it is the reserve that refills the machine. A small **tax-collection contract** on Ethereum sweeps the 0.3% tax through to the treasury and holds only dust between sweeps. Fourth, and largest of all, the **staking pool** holds **1,737.3B FLOKI** on three-to-forty-eight-month holder locks — and it released a net **25.0B** of float over the same 90 days as stakers exited, a steady drain of roughly **278M a day** with no step event.

That last figure is shown but left out of the sell column on purpose, and the judgement is worth stating openly: it is holders choosing to exit an optional lock, not Floki releasing supply, and staked FLOKI was already counted as circulating. There is no symmetric row for holders choosing to stake, so booking one side of that behaviour would have inflated the read on a signal that is not a programme event at all. It is disclosed and tracked as overhang instead. Every one of these balances is read again on each update, and if any of them falls in between, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next update.

## How FLOKI compares to other fixed-supply memecoins

The first comparison is issuance. Most large memecoins share FLOKI's headline property — a fixed supply, no mint, sometimes a renounced contract — and on that measure FLOKI is at the strong end: renounced on both chains, verifiable in one call, with the burn address holding **10.35T** of the **20T** ever created. Compare that with an uncapped proof-of-stake chain, where issuance is a permanent line item that scales with the validator set, or with a game or app token still working through a multi-year unlock calendar. FLOKI has neither. The supply-cliff risk that drives most memecoin drawdowns simply is not present here.

The second comparison is what actually moves the float, and it is where FLOKI parts company with the pure memecoins. A pure memecoin with a renounced contract and a dead treasury has no supply story at all after launch — the ledger is flat forever. FLOKI runs an ecosystem of fee-generating products, which gives it a real buy-and-burn, but it also runs a funded DAO treasury that pays for that ecosystem by selling the token. This quarter the treasury out-spent the burn about **13 to 1**. Against exchange tokens that run quarterly buybacks, the difference is direction: those programmes are revenue converted into permanent supply reduction, whereas Floki's revenue burn is currently the smaller of two opposing flows. The mechanism is the right shape; the scale is not there yet.

The third comparison is measurability, and here FLOKI is unusually clean. Because the float is arithmetically the 20 trillion ever created minus the burn-address balance, and because both treasuries and the staking pool are readable at named addresses on two chains, every number on this page comes from a balance or a transfer rather than an estimate. That is rarer than it sounds in this asset class — and it is also why our own monitor disagrees, since a metric built on the counted supply cannot see coins moving out of a wallet that was already counted.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the late-**Aug 2026** treasury tranche: the pattern has fired on the 25th to 27th for three consecutive months, and a missed month would cut the forward reading roughly in half. Second, the replacement for the retired trading bot — if it ships with a comparable fee-to-burn share, the buy side recovers from **1.22B** toward the **3.74B** pace of this window. Third, the BNB Chain treasury at **24.7B FLOKI**: it has only accumulated so far, and its first outflow would add a second sell engine to a page that currently has one. Fourth, the staking pool, which has released **25.0B** of float in 90 days without pausing; a change in the reward token or lock terms would show up there first. Fifth, any DAO vote to burn treasury holdings outright, which is the one action that would flip this page negative in a single event.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads FLOKI at **+0.45% net** over the last 90 days and **+0.48%** over the next 90. The structural mechanism is a genuinely fixed **20 trillion** supply split across Ethereum and BNB Chain with both contracts renounced to the burn address, more than half already destroyed, and no unlock calendar anywhere — so all supply pressure is discretionary treasury spending rather than issuance. The key risk is that this spending is monthly, sized in dollars, and out-runs the revenue buy-and-burn about **13 to 1**, and the burn engine just lost a fee source it has not yet replaced. The ceiling is the comfort: the treasury holds **46.2B** on Ethereum and **24.7B** on BNB Chain, together under **0.8%** of circulating supply, and nothing in the system can ever create more FLOKI than the 20 trillion that already exists.

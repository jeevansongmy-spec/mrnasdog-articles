# ETHFI Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 16 to Nov 14 2026 the ETHFI supply moved by a net **0.00%**, and the next 90 days also come to **0.00%**. Other trackers show **+2.56%**, a difference of **2.56 percentage points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. That higher number comes from a circulating figure that sat near 965.35M ETHFI from Aug 27 to Oct 22 2026 and then jumped **33.19M ETHFI** on Oct 23 2026 to equal the full supply on the chain. The chain itself held at 998,535,999 ETHFI from the first day of the window to the last. In one line: ETHFI is a fixed-supply app token whose remaining team unlocks and weekly buybacks only move coins that are already counted.

## Sell pressure: where new ETHFI comes from

Protocol inflation is zero. All 1B ETHFI were created when the token launched in March 2024, and the token has no mint function and no owner who could add one, so no new ETHFI can ever appear. The supply sits just under 1B only because holders burned about 1.46M ETHFI in Aug and Sep 2025.

Vesting unlocks book zero, even though they have not finished. The core team holds 21.47% of all ETHFI, and its coins come out in monthly parts of **8.95M ETHFI** on the 15th of each month until March 2027. Two parts fell inside the window, on Sep 15 and Oct 15 2026, and about **44.73M ETHFI** are left. Since Oct 23 2026 every ETHFI, including these team coins, is counted as circulating, so a part coming out adds nothing new to the count. Investor coins finished vesting earlier in 2026.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book zero. The biggest move in the window was the ether.fi treasury sending **20M ETHFI** to a Foundation rewards wallet on Oct 4 2026, a buffer the community approved on Sep 3 2026 to top up user cashback. Those coins were already counted, so moving them changes nothing. Long-term locked and bankruptcy supply is also zero: no court case or failed firm holds ETHFI, and the roughly 11M ETHFI the Foundation lent to market makers in Aug 2025 is still its own and already counted.

## Buy pressure: where new ETHFI goes

The programmatic buyback is real but books zero. Since September 2026 ether.fi sends part of its card, swap and staking income to the Foundation each week, about **$225K**, and the Foundation buys ETHFI with it. By Oct 18 2026 it had bought **1.81M ETHFI**, and almost all of it went back out to users as card cashback. The bought coins stay in the market, in the Foundation's wallet or in users' accounts, so none of them leave the count.

The fee burn is zero. ETHFI has no built-in burn of fees, and no holder burned any ETHFI between Aug 16 and Nov 14 2026. The Foundation buy row is zero as well: the $50M buyback approved in Nov 2025, for when ETHFI trades under $3, also buys coins already in circulation. New long-term locks are zero too. About **108.85M ETHFI** sits in the staking vault, down from 112.56M on Aug 16 2026, and staked ETHFI still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The ether.fi treasury and Foundation hold the largest team-controlled balances. The main treasury wallet holds about **153.2M ETHFI**, after the 20M sent out on Oct 4 2026. Two more treasury wallets hold about 12.88M and 0.68M ETHFI; the second sent 1M out in early October. The Foundation rewards wallet holds about **16.5M ETHFI** of the 20M buffer after paying 3.5M toward cashback on Oct 22 2026, and an operating wallet holds about 0.4M ETHFI. The wallet that receives the weekly buyback held about 47,000 ETHFI on Nov 14 2026.

The remaining core team parts, about 44.73M ETHFI, are the one scheduled overhang, released monthly until March 2027. If a treasury, Foundation or buyback wallet balance falls, the outflow is booked at the next check, and if the circulating figure ever drops back below the full supply, the team parts start counting again.

## How ETHFI compares to other DeFi app tokens

ETHFI belongs to the group of fixed-supply app tokens, like Uniswap's UNI or Aave's AAVE, where every coin was made at launch and supply only changes through unlocks and burns. That sets it apart from chains like Ethereum, which pay validators in new coins every few minutes with no cap. For ETHFI, the questions are only who holds the coins that already exist, and whether any of them are taken out of the market for good.

On that second question, ETHFI's buyback works differently from the strongest buyback tokens. Hyperliquid's HYPE and Pump.fun's PUMP buy their own token and hold or burn it, which takes coins out of the market. ether.fi's weekly buyback mostly hands the coins straight back to users as card rewards, so it supports the price on the day it buys but does not shrink the supply. A burn, or a buyback held in a locked wallet, would.

On unlocks, ETHFI is near the end of its schedule. Investors are fully unlocked and the core team has about five monthly parts left, while many tokens launched in 2024 and 2025 still have years of large unlocks ahead of them.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The next core team parts of 8.95M ETHFI fall on Nov 15 2026, Dec 15 2026 and Jan 15 2027; they are already counted, but large moves from the receiving wallets to exchanges would show where they go. The weekly buyback and cashback flow is the second line to watch, especially any week where users sell more ETHFI than the Foundation buys, as happened in the week of Oct 12 2026. Any new community vote to burn bought ETHFI, or to lock it away instead of paying it out, would turn the buyback into real buy pressure. The 20M rewards buffer, of which about 16.5M ETHFI was left on Nov 14 2026, shows how much the Foundation still has to top up cashback. Around Jan 21 2027 the other trackers' 90-day number should fall back toward zero, once the Oct 23 2026 recount leaves their window.

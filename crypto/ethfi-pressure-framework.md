# ETHFI Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

ETHFI supply is growing, and it is set to keep growing until March 2027. No new ETHFI can ever be minted, but the last core-contributor grant still adds about **17.65M ETHFI** to the circulating supply every 90 days, while nothing is burned or locked away. That puts net supply growth at **+1.83%** over the last 90 days and **+1.83%** over the next 90, against a monitor reading of **+4.06%**. The ceiling is fixed: on-chain supply is **998,535,999 ETHFI**, and only about **33.2M** of it is still waiting to vest.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Sep 29 2026, ether.fi's ETHFI token added **17.65M ETHFI** to its float and removed none, for net supply growth of **+1.83%** of the **965.35M** circulating. The next 90 days project the same **+1.83%**, because the grant behind it pays out evenly each day until Mar 18 2027. Our supply monitor reads **+4.06%** for the same window, a gap of **2.24 percentage points**, so the page carries a monitor-gap warning. The gap comes from the circulating count the monitor reads, which was stepped up by about 44.7M ETHFI on Jul 18 2026 and again on Aug 18 2026, then cut back to 965.35M on Aug 28 2026, while on-chain supply did not move at all. ETHFI is a fixed-supply token with a vesting tail: mildly inflationary on the active float, and only until the last grant runs out.

## Sell pressure: where new ETHFI comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero**. All one billion ETHFI were created at launch in March 2024, and the ETHFI token contract has no mint function, no owner and no upgrade path, so no staking reward, emission or governance vote can ever create more. Supply can only go down, through a burn.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **17.65M ETHFI** in the last 90 days and the same in the next 90. ether.fi gave its core contributors **214.7M ETHFI**, 21.47% of supply, on a three-year vesting schedule from Mar 18 2024 with a one-year cliff. The first third opened at the cliff on Mar 18 2025; the remaining two-thirds vests evenly until Mar 18 2027, about **196,000 ETHFI a day**. The grant already sits in team wallets rather than in a lock contract, so the published vesting schedule is the measure. About **33.3M** is still due on paper, which lines up with the roughly **33.2M** of on-chain supply not yet counted as circulating. The investor allocation, 33.74% of supply, finished vesting on Mar 18 2026 and adds nothing now.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **zero**. The ether.fi DAO treasury and the Foundation hold large balances, but every one of those coins is already counted as circulating, so spending or selling them moves coins that are already in the float rather than adding new ones. Long-term locked coins and bankruptcy releases are also **zero**: there is no estate and no trustee schedule. The **30.82M ETHFI** held in the bridge contract backs ETHFI on other chains and releases nothing.

## Buy pressure: where new ETHFI goes

The programmatic buyback is real but books **zero**. ether.fi has bought back ETHFI with revenue since 2025, and a DAO vote that closed on Sep 3 2026 made it weekly and automatic: a share of card, swap and staking revenue now buys ETHFI on the market, and card cashback is paid in ETHFI bought the same way. The bought ETHFI goes to the Foundation treasury, to sETHFI stakers and to card users. All three sit inside the circulating count, so the buyback moves coins around the float instead of taking them out of it.

The protocol fee burn is **zero**. ether.fi has no fee burn. Earlier buybacks destroyed **1,464,001 ETHFI** between July and November 2025, bringing supply from one billion down to 998,535,999, but supply has not changed since and read the same at both ends of this window. A Foundation buy is **zero** as a separate row, because the Foundation's buying is the weekly buyback and is not counted twice. A new long-term lock is **zero** too: the staked-ETHFI contract grew from **101.34M** to **110.52M** this window, and staking now raises card membership tiers, but staked ETHFI can be withdrawn and stays in the float.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest team-controlled balance is the ether.fi DAO treasury wallet, holding **173.20M ETHFI**. It has sent nothing out since Jun 5 2025. A second treasury wallet holds **13.88M**, also unchanged across the window. A wallet that received a large part of the team allocation in February 2025 holds **101.55M** and has not moved since. The Foundation may also use up to **20M ETHFI** of its own holdings as a backup buffer for card rewards, as the September 2026 vote allows. We re-read these balances on-chain at every rebuild. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How ETHFI compares to other DeFi governance tokens

ETHFI belongs to the fixed-supply DeFi governance group: every token was minted at launch, and supply growth comes only from vesting, not from emissions. That separates it from staking-emission tokens, where new coins are minted forever as rewards. For ETHFI the end date is known: once the core-contributor grant finishes on Mar 18 2027, new sell pressure from vesting drops to zero, and only treasury decisions could add supply to the float.

On the buy side ETHFI sits between two models. Some DeFi tokens burn what they buy back, which shrinks supply. Others keep bought tokens in a treasury or pay them out to stakers. ether.fi burned for a time in 2025 but now holds and distributes, so its buyback supports demand for ETHFI without reducing the supply count. A buyback that burned would show up here as a real offset against the vesting; this one does not.

Compared with liquid-staking tokens that are still early in vesting, ETHFI's remaining overhang is small: about 3.3% of supply left to vest, against the 96.7% already counted as circulating. The bigger question for ETHFI supply is not new coins but the large treasury balance that is already counted, and how fast it gets spent.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the core-contributor vesting keeps adding about 17.65M ETHFI through Dec 28 2026, and the grant ends on Mar 18 2027. Second, the weekly buybacks approved on Sep 3 2026: watch whether the Foundation ever burns what it buys, which would open a real buy-side row. Third, the DAO treasury wallet with 173.20M ETHFI: any outflow is the first thing we check. Fourth, the circulating count the monitor reads: another step up or cut back would move the gap, not our number. Fifth, the staked-ETHFI total, now 110.52M, as card membership pulls more ETHFI into staking.

## Summary

ether.fi's ETHFI is a fixed-supply token whose circulating supply still grows by about **+1.83% every 90 days**, because the last core-contributor grant releases about 17.65M ETHFI per quarter until Mar 18 2027. No ETHFI can be minted, nothing was burned in the window, and the buyback sends bought coins to the Foundation, stakers and card users rather than destroying them. The key risk is the **173.20M ETHFI** DAO treasury, which is already counted but could be spent. The ceiling is hard: **998,535,999 ETHFI** exist, and only about 33.2M of them are still to vest.

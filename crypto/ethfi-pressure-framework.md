# ETHFI Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

**Mixed flows · supply roughly steady** describes ETHFI’s quantified ledger, not a promise of no vesting. The framework books **0** verified net additions and **0** burns, leaving **0.00%** quantified net against **965.35M ETHFI** circulating, while the monitor reads **+4.13%**. The token cannot mint, but the custody and circulation boundary for its remaining contributor vesting is unresolved; that uncertainty is displayed rather than converted into a precise unlock forecast.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The ledger reads **0.00%** over the trailing window and **0.00%** in the quantified forward column through **Dec 19 2026**. The appropriate description is fixed issuance with an unresolved release boundary, rather than a completed vesting programme or a confidently neutral supply forecast.

## Sell pressure: where new ETHFI comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. ETHFI is an Ethereum governance token, not the asset issued to Ethereum validators. Its deployed token has no ongoing mint path or upgrade route, and the outstanding supply remained **998.536M ETHFI** at both boundaries. Restaking yield, lending activity and the consumer-finance business do not automatically create additional ETHFI.

Vesting unlocks are recorded as an **opaque zero** because the eligible circulating addition is not independently resolved. The project describes a **214.7M** contributor allocation with a three-year schedule and a one-year cliff. Current calendars disagree on whether the cliff is included in the linear period, and an older project-prepared transparency report describes a different completion date. The report also identifies dispersed qualified-custodian holdings instead of one readable vesting escrow.

A common calendar interpretation produces approximately **26.838M ETHFI** over three months. That is a scenario, not the locked ledger value: its remaining entitlement exceeds the entire **33.186M** gap between outstanding and classified circulating supply. The analysis cannot assume every token on that contractual calendar still sits outside circulation. Foundation and unscheduled unlocks remain unquantified where that same boundary is unknown; transfers between disclosed treasuries are not automatically new supply. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply contributes **0** because no independent estate release has been identified.

## Buy pressure: where new ETHFI goes

Programmatic buyback contributes **0** measured removal. The purchase programme redistributes ETHFI to staking holders rather than destroying the token. Its destination vault holds **111.263M ETHFI**, far larger than the entire excluded supply bucket. Counting the whole staking destination as newly removed would contradict the supply boundary, while using its balance growth as purchases would mix user deposits, withdrawals and reward distributions.

Protocol fee burn is **0**, verified on both relevant token surfaces. Outstanding ETHFI stayed unchanged and the zero and dead-address balances did not increase between the window boundaries. Ethereum transaction fees destroy ETH, not ETHFI. A business can generate revenue, spend it on its governance token and deliver that token to another holder without reducing its supply.

Foundation buy is **0** separately measured beyond the identified revenue-funded programme. A transfer into an operating address is not proof of a market purchase. New long-term lock is **0** because membership staking is withdrawable and no separate dated lock has a verified amount outside circulation. The practical distinction is between demand for ETHFI and a lasting reduction in counted token availability.

## Foundation and overhang

Four treasury addresses named in the current governance documents hold approximately **187.831M ETHFI** together. The separately disclosed operating wallet holds **2.165M**, and a grants distributor holds approximately **0.455M**. Several former treasury and insider staging addresses are empty. Empty staging wallets do not establish that the distributed tokens have been sold, unlocked or removed from the circulation count; the final custody matters.

The buyback transit wallet is also empty at the window end, while the staking destination holds **111.263M**. Individual employee grants and dispersed investor or contributor custody remain incompletely mapped. A historical employee-grant figure is not carried forward as a current balance, and the bridge lockbox is not added as an independent reserve because it backs tokens on other networks.

Readable balances are checked at each rebuild, with a daily monitoring target; custody and employee disclosures need at least a fortnightly review. If an overhang balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 review at the next refresh. A verified crossing into circulation is counted once, while transfers among balances already inside circulation remain documented concentration and execution risks.

## How ETHFI compares to other governance tokens

ETHFI resembles a fixed-issuance governance token more than a continuously issued network asset. A chain may pay validators through newly minted native coins, but ETHFI’s economic programmes distribute a stock created earlier. The hard issuance limit answers whether additional tokens can be manufactured; it does not answer when an existing contractual grant becomes available to a contributor.

ETHFI also differs from a governance token whose fee mechanism irrevocably burns each purchased unit. Both programmes can generate market demand, but redistribution gives another holder the ability to sell the purchased token. That difference determines whether the programme reduces counted supply. A withdrawable staking receipt should not be treated as equivalent to destruction simply because the underlying token resides in a contract.

The most useful comparison is with another token nearing the end of insider vesting. An allocation percentage and an end date are insufficient when the circulating classifier already includes part of the custody. The analyst needs a consistent map from the contractual entitlement to excluded accounts, actual releases and the denominator. Without that map, a detailed-looking quarterly figure can be less reliable than an explicitly incomplete ledger.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Through **Dec 19 2026**, the first watch line is a current contributor-custody disclosure that reconciles remaining grants with excluded supply. The second is a dated purchase and distribution report separating ETHFI acquisitions from deposits into the staking vault. The third is any actual token destruction, which must appear either as reduced outstanding supply or increased irreversible dead holdings. The fourth is the grants distributor and operating treasury: material outflows need destination and circulation checks before entering sell pressure. A revised vesting disclosure would replace the opaque placeholder with a defensible amount.

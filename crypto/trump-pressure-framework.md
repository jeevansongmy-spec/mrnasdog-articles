# TRUMP Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Official Trump (TRUMP) cannot be minted — its minting key is gone — and yet the Pressure Framework reads TRUMP at **+15.77%** over the last 90 days and **+15.77%** over the next 90. All of it comes from one place: a single creator vesting escrow on Solana, which let out **44.45M TRUMP** in the window, against **0** removed by any buyback, burn or lock. The escrow still holds **718.13M TRUMP**, more than two and a half times the whole circulating float, and the monitor reads **+19.12%** for the same window.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **281.87M TRUMP**, the framework books **44.45M TRUMP** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days, a net of **+15.77%**, and projects **+15.77%** for the next 90 days at the same pace. The inflation monitor reads **+19.12%**, a gap of **3.35 percentage points**, which is over the 0.5-point tolerance and ships with a monitor-gap warning on the overview page. The gap splits cleanly: **2.95 points** is base convention, because the monitor divides the same release by the smaller float of 90 days ago (18.72% against 15.77%), and the other **0.39 points** is the monitor’s price-derived supply estimate running slightly above the chain. The label for TRUMP is **a fixed-supply memecoin with a creator escrow still unwinding**: the token cannot inflate, but its tradable float can, and right now it grows fast.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

It does not come from minting. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**. The TRUMP mint on Solana has no mint authority and no freeze authority; both were set to nothing, and on Solana that cannot be undone. The 1B TRUMP created at launch on Jan 17 2025 is the ceiling for good, and the chain now counts **999.999M TRUMP** in existence after tiny burns. This row carries a permanent tag because it rests on that removed key, not on a number that happened not to move.

The entire supply story is Sell #2, vesting unlocks, at **44.45M TRUMP**. At launch, 800M TRUMP, the creator allocation held by CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC, was placed in one escrow run by a vesting program. Nothing left it for a full year. Over this window the escrow fell from **762.59M** to **718.13M TRUMP**, across nine claims. Three older claim wallets took **13.45M** between them on vesting schedules that add a little each day. A fourth wallet, drawing on a 180M allocation, began claiming on **Sep 1 2026** and took **31.00M** in three round-number claims on Sep 1, Sep 6 and Sep 20. The unlock trackers show a larger calendar, close to a million TRUMP a day; the framework books what actually left the escrow, because coins that vest on paper but stay inside the escrow never reach the market.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0**: every creator coin still held back sits inside that same escrow, so there is no second pool with its own release. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0** as well; TRUMP has no bankruptcy estate, trustee or court-ordered distribution.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

Nowhere. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**. In October 2025 the issuer talked about raising $200M to $1B for a company that would buy TRUMP on the market. No raise closed, and no purchase happened in this window.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**. TRUMP is a plain Solana token with no fee switch and no burn engine. Both burn surfaces were read. The count of TRUMP in existence is **999,998,939 TRUMP**, so only about 1,061 TRUMP has been destroyed since launch in total, which caps this window’s burn at under 0.001M. The known dead addresses hold under 23 TRUMP between them.

Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0**; the creator companies only moved coins out of the escrow and on toward exchanges. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**, because TRUMP has no staking and no lock programme.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang on TRUMP is one very large balance: the creator escrow at **718.13M TRUMP**, about 72% of all TRUMP ever made. Four allocations have claimed from it so far, **81.87M** in total. Their own vesting terms are readable on chain, and they show about **62.94M TRUMP** already vested and still unclaimed, **60.05M** of it belonging to the newest wallet alone. That can reach the market on any day without a new unlock. The other **540.13M TRUMP** belongs to allocations that have never claimed once, and their dates are not published anywhere readable.

Two smaller items are tracked beside it. The team said in July 2026 that up to **96M TRUMP** may go to partners, deals, a holder club and a mobile game, with no dates given. The newest claim wallet holds **10.46M TRUMP**, which is already counted in the float, so its moves to exchanges add nothing new. The escrow is read from the chain at every rebuild. If its balance falls faster than the pace booked here, the extra outflow enters the sell side at the next refresh.

## How TRUMP compares to other fixed-supply tokens

On paper TRUMP sits in the strictest supply class there is. A halving chain like Bitcoin still creates new coins on every block, just fewer over time. A staking chain creates new coins every day to pay validators. TRUMP creates none at all, and never can, because the key that could do it no longer exists. On the issuance axis, TRUMP is stricter than Bitcoin.

Yet TRUMP reads **+15.77%** over 90 days, while Bitcoin reads a fraction of one percent. The reason is that a fixed cap limits total supply, not the tradable float, and those two move apart. TRUMP launched with only 200M of its 1B in the market, so it looks much more like a young token working through a team vest than like a mature capped coin. The difference from a normal vest is control: the claims are made by the creators when they choose, so the release comes in bursts rather than on a smooth line.

The last comparison is with tokens that run buybacks or fee burns, which can offset their unlocks and even shrink. TRUMP has neither. There is no revenue stream wired to buy TRUMP, and the only burns are wallets cleaning up dust. Nothing on the buy side can absorb what the escrow releases.

Second, the newest claim wallet, which took 31.00M in its first three weeks and still has about 60.05M vested and unclaimed; if it keeps its September pace, the next reading will land well above 15.77%. Third, any first claim from the 540.13M TRUMP that has never moved, which would open a new stream with no warning. Fourth, the up to 96M TRUMP the team said it may hand to partners and a holder club. Fifth, the buyback company first talked about in October 2025; if it ever buys on the market, it becomes the first buy-side force TRUMP has had.

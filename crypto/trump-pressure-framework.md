OFFICIAL TRUMP can never issue another coin — the mint authority on the Solana token was renounced at launch and reads empty today — and yet the Pressure Framework reads TRUMP at **+13.08%** over the trailing 90 days and **+11.13%** over the next 90. All of it comes from one place: a single creator vesting escrow holding the CIC Digital and Fight Fight Fight allocation, which released **35.7M TRUMP** into the tradable float over the window. Sell pressure is **35.723M TRUMP**, buy pressure is **0**, and the ceiling is a hard, on-chain **1,000M TRUMP** that nobody has the authority to raise.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **273.137M TRUMP**, the framework books **35.723M TRUMP** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days — a net of **+13.08%** — and projects **+11.13%** for the next 90 days. The inflation monitor reads **+14.44%** for the same window, a gap of **1.36 percentage points**, which is over the framework's 0.5pp tolerance and therefore ships with a monitor-gap warning on the overview page. That gap decomposes cleanly and completely: **1.97pp** is base convention, because the monitor divides its supply change by the 90-day-old float while the framework divides by today's, and **0.61pp** runs the other way because the monitor's price-derived supply estimate sits below the chain's own account state. The label for OFFICIAL TRUMP is **a capped memecoin whose creator escrow is doing all the inflation**: the token cannot be minted, but its tradable float can grow, and right now it grows fast.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

It does not come from minting, and on OFFICIAL TRUMP it never can. The Solana mint record for TRUMP returns an empty mint authority and an empty freeze authority; on this network a renounced mint authority cannot be restored by anyone, including CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC. There is also no protocol behind the token — no fee switch, no staking, no validator rewards, no governance — so there is nothing that would pay anyone in newly created coins. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**, and it is one of the few rows in this framework that is closed permanently rather than merely watched.

The entire supply story is Sell #2, vesting unlocks, at **35.723M TRUMP**. The 800M creator allocation — 80% of the OFFICIAL TRUMP supply, held by CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC — sits in one on-chain escrow account released through a vesting program. That escrow is the only bucket the market's circulating figure excludes, which means the float is defined as the 1,000M cap minus that balance, to eight decimal places. The escrow held **762.6M TRUMP** at the window start and **726.9M** at the end, and that fall is the number. It is worth being precise about what this is: no TRUMP is created by a vesting unlock. The coins already existed and were already inside the 1,000M ceiling. What changes is that they stop being locked and become tradable float — which is exactly the thing the Pressure Framework measures, and exactly the thing a renounced mint authority does not protect a holder from. Two thirds of the window's release came in the first week of September, from a fourth claim stream whose on-chain allocation account did not exist before **Aug 30 2026** and which took **22.3M TRUMP** in two round-number withdrawals five days apart.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0** — there is no foundation, no DAO and no treasury contract, because the issuers are private companies rather than a protocol. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0** as well: OFFICIAL TRUMP has no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution attached to it.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

Nowhere, this window, and nowhere in the token's entire life. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**. Fight Fight Fight floated a plan in late 2025 to raise between 200M and 1,000M dollars for a treasury company whose purpose would be to buy OFFICIAL TRUMP on the open market. Nearly a year on, no raise has closed, no vehicle has been registered, and the 90-day sweep found no purchase. If it ever completes, it would be the first buy-side mechanism this memecoin has ever had.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**, and the reason is structural rather than circumstantial: there is no protocol to collect fees for a burn. The framework still read both burn surfaces rather than trusting either alone. The known dead addresses on Solana hold **22.9 TRUMP** between them, and the count of TRUMP in existence stands at **999,999,011** against the **1,000,000,000** minted at launch — so **989 TRUMP** have been destroyed in the token's entire nineteen-month life, by individual holders acting on their own. Any 90-day burn is therefore bounded under that figure, which is under **0.0004%** of the float. This row is a zero with an arithmetic ceiling on it, not an assumption. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0** — the issuers are net sellers, and every wallet that received an escrow claim forwarded almost all of it onward within days. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**: there is no staking contract and no re-lock path, the escrow only runs one way, and the sole inbound transfer it has received since launch was **0.69 TRUMP** in February 2026.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang on OFFICIAL TRUMP is enormous and it is the reason this page exists. The dominant item is the creator escrow itself: **726.9M TRUMP** still locked, which is **2.7 times** the entire circulating float, held by CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC. That balance is a single readable account and is refreshed from the chain at every rebuild. The second item is up to **96M TRUMP** that the issuers said in July 2026 they may deploy into partnerships, acquisitions, a holder club and a mobile game, with no tranche dates or sizes published; nothing has been released from it yet, so it books zero and is watched. The third item is the claim-routing wallets, which pass tokens straight through and hold nothing today except **12.1M TRUMP** in the wallet that took September's release. There is no buyback accumulation wallet to track, because there is no buyback, and there is no bankruptcy estate residual.

What makes this overhang unusual is that almost all of it is discretionary in practice. The escrow's releases are authorised off the chain, so the forward calendar cannot be read from the chain at all, and an allocation that has never claimed leaves no on-chain trace — which means the carve-up of the remaining **726.9M TRUMP** across future streams is genuinely unknown, even though the balance itself is exact. The trigger sentence applies to every item: if the escrow's balance, or the 96M deployment pool, or a claim wallet's balance falls between refreshes by more than the observed streams account for, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How TRUMP compares to other capped, non-minting tokens

On the pure issuance axis, OFFICIAL TRUMP is stricter than almost anything in the market. A halving-model chain like Bitcoin still mints on every block, just at a decaying rate, so its inflation reading is small but positive on a known clock. An uncapped continuous-emission Cosmos L1 pays a staking-linked emission every block forever. OFFICIAL TRUMP mints nothing at all, and cannot: the mint authority is renounced, which is a harder commitment than a halving schedule because it is not a policy anyone can vote to change. And yet TRUMP reads **+13.08%** while a mid-cycle Bitcoin reads a fraction of a percent. That is the whole lesson of the comparison — a hard cap constrains total supply, not tradable float, and those are different quantities that move independently.

The right structural analogue is not a capped chain at all; it is a recently-launched token working through a multi-year insider vest. OFFICIAL TRUMP is an extreme version of that shape, because the vested cohort holds 80% of the cap and the public float was only 20% on day one. The difference from a typical vest is that a typical vest is enforced by a schedule anyone can read; here the escrow is on chain but the release authorisation is not, so the calendar is effectively the issuers' decision. That is why the realised release and the published schedule diverge so widely: the trackers' roughly **28M TRUMP** per month would have implied around **85M** across this window, and what actually left the escrow was **35.7M**.

The other structural comparison is to exchange tokens that run quarterly buybacks and burns. Those offset issuance with a demand-linked removal that scales with usage, and their inflation readings can go genuinely negative. OFFICIAL TRUMP has no such mechanism on paper or in code — no fee stream, no burn engine, no buyback contract — so there is nothing on the buy side that could ever grow into an offset. The announced treasury vehicle is the only candidate, and it has produced nothing in eleven months.

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads OFFICIAL TRUMP at **+13.08%** over the trailing 90 days and **+11.13%** projected forward: supply growing, projected to keep growing. The structural mechanism is not inflation but release — nothing can be minted, essentially nothing has ever been burned, and a hard on-chain ceiling of **1,000M TRUMP** holds, while one creator escrow moved **35.7M TRUMP** into the float out of a remaining **726.9M** still locked. The renounced mint authority is the one genuine comfort — no vote can create another TRUMP — but on this token that protects the ceiling, not the price.

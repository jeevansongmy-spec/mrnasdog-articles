# TRUMP Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about Official Trump, see [mrnasdog.com/research/trump](https://mrnasdog.com/research/trump).

Official Trump (**TRUMP**) is inflationary on its tradable float even though no new coin can ever be minted. In the 90 days to Oct 17 2026 the team took **44.45M TRUMP** out of its vesting vault, against a circulating supply of **281.87M**, and nothing bought or burned any back, so the float grew about **+15.77%**. The same pace is the base case for the next 90 days, and the vault still holds **718.13M TRUMP**, more than two and a half times everything that trades today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads TRUMP at **+15.77%** net supply over the last 90 days and **+15.77%** projected for the next 90. Our supply monitor reads **+18.67%**, a gap of **2.90 percentage points**, which is above our 0.5-point line, so we walked every source again. The gap closed: both readings see the same coins leaving the same team vault in July, August and September, and the difference comes only from the starting point. The monitor divides the new coins by the float of 90 days ago (about **237.1M**), while we divide by today's float of **281.87M**. No warning chip ships. In one line: TRUMP is a fixed-supply meme coin that is inflationary by team unlock, not by code.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**, and it is zero for good. TRUMP is a plain Solana token whose mint key was removed, so no wallet and no program can create another coin. All 1B TRUMP were made on launch day in January 2025, and the total has only fallen since, by about 1,135 coins that holders destroyed.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **44.45M TRUMP** in 90 days. At launch the 800M team coins, held for CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC, went into one vesting vault on Solana. The coins vest month by month over three years, and on paper about 750M have already vested. But a vested coin only reaches the market when the team claims it, and so far the team has claimed only **81.87M** in total. In these 90 days it claimed **10.84M** on Jul 24–25 2026, **2.62M** on Aug 22 2026 and **31.0M** across Sep 1, Sep 6 and Sep 20 2026. We read every transaction of the vault on-chain, and the sum matches the fall in its balance to the last coin. Nothing has left since Sep 20 2026. For the next 90 days we keep the same pace, **44.45M**, because no new rule changed how the team claims.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book **0** as their own row. TRUMP has no foundation; the team itself is the overhang, and every coin it takes from the vault is already counted in the vesting row. Long-term locks and bankruptcy also book **0**: no court estate or trustee holds TRUMP, and the team vault is the only lock there is.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

Every buy row is **0**. TRUMP has no programmatic buyback. A company that would raise up to $200M to buy the coin was first reported in October 2025, but it was still not confirmed in August 2026 and we found no purchase in this window. There is no protocol fee burn either: the token charges no fee, and the roughly 1,135 TRUMP destroyed since launch are far too few to count.

The team does not buy TRUMP; it sells and hands it out. Rewards in lending vaults, prizes in the TRUMP game and gifts for top holders are paid from coins already in the float. New long-term locks are also **0**. The holder contests reward people for keeping their coins until a snapshot date, but they lock nothing, and coins parked in lending vaults still count as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang is one wallet: the team vesting vault, holding **718.13M TRUMP** on Oct 17 2026, or **71.8%** of all coins. About **250M** of that has not vested yet; the other **~468M** has vested and simply was never claimed, which means the team could release far more than our 90-day figure whenever it chooses. The team's own market update of Jul 15 2026 says up to **9.6%** of supply, about **96M TRUMP** of already-unlocked coins, may be sold or used for deals, games and holder clubs over the coming months. About **44.45M** has left the vault since then, so roughly **51.5M** of that plan is left.

The wallets that received the past claims are empty again; the last one passed on its final 6.14M TRUMP on Oct 14 2026. Those wallets are inside the float, so moving their coins to exchanges adds nothing new. We read the vault on-chain every day, and if its balance falls between our checks, the outflow enters the sell side at the next refresh.

## How TRUMP compares to other meme coins

Most large meme coins fall into one of two supply shapes. Dogecoin has no cap and no team: miners receive a fixed 10,000 DOGE per block forever, so its supply grows slowly and evenly, a small and predictable inflation. Pepe was handed out almost entirely at launch, with no team vesting, so its float barely moves. TRUMP is neither. Its total is fixed at 1B and can never grow, yet only 28.2% of it trades, and the rest belongs to the team on a three-year schedule.

That makes TRUMP look more like a young venture-backed token than a classic meme coin: the risk is not new coins from code, it is old coins leaving one insider wallet. A fixed cap protects TRUMP holders only in the long run, when the vault is empty. Until then, each claim by the team grows the float faster than a mining or staking emission would on most large coins, and there is no fee burn or buyback to offset it, unlike exchange tokens that buy back their supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The next paper unlock dates are Oct 18, Nov 18 and Dec 18 2026. Each adds roughly 28M vested TRUMP to the team's unclaimed pile; the market only feels them if the team claims.

The team vault balance itself is the number that matters: a claim larger than the September run of 31.0M would push the next 90 days above our **+15.77%**.

The holder contest snapshot on Nov 12 2026, for a dinner on Nov 22 2026, may pull coins off exchanges into holder wallets, but that moves coins inside the float and changes no supply figure.

Any confirmed buyer, such as the long-reported TRUMP treasury company, would be the first real buy pressure; until it buys, it books nothing.

## Summary

TRUMP, the Official Trump meme coin on Solana, cannot mint a single new coin, but its tradable supply grew **+15.77%** in the 90 days to Oct 17 2026 because the team took **44.45M TRUMP** out of its vesting vault, and we project about the same for the next 90 days. Nothing buys or burns the coin back. The key risk is the **718.13M TRUMP** still in that vault, most of it already vested, which the team can claim at any time. The hard cap of 1B coins is the ceiling, and it only protects holders once that vault is empty.

Official Trump (TRUMP) is a capped Solana memecoin — its mint authority is renounced on-chain, so the fixed **1B** supply can never grow. The pressure comes from distribution, not issuance: **80%** of supply is an insider allocation vesting through **December 2027**, and about **10.5M TRUMP** a quarter is actually reaching the market. With a buy side of **zero** — no buyback, no burn, no lock — that nets to roughly **+4.23%** over 90 days, and our supply monitor agrees at **+4.37%**, a gap of only **0.14 percentage points**, so no warning chip ships. TRUMP is structurally inflationary on its active float even though its cap is fixed.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 9 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **TRUMP at about +4.23% net**, both trailing and forward — an empty buy side and a vesting-driven sell side of roughly **10.5M TRUMP** reaching the market against a circulating float near **248.3M**. Our supply monitor reads **+4.37%** for the same trailing window, a gap of just **0.14 percentage points** — inside the framework's 0.5-point tolerance, so no monitor-gap warning is attached. The two views agree because both measure the same thing: TRUMP's tradable float is growing as insiders distribute vested tokens, even though the token itself is **hard-capped at 1B**. The cite-able label is simple — TRUMP is **structurally inflationary on its active float**, an unlock story with no offset.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

There is only one source, and it is not issuance. Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero**: TRUMP has no emission, no staking rewards and no mint path, because the mint authority is renounced on-chain, read null this session. The on-chain supply is a fixed **1B** (a trivial ~931 tokens net burned), and it can only fall. Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — is the whole story, at about **10.5M TRUMP** reaching the market over the last 90 days. Of the 1B supply, **80%** is owned by two Trump-affiliated companies on a three-year linear vest that runs from **January 2025** to **December 2027**; only about **200M** was public at launch.

The subtlety in the vesting is why this page books a smaller number than the unlock trackers show. The published calendar releases roughly **28M TRUMP** a month, which would imply about **84M** over a 90-day window. But that gross release lands in team wallets, not on the market: the classified circulating float has grown only about **10.5M** over the last 90 days, and just **200M to 248M** across the 19 months since launch — around a third of the scheduled pace. The framework measures supply reaching the market, so it books the **realised** outflow of about **10.5M** a quarter and treats the rest as a held overhang. Sell #3 — foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is therefore **zero** on value but carries a very large watch item (covered below). Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero** because no bankruptcy estate, court distribution or trustee schedule applies to TRUMP.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

This is the shortest section on the page, because there is nothing to offset the vest. Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is **zero**: TRUMP has no protocol buyback. One Trump-affiliated entity was reported to be raising between **$200M** and **$1B** to build a treasury vehicle that would accumulate TRUMP, but no completed raise or on-market buying was confirmed as of mid-2026, so the framework books nothing. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is **zero**: TRUMP is a bare token with no protocol, no fee switch and no burn sink. Buy #3 — foundation buy — is **zero**, with no disclosed discretionary open-market buying. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is **zero**, because TRUMP has no staking mechanism that removes coins from circulation and no new lockup contract was deployed. The buy ledger is empty in every row, which is why the vest passes straight through to a positive net.

## Foundation and overhang

TRUMP's overhang is one of the largest on the site relative to its float. About **752M TRUMP** — roughly **75%** of the total supply — is non-circulating, and almost all of it is the **80% insider block**: the two affiliated allocations plus creator tranches, either still locked in the vest or vested and held off-market. Because the scheduled unlock (~28M a month) runs well ahead of what actually reaches the market (~10.5M a quarter), the held overhang keeps growing even as insiders sell — vested tokens pile up in team custody faster than they are distributed. The framework refreshes this by hand against the vesting calendar and the circulating float. The trigger is simple: if this overhang's balance falls between refreshes as insiders push more tokens onto the market, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh, and the net would climb above the ~+4.23% booked here.

## How TRUMP compares to other memecoins

TRUMP sits in the opposite corner from a fair-launch memecoin. Against a token like **BONK** or **Dogecoin**, the contrast is total on the supply axis: BONK is **100% unlocked** with a renounced mint and no insider vest, so its inflation is essentially zero; DOGE has no cap but distributes its issuance to miners in the open market. TRUMP shares BONK's renounced mint and hard cap, but almost none of its supply is free — the number that moves the inflation reading is not new issuance, it is the **insider unlock**. That makes TRUMP behave less like a memecoin and more like a **high-FDV, low-float** venture token, where a small tradable slice is diluted month after month by a large locked allocation coming unvested.

The key mechanism-based difference from the exchange tokens that run quarterly buyback-and-burn is direction. Those tokens use real revenue to **shrink** their float and can post negative net supply; TRUMP has **no revenue and no buy-side mechanism at all**, so every vested token that reaches the market is pure one-way dilution of the active float. And unlike a fixed-supply memecoin whose burn is a rounding error, TRUMP's unlock is material — about **+4.23%** a quarter on the circulating base — which is why, despite the cap, it reads as one of the more inflationary assets the framework tracks on a float basis.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the monthly vesting unlock, next dated around **Aug 18 2026** and recurring through the window: the scheduled amount is about **28M TRUMP**, and what matters is how much of it actually reaches the market versus staying in team custody. Second, the pace of realised selling — if insiders accelerate distribution and the circulating float starts tracking closer to the ~28M-a-month schedule, the net would jump well above **+4.23%** and the inflation score would stay pinned at the floor. Third, any completion of the reported treasury raise, which would be the first genuine buy-side mechanism TRUMP has ever had and the only thing that could offset the vest. Fourth, the run toward full vesting by **December 2027**, after which no scheduled supply remains to unlock. Fifth, any disclosure of a burn, lock or utility mechanism, none of which exists today.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads TRUMP at about **+4.23% net** over the next 90 days, from a sell side that is entirely an **insider vest** — roughly **10.5M TRUMP** reaching the market a quarter — and a buy side that is **zero in every row**. The structural mechanism is a fixed, **renounced-mint** cap of **1B** paired with an **80% insider allocation** still unlocking through 2027, so the token cannot inflate by issuance but is steadily diluting its active float. Our monitor reads **+4.37%** for the same window, a **0.14-point** gap that leaves the two views in agreement. The key risk is that realised selling accelerates toward the ~28M-a-month scheduled pace, pushing the net higher; the only offset would be a buy-side program that does not yet exist. Capped but not clean — TRUMP's supply story is who is still unlocking, and there is nothing on the other side of the ledger.

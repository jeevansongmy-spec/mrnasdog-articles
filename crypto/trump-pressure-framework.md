# TRUMP Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

**Official Trump (TRUMP)** is inflationary on its tradable float even though no new TRUMP can ever be minted. In the last 90 days the creator companies pulled **44.45M TRUMP** out of their vesting vault, against a circulating supply of **281.87M**, and nothing took any coins back out — a net rise of **+15.77%**, with about the same expected over the next 90 days. The cap is the vault: **718.13M TRUMP** is still inside, more than 2.5 times everything in circulation today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads TRUMP at **+15.77%** net supply growth over the last 90 days (Jul 1 to Sep 29 2026) and projects **+15.77%** for the next 90 days. Our supply monitor reads **+18.43%** over the same stretch, a gap of **2.66 percentage points**. We walked that gap down to its parts: the monitor divides the same unlock by the float of 90 days ago (about 237.41M TRUMP), which on its own lifts the figure to 18.72%, and its supply estimate from market data sits 0.29 points lower than the chain. Both readings see the same 44.45M TRUMP leaving the vault, so no warning chip is shown. In one line: **a fixed-supply meme coin whose float grows only by insider unlocks**.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. The TRUMP token on Solana has no mint authority left, so the total can never rise above 1 billion. There are no block rewards, no staking rewards and no emission programme of any kind — every TRUMP that will ever exist was created at launch on Jan 17 2025.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **44,453,896 TRUMP** in 90 days. At launch, 200M TRUMP were free — 100M for the public sale and 100M for liquidity — and the other **800M TRUMP**, the creator share held for CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC, went into one on-chain vesting vault. The first unlock, planned for April 2025, was pushed back, and not a single coin left that vault until Jan 30 2026. In this window four creator wallets pulled coins out: 10.84M TRUMP in late July, 2.62M on Aug 22, and then one wallet took 11.01M on Sep 1, 11.26M on Sep 6 and 8.73M on Sep 20. We read the vault balance on-chain at both ends — 762.59M before, 718.13M after — and the drop matches those nine pulls to the last token.

We count what really left the vault, not what the paper schedule says has vested. Unlock trackers list about 28.7M TRUMP a month, and the team said in July that about 670M TRUMP had unlocked; only about 82M has actually been pulled since launch. For the next 90 days we project the same **44.45M TRUMP** as the last 90. That may be low: September alone brought 31M, and the claims we can read already hold about 64.6M of vested TRUMP that nobody has pulled yet.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book **0**, because every coin that can leave the vault is already counted in the vesting row above, and there is no separate foundation or treasury outside it. Long-term locks and bankruptcy also book **0**: no estate, trustee or court payout holds TRUMP.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

There is no programmatic buyback, so that row is **0**. In October 2025 the issuer was reported to be raising $200M to $1B for a treasury company that would buy TRUMP, but we found no sign that deal closed or bought a single coin; a buyer on the open market would, in any case, only move coins already in circulation.

The protocol fee burn is **0**. Trading fees on TRUMP are paid to the issuers, not destroyed. Holders have burned only about 1,075 TRUMP since launch, far too little to register over 90 days. The foundation-buy row is **0** as well: there is no foundation, and the creator side sells — after the Sep 18 2026 unlock date, team wallets sent about 8.4M TRUMP to an exchange within four days. New long-term locks add **0**, because TRUMP has no staking and coins in liquidity pools stay in the float.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang is the creator vesting vault, and it is enormous: **718.13M TRUMP**, about 2.5 times the 281.87M in circulation. We can read the terms of the four claims made so far. One is a 180M allocation that paid 45M on Jan 17 2026 and then vests evenly over two years; the others are smaller, 20M to 40M each. Across those four, about **64.6M TRUMP** has vested but not been pulled, and another 24.8M vests in the next 90 days. The remaining **540.13M TRUMP** sits in allocations that have never been claimed, so their dates stay hidden until the first pull.

On top of that, the team said in July 2026 that up to 96M TRUMP of already-unlocked supply may be used for partners, acquisitions, its holders' club and its mobile game. The wallet that took 31M TRUMP out of the vault in September still holds about 10.46M. We check the vault on-chain at every rebuild and the news by hand: if the vault's balance falls between checks, that outflow enters the vesting row at the next rebuild.

## How TRUMP compares to other celebrity and meme coins

Most large meme coins are fully in circulation from day one, with no team vault at all. For those coins supply only changes through burns, so their 90-day supply reading sits near zero, and price follows demand alone. TRUMP is built the other way round: the total is fixed, but only 28% of it trades today, so the float grows every time an insider pulls from the vault. That makes TRUMP look more like a venture-backed token with a long vesting cliff than like a fair-launched meme coin.

Against tokens that also unlock insider supply, TRUMP has two harder edges. It has no buyback and no fee burn to absorb anything, so every unlocked coin lands on the market side of the ledger. And its unlocks are pulled when the holders choose, not on a fixed calendar, which is why real outflow has run well below the paper schedule — leaving a large vested backlog that could arrive in bigger pieces.

Compared with a proof-of-work coin such as Bitcoin, where new supply is small, known years ahead and paid to thousands of miners, TRUMP's new float comes from a handful of wallets tied to the issuers. A 15.77% rise in 90 days is among the fastest supply growth of any large coin we track.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the vault balance, now 718.13M TRUMP: any drop is new supply, and the pace since Sep 1 2026 has been faster than the 90-day average we project. Second, the monthly unlock dates on public trackers, next around Oct 18 2026, Nov 18 2026 and Dec 18 2026 — they are paper dates, but team wallets have moved coins to exchanges soon after them. Third, the 180M allocation that started pulling on Sep 1 2026: it already has about 61.2M TRUMP vested and unclaimed. Fourth, the first claim from any of the 540.13M TRUMP in never-claimed allocations, which would show their schedule for the first time. Fifth, any sign that the proposed TRUMP treasury company actually raises money and buys coins.

## Summary

Official Trump (TRUMP) has a fixed 1 billion supply and can never be minted again, yet its tradable float grew **+15.77%** in the last 90 days as creator wallets pulled 44.45M TRUMP from their vesting vault, and we project the same for the next 90 days. Nothing works on the other side: no buyback, no fee burn, no staking. The key risk is the size of what is left — 718.13M TRUMP in the vault, including about 64.6M already vested and waiting. Until that vault is empty, expected in early 2028, TRUMP's supply keeps growing whenever its insiders decide to sell.

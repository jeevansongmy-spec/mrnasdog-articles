# TRUMP Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The tradable supply of Official Trump (TRUMP) is growing fast, even though no new TRUMP can ever be minted. In the 90 days to Oct 8 2026 the creators withdrew **44.45M TRUMP** from their on-chain vesting vault, nothing was bought back or burned, and the float grew by **+15.77%**. The inflation monitor reads **+18.76%**; the difference is only the base each side divides by. The ceiling is fixed at 1B TRUMP, but **718.13M** of it still sits in the creators' vault, waiting to be withdrawn.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework puts TRUMP at **+15.77%** net new supply over the last 90 days and **+15.77%** for the next 90 days, measured against the **281.87M** TRUMP that trade today. The inflation monitor shows **+18.76%**, a gap of **2.99 percentage points**. We traced it fully: both sides see the same withdrawals (about 44.45M TRUMP), but the monitor divides by the smaller supply of 90 days ago (about 237.4M), while we divide by today's. On that older base our own number would read +18.72%, within a few hundredths of the monitor. Because the gap is explained to the coin, no warning chip is shown. In one line: TRUMP is a fixed-cap meme coin whose float is still being filled from a large creator vault, and every withdrawal is new supply for the market.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

Protocol inflation is **zero** and will stay zero. TRUMP is a plain token on Solana, and the right to mint more was given up on-chain, so the total can never pass 1B TRUMP. There are no block rewards, no staking rewards and no emission programme of any kind.

Vesting unlocks are the whole story: **44.45M TRUMP** in the last 90 days. When TRUMP launched on Jan 17 2025, 200M coins went to the public and to trading pools, and the other 800M, owned by CIC Digital LLC (a Trump Organization affiliate) and Fight Fight Fight LLC, went into one vesting vault on-chain. Coins vest there on paper, but they only reach the market when someone withdraws them. Nothing at all left the vault until Jan 30 2026. In this window there were nine withdrawals, all signed by the same operator key: about **10.8M** on Jul 24 and Jul 25 2026, about **2.6M** on Aug 22 2026, and **31.0M** on Sep 1, Sep 6 and Sep 20 2026. The September coins went to a single wallet that passed most of them on within days, which news reports tied to exchange deposits. The vault fell from about 762.59M to **718.13M** TRUMP, and the withdrawals add up to that drop exactly.

For the next 90 days we book the same **44.45M TRUMP**, because the withdrawals keep coming every month and nothing in the rules changed. That is the careful middle. September alone moved 31.0M in under three weeks, and the four allocations we can read on-chain already hold about **67.06M** TRUMP that has vested but not been taken out, with another 24.77M vesting by early January 2027. If the September pace returns, the real number will be higher than ours.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book **zero**: every coin the team controls is inside the vault, so anything it releases is already counted as a vesting withdrawal above, and coins that already left the vault are not counted twice. Long-term locks and bankruptcy payouts are also **zero**; TRUMP has no estate, trustee or court process holding coins.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

There is no programmatic buyback: **zero**. Trading fees from the official pools go to the companies behind the coin, and no plan or contract to buy TRUMP back was announced or seen on-chain in these 90 days.

There is no protocol fee burn either: **zero**. The token has no burn built in. Holders have destroyed about 1,083 TRUMP by hand since launch, which is too small to register against a float of 281.87M.

A foundation buy books **zero**. In 2025 the team talked about a company that would hold TRUMP on its balance sheet, but no purchase was found in this window, and the team's wallets only sent coins out. A new long-term lock is also **zero**: TRUMP has no staking. The holders' contest for the Nov 22 2026 dinner rewards people for holding, but nothing is locked in a contract, so no coins leave the market.

## Foundation and overhang

TRUMP has one overhang, and it is large: the creators' vesting vault with **718.13M TRUMP**, about 72% of all coins ever made. We read its balance from the chain at every rebuild. Of that amount, about **67.06M** belongs to the four allocations that have already been claimed from and is vested but not yet withdrawn, and about **540.13M** belongs to allocations that have never been claimed, whose terms will only become readable on-chain after a first withdrawal. In July 2026 the team said it may use up to **96M** TRUMP over the coming months for partners, deals, a holders' club and a mobile game; those coins come from the same vault and will show up as withdrawals.

Two smaller piles sit outside the vault and are already counted as trading supply: the wallet that took September's withdrawals still holds about 6.14M TRUMP, and team wallets also hold positions in the trading pools, which they partly pulled during a price jump in August 2026. Moving those coins adds nothing new to the float. The rule we follow is simple: if the vault's balance falls between two checks, the outflow enters the sell side at the next refresh.

## How TRUMP compares to other meme coins

Most large meme coins have no insider vault at all. Dogecoin has no cap and pays miners a fixed reward with every block, so its supply grows by a steady, known amount each year, but nobody holds a hidden pile. Shiba Inu and Bonk are almost fully in circulation and rely on burns to take coins away. Against those, TRUMP is the opposite shape: it can never mint a single new coin, yet most of its supply is still held back by its creators and released at their pace.

That makes TRUMP closer to a newly launched project token with team vesting than to a classic meme coin. Coins like Pudgy Penguins' PENGU also release team and insider allocations on a schedule. The difference with TRUMP is the size of the share still held back, 72% of all coins, and the fact that withdrawals are not tied to fixed dates: the vault releases coins only when the operator withdraws, so the flow comes in bursts, from 2.6M in one month to 31.0M in the next.

A fixed cap only protects holders once the locked part is gone. Until the vault is empty, the cap mostly tells you where the dilution stops, not how fast it arrives.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the next vault withdrawal. Nothing has left the vault since Sep 20 2026; a new withdrawal of the September size would put TRUMP on track for well above our 44.45M forecast.

Second, the holders' contest: its scoring window runs from Sep 30 2026 to Nov 12 2026, and the dinner for the top 185 holders is on Nov 22 2026. It moves no supply by itself, but team selling around it would show up as withdrawals.

Third, the up-to-96M deployment the team announced in July 2026, and any sign that the long-discussed treasury company buys TRUMP, which would be the first buy-side flow this coin has had.

Fourth, a first claim from one of the never-claimed allocations, about 540.13M TRUMP in total. Its terms would become readable on-chain at that moment, and it could raise the monthly flow sharply.

## Sell pressure: where new TRUMP comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**, and it is zero by construction rather than by a quiet quarter. Reading the TRUMP mint account on Solana returns a mint authority of **null** and a freeze authority of **null**: no key exists anywhere that can issue another Official Trump token or freeze a holder's account, and on Solana a renounced mint authority cannot be restored by anyone, including the issuers. Supply reads **999,999,046** against the **1,000,000,000** minted at launch on **Jan 17 2025**, so roughly **954** TRUMP have been destroyed in the token's entire life. There is no protocol behind Official Trump — no validator subsidy, no staking emission, no liquidity programme — so this row is permanent.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **10.84M TRUMP**, and it is the entire page. The **800,000,000** creator allocation held by CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC sits in one readable on-chain vesting vault on Solana, so the framework applies its released-beats-scheduled rule and books what actually left the vault rather than what the calendar entitled. The vault held **762,585,959.66** at the start of the window and **751,748,850.07** at the end, a realised outflow of **10,837,109.59**. Every one of the vault's **291** lifetime transactions was enumerated rather than sampled, so nothing can hide between the two endpoints: the balance was flat from **May 11 2026** until a single claim batch on **Jul 24 2026** and **Jul 25 2026**, and flat again since. Those claims went to three recipient wallets that were forwarded on within minutes and hold **0 TRUMP** today, which is how we know the release reached the market rather than being reshuffled between insider addresses.

The gap between that figure and the published vesting calendar is the most important thing on this page. Unlock trackers quote a daily drip of roughly **904,000** to **909,000** TRUMP, about **28M** a month and around **82M** across a 90-day window — eight times what the vault actually released. That calendar figure is the entitlement accruing inside the vault, not supply reaching the market, and booking it would invent sell pressure that has not happened. The scale of the divergence is easy to state: of the **800,000,000** creator allocation, only **48,251,150** has ever been claimed, in nineteen months, and none of it in the first **378** days. The aggregators are unreliable here in both directions — one unlock tracker's page header reads "fully unlocked" while its own table shows **751,748,851** locked, and another serves a vesting tab listing allocations this token does not have alongside a next unlock larger than the entire supply.

The remaining sell rows are zero for checked reasons. Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0** — no public evidence of release in window. Official Trump has no foundation, no DAO and no treasury contract; the only entity-controlled balance in existence is the vault, which is Sell #2's source and is carried as an overhang below rather than counted twice. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**: there is no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution attached to Official Trump.

## Buy pressure: where new TRUMP goes

All four buy rows are **0**, and on Official Trump that is a structural fact rather than a slow quarter. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**: there is no revenue contract, no fee switch and no treasury that repurchases the token. The official site's only revenue language runs the opposite way — trading revenue flows to the affiliated entities, not back into the coin. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0** for the same reason the mint row is: network fees on Solana are paid in SOL, and Official Trump is a plain SPL token with nothing attached that could destroy a unit. The burn address holds **0 TRUMP**, and the **954** coins missing from the cap are incidental holder burns spread across nineteen months.

Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0** — no public evidence of release in window. No wallet connected to CIC Digital or Fight Fight Fight bought Official Trump on the open market during the window. The one thing that could have changed this was a **200 million dollar** to **1 billion dollar** corporate treasury vehicle announced in **Oct 2025** that would have accumulated TRUMP; it was never closed, never registered and never executed, and it has been silent since. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**: Official Trump has no staking contract and no lockup a holder could enter. The issuers run holder tiers that reward keeping the coin, but those tiers never take custody, so no supply is actually removed from the market. Every vehicle that might have absorbed TRUMP has stalled — a spot TRUMP exchange-traded fund registration has seen no filing since **Feb 13 2026**, and three related crypto fund registrations were withdrawn on **May 19 2026**.

## Foundation and overhang

There is exactly one team-controlled overhang on Official Trump, and it is enormous. The creator vesting vault holds **751,748,850.07 TRUMP** as of **Aug 18 2026** — **75.2%** of the entire supply, and just over three times the tradable float. It is a single on-chain address refreshed on every rebuild, so its balance is verifiable rather than disclosed. Two things make it heavier than a normal unfinished vest. First, the issuers stated on **Jul 15 2026** that roughly **67%** of total supply has already passed its vesting date, meaning most of the vault is claimable on demand and the pace is a decision rather than a schedule. Second, the same update earmarked up to **9.6%** of total supply, around **96M TRUMP**, for deployment into partnerships, acquisitions and holder programmes over the coming months — roughly **39%** of the current float. That is announced capacity, not observed flow, so the framework books it at **0** and watches it. The three wallets that receive vault claims hold **0 TRUMP** each and are refreshed alongside the vault. If the vault's balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #2 at the next refresh, and any residual left sitting in a recipient wallet enters Sell #3.

## How TRUMP compares to other capped memecoins

Official Trump belongs to the class of hard-capped memecoins with a large insider allocation, and within that class the cap is the least informative fact about it. Fair-launch memecoins such as Dogecoin's uncapped tail or the fully-distributed Solana memecoins have the opposite shape: their supply is either genuinely fixed and fully circulating, so the framework reads close to **0%**, or it inflates through a documented block subsidy that is predictable to the coin. Official Trump inflates through neither. Its cap is real and permanent, its mint is renounced, and yet its tradable float has grown **24%** since launch purely through distribution — from **200M** at day one to **248.3M** today.

The closer structural analogues are venture-backed tokens with multi-year cliff schedules — the class where a vesting contract, not a block reward, is the dominant supply force. Against those, Official Trump differs in two ways that both cut the same direction. A typical venture unlock schedule fires on published dates that a reader can diary, so the next 90 days is knowable; here the vault is claimed at the issuers' discretion, in irregular batches, and the calendar the trackers publish has borne no relationship to the outflow. And a typical venture-backed token has at least one buy-side mechanism — a fee burn, a staking sink, a revenue buyback — that offsets some fraction of the unlock. Official Trump has none of the four. The framework's buy total is not small on this coin; it is exactly zero, which means the net reading is the gross reading and there is nothing structural to lean against.

That is why the fixed cap should not be read as scarcity. A hard cap bounds where supply can end, not how fast it gets there, and with **751.7M** still to distribute against a **248.3M** float, the distance left to travel is far larger than the market that has to absorb it.

# CRO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

**CRO supply is growing fast: +7.43% in the last 90 days, and about +6.90% projected for the next 90.** The driver is the Cronos Strategic Reserve, a 70B CRO pool re-created in March 2025 that unlocks **1.17B CRO** every month until March 2030, which added **3.50B CRO** this window. Staking added **195.7M CRO** more, while the burn address took in only **4,500 CRO**; a **228M CRO** community burn voted on Oct 3 2026 trims the next window but does not change its direction. The protocol caps total supply at **100B CRO**, and **98.96B** already exists.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 2 2026, CRO added **3.70B** coins to a circulating supply of **49.76B**, a net change of **+7.43%**. Our monitor reads **+8.03%** for the same stretch, a gap of **0.60 percentage points**. Both readings count almost exactly the same new coins (3.70B); the monitor divides by the smaller supply of 90 days ago, while we divide by today's. On the same base the two agree to within 0.01 points, so no warning chip is shown. For the next 90 days we project **+6.90%**: three more reserve tranches, a slightly smaller staking mint, minus the 228M burn. In one line, CRO is **inflationary by schedule**: a monthly reserve unlock that no burn on record comes close to matching.

## Sell pressure: where new CRO comes from

**Protocol inflation added 195.7M CRO.** Every block on the Cronos POS chain mints new CRO for validators and the people who stake with them. The May 2026 v7 upgrade added a decay: the yearly rate now shrinks by 6.8% every month on its way toward zero. Inside this window it slid from 0.90% to 0.73% a year, and total supply read at both ends of the window rose by 195,672,357 CRO. Fifteen percent of each mint goes to the community pool instead of stakers. If the decay keeps its pace, the next 90 days mint about **159.7M CRO**.

**Vesting unlocks added 3.50B CRO, by far the biggest line.** In March 2025 Cronos governance re-created 70B CRO that had been burned in 2021 and placed it in a single Strategic Reserve account. That account vests in 60 equal monthly steps of 1.17B CRO, from March 2025 to March 2030. Three steps landed in this window, on Jul 17, Aug 17 and Sep 16 2026, and three more land on Oct 17, Nov 16 and Dec 16 2026. The reserve wallet then sends unlocked coins on to a spending wallet; it sent 4.67B CRO this window, which includes a June tranche that vested before the window began. CRO counts as circulating the day it vests, so the page books the 3.50B that vested, not the 4.67B that moved. After 18 steps, **49.0B CRO** is still locked.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero.** The coins the reserve has already unlocked sit in team wallets, but they were counted as circulating when they vested, so moving or selling them now adds nothing new. **Long-term locks and bankruptcy are zero too:** no estate or trustee holds CRO, and the only long lock is the reserve schedule itself.

## Buy pressure: where new CRO goes

**Programmatic buyback is zero for now.** Proposal 37, a signalling vote closing Oct 3 2026, commits all revenue from the Ult trading app and the Cronos Launch platform to buying CRO on the open market and burning it every month, with each transaction published on-chain. The buy-and-burn contract is still being built and no revenue figure has been published, so we count nothing until the first burn shows up on-chain.

**The burn removed 4,500 CRO in the last 90 days.** Cronos has no automatic fee burn; CRO is destroyed only when it is sent to a burn address nobody controls, which now holds 200.0M CRO from four earlier community burns. The next one is much larger: proposal 36 moves **228M CRO** from the community pool to that address. On Oct 2 2026 it had 48.5% of all staked CRO voting yes and nothing against, above the 33.4% quorum, and the transfer runs by itself when voting ends on Oct 3 2026. We count it in the next 90 days.

**Foundation buying is zero.** A planned CRO treasury company with Trump Media was called off on Aug 7 2026, and no team purchase is on record. **New long-term locks are zero as well.** Since the 2026 upgrades holders can lock staked CRO for 1, 2 or 4 years for a bonus on top of the base staking reward, and 14.47B CRO is staked in all, but staked and locked CRO still counts as circulating, so these locks take nothing off the market.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the Strategic Reserve itself: **49.0B CRO** still locked, released 1.17B at a time. That pile is already on the schedule above, and we read its balance from the chain at every rebuild. Behind it sit the wallets that receive unlocked reserve coins: the main spending wallet holds about **12.62B CRO** (8.45B at the start of the window, plus 4.67B in, minus a 500M transfer on Sep 13 2026), an older wallet holds 5.83B CRO and did not move, and the wallet that got the 500M still holds it. The community pool holds 235.2M CRO, almost all of which is due to be burned. Trump Media reports 756M CRO, of which 684M is under a sale lock that eases from Aug 26 2026 and ends in Aug 2029.

All of these balances are already counted as circulating, so none of them adds to the ledger when it moves. We still read the team wallets from the chain at every rebuild, and Trump Media's figure from its filings. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow is reviewed at the next refresh and booked under foundation and unscheduled unlocks wherever it is new supply.

## How CRO compares to other exchange-linked chains

CRO belongs to the family of tokens tied to a big exchange, and its supply story runs the opposite way to most of them. BNB burns coins every quarter by a set rule and also burns part of every gas fee, so its supply shrinks over time. OKB burned most of its supply in 2025 and fixed what was left. CRO went the other way: in 2021 it burned 70B coins, and in 2025 its governance re-created those same 70B coins and set them to unlock month by month. Today the reserve unlock is about eighteen times the staking mint, and nearly 800,000 times what the burn address took in this window.

Against other proof-of-stake chains built on the Cosmos stack, CRO's own minting is now small and falling: a decaying 0.73% a year, heading toward zero, compared with chains that keep a fixed floor rate forever. The difference is that those chains have no 49B CRO-sized unlock queue behind the mint. CRO also has a hard ceiling that most staking chains lack: **100B CRO**, enforced by the chain. With 98.96B already in existence, minting can add at most about 1.04B more, so once the reserve finishes unlocking in 2030 the supply story will be decided by burns rather than by issuance.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 3 2026:** proposal 36 ends and, if it passes as the tally shows, 228M CRO moves to the burn address the same day. Proposal 37, the revenue buy-and-burn and reserve-funded staking plan, closes at the same time.

**Oct 17, Nov 16 and Dec 16 2026:** Strategic Reserve tranches 19, 20 and 21 unlock, 1.17B CRO each. Watch how much the reserve sends on to its spending wallet, and whether that wallet sends coins on to exchanges.

**The first revenue burn:** once the buy-and-burn contract goes live, the monthly burns will start to show up at the burn address. Their size against the 1.17B monthly unlock is the number that matters.

**Staking funded by the reserve:** proposal 37 says the reserve will top up staking rewards as minting decays. That moves already-unlocked coins to stakers and does not change the vesting clock, but a governance change to the 6.8% decay or to the vesting account would change this ledger.

## Summary

CRO supply grew **+7.43%** in the 90 days to Oct 2 2026 and is projected to grow about **+6.90%** in the next 90, because the 70B Cronos Strategic Reserve unlocks 1.17B CRO every month until March 2030 and staking still mints a small, shrinking amount on top. Burns are tiny next to that: 4,500 CRO this window, and a one-off 228M community burn voted on Oct 3 2026. The key risk for holders is the 49.0B CRO still waiting to unlock, worth roughly the whole circulating supply again. The ceiling is fixed at 100B CRO, so issuance cannot run forever, but until the reserve finishes vesting, CRO stays inflationary by schedule.

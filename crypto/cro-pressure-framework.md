# CRO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

CRO supply is growing fast: between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026 the locked Cronos Strategic Reserve released **3.50B CRO** and stakers were paid **175.10M CRO** in new coins, while a community vote burned **228M CRO**, so net supply grew **+6.63%** in 90 days. The next 90 days bring three more reserve releases and a smaller staking payout, about **+7.01%**, with a hard cap of 100B CRO. Other trackers show **+7.20%** for the same window because of a low starting reading, not because of extra CRO.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 20 2026, **3.45B CRO** joined the circulating float, a net rise of **+6.63%**, and the next 90 days project **+7.01%**. Other trackers print **+7.20%**, a gap of **0.57 points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. Their Aug 22 2026 reading put circulating CRO about 43M lower than the chain did, and they count the rise from the smaller late-August float; neither is new CRO. On the chain itself, CRO is a strongly inflationary coin today, and almost all of that comes from one place: a 70B CRO reserve that opens a fixed slice every month until March 2030. Burns are real but small next to it.

## Sell pressure: where new CRO comes from

The first source is protocol inflation. The Cronos POS chain pays the people who stake CRO with newly created coins. From Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026 that payout added **175.10M CRO**. Since a chain upgrade on May 20 2026 the payout rate shrinks by **6.8%** every month, so the next 90 days should add only about **142.87M CRO**, and the payout keeps fading after that.

The second source, and by far the largest, is the Strategic Reserve. In March 2025 Cronos holders voted to re-create the **70B CRO** that had been burned in 2021 and place it in a locked account. That account opens **1.17B CRO** a month for five years. Three releases landed in this window, on Sep 16, Oct 17 and Nov 16 2026, for **3.50B CRO** in total. The next three are due on Dec 16 2026, Jan 16 2027 and Feb 15 2027, another **3.50B CRO**. On its own, each release adds a little over 2% to the circulating float.

The third row, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, books zero. The released reserve coins pass to one Cronos wallet that pays staking rewards and growth spending, and that wallet sent 500M CRO onward on Sep 13 2026. Those coins were already counted as circulating when they left the lock, so spending them adds nothing new. The fourth row also books zero: no court or bankrupt firm holds CRO for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new CRO goes

The largest sink in the window was a community burn. Part of every staking payout flows into a community pool, and holders vote from time to time to burn what has piled up. The vote that closed on Oct 3 2026 burned **228M CRO**, the biggest burn so far, bringing the lifetime total to 428M CRO. These burns come only by vote, with no fixed date, and the one before this came in March 2025, so none is counted for the next 90 days. The pool has refilled to about **20.89M CRO**.

A second sink started this autumn. Since an Oct 3 2026 vote, all revenue from the Ult trading app and Cronos Launch buys CRO on the open market once a month and burns it. The first burn, on Oct 30 2026, destroyed **1.79M CRO**, worth about $92K. Those coins went to a dead address on Cronos EVM that the published circulating figure still includes, so the buyback row books zero for now; it is tracked every month.

Cronos Labs does not buy CRO for a treasury, so the foundation buy row is zero. Staking, including the new locked staking tiers of one, two and four years, keeps coins counted as circulating, so the new long-term lock row is zero too, even with about 15.14B CRO staked.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the Strategic Reserve itself: **46.67B CRO** is still locked, and it leaves in equal monthly steps until March 2030. That schedule is already in the sell rows above, so it is a known flow rather than a surprise.

The second is the Cronos wallet that receives each release. It held about **14.95B CRO** on Nov 20 2026, close to 29% of all circulating CRO. Under a vote that passed on Oct 3 2026, this money pays staking rewards as the newly minted payout fades, and some is set aside for growth. Those coins already count as circulating, so they do not change the supply figure, but where they go next matters for the market. An older reserve wallet holds another **5.83B CRO** and did not move between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026. The community pool, at about 20.89M CRO, is the smallest pile.

## How CRO compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake Layer 1 coins grow through staking pay that runs year after year, and some take part of it back through a fee burn. Cronos is different in two ways. Its own staking pay is small and shrinking by design, heading toward zero over the next few years. But on top of it sits a scheduled reserve release, which behaves much more like a team or investor unlock on a young token than like staking pay on a mature chain.

Cronos also has a hard cap of 100B CRO, which most proof-of-stake chains do not. The catch is that today's 99.05B CRO already sits close to it, because the burned 70B CRO was re-created in 2025, so the cap limits new creation, not the release of coins that already exist. Its burns come from votes and from product revenue rather than from every transaction, so they arrive in steps rather than as a steady drip.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Dec 16 2026**: the next Strategic Reserve release of about 1.17B CRO, followed by **Jan 16 2027** and **Feb 15 2027**. Each one is fixed in the account's code and cannot be skipped.

**Late November and December 2026**: the second and third monthly revenue burns. The first was 1.79M CRO; a much larger burn would only matter if Ult and Cronos Launch revenue grows many times over.

**Any new burn vote**: the community pool refills by a few million CRO a month. A new proposal to burn it would show up on the chain two weeks before it takes effect.

**The 14.95B CRO payout wallet**: large transfers out of it, beyond staking rewards, would be the first sign of reserve coins reaching the market.

## Summary

CRO supply grew **+6.63%** between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026, and the next 90 days project **+7.01%**. Almost all of the new supply comes from the Strategic Reserve, which releases 1.17B CRO every month until March 2030, while the staking payout of new coins is small and shrinking by 6.8% a month. The 228M CRO community burn on Oct 3 2026 and the first 1.79M CRO revenue burn on Oct 30 2026 are real steps, but together they offset well under a tenth of one quarter's releases. Other trackers show **+7.20%** because of a low starting reading in late August, not because of extra CRO.

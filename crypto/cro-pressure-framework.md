# CRO Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

**CRO** supply is growing fast: the circulating count of the **Cronos** coin rose by a net **+7.43%** over the last 90 days and is projected to rise about **+7.36%** over the next 90. Almost all of it comes from one place — the **70B CRO Strategic Reserve**, which unlocks **1.17B CRO** every month until March 2030 — while staking rewards add a shrinking **197.16M CRO** and burns remove only **4,500 CRO**. The chain enforces a **100B CRO** cap, and about half of that is already circulating.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Sep 29 2026, **3.70B CRO** entered the circulating float and only **4,500 CRO** left it, a net change of **+7.43%** on a float of **49.75B CRO**. The next 90 days look almost the same at **+7.36%**: three more reserve unlocks are fixed in the vesting account, and staking issuance keeps falling slowly. The inflation monitor reads **+8.08%** for the same window, a gap of **0.65** percentage points. We walked every source class and the gap closes: about 0.60 points comes from the monitor dividing the same change by the smaller float it saw 90 days ago, and the last 0.06 points from its market-based supply estimate running slightly above the chain's own count. No warning chip is needed. In one line: CRO is a **scheduled-unlock coin, inflationary on the active float until 2030**.

## Sell pressure: where new CRO comes from

**Protocol inflation — 197.16M CRO.** The Cronos POS chain pays stakers in newly minted CRO. Its base rate is pinned at 1% a year, but since the v7 upgrade on May 20 2026 a decay of **6.8% a month** is applied on top, block by block. Over this window the rate fell from about **0.91%** to **0.74%** a year, and total supply rose by **197.16M CRO**, matching the chain’s own reward math to within a few thousand CRO. Delegators receive 85% of the new CRO and the community pool 15%; both count as circulating. Because the decay continues, the next 90 days should add about **160.76M CRO**.

**Vesting unlocks — 3.50B CRO.** In March 2025 Cronos governance re-created the 70B CRO that had been burned in 2021 and placed it in a single reserve account on the POS chain. The account releases **1.17B CRO** every 30.4 days for 60 months. Unlocks landed on Jul 17, Aug 17 and Sep 16 2026, and **3.5B CRO** actually left the reserve on Jul 21 and Aug 18 2026, so what was released matches what was scheduled. **49.0B CRO** is still locked. This one mechanism is about 95% of CRO's new supply.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — 0.** Every team-held wallet we can identify is already counted as circulating, so selling from it moves coins that were already in the float. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy — 0.** CRO has no bankruptcy estate or court schedule, and coins in the new one-to-four-year staking locks are already counted as circulating.

## Buy pressure: where new CRO goes

**Programmatic buyback — 0.** Cronos has no live buyback. A governance vote that closes on Oct 3 2026 asks holders to commit all revenue from the Ult trading app and Cronos Launch to monthly open-market buy-and-burns, but the buy-and-burn contract is still in development and no revenue figure has been published, so nothing is counted yet.

**Protocol fee burn — 4,500 CRO.** Transaction fees on Cronos are paid to validators, not burned. The only CRO destroyed in the window was **4,500 CRO** sent to the chain's burn address in July, which now holds about **200.0M CRO** from four earlier community burns. A second vote, also closing Oct 3 2026, would move **228M CRO** from the community pool to that burn address. If it passes, it would be the largest single CRO burn so far, but it would still equal only about six days of reserve unlocks.

**Foundation buy — 0.** No team or treasury bought CRO for the project in the window; the plan for a listed CRO treasury company was cancelled on Aug 7 2026. **New long-term lock — 0.** About **14.47B CRO** is staked, and holders can now lock for one, two or four years for a bonus, but staked and locked CRO still counts as circulating, so staking removes nothing from the float.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the reserve itself: **49.0B CRO** still locked, released on a fixed schedule we read from the chain every day. Unlocked reserve coins are swept to an operating wallet that now holds about **11.45B CRO**, up from 8.45B at the start of the window; it sent 500M CRO onward on Sep 13 2026. An older reserve wallet holds about **5.83B CRO** and did not move. The community pool holds about **234M CRO** and is spent only by governance, and the staking-bonus pot holds about **47.4M CRO**. A listed US company holds about **684M CRO** whose sale limit began to ease on Aug 26 2026.

All of these balances except the locked reserve are already inside the circulating count, so they add nothing new when they move. We re-read the on-chain wallets every day and the company holding every two weeks. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How CRO compares to other exchange-linked Layer 1s

Exchange-linked chains usually pair their coin with a burn. BNB runs a quarterly auto-burn plus a gas-fee burn, and its supply shrinks every quarter. CRO did the opposite in 2025: it reversed its own 2021 burn and turned the 70B coins into a 60-month unlock, so the dominant flow on Cronos is new supply from a scheduled reserve, not destruction from exchange profits.

Against uncapped proof-of-stake chains that pay stakers several percent a year, CRO's staking issuance is small and falling: about **0.74%** a year now, cut by 6.8% every month, under a hard **100B CRO** cap. The vesting schedule, not issuance, is what sets CRO's inflation — closer to a young token with a large team unlock than to a mature Layer 1.

The proposed revenue buy-and-burn would move Cronos toward the exchange-token model. To offset the reserve alone it would need to burn about **1.17B CRO** a month, far more than any burn Cronos has done so far.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 3 2026:** the votes on the 228M CRO community-pool burn and on the revenue buy-and-burn both close; a passed burn executes automatically and would lower the next-90-day figure from +7.36% to about +6.9%.

**Oct 17, Nov 16 and Dec 16 2026:** the next three reserve unlocks of **1.17B CRO** each.

**The buy-and-burn contract:** whether it goes live, and the first published monthly burn with its transaction hashes.

**Governance changes to the decay or the reserve:** the decay rate is adjustable by vote, and the revenue proposal would also use the reserve to fund staking rewards — which changes who receives the unlocked coins, not when they unlock.

## Summary

CRO is firmly inflationary: net supply grew **+7.43%** in the 90 days to Sep 29 2026 and is projected to grow **+7.36%** in the next 90. The driver is the 70B CRO Strategic Reserve, which unlocks **1.17B CRO** a month until March 2030, while staking issuance of about **0.74%** a year is decaying and burns are close to zero. The key risk for holders is that **49.0B CRO** — almost as much as the whole circulating float — is still waiting to unlock. The ceiling is the **100B CRO** cap, and only a large, working buy-and-burn would change the direction.

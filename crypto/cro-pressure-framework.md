# CRO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the Cronos coin page for the short answer (should you buy CRO?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/cro](https://mrnasdog.com/research/cro)

CRO supply is growing fast, and the main reason is a schedule, not a market. The Cronos Strategic Reserve — 70B CRO re-created by a March 2025 vote — unlocks **1.17B CRO** every month until March 2030. In the last 90 days three tranches added **3.50B CRO** and staking minted **190.9M**, while a community vote burned **228.0M**. Net, Cronos supply grew **+6.99%** in 90 days, and we project **+7.38%** for the next 90. The monitor reads **+7.52%**; the difference is how the two numbers are divided, not a missing flow. The 100B CRO cap is far away, but 49.0B CRO is still waiting in the reserve.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 13 2026, CRO added **3,690.9M** coins to the float and took **228.0M** out, a net of **+6.99%** on 49.55B circulating. The inflation monitor reads **+7.52%** over its own 90 days, a gap of **0.53** percentage points. We checked it line by line: the monitor counts almost exactly the same flow (+3,464.6M against our +3,462.9M) but divides it by the supply of 90 days ago, while we divide by today's supply. On the same base the two agree within **0.005** points, so no warning chip is shown. The next 90 days look the same: **+7.38%**, because three more reserve tranches are due and no burn is scheduled. CRO is inflationary by schedule: a large locked reserve released in equal monthly steps.

## Sell pressure: where new CRO comes from

**Protocol inflation: 190.9M CRO.** Cronos POS pays stakers with newly minted CRO every block. Since the v7 upgrade on May 20 2026 that reward rate shrinks by 6.8% a month, so the yearly rate slid from 0.88% to 0.72% during this window. On-chain supply rose by 190.87M CRO between the two ends of the window, and the reward rate, applied block by block, gives the same figure to within a few thousand coins. Because the decline is written into the chain, we project the next 90 days from the same curve: about **155.8M CRO**.

**Vesting unlocks: 3.50B CRO.** This is the row that decides the page. The Strategic Reserve sits in one vesting account that releases sixty equal tranches of 1.17B CRO, one roughly every 30 days, from April 2025 to March 2030. Tranches unlocked on Jul 17, Aug 17 and Sep 16 2026. The team swept them out of the reserve on Jul 21, Aug 18 and Sep 30 2026, along with one June tranche that had unlocked before the window — 4.67B CRO left the account in total. The June coins were already counted as circulating the day they unlocked, so only the three new tranches, **3.50B CRO**, are new supply here. The next three unlock on **Oct 17, Nov 16 and Dec 16 2026**, another 3.50B CRO.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** Unlocked reserve coins land in team wallets, and those wallets are already counted as circulating, so spending from them moves coins between holders without adding supply. **Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No bankruptcy estate or court-ordered release holds CRO; the reserve is the only long lock, and it is covered by the vesting row.

## Buy pressure: where new CRO goes

**Protocol fee burn: 228.0M CRO.** 15% of every new CRO is set aside in the Cronos POS community pool, and from time to time holders vote to burn it. The fifth such vote passed on Oct 3 2026 with no votes against, and 228.0M CRO moved from the pool to the burn address that day. That brings all CRO burned this way to 428.0M. The burns are rare — the one before was in March 2025 — so we count none for the next 90 days, even though about 23M CRO should build up in the pool.

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** A second vote on Oct 3 2026 committed all revenue from two Cronos products to buying CRO on the market and burning it every month. The contract that will do the buying is still being built and no revenue figure is published, so nothing has been bought yet. When it starts, the coins will go to the same burn address, which we read on-chain. **Foundation buy: 0.** No team or treasury bought CRO in this window, and the company that was meant to buy CRO for its treasury was called off in August 2026. **New long-term lock: 0.** Stakers can now lock CRO for one, two or four years for a bonus, and about 14.47B CRO is staked, but staked coins still count as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the reserve itself: **49.0B CRO** still locked, released at 1.17B a month until March 2030. That is about the same size as the whole circulating supply today, which is why CRO will keep printing new float for years. We read the reserve balance on-chain every day.

Once unlocked, reserve coins collect in team wallets. The main one holds **12.62B CRO**, up 4.17B in 90 days after it took in four tranches and sent 500M out on Sep 13 2026. An older team wallet holds 5.83B CRO and did not move. The community pool now holds about 10.4M CRO after the burn, and a staking-bonus pot holds about 45.9M. Trump Media reported 756.1M CRO on Jun 30 2026, most of it under a sale limit that eases in steps until August 2029. All of these are already inside the circulating count, so we track them as overhang rather than as new supply. If any of their balances fall between our checks, the outflow enters the foundation row at the next refresh.

## How CRO compares to other exchange-linked chains

Most exchange-linked chain tokens are built to shrink. Their exchanges buy coins back or burn a share of profits on a set timetable, so the buy side of the ledger is large and predictable. Cronos is the opposite shape today: its burn is a rare community vote and its revenue buyback has not started, while its sell side is a fixed monthly unlock from a reserve that is almost as big as the float.

Against general-purpose Layer 1 chains, CRO's staking inflation is small and falling — under 1% a year and shrinking 6.8% a month — which is lower than most proof-of-stake chains. The difference is the vesting row. A chain whose supply is fully unlocked only has to absorb its staking rewards; Cronos has to absorb about 14.0B CRO of reserve unlocks over the next year on top of them.

The 100B CRO cap is real and written into the chain, so CRO cannot grow without limit. But the cap is not what matters for the next few years. What matters is how fast the locked 49.0B CRO turns into circulating coins, and on the current schedule that is steady, monthly and known in advance.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Oct 17 2026:** the next reserve tranche of 1.17B CRO unlocks, followed by **Nov 16 2026** and **Dec 16 2026**. Each one adds about 2.4% to the float.

**The first revenue burn.** The monthly buy-and-burn approved on Oct 3 2026 starts once its contract is live. The first published buy, and its size against 1.17B CRO a month of unlocks, is the number to watch.

**Reserve-funded staking rewards.** The same vote lets the reserve top up staking rewards as minting declines. Coins already unlocked would pay stakers, which moves coins inside the float and adds no new supply, but a change to the reserve schedule itself would change this page.

**Another community burn vote.** About 23M CRO should collect in the community pool by early January 2027; a new burn vote would add it to the buy side.

## Summary

CRO supply grew **+6.99%** in the 90 days to Oct 13 2026 and is projected to grow **+7.38%** in the next 90, because the 70B Cronos Strategic Reserve releases 1.17B CRO every month until March 2030. Staking adds a smaller and shrinking amount, and a rare community vote burned 228.0M CRO on Oct 3 2026. The key risk is the 49.0B CRO still locked in the reserve, which keeps arriving on a fixed timetable. The 100B CRO cap sets the ceiling, but until a revenue buyback grows large, unlocks will set the pace.

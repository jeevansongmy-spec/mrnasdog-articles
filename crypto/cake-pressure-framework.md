# CAKE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking

CAKE, the coin of PancakeSwap (renamed Pancake on Nov 18 2026), is **deflationary**: each week the exchange spends part of its trading fees buying CAKE and burns every coin it buys, and the governance cap on all CAKE is **400M**. From Aug 26 to Nov 24 2026 the farm contract paid out **1,609,512 CAKE** and holders took **748,201 CAKE** out of old locks, while buybacks and side products burned **5,961,668 CAKE**, so supply shrank by **1.14%**, close to the **−1.06%** other trackers show.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 24 2026, CAKE's circulating supply fell by about **1.14%**, and on today's rates it falls by another **0.57%** in the next 90 days. Other trackers show **−1.06%** for the same stretch, only **0.08 points** apart, so the coin page carries no warning note. The verdict is **Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking**. The shrink is slowing, though: the buyback lost a large part of its income when most trading fees moved to liquidity providers in November 2026, while the farm payouts have not yet come down.

## Sell pressure: where new CAKE comes from

Protocol inflation added **1,609,512 CAKE**. PancakeSwap's farm contract creates new CAKE with every block and keeps a small, fixed share for farms, product rewards and the team's ecosystem growth fund, which took the biggest part, **1,036,236 CAKE**. The same contract prints far more, about **768M CAKE** in the window, and burns it within days; those coins never reach a wallet, so they add nothing and remove nothing. A vote that closed on Oct 24 2026 approved stepping payouts down over the coming months, but the rate was unchanged on Nov 24 2026, so the next 90 days carry the same **1.61M CAKE**.

Vesting unlocks added **0**. CAKE launched in September 2020 with no presale and no team or investor share, so nothing is waiting on a schedule.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **0**. The ecosystem growth fund spends from coins that were counted when the farm contract paid them in, so its spending creates no new CAKE.

Long-term locked coins added **748,201 CAKE**. PancakeSwap retired its staking and voting locks in 2025, and holders can take their CAKE out whenever they like; until they do, those coins are not counted as circulating. One wallet pulled **573,945 CAKE** on Aug 26 2026, and hundreds of smaller holders took out the rest. Only the steady small withdrawals, about **174,288 CAKE**, are carried into the next 90 days, because one large exit says nothing about when the next one comes.

## Buy pressure: where new CAKE goes

The programmatic buyback removed **5,955,597 CAKE**. Part of each trading fee buys CAKE on the open market, and the coins are sent to the burn address every week, where anyone can see them. Those purchases cost about **$12.86M** across the window. The run of weekly burns broke once: the Nov 9 2026 burn was held back for a security upgrade of the buyback contracts and went through in full on Nov 16 2026.

The buyback is smaller from here. Under the plan called The Flip, the people who supply trading pairs keep 88% of fees on the newer pools, up from about two thirds, and nine of PancakeSwap's eleven chains had switched by Nov 12 2026. The first full week after that, Nov 16 to Nov 22 2026, bought **280,493 CAKE**. At that pace and today's price, the next 90 days burn about **3.59M CAKE**, well below the last 90.

The protocol fee burn removed **6,071 CAKE**, from side products sending CAKE straight to the burn. Since a June 2026 vote most of their fees go to the treasury, so this stays small.

Foundation buying removed **0**: the team buys CAKE only through the fee buyback, and all of it is burned. New long-term locks removed **0**, because no new locks have opened since 2025.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest team-run balance is the ecosystem growth fund, a multi-signature wallet holding **6.08M CAKE** on Nov 24 2026, up from **5.04M** on Aug 26 2026. It sent out only **3,000 CAKE** in the window. Under The Flip its stream of new CAKE becomes the Pantry, a fund the team can spend on marketing, partner deals and new pools, so we watch this wallet for outflows.

Holders' own coins in the retired lock contracts are the second overhang: **5.14M CAKE** in the old voting contract and **10.63M CAKE** in the old staking pool's locked balance. The buyback contracts held about **42,925 CAKE** waiting for the next weekly burn. The treasury that receives the 12% protocol fee does not publish its CAKE balance.

## How CAKE compares to other DEX tokens

CAKE is one of the few exchange tokens whose supply is falling because of its own fee income. Many exchange tokens send fees to stakers or to a treasury and leave supply untouched; CAKE turns a slice of trading fees into a weekly burn and has done so for more than three years, with October 2026 the 38th month in a row of net shrinkage according to the team's own reports.

The trade-off is now visible. The Flip pays liquidity providers more to bring deeper pools, which gives the burn less to work with on most chains. BNB Chain, which supplies most of the burn, keeps its old split until the final switch, so CAKE should keep shrinking, only more slowly than in September and October 2026.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Every week:** the buyback and burn. The weeks after Nov 23 2026 show how much buying the remaining fee share really funds.

**The emission step-down:** The Flip cuts the flow of new CAKE in stages as chains switch. No date is set, and a cut would lower the sell side straight away.

**Solana and BNB Chain fee switches:** Solana has yet to move to the 88% split. BNB Chain is planned to switch last, once total supply is about 20% below its April 2025 level; until then it keeps feeding the burn.

**The ecosystem growth fund:** it holds 6.08M CAKE and becomes the Pantry. A large outflow would not add new CAKE, but it would show the team spending.

**The mobile app:** Pancake said on Nov 18 2026 it would ship an app within weeks. More trading means more fees, and more fees mean a bigger weekly buy.

## Summary

CAKE's supply fell **1.14%** over the 90 days to Nov 24 2026, with weekly fee buybacks burning **5.96M CAKE** against **1.61M** paid to farms and the growth fund and **748K** taken out of old locks.

The next 90 days still point down, by about **0.57%**, because the buyback outweighs new CAKE even after The Flip cut its income.

CAKE has no vesting and a 400M governance cap, and every burned coin sits at a public burn address.

The two numbers that decide the next quarter are the size of the weekly buyback and the date the farm payouts finally step down.

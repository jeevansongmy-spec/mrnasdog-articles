# ASTER Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

ASTER supply was close to flat over the last 90 days, but it is set to grow over the next 90. Stakers on Aster received **6.75M ASTER** of new coins between Aug 5 and Nov 3 2026, and nothing took coins off the market, so tradable supply rose **+0.27%**. The next 90 days add the vested half of the last airdrop round, about **63.38M ASTER** on Nov 4 2026, which lifts the forecast to **+2.71%**. Other trackers show **−6.67%** for the same window because of a one-day recount on Oct 23 2026, not because coins left the market.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 5 to Nov 3 2026, ASTER's tradable supply grew by a net **+0.27%**, from new staking rewards alone. Other trackers report **−6.67%** for the same window, a gap of **6.94 percentage points**. The gap comes from a single day: on Oct 23 2026 the supply figure those trackers use dropped by **210.02M ASTER**, when coins burned since launch stopped being counted as circulating. Those coins were burned earlier, mostly from the locked team wallet, and none of them was taken off the market in this window. Because the gap is that large, the coin page carries a warning note that explains it. Looking forward, ASTER moves from **+0.27%** to about **+2.71%** in the next 90 days, almost all of it from one airdrop claim. In one line: ASTER is a perpetual-futures exchange token with a large burn that touches only locked coins, so the burn does not offset the coins that reach the market.

## Sell pressure: where new ASTER comes from

**Protocol inflation: 6.75M ASTER.** Aster pays stakers 450,000 ASTER a week, 150,000 in base rewards and 300,000 in loyalty rewards, out of the ecosystem and community pool. The pool does not pay out every day. It sends blocks of **2.25M ASTER**, five weeks of rewards at a time, and three of those blocks left the pool in the window: on Aug 17, Sep 21 and Oct 26 2026. The pool fell from 1.24495B to 1.2382B ASTER, exactly the 6.75M that was sent out. On the same five-week rhythm, two more blocks fall inside the next 90 days, around Nov 30 2026 and Jan 4 2027, so the forecast for this row is **4.5M ASTER**.

**Vesting unlocks: 0 last, 63.38M ASTER next.** The Aster airdrop runs in stages, and each stage gave users a choice: take half the coins at once, or wait six months and take the full amount. Stage 6, the last announced round, held **64M ASTER**. Its quick-claim half was settled on May 4 2026, and the full vested claim opens on **Nov 4 2026** and runs to Dec 4 2026. On the two earlier rounds, the locked airdrop wallet released the whole vested remainder on the day the claim opened: 118.49M ASTER on Apr 28 2026 for Stage 4 and 95.09M ASTER on Jun 9 2026 for Stage 5. Stage 6 follows the same pattern, so about 63.38M ASTER enter the market on Nov 4 2026. The team allocation adds nothing in either window: its cliff was moved a full year, from Sep 17 2026 to Sep 17 2027.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0.** Apart from the staking blocks above, none of the five locked wallets released a coin in the window. The treasury has not moved since launch, and no new airdrop round has been announced after Stage 6.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** There is no court estate or bankruptcy holding of ASTER waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new ASTER goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** Since Jun 17 2026, 99% of the trading fees Aster earns each day buy ASTER on the open market, and a 50,000 USDT fee on each new spot listing buys more. This is a real buyer, about 26.68M ASTER over the 90 days, the same amount as the burn below. But every bought coin is handed to veASTER stakers as loyalty rewards each week. The coins go out of sellers' hands and straight into holders' hands, so the buyback moves coins around the market without shrinking it.

**Protocol fee burn: 0.** For every ASTER bought back, Aster burns one more every two weeks, and it takes those coins from the locked team wallet first. Eight burns ran in the window, from Aug 10 to Nov 2 2026, for a total of **26.68M ASTER**; the latest, on Nov 2 2026, was 2.6M. The team wallet fell from 391.77M to 365.09M ASTER by exactly that amount. Total ASTER ever burned now stands at **212.69M**, and the burn is set to continue until total supply reaches 3B. Because the burned coins were never on the market, the burn shrinks the locked team pile and the total supply, but not the coins people can trade, so this row books 0.

**Foundation buy: 0.** No separate buying by the team or the treasury was found. The fee buyback is the only buyer the protocol runs.

**New long-term lock: 0.** ASTER can be locked for up to four years to earn more loyalty rewards, but locked coins still count as circulating, so a growing lock takes nothing out of the count.

## Foundation and overhang

About two thirds of all ASTER sits in five locked wallets. The airdrop wallet holds the largest share, **2.92B ASTER**; after the Stage 6 claim it drops to about 2.85B, and no later round has a date. The ecosystem and community pool holds **1.24B ASTER** and pays out staking rewards in 2.25M blocks. The treasury holds **560M ASTER**, untouched since launch and, under the project's rules, released only through governance. The team wallet holds **365.09M ASTER**, locked until Sep 17 2027 and burned a little every two weeks. A fifth reserve wallet holds **198.3M ASTER** with no public role.

Outside those five, a payout relay that passes staking blocks along holds about 313.6M ASTER that already count as circulating. If any of their balances falls between rebuilds in a way the schedule above does not explain, the outflow enters the foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next rebuild.

## How ASTER compares to other perpetual-exchange tokens

ASTER belongs to the group of exchange tokens that turn trading fees into buying. The best-known model in that group sends fee buybacks into a fund that keeps the coins, or burns them, so every dollar of fees takes coins off the market for good. ASTER takes a different route: the buyback is paid back out to stakers, and the burn comes from a locked team allocation instead of from the market. The result looks deflationary in total supply, which falls with every burn, but on the tradable float the effect is neutral. A reader comparing ASTER with another perpetual exchange should compare the coins that leave the market, not the headline burn.

ASTER also differs in where its new coins come from. Many exchange tokens finished their airdrops at launch and now release supply mainly through team and investor vesting. ASTER ran six airdrop stages, each with a six-month vested option, so its biggest supply days are claim days: Apr 28 2026, Jun 9 2026 and now Nov 4 2026. With the team cliff pushed to Sep 17 2027, staking rewards and these claims are the only new supply for the coming year.

**Every two weeks, next around Nov 16 2026:** the buyback-and-burn cycle; each burn shows how much fee money is buying ASTER, and each one shrinks the locked team wallet.

**Any new airdrop round:** Stage 6 is the last one announced; a seventh stage would draw on the 2.85B left in the airdrop wallet and could change the forecast quickly.

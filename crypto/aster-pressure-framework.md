# ASTER Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

## The verdict, in one paragraph

On-chain, Aster's unlocked supply rose **+2.64%** in the 90 days to Nov 21 2026, and our reading for the next 90 days is **+0.26%**. Other trackers show **−4.45%** for the same 90 days, a difference of **7.09 points**, so the ASTER coin page carries a warning note. The difference is not a supply flow. On Aug 23 2026 the figure those trackers use still counted about 189M burned ASTER as if they were on the market, and on Oct 23 2026 it dropped about 210M burned coins in one step. Those coins were burned months earlier, so the fall is a change in counting, not coins leaving the market. The warning clears on its own around Jan 21 2027, once that step is older than 90 days. Our label for ASTER right now: **a shrinking total, a growing float**.

## Sell pressure: where new ASTER comes from

Staking pay added **4.5M ASTER**. Aster Chain pays its stakers 450,000 ASTER a week from the ecosystem pool, and the pool tops up those payouts in blocks of 2.25M ASTER about every 35 days. Two blocks landed in the window, on Sep 21 and Oct 26 2026. Three more fall in the next 90 days, around Nov 30 2026, Jan 4 2027 and Feb 8 2027, so our next reading books **6.75M ASTER** of staking pay.

The Stage 6 airdrop released **63.38M ASTER** on Nov 4 2026. Stage 6 was the trading-points round where traders could take half their reward in May 2026 or wait six months for all of it. The coins for everyone who waited left the airdrop pool on Nov 4 2026, and those traders can claim them in the app until Dec 4 2026. This one release is more than 90% of all new ASTER in the window. No Stage 7 or later round has been announced, so the airdrop row books nothing for the next 90 days.

The Foundation and unscheduled row is **0**. The treasury's 560M ASTER have not moved since launch, and none of the other locked pools released coins beyond the staking pay and the Stage 6 airdrop. The bankruptcy row is also **0**: no court or failed firm holds ASTER for a later payout, and the $4.56M a user lost through a leaked trading key on Nov 13 2026 was in dollar coins and bitcoin, not new ASTER.

## Buy pressure: where new ASTER goes

The buyback row is **0**, even though the buyback is real. Since Jun 17 2026, 99% of the trading fees Aster earns each day buy ASTER on the market, about **27.31M ASTER** between Aug 23 and Nov 21 2026. Every bought coin is then paid out to people who lock ASTER for staking, so the coins change hands and stay on the market. The buyback supports the price as a steady buyer, but it does not shrink the unlocked supply.

The burn row is **0** for the same kind of reason. For each ASTER bought back, Aster burns the same amount from its locked team share every two weeks. Eight burns in the window destroyed **27.31M ASTER**, the latest on Nov 16 2026, and total supply is now 7.78B, down from 8B at launch. Those team coins were locked and never counted as unlocked, so burning them lowers the total but leaves the float where it was. The burns continue until total supply reaches 3B.

The Foundation buy row is **0**: the treasury did not buy ASTER. The new long-term lock row is also **0**. ASTER locked for staking, for up to four years, still counts as unlocked, and stakers who leave early pay a penalty that goes back to other stakers and the ecosystem pool rather than to a burn.

## Foundation and overhang

Most ASTER still sits in pools the project controls. The airdrop pool holds **2.85B ASTER** and only moves when a new round is paid. The ecosystem pool holds **1.24B ASTER** and now only pays stakers. The treasury holds **560M ASTER**, locked until a governance decision uses it. The team share holds **361.6M ASTER**, locked until Sep 17 2027 and then due to unlock 10M a month, although the burns keep shrinking it first. An unused part of the exchange-listing pool holds **198.3M ASTER**. One more payout wallet holds **313.6M ASTER** left from the early ecosystem release of Oct 2025 to Jan 2026; those coins already count as unlocked.

We read these balances on-chain at every rebuild. If any of these pools falls between rebuilds, the coins that left go into the Foundation and unscheduled row at the next rebuild.

## How ASTER compares to other exchange-token chains

ASTER sits with exchange tokens that turn trading fees into buying. Many of them, such as Hyperliquid's HYPE, send the bought coins to a fund that holds them, which takes the coins off the market. Others, such as BNB, burn coins that were already in circulation. Aster does neither with the coins it buys: it hands them to stakers, and it burns locked team coins instead. That makes ASTER's total supply fall fast while its unlocked supply does not fall at all.

Like most young exchange tokens, ASTER still has a large locked share. About two thirds of all ASTER is in project pools, so the real swing factor is how fast the airdrop and ecosystem pools are paid out. In the last 90 days a single airdrop round was worth about fourteen times the staking pay. A coin like BNB, whose coins are almost all unlocked, has no such step changes; ASTER will keep having them for as long as new airdrop rounds come.

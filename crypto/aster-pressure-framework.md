# ASTER Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the Aster coin page for the short answer (should you buy ASTER?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/aster](https://mrnasdog.com/research/aster)

ASTER supply grew over the last 90 days, and the pace should slow sharply in the next 90. Between Aug 13 and Nov 11 2026, **70.13M ASTER** reached the market: **63.38M** from the last airdrop round and **6.75M** as staking pay. Fee buybacks took **0** off the market, so the net change is **+2.73%**, with about **+0.26%** expected next. Other trackers show **−4.49%**, and the coin page carries a warning note explaining why.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

ASTER is inflating, but most of it came in one day. On Nov 4 2026 the airdrop reserve released the Stage 6 rewards, and that single release is nine tenths of the 90-day total. The net result is **+2.73%** on the **2.57B** ASTER on the market. With no further airdrop round announced and the team share locked until Sep 17 2027, the next 90 days should add only the staking pay, about **+0.26%**. Other trackers show **−4.49%** because the circulating figure they use counted about 189M already-burned ASTER at the start of the window and then removed about 210M burned coins at once on Oct 24 2026. That was a fix to the count, not coins leaving the market, so the coin page carries a warning note. Our label: supply was growing, and the trend is cooling.

## Sell pressure: where new ASTER comes from

Staking pay is the steady source: Aster pays stakers **450,000 ASTER** a week, 150,000 as base pay to everyone who stakes with a validator and 300,000 as loyalty pay that grows with how long a holder locks. The coins come from the ecosystem pool, which sends them out in blocks of 2.25M every five weeks. Three blocks landed in the window, on Aug 17, Sep 21 and Oct 26 2026, for **6.75M ASTER**. The same rhythm points to three more by early February 2027.

The airdrop release is the big one. Aster gave out ASTER in six trading-rewards rounds. In the last one, Stage 6, traders could take half their reward in May 2026 and give up the rest, or wait six months for the full amount. The waiting group's share, **63.38M ASTER**, left the airdrop reserve on Nov 4 2026, and it can be claimed until Dec 4 2026. Aster has described Stage 6 as its final round based on trading, so no further release from this reserve is planned in the next 90 days.

Team vesting adds nothing yet. The team holds 5% of the original 8B supply. Its first monthly unlock was due on Sep 17 2026, but Aster pushed it back a full year, to Sep 17 2027. The team share has stayed fully locked since the token launched, so the vesting row is the airdrop release alone.

No hack, court case or failed firm is holding ASTER that could be paid out later, so that row is zero.

## Buy pressure: where new ASTER goes

The fee buyback is large, but it is not a sink. Aster spends **99%** of its trading fees buying ASTER, spread across each day. Over the 90 days that was about **23.83M ASTER**. The catch is where those coins go: every one is added to the stakers' loyalty pay in the next weekly round. So the buyback is a real buyer in the market, but the coins it buys return to holders within days, and the amount on the market does not shrink, so this row is zero.

The burn is real, but it hits locked coins. For each coin bought back, Aster burns the same amount from the locked team share. Seven burns between Aug 24 and Nov 2 2026 destroyed **23.83M ASTER**, and the total supply is now **7.79B**, down from 8B at launch. Those coins were never on the market, though, so the tradable supply stays the same. The burn shrinks the future team unlock instead: the team share is down to 365.09M, and the burns continue until total supply reaches 3B.

No project wallet buys ASTER beyond the fee buyback. Staked ASTER, even on a four-year lock, still counts as circulating, so more staking removes nothing from the count. Both rows are zero.

## Foundation and overhang

Two thirds of all ASTER still sits in five project wallets. The airdrop reserve holds **2,853.86M**, kept for future community rewards with no round announced. The ecosystem pool holds **1,238.2M** and pays the staking rewards. The treasury holds **560M**, untouched since launch and released only by a governance decision. The team share holds **365.09M**, locked until Sep 17 2027 and shrinking with every burn. One more reserve holds **198.3M** and has not moved. None of these wallets sent coins to the market in the window apart from the staking pay and the Stage 6 release, and none has a release date. That is about **5.22B ASTER** waiting, twice the coins on the market today, so any new rewards round would matter far more than the weekly staking pay.

## How ASTER compares to other perpetual exchange tokens

Most exchange tokens that buy themselves back do it to take coins away. Hyperliquid's fee fund has kept the HYPE it buys, so those coins leave the market. ASTER takes a different route: its buyback is a reward to stakers, and the burn comes from locked team coins. That makes staking pay generous and shrinks the future team unlock, but it does not shrink today's supply.

ASTER also still has most of its supply ahead of it. Exchange tokens that launched with a large community share often release it over several years. For ASTER, the pace now depends on Aster's own choices about future rewards rounds, the treasury and the team share, rather than on a fixed curve. Inflation from staking alone is small, about a quarter of a percent each quarter.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The Stage 6 claim window closes on Dec 4 2026. Aster says unclaimed airdrop coins go back to the community reserve. Any coins that return would come off the market again, which would lower the count.

The staking pay blocks should land about Nov 30 2026, Jan 4 2027 and Feb 8 2027, each adding about 2.25M ASTER. A bigger block, or a faster rhythm, would mean the staking pay has been raised.

The buyback and burn report comes every two weeks. The latest, on Nov 2 2026, burned about 2.60M ASTER. Bigger reports mean more fees, and more pay for stakers, but they still do not shrink the coins on the market unless Aster changes where the bought coins go.

A new rewards round is the big unknown. The airdrop reserve still holds 2,853.86M ASTER. A new round would be the one thing that could push supply growth back above the last quarter's pace.

## Summary

ASTER supply grew **+2.73%** in the 90 days to Nov 11 2026, mostly from the 63.38M ASTER Stage 6 airdrop release on Nov 4 2026.

Aster's fee buyback bought about 23.83M ASTER, but it pays those coins to stakers, and the matching burn comes from locked team coins, so neither takes ASTER off the market.

With no airdrop round announced and the team share locked until Sep 17 2027, the next 90 days should add only about 6.75M ASTER of staking pay, near **+0.26%**.

About 5.22B ASTER still waits in project wallets, so the real question for ASTER supply is what Aster does with its airdrop reserve, treasury and team share.

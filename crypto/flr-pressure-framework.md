# FLR Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Supply is growing and is projected to keep growing. The Pressure Framework records **890.0M FLR** entering the circulating boundary against **33.4M FLR** of removals, giving **+0.99%** over the trailing 90 days and **+1.05%** projected forward; the monitor reads **+0.35%**. Flare has continuing issuance, but counting every transfer to the burn address as a removal from circulating supply would overstate the offset.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The framework reads **+0.99%** trailing inflation on the **86,838.8M FLR** circulating base. The monitor’s **+0.35%** is **0.64 percentage points** lower, so a warning remains on the overview. This refresh separates newly spendable rewards, movement between excluded reserve pools and destruction of rewards that never reached circulation. The remaining difference is not filled with an invented treasury distribution or a larger burn. Flare is a continuously issuing network with a second supply stream from vested ecosystem incentives.

## Sell pressure: where new FLR comes from

Protocol inflation contributes **629.9M FLR** of realized additions. The amount comes from changes in the network’s reward-pool counters after cancelling internal funding movements and reward destruction before circulation. An authorization to issue is different from an actual release to spendable supply. For the next 90 days, the current 30-day authorization implies **655.7M FLR** as a forecast; realization can differ as claims and reward timing change.

Vesting unlocks contribute **260.0M FLR**. The incentive reward system places coins into 12-month linear restrictions before users can withdraw the released portion. Gross withdrawals include penalties on early exits, so the framework subtracts the **12.7M FLR** that was destroyed while still locked. The remaining quantity is the amount eligible to enter circulation. Forward vesting uses the recent realized claim pace of **260.0M FLR**, an estimate rather than a single guaranteed cliff.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add **0** beyond those two rows. Funding an incentive account from another excluded reserve does not itself make those coins circulate. Long-term locked or bankruptcy releases add **0** because no separate estate or trustee distribution was verified. The completed historical FlareDrop programme is not restarted inside this ledger simply because its earlier schedule remains easy to find.

## Buy pressure: where new FLR goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**. A reserve or investment mandate does not establish an executed purchase quantity and a verified destination. Protocol fee burn is **5.0M FLR** over the trailing quarter, derived after separating traced transfers and independent reward-expiry burns from the total increase at the burn address. A separate sample of transaction receipts, corrected for the network’s fee-refund rules, supports that quantity.

Foundation buying is **0** because an increase in a controlled reserve is not proof of an open-market purchase. New long-term lock is **0**: incentive funding moved between pools that were already outside circulation, so it cannot receive an additional removal credit.

Two extra rows make the remaining flows visible. Other circulating-token burns removed **25.7M FLR**, principally a large direct destruction from a controlled account. Excluded-reserve accumulation removed **2.7M FLR** across the network’s designated Foundation addresses. Neither has an established recurring cadence, so both project **0** next quarter. The forecast fee-burn quantity is **5.0M FLR**, rebased to activity after the **Jul 16 2026** fee change. A separate corrected receipt sample supports that forward pace; it is not a promise of future activity.

## Foundation and overhang

The pending incentive reward balance is approximately **1,287.9M FLR** after accounting for claims. The separate ecosystem incentive treasury holds **17,354.5M FLR**. These are standing reserve balances, so transfers between them must not be counted as fresh circulation. The reward-contract state can be refreshed daily; the important change is the amount becoming spendable after its restriction and any early-exit penalty.

The early-backer overhang is **204.5M FLR**. That is a remaining balance to monitor, not evidence that the old monthly destruction programme continues indefinitely. The named Foundation exclusion set is read directly from the network’s supply contract; it accumulated **2.7M FLR** net during this window. Those addresses are refreshed daily, and any new governance or Foundation announcement should be checked for a dated release rather than inferred from capacity.

If a controlled balance falls between refreshes beyond the already counted vesting or reward flow, the additional evidenced outflow enters the Foundation row at the next refresh. If the funds merely move to another excluded pool, no circulating addition is booked. This distinction prevents the same incentive allocation being counted when funded, when vested and again when claimed.

## How FLR compares to other reward-funded chains

Flare combines ongoing network rewards with release of previously allocated incentive reserves. A capped token with a completed vesting schedule would have neither of those continuing sources. An uncapped staking chain can have the first without the second. Comparing only published annual mint percentages therefore misses an important part of FLR’s tradable-supply change.

The burn side needs the same boundary discipline. Destroying an already circulating coin reduces available supply. Destroying an unclaimed reward prevents a future addition but does not remove a coin that a holder could already trade. This quarter included substantial expired rewards and early-exit penalties, which belong outside both sides of the circulating-flow ledger. A large all-purpose burn counter alone cannot show how much circulating supply was offset.

Flare’s current annual issuance limit constrains one period’s reward creation; it is not a fixed lifetime supply cap. Usage-driven gas destruction can reduce the net addition, while the incentive reserve can continue feeding vesting accounts. Both the changing fee regime and actual reward claims matter when comparing Flare with networks that issue and burn through simpler mechanisms.

Require an identifiable destination and executed amount before booking a new reserve-funded buyback. Refresh the **204.5M FLR** early-backer balance and the Foundation exclusion set for a new dated distribution.

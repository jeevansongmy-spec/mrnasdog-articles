**TL;DR.** Optimism mints no OP, and OP still grew **5.99%** in a quarter. The whole supply of **4,294,967,296** OP was created at genesis, the contract's supply reading is identical at both ends of the window to within **0.018** OP, and yet **137.1M** OP crossed from locked to freely tradable between Jun 1 and Aug 30 2026 — a monthly vesting contract paying out, plus the four-year lock on the 2022 investor and core-contributor allocations reaching its end. Against that, the buy ledger is empty in all four rows: the revenue-funded buyback holds the same **9,451,924** OP at both window ends and has not settled a purchase since May 1 2026. Net **+5.99%** last 90 days against the inflation monitor's **+6.34%**, a gap of **0.35** percentage points. The forward read is **+3.50%**: the one-off lock expiry is spent, but the Collective's own Year 5 budget still plans a heavy year of ecosystem deployment.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Optimism at **+5.99%** net supply growth over the trailing 90 days and **+3.50%** over the next 90, on a circulating base of **2,287,994,831** OP. The inflation monitor reads **+6.34%** for the same trailing window, leaving a gap of **0.35** percentage points — inside tolerance, so no data-conflict chip is raised on the OP overview. That agreement is arithmetic rather than independent: the monitor and this ledger both start from the Optimism Foundation's own published circulating-supply figure, so it confirms the sum, not the classification. On mechanism, OP is a **fixed-supply, pre-minted allocation token whose inflation is entirely a distribution schedule**. There is no block reward, no staking emission and no fee burn anywhere in the design. The only lever that could create a new OP is a mint contract that governance has set to a rate of zero, and even its code ceiling of **2%** a year is locked shut until Oct 28 2026.

## Sell pressure: where new OP comes from

Sell row one, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and on Optimism that is provable rather than assumed. The OP contract's total supply reads **4,294,963,292.4987** at the block that opens the window and **4,294,963,292.4807** at the block that closes it — a difference of eighteen thousandths of one OP across ninety days. The token's owner is a mint contract held by the Collective's reserve safe, and its next-permitted-mint timestamp resolves to **Oct 28 2026**. If governance ever raised the rate from zero to the coded ceiling, the annual cap would be about **85.9M** OP. Nobody has proposed it. Capacity is not a schedule, so the row books zero and the date is carried in the watch list instead.

Sell row two, vesting unlocks, carries the entire quarter: **137.1M** OP. Optimism's own supply file moved from **2,150,875,957** to **2,287,994,831**, and because nothing was minted, every one of those tokens already existed inside a locked allocation. Two mechanisms produced it. An on-chain vesting contract paid out **32.6M** OP in three monthly instalments — Jun 24, Jul 28 and Aug 28 2026 — and the four-year lock on the investor and core-contributor allocations from the 2022 launch reached its end, freeing the rest from wallets those holders already controlled. The second half is now finished: the Foundation's allocation table shows the investor bucket at **735,858,894** OP against an allocation of **735,858,894**, which is 100% released and cannot repeat.

Sell row three, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **zero** for the trailing quarter — and that is a measurement, not a shrug. Every identified Collective wallet holds an identical OP balance at both ends of the window. The one enormous movement, **142,000,000** OP on Aug 7 2026, went from one Collective safe to another; the receiving safe still holds all **142,000,010** OP, and the published circulating supply did not move across the transfer. Reading that as sell pressure is the single most common error made on this token, and the framework does not make it. Sell row four, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is zero because Optimism has no bankruptcy estate at all — OP launched through an airdrop and a set of allocations in 2022, never through an exchange that later failed.

## Buy pressure: where new OP goes

Every buy row on Optimism is **zero**, and the most interesting one is the buyback. Governance approved a twelve-month programme on Jan 28 2026 directing up to half of Superchain revenue into monthly open-market OP purchases, settling into one published wallet. That wallet holds **9,451,924.21** OP at the opening block and the identical **9,451,924.21** OP at the closing block. Its entire inbound history is three deposits — **1,574,817** OP on Mar 2 2026, **6,951,453** on Apr 1 and **925,654** on May 1 — and nothing since. A governance update on Aug 7 2026 announced **7,877,107** OP of buybacks, which reads as fresh buying but is the April and May settlements being reported late. Four monthly windows have now passed with no purchase, and what the programme did buy is held in treasury rather than burned, so it is an overhang as well as a past bid.

Protocol fee burn is zero because gas on Optimism settles in ETH, so network fees never touch the OP token at all. The check was still run from both sides: the dead address took in **10.84** OP across the whole quarter while the contract's supply reading fell by **0.018** OP — two independent surfaces, both dust against a supply of 4.29 billion. Foundation buy is zero because the only Collective purchasing is the programme above, and booking it twice would invent a buy side that does not exist. New long-term lock is zero because OP has no staking contract, no bonding and no lockup vault; delegating a vote leaves the coins in the holder's own wallet and freely transferable, so governance participation removes nothing from the float.

## Foundation and overhang

Roughly **2.007B** OP — about **47%** of all OP that will ever exist — sits outside the tradable float, and almost all of it is identified. The Collective reserve safe holds **1,294,044,371** OP and did not move a single token in the window. The operating treasury holds **318,475,105** OP after the Aug 7 2026 internal transfer, and the new custody safe that received it holds **142,000,010** OP. Two further identified safes hold **90,104,652** and **59,036,793** OP, the grants distribution wallet holds **21,390,704** OP and paid nothing all quarter, the vesting contract still holds **174,611,303** OP, and the buyback wallet holds **9,451,924** OP.

Two allocation-level overhangs sit on top of the wallet list. On Aug 19 2026 the Token House approved re-designating the unspent user airdrop allocation — **546.9M** OP — as a new Strategic Ecosystem Fund, by **17.97M** votes to **10.93M**. No OP reached the market: the tokens moved between two non-circulating buckets. What changed is intent. A pool that had been earmarked for airdrops nobody was planning is now explicitly deployable into partnership and enterprise deals, which is a live supply risk rather than a dormant one. Alongside it, **777.6M** OP of retro-funding allocation is untouched with the programme paused, and **105.4M** OP of the governance fund is uncommitted. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How OP compares to other layer-2 governance tokens

The comparison that matters is not Optimism against a proof-of-work chain but Optimism against every other pre-minted allocation token — the Arbitrum, Starknet and zkSync shape, where a fixed genesis supply is released on a calendar rather than mined. In that class, the mint is irrelevant and the schedule is everything. A halving-model chain like Bitcoin knows its issuance for the next century; a token like OP knows only what a foundation and a DAO decide to hand out next quarter. That is why OP can read **+5.99%** supply growth while its contract mints literally nothing, and why the framework reads the distribution file rather than the supply function.

Against exchange tokens that run quarterly burns, OP is the mirror image. A burn-model token shrinks its float with revenue; Optimism collects Superchain revenue and routes up to half of it into buybacks that are then **held, not burned** — so even a working programme would move tokens from the market into a treasury rather than out of existence, and this one has not moved anything since May 1 2026. Against an uncapped continuous-emission layer-1, OP looks better on the long horizon and worse on the short one: a fixed **4,294,967,296** ceiling means the dilution is finite and roughly half-spent, but the remaining **2.007B** can arrive on any timetable governance chooses, whereas an emission curve at least tells you the shape in advance.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The vesting contract pays again in late Sep, late Oct and late Nov 2026, at roughly **10.9M** OP a firing — the three that fall inside the forward window are what carries Sell #2 to **32.6M**. On **Oct 28 2026** the mint lever becomes callable for the first time since 2025; the governance-set rate is zero and no proposal to raise it is live, but that is the date the **2%** ceiling stops being theoretical. The buyback is the third watch and the sharpest: the twelve-month mandate runs to roughly Jan 2027 with four monthly settlement windows already missed, so either a catch-up purchase lands and the buy side stops being empty, or the programme quietly expires having bought **9.45M** OP in total. Fourth, the Strategic Ecosystem Fund approved on **Aug 19 2026** has no published release schedule, so its first deployment out of the **546.9M** pool is the event that would move Sell #3 hardest. Fifth, the Collective's next full budget update is due by Jun 2027, but any mid-year revision to the **200M** OP ecosystem forecast would re-base the forward read directly.

## Summary

Optimism is a fixed-supply token with an inflation problem that has nothing to do with minting. The OP contract created **4,294,967,296** tokens in 2022 and has created none since — its supply reading is identical at both ends of this quarter — yet **137.1M** OP became freely tradable between Jun 1 and Aug 30 2026 as a monthly vesting contract paid out and the four-year investor and core-contributor lock expired. The buy side is empty in all four rows, with a revenue-funded buyback that has not settled a purchase since May 1 2026 and holds rather than burns what it bought. That is **+5.99%** net supply growth last quarter and a projected **+3.50%** next, against the inflation monitor's **+6.34%** and a gap of **0.35** percentage points. The key risk is not dilution from code but dilution from decisions: **2.007B** OP remains outside the float, **546.9M** of it was just re-pointed from paused airdrops to active ecosystem deals, and the ceiling that caps it all is a hard **4,294,967,296** that is now roughly half spent.

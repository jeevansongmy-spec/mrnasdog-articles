# CC Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Canton Coin (CC) supply is growing. Between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026 the Canton Network created about **2.03B CC** through rewards, development-fund grants and voted payouts, while traffic fees burned **1.34B CC**, so net supply rose **+1.73%**. CC has no fixed cap: the network may pay out up to 10B CC a year, and a vote that took effect on Nov 5 2026 lowers the pace to about **+1.43%** for the next 90 days.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 20 2026, Canton Coin supply grew **+1.73%** net, and other trackers show **+1.73%** as well, a gap of **0.01 points**. The two readings agree, so the coin page carries no warning note. Looking ahead, the same reward and burn pattern points to about **+1.43%** over the next 90 days, lower than before because app rewards were cut on Nov 5 2026. The label that fits Canton Coin today: inflationary by design, with a fee burn that covers about 70% of the reward pay.

## Sell pressure: where new CC comes from

Protocol inflation is the main source. Every round, roughly every ten minutes, the Canton Network pays new CC to the validators that confirm transactions, the featured apps that bring traffic and the Super Validators that run the core network. From Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026 those rewards came to about **1.92B CC**, near **21.6M CC a day** until Nov 5 2026. On that day a governance vote cut the most a featured app can earn from $1.50 to $0.79 for each $1 of fees its users pay, and Canton Coin rewards have run about 7% lower since, near **20M CC a day**. The yearly ceiling is 10B CC; rewards that nobody claims are not paid out at once.

Vesting unlocks add **0**. Canton Coin never had a token sale or a team and investor allocation: every CC was born as a reward on the live network, so nothing waits on a vesting calendar.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add **63.76M CC**. Five percent of every round goes into a development fund that the Canton Foundation pays out as grants to builders. Ninety grants were paid between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026, and grants have been paid every month since May 2026, so the same pace is expected next.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply adds **0**: no bankrupt firm or court holds Canton Coin for a later payout.

A fifth source is the voted payout of unclaimed rewards, which added **49.78M CC**. Rewards nobody claimed sit in a pool until the Super Validators vote to pay part of it out, mostly to new Super Validators that met their joining goals. Seven payouts landed in the window, the largest **25.42M CC** to Zenith on Aug 28 2026 and **15.09M CC** to Wormhole on Sep 10 2026. Votes like this have passed in 13 of the last 14 months.

## Buy pressure: where new CC goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**. No contract or treasury buys Canton Coin back from the market.

The protocol fee burn is the one real sink. Apps and validators pay for network traffic by burning CC at a set dollar price per megabyte, so the busier the Canton Network, the more CC disappears. From Aug 22 to Nov 20 2026 the burn destroyed **1.34B CC**, about **14.9M CC a day**, rising to about 15.7M a day in November 2026. All-time burns passed **6.13B CC** in mid-November 2026. Because rewards are paid for the same fees, the burn alone cannot shrink supply while the Super Validator share, the grants and the voted payouts keep adding coins.

Foundation buying is **0**. A listed company keeps Canton Coin as its treasury, but it buys coins that already circulate, so its buying does not change the count.

New long-term locks are **0** as well. Under the network's locking rules, Super Validators now hold about **19.69B CC** in 136 locking wallets and featured apps about **1.14B CC**. That is real commitment, yet the locked Canton Coin still counts as circulating, so the locks remove nothing from the supply count.

## Foundation and overhang

Three pools could add Canton Coin later. The development fund has built up about **171.49M CC** that is not yet granted, plus about **7M CC** granted but not yet paid; the Canton Foundation decides when it goes out, and recent months have seen 20M to 25M CC a month. Super Validator pay held back for firms that have not yet met their joining terms totals about **897M CC** issued so far, spread over 26 reserved seats; it is not paid out today and could return through voted payouts. The Foundation's own Super Validator reward wallet holds about **127K CC**.

We check these balances at every rebuild. If any of them falls between checks and the coins reach the market, that outflow is added to the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next refresh.

## How CC compares to other payment-network coins

Canton Coin runs a burn-and-mint design: users burn the coin to pay fees, and the network mints new coins to reward the operators. That is close in spirit to Helium's burn-and-mint model and to fee-burn chains like Ethereum, but with one big difference: Canton Coin rewards are tied to fees, so a busier network raises both sides at once. Ethereum burns the base fee and pays validators a stake-based reward, which keeps its supply near flat; Canton Coin still adds about **1.73%** in 90 days.

Unlike a fixed-cap coin such as Bitcoin, Canton Coin has no hard supply limit; its ceiling is a falling issuance curve that drops from 10B CC a year to 5B CC a year around mid-2029 and to 2.5B CC a year later on. Unlike most venture-backed layer-1 tokens, it has no investor unlock calendar at all: every new coin is paid for work on the network, which makes the reward pay, not vesting cliffs, the number to watch.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**December 2026:** the Canton Network plans a dry run of rewards based on the traffic each app really uses. It changes nothing yet, but it previews how app rewards could shrink further.

**Early 2027:** the universal burn approved on Oct 23 2026 makes every private Canton network pay for traffic by burning CC; phase one is estimated for the first quarter of 2027, and the team calls that date a goal, not a deadline.

**New Super Validators:** Nomura (approved Nov 10 2026) and other firms hold reserved seats. Each time one meets its joining goals, the Super Validators may vote a payout from the unclaimed pool, which adds supply.

**Development-fund grants:** about 171.49M CC is waiting to be granted, and a faster grant pace would push the Foundation row up.

## Summary

Canton Coin supply grew **+1.73%** between Aug 22 and Nov 20 2026, with about 1.92B CC paid as rewards, 63.76M CC in development-fund grants and 49.78M CC in voted payouts against a 1.34B CC fee burn. CC has no vesting and no fixed cap; new coins come only from rewards, grants and voted payouts, and the traffic fee burn is the only sink. The Nov 5 2026 cut to app rewards lowers the expected growth to about **+1.43%** over the next 90 days. The key risk is the 897M CC of held-back Super Validator pay and the 171.49M CC development-fund balance, both of which can reach the market by vote.

# ALGO Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

ALGO's circulating supply grew **+0.51%** between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026, and we expect about **+0.35%** over the next 90 days. Algorand never creates new coins: all **10 billion** ALGO were made at launch, and supply grows only when the Algorand Foundation lets coins out of its own wallets. In the window that meant **18.65M ALGO** paid to stakers and **28.41M ALGO** paid out by the Foundation, against **301.72K ALGO** taken back in, for a net of **46.77M ALGO**; other trackers show **+0.49%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Algorand's supply was growing and the pace is cooling. Over the last 90 days the coins in the market rose by **46.77M ALGO**, or **+0.51%** of the **9.08B ALGO** now circulating. Other trackers show **+0.49%**, only **0.03 points** away, so the two views agree and the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days we expect about **31.47M ALGO** of net new supply, or **+0.35%**. The drop comes from two large one-off Foundation payments that will not repeat on any known schedule, and from a staker payment that shrinks a little every month. Our label: modest, steady dilution, set by one holder.

## Sell pressure: where new ALGO comes from

**Staking pay.** No ALGO is minted, but most blocks pay the staker who proposed them about **8.1 ALGO**, plus half of the block's fees. That money comes from the network's fee pot, which the Foundation keeps topped up and which sits outside the circulating count. From Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026 stakers outside the Foundation received **18.65M ALGO** this way. The payment per block falls 1% every million blocks, roughly once a month, so the next 90 days should bring about **18.14M ALGO**. On Nov 6 2026 the Foundation said these rewards continue through January 2028.

**Vesting unlocks.** Zero. Algorand's early backers finished vesting in 2021, with the last small part in early 2022, so no team or investor batch is waiting to unlock.

**Foundation payouts.** This is the biggest line. Between Aug 25 and Nov 23 2026 the Foundation's wallets sent **28.41M ALGO** to outside addresses. Two payments stand out: one of **13.35M ALGO** on Sep 22 and Oct 1, and one of **1.63M ALGO** on Sep 30 and Oct 1. Neither has been spent since. The rest is the usual monthly flow: about **2.50M ALGO** in lots to a sales desk on Aug 26, Oct 1, Oct 2 and Nov 19, plus grants, xGov awards, partner payments and running costs. Without the two one-offs, we expect about **13.44M ALGO** in the next 90 days.

**Locked or bankruptcy coins.** Zero. No bankrupt firm, court or locked fund holds ALGO for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new ALGO goes

**Buyback.** Zero. No contract or treasury buys ALGO back from the market.

**Burn.** Zero. Algorand burns nothing, and the full 10 billion ALGO stays in existence.

**Foundation buying.** Zero. The Foundation funds itself by selling and spending ALGO; it has no plan to buy.

**New long-term lock.** Zero. Staked ALGO still counts as circulating. On Oct 22 2026 the Foundation moved **193.59M ALGO** from five of its wallets into one new wallet, but those coins were never in the market, so the move changes nothing.

**Fees kept by the network.** Every transaction fee goes into the fee pot, out of the circulating count. Users paid **114.59K ALGO** in fees in the window, and half of it flowed straight back out to stakers. Expect about the same next.

**Coins sent back.** Outside wallets returned **187.14K ALGO** to the Foundation, mostly on Nov 4 2026. It has no schedule, so we count nothing for the next 90 days.

## Foundation and overhang

The Algorand Foundation is the one large holder outside the market. It holds about **917.57M ALGO**, close to a tenth of all coins: **694.45M ALGO** in its main wallets, of which **392.56M ALGO** is staked to help run the network; **193.59M ALGO** in the wallet it filled on Oct 22 2026; **29.53M ALGO** in an old wallet; and **3.64M ALGO** in a selling wallet that did not move all window. Its sales are capped by limits tied to trading volume and pause after a sharp price drop. Every coin that leaves these wallets adds to supply, so this pile, not a vesting chart, is what decides how fast ALGO dilutes.

## How ALGO compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake chains pay stakers by minting new coins on a fixed rate, and many burn part of each fee to push back. Algorand does neither. Its supply cap of **10 billion** is fixed, and staker pay comes out of coins that already exist, funded by the Foundation. On Nov 6 2026 the Foundation also dropped a plan that would have lifted that cap. That makes Algorand closer to a treasury-funded network than an inflation-funded one: dilution tracks the Foundation's spending choices, and it stops when the Foundation's wallets run dry or the rewards program ends.

The trade-off is clear. Holders face no open-ended issuance, but they rely on one organisation's budget, and the fee pot keeps only **114.59K ALGO** a quarter, far too little to pay stakers on its own.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Around Dec 18 2026 and Jan 19 2027:** the staker payment per block steps down 1% each time, already counted in the next-90-day figure.

**The 13.35M and 1.63M ALGO payments:** both have sat unspent since Oct 1 2026. If they reach exchanges, prices could feel it, but they are already in the circulating count.

**Sales desk lots:** the most recent was **736.84K ALGO** on Nov 19 2026. A bigger or faster pace would lift the next-90-day number.

**The Foundation's next quarterly report:** it shows how much it sold and spent in the third quarter of 2026.

## Summary

ALGO's circulating supply rose **+0.51%** from Aug 25 to Nov 23 2026, and we expect about **+0.35%** over the next 90 days.

All of that growth comes from the Algorand Foundation's wallets: **18.65M ALGO** of staker pay and **28.41M ALGO** of payouts, with no minting, no vesting and no burn.

The Foundation still holds about **917.57M ALGO**, so its spending pace is the number that matters most for ALGO's future supply.

Other trackers agree within **0.03 points**, and the coin page carries no warning note.

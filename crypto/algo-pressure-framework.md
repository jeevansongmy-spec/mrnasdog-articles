# ALGO Inflation Analysis · July 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Algorand has a genuine hard cap and no mint function — all **10,000,000,000 ALGO** were created in the 2019 genesis block — and it is still diluting. About **53.0M ALGO** reached the market over the trailing 90 days and **52.3M** is projected for the next 90, split between a Foundation-funded block bonus of roughly **20.0M** and Algorand Foundation reserve release of roughly **33.0M**, against a buy side of exactly **zero**. That leaves the MrNasdog Pressure Framework at **+0.59%** net, with about **1.03B ALGO** of reserve still standing between today's float and the cap.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window opening **Jul 27 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **ALGO at +0.58% net** forward, after a measured **+0.59%** over the trailing 90 days. Our supply monitor reads **+0.72%**, a gap of **0.13 percentage points** — comfortably inside the framework's 0.5-point tolerance, so no monitor-gap chip ships on the ALGO overview. The two readings agree because the mechanism is unusually legible: the Algorand Foundation publishes its own ALGO balance movement every quarter, and the block bonus is readable directly in each block header. Algorand is best characterised as **a hard-capped chain that is still inflationary on its tradable float** — the ceiling is real, but the distance to it is being spent down at a steady rate, and there is no offsetting mechanism anywhere in the protocol.

## Sell pressure: where new ALGO comes from

Sell #1 — protocol inflation — runs at about **20.0M ALGO** per 90 days, and the label needs a caveat on Algorand: nothing is minted. The protocol has no mint function, so what the framework books here is a block-production bonus paid out of a reservoir the Algorand Foundation pre-funded from its own reserve. Read on-chain at round **63,482,700**, the bonus stands at **8.429426 ALGO** per block, plus **50%** of that block's transaction fees, paid to whichever staked account proposed it. The programme opened at **10 ALGO** per block in **Jan 2025** and decays **1%** every million blocks, which at the observed **31,428** blocks a day is a step roughly every **32** days; seventeen steps have already fired. The Algorand Foundation's published monthly totals corroborate the on-chain read almost exactly — validators earned **6.62M ALGO** in April 2026, **6.93M** in May 2026 and **6.57M** in June 2026, and the quarterly reconciliation booked **20.025M** for the first quarter of 2026.

Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — is **zero**, and permanently so. Every genesis tranche on Algorand, covering the public sale, node-running grants, the team and early backers, participation rewards and end-user grants, finished releasing in **2024**. There is no vesting contract left to read and no cliff on any calendar, inside the window or beyond it. Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is the larger tap at about **33.0M ALGO** per 90 days, and it is the reason a capped asset still dilutes: the Algorand Foundation's own quarterly reconciliation shows its ALGO holdings falling from **1,134.5M** to **1,078.9M** across the first quarter of 2026, a release of **55.7M ALGO**, of which **20.0M** was the staking bonus above and **35.6M** was discretionary. Reading the Algorand Foundation's published circulating-supply series across the second quarter gives an independent **30.7M** for the same discretionary channel; the framework ships the midpoint. Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero**, because no bankruptcy estate, trustee schedule or court-ordered distribution holds ALGO.

## Buy pressure: where new ALGO goes

Every buy row on Algorand is **zero**, and that is the sharpest fact on this page. Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is zero because the Algorand Foundation is a structural net seller: its published programme sells reserve ALGO into the market from two disclosed wallets, and it has never announced or executed a repurchase. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is zero because Algorand destroys nothing. Transaction fees split **50%** to the block proposer and **50%** into the protocol fee sink, and the fee sink is the same account that funds the block bonus, so fees are recycled straight back out rather than removed. The magnitudes make the point regardless of accounting: Algorand collected about **47K ALGO** in fees in April 2026 against **6.62M ALGO** paid out in rewards, so user fees cover well under **1%** of what the network distributes.

Buy #3 — Foundation buy — is zero: no open-market ALGO purchase by the Algorand Foundation or any ecosystem entity has been disclosed, and no accumulation wallet exists to read. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is zero because staking on Algorand does not lock anything. Consensus participation is non-custodial: staked ALGO never leaves the holder's own wallet, stays fully spendable and can be moved in the same block, so the **2.02B ALGO** now participating in consensus is still part of the tradable float. Growth in staked ALGO is therefore not supply absorption on Algorand the way a bonded-stake chain's unbonding period would be, and the framework books nothing for it.

## Foundation and overhang

The team-controlled overhang on Algorand is unusually simple to enumerate, because it is essentially one entity. The Algorand Foundation held **1,078.9M ALGO** at **Mar 31 2026** — the whole distance between the current float and the **10B** cap, now around **1.03B ALGO** — with no published wallet-level release schedule, walked on the web roughly every two weeks against the quarterly reconciliation. Beneath it sit three smaller, on-chain-readable pools. The two disclosed selling wallets, **37VPAD3C…N7YKI** and **44GWRTQG…RMGH7E**, are pass-throughs and held **2.867** and **0.996 ALGO** respectively when read this session — they route sales rather than store reserve. The protocol fee sink held **16,444,372 ALGO**, and that balance is the live reservoir for the block bonus, so its drain is exactly Sell #1. Community-voted grant money still unspent from prior governance periods stood at **2.6M ALGO**. There is no separate treasury, no labs entity holding a second block, and no bankruptcy residual. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh; the on-chain pools are read directly, and the Foundation reserve is confirmed against each quarterly reconciliation.

## How ALGO compares to other hard-capped layer-1 chains

ALGO sits in an awkward corner of the hard-capped class. Against a halving-model chain with a fixed subsidy, the comparison is instructive: a halving chain mints new coins above the existing float, but its issuance is protocol-encoded, mechanically scheduled and known decades ahead, and it steps down on a fixed calendar nobody controls. Algorand mints nothing at all — the cap is a genuine ceiling, not a target — yet its dilution rate is set by a foundation's spending decisions rather than by code. The block bonus decays on a rule, at **1%** every million blocks, so that half of the flow behaves like a halving chain's subsidy. The other and larger half, the discretionary reserve release, does not: it can accelerate, pause or stop by decision, and the only visibility into it is a quarterly report published after the fact.

Against an uncapped continuous-emission layer-1 with a fee burn, Algorand is worse on the offset and better on the ceiling. Chains that burn a base fee genuinely can turn net deflationary when demand is high; Algorand has no burn at all, so no amount of usage removes a single ALGO from supply, and its fee revenue is currently under **1%** of what it distributes anyway. But an uncapped chain's dilution never ends, while Algorand's does: at the current pace the **1.03B ALGO** of remaining reserve is finite, and once the reserve is spent the float stops growing entirely because there is no mint behind it. Against an exchange token that buys back and burns from revenue, Algorand is the mirror image — those assets convert cash flow into supply reduction, while Algorand converts reserve into operating budget. The right mental model for ALGO is not an inflating asset but a **fixed pie being served out**, and the inflation question is simply how fast the last **10.3%** of the pie reaches the table.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Watch the Algorand Foundation's quarterly reconciliation for the second quarter of 2026, due around **Jul 31 2026** on the same quarterly rhythm that put the first-quarter report out on **Apr 30 2026**: it is the single document that fixes Sell #3, and a structured-selling figure materially above or below the first quarter's **24.0M ALGO** moves the whole ledger. Watch the block bonus itself, which steps down about **1%** every million blocks and will fall roughly **2.8%** across this window from today's **8.429426 ALGO** per block. Watch the end of the Algorand Foundation's bonus commitment: it was framed as approximately **24 months** from **Jan 2025**, which lands just past this window in early **2027**, and any announcement about extending, cutting or replacing it would reset Sell #1 outright. Watch the monthly Algorand supply reports through **Aug 2026** to **Oct 2026** for the rewards line, which has been drifting down from **6.93M** in May 2026 to **6.57M** in June 2026. And watch the xGov governance platform, which the Algorand Foundation has said will absorb general governance during 2026 — any decision routing protocol revenue toward a burn, or toward a larger community allocation, would be the first real change to the buy side ALGO has ever had.

## Summary

Algorand is a hard-capped chain with no mint function whose supply still grows, because dilution on ALGO comes from a reserve being spent rather than from coins being created. About **53.0M ALGO** reached the market over the trailing 90 days — roughly **20.0M** in block-production bonus at **8.429426 ALGO** per block plus half of transaction fees, and roughly **33.0M** released from the Algorand Foundation's reserve — and **52.3M** is projected forward, leaving the framework at **+0.59%** measured and **+0.58%** forward against a supply monitor reading **+0.72%**, a **0.13-point** gap well inside tolerance. The key risk is that the larger tap is discretionary: the block bonus decays on a published rule, but the roughly **33.0M** of reserve release is a spending decision disclosed only in arrears, and the Algorand Foundation still holds about **1.03B ALGO**. The ceiling, however, is genuine — **10,000,000,000 ALGO** is the absolute maximum, nothing can be minted above it, and the dilution stops for good when the reserve runs out.

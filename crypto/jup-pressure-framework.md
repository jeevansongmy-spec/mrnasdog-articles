# JUP Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

JUP, the token of Jupiter on Solana, has no mint at all — the JUP mint authority is empty, so no new JUP can ever be created — and the three Jupiter reserves that sit outside the circulating count, **3.54B JUP** in total, did not move once in the last 90 days. The only change to JUP supply was a burn: Jupiter's token-verification fee wallet destroyed **943,764 JUP** on **Sep 10 2026**, and users burned about **4,439 JUP** more. That is **0.95M JUP** removed against **zero** added, so the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads JUP at **−0.03% net** over the last 90 days, against a supply-monitor reading of **−0.17%** — a gap of **0.14 percentage points**, which is agreement, not conflict. The Litterbox buyback kept running, but it holds the JUP it buys inside the float, so the ceiling on JUP supply is the **6.86B JUP** that exists today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Sep 25 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **JUP at −0.03% net**: nothing on the JUP sell side added a coin to the float, and the buy side removed **948,203 JUP** through burns, on a circulating supply of **3.32B JUP**. The independent supply monitor reads the realised 90-day change at **−0.17%**. The gap is **0.14 percentage points**, well inside the framework's half-point tolerance, so JUP ships with **no data-conflict flag**. The forward column rounds to **0.00%**, because the verification-fee wallet burns in irregular batches and the last two came months apart, so the next one is not expected inside the next 90 days. The label for JUP is **fixed supply, parked reserves**: a token that can never be minted, whose big reserves are frozen by a governance vote, and whose buyback moves coins within the float rather than out of it.

## Sell pressure: where new JUP comes from

Nowhere, this window. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero** and permanent: JUP is a Solana token whose mint authority is empty, and an empty mint authority can never be set again, so Jupiter has no way to create a single new JUP. The JUP supply surface moved this window only downward, through the burn described below. Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **zero**, and this is where the February vote shows up. Until **Jan 31 2026** the Jupiter team reserve released a tranche every quarter. The Net-Zero Emissions vote, held **Feb 17–21 2026** and passed with about three quarters of the vote, paused team emissions, postponed the last Jupuary airdrop and set Jupiter to offset any selling by early Mercurial stakeholders. The team reserve has not moved since: the tranches that would have landed on **Apr 30** and **Jul 31 2026** never went out. The framework books what was released, not what a calendar once said, and here that is nothing. Unlock trackers now agree that no JUP schedule remains. Small contributor vesting locks did release about **2.25M JUP** in the window, but those locks already count inside the JUP circulating supply, so moving the coins to their owners adds nothing new to the float.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **zero**, and it was measured rather than assumed. The JUP circulating figure leaves out exactly three reserves — the community reserve at **1.70B JUP**, which holds the postponed airdrop, the team reserve at **1.68B JUP** and a third reserve wallet at **159.89M JUP**. Together they match the gap between JUP total supply and JUP circulating supply to within **0.47M JUP**, and none of the three made a single transaction in 90 days. Every other Jupiter wallet already counts as circulating, so its payouts are moves inside the float: the community wallet funded the quarterly Active Staking Rewards round with **47.90M JUP** on **Jul 7 2026**, and the team distribution wallet moved **72.00M JUP** into staking accounts. Neither added a JUP to the float. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy supply, is **zero**: no estate distributes JUP, and the largest team member locks do not open until early 2027.

## Buy pressure: where new JUP goes

Buy #2, the protocol fee burn, is the only non-zero row, at **0.95M JUP**. Projects that want a fast Jupiter token-verification review pay **1,000 JUP**, and the fees collect in a burn wallet that Jupiter empties in batches. On **Sep 10 2026** that wallet destroyed **943,764 JUP** in one transaction; its previous batch was **1.55M JUP** on **Apr 27 2026**. Ordinary users burned about **4,439 JUP** more across the window. JUP burns cut the token supply directly, so the fall in JUP total supply and the burn log are two views of one flow, booked once — and the two agree to the unit. Because the batch burn has no published schedule and fired roughly four and a half months apart the last two times, the forward column carries only the small user-burn trickle.

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is **zero**, and that needs explaining, because the Jupiter buyback is real and busy. Half of Jupiter's platform revenue buys JUP on the open market every day for the Litterbox Trust, which took in **29.65M JUP** over the window — from **142.52M** to **172.18M JUP** — and sent none out. But the Litterbox already counts inside the JUP circulating supply, so each purchase moves coins from one circulating wallet to another and takes none off the market. The Litterbox would count the day its JUP is burned, as the Jupiter DAO voted to do once, on **Nov 25 2025**. Buy #3, foundation buying, is **zero**: any offset buying promised in the February vote lands in Jupiter wallets that are already circulating. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is **zero**: JUP staking is active, but staked JUP stays inside the circulating count, so new stake removes nothing.

## Foundation and overhang

The JUP overhang is large and concentrated. Outside the float sit the community reserve (**1.70B JUP**), the team reserve (**1.68B JUP**) and the third reserve wallet (**159.89M JUP**) — together more than the whole circulating supply again. The community reserve can only move after a new Jupiter DAO vote, and the team reserve is paused "for the foreseeable future", which is a policy, not a code lock. Inside the float, Jupiter also holds the community wallet that pays staking rewards (**223.40M JUP**, about **50M JUP** a quarter), the team distribution wallet (**191.57M JUP**), the Litterbox Trust (**172.18M JUP**) and a team liquidity wallet (**126.38M JUP**). All of them are read on-chain on every refresh. If any of the three outside reserves falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh; if the Litterbox burns, it enters the buy side.

## How JUP compares to other DeFi exchange tokens

Most DeFi exchange tokens fall into one of two supply shapes. Some mint on a schedule — liquidity-mining emissions or open-ended staking rewards — and rely on a fee buyback to offset the new coins. Others are fixed-supply tokens whose float grows only as a team and investor schedule unlocks. JUP now sits in a third, quieter position: fixed supply with no mint, and a schedule that governance has frozen, so the float neither grows from issuance nor from unlocks. What JUP holders receive as staking rewards is paid from coins that already circulate, which is a redistribution between holders rather than new supply.

The JUP buyback is where the comparison is sharpest. A buyback that burns what it buys removes coins from the float every day and shows up straight away in the supply reading. A buyback that holds its purchases in a trust moves coins between circulating wallets: it supports the price through the bid, but the float does not shrink until a later vote burns the pile. Jupiter runs the second kind, with **172.18M JUP** now parked in the Litterbox — a stock of coins the DAO has burned once before. Against proof-of-stake chains that pay validators in new coins, JUP has no issuance to fight at all; against exchange tokens with a scheduled quarterly burn, JUP's only regular destruction is a small, irregular fee burn.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, any Jupiter DAO vote on the postponed Jupuary tokens in the community reserve: releasing them would open a Sell #3 row that has been zero all window. Second, the paused team reserve at **1.68B JUP**: a restart of team emissions would reopen Sell #2. Third, the forum proposals — still discussion only, with no vote scheduled — to raise the buyback share of revenue from 50% to 70% and to burn the purchases; a burn of the Litterbox would move the largest buy line on the page overnight. Fourth, the next verification-fee batch burn, with **165,215 JUP** already waiting in the burn wallet on **Sep 25 2026**. Fifth, the next staking-rewards round in early October, which pays about **50M JUP** from inside the float and does not change JUP supply.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Jupiter's JUP at **−0.03% net** over the trailing 90 days and a forward reading that rounds to **0.00%**, on a sell side that is zero across all four canonical rows. The structural mechanism is a fixed-supply token with no mint whose reserves outside the float were frozen by the February 2026 Net-Zero vote, while a revenue-funded buyback holds the **29.65M JUP** it bought this window inside the float and a small verification-fee burn removed **948,203 JUP**. The key risk is the **3.54B JUP** still held in those three reserves, whose pause is a policy that a future vote can lift. The ceiling is hard: with no mint, JUP total supply can never rise above the **6.86B JUP** that exists today.

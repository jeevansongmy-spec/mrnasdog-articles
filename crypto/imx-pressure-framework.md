IMX supply is growing even though every coin already exists. The IMX token has a hard limit of **2B** coins and all of them were created long ago, but only **904.47M** IMX count as circulating, and Immutable's treasuries release the rest a piece at a time. From Aug 24 to Nov 22 2026 the Foundation Treasury released **25.00M IMX** while nothing was bought back or burned, so circulating IMX grew **+2.76%**; other trackers show **+2.84%**, and the next 90 days point to about **+2.12%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 22 2026, IMX in circulation grew by **+2.76%**, all of it from one Foundation Treasury release of **25.00M IMX** on Sep 29 2026. Other trackers put the same window at **+2.84%**, a gap of only **0.07 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days the Immutable treasury's own record over the past year points to about **19.19M IMX** more, or **+2.12%**. IMX is a **capped coin with a treasury tap**: no new IMX can be created, but about half of all IMX still sits in two Immutable treasuries, and every release from them adds to the coins on the market.

## Sell pressure: where new IMX comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. The IMX token contract on Ethereum caps supply at **2B** IMX, that cap is fixed in the code, and total supply already equals it. The contract refuses any mint above the cap, so IMX has no staking issuance, no block rewards and no way to print more.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. IMX's multi-year vesting is over. The last scheduled release reached the Foundation Treasury on **Mar 30 2026**, and both vesting contracts that fed Immutable's treasuries now hold nothing. Some unlock calendars still show small IMX releases every four weeks, the next on Nov 28 2026, but no contract on-chain holds coins for them.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **25.00M IMX**, the only live sell row. On **Sep 29 2026** the Foundation Treasury sent 25.00M IMX to the Immutable wallet it pays out from, and that wallet passes IMX on to Immutable Chain in steps of 1M to 2M. The Foundation Treasury paid out **77.84M IMX** in 14 transfers over the past year, most of them 3M to 5M IMX a month or so, with one 10M payout in May 2026 and the 25M payout in September. Spread over the year, that comes to about **19.19M IMX** for the next 90 days.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**. No court, bankrupt lender or long lockup is holding IMX for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new IMX goes

Programmatic buyback is **0**. Immutable runs no IMX buyback, and neither treasury took in any IMX between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026.

Protocol fee burn is **0**. IMX pays for gas on Immutable Chain, but the burned part of each fee sits at a tiny floor, so the IMX destroyed on Immutable Chain rounds to nothing. The IMX token on Ethereum has no public burn at all, and its total supply stayed at exactly 2B.

Foundation buy is **0**. No Immutable treasury bought IMX on the market or took coins back in the window.

New long-term lock is **0**. IMX staked on Immutable Chain and IMX moved across the bridge both still count as circulating, so staking and bridging take nothing out of the float.

## Foundation and overhang

Two Immutable treasuries hold every IMX that is not yet circulating. The Foundation Treasury holds **721.51M IMX** and pays out every month or two; its last payout was 25.00M IMX on Sep 29 2026. Project Development Treasury 2 holds **374.01M IMX**; it sent 5M IMX five times between Nov 11 2025 and Feb 10 2026 and has not moved since. Together they hold about **1.10B IMX**, more than the whole circulating supply.

Inside the float, the Immutable payout wallet holds **22.36M IMX** after the September payout. Those coins are already counted, so moving or selling them adds nothing to supply. Both treasuries are read on-chain every day: if either balance falls, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check.

## How IMX compares to other gaming and Layer 2 tokens

IMX sits in the group of capped gaming tokens where all coins were created at launch and a treasury decides the pace of release. That is a different shape from an uncapped Layer 1 such as Ethereum, which pays validators in new coins and burns part of every fee. IMX has neither side: no new coins for validators, and no meaningful burn. Its supply moves only when an Immutable treasury moves.

Compared with gaming tokens that still run long vesting calendars, IMX is past its schedule, so there is no dated cliff to plan around. The catch is that a treasury payout has no calendar either. A capped token with a vesting contract releases a known amount on a known day; IMX releases whatever the Foundation Treasury sends, which over the past year ranged from 2.9M to 25M IMX in a single transfer.

Compared with Layer 2 tokens used for governance only, IMX also pays for gas on its own chain, yet that use burns almost nothing because the fee floor is so low. Until Immutable adds a buyback or a real burn, IMX supply can only go one way: up, as the two treasuries pay out, until the last of their 1.10B IMX is gone.

**Foundation Treasury payouts**: the treasury has paid out in most months of the past year, and nothing has left since Sep 29 2026. A payout before Feb 20 2027 is likely; a balance below 721.51M IMX is the signal.

**Project Development Treasury 2**: silent since Feb 10 2026 with 374.01M IMX. A first move in more than nine months would add to the next 90-day count.

**Immutable Chain's move to the OP Stack**: announced on Nov 6 2026 with a target of mid-2027. Immutable says balances and addresses carry over and fees rise slightly; no change to IMX supply has been announced.

## Summary

IMX is a capped coin: all **2B** IMX already exist and no more can be created. Even so, circulating IMX grew **+2.76%** in the 90 days to Nov 22 2026 because the Foundation Treasury released **25.00M IMX**, and nothing was bought back or burned. The next 90 days point to about **+2.12%**, and the real risk is size: two Immutable treasuries still hold about **1.10B IMX**, more than everything on the market today. IMX supply will keep rising in steps until those treasuries are empty or Immutable adds a way to take coins back.

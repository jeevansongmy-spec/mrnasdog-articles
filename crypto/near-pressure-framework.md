NEAR, the native coin of NEAR Protocol, has no supply cap: NEAR Protocol mints new NEAR at every epoch at **2.5% a year**, the rate since the NEAR inflation halving of **Oct 30 2025**. Over the last 90 days that mint added **7.99M NEAR**, and NEAR Foundation payouts to grantees and contractors released another **1.62M NEAR**. Against that, NEAR Intents swap fees bought **0.98M NEAR** that is held rather than burned, and the NEAR gas burn destroyed **59.8K NEAR**. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads NEAR at **+0.66% net** over 90 days against a supply-monitor reading of **+0.30%** — a gap of **0.36 percentage points**, inside tolerance. NEAR supply is growing, and the fee-funded buying covers about an eighth of the mint.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Sep 13 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads **NEAR at +0.66% net**: the sell side added **9.62M NEAR** to the market and the buy side took back **1.04M NEAR**, on a circulating supply of **1.31B NEAR**. The independent supply monitor reads the 90-day change at **+0.30%**. The gap is **0.36 percentage points**, inside the framework's half-point tolerance, so NEAR ships with **no data-conflict flag**. The difference has a plain cause: the monitor's supply figure moves by about two tenths of a percent from one day to the next on price rounding alone, and it cannot see either the Foundation payouts or the held buyback, because both happen inside coins it already counts. The forward column reads **+0.67%**, a touch higher, because the mint grows with the supply it is paid on and a new vesting grant releases more over the next 90 days. The label for NEAR is **mildly inflationary, with buybacks that hold rather than burn**.

## Sell pressure: where new NEAR comes from

Most new NEAR comes from the protocol itself. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **7.99M NEAR** over 90 days. NEAR Protocol pays validators by minting NEAR at each epoch boundary at 2.5% of total supply a year, and the figure here was read off total supply at all 296 epoch boundaries in the window, not taken from the published rate. It came to 99.5% of the full rate, because validators that miss blocks forfeit part of their reward and that part is never minted. NEAR blocks arrive every 0.61 seconds, well faster than the one-second design, but the NEAR epoch reward is paid per unit of time rather than per block, so the faster chain adds no extra NEAR. A tenth of every mint goes to the NEAR protocol treasury, which holds **1.09M NEAR** and sent nothing out in 90 days.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **14.0K NEAR**. The original NEAR allocations are fully unlocked, but one new grant was placed in a six-month NEAR vesting lockup this window: **238,951 NEAR** from the Foundation's operating wallet, vesting from **Jul 26 2026** to **Jan 25 2027**. Only coins actually withdrawn count, and the owner withdrew 14,000 NEAR on **Aug 19 2026**; 175,540 NEAR is still locked. Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **1.62M NEAR**. The NEAR Foundation pays grantees, contractors and individual payees from four payment accounts that its operating wallet tops up, and those payouts reached outside recipients in every month of the window, so the same pace is carried forward. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy supply, is **zero**: NEAR has no bankruptcy estate, and the genesis-era lockup account, holding 171K NEAR, did not move.

## Buy pressure: where new NEAR goes

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is **0.98M NEAR**. Since **Feb 23 2026** NEAR Intents, the cross-chain swap service built on NEAR Protocol, takes its fees in NEAR — bought on the market as trades settle — and pays them into three NEAR revenue wallets. That NEAR is held, not burned: the three wallets rose from 2.34M to **3.33M NEAR** across the window, and the only coins that left were 12,000 NEAR sent to the NEAR Intents bridge operator. Total NEAR supply confirms it — supply moved only by the mint and the gas burn, so none of the bought NEAR was destroyed. The pace is rising, from about 260K NEAR in July to 405K NEAR in August, but the forward column holds the trailing 90-day amount rather than the faster recent one.

Buy #2, the protocol fee burn, is **59.8K NEAR**. Every NEAR transaction pays gas in NEAR, and gas is destroyed as it is spent: all of it on plain transfers, and 70% of it when a contract is called, with the other 30% paid to the contract's owner. The NEAR burn was measured at every epoch in the window, and sampled runs of blocks matched the fall in total supply to the last unit, so the burn and the supply drop are one flow seen twice rather than two. It came to about 79% of the **75.6K NEAR** users paid in fees. Buy #3, Foundation buying, is **zero** — no NEAR treasury programme buys on the market, and the revenue-funded buying is already counted in Buy #1. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is **zero**: NEAR staking is liquid, with unstaked coins returning in about 30 hours, staked NEAR stays inside the circulating count, and the NEAR governance lock shrank across the window.

## Foundation and overhang

Three NEAR balances controlled by the project sit behind this ledger. The first is the NEAR bought with swap fees, **3.33M NEAR** across the three revenue wallets, held by project governance with the option to spend, stake or lock it. The second is the NEAR protocol treasury at **1.09M NEAR**, filled by a tenth of every epoch mint. The third is the NEAR Foundation's operating wallet, which held **6.64M NEAR** at the end of the window after taking in **11.1M NEAR** from accounts that carry no public label. Beyond the payouts counted in Sell #3, that wallet sent **1.56M NEAR** to other ecosystem and staking treasuries and at least **2.2M NEAR** into cross-chain deposit routes whose final owner cannot be read from the chain. Those transfers are tracked, not counted, because nothing shows the NEAR reaching a market. All of these balances are on-chain and re-read at every refresh: if the revenue wallets, the protocol treasury or the Foundation wallets send NEAR to outside hands between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How NEAR compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

NEAR belongs to the class of uncapped proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains that pay validators in freshly minted coins, and within that class the structural question is always what offsets the mint. Many uncapped chains lean on a base-fee burn tied to their own gas; on NEAR that burn is small, **59.8K NEAR** against a **7.99M NEAR** mint, because NEAR gas is cheap and much of the paid activity happens in a product layer rather than in raw chain gas. NEAR's larger offset comes from that product layer: NEAR Intents swap fees, taken in NEAR and held. That makes NEAR different from chains whose only sink is a gas burn, and different again from capped proof-of-work chains, whose issuance follows a fixed halving schedule toward a ceiling while NEAR's follows a percentage of a supply that keeps growing.

Against tokens that route revenue into buybacks, the NEAR distinction is destination. A buyback that burns reduces supply for good; the NEAR buyback holds, so the coins leave the market but stay in total supply, and a later governance decision could spend them. The other NEAR difference is the size of the fee base under the chain itself: NEAR Protocol collected about **$145K** of gas over 90 days, roughly **0.02%** of its market value a year, while NEAR Intents fees run into millions of dollars a month. The NEAR inflation reading therefore depends far more on how much swap revenue is captured, and on how fast the Foundation spends, than on how busy NEAR blocks are.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the end of the NEAR developer gas rebate: House of Stake ratified the change so all NEAR gas burns, and it ships with the next nearcore release, which had not appeared by **Sep 13 2026**; when it activates, Buy #2 rises toward the full fee line. Second, the proposed NEAR Sovereign Fund, floated on **Aug 4 2026** with about **30M NEAR** drawn from the protocol treasury to hold and earn yield — it is still at the discussion stage, with no vote and no transfer, and a final design could change where future mint and revenue go. Third, the pace of NEAR Foundation payouts, which peaked in August, and the unlabelled cross-chain transfers from its operating wallet, either of which could lift Sell #3. Fourth, the three NEAR revenue wallets at **3.33M NEAR**: any spend from them would turn part of Buy #1 into sell pressure. Fifth, the NEAR vesting grant, with about **118K NEAR** vesting over the next 90 days ahead of its end date of **Jan 25 2027**.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads NEAR at **+0.66% net** over the trailing 90 days and **+0.67%** over the next 90, so NEAR supply is growing. The structural mechanism is a 2.5% yearly NEAR mint, **7.99M NEAR** per 90 days, plus **1.62M NEAR** of Foundation payouts, offset by **0.98M NEAR** of held NEAR Intents buybacks and a **59.8K NEAR** gas burn. The key risk is that the buyback holds rather than burns and the Foundation's wider transfers cannot all be traced, so project-controlled NEAR can reach the market without any change to the mint. NEAR has no supply cap: until swap revenue captured in NEAR grows several times over, the NEAR mint stays the larger force.

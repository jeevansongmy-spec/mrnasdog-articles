NEAR Protocol minted **7.97M NEAR** over the last 90 days. That figure was not taken from the published rate — it was measured, by reading the NEAR chain's own supply on both sides of all **294** epoch boundaries inside the window. Annualised it is **2.49% a year**, against the 2.5% ceiling NEAR has run since the halving activated on **Oct 29 2025**. Against that mint, the NEAR Intents buyback took **0.66M NEAR** off the open market and holds it, and the gas burn destroyed **0.06M**. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **+0.56% net** over the last 90 days and **+0.56%** forward, against a supply-monitor reading of **+0.93%**. NEAR is **uncapped and effectively 100% circulating** — the mint, not an unlock calendar, is the whole story.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads NEAR at **+0.56% net**: **8.06M NEAR** of sell-side supply against **0.72M NEAR** of buy-side offset, on a circulating base of **1,304.9M NEAR**. The supply monitor reads the same trailing window at **+0.93%**, a gap of **0.37 percentage points** — inside tolerance, so this build ships **no monitor-gap chip** and needs no reconciliation walk. Most of that residual is mechanical: the framework credits the NEAR Intents buyback as buy pressure while the monitor still counts the buyback multisig as circulating supply. Forward, the framework reads **+0.56%** for the next 90 days, because every mechanism in the NEAR ledger — the epoch mint, the Intents buyback, the gas burn — was live for the whole window and is still live now. NEAR is **mildly and persistently inflationary by design**: an uncapped proof-of-stake chain whose halved mint is being chipped at, not cancelled, by its own buy side.

## Sell pressure: where new NEAR comes from

Almost all of NEAR's sell pressure is one row. NEAR Protocol pays validators by minting new NEAR at the end of every epoch, and the mint is sized from the epoch's elapsed time rather than its block count — which matters, because NEAR blocks are currently landing every **0.61 seconds** and a NEAR epoch is closing in about **7.3 hours**, not the twelve the nominal schedule implies. Measured across all 294 epoch boundaries in the window, the NEAR mint came to **7.97M NEAR**, or **2.49% a year** — a whisker under the 2.5% ceiling, the difference being validator uptime, since reward a validator does not earn is never minted at all. Roughly a tenth of each epoch mint is credited to NEAR's ecosystem account rather than to validators; both legs are new NEAR and both sit inside this row.

Vesting unlocks contribute **nothing**. NEAR's original distribution to team, backers and foundation finished releasing on **Oct 13 2025**, and the unlock trackers now read NEAR as fully unlocked with no event scheduled through the rest of 2026. There is no cliff, no linear release, no lockup contract still draining — which is why NEAR's circulating supply and its total supply are the same number. Foundation and unscheduled releases contribute **0.09M NEAR**: a NEAR Foundation payments account fell from 82.0K NEAR to 0.7K across the window, and one of two community fund accounts shed 7.3K. That is a real, dated, on-chain release of previously idle NEAR, and it is booked as such — but at six thousandths of a percent of supply it is a rounding entry beside the mint. The long-term-locked and bankruptcy row is **zero** and always has been: no estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution holds NEAR.

## Buy pressure: where new NEAR goes

NEAR's largest buy row is a programmatic buyback funded by NEAR Intents, the network's cross-chain intent and swap layer. Since **Feb 23 2026**, the revenue NEAR captures from Intents is spent buying NEAR on the open market. The number that matters is the captured revenue, not the headline take: users paid roughly **$9.9M** of Intents fees over the window, but only **$1.8M** of that — about a fifth — is NEAR's own, the rest going to the frontends and distribution partners that route the flow. Reading the buyback multisig at both ends of the window rather than trusting a cumulative dashboard total, it grew from **0.26M** to **0.92M NEAR**: **0.66M** of realised buying. Those NEAR are held. They are not burned and not staked, and the wallet has never sent anything out — so the buyback removes NEAR from the tradable float without shrinking NEAR's total supply.

The protocol fee burn adds **0.06M NEAR**. NEAR destroys 70% of every gas fee and rebates the other 30% to the contract owner, a split confirmed live on chain at this build. There is no burn wallet to watch on NEAR — the fee is erased from the supply figure itself, so the supply reading is the surface, and a separate count of fees erased block by block agrees with it to within 6%. The burn is small because NEAR's gas price sits at the protocol floor: cheap blockspace makes a thin burn. Foundation buying is **zero** beyond the Intents buyback already counted, and new long-term locking is **zero** too — a NEAR delegator unbonds within days, so staking is not a lock, and the rewards staking pays are freshly minted NEAR already counted on the sell side. A high staked share does not offset the NEAR mint; it is the mint.

## Foundation and overhang

Four identified NEAR balances are watched, and three of them grew rather than sold. The Intents buyback multisig holds **0.92M NEAR**, up from 0.26M three months ago, with zero outflows since it was created. NEAR's protocol ecosystem account holds **0.96M NEAR**, up from 0.16M, and keeps taking its tenth of every epoch mint. The two community fund accounts hold **1.64M** and **0.50M** respectively — the first grew, the second shed the 7.3K already booked. The NEAR Foundation payments account that supplied the rest of that release now holds **0.7K NEAR** and is refilled from treasury reserves the Foundation does not publish as a single address, so the wider NEAR Foundation balance stays an opaque overhang tracked through disclosure rather than through a wallet read. On top of all of it sits a proposal, floated on **Aug 4 2026**, to seed a **30M NEAR** sovereign fund out of protocol treasury and revenue — capacity with no vote, no quorum and no execution behind it. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled row at the next refresh.

## How NEAR compares to other uncapped proof-of-stake chains

NEAR sits in the same structural class as the large uncapped proof-of-stake layer-1s — chains that pay for security by printing, with no hard ceiling to fall back on. Against that class NEAR looks unusually disciplined on the sell side. Chains that still run a five-percent-plus staking emission are pushing several times NEAR's **2.49%** into the float every year, and chains with a live vesting calendar are stacking scheduled unlocks on top of the mint. NEAR has neither: the emission was halved on Oct 29 2025 and the unlock calendar is finished, so the epoch mint is the only supply-creating mechanism NEAR still has.

Where NEAR differs from the fee-burn chains is the shape of its buy side. A chain that burns its base fee turns usage directly into supply destruction and can flip deflationary when activity spikes. NEAR burns too, but only 70% of a fee priced at the protocol floor, so the burn is a rounding entry — **0.06M** against a **7.97M** mint. Instead NEAR's real buy-side mechanism is a revenue buyback, which puts it closer to the exchange tokens and perp venues that convert fee revenue into open-market purchases. The critical difference is that NEAR's buyback **holds** rather than burns, and captures only about a fifth of the fee stack rather than all of it, so the offset is smaller and softer than a burn of the same headline size. For NEAR to reach net-flat, the Intents buyback would need to run roughly **twelve times** its current rate, or NEAR's emission would need another halving. Both are on the table in NEAR's governance, and neither has a vote behind it.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the developer gas rebate. A House of Stake vote that closed on **Jul 3 2026** passed comfortably and removes the 30% rebate so that all contract gas burns; it ships with a client release targeted around **Sep 2026**, and the NEAR chain was still reading the 30% rebate at this build. When it activates, the NEAR fee burn rises from about **0.06M** to about **0.09M** per 90 days — real, but not enough to change the sign. Second, the NEAR Sovereign Fund proposal of **Aug 4 2026**: if it reaches a vote with quorum and an execution height, its stated ambition of moving NEAR to a fixed supply is the single largest available change to this page. Third, the Intents revenue line, which is what sizes the buyback — the capture ratio, currently about a fifth of gross fees, matters more than the headline volume. Fourth, the buyback multisig itself: it has never sent NEAR out, and the first outflow would move that balance from a buy row to a sell row. Fifth, NEAR's ecosystem account, which has been accruing its tenth of the mint without spending it.

## Summary

NEAR Protocol is an uncapped proof-of-stake layer-1 whose supply grows by roughly **2.49% a year** — a rate measured off the NEAR chain itself rather than read from a published parameter, and half what it was before the Oct 29 2025 halving. Over the last 90 days that produced **7.97M NEAR** of new supply against **0.72M** of buying and burning, for **+0.56% net**, and the same again projected forward. The structural mechanism is clean and easy to audit: no vesting left, no cliff, no unlock calendar, circulating equal to total, and one mint that never stops. The key risk is precisely that permanence — NEAR has no cap to grow into, so the only paths to flat run through governance, and the two that could get there, a bigger buyback and a fixed supply, are proposals rather than schedules.

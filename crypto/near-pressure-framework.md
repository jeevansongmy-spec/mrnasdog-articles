The NEAR Protocol coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/near](https://mrnasdog.com/research/near). Below: supply, line by line.

NEAR supply is growing, and it will keep growing at about the same pace. Between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026 the network created **8,026,459 NEAR** for stakers and its treasury, while gas burned **80,364 NEAR**, for a net rise of **+0.61%** in 90 days (other trackers show **+0.54%**). NEAR Protocol has no supply cap: new coins come from a yearly ceiling of 2.5%, which a vote passed in November 2026 will lower to 1.6% over two years, starting no earlier than February 2027.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 19 2026, NEAR supply rose by **+0.61%**, and the next 90 days look the same at **+0.61%**. Other trackers show **+0.54%** for the same stretch, a difference of only **0.06 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. Almost everything comes from one source: the new NEAR printed every epoch to pay the people who secure the network. The burn, the only thing that removes coins, takes back about one coin in every hundred printed. The label that fits is plain: NEAR is inflationary by design, at a slow and steady pace.

## Sell pressure: where new NEAR comes from

**Protocol inflation** is the whole story on the sell side. NEAR runs in epochs of a little over seven hours, and at the start of each one the network creates fresh NEAR. Nine tenths go to validators and the people who stake with them, and one tenth goes to the protocol treasury. Across the 300 epochs from Aug 21 to Nov 19 2026 that came to **8,026,459 NEAR**, close to **89,200 NEAR** a day. The amount is tied to time, not to how many blocks are made, so faster blocks do not raise it. Because the ceiling is a share of total supply, the daily figure creeps up a little as supply grows.

**Vesting unlocks** add nothing. The lockup accounts from the 2020 launch already sit inside the circulating figure, and no team or investor batch is waiting to be released.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks** also add nothing. The protocol treasury, the NEAR Foundation and the wallets that hold bought-back NEAR all hold coins that already count as circulating, so when they spend or sell, the count does not change.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy** coins add nothing either. No court case or failed firm is holding NEAR for a later payout.

## Buy pressure: where new NEAR goes

**The protocol fee burn** is the one real sink. Gas paid for transactions is destroyed, and from Aug 21 to Nov 19 2026 that removed **80,364 NEAR**. On Nov 4 2026 a governance change took effect: until then, 30% of the gas spent on apps went to the people who built them, and since then all of it is burned. The burn now runs near **908 NEAR** a day, which points to about **81,757 NEAR** over the next 90 days. Even so, that is about one coin destroyed for every hundred created, because gas on NEAR is very cheap.

**The programmatic buyback** is real money but books zero. Since February 2026, part of the fees from NEAR Intents, the network's cross-chain swap service, buys NEAR on the open market. The wallets that receive it grew by **1,469,285 NEAR** between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026. Those coins are kept, not destroyed, so they still count as circulating, and anyone can watch the wallets on-chain. The buying matters for price, which the demand side of the page covers, but it does not shrink supply.

**Foundation buys** are zero: the NEAR Foundation has no plan to buy NEAR back. The US spot fund that began trading on Sep 29 2026 and the listed companies that hold NEAR all buy coins that are already circulating.

**New long-term locks** are zero as well. Staked NEAR, and NEAR locked to vote in House of Stake, the network's governance body, still count as circulating, so more locking takes nothing out of the count.

## Foundation and overhang

Three groups of team-controlled coins sit inside the float. The protocol treasury holds **1,689,903 NEAR** on Nov 19 2026, up from **883,789 NEAR** on Aug 21 2026, because its tenth of every epoch's new coins lands there. The three NEAR Intents buyback wallets hold **4,368,016 NEAR** between them, up from **2,898,731 NEAR**. The NEAR Foundation's known payment accounts hold under **16,000 NEAR**, and the Foundation has published no recent figure for its full reserve.

None of these balances is new supply, so none of them adds to the 90-day count. We read them again at every rebuild. If the treasury's or the buyback wallets' balance falls between rebuilds, the coins that left are named on the page at the next rebuild, so a sale from them cannot pass unseen.

## How NEAR compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

NEAR belongs to the group of proof-of-stake chains with no hard cap, where new coins pay for security and a fee burn pushes back. Ethereum works the same way, but Ethereum's issuance rises with the amount staked, while NEAR's is a fixed share of total supply each year. Solana also has no cap and burns part of its fees, but its yearly rate falls on a set schedule toward a long-term floor, while NEAR's rate only changes when holders vote for it.

Chains with a hard cap, such as Bitcoin, or a capped coin like Avalanche, end their new supply at a known point. NEAR does not. Its rate is set by governance: in October 2025 it was cut from 5% to 2.5%, and in November 2026 holders approved a slow slide to 1.6%. That makes NEAR a chain whose supply growth is low and falling, but still clearly above zero.

On the burn side, NEAR sits at the small end. Gas is cheap by design, so the burn removes about one coin in a hundred created. NEAR's buyback, paid for by swap fees from NEAR Intents, is larger than its burn, but it keeps the coins instead of destroying them, unlike chains whose buybacks burn what they buy.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The issuance cut.** House of Stake ratified the cut from 2.5% to 1.6% on Nov 16 2026. A 90-day grace period follows, so the first small step down cannot come before about Feb 14 2027, and validators still have to adopt the upgrade that carries it.

**The full gas burn.** Since Nov 4 2026 every unit of gas is burned. The coming weeks show whether the burn holds near 908 NEAR a day or moves with how busy the network is.

**The buyback wallets.** The three wallets held 4,368,016 NEAR on Nov 19 2026. If any of it is ever destroyed, it would become a real sink; if it is sold, it is spent from coins already counted.

**The treasury.** The protocol treasury keeps filling at about 8,900 NEAR a day. A large outflow would show how the network spends it.

## Summary

NEAR supply grew **+0.61%** between Aug 21 and Nov 19 2026: the network created **8,026,459 NEAR** for stakers and its treasury, while gas burned **80,364 NEAR**. NEAR Protocol has no supply cap and no vesting left, so the epoch payout is the only source of new NEAR and the gas burn the only sink; the Intents buyback keeps the coins it buys. The next 90 days look the same at about **+0.61%**, even with all gas now burned. The main change ahead is the approved slide from 2.5% to 1.6% a year, which cannot begin before about February 2027.

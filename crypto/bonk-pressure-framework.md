**TL;DR.** Bonk is a token that cannot inflate and still put **5% of its supply** on the market in a single day. The BONK mint on Solana has an empty mint authority, so the **87.99T BONK** in existence is final — but on **Jul 6 2026** a malicious governance proposal emptied the BonkDAO treasury of **4.43T BONK**, and by **Jul 20 2026** that balance had dispersed into exchange-facing wallets. The Pressure Framework reads BONK at **+5.03%** for the trailing 90 days against a supply monitor at **+0.05%**, and at **0.00%** forward, because the treasury that produced the number no longer holds anything.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 13 2026**, the framework reads **BONK at +5.03% net**. Sell pressure totals **4.43T BONK**, buy pressure totals **zero**, against a circulating base of **87.99T BONK**. Our supply monitor reads the same window at **+0.05%**, a gap of **4.98 percentage points**, so a monitor-gap chip ships on the BONK overview page. The gap is structural rather than a disagreement about facts: the monitor watches coins being created, and no BONK was created — the coins that moved already existed and were already counted as circulating, because BONK's counted float equals its on-chain supply to the unit. Forward, the framework reads **0.00%**: the BonkDAO treasury account holds **0.00033 BONK** today, so the one sell engine on this ledger has nothing left to fire. BONK is best characterised as a **hard-capped supply with a governance-shaped float** — the risk is never issuance, it is custody.

## Sell pressure: where new BONK comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and it is zero in the strongest form available. The BONK mint account was read directly this session and its mint authority field is empty — the key that would create new BONK was destroyed, and the freeze authority with it. There is no block reward, no staking emission and no rebase on the token, so total supply can only ever fall, and only by burning. Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **zero** as well: BONK was distributed by airdrop in **Dec 2022** with no venture cap table behind it, the single early-contributor tranche finished its three-year release earlier in 2026, and the counted circulating figure now equals the full on-chain supply of **87,994,589,272,353.95 BONK**. There is no locked bucket left for a cliff to release. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero**: BONK has no estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution.

That leaves Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, carrying the entire sell side at **4.43T BONK**. The mechanism was one transaction. Proposal **BIP #76** was posted to BonkDAO's governance portal on **Jun 30 2026**, framed as a governance renewal plan; an attacker bought just over the quorum threshold — about **882.38B BONK** against a required **879.95B** — and on **Jul 6 2026** the proposal executed on a **2.9%** turnout. The DAO treasury account fell from **4,426,104,450,305.97 BONK** to **0.00033** in that single execution. What matters for the framework is what happened next, and that is readable on-chain: the receiving wallet and its successor both hold nothing today, and the balance resolves into a **1.19T** deposit at an exchange address on **Jul 16 2026** plus **3.20T** fanned out in 400B-to-800B parcels between **Jul 17 2026** and **Jul 20 2026**, one leg of which touches a Binance hot wallet. Every destination reads zero now. This was not a custody freeze; it was a distribution.

## Buy pressure: where new BONK goes

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is **zero**, and the reason is the most important tokenomics fact about BONK today. The launchpad that funds the BONK ecosystem still routes **51%** of its revenue into buying BONK, but since **Dec 3 2025** that BONK goes into a listed company's digital-asset treasury instead of being destroyed. The wallet the launchpad publishes for those purchases holds no BONK and last transacted on **May 4 2026**, before this window opened, so the buying now runs through off-chain custody the framework cannot measure. Scale settles the rest of the argument: the launchpad reported about **$29.0K** of revenue so far in **Aug 2026**, roughly **$2.2K** a day. Even a full quarter at that rate would be a rounding error against an 88 trillion supply. Carried at zero until a readable figure exists.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **zero**, which contradicts most of what is written about BONK. The buy-and-burn line was removed from the launchpad's revenue split on **Dec 3 2025** and replaced by the treasury buying above, and the burn wallet the project still publishes holds **217.2B BONK** that was never actually destroyed — its last movement was **Apr 10 2026**, five weeks before this window opened. Nothing was burned in these 90 days, and the token itself has no fee burn built in. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **zero**: no project entity disclosed a dated, sized open-market BONK purchase inside the window, and the insider buying reported in **Jun 2026** and **Jul 2026** was of company shares, not tokens. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero**: no lockup contract was deployed, and the community-approved **1T** burn is still waiting on a holder milestone that has not been reached.

## Foundation and overhang

Three overhangs are tracked for BONK. The first, the BonkDAO treasury, is now spent: the governance account holds **0.00033 BONK** and is refreshed by chain read. The second is the corporate treasury of the Nasdaq-listed company that buys BONK with launchpad revenue — roughly **3%** of supply on its last disclosure, with a stated target of **5%** — held in broker custody rather than a single readable wallet, so it is refreshed by a bi-weekly walk of company disclosures. The third is the **217.2B BONK** sitting in the launchpad's burn wallet, which was bought to be destroyed and never was; it is readable on-chain and re-checked at every rebuild. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh. The corporate treasury is the one that matters most: it is the only remaining pool large enough to repeat what July did, and unlike the DAO it has a commercial reason to sell.

## How BONK compares to other fixed-supply memecoins

Against its structural peers, BONK's issuance side is as clean as the class allows. A fixed-supply memecoin with a renounced mint — the same shape as the large Ethereum-and-BNB-Chain meme tokens — has no emission curve, no validator subsidy and no unlock calendar, so the usual sources of dilution simply do not exist. Where those peers differ is on the two discretionary levers, and BONK currently scores badly on both. Peers that run a live revenue buy-and-burn shrink their float every quarter, however slowly; BONK's burn was switched off in **Dec 2025** and replaced by an accumulation the market cannot verify, which converts a deflationary mechanism into a neutral one.

The sharper contrast is governance. A memecoin whose float is fully distributed and whose treasury is small has almost no custody risk; BONK carried roughly **5%** of its supply in a DAO treasury executing on-chain votes with no timelock and no multisig backstop, and that design is what turned a cheap proposal into a **4.43T BONK** transfer. Chains with quarterly buyback tokens or foundation-held reserves face the same class of risk, but usually behind a human approval step. For BONK the practical comparison is this: the supply is as hard as bitcoin's cap in one sense — no new coins, ever — while the tradable float is softer than a typical foundation-run L1, because a single vote could move it. Judge BONK on custody and on whether a burn ever restarts, not on issuance.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, whether BonkDAO ships the governance repairs discussed after the attack — an execution timelock, a multisig veto on large treasury movements and a real quorum floor. Second, whether the launchpad's revenue split ever restores a burn line; today it has none, and the published burn wallet has been idle since **Apr 10 2026**. Third, the corporate treasury's disclosures: a purchase toward the stated **5%** target would create the first measurable buy row this ledger has had all year, and a sale would create a new Sell #3. Fourth, the pending **1T** milestone burn, which fires only when unique holders pass one million — that single event would be worth about **1.1%** of supply removed. Fifth, any recovery or freeze of the July loot; the wallets read zero today, but a clawback would change the trailing reading at the next rebuild.

## Summary

BONK's issuance risk is zero and permanent: the mint authority on the Solana mint is empty, vesting is finished, and the counted float already equals every BONK that exists. Its real risk is custody, and that risk materialised — a **Jul 6 2026** governance execution moved **4.43T BONK** out of the BonkDAO treasury and into the market inside two weeks, which is the whole of the framework's **+5.03%** trailing reading. Forward the number is **0.00%**, not because anything improved but because that treasury is empty and the buy side is switched off: the launchpad buy-and-burn was abolished in **Dec 2025** and its burn wallet still holds **217.2B BONK** it never destroyed. The ceiling is fixed at **87.99T BONK** and can only fall; whether it ever does depends on a burn restarting, not on the protocol.

BONK is a hard-capped Solana memecoin: its mint authority is renounced on-chain, so its supply of about **87.99T** can only fall. The sell ledger is zero in every row — no emission, no live vesting, no bankruptcy estate — while the buy ledger removes only about **0.47B BONK** a quarter through a small residual burn, because the launchpad buyback now buys and holds rather than burns. That is a net of about **0.0%**, and our supply monitor agrees at **+0.11%** — a gap of just **0.11 percentage points**, well inside tolerance, so no warning chip ships. BONK is a fixed-supply token whose only supply force is a slow, permanent burn.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 5 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **BONK at about 0.0% net**, both on the trailing view and forward — a sell side that is empty and a buy side that removes roughly **0.47B BONK** against a supply near **88T**, which is about **0.0005%** and rounds to flat. Our supply monitor reads **+0.11%** for the same trailing window, a gap of only **0.11 percentage points** — under the framework's 0.5-point tolerance, so no **⚠ monitor-gap** chip is attached. The small monitor reading is rounding noise in a market-cap-over-price supply estimate, because BONK's on-chain total cannot actually grow. Both views agree on the shape: BONK is **fixed by design with a marginal burn** — a capped memecoin whose supply story is settled.

## Sell pressure: where new BONK comes from

The answer is nowhere, and that is the whole point. Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero** because BONK has no emission and no mint path: the token's mint authority is renounced on-chain, read null this session, so no new BONK can ever be created. There is no staking-reward stream and no protocol issuance of any kind. Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — is **zero** because the only vesting BONK ever carried was the 21% early-contributor allocation on a three-year linear schedule that began **Jan 1 2023** and physically expired in **2026**; unlock trackers now read BONK as **100% unlocked** with the schedule ended, so no cliff can fall inside the window.

Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is **zero** on value but carries the one overhang worth watching: the BonkDAO treasury. On **Jul 6 2026** a governance attack passed a malicious proposal, Bonk Improvement Proposal #76 on Solana's Realms tooling, and moved roughly **4.43T BONK** out of the treasury to an attacker-controlled multisig. That is a large number, but those coins were already inside the circulating count — BONK is fully unlocked — so the transfer added **no new supply** to the market, which is exactly why the monitor stayed flat. Crucially, the attempted sell-off was **largely blocked**: exchanges suspended BONK deposits, about **$19M** of the loot still sits unmoved in the attacker's multisig, and only about **$0.19M** ever reached an exchange as an attempted cash-out. The framework therefore books no realised sell pressure — only a hostile overhang to watch. Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero** because no bankruptcy estate, court distribution or escrow applies to BONK.

## Buy pressure: where new BONK goes

BONK does have a real buy-side mechanism, and it changed shape in late 2025. Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is booked at **zero**, but not because nothing is happening: since a **Dec 3 2025** deal, a Nasdaq-listed company, **Bonk Inc**, has taken half of the LetsBonk.fun launchpad revenue and used it to buy BONK on the open market and **hold** it in a corporate treasury, targeting **5%** of supply. That is a switch away from the old buy-and-burn toward buy-and-hold. Its one large purchase — about **2.26T BONK**, roughly **3%** of supply — landed in **Oct 2025**, outside this window, and the in-window accumulation is funded by only a few million dollars a quarter of platform revenue. Because the treasury custody is not a single readable wallet and the on-chain revenue router holds nothing at rest, the amount added inside the window is opaque, so the framework leaves the row at zero rather than guess a figure it cannot defend.

Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is what is left of the burn narrative, at about **0.47B BONK** over the last 90 days, the residual run rate that survived the shift to holding. Every burn is permanent, because the renounced mint means destroyed BONK can never be re-issued. But against a supply near **88T**, 0.47B is about **0.0005%** — far too small to move the headline. Buy #3 — Foundation buy — is **zero**: no discretionary open-market buying is disclosed outside the Bonk Inc treasury program already described. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is **zero** because BONK has no staking mechanism that takes coins out of circulation and no new lockup contract was deployed; the Bonk Inc treasury is a corporate holding, not a locked contract.

## Foundation and overhang

BONK carries two team-controlled overhangs. The first is the **BonkDAO treasury**, originally the **15.79%** allocation set aside for the DAO, and it is the one that was hit: the **Jul 6 2026** governance attack drained about **4.43T BONK** from it in a single proposal. Because BONK is fully unlocked, both the treasury and the attacker's wallet fall inside the circulating count, so this changed custody rather than supply. Crucially, the sell-off was **largely blocked**: exchanges suspended BONK deposits, about **$19M** of the loot still sits unmoved in the attacker's multisig, and only about **$0.19M** reached an exchange as an attempted cash-out — so the framework books no realised sell pressure, only a hostile overhang to watch. The second overhang is the **Bonk Inc** corporate treasury, about **2.26T BONK** or **3%** of supply, which the company intends to grow rather than sell but which could one day reverse. The framework tracks both by hand. The trigger sentence follows: if either balance falls between refreshes as BONK is sold onto the market, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How BONK compares to other fixed-supply memecoins

BONK belongs to the class of **fixed-supply memecoins with a burn** — the same structural family as Shiba Inu, and the opposite of Dogecoin. Against **Dogecoin** the contrast is total: DOGE is an uncapped proof-of-work coin that mints a fixed **5B DOGE** every year forever, a permanently inflationary tail, whereas BONK has a renounced mint and can never issue another token. Against **Shiba Inu** the comparison is closer — both are hard-capped and both lean on a burn narrative — but the mechanics differ: SHIB's burn is community-driven and sporadic, while BONK's launchpad revenue has now been redirected from burning into a corporate **buy-and-hold** treasury run by a Nasdaq-listed company.

The important truth for all three is that a burn only matters relative to supply. BONK removing **0.47B** a quarter against **88T** is a rounding error, exactly as SHIB's burns are against its own multi-hundred-trillion float; neither is meaningfully deflationary at these rates, however loud the marketing. Against exchange tokens that run quarterly buyback-and-burn from real revenue and genuinely shrink their float by whole percentage points, BONK is the mirror image — the burn is there, but the base is so vast that supply is effectively frozen. For an inflation lens, that makes BONK unusually clean to read: with no emission and no live vesting, the only way its number moves is a burn large enough to register, or a treasury event large enough to flood the market.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the fallout from the **Jul 6 2026** governance attack: whether the roughly **4.43T BONK** in the attacker's multisig gets frozen and recovered, or begins hitting exchanges — a sustained sell-down of that stash is the one event that could put a real number in Sell #3. Second, the DAO-approved **1 trillion BONK** burn tied to the 1-million-holder milestone, which is approved but still pending execution; if it fires it would remove about **1.1%** of supply in one event and briefly turn the page deflationary. Third, the annual **BURNmas** event, historically late December, which sits outside this window but is the largest scheduled burn on the calendar. Fourth, the ecosystem buyback run rate: a sharp change in launchpad revenue would move the quarterly burn figure, though it would need to grow by orders of magnitude to matter. Fifth, any governance reform after the attack, since the treasury's security now bears directly on the size of the overhang this page tracks.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads BONK at about **0.0% net** over the next 90 days, from an empty sell ledger and a buy side that burns only about **0.47B BONK** against an **88T** supply. The structural mechanism is a **renounced mint**: no new BONK can ever be created, the token is **100% unlocked** with no live vesting, and supply can only move down. The key risk is not dilution but overhang: the **4.43T BONK** pulled from the BonkDAO treasury in the July 2026 governance attack — whose sale was largely blocked, with most still frozen and almost none reaching the market — could become sell pressure if the attacker ever liquidates. Our supply monitor reads **+0.11%**, a gap of just **0.11 percentage points** that stays inside tolerance and ships no chip. The cap is effectively fixed, the burn is real but marginal, and there is no emission anywhere in the design that could push supply the other way.

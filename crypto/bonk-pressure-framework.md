**TL;DR.** BONK cannot inflate. The Solana mint authority on the BONK token reads **null**, vesting finished long ago, and every one of the **87,994,583,899,213 BONK** in existence is already counted as circulating — and yet the Pressure Framework reads BONK at **+5.03%** for the 90 days to **Aug 23 2026**. The reason is a single line in the ledger: the BonkDAO treasury released **4,426.10B BONK** in one governance execution on **Jul 6 2026** and the chain shows every wallet on that trail emptied within two weeks. Against it, the measured BONK burn for the whole quarter was **147.5M BONK** — about **$480**. Our supply monitor reads **-0.10%**, a gap of **5.13 percentage points**, because the monitor counts coins created and none were. BONK is **a fixed-supply memecoin whose only real inflation is custody changing hands**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 23 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **BONK at +5.03% net**: sell pressure of **4,426.10B BONK** from the BonkDAO treasury, against buy pressure of **147.5M BONK** of measured burn, on a circulating base of **87,994,583.899M BONK**. Our supply monitor reads **-0.10%** for the same window, so the gap is **5.13 percentage points** and a monitor-gap flag ships with this build. The disagreement is mechanical rather than an error on either side: the monitor infers supply from market capitalisation divided by price, which recovers the Solana mint supply — and the mint supply did not move, because the BONK mint authority is null and no BONK was created. The framework measures something the mint supply cannot see, which is a team-controlled overhang leaving team control and reaching the open market. Both readings are correct about different questions. BONK is best labelled **a coin that cannot inflate, hit by a one-off custody shock inside the window**.

## Sell pressure: where new BONK comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **zero**, and for BONK that is the strongest possible zero. Reading the BONK mint account directly on Solana returns a **mintAuthority** field of **null** and a **freezeAuthority** field of **null**. Null is not the same as unused. An unused authority is a key somebody still holds; a null authority means the Solana token program will reject a mint instruction from every key that exists, forever. BONK has no staking emission, no block subsidy and no liquidity-mining programme, so there is no second issuance channel to check. The supply field on that mint can only fall.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **zero** as well. BONK launched with its entire distribution released across eight community buckets and the unlock calendar has been finished for some time — the vesting aggregators we checked this build report **100.00%** of BONK unlocked and not a single dated unlock remaining in 2026 or beyond. There is no cliff behind this row and no schedule that could reopen it.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is the entire quarter: **4,426.10B BONK**. On **Jul 6 2026** a governance proposal that had been submitted on **Jun 30 2026** passed with no timelock and paid **4,426,104,450,305 BONK** out of the BonkDAO treasury account in one instruction. That treasury account holds **6 BONK** today. We traced the outflow rather than trusting the reporting, and the trail closes: **40B BONK** went the same afternoon to a single-purpose address that swept itself to zero, and **4,386.10B BONK** went into a three-key multisig which forwarded the entire balance onward on **Jul 16 2026** and now reads zero. That balance then dispersed in **400B** tranches across **Jul 16 2026** to **Jul 20 2026** into deposit-shaped wallets that hold nothing today. Every account on the trail reads about zero, and the balance changes across every fetched transaction sum to exactly the difference between the first and last balances on each hop — so no page of history was truncated and nothing was inferred from a gap. Under the Pressure Framework, a team-controlled overhang whose balance falls between refreshes books the outflow as sell pressure, and this overhang did not fall — it emptied.

Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **zero**. There is no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution attached to BONK, so this row has never had a mechanism behind it.

## Buy pressure: where new BONK goes

Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero** — and the reason is a mechanism change most BONK coverage has not caught up with. The BONK launchpad used to route a slice of every platform fee into buying BONK and burning it. Effective **Dec 4 2025** that slice was repointed: **51%** of platform fees now buy BONK for a Nasdaq-listed treasury company's digital-asset trust instead of destroying it. Buying is not burning, and buying into custody is not even measurable the way an on-chain burn is. The one Solana address the project names for that treasury holds **0 BONK**, so nothing accumulates anywhere we can read, and the platform's own revenue page reports **$59.65K** of fees for the whole of August 2026. With a single unverifiable quantum and an unreadable destination, the framework marks this row open at zero rather than estimating it.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **147.5M BONK**, and it is the row where BONK sets its trap. "Burn" is claimed for BONK in three different shapes and only one of them destroys anything. The first is a reduction of the BONK mint supply itself, which is what a Solana token burn does — measured across the window, supply fell from **87,994,731,406,479** to **87,994,583,899,213**, a reduction of **147,507,266 BONK** or **0.000168%** of supply, worth roughly **$480**. The second is a dead address: the Solana incinerator holds **276,674 BONK**, which is dust. The third is the launchpad's own named burn wallet, and this is the one worth staring at — it still **holds** **217,201,065,347 BONK** it never destroyed, and its token account's most recent transaction of any kind is dated **Apr 10 2026**, before this window even opened. A named burn address is a claim; the mint supply field is the measurement. BONK's famous cumulative burn headlines — **2.47T** burned, the **1.69T** holiday burn — are historical and sit entirely outside this window.

Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **zero**. The listed treasury company reports accumulating BONK in its filings, but nothing arrived at an identified on-chain project wallet inside the window that we could read, so there is no measurable purchase to book. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero** and cannot be anything else. BONK's total supply and its circulating supply differ by about **3,100 BONK** out of **88T** — the counted float already includes every coin that exists. Staking BONK, pairing BONK into liquidity or locking BONK for rewards moves it sideways within that float; none of it can be taken off a market that already counts it all.

## Foundation and overhang

Four team-controlled overhangs were enumerated for BONK and all four are now visible on the page. The first was the BonkDAO treasury, historically the single largest holder of BONK, which released **4,426,104,450,305 BONK** on **Jul 6 2026** and holds **6 BONK** today — it is spent, and it is the whole of Sell #3. The second is the launchpad's burn wallet, which still holds **217,201,065,347 BONK** and has been idle since **Apr 10 2026**; that BONK was bought with platform fees for the stated purpose of destruction and never destroyed, so it remains a live overhang rather than a completed burn, and it is read on-chain at every refresh. The third is the buying that replaced the burn: **51%** of platform fees now purchase BONK into a listed company's custody, whose named Solana address holds **0 BONK**, so that overhang is opaque and is tracked through corporate disclosure rather than through a balance; the same company already discloses holding close to **3%** of every BONK in existence, roughly **2.4T BONK**, with a stated target of **5%**. The fourth is the group of wallets that took the treasury on **Jul 6 2026**; every one of them reads about zero today, so the overhang is spent rather than pending. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh — that is the rule, and the BonkDAO treasury is what it looks like when it fires.

## How BONK compares to other fixed-supply memecoins

BONK belongs to the class of memecoins whose issuance is closed by construction rather than by policy — the same structural class as a hard-capped Solana launch token whose mint authority has been renounced. Within that class, the useful axis is not whether new coins can appear, because they cannot, but where the pre-existing coins sit. A capped memecoin whose float is fully distributed, like BONK, has no escrow to drain and no cliff calendar to fear; its only supply risk is concentration. A capped memecoin whose float sits mostly inside one vesting escrow has the opposite profile: issuance is impossible, yet supply reaching the market grows every quarter as the escrow is drawn down at the creators' discretion. BONK looked like the safe end of that axis right up until Jul 6 2026, when the concentration risk fired in a way no unlock calendar would ever have shown.

Against uncapped continuous-emission chains, the contrast is sharper still. A proof-of-stake Layer 1 pays a staking subsidy every day whether anyone wants the coins or not, so its inflation is a rate you can forecast from protocol parameters. BONK has no rate to forecast — its forward sell side is zero because there is nothing left to release and no way to create more. That makes BONK forward-quiet in a way an emitting chain never is, and it means a Pressure Framework reading of BONK is dominated by discrete events rather than by a curve.

Against the buyback-and-burn exchange-token model, BONK is the cautionary comparison. An exchange token that burns quarterly publishes a destruction figure you can check against the token's own supply field, and the two agree. BONK carries all the vocabulary of that model — a burn wallet, a revenue share, cumulative burn totals in the trillions — while its supply field moved by **0.000168%** in a quarter and the wallet doing the "burning" is holding **217B** of undestroyed tokens. The mechanism to check is always the supply field, never the branding.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, whether the launchpad's burn wallet moves. It has held **217,201,065,347 BONK** untouched since **Apr 10 2026**; a burn instruction would show up immediately as a fall in the BONK mint supply, while a transfer out would enter Sell #3. Second, whether the **51%** fee split that now buys BONK into corporate custody is reported with an on-chain address, which would turn Buy #1 from an opaque zero into a measurable row. Third, whether any part of the **4,426.10B BONK** taken on **Jul 6 2026** is recovered or frozen — recovery into a DAO-controlled wallet would be a genuine buy-side event of a size nothing else on this page can match. Fourth, whether governance is repaired with a timelock or a quorum change, because the absence of a timelock is what made a single proposal executable in the same block it passed. Fifth, the community's standing pledge to burn **1T BONK** at one million holders, which is the only future event large enough to move the supply field by a visible amount — it carries no date, so it is a watch line rather than a forecast.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads BONK at **+5.03%** net for the 90 days to **Aug 23 2026** and **0.00%** for the 90 days ahead. The structural mechanism is that BONK cannot inflate at all — the Solana mint authority is null, vesting is finished, and the counted float already contains every BONK that exists — so the quarter's entire reading comes from one event: the BonkDAO treasury releasing **4,426.10B BONK** on **Jul 6 2026** through a governance proposal executed with no timelock, which the chain shows dispersed into market-facing wallets by **Jul 20 2026**. The key risk is that BONK's deflationary reputation is not supported by measurement: the real burn for the quarter was **147.5M BONK**, worth about **$480**, the fee split that used to fund burning now funds buying into custody, and the wallet the project calls its burn wallet is still holding **217.20B BONK** it never burned. The ceiling is the one genuinely reassuring number here: with the mint authority null, the **87,994,583,899,213 BONK** that exist today is the most that will ever exist.

# PENGU Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · team and company payouts reach the market

PENGU supply is growing, and none of it comes from new coins. Pudgy Penguins can never mint another PENGU, so every bit of new supply is the team and company share leaving its wallets: **1.63B PENGU** in the last 90 days, with **nothing burned or bought back**. That is **+2.60%** net against **62.86B** circulating, and we expect about the same over the next 90 days, while other trackers show about **0%** because their circulating figure has not moved since launch.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Jul 30 to Oct 28 2026, PENGU supply on the market grew by **+2.60%**, and the next 90 days, to Jan 26 2027, read **+2.60%** as well. Other trackers read **about 0%** (−0.0014%), a gap of **2.60 points**. That gap is real and it does not close: the circulating figure those trackers use has sat near 62.86B PENGU since Dec 17 2024, while the team and company wallets have paid out coins every month since Dec 2025. So the Pudgy Penguins coin page carries a warning note that explains the difference. In one line: PENGU is a fixed-supply token whose float grows by scheduled team and company payouts, with nothing on the other side.

## Sell pressure: where new PENGU comes from

**Protocol inflation is zero, for good.** PENGU is a plain token on Solana, and the right to create new PENGU has been switched off on-chain, so the supply can only stay flat or fall. There is no staking reward and no emission. Today **76.72B** PENGU exist, down from the 88.89B made at launch.

**Vesting unlocks are the whole story: 1.63B PENGU.** The team holds 17.8% of the launch supply and the company, Igloo, holds 11.48%, together **26.03B PENGU**. After a one-year cliff, that share unlocks month by month from Dec 17 2025 to Nov 17 2028, about **722.96M** a month on paper. What counts for supply is what actually leaves the team and company wallets. Between Jul 30 and Oct 28 2026 that was **1.63B PENGU**, in payouts around Aug 17, Sep 17 and Oct 19 2026, about three quarters of the **2.17B** the calendar allowed for the same three months. The main wallet alone sent out about 560M, and team members' own wallets sold or moved the rest to exchanges.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add nothing extra.** Every PENGU that left the team and company wallets is already counted in the vesting line above, so this line stays at zero. The launch wallet, which still holds **5.00B PENGU**, paid out 11.1M in the window, but those coins already counted as circulating.

**Long-term locks and bankruptcy: zero.** No court or bankruptcy estate holds PENGU. The small slice set aside for FTX token holders was handed out at launch in Dec 2024.

## Buy pressure: where new PENGU goes

**Programmatic buyback: zero.** Igloo has not announced any plan to buy PENGU back, and no wallet does it.

**Protocol fee burn: zero.** PENGU has no network fees, so there is nothing to burn. Holders can destroy their own coins, but these burns are tiny. The one big PENGU burn was in Feb 2025, when **12.17B** unclaimed airdrop coins were destroyed, long before this window.

**Foundation buy: zero.** Igloo holds and pays out team and company PENGU; it does not buy coins on the market.

**New long-term lock: zero.** Nothing new was locked away. About **576M PENGU** sit on the Abstract network, which Igloo will close on Dec 15 2026. Some of those coins could end up stuck there, but there is no number for that yet, so nothing is counted.

## Foundation and overhang

The main team and company wallet holds **7.19B PENGU**, and about 50 more team wallets hold **12.64B**, for **19.83B PENGU** in team and company hands. That is about **1.76B** more than the calendar says should still be locked, because the payouts have run behind the 722.96M monthly pace. Some of those team wallets still hold their full grants of 1.17B, 917M or 667M PENGU and have not sold a coin. The launch wallet's **5.00B PENGU** already counts as circulating. We re-read these wallets at every rebuild; if the team and company balance falls faster than the monthly payouts, the extra goes into the unscheduled-unlock line at the next refresh.

## How PENGU compares to other meme coins

Many big meme coins have no team share at all. DOGE pays miners new coins forever, while PEPE had its whole supply out at launch and can only shrink when holders burn coins. PENGU sits in a third group: a fixed supply that can never grow, but with almost a third of it held back for the team and the company and released on a four-year plan.

That makes PENGU closer to a venture-backed token than to a fair-launch meme. The new supply does not come from a printing press; it comes from insiders whose coins unlock every month. A fair-launch meme coin with a burn can shrink its float over time; PENGU's float can only grow until Nov 2028, unless Igloo adds a buyback or a burn.

The 12.17B burn of Feb 2025 is the one thing PENGU shares with burn-heavy meme coins, and it was a one-time event: the unclaimed part of the airdrop, destroyed when the claim window closed.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 17 2026, Dec 17 2026 and Jan 17 2027.** These are the next three monthly release dates. If each payout matches the last three, about **544M PENGU** reaches the market each month; if the team starts paying the full 722.96M, or works off part of its 1.76B backlog, the next-90-day number rises toward 3.45%.

**Dec 15 2026, Abstract closes.** Igloo is shutting its own network to focus on Pudgy Penguins and PENGU. Coins left on Abstract after that date could be stuck, and people who farmed points there have asked to be paid in PENGU; any such payment would come out of the team and company wallets.

**A buyback or burn plan.** None exists today. A real one, with a size and a wallet, would be the first entry on the buy side of the PENGU ledger.

**Sales by team members.** Several team wallets have sent coins straight to exchanges after each monthly payout. A big jump in those sales would show up in the next reading.

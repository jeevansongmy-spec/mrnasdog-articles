# PENGU Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

PENGU supply is growing and is projected to keep growing, even though no new PENGU can ever be minted. The Pressure Framework reads Pudgy Penguins' token at **+2.06%** over the last 90 days and **+2.18%** over the next 90, because the team and company allocation is paid out of its holding wallets on the 17th of every month: **1,298.0M PENGU** of sell pressure against **0** of buy pressure. The Solana mint has no minting key, so the only ceiling that matters is the **19,132.8M PENGU** still held back for the team and company.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Against a circulating base of **62,860.4M PENGU**, the framework books **1,298.0M PENGU** of sell pressure and **0** of buy pressure over the trailing 90 days, a net of **+2.06%**, and projects **+2.18%** for the next 90 days from three more monthly payouts. The inflation monitor reads **+0.08%** for the same window, a gap of **1.98 percentage points**. That is over the framework's 0.5-point tolerance, so the overview page carries a monitor-gap warning. The gap has one cause. The monitor's supply figure has sat at **62,860.4M PENGU** every day since **Dec 17 2024**, which is exactly the **88,888.9M** launch total minus the **26,028.5M** team and company allocation. It was fixed on launch day and has never moved since, so it cannot see those coins being paid out. The label for PENGU is **a no-mint token whose insider allocation is still being paid out**.

## Sell pressure: where new PENGU comes from

It does not come from minting. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0**, and this is one of the few rows the framework can call permanent. The PENGU token on Solana has had its minting key removed, and the Solana token program refuses to mint without one. Removing that key cannot be undone. Total supply read **76,722.8M PENGU** twice, about 25 minutes apart, on **Sep 22 2026**. It can go down when someone burns coins, but it can never go up.

The whole supply story is Sell #2, vesting unlocks, at **1,298.0M PENGU**. At launch on **Dec 16 2024**, the Pudgy Penguins team and company allocation of **26,028.5M PENGU** went into one custody wallet. It stayed locked for a year, and since Dec 2025 it has been paid out monthly on a published 36-month calendar that ends in **Dec 2028**. In this window the main wallet sent out **734.1M PENGU** on Jul 17, Aug 17 and Sep 17 2026. Six smaller holding wallets, each of which looks like one person's share, sent **563.8M PENGU** more. Most of that went on **Sep 17 2026**, when they paid two months at once after skipping July and August. The calendar says **2,169.0M PENGU** was due in these 90 days. The framework books only what actually left the holding wallets, because coins still sitting there are not on the market yet.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0**. Everything still held back belongs to the same monthly calendar, not to a separate pool. The launch payout wallet still holds **5,010.3M PENGU**, but those coins were counted as circulating from day one, so moving them adds nothing to the float. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**: no bankruptcy estate or trustee is tied to PENGU.

## Buy pressure: where new PENGU goes

Nowhere, this window. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0**. A game-console partner has promised since **Jul 2025** to buy and burn PENGU on every console sold, and Pudgy Penguins leaders have talked about buybacks funded by toy and licensing revenue. No buyback wallet has been published, and none of the largest holders was collecting coins in this window.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is also **0**. PENGU has no fee burn, and the framework checked for burns two ways. First, total supply is **12,166.1M PENGU** below the launch total, and **12,164.7M** of that is the unclaimed airdrop burned on **Feb 5 2025**. Everything burned since then, console pledge included, adds up to about **1.4M PENGU**over 19 months. Second, the burn address holds about 2 PENGU. So this window's burn rounds to zero.

Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0**: the company's wallets only sent PENGU out. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**. PENGU has no staking or lock-up program, and a US fund that would hold PENGU has been filed but has not launched.

## Foundation and overhang

The overhang on PENGU is large, and it can all be read on-chain. The main team and company wallet holds **7,372.5M PENGU** and pays out on the 17th. Six per-person wallets that also pay out monthly hold **2,903.5M PENGU**. Another **8,856.8M PENGU** sits in 21 wallets that did not move at all in 90 days. Their balances, from 64.1M to 1,166.7M, look like shares that have not started paying out yet. Together that is **19,132.8M PENGU**, close to the **18,798.4M** the calendar says should still be locked. So payouts to date are running close to plan overall, even though this quarter ran below it.

All of these wallets are read on-chain and refreshed at every rebuild. If any team or company wallet balance falls between refreshes by more than the monthly payout, that extra outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh. There is no buyback wallet to track and no bankruptcy residual.

## How PENGU compares to other fixed-supply brand tokens

PENGU belongs to the group of Solana tokens that have given up their minting key: a fixed number of coins, with no way to create more. That is a stronger promise than a halving chain like Bitcoin, which still creates new coins every block, only fewer over time. It is also stronger than staking chains, whose supply limit is a governance setting rather than a hard rule.

Even so, PENGU reads **+2.06%** while a mid-cycle Bitcoin reads a fraction of a percent. A fixed total does not protect the float. What moves PENGU is an insider allocation paid out month by month, which makes it closer to a young token working through a four-year team and investor vesting schedule than to a mature capped coin. The difference from a typical monthly-unlock token is that the payouts here come from ordinary wallets, not from a lock contract. They can run late, as they did in July and August, or catch up, as they did on Sep 17.

Tokens with a steady buy-and-burn, such as exchange tokens, can offset payouts like these and even shrink their supply. PENGU has a burn pledge on paper, but it has removed about 1.4M PENGU in 19 months. That is a tiny fraction of a single monthly team and company payout, so for now the pledge does not change the reading.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the three monthly payouts on **Oct 17 2026**, **Nov 17 2026** and **Dec 17 2026**, each around **456M PENGU** at the current pace. Second, whether the six per-person wallets keep paying monthly or skip again, which moves the next reading by up to **1.3 percentage points**. Third, the 21 wallets that have not moved yet: holding **8,856.8M PENGU**, they are the largest pool that could start paying out without warning. Fourth, any published buyback wallet or a real rise in burns. Fifth, launch of the proposed US PENGU fund, which would hold coins in custody but would not remove them from the float.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads PENGU at **+2.06%** over the trailing 90 days and **+2.18%** projected forward: supply growing, projected to keep growing. The mechanism is payouts, not minting. The PENGU minting key is gone for good, but the Pudgy Penguins team and company allocation leaves its holding wallets on the 17th of every month, **1,298.0M PENGU** in the last quarter. The key risk is the **19,132.8M PENGU** still held back, paid out on a calendar that runs to Dec 2028. The one real comfort is the fixed supply: no new PENGU can ever be made, so the payout calendar is the whole risk.

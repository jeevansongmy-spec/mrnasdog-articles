## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 6 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **PENGU at about +3.39% net**, and the forward view is a touch higher at about **+3.45%** — a sell side driven by roughly **2.13B** of vesting unlocks and a buy side of **zero**. Our supply monitor reads only **+0.02%** for the same trailing window, a gap of about **3.37 percentage points**, which is far outside the framework's 0.5-point tolerance, so a **⚠ monitor gap** chip is attached. The reason is not a disagreement about the schedule but about classification: the monitor follows a circulating-supply figure pinned near **62.86B**, which does not yet count freshly-unlocked team and investor tokens as circulating, while the framework books the published unlocks that on-chain are already being claimed and dispersed. PENGU is **inflationary by design on its active float** — a fixed-supply token whose dilution comes entirely from vesting, not issuance.

## Sell pressure: where new PENGU comes from

The supply is fixed, so "new" PENGU is really pre-minted PENGU leaving its lock. Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero**: the Solana mint's authority is renounced, read null this session, so no new PENGU can ever be created and there is no staking-reward or issuance stream. Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — is the whole story, at about **2.13B PENGU** for the window. The team, company and investor allocations sit on a 36-month linear vest whose one-year cliff hit **Dec 17 2025**, releasing roughly **703M PENGU** on the 17th of each month; three cliffs fall inside this window — **May 17 2026** at about 703M, **Jun 17 2026** at about 703M, and **Jul 17 2026** at about 723M, roughly **2,129M** in total. On-chain the unlock wallet claims each tranche and disperses it across many addresses shortly after release, so these are genuinely reaching the float, not sitting untouched in escrow — which is why the framework books the schedule in full, about **+3.4%** against the **62.86B** circulating.

Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is **zero** on value but carries the overhang worth naming: the still-locked bucket of roughly **16.6B to 20.4B PENGU** held by the team, company and treasury and vesting on the same linear schedule. Its scheduled portion is already counted under vesting above, and no discretionary, out-of-schedule release is evidenced in the window, so there is nothing to add here beyond what Sell #2 already captures. Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero** because no bankruptcy estate or court distribution applies to PENGU; the small **0.35%** allocation once given to FTX FTT holders was a one-time launch airdrop, already distributed, not a recurring estate wave.

## Buy pressure: where new PENGU goes

There is nothing on the buy side, and that is the key asymmetry. Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is **zero**: PENGU has no published rule routing brand or platform revenue into buying the token back, unlike a revenue-sharing exchange token. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is **zero** because PENGU is a brand and community token, not a fee-generating protocol, so there is no base-fee burn of the kind an L1 runs; the mint is renounced, but nothing actively removes supply either. Buy #3 — Foundation buy — is **zero**: **Igloo Inc**, the company behind Pudgy Penguins, has real revenue from toy sales and the March 2026 Visa **Pengu Card**, but it has disclosed no open-market accumulation of PENGU with that revenue. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is **zero** because no new lockup was deployed and there is no staking mechanism that takes PENGU out of circulation. With every buy row empty, nothing offsets the monthly unlocks.

## Foundation and overhang

PENGU's one team-controlled overhang is the still-locked allocation itself — about **16.6B to 20.4B PENGU** across the team, company and treasury buckets, roughly a fifth to a quarter of the **88.89B** cap, all draining onto the market through the 36-month linear vest at about **703M** a month. Unlike a discretionary foundation treasury that might deploy at any time, this overhang has a **known monthly rhythm**: it is the same schedule that feeds Sell #2, so it is tracked by re-reading the published vesting calendar and the unlock wallet by hand on each refresh. The trigger sentence follows: if the still-locked balance falls faster than the monthly schedule implies — an off-calendar release or a treasury movement — that additional outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh. As of this build there is no such off-schedule release, so the overhang's in-window value stays at zero above what the vesting row already books.

## How PENGU compares to other capped brand tokens

PENGU belongs to the class of **hard-capped tokens with a renounced mint but a heavy vesting overhang** — the launch-era shape, where the cap is real but most of the supply started locked. Against a fixed-supply memecoin like **BONK**, the mint story is identical — both have renounced authorities and neither can issue another token — but the direction of travel is opposite: BONK is fully unlocked and only ever burns, so its number rounds to flat, while PENGU is still only about **70%** unlocked and is actively diluting as the team-and-investor tranches vest. The renounced mint that reads as "no inflation" on a supply page is misleading here: the inflation is real, it just comes from the lock schedule rather than from issuance.

The comparison that matters most is with tokens that pair unlocks with an **offset**. An exchange or L1 token that vests on a schedule but also runs a fee burn or a revenue-funded buyback can absorb some or all of its own unlocks, so its net number is the unlock minus the buy-back. PENGU has the unlock and none of the offset — no burn, no buyback, no treasury bid — so the full **2.13B** lands on the float uncushioned. That is the honest read the monitor misses: a supply page that watches only the circulating field sees PENGU as flat, because that field lags insider unlocks, whereas the vesting schedule shows a token adding about **3.4%** to its tradable supply every 90 days with nothing pulling the other way.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the monthly cliffs: **Aug 17 2026**, **Sep 17 2026** and **Oct 17 2026** each release roughly **723M PENGU**, so the forward window carries about **2.17B** of scheduled unlocks — the single most important recurring event on the page. Second, whether the circulating-supply figure the market watches finally catches up to the unlocks; if it re-classifies the vested team tokens as circulating, the monitor gap would close from the monitor side without any change to the framework read. Third, any move by Igloo Inc to route brand or Pengu Card revenue into an actual buyback — that is the one development that could put a real number on the empty buy ledger and start offsetting the vest. Fourth, on-chain behaviour of the unlock wallet: a shift from claim-and-disperse to direct exchange deposits would confirm the vested supply is being sold rather than redistributed. Fifth, the end of the linear vest, still years out in **Dec 2028**, after which this dilution stops for good.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads PENGU at about **+3.45% net** over the next 90 days, from a sell ledger that is nothing but vesting unlocks — roughly **2.13B PENGU** across three monthly cliffs — and a buy ledger that is empty. The structural mechanism is a **renounced mint with a heavy lock**: no new PENGU can be created, but a large team, company and investor allocation is still draining onto the market at about **703M** a month through a 36-month schedule. The key risk is exactly that dilution with no offset — no burn and no buyback absorb it — and the reason our monitor reads flat is a lagging circulating-supply field, not an absence of unlocks. The cap is fixed at **88.89B**, but the float will keep climbing toward it until the vest ends in **2028**.

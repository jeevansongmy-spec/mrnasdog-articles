Pudgy Penguins' PENGU cannot be minted — the Solana token's mint authority is renounced — so the only force adding supply is team and company allocations vesting out on a monthly drip. The calendar scheduled about **2,169M PENGU** across three tranches last quarter, but on-chain only around **550M** was actually claimed and dispersed toward the market. The framework reads **+0.87%** net over the trailing 90 days and about **+0.87%** forward, against a buy side of exactly **zero**: an announced buyback that has not executed, and no burn.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window closing **Jul 30 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **PENGU at +0.87% net** over the trailing window and **+0.87%** forward. Our supply monitor reads **+0.09%**, a gap of **0.78 percentage points** — just outside the 0.5-point tolerance, so a **⚠ monitor-gap chip** ships on the PENGU overview. The gap is not an error on either side; it is a classification wedge. The monitor tracks the circulating float, and that float barely moved, because the newly-vested coins land in team and treasury wallets the monitor counts as non-circulating. The framework tracks supply reaching the market, and on that measure about **550M PENGU** was claimed out of the vesting contract and dispersed toward liquid holders over the window. PENGU is therefore best labelled a **fixed-supply token with a discretionary vesting drip**: no issuance risk at all, but a steady, insider-controlled release that the published calendar overstates by roughly four times.

## Sell pressure: where new PENGU comes from

Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero**, in the strongest sense the framework recognises. Reading the PENGU mint directly on Solana this session returns a mint authority of null and a freeze authority of null; the on-chain supply is **76.72B** and it can only fall. PENGU is a brand token, not a chain's gas asset, so there is no block reward, no staking issuance and no emission curve. Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — carries the whole quarter, but the number that matters is the realised one. The vesting calendar scheduled three tranches inside the window — **May 17**, **Jun 17** and **Jul 17 2026**, about **723.0M PENGU** each, roughly **2,169M** in total — from the team and company allocations that sat on a one-year cliff from the December 2024 launch and now vest linearly over three years. On-chain, far less reaches the market: the tranches are claimed and only partly dispersed onward, about **182.8M** across nineteen wallets per unlock, so the realised release is nearer **550M**. Booking the gross calendar would invent sell pressure that has not arrived; the framework books the **550M** that did.

Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is **zero** for value but large for scope. About **13.86B PENGU** is minted but not yet circulating: the undrawn **1,619M** of this quarter's vest that stayed in team custody, plus the team, company, liquidity and treasury reserves that release only on the published monthly schedule, and roughly **26B** separates circulating from the **88.89B** maximum. None of it fired discretionarily inside the window, so it is tracked, not booked. Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is **zero**: no estate, trustee schedule or court-ordered distribution holds PENGU.

## Buy pressure: where new PENGU goes

Every buy row is **zero**. Buy #1 — programmatic buyback — is the one to watch: a Foundation-approved buyback of **at least 10 million dollars** was announced on **Jun 30 2026**. But an authorized ceiling is not a purchase, and no on-chain execution has been observed, so the framework books zero rather than the announced maximum — the rule is to count the actual amount bought, never the pledge. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is **zero**: PENGU is not a gas token, so the fees paid to use Solana burn that chain's asset, not PENGU. The large burn associated with the name was a one-off — about **12.16B** of unclaimed airdrop tokens destroyed after the launch claim window closed in early 2026 — and it sits outside this quarter. Buy #3 — Foundation buy — is **zero**: no dated, quantified open-market purchase separate from the announced program was disclosed. Buy #4 — new long-term lock — is **zero**: there is no PENGU staking program that locks supply, and no new lock was announced.

## Foundation and overhang

PENGU's overhang is almost entirely the vesting reserve. The largest identified block is the **team and company allocation**, together about **29%** of supply, which unlocks on the monthly schedule and is the source of the realised **550M** that reached the market this quarter. Behind it sit the **liquidity and treasury reserves** and the undrawn portion of each unlock — roughly **13.86B PENGU** minted but not circulating, with about **26B** still short of the **88.89B** cap. There is no DAO gating this release; it is brand-run and schedule-driven, tracked through the unlock calendar and on-chain claims rather than a governance vote. Each block is refreshed on the fortnightly walk. If any of these balances falls faster than the scheduled drip between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How PENGU compares to other fixed-supply tokens

PENGU sits in the class of **fixed-supply tokens with the mint renounced but vesting still running** — the same structural shape as the large fixed-cap launch tokens where the contract can never issue another unit, yet insiders are still emerging from multi-year lockups. Against a proof-of-work coin with a permanent tail emission, PENGU looks strictly better: there is no ongoing protocol issuance to absorb, ever. Against an exchange token that buys back and burns from revenue every quarter, PENGU looks worse, because it removes nothing — its only burn was a one-time airdrop clean-up, and its announced buyback has not spent a dollar on-chain.

The sharper comparison is with another fixed-supply launch token whose insiders claim out of a vesting contract on a discretionary drip. In both cases a renounced mint bounds the numerator — no new supply can be created — but says nothing about how fast the already-minted reserve is pushed at the market. What separates them is the realised claim rate: the published calendars for this cohort routinely overstate real release by several times, because tokens vest on paper into a contract or a team wallet and only a fraction is actually dispersed toward liquidity. PENGU is a textbook case — a **2,169M** scheduled quarter that delivered a realised **550M**. For an inflation lens, the token is quiet by design and only mildly diluting in practice, entirely at the discretion of the insiders holding the unlock.

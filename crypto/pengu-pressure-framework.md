# PENGU Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Pudgy Penguins can never mint another PENGU — the mint authority on the Solana token reads null — and yet PENGU supply still grew over the last 90 days, because the entire team and company allocation sits in a vesting escrow that pays out on the 17th of every month. That escrow released **744.9M PENGU**, and three downstream lock wallets released a further **229.2M**, for total sell pressure of **974.1M PENGU** against buy pressure of exactly **zero**. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads PENGU at **+1.55%** net for the window and **+1.19%** forward, against a monitor reading of **-0.05%** — a gap of **1.60 percentage points** that exists because the published circulating figure for PENGU has not moved since launch day.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 28 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads Pudgy Penguins at **+1.55%** net new supply — sell pressure of **974.1M PENGU** against buy pressure of **0**, divided by a circulating supply of **62,860M PENGU**. Our independent supply monitor reads **-0.05%** for the same window, a gap of **1.60 percentage points**, so a data-conflict flag ships on the PENGU overview. The deep walk found the reason and it is not a dispute about the chain: the monitor's circulating figure for PENGU has held **62.86B** — exactly the **70.72%** that was unlocked at the Pudgy Penguins token launch in December 2024 — for more than eighteen months, never adjusted for the **12.17B** burn of the unclaimed airdrop and never adjusted for a single monthly vesting release since. It is a frozen constant, so it cannot see a release even when the escrow is emptying in front of it. PENGU in August 2026 is a **hard-capped token with a discretionary escrow**: the protocol half of its inflation is permanently zero, and the vesting half is the entire story.

## Sell pressure: where new PENGU comes from

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0** and always will be. Reading the PENGU mint account directly returns a null mint authority and a null freeze authority, which means no wallet on Solana — Igloo Inc included — retains the power to create another PENGU or to freeze one. The total on the chain is **76,722,843,488 PENGU** against a ceiling of **88,888,888,888**, and the gap between them is the **12.17B** of unclaimed airdrop Pudgy Penguins destroyed in February 2025. That burn is real, permanent and far outside this window; nothing has replaced it.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is where all of PENGU's inflation lives, and it is worth being precise about the number because the widely quoted one is wrong. The Pudgy Penguins tokenomics give **17.80%** to the team and **11.48%** to the company — together **29.28%**, or **26,026,666,666 PENGU** — under a one-year cliff that fell in December 2025 followed by a 36-month linear vest. Divide that allocation by 36 and you get **723.0M PENGU** a month, which is the figure every unlock tracker publishes for PENGU. It is arithmetic, not observation. On chain, the whole allocation was moved into a single escrow account on **Dec 16 2024**, and that escrow is readable, so the framework reads it at both ends of the window instead of trusting the calendar. It released **193.9M** on Jun 17 2026, **355.7M** on Jul 17 2026 and **195.3M** across Aug 17 and Aug 18 2026 — a realised total of **744.9M PENGU**. The calendar entitlement for the same three dates was **2,168.9M**. The escrow paid about a third of what it was entitled to pay, and the remainder is still sitting inside it.

Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, carries **229.2M PENGU**. Downstream of the escrow sit twenty individual lock wallets, funded in January 2026 through three distribution hubs that now read dust. Three of those twenty released on **Jun 17 2026** — one paid out **97.2M**, one **76.4M** and one **55.6M**, each exactly one twelfth of its tranche — and the other seventeen have not recorded a single transaction since Apr 9 2026. Nothing fired from this branch on Jul 17 or on Aug 17, which is why the forward column for Sell #3 is **0**: one firing in a 90-day window with two silent months after it is not a pattern the framework will project.

Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**. PENGU has no bankruptcy estate distributing tokens on a trustee schedule. The one creditor-linked slice, **0.35%** reserved for former exchange claimants, was a single claim window at launch, and whatever went unclaimed was destroyed alongside the rest of the unclaimed airdrop.

## Buy pressure: where new PENGU goes

It does not go anywhere. All four buy rows read **0**, and that is the single most important structural fact about PENGU inflation: there is no counterweight at all. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0** despite two separate promises on the record. A hardware partner sells a limited-edition Pudgy Penguins handheld console and says it buys and burns PENGU on every unit sold, but has never published a per-unit quantum or a destination. Separately, a company-approved buyback of at least ten million dollars was announced at the end of June 2026 and still has no published destination wallet — at the current PENGU price that would be roughly **1,060M PENGU**, and no wallet read this session accumulated anything close to it.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0** for a structural reason: PENGU is a brand and intellectual-property token, not a fee-earning network, so there is no revenue stream to burn against. The framework checks both places a burn can show up. The Solana incinerator's PENGU account holds **0.000001 PENGU**, so no dead-address burn is running; and the total supply of PENGU, which does fall when the supply-reducing burn instruction is used, moved by roughly a thousand PENGU over the eleven days to Aug 28 2026. Both surfaces agree that nothing meaningful is being destroyed. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **0** — a treasury wallet did grow by **581.4M PENGU** on Jun 4 2026, but the tokens arrived from an internal distributor rather than from the open market, so it is a transfer, not a purchase. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0**: Pudgy Penguins has no staking contract and no protocol lockup, and the locked-earn product an exchange launched in August 2026 is a custodial promotion on that venue which removes nothing from the chain.

## Foundation and overhang

The team-controlled overhang on PENGU is large, fully enumerated, and the reason the forward reading matters more than the trailing one. First, the vesting escrow itself, holding **7,555.6M PENGU** and refreshed on every rebuild by a direct chain read. Second, the twenty downstream lock wallets, holding roughly **9,779.3M PENGU** between them, seventeen of which are motionless. Third, a treasury cold wallet holding **3,493.6M PENGU** that has taken tokens in twice and has never sent any out in its entire life. Together that is **20,828.5M PENGU** under team control — about a third of the circulating float. It reconciles closely with what the vesting schedule says should still be locked at this point, **20,243.0M PENGU**, which is the check that tells us nothing large is unaccounted for.

All three are watched by direct chain read every day, and the rule is the same for each: if the escrow, the lock wallets or the treasury wallet shows a lower balance between refreshes, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh. The same trigger runs in reverse on the buy side — if either announced buyback ever publishes a destination, or if a wallet is observed accumulating PENGU at the scale the announcement implies, Buy #1 stops being zero.

## How PENGU compares to other NFT and brand tokens

PENGU belongs to a small class: tokens attached to a consumer intellectual-property brand rather than to a network that earns fees. That shapes both halves of the ledger. On the sell side PENGU is unusually clean — a renounced mint authority is rarer than it sounds, and it places PENGU alongside fixed-supply assets rather than alongside the layer-1s and appchains whose validators are paid in new issuance every block. A proof-of-stake chain with a 4% staking emission would put roughly **1%** of its supply into the market every quarter forever; PENGU's protocol issuance is permanently zero and cannot be restarted by any vote.

The buy side is where the brand-token structure hurts. An exchange token or a perpetuals-venue token routes a documented slice of real trading revenue into a buyback or a burn, so its ledger has a genuine second column and can run net deflationary through a vesting period. Pudgy Penguins sells plush toys, comic books and consoles, and none of that revenue is contractually connected to the PENGU token. The buyback promises that do exist are corporate intentions rather than protocol-encoded mechanisms, which is exactly why the framework holds them at zero until a destination is published and a balance can be read at two window ends. The comparison that matters, then, is not PENGU against a layer-1; it is PENGU against other cliff-and-linear token launches with no revenue recycling. In that group the distinguishing question is only ever how fast the escrow drains, and PENGU's escrow is draining slower than its own published calendar claims.

One more structural note separates PENGU from most of its peers: the exchange-traded fund filed on PENGU is demand, not supply, and the framework excludes it from the ledger entirely. A fund that buys PENGU on the open market changes the price, not the number of PENGU in existence. The Pressure Framework's Metric 1 measures supply only.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The vesting escrow fires on **Sep 17 2026**, **Oct 17 2026** and **Nov 17 2026**; the size of each firing, not the calendar entitlement, is what the next rebuild will book. Whether the twenty downstream lock wallets wake up matters almost as much — they released once on Jun 17 2026 and have been silent since, and a resumption would add roughly **229M PENGU** a firing. The company-approved buyback announced in June 2026 remains the single event that could move PENGU from an inflationary reading to a neutral one, and it becomes real the moment a destination wallet is published. The **3,493.6M PENGU** treasury cold wallet has never sent a token and its first outflow would be a genuine regime change. Finally, the published circulating figure for PENGU has been frozen since December 2024; if it is ever corrected for the February 2025 burn and the vesting releases since, the monitor gap on the overview closes on its own without any change to the chain.

## Summary

Pudgy Penguins is a hard-capped Solana token whose mint authority has been renounced, so its protocol inflation is permanently zero — and it is still growing supply at **+1.55%** per 90 days, because the whole team and company allocation sits in a vesting escrow that pays out monthly and there is nothing on the buy side to absorb it. The structural mechanism is a cliff-and-linear vest running to December 2028, and the honest number is the escrow's realised **744.9M PENGU**, not the **2,168.9M** the published unlock calendar advertises for the same three dates. The key risk is the **20,828.5M PENGU** still under team control, roughly a third of the float, with no buyback, no burn and no staking lock standing against it. The ceiling is real and permanent at **88,888,888,888 PENGU**, of which **12.17B** is already destroyed — but a ceiling is not a brake, and for PENGU the escrow is the only thing that matters.

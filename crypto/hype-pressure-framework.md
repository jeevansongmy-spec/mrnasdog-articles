# HYPE Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

HYPE supply is growing even with one of crypto's largest buybacks running every day. Over the 90 days to Oct 11 2026, Hyperliquid's team paid out **4.62M HYPE** and staking pay and Foundation sends added **0.85M** more, while the keyless buyback fund took **2.18M HYPE** off the market and the fee burn destroyed about **0.23M** more. Net, supply rose **+1.38%**, and the next 90 days project **+1.41%**. The 1 billion HYPE cap still holds; the open question is how fast the team's 237.90M staked HYPE reaches the market.


## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads HYPE at **+1.38%** net supply growth over the last 90 days and **+1.41%** for the next 90, measured against **222.45M HYPE** in circulation. The inflation monitor reads **+0.00%**, a gap of **1.38 percentage points**, which is over our 0.5-point line, so the page carries a warning chip. We walked every source and the gap does not close: the circulating figure the monitor reads has stayed at 222.45M HYPE every single day since May 27 2026, so it cannot show any new coins at all. On-chain, the team wallet, the staking reward reserve and the buyback fund all moved by millions of HYPE in the window. Our reading stays. In one line: HYPE is a buyback coin whose team payouts now outrun the buyback.

## Sell pressure: where new HYPE comes from

Protocol inflation added **748,350 HYPE**. Hyperliquid pays stakers in new HYPE from a reserve of 410.85M that was set aside at launch for future emissions. The pay falls as more HYPE is staked; with **440.05M HYPE** staked across 35 validators it is about 2.2% a year, or roughly 2.43M new HYPE every 90 days. More than half of that, 1.31M HYPE, went to the team's own wallet, which keeps its coins staked and out of the market, so we count those coins only when the team pays them out. The Hyper Foundation, the community grants wallet and the Foundation's five validators took about 377,000 more, which also stays outside the circulating count. The remaining 748,350 HYPE went to outside stakers.

Vesting unlocks added **4.62M HYPE**, the largest sell row. The core contributors' 23.8% share sits in one wallet, staked, and the team unstakes and pays out once a month after a 7-day wait. In this window it paid out 433,025 HYPE on Aug 6, 433,416 HYPE on Sep 6 and then **3.75M HYPE** on Oct 7 2026. That last batch was sold to an outside institution in a private deal announced on Sep 30 2026, and it landed in five wallets of 750,000 HYPE each. The paper vesting schedule allows far more each month than the team actually takes, so we book what really left the wallet. For the next 90 days we expect payouts on about Nov 6 2026, Dec 6 2026 and Jan 6 2027, and we size them at the average of the last three, which again gives **4.62M HYPE**.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **104,498 HYPE**. The Hyper Foundation sent out 50,000 HYPE on Aug 4 and 50,000 on Aug 31 2026, and the community grants wallet paid 4,498 HYPE in small grants. Neither wallet is part of the 222.45M circulating, so these sends are new supply. The Foundation has sent coins out every few months since March 2026, so we expect about the same in the next 90 days. Long-term locks and bankruptcy add **0**: no estate, trustee or court-run lock holds HYPE, because the token launched in November 2024 by airdrop with no private sale.

## Buy pressure: where new HYPE goes

The programmatic buyback took **2.18M HYPE** off the market. Almost all of Hyperliquid's trading fees flow into the Assistance Fund, a system wallet with no private key that buys HYPE on the order book every day and has never sold any. Validators voted in December 2025 to treat the fund's coins as burned. The fund held 45.78M HYPE on Jul 13 2026 and **47.95M HYPE** on Oct 11 2026. Since Oct 4 2026 it also receives most of the interest earned on the exchange's dollar reserves, under a plan validators approved in June 2026; the first payment was **$14.58M**, and it was spent on HYPE within a week. At today's price of about $85, trading fees plus two more interest payments should buy about **2.13M HYPE** in the next 90 days. Because the fund spends dollars, a higher HYPE price means fewer coins bought.

The protocol fee burn took about **0.23M HYPE**. Some fees are destroyed outright, 1.13M HYPE since launch. Total supply fell from 998,896,880 HYPE on Sep 30 to 998,867,524 on Oct 12 2026, about 2,540 HYPE a day, so the burn is a small but steady second buyer next to the fund. The Foundation bought no HYPE in the window, so Foundation buy is **0**. New long-term locks are **0** too: staking needs only 7 days to undo, and staked HYPE still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest overhang is the core contributors' wallet: **237.90M HYPE**, all staked and still earning about 14,000 HYPE a day. It has no fixed monthly payout size, so a single month could be as small as August or as large as October. The Hyper Foundation holds **60.55M HYPE**, nearly all of it staked, and the community grants wallet holds **2.97M**; neither is part of the 222.45M circulating, so every coin they send out counts as new supply. The future-emissions reserve holds **410.85M HYPE** and only drips out as staking pay. The buyback fund holds 47.95M HYPE with no key and cannot sell. We re-read the team, Foundation and grants wallets at every rebuild; if any of their balances falls between checks, the outflow enters the sell side at the next refresh.

## How HYPE compares to other exchange tokens

HYPE is an exchange token with a fixed 1 billion cap and a fee-funded buyback, which puts it next to BNB, the Binance coin that shrinks supply with a scheduled quarterly burn. The difference is the pipe. BNB's burn follows a set rule that fires on a calendar, while Hyperliquid's Assistance Fund buys every day in size set by trading fees, so HYPE's buy side rises and falls with trading volume rather than with a schedule.

The sell side is where HYPE differs most from older exchange tokens. Most of them finished vesting years ago, so their supply only moves one way. HYPE is younger: the team's 23.8% share began vesting in November 2025 and still has 237.90M HYPE to go, and the staking reserve keeps printing new coins. Compared with a pure fee-burn chain such as Ethereum, where burned coins are destroyed, HYPE's bought-back coins sit in a wallet that validators have agreed to treat as burned, which works the same for supply as long as that agreement holds.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 6 2026, Dec 6 2026 and Jan 6 2027:** the next three team payouts. A batch like October's 3.75M would push the next-90-day reading well above +1.41%; a batch like August's 433,025 would pull it down.

**Early November 2026:** the second interest payment to the buyback fund, expected 30 to 38 days after the first. Its size shows how much the dollar reserves really add to the buyback.

**Trading volume:** the buyback scales with fees, so a busy or quiet quarter moves the buy side directly, and a higher HYPE price means each dollar buys fewer coins.

**Who bought the October batch:** the 3.75M HYPE sold privately sits in five wallets. If those coins move to exchanges, they become real selling in the market.

## Summary

HYPE supply grew **+1.38%** over the 90 days to Oct 11 2026 and is projected at **+1.41%** for the next 90. The Assistance Fund buyback is large and constant at about 2.2M HYPE a quarter, but the core contributors' monthly payouts, 4.62M HYPE this window, plus about 0.85M HYPE of staking pay and Foundation sends outran it. The key risk is the size of each team payout, with 237.90M HYPE still in the team wallet; the ceiling is the fixed 1 billion HYPE cap.

# HYPE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

HYPE supply grew about **+1.00%** in the 90 days to Nov 18 2026, and we expect it to shrink slightly, about **−0.15%**, in the next 90 days. The team took **4.36M HYPE** out of its wallet and stakers outside the team earned **747,860**, while Hyperliquid's fee buyback took **2.56M HYPE** off the market and burns destroyed **286,480**. HYPE can never pass **1B** coins, and other trackers show **+6.91%** for the same period, so the coin page carries a warning note.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over Aug 20 to Nov 18 2026, **5.21M HYPE** reached the market and **2.84M** left it, a net rise of **+1.00%** on the **237.71M** HYPE that trade today. Other trackers show **+6.91%**, a gap of **5.92 points**. That gap comes from their own supply figure, which sat near 222.4M HYPE until Oct 27 2026, jumped 17.0M in a single day on Oct 28 2026 and fell 1.66M on Nov 14 2026, with no coins of that size moving on-chain. So the warning note stays on the coin page. More than four fifths of the quarter's new supply was one thing: the team's payouts, led by a 3.75M HYPE private sale on Oct 7 2026. Without that sale, the buyback would have taken out more HYPE than came in. The label that fits: **a buyback coin whose team payouts outran the buyback for one quarter**.

## Sell pressure: where new HYPE comes from

Staking pay comes first. Anyone who stakes HYPE is paid each day from a reserve of **409.81M HYPE** that was set aside when the coin launched in November 2024. The rate falls as more HYPE is staked: with **438.45M** staked today it works out to about 2.2% a year, or roughly 2.42M HYPE every 90 days. More than half of that goes to the team and Foundation wallets, which keep it staked and off the market. The part that reached ordinary stakers, who can sell it, was **747,860 HYPE**.

Team payouts are the big one. The core contributors' **238.26M HYPE** sits staked in a single wallet, and the team takes out a slice about once a month. It took **433,416 HYPE** on Sep 6 2026, **3.75M** on Oct 7 2026, sold in one private deal to a single buyer, and **179,706** on Nov 4 2026, for a total of **4.36M HYPE** in 90 days. The published schedule allows about 9.92M a month, so the team has taken far less than it could. Over the past year it took out about 2.43M every 90 days, and that is what we expect by mid-February 2027.

The Hyper Foundation sent out **50,000 HYPE** on Aug 31 2026 and 50,000 more on Nov 13 2026, and a small grants wallet paid out 1,500, for **101,500 HYPE** in all. These sends follow no schedule, so none is booked for the next 90 days. No bankrupt firm or court holds HYPE for a later payout, and HYPE never sold coins to venture funds, so there are no investor unlocks to wait for.

## Buy pressure: where new HYPE goes

The buyback is the engine. Almost every dollar of trading fees on Hyperliquid goes to the Assistance Fund, which buys HYPE on the market around the clock. Since October the fund also receives most of the interest earned on the USDC parked on the exchange: **$14.58M** on Oct 4 2026 and **$15.92M** on Nov 3 2026. The fund grew from **46.49M** to **49.07M HYPE** in the 90 days, a buyback of **2.56M HYPE**. The fund has no key and has never sent a coin out, and validators voted in December 2025 to treat its HYPE as burned. At today's price, the same dollars would buy about **3.24M HYPE** in the next 90 days.

Burns add a smaller second stream. Gas on the HyperEVM, fees for priority on the order book and the cost of launching new markets are paid in HYPE and destroyed. Total supply fell from 998.96M on Sep 4 2026 to 998.72M on Nov 18 2026, a pace of **286,480 HYPE** every 90 days.

The Foundation does not buy HYPE, so that row is zero. Listed companies holding HYPE and the US spot funds buy coins that already trade, which moves nothing in or out of the market. Staking is not a lock either: staked HYPE comes back out in 7 days, and no new long-term lock was set up in the quarter.

## Foundation and overhang

Five large balances sit outside the market. The staking reserve holds **409.81M HYPE** and only pays out as staking pay. The team wallet holds **238.26M HYPE**, all staked, and is the one balance that can reach the market quickly, since its monthly payouts are the team's choice. The Hyper Foundation holds **60.64M HYPE**, all staked, and the grants wallet holds **2.98M**. The Assistance Fund holds **49.07M HYPE** that counts as burned. We read each of these balances every day. If any of them falls between two reads, the coins that left are counted as new supply at the next update.

## How HYPE compares to other trading-chain coins

Most chain coins pay for security by printing new coins and lean on a fee burn to take some back. HYPE works the other way round. Its staking pay is small, about 2.2% a year and drawn from a fixed reserve inside the 1B cap, while the buyback is large because it is paid by trading fees on a busy exchange. In this quarter the buyback and burns removed about 3.8 times what staking pay added to the market.

Exchange coins such as BNB also shrink supply with money from trading, but they do it in large batches a few times a year, decided by the company. HYPE's buyback runs every few seconds, set by the protocol, and anyone can watch the fund grow. The weak spot is the same for both: when trading slows, fees fall and so does the buying. Interest on parked USDC now adds a second, steadier stream, but it shrinks if rates or deposits fall.

The team allocation is what sets HYPE apart from most young coins. There were no venture rounds, so no investor unlocks hang over the market, but the team still holds about a quarter of the cap and decides each month how much to take out. That makes HYPE's supply path less predictable than a coin with a fixed unlock calendar.

## Summary

HYPE supply rose about **+1.00%** in the 90 days to Nov 18 2026 because the team took out **4.36M HYPE**, most of it in one private sale, while the fee buyback removed **2.56M** and burns another 286,480. HYPE pays stakers little and buys back a lot, so without large team payouts its supply shrinks. For the next 90 days we expect a slight fall of about **−0.15%**. The key risk is the team wallet's **238.26M HYPE**, which can be paid out at the team's choice, and the hard ceiling is the 1B cap.

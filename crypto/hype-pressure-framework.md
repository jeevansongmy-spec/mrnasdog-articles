# HYPE Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply was growing · trend cooling

HYPE supply grew **+1.04%** in the 90 days to Nov 8 2026, and we expect it to shrink about **0.41%** over the next 90 days. The growth came almost entirely from the team: Hyperliquid's core contributors paid out **4.36M HYPE**, 3.75M of it in one private sale, while stakers earned about **777K** new HYPE. Against that, the Assistance Fund bought back **2.44M HYPE** with trading fees and burns destroyed **266K** more. HYPE has a hard cap of 1B coins, and other trackers show **+7.67%** because of a one-day jump in the figure they use.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 10 to Nov 8 2026, **5.19M HYPE** reached the market and **2.70M HYPE** left it, so the circulating supply of about 239.41M HYPE grew a net **+1.04%**. Other trackers show **+7.67%** for the same 90 days, a gap of **6.63 points**, so the coin page carries a warning note. The supply figure those trackers use sat near 222.4M HYPE from Aug 10 to Oct 27 2026, then jumped 17.0M HYPE in one day on Oct 28 2026, and no coins of that size moved on the chain that day. The next 90 days look different: the team's regular payouts are much smaller than the October sale, and the buyback now gets extra cash, so HYPE should shrink about **0.41%**. In one line, HYPE is a fee-buyback token whose supply rises only when the team pays out.

## Sell pressure: where new HYPE comes from

**Staking pay: about 777K HYPE.** Hyperliquid pays stakers from a reserve set aside at launch, at a rate that falls as more HYPE is staked. With about 439M HYPE staked, the reserve pays out about 2.45M HYPE every 90 days. Most of the stake belongs to the team and the Hyper Foundation, and their pay stays locked up with them, so only about 777,315 HYPE reached ordinary holders between Aug 10 and Nov 8 2026.

**Team payouts: 4.36M HYPE.** The team's coins sit staked in one wallet and are paid out about once a month after a seven-day wait. The wallet sent 433,419 HYPE on Sep 6, 3,750,000 HYPE on Oct 7 and 179,706 HYPE on Nov 6 2026. The October batch was sold in one private deal to a large buyer, a co-founder said on Sep 30 2026, so it did not hit the open market at once, but it still added to the coins anyone can trade. Unlock calendars list about 9.92M HYPE a month for the team, yet the team has paid out far less, and the rest stays staked in the same wallet. For the next 90 days we expect three payouts, around Dec 6 2026, Jan 6 2027 and Feb 5 2027, at about 433K each, the size of a normal month, which is **1.30M HYPE** in total.

**Foundation sends: 51.5K HYPE.** The Hyper Foundation sent 50,000 HYPE out on Aug 31 2026, and a small grants wallet paid 1,500 HYPE to grant winners. The Foundation sends HYPE only now and then, in February, May and August this year, with no schedule, so we book nothing for the next 90 days and watch the wallet instead.

**Locked or bankruptcy coins: zero.** No court case or failed company holds HYPE waiting to pay it out.

## Buy pressure: where new HYPE goes

**Assistance Fund buyback: 2.44M HYPE.** Almost every trading fee on Hyperliquid goes to the Assistance Fund, which buys HYPE on the market every few seconds. Since Oct 3 2026 it also gets most of the interest earned on the dollars people keep on Hyperliquid, paid every 30 days: about $14.58M on Oct 4 and $15.92M on Nov 3 2026. Between Aug 10 and Nov 8 2026 the fund bought 2,436,755 HYPE, close to $198M worth. The fund has no private key, has never sent a coin out, and a validator vote in December 2025 treats its HYPE, now 48.75M, as burned. For the next 90 days we expect about **2.78M HYPE**, helped by one more interest payment, since three fall in the next window against two in this one.

**Burns: about 266K HYPE.** Gas paid on the HyperEVM is destroyed, both the base fee and the tip, and so are the fees traders pay to put their orders first and the fees to launch a new token or market. Together they removed about 265,537 HYPE from the total supply in 90 days, close to 3,000 a day.

**Foundation buying: zero.** The Foundation does not buy HYPE back. Treasury companies and US funds do buy HYPE, but they buy coins that already circulate, so their buying does not change the count.

**New long-term locks: zero.** Staked HYPE still counts as circulating, and that includes the 500,000 HYPE a builder must stake to launch a market, so a bigger stake removes nothing.

## Foundation and overhang

Five pools sit outside the circulating count. The biggest is the staking reserve, **410.08M HYPE**, which only drips out as staking pay. The team wallet holds **238.12M HYPE**, all staked, and is the one pool that pays out every month. The Hyper Foundation holds **60.60M HYPE** staked plus 50,000 HYPE it unstaked on Sep 7 2026 and has not sent yet. A grants wallet holds about 2.98M HYPE. The Assistance Fund holds 48.75M HYPE but has no key and never sends, so it is treated as burned rather than as a pool that could sell.

If the team, Foundation or grants balance falls between two checks, the coins that left count as new supply at the next check.

## How HYPE compares to other exchange tokens

Most exchange tokens shrink their supply on a calendar. BNB burns a set batch every quarter, and older exchange tokens buy back with a set share of profits, often in one lump. HYPE works differently: the protocol itself turns almost all trading fees into HYPE purchases every few seconds, with no company in the middle, and the coins land in a fund that can never sell. That makes the buyback size follow trading volume and the price day by day, and it is why HYPE's buy side, about 2.70M HYPE in 90 days, is large next to most exchange tokens of its size.

The trade-off is the unlock side. Many exchange tokens finished vesting long ago, while HYPE's team allocation is still mostly locked and is released by the team's own choices each month. A quiet month like November adds a few hundred thousand HYPE; a month with a private sale, like October, adds millions. HYPE also pays stakers from a reserve rather than from fees, much like a proof-of-stake chain, which adds a small, steady flow on top.

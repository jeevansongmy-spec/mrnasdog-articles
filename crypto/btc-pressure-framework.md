# BTC Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

Bitcoin supply is roughly steady. The only source of new BTC is the block subsidy: every block pays its miner **3.125 BTC**, and the total can never pass **21M**. From Aug 3 to Nov 1 2026 miners received **41,419 BTC**, while nothing was burned or bought back, so supply grew **+0.21%** in 90 days. Other trackers read **+0.19%**, close to our figure, and the next 90 days should look the same.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Bitcoin added **41,419 BTC** to a supply of **20.11M** BTC in the 90 days to Nov 1 2026, a net rise of **+0.21%**, and the next 90 days should add about the same amount. Other trackers show **+0.19%** for the same stretch, a gap of about two hundredths of a point, so the coin page carries no warning note. The label is mixed flows with supply roughly steady: there is a steady trickle of new coins and nothing on the other side, but the trickle is small next to the coins already out. About **95.7%** of all the bitcoin that will ever exist has already been mined.

## Sell pressure: where new BTC comes from

**Mining is the whole sell side.** Bitcoin makes new coins in exactly one way: the miner who finds a block may pay itself the subsidy, which has been **3.125 BTC** per block since the April 2024 halving. Between Aug 3 and Nov 1 2026 miners found **13,254 blocks**, about 147 a day, which made **41,419 BTC**. The network aims for one block every ten minutes, or 144 a day. Blocks came a little faster than that because more mining machines kept joining, and the network only adjusts its difficulty every 2,016 blocks, about every two weeks. Miners sell part of this new BTC to pay for power and machines, which is why it is the sell pressure that matters here.

**Vesting unlocks: zero.** Bitcoin never had a token sale, a team share or a fund share. Every coin in existence was mined, so no locked tranche is waiting to reach the market.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: zero.** No foundation, company or treasury holds Bitcoin back from the market. All **20.11M** mined coins already count as circulating.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: zero.** The Mt. Gox estate still holds about **34,387 BTC** for creditors of the exchange that failed in 2014, and its trustee pushed the payback deadline out again on Oct 16 2026, this time to Oct 31 2027. Those coins were mined long ago and already count as circulating, so paying them out would add selling but no new supply.

## Buy pressure: where new BTC goes

**Programmatic buyback: zero.** Bitcoin has no treasury and no protocol revenue, so nothing buys coins back on the network's behalf.

**Protocol fee burn: zero.** Bitcoin burns no fees. Every fee goes to the miner who includes the transaction. People did send about **0.004 BTC** to well-known dead addresses during the 90 days, far too little to count.

**Foundation buy: zero.** There is no foundation to buy. Spot funds and listed companies buy and sell bitcoin all the time, but they trade coins that already circulate, so they take nothing out of the count. On Oct 30 2026 one listed holder even said it may sell its **5,833 BTC**.

**New long-term lock: zero.** Bitcoin has no staking, so the network locks nothing. Coins that sit still for years, including the early coins people link to Satoshi Nakamoto, still count as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

Bitcoin has no team-controlled overhang at all. There is no foundation wallet, no investor lockup and no treasury, and the total supply equals the circulating supply. The big holders are outsiders whose coins already count: the Mt. Gox estate with about **34,387 BTC**, governments holding seized coins (the US moved part of its Bitfinex-hack seizure on Oct 7–8 2026 without a confirmed sale), and the hacker behind the Oct 29 2026 Binance theft, who took about **18,270 BTC** that no one can freeze. Any of them could sell, and that would weigh on price, but none of it is new supply.

## How BTC compares to other proof-of-work chains

Bitcoin follows the classic proof-of-work design: a fixed subsidy per block that halves every 210,000 blocks, a hard cap and no burn. Litecoin copies that design with a larger cap of 84M and faster blocks, so the shape of its supply curve is the same while its halving clock runs on a different date. Zcash also halves on Bitcoin's model and keeps a 21M cap, but part of its newer design sends block rewards to development funds rather than only to miners. Dogecoin is the odd one out among the big proof-of-work coins: it pays a flat 10,000 DOGE a block forever with no cap, so its new supply never shrinks. Next to these, Bitcoin sits at the strict end, with the slowest and most predictable path to a final supply.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Block pace through Jan 30 2027.** If mining machines keep joining or leave in big numbers, the count drifts a few percent above or below the **41,419 BTC** we expect, but the subsidy itself cannot change.

**Mt. Gox estate wallets.** The trustee now has until Oct 31 2027. A move of the **34,387 BTC** to exchanges would add selling, not supply.

**The Binance hacker's 18,270 BTC.** Stolen on Oct 29 2026 and not freezable. If those coins are sold, that is selling pressure on price; the supply count stays the same.

**Quantum freeze debate.** A draft plan published in April 2026 would give owners of old address types five years to move their coins before freezing them. It has no activation date, so it changes nothing this quarter.

**Next halving, around April 2028.** Block 1,050,000 cuts the subsidy to **1.5625 BTC**. It is well outside this window but sets the next big step down in new supply.

## Summary

Bitcoin's only new supply is the miner subsidy, which made **41,419 BTC** between Aug 3 and Nov 1 2026. Nothing on the network burns, buys back or locks BTC, so supply grew **+0.21%** in 90 days, with about the same expected next. Large holders such as the Mt. Gox estate and the Binance hacker can add selling, but their coins already count as circulating. The next real change in Bitcoin's supply is the halving around April 2028.

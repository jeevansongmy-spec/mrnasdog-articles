# LIT Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

Lighter (LIT) supply was flat over the 90 days from Jul 27 to Oct 25 2026, at **0.00%**, but it is set to grow about **+5.41%** in the next 90 days, when the first team and investor release of about **13.51M LIT** falls due around Dec 30 2026. Lighter bought **2.40M LIT** with trading fees in the window and burned **2.45M LIT** on Oct 9 2026, yet neither changed the coins in circulation. LIT has a fixed total of 1B coins, so the question for LIT is not new minting but how fast the 500M insider coins and the 231.81M reserve reach the market.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the last 90 days the circulating supply of LIT stayed at **250M**, a net **0.00%**: no locked coin was released, the fee buyback recycled its coins to stakers, and the burn took coins from the unreleased reserve. Our projection for the next 90 days is **+5.41%**, all of it from the first monthly insider release on Dec 30 2026. Other trackers show **−0.32%** for the same 90 days, a gap of **0.32 points**, which is small, so the coin page carries no warning note. The trackers move a little from day to day around the same 250M figure; that is not a real change in supply. The label for LIT today: a quiet float in front of a large, dated insider unlock.

## Sell pressure: where new LIT comes from

Protocol inflation is **0**. All 1B LIT were created once at launch, and the LIT token contract on Ethereum has no function that can create more. Stakers do earn a fixed **6%** a year, and the Lighter staking pool received **1.67M LIT** between Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026, about **18,600 LIT** a day on roughly **111.61M LIT** staked. That pay comes from coins Lighter bought back with fees, which already count as circulating, so it moves LIT from one holder to another without adding supply.

Vesting unlocks are **0** for the last 90 days and about **13.51M LIT** for the next 90. Half of all LIT, **500M**, belongs to the Lighter team (260M) and its investors (240M). Lighter announced a one-year lock from the Dec 30 2025 launch, then three years of even vesting. That puts the first monthly release at about **13.51M LIT** around Dec 30 2026, then a similar amount each month until late 2029. Unlock calendars do not all agree on the first day; some put the first release on Jan 28 2027, which would push it just outside the next 90 days, and some put it a few days earlier. This page counts the full release on Dec 30 2026. The insider coins already sit in the holders' own wallets, so the lock is a legal promise, not an on-chain contract. On-chain, the 500M LIT insider group was the same size at both ends of the window: no insider coin left it.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0**. The Lighter ecosystem reserve holds **231.81M LIT** with no release schedule. Inside the window it moved only once, to fund the Oct 9 2026 burn; its last payout into the market was **100K LIT** on Jul 3 2026. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**: no estate, court-held pile or lock contract holds LIT.

## Buy pressure: where new LIT goes

The programmatic buyback books **0**, even though it is real. Lighter spends its trading fees on LIT every day: about **2.40M LIT** for **$8.02M** between Jul 27 and Oct 25 2026, and about **2.51M LIT** over the next 90 days at today's price of about $3.19. The bought LIT sits in the Lighter treasury account until the end of each quarter, then moves to the operating wallet that pays stakers and partners. Because those coins stay in circulation, the buyback does not shrink the float.

The protocol fee burn also books **0**. On Oct 9 2026 Lighter sent **2.45M LIT** to a dead address, the size of its third-quarter buyback, after a **15.64M LIT** burn on Jul 10 2026. In both burns the destroyed coins came from the unreleased ecosystem reserve, not from the bought coins. The burns lower the total supply to **981.91M LIT** and shrink the reserve, so less LIT can be released later, but they take nothing out of the circulating 250M today.

Foundation buys are **0**: no company or foundation buys LIT outside the fee buyback, and the two LIT funds listed in Europe hold coins that already circulate. New long-term locks are **0**: the **111.61M LIT** staked can leave after a three-day wait and still counts as circulating.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the team and investor group: **500M LIT** held across about 260 wallets, scheduled to vest monthly from Dec 30 2026 to late 2029. The second is the Lighter ecosystem reserve with **231.81M LIT**, unscheduled, set aside for future points seasons, partners and incentives. Lighter has set aside 11M LIT for trading incentives tied to its Robinhood partnership. Two more wallets hold bought-back coins that already circulate: the operating wallet with **10.26M LIT**, which paid out **7.54M LIT** in the window to stakers and partners, and the treasury account with **679.37K LIT** bought since Oct 1 2026. We read all four every day on-chain. If the reserve or the insider group falls between two readings, the coins that left are added to sell pressure at the next reading.

## How LIT compares to other perp exchange tokens

Most perpetual exchange tokens now turn trading fees into buybacks, but they differ in where the bought coins end up. On Hyperliquid, fees buy HYPE into a fund that keeps it, so the bought HYPE leaves the market. Some other exchanges destroy what they buy. Lighter does a third thing: the bought LIT goes back out as staking pay and partner payments, and an equal amount of never-released reserve LIT is burned. The result is a smaller total supply and a smaller reserve, but an unchanged float.

The bigger difference is the insider share. LIT launched with half of its supply for the team and investors and a single one-year lock, so the unlocks start all at once on a known date and then run monthly for three years. Each monthly LIT release of about **13.51M** is more than five times what Lighter's fees bought in the last 90 days. Exchange tokens that started with a smaller insider share, or that vest from the first month, do not face a cliff of this kind.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Dec 30 2026 is the key date: the first team and investor release of about **13.51M LIT**, or the first sign of whether the vesting runs on a different calendar. Watch the 500M insider wallets for the first transfers to exchanges after that date.

Early January 2027 should bring the fourth-quarter burn, sized to the October to December buyback; at the current pace that is about **2.5M LIT**. If it again comes from the reserve, it books 0 against circulation; if Lighter burns the bought coins instead, it would count as a real removal.

In November 2026 Lighter plans to file for a US derivatives license. That is a demand story, not a supply one, but more trading means a bigger fee buyback. Lighter can also change the 6% staking rate at any time, and any new points season paid from the reserve would add new LIT to the market.

## Summary

LIT supply was flat at **0.00%** over the 90 days to Oct 25 2026 and is projected to rise about **+5.41%** in the next 90 days. Lighter's fee buyback of **2.40M LIT** pays stakers rather than removing coins, and its burns destroy reserve LIT that never circulated. The main risk is the **500M LIT** team and investor allocation, which starts unlocking at about **13.51M LIT** a month from Dec 30 2026. With a fixed 1B total and no new minting, the circulating LIT could still grow to nearly four times today's 250M as the insider coins and the reserve are released.

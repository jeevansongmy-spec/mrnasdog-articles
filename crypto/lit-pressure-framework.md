# LIT Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

LIT supply was flat over the last 90 days, at **0.00%**, and is set to grow about **+6.38%** in the next 90 days, when the team and investor lock ends. Lighter made all **1B LIT** at launch and can never make more; from Aug 6 to Nov 4 2026 its fee buyback bought **2.76M LIT** and paid the same kind of coins back out to stakers, while the Oct 9 2026 burn of **2.45M LIT** used reserve coins that never circulated. Other trackers show about **−0.17%** for the same 90 days, close to our reading.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Net new LIT on the market over the 90 days to Nov 4 2026 was **0.00%** of the **250M LIT** in circulation. Other trackers read **−0.17%** for the same window, a gap of **0.17 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The next 90 days look very different: about **15.95M LIT** of team and investor coins are due to unlock between Dec 29 2026 and Feb 2 2027, about **+6.38%** of circulation, and nothing on the buy side takes coins out for good. In one line, LIT is a fixed-supply exchange token that has been quiet so far and turns into an unlock story at the end of December.

## Sell pressure: where new LIT comes from

LIT has no protocol inflation in the usual sense, because every one of the **1B LIT** was made when the token was created and the token contract has no way to make more. Stakers do earn rewards of about 6% a year, and Lighter sent **1.72M LIT** to its staking reward wallet between Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026, for about **106.43M LIT** staked. Those coins came out of Lighter's treasury wallet, which holds bought-back LIT that already counts as circulating, so this row books **0**.

Vesting is the big one. Half of all LIT, **260M** for the team and **240M** for investors, has been locked since launch on Dec 30 2025, with a one-year lock and then three years of straight-line release. Not one of those **500M LIT** reached outside hands between Aug 6 and Nov 4 2026, so the last 90 days book **0**. From Dec 29 2026 the release runs at about **3.19M LIT** a week, and five weekly releases fall before Feb 2 2027, about **15.95M LIT**. Unlock trackers still disagree on the exact start day, from Dec 27 2026 to Jan 28 2027, and on whether the coins come weekly or monthly; the weekly reading sits in the middle of their range.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book **0**. The ecosystem reserve, which pays for future points seasons, partners and staking, moved only once in the window, for the Oct 9 2026 burn, and has no public payout date. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply also books **0**: there is no court estate or recovered hack pile of LIT waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new LIT goes

Lighter spends its trading fees on LIT every day, in small orders spread across each 24 hours on its own exchange. From Aug 6 to Nov 4 2026 that bought **2.76M LIT** for about **$9.44M**, with October the busiest month at about $4.1M. At today's price of about $3.36, the same dollar pace would buy roughly **2.81M LIT** in the next 90 days. The buyback still books **0**, because the coins do not leave circulation: each quarter they move to Lighter's treasury wallet and are paid out again, to stakers, an exchange account and partners.

The burn books **0** for a related reason. On Oct 9 2026 Lighter burned **2,452,017 LIT**, matching what it bought from July to September, but the burned coins came from the ecosystem reserve, and the bought coins went to the treasury wallet less than an hour later. Lighter says the two are worth the same to holders. On the market count, though, the reserve never circulated, so circulation did not shrink. Burns now total **18.09M LIT**, which is why the total supply reads 981.91M rather than 1B.

There is no separate foundation buy beyond the fee buyback, and the two exchange-traded products listed in Europe, one on Sep 23 2026 and one in late October 2026, buy coins that already circulate, so that row books **0**. Staking is not a long-term lock either: stakers can leave on three days' notice, so the **106.43M LIT** in the staking pool still counts as circulating and the lock row books **0**.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest overhang is the team and investor allocation: **500M LIT** spread over 262 funded wallets, the biggest holding **58.92M**, **51.22M** and **42.39M LIT**, with **36.12M LIT** still in one of the two wallets that handed out the allocation. These coins sit in ordinary wallets rather than a lock contract, so the lock is a promise, and the release from late December is what the Sell pressure section counts.

The second is the ecosystem reserve, with **231.81M LIT**, the remaining quarter of supply set aside for points seasons, partners and staking. Its last payout into circulation was 100,000 LIT on Jul 3 2026. Two smaller balances sit inside circulation already: Lighter's treasury wallet holds **7.88M LIT** after paying out **9.65M LIT** in the window, and the treasury account on the exchange holds **1.36M LIT** bought since Oct 1 2026. If the team and investor wallets or the reserve send coins to outside hands, those coins count as new supply on this page the next time it is checked.

## How LIT compares to other perp exchange tokens

Most exchange tokens that compete with Lighter use fees to buy their own token, and the real question is where those coins end up. Hyperliquid's fee fund also buys HYPE every day, but it keeps what it buys, so that buying takes coins off the market. Lighter buys every day too, then pays the coins back out, mostly as staking rewards, so its buyback acts like a staking budget funded by traders rather than a way to shrink supply.

The other difference is the shape of the insider release. LIT gave a quarter of supply to users on day one and locked half for the team and investors for exactly one year, so the token has run for twelve months with no insider selling and now moves to a steady weekly flow of about 3.19M LIT for three years. Against a fee buyback that took about 2.76M LIT in a whole quarter, the unlock will be several times larger than the buying from the first week of January.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The first date is the end of the team and investor lock, between Dec 27 and Dec 30 2026, with weekly releases of about **3.19M LIT** from Dec 29 2026; watch whether the first coins go to exchanges or stay put. The second is the fourth-quarter burn, expected in early January 2027 and about the size of the **1.36M LIT** bought since Oct 1 plus what is bought until Dec 31: if it again uses reserve coins, it changes nothing on the market count, but a burn of the bought coins themselves would start to shrink circulation.

The third is the treasury wallet. It paid out about 3.2M LIT a month over the last quarter, so even with the January top-up from the buyback it could run low around February 2027. Lighter has said staking rewards come from the ecosystem reserve; once payouts start coming from the reserve itself, they count as new supply. The fourth is Lighter's planned US license application, which the founder said would be filed in November 2026; it does not change supply, but it is the main thing traders are watching. A third points season, often talked about, has no date or size yet.

## Summary

LIT supply was flat at **0.00%** over the 90 days to Nov 4 2026, close to the **−0.17%** other trackers show. The fee buyback of **2.76M LIT** in that time ends up paying stakers, and the Oct 9 2026 burn used reserve coins, so neither shrinks circulation. The team and investor lock ends in late December 2026, and about **15.95M LIT** unlock by Feb 2 2027, about **+6.38%** of circulation. The ecosystem reserve of **231.81M LIT** is the other large balance that could reach the market without a published date.

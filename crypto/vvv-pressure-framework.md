# VVV Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

VVV, the token of the private AI app Venice, is **inflationary**: circulating supply grew by about **+3.37%** in the 90 days to Nov 13 2026 and is on course for about **+3.03%** in the next 90. Team vesting, staking pay and company payouts added **1.90M VVV** to the market, while Venice's revenue burns and coins flowing back to company wallets took out **253K VVV**. VVV has no supply cap, and the team vesting lock that drives most of the new supply empties on **Jan 27 2027**; other trackers show **+2.72%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days from Aug 15 to Nov 13 2026, VVV supply on the market rose by a net **1.65M VVV**, or **+3.37%** of the **48.97M VVV** in circulation. For the next 90 days, to Feb 11 2027, the VVV ledger projects **+3.03%**. Other trackers show **+2.72%** for the same window, a gap of **0.65 points**, so the VVV coin page carries a warning note. The gap has one cause: on Aug 17 2026 those trackers moved a staff vesting lock holding about 316,000 VVV onto their locked list, and their circulating figure fell **309,171 VVV** in one day while no coins moved on the Base chain. Counted the same way at both ends, the Venice Token reading stays at +3.37%. The short label for VVV today: **inflationary by team unlocks, softened by revenue burns**.

## Sell pressure: where new VVV comes from

Protocol inflation added **553,074 VVV** to holders. VVV is minted every second and paid to stakers, and Venice keeps cutting the rate: from 3M VVV a year to 2.5M on Sep 1 2026, then to 2M on Oct 1 2026. About 582,600 VVV were minted in the window, but roughly 5% of each mint goes to the Venice treasury wallet, which sits outside the circulating count, so 553,074 reached the market. At the new 2M-a-year rate the next 90 days add about **468,000 VVV**.

Vesting unlocks were the biggest source, at **1.24M VVV**. Venice's 10M VVV team grant streams out of an on-chain vesting lock second by second, and a smaller staff lock does the same. Team members withdrew 1,184,117 VVV from the main lock and 55,160 VVV from the staff lock between Aug 15 and Nov 13 2026. The main team lock ends on Jan 27 2027, so every one of the **1.01M VVV** still inside it opens inside the next 90 days, plus about 53,000 VVV from the staff lock, which runs into 2028 and beyond.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks added **109,565 VVV**. The Venice treasury sends roughly 25,000 VVV a month to its payroll wallet: 24,970 on Aug 31, 25,546 on Sep 29 and 46,915 on Oct 29 2026. A second company wallet paid out 9,134 VVV on Sep 17 2026, and the liquidity wallet sent out 3,000 VVV in small amounts. Three more payroll sends would add about 97,000 VVV by Feb 11 2027.

The long-term locked or bankruptcy row is **zero**. No court estate or failed firm holds VVV waiting to be paid out, and the 33.5M VVV burned in March 2025, mostly unclaimed airdrop coins, can never return.

## Buy pressure: where new VVV goes

The programmatic buyback burned **73,836 VVV**. Every new Venice subscription and five dollars of every hundred spent on AI credits buy VVV on the market and burn it at once. Those burns were worth about **$1.63M** over the window; at today's price of about $19.55 the same spending would burn about **83,000 VVV** in the next 90 days.

The protocol fee burn is **zero**. VVV is a token on Base, not a chain of its own, so no network fee is destroyed in VVV; every Venice Token burn is paid for out of Venice's revenue.

The Foundation buy burned **54,344 VVV**. Once a month Venice uses part of its cash flow to buy VVV and burns it in one transaction: 16,563 VVV on Sep 8, 16,124 VVV on Oct 7 and a record 21,657 VVV on Nov 9 2026, worth about $391K, $414K and $458K. Three more burns of that size would remove about **65,000 VVV** at today's price.

New long-term locks are **zero**. About 34.57M VVV is staked, and part of the stake is locked to mint DIEM, the Venice credit token worth one dollar of AI use a day. Staked VVV still counts as circulating, so staking and DIEM remove nothing.

A fifth row, coins returned to company wallets, took back **124,908 VVV**: 80,000 VVV into the second company wallet on Oct 5 2026 and 44,908 VVV into the liquidity wallet as it pulled VVV out of trading pools. These are one-off moves, so the next 90 days book none.

## Foundation and overhang

Venice the company is the largest VVV holder. Its main treasury wallet, which received all 100M VVV at launch, holds **20.71M VVV** plus **1.20M VVV** staked. A second company wallet holds **8.71M VVV**, and a liquidity wallet holds **1.58M VVV**. The two vesting locks hold **1.01M VVV** (team, to Jan 27 2027) and **264,298 VVV** (staff, into 2030). Together these five balances are the whole part of the Venice Token supply that is not counted as circulating.

Venice's equity investors also hold a grant of 1.5M VVV and warrants for 5M VVV more from the Series A round of Jul 1 2026; both stay locked until about Jul 2027, outside this window. The burn wallets and the payroll wallet hold only small working balances and sit inside the circulating count. Every company and vesting balance is read on the Base chain at each check: if any of them falls between checks, the outflow enters the VVV sell rows at the next check.

## How VVV compares to other AI and revenue-burn tokens

Against other AI tokens, VVV sits between two designs. Bittensor's TAO has a hard cap of 21M and halves its emission on a fixed schedule; Virtuals' VIRTUAL has a fixed supply of 1B with no new minting. VVV has no cap at all, but Venice cuts the emission by announcement, and those cuts have taken it from 14M VVV a year at launch to 2M today. The cap question matters less for VVV than the pace of cuts and the size of the burn.

Against buy-and-burn tokens such as Hyperliquid's HYPE, where trading fees buy the token back, VVV's burn is funded by a company's revenue rather than by a protocol. That makes the Venice burn bigger when subscriptions and credit sales grow, and smaller if they slow. Today the burn covers only about a quarter of the new staking coins and a far smaller share of the team unlocks.

The difference that matters most for the next quarter is the team lock. Many tokens release team coins in monthly cliffs; Venice streams them every second, so there is no single unlock day, just a steady flow that stops when the lock runs out on Jan 27 2027. After that date, staking pay and the payroll sends are the only large sources of new VVV left.

# VVV Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

*Originally published at [mrnasdog.com/research/vvv/inflation](https://mrnasdog.com/research/vvv/inflation).*

This is the long supply read. For the signal, the price drivers and the questions people ask about Venice Token, see [mrnasdog.com/research/vvv](https://mrnasdog.com/research/vvv).

Circulating VVV is growing and should keep growing: from Aug 24 to Nov 22 2026 stakers were paid **557,931 VVV** in new coins and team and staff vesting released **1.25M VVV**, while Venice bought and burned **130,628 VVV** with its own income, so the float rose **+3.32%**, with about **+2.82%** expected next. VVV has no supply cap; new coins come from a staking payout that Venice has cut to 2M a year, and the team's vesting stream ends on Jan 27 2027. Other trackers show **+3.29%** for the same window, in line with the chain.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 22 2026, circulating VVV grew by **1.63M VVV**, a net rise of **+3.32%**, and the next 90 days project **+2.82%**. Other trackers print **+3.29%**, a gap of only **0.03 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. The rise has two engines: the steady staking payout and the team's vesting stream, which together released more than ten times what the burns removed. Venice's burns are real and growing, but on today's numbers VVV is an inflationary token whose float grows faster than its income can buy it back, at least until the team stream runs dry in late January 2027.

## Sell pressure: where new VVV comes from

The staking payout is the first source of new VVV. Venice's staking contract creates new VVV every second and hands about 95% of it to people who stake, with the rest going to a Venice wallet. Between Aug 24 and Nov 22 2026 it created **557,931 VVV**. The yearly pace was 3M VVV at the start of the window, fell to 2.5M on Sep 1 2026 and to 2M on Oct 1 2026, both steps announced by Venice in August. At 2M a year the next 90 days add about **493,151 VVV**, and Venice has not announced a further cut.

Vesting is the largest source. The team's 2025 grant streams out of a vesting contract every second until Jan 27 2027, and staff grants stream out of a second contract on schedules that run as late as 2030. Holders claimed **1.25M VVV** from the two contracts in the window: 1,201,250 VVV from the team stream and 48,900 VVV from staff streams. The team contract still held **878,473 VVV** on Nov 22 2026, and all of it can be claimed by Jan 27 2027; the staff streams add about 55,691 VVV by Feb 20 2027. That puts about **934,164 VVV** of vesting into the next 90 days.

Venice's own wallets released **81,075 VVV** net. The main Venice wallet sent 97,431 VVV in monthly payments to the wallet that pays its people, on Aug 31, Sep 29 and Oct 29 2026, while it kept about 28,286 VVV of new staking coins; the incentive fund paid out 9,133 VVV on Sep 17 2026; and the liquidity wallet sent small amounts. At the same monthly pace the next 90 days bring about **72,773 VVV** net from the main wallet, and the other two wallets have no schedule.

Long-term locks add nothing in this window. No bankrupt firm or court holds VVV, and the 1.5M VVV grant plus rights to 5M more that Venice gave its Series A investors on Jul 1 2026 are locked for a year, so none of it can reach the market before about Jul 2027.

## Buy pressure: where new VVV goes

The automatic buy-and-burn is the steadiest buyer. Every paid Venice plan and every purchase of developer credit sets aside a few dollars, 5% in the case of credits, that buy VVV and burn it. That burned **76,284 VVV** in the window, worth about $1.71M when it happened. At today's price near $26, the same spending burns about **65,926 VVV** in the next 90 days, so a higher price means fewer coins burned for the same dollars.

VVV has no network fee burn, because it is not the fee coin of a chain; every burn comes from Venice's income, not from the network itself.

The monthly company burn is the second buyer. Once a month Venice buys VVV in the market and burns it in one shot: **16,563 VVV** on Sep 8, **16,124 VVV** on Oct 7 and a record **21,657 VVV** on Nov 9 2026, about $1.24M in total. If the same dollars keep coming, three more burns in the next 90 days would remove about **47,832 VVV**.

Staking takes nothing out of the count. About 34.1M VVV is staked, and part of it is locked to create DIEM, Venice's credit token, but staked and locked VVV still counts as circulating.

Coins also flowed back into Venice's wallets: **79,999 VVV** into its incentive fund on Oct 5 2026 and 44,908 VVV into its liquidity wallet in September and early November, for **124,907 VVV** in all. These returns have no schedule, so the next 90 days book none.

## Foundation and overhang

Venice's main wallet is the largest overhang, with **20.71M VVV** on Nov 22 2026. It also controls the staking contract and receives about 5% of new staking coins, and Venice says it does not plan to sell its treasury. The incentive fund holds **8.71M VVV** for ecosystem grants and cannot pay Venice staff. The liquidity wallet holds **1.58M VVV**. The team vesting contract holds **878,473 VVV**, all due by Jan 27 2027, and the staff vesting contract holds **261,927 VVV** on schedules to 2030. A company wallet that buys coins for the monthly burn held 10,043 VVV, waiting for the December burn. We read every one of these wallets on the chain each day; if a wallet's balance falls between checks, the coins that left join the sell side at the next check.

## How VVV compares to other AI app tokens

Most tokens tied to a business fall into two groups. Some, like VIRTUAL, have no buyback at all, so new coins meet only market demand. Others, like PUMP and HYPE, spend a large share of their income on buybacks. VVV sits between the two: its burns are wired into every sale, which few app tokens do, but the burn budget is a small slice of Venice's income next to the coins it pays out to stakers and staff.

What sets VVV apart is how fast its new supply is shrinking. The staking payout fell from 14M VVV a year at launch in January 2025 to 2M a year on Oct 1 2026, and the team stream stops in January 2027. After that, about 2M VVV a year of staking pay meets burns worth about 460K VVV a year at today's price, so the gap narrows a lot, though the float would still grow unless Venice spends more or cuts the payout again.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Early December 2026:** the next monthly company burn. The last three landed on Sep 8, Oct 7 and Nov 9 2026, each a little larger in coins or dollars than the one before.

**Jan 27 2027:** the team vesting stream ends. The 878,473 VVV still inside the contract on Nov 22 2026 is fully released by then, and after that date only the small staff streams keep vesting.

**Any emission news:** Venice has cut the staking payout step by step, from 14M VVV a year at launch to 2M since Oct 1 2026. A new cut would lower the 493,151 VVV expected in the next 90 days; none is announced as of Nov 22 2026.

**Mar 2 2027:** one more staff stream of 50,000 VVV starts, just after this window; and the Series A investors' lock runs to about Jul 2027.

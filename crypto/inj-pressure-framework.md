# INJ Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Supply is growing and is projected to keep growing. The Pressure Framework estimates **1.042M INJ** of protocol issuance against **96.1K INJ** destroyed in three completed buyback rounds, giving **+0.95%** over the trailing 90 days and **+1.01%** projected forward; the monitor reads **+0.22%**. Injective has no fixed supply cap, and movements between its native coin and bridged representations must be separated from genuine issuance.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The trailing framework reading is **+0.95%**, compared with the monitor’s **+0.22%**. The monitor is **0.72 percentage points** lower, so the overview retains a warning. The framework uses the required **100.0M INJ** circulating denominator, although the combined native and unbridged balances imply approximately **128.13M INJ** globally. The historical mint figure is a protocol-math estimate supported by actual block counts and bridge balances, not a directly observed quarter-long supply delta. The resulting characterization is an uncapped staking chain whose recurring burn remains smaller than its issuance.

## Sell pressure: where new INJ comes from

Protocol inflation is estimated at **1.042M INJ** over the trailing window. The active annual rate reads **4.4%**, with an adjustable range of **2.2%–4.4%**. Issuance is calculated per block using the native supply base and the configured blocks per year. The chain produced approximately **12.57M blocks** during the measured quarter; actual block cadence therefore matters more than multiplying a headline annual percentage by a calendar fraction.

The model also accounts for a major bridge change. Locked original tokens on Ethereum increased from **82.89M INJ** to **94.81M**, which permits corresponding native representation to expand without creating global supply. Thirteen fresh bridge-balance snapshots support the historical supply-base estimate. Earlier native state was pruned, and interpolating those snapshots introduces modeling uncertainty. Recent actual bank growth independently supports the order of magnitude. Forward protocol issuance uses the current observed block pace and is estimated at **1.108M INJ**.

Vesting unlocks are **0** because the original allocation programme is complete. Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add **0** new units: community reward disbursements move coins that have already been issued. Long-term locked or bankruptcy releases are **0**; normal staking exits are movements within the existing float rather than a newly identified estate distribution.

## Buy pressure: where new INJ goes

Programmatic buyback records **96.1K INJ** of destruction. The completed rounds on **Jul 8 2026**, **Aug 5 2026** and **Sep 2 2026** removed **43.5K**, **27.4K** and **25.2K INJ**, respectively. A historical native supply read at the September settlement independently confirms the burn after adding back that block’s small mint. Committed tokens are destroyed by the protocol; they do not remain in an accumulation wallet that could later sell them.

The next 90-day estimate is **96.1K INJ**, using three scheduled rounds and the mean of all three completed settlements in the current window. Individual round sizes vary, so a smaller recent observation is not treated as a permanent new regime. This is a cadence-based estimate, not a promised purchase amount.

Protocol fee burn is **0** as a separate row because the fee-funded auction destruction is already counted under programmatic buyback. Foundation buying is **0** in this window, and new long-term lock is **0**. Proposed investment products, bridge custody and ordinary staking do not independently establish a new removal of tradable INJ.

## Foundation and overhang

The governance community pool held approximately **6.8K INJ** at the fresh read. Its distributions require governance approval and should be checked daily. Passed liquidity-provider rewards in this quarter redistribute existing community funds, which were already included when minted; treating the same rewards as another supply increase would count them twice.

A listed corporate holder disclosed a **560.6K INJ** purchase in February, before this window. Its wallet identity was not published in the reviewed release, so filings and corporate announcements remain the verification route, refreshed at least every two weeks. A purchasing authorization is not a completed trade, and staking those holdings does not by itself establish exclusion from circulation. If a controlled balance falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the Foundation row only when it represents a newly available quantity beyond issuance already counted. There is no buyback inventory: completed rounds destroy the tokens.

## How INJ compares to other continuous-emission chains

Injective resembles other proof-of-stake networks that issue rewards continuously, with the extra feature of a revenue-funded destruction cycle. The relevant comparison is how much issuance the burn covers. A chain can describe an effective burn mechanism while still expanding its net supply if the reward stream is larger. For this quarter, the measured auction destruction offsets only a small portion of estimated staking issuance.

Compared with a hard-capped halving model, INJ has no fixed terminal quantity. Compared with a token whose full genesis allocation is unlocked, Injective shows why completed vesting does not imply zero future inflation. Native issuance continues after investor schedules finish. Cross-chain representations add another accounting requirement: tokens locked in one network and represented in another must be consolidated before any global supply conclusion.

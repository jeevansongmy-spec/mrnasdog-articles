## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Jul 31 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **FET at about -0.06% net** — roughly steady, with the Earn & Burn just edging out the migration unlock drip on a hard-capped supply. Our supply monitor reads the realised 90-day change deeper, at **-0.67%**, a gap of about **0.61 percentage points** that clears the 0.5-point tolerance, so a monitor-gap chip ships. The gap is a scheduled-versus-realised story: the framework books the full scheduled migration cliffs, while the monitor's classified circulating supply captures the realised flow — where migration claims to the float run below the calendar and the Earn & Burn is settling coins out of circulation. Both point the same way. FET is a **quiet, hard-capped AI token** sitting on the flat line between mild deflation and zero growth.

## Sell pressure: where new FET comes from

Sell #1 — protocol inflation — is **zero**, and that is the structural heart of FET. The token is hard-capped, with total supply already equal to max supply at **2.71B FET**, so every FET that will ever exist has been minted. Staking rewards on the network — about **5.6%** yield on the roughly **20%** of FET that is staked — are paid from a pre-allocated reward reserve that already sits inside the cap, not from new issuance. Because nothing is minted above the ceiling, there is no continuous protocol inflation to book, which is unusual for a staking token.

Sell #2 — vesting unlocks — is the only live sell-side flow, at about **7.6M FET** over 90 days. Legacy holders of the two merged tokens keep converting into FET on a recurring monthly cliff of about **2.53M** coins on the 28th, and three of those cliffs — in **May**, **June** and **July** — fall inside this window. These are reclassifications of already-minted, mostly already-tradeable supply rather than new mint, and the framework applies its released-beats-scheduled rule here: the migration allocation sits in an on-chain contract, and the amount actually claimed to the float runs below the calendar, which is a large part of why the monitor reads deeper than the framework. Sell #3 — Foundation and unscheduled unlocks — is zero as a flow: the founders, foundation and future-release reserves book no dated discretionary release. Sell #4 — long-term locked or bankruptcy — is zero, because no bankruptcy estate or court distribution applies to FET.

## Buy pressure: where new FET goes

Buy #1 — the programmatic buyback — is the only active offset, at about **9M FET** over 90 days. The **$50M** Earn & Burn programme spends ecosystem-service fees buying FET on the market and burning it, on a stated path to cut supply from **2.8B** toward **2.7B** — roughly **100M** coins over the initiative. Its first burn removed **5M FET** on **Jan 10 2026**, and at that programme pace it takes about **9M FET** off the market per 90 days. Because the destination is a burn address, those coins are gone for good, so the buyback counts cleanly on the buy side. Buy #2 — protocol fee burn — is carried at zero, because the chain has no separately quantified base-fee burn and the Earn & Burn is the only active burn. Buy #3 — Foundation buy — and Buy #4 — new long-term lock — are both zero, with no discretionary open-market buying or new escrow announced in the window, and the roughly 20% of FET already staked is existing float rather than a fresh lock.

## Foundation and overhang

FET's overhang is locked, not scheduled at the discretion of the team. About **474M FET** remains locked — the gap between the roughly **2.24B** circulating and the **2.71B** capped total, or about **17.5%** of supply — and most of it is the remaining AGIX and Ocean migration allocation plus the founders, foundation and future-release reserves. The migration portion drips into circulation on its cliff schedule and is captured in Sell #2; the founders and foundation reserves book no dated release and sit in Sell #3 at a flow of zero. The framework re-checks the locked-pool balances and the burn reports on a roughly bi-weekly walk; if any of those reserve balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How FET compares to other hard-capped tokens

FET belongs to the class of **hard-capped tokens with no protocol inflation** — closer to a fixed-supply asset than to an uncapped, continuous-emission layer-1. Unlike a staking chain that mints fresh rewards every block and dilutes holders continuously, FET pays staking yield from a reserve already counted inside its 2.71B cap, so its supply cannot grow past the ceiling. That puts it alongside exchange and utility tokens that pair a fixed cap with a fee-funded burn, rather than alongside inflationary proof-of-stake chains.

The contrast worth drawing is with tokens that burn aggressively enough to go firmly net-deflationary. FET's Earn & Burn is real but modest — about **9M FET** a quarter against a **2.24B** float — so it roughly cancels the migration unlocks rather than driving meaningful scarcity. For an inflation lens specifically, that means FET reads as flat to mildly shrinking: no new mint, a winding-down migration tail, and a burn that just outpaces it. The supply story is decided by how the burn scales as the migration unlocks finish, and by how much of the migration allocation is ever actually claimed to the float.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

Watch the pace of the Earn & Burn — the size of each burn is the single number that decides whether FET tips more firmly deflationary as the migration tail thins. Watch the next migration unlock cliff on **Aug 28 2026** at about **2.5M FET**, and the near-monthly steps behind it, since those are the whole of the sell side. Watch the **474M FET** locked pool, especially the founders and foundation reserves, for any first sign of a discretionary release. And watch how the monitor gap moves: if realised circulating keeps running deeper than the scheduled unlocks imply, it confirms that migration is being reclassified faster than it is claimed to the float.

## Summary

FET is a hard-capped, non-inflationary AI token whose supply cannot grow past 2.71B, because every coin is already minted. With protocol inflation at zero, the only sell-side flow is about **7.6M FET** of migration unlocks over 90 days, while the Earn & Burn buys and burns roughly **9M FET**, leaving the framework at about **-0.06% net**. Our supply monitor reads **-0.67%** realised — a **0.61-point** gap that ships a monitor-gap flag and points, if anything, to slightly more deflation than the scheduled ledger shows. The key risk is the 474M FET locked pool of migration and reserve coins — but none of it books a dated discretionary release, and the cap is the firm ceiling on everything.

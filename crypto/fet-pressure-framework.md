# FET Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the ASI Alliance coin page for the short answer (should you buy FET?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/fet](https://mrnasdog.com/research/fet)

FET is inflationary, even though no new FET was created on Ethereum. From Aug 28 to Nov 26 2026 the team-run bridge reserve paid **195.19M FET** to an exchange and the Fetch.ai admin multisig sold **23.41M FET**, so the circulating supply grew a net **+9.46%**, with about **+5.50%** more possible in the next 90 days. Nothing was burned and nothing was bought back; other trackers show **+2.17%** because their circulating figure stopped moving on Sep 4 2026.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

**Supply growing, and projected to keep growing.** In the 90 days to Nov 26 2026, **218.60M FET** left wallets run by the team and reached the market, against about **2.31B FET** counted as circulating. That is **+9.46%** in one quarter. Other trackers show only **+2.17%**, because the circulating figure they use has not moved since Sep 4 2026 and so misses everything paid out after that day. The gap between the two numbers is **7.29 points**, and the coin page carries a warning note that explains it. The next 90 days look smaller, about **+5.50%**, only because the two team wallets that fund these payouts now hold **127.05M FET** between them.

## Sell pressure: where new FET comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0 on the counted supply.** The FET token on Ethereum has no built-in issuance; its total stayed at **2.71B FET** for the whole window. FET's own chain, where people stake FET to secure the network, does pay its stakers about **3% a year** in new coins, roughly **11.32M FET** over these 90 days. Those coins only join the counted supply when someone moves them across the bridge to Ethereum, and that path is already counted in the bridge row below.

**Vesting unlocks: 0.** No vesting batch opened between Aug 28 and Nov 26 2026. The contract that swaps old AGIX tokens into FET was emptied on Sep 19 2026 and conversions have been paused since Sep 20 2026. Its coins were already counted as circulating, so a release some trackers list for Nov 28 2026 adds nothing.

**Foundation and unscheduled releases: 23.41M FET.** On Oct 14 2026 the Fetch.ai admin multisig sent **23.41M FET** to a brand-new wallet, which passed it on to a second wallet that sold all of it on a decentralized exchange in about two hours. It is the only sale of this kind from the multisig that we found, and no schedule says when, or whether, another one comes.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0.** No court case or bankrupt firm is holding FET for a later payout. The **8.72M FET** taken from the AGIX converter on Sep 19 2026 was already circulating, so the theft moved coins without adding any.

**Bridge reserve paid out to an exchange: 195.19M FET.** This is the big one. FET lives on two chains, Ethereum and FET's own chain, and a bridge moves it between them. The Ethereum side of that bridge holds a reserve run by the team. Between Aug 28 and Nov 26 2026 it paid out **195.19M FET** net, nearly all of it to a Binance deposit address, in five bursts: Sep 2–3, Oct 1–2, Oct 9, Oct 28–31 and Nov 18–23. On FET's own chain, the bridge admin created **100M new FET** on Aug 28 and Sep 18 2026 and kept sending FET to the same exchange, which swapped it across to Ethereum. Each time the reserve ran low, the admin multisig topped it up in lots of **10M FET**. The swap gate on FET's own chain was paused on Nov 25 2026, but the reserve and the multisig still hold **127.05M FET** that could take the same path.

## Buy pressure: where new FET goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0.** No buyback ran in the window. The only FET that flowed back into a team wallet came from small bridge swaps by ordinary holders, about **1.68M FET**, and those are already netted inside the bridge figure.

**Protocol fee burn: 0.** Nothing was burned. The total on Ethereum did not move, and the burn address gained about **2 FET** in 90 days. A proposal to burn **110M FET** left over from the Ocean Protocol merger was voted down on FET's own chain in Oct 2025.

**Foundation buy: 0.** The Foundation wallet did not buy any FET; it did not move at all between Aug 28 and Nov 26 2026.

**New long-term lock: 0.** FET staked on its own chain still counts as circulating, and the old staking contract on Ethereum took in nothing new, so no lock removed any FET from the count.

## Foundation and overhang

Four team-run wallets on Ethereum sit outside the circulating count. The Fetch.ai admin multisig holds **125.81M FET**; it grew in the window because it pulled **200M FET** out of the bridge on Sep 23 2026, days after the exploit, and has been sending it back in **10M FET** lots ever since. An old staking contract holds **80.62M FET** and the Foundation wallet holds **26.69M FET**; neither moved in the window. The bridge reserve itself is down to **1.24M FET**, from **264.43M FET** on Aug 28 2026.

On FET's own chain, the bridge admin wallet holds about **40.48M FET** and can create new FET through the bridge contract, as it did with **40M FET** on Aug 28 and **60M FET** on Sep 18 2026. A large Ethereum wallet left from the 2024 merger, **277.55M FET**, already counts as circulating and did not move.

## How FET compares to other AI tokens

Most AI tokens add supply in one of two ways: a mining or staking reward written into the protocol, or a vesting calendar for the team and early backers. FET's supply grows through neither. Its new supply comes from a reserve that the team controls by hand, paid out through the bridge and a multisig, with no public schedule.

That makes FET harder to read than a coin with a fixed reward or a published unlock table. A reward rate or an unlock date can be planned for; a team reserve can be paid out fast, as it was this quarter, or paused, as the swap gate was on Nov 25 2026. And unlike tokens that pair new supply with a buyback or a fee burn, FET has had neither in the last 90 days.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The paused swap gate.** The bridge's public swap from FET's own chain to Ethereum was paused on Nov 25 2026. It was also paused from Sep 20 to Sep 30 2026 and then reopened, after which the payouts to the exchange picked up again.

**The admin multisig.** Its **125.81M FET** funds every refill of the bridge reserve. Each new **10M FET** lot sent to the bridge has been followed by payouts to the exchange within minutes.

**Nov 28 2026, the listed AGIX release.** Some trackers show about **2.4M FET** released for AGIX holders that day. With the converter empty and paused, it should add nothing.

**Dec 7 2026, Bithumb ends FET trading.** The Korean exchange stops FET trading that day and closes withdrawals on Jan 7 2027. It moves no supply, but coins leaving the exchange could change where FET trades.

**New mints on FET's own chain.** The bridge admin created **100M FET** in two lots in this window. Another mint would be an early sign of more payouts to come.

## Summary

FET's circulating supply grew **+9.46%** between Aug 28 and Nov 26 2026, as **218.60M FET** left team-run wallets for the market.

Most of it, **195.19M FET**, came out of the bridge reserve to a Binance deposit address, and the Fetch.ai admin multisig sold another **23.41M FET** on Oct 14 2026.

Nothing was burned or bought back, and other trackers show only **+2.17%** because their circulating figure has not moved since Sep 4 2026.

The team wallets that fund the payouts still hold **127.05M FET**, enough for about **+5.50%** more in the next 90 days if the paused swap gate reopens.

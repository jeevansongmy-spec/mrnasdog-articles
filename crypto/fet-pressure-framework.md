# FET Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the ASI Alliance coin page for the short answer (should you buy FET?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/fet](https://mrnasdog.com/research/fet)

**FET**, the coin of the **ASI Alliance** built by Fetch.ai, is inflationary on its counted float even though its Ethereum supply is fixed at **2.71B**. No new FET was made on Ethereum and none was burned, but **197.98M FET** that the team had held back reached the market between Aug 8 and Nov 6 2026, mostly over the bridge from Fetch.ai's own chain into Binance. That puts FET supply up **+8.56%** in 90 days, with about **+7.55%** likely next, while other trackers show **+3.60%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

FET supply on the counted float grew **+8.56%** in the 90 days to Nov 6 2026, and the next 90 days point to about **+7.55%**. Other trackers show **+3.60%**, a gap of **4.96 percentage points**, so the FET coin page carries a warning note. The reason is simple: the circulating figure those trackers use has not moved since Sep 4 2026. It caught the 80M FET that crossed the bridge in August and early September, but not the 95M FET that crossed in October or the 23.41M FET the team multisig sold on Oct 14 2026. Our label for FET today: a fixed-cap token whose held-back supply is steadily being released.

## Sell pressure: where new FET comes from

Protocol inflation books **0** for FET. The Ethereum token has a fixed supply of 2.71B, and the total did not change at all in the window. Fetch.ai's own chain does pay its stakers about 3% a year, close to 11.4M FET every 90 days, but coins on that chain only join the FET count when they cross the bridge to Ethereum, and the bridge is already counted in full below.

Vesting unlocks book **0**. Every FET from the 2019 launch and the 2024 merger with AGIX and OCEAN has already been made, so no locked tranche is waiting on a calendar. The FET set aside for old AGIX holders already counts as circulating, and those swaps have been paused since the exploit on Sep 19 2026.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are where FET supply grows: **197.98M FET** left wallets that were not yet counted. The larger part, **174.57M FET**, came out of the Ethereum bridge in lots of 5M to 10M FET on Aug 19–20, Sep 2–3, Oct 1–2, Oct 9 and Oct 28–31 2026. Every lot landed in two Binance hot wallets, after the team's own account on Fetch.ai's chain had kept sending FET to the same exchange. When the bridge ran low, the team multisig refilled it with **90M FET** in October. The rest, **23.41M FET**, left that same team multisig on Oct 14 2026 for a wallet that sold it through a market maker and a swap router. The bridge lots have come every month since August, so we expect about **174.57M FET** more in the next 90 days. The Oct 14 sale has no schedule, so it is not carried forward.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply books **0**. No court estate holds FET. The 8.72M FET taken from the AGIX swap contract on Sep 19 2026 was already counted as circulating, so the theft moved FET without adding any.

## Buy pressure: where new FET goes

The programmatic buyback books **0**. Nothing buys FET back on a schedule, and the Earn & Burn plan left no trace on Ethereum between Aug 8 and Nov 6 2026: no FET went into a burn or a locked wallet.

The protocol fee burn books **0**. The FET total supply stayed at 2,714,384,547 for the whole window and the dead address did not move. The last FET burn was 109,350 FET in late 2025.

The Foundation buy books **0**. The team bought no FET back; its Ethereum wallets only sent coins out.

A new long-term lock books **0**. Users moved 1.91M FET into the bridge toward Fetch.ai's chain, which is already netted inside the 174.57M bridge figure, and a 2025 proposal to burn 110M FET was voted down on Nov 2 2025. With no buyback, no burn and no lock, nothing offsets the FET release.

## Foundation and overhang

Four FET wallets still sit outside the counted float, together **285.27M FET**. The team multisig holds **175.81M FET**, up from 81.21M on Aug 8 2026 because it pulled 200M FET out of the bridge on Sep 23 2026 as a safety step after the exploit. An old staking contract holds **80.62M FET** and a team Safe holds **26.69M FET**; neither moved in the window or in the year before it. The Ethereum bridge itself is down to **2.16M FET**, from 294.73M on Aug 8 2026, so every new bridge lot now needs a refill from the team multisig.

A fifth FET wallet is worth knowing about even though it already counts as circulating: the Safe that received the 2024 merger supply still holds **277.55M FET** and has not moved this year. We read all of these FET balances on-chain at every rebuild. If any of them falls between rebuilds, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next one.

## How FET compares to other AI token chains

Most AI tokens grow their supply through a published emission. Bittensor's TAO pays new coins to miners and validators on a capped schedule that halves over time. Render uses a burn-and-mint design, where work paid in RENDER is burned and new coins are minted to node operators. NEAR pays validators through protocol inflation and burns part of every fee. In each case a buyer can read the rate in advance.

FET is different. On Ethereum, FET has a hard cap that is fully minted, so it looks like a fixed-supply coin with no inflation at all. The real FET supply growth comes from coins the team held back, released without a published schedule, and from a second chain whose own staking pay only shows up when coins cross the bridge. That makes FET harder to forecast than a coin with a clear emission curve, and it means the headline cap says little about how much new FET the market must absorb.

FET also has no burn working against the release. Coins with a fee burn or a buyback can offset part of their new supply; FET offset none of its 197.98M in this window.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Bridge lots after Nov 6 2026:** the FET bridge has paid out every month since August, and any new lot, after a refill from the team multisig, adds to supply at once.

**Nov 28 2026:** an unlock calendar lists an AGIX Migration release for this day; it adds no new FET to the count, because those coins already circulate and the swaps are paused.

**Dec 7 2026:** two Korean exchanges, Bithumb and Coinone, end FET trading, citing the Sep 19 2026 exploit; withdrawals stay open until Jan 7 2027. That can move FET between holders but does not change supply.

**The team multisig balance:** at 175.81M FET it is the largest held-back pile, and its next move decides how fast FET keeps growing.

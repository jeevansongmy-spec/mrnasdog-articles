# FET Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

FET is **inflationary**, and not because of a mint schedule. The token on Ethereum is fully created at **2.71B FET** and none was added or burned in the last 90 days. The growth comes from team wallets that do not count as circulating: between Aug 18 and Nov 16 2026 they released **197.93M FET**, against **0** FET bought back or burned. ## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over 90 days the ASI Alliance token added **197.93M FET** to the market and took **0** away, a net rise of **+8.56%**. The next 90 days look much the same at about **+7.55%**, because the bridge that paid out most of the coins is still being topped up by the team multisig. Other trackers put the last 90 days at **+3.58%**, a gap of about **5 points**. Their circulating figure has not changed since Sep 4 2026, so it misses every payout after that day, and the coin page carries a warning note that says so. The label for FET this month is simple: **supply growing, and projected to keep growing**.

## Sell pressure: where new FET comes from

**Protocol inflation adds nothing to the counted supply.** FET on Ethereum has no built-in issuance. Fetch.ai's own chain does pay stakers about 3% a year in new coins, close to **11.4M FET** every 90 days, and the chain's bridge created a further **100M FET** there on Aug 28 and Sep 18 2026. Those native coins only reach FET holders on Ethereum by crossing the bridge, so they are counted once, in the third row below.

**Vesting unlocks added nothing.** No locked batch opened in the window. Unlock trackers show an AGIX swap release on Nov 28 2026, but AGIX-to-FET swaps have been paused since the Sep 20 2026 exploit, and the FET set aside for those swaps is already counted as circulating.

**Team and bridge releases added 197.93M FET.** The Ethereum bridge paid out **174.53M FET** in round lots of 5M and 10M on Aug 19–20, Sep 2–3, Oct 1–2, Oct 9 and Oct 28–31 2026. Almost every lot went through one forwarding wallet straight to an exchange. On Oct 14 2026 the team multisig also sent **23.41M FET** through a Safe to five outside wallets. Both the bridge and the multisig sit outside the circulating count, so every coin that left them is new supply for the market.

**Long-term locks and bankruptcies added nothing.** No court case or bankrupt firm holds FET waiting to pay it out. The **8.72M FET** taken from a SingularityNET swap contract on Sep 19 2026 had already been circulating, so the theft moved coins without creating any.

## Buy pressure: where new FET goes

**There is no working buyback.** The $50M buy-and-burn plan announced in 2025 bought nothing into a team wallet between Aug 18 and Nov 16 2026, and the token's total supply did not move at all.

**There is no fee burn.** The burn address gained about 2 FET in 90 days, which is too small to matter, and the supply stayed at exactly 2.71B FET.

**The team bought nothing back.** About **1.78M FET** flowed into the bridge from holders moving coins to Fetch.ai's own chain. That small return is already subtracted from the 197.93M figure, so it is not counted twice.

**No new lock took FET away.** Roughly 641M FET is staked on Fetch.ai's own chain, but those coins sit outside the Ethereum count, so more staking there does not shrink the supply measured here.

## Foundation and overhang

Four team wallets on Ethereum hold FET that does not count as circulating. The team multisig holds **175.81M FET**, more than double its 81.21M on Aug 18 2026, because it pulled 200M out of the bridge on Sep 23 2026 after the exploit and has fed 90M back since. The old staking contract holds **80.62M FET** and the wallet marked as the Fetch.ai Foundation holds **26.69M FET**; neither moved in the window. The bridge itself is down to **2.12M FET** from 294.65M. Together that is **285.24M FET** still able to reach the market, about 12% of today's circulating supply.

On Fetch.ai's own chain, the bridge admin holds about **36.88M FET** plus **57.49M FET** staked. A separate Safe holding **277.55M FET** from the 2024 merger has not moved since September 2024; it is already counted as circulating, so a move from it would change nothing here.

## How FET compares to other AI tokens

Most large AI tokens grow their supply through a rule anyone can read. Bittensor pays miners and validators new TAO on a fixed schedule that halves over time. Render pays node operators new tokens and burns tokens when jobs are paid for, so heavy use can offset the new supply. FET works differently. Its Ethereum supply is fixed and fully made, and the coins that reach the market come from team-run wallets and a two-chain bridge, released in lots with no published calendar.

That makes FET harder to plan around than a coin with a schedule. A quiet month can turn into a **50M FET** week with no warning, as it did on Oct 28–31 2026. It also means the number other trackers print can drift far from the chain when their circulating figure stops updating, which is what happened after Sep 4 2026.

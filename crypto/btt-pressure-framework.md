## Sell pressure: where new BTT comes from

Nowhere, and on BitTorrent that is a statement about the deployed code rather than about the team's intentions. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0** because the BTT contract on TRON has no mint entry point — not a renounced one, an absent one. Its complete function list is transfers, approvals, allowances and the standard read methods; there is no owner, no proxy and no upgrade path. The supply read returns **990,000,000,000,000 BTT** and has returned that same figure since the contract was deployed in December 2021, when the redenominated BitTorrent token replaced the original BTT at a thousand to one.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **0** because the release calendar that once drove BTT supply has finished. The monthly airdrop to TRON holders ran on an escalating schedule from **Mar 11 2019** to **Feb 11 2025**, ending with a final tranche of **1,402,500,000** old BTT. There is no vesting contract or escrow left to read. Residual partnership and ecosystem allocations are documented as releasing out to 2028, which is why the row is carried as a checked zero rather than a permanent one — but no dated cliff with a published quantum falls inside this window, and the counted float did not rise.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0**. Subtracting the circulating figure from the on-chain total leaves **2,962,114,159,325 BTT** — **0.30%** of the cap — outside the counted float, with no published release date and no wallet we could tie it to. The two largest non-exchange BTT wallets on TRON, holding **261.99 trillion** and **112.60 trillion**, recorded not a single BTT transfer across the whole 90-day window. Sell #4 is **0** as well: BitTorrent has no bankruptcy estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution. The one large lock in the BitTorrent Chain system runs the other way — the validator stake contract on TRON ended the window holding **26.48 trillion BTT**, a net **5.30 trillion** more than it started with.

## Buy pressure: where new BTT goes

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is the row that will change this page. On **Jul 6 2026** BitTorrent announced a long-term quarterly buyback funded by the whole revenue of its decentralised services, including BitTorrent Speed and BTTInferGrid, the AI-inference GPU network launched on **Jun 17 2026**. Repurchased BTT is to be sent to a burn address, with the first burn and the first report — amount, share of supply, transaction hash — published in **mid-Oct 2026**. No size, no rate, no revenue figure and no burn address have been disclosed, and the on-chain check agrees that nothing has run: TRON's black-hole address holds **7,905,128,743 BTT** in total and has received none since **Dec 5 2025**. One announcement with no quantum is not a number, so the row ships at **0** and the event rides the upcoming strip instead.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**: BitTorrent Chain fees pay validators, and the BTT contract has no burn function, so the supply figure itself can never fall. That last point creates the trap on this coin. On **Jul 7 2026** a bridge transaction sent **34.83 trillion BTT** to a dead address on Ethereum, which a headline could easily read as a three-percent burn. It was not one. Those coins were bridge inventory of a mirrored BTT that the bridge can mint again on demand, so nothing redeemable left the float, and the row stays at zero. Buy #3, foundation buying, is **0** with no public evidence of purchases in the window. Buy #4, new long-term locks, is also **0**: one exchange moved **6 trillion BTT** of its own inventory into validator staking on **Jul 1 2026**, but the same wallet pulled **2.5 trillion** back out in **Apr 2026**, so this is reversible inventory rotation rather than a lock.

## Foundation and overhang

BitTorrent has one identified overhang and it is a classification residual rather than a named treasury: **2.96 trillion BTT**, **0.30%** of the 990 trillion cap, counted as non-circulating with no published release schedule and no project-disclosed wallet. It is watched on a two-week walk. The second item is the buyback destination itself: the programme announced on **Jul 6 2026** says burn rather than accumulate, so no wallet overhang exists today, but the mid-October report could name a holding address instead of a dead one, and that would create one. Everything else in the BTT holder list was checked and excluded — the largest wallets are exchange custodial accounts holding depositor coins, and the validator stake contract holds user deposits, not team supply. If either overhang's balance falls between refreshes, that outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How BTT compares to other fixed-supply TRON tokens

Structurally, BTT belongs with the capped, fully-distributed utility tokens rather than with the emission-driven chains. An uncapped proof-of-stake network issues new units every block and has to out-burn its own validators to stay flat; BitTorrent has no issuance to out-burn, because the BTT contract cannot mint. That puts BTT closer to a fixed-supply memecoin than to its own host chain: TRON itself runs a fee burn and a live issuance schedule, so TRX supply moves in both directions every day, while BTT's 990 trillion is frozen in both directions at once.

The comparison that matters most is with exchange tokens that run quarterly buybacks. Those programmes reduce supply because the token contract exposes a burn function, so a buyback permanently lowers the reported total. BTT cannot do that. Any BitTorrent burn can only park coins at an address nobody holds the key to, which removes them from the tradable float while leaving the 990 trillion supply line exactly where it is. For a reader that distinction is the whole story: on BTT the buyback is measured at the dead address, never at the supply figure.

BTT also carries the multi-chain confusion those comparisons usually lack. Mirrored BTT exists on Ethereum at **36.54 trillion** and on BNB Chain at **36.72 trillion**, and BitTorrent Chain's own native contract reports a hardcoded **10,000 trillion** that is larger than the cap itself. None of those are additional supply. BitTorrent Chain's bridge documentation states plainly that tokens leaving the root chain are locked and mapped copies are minted, so total circulation is unaffected; both mirror legs read exactly the same figure at the start and the end of this window, which is the constant offset a mirror leaves behind. Adding them up would put BTT at 1,063 trillion against a 990 trillion cap.

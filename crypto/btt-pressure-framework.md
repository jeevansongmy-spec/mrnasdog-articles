# BTT Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

BTT is a fixed **990T** TRON token with no way to mint more, and **99.7%** of it is already circulating. The one new force is a revenue-funded buyback and burn whose first burn is due in mid-October 2026 — its size has not been published.

## Sell pressure: where new BTT comes from

Protocol inflation is **0 BTT**, and it cannot be anything else. We read the BTT token contract on TRON directly: it answers only the eleven standard token functions — balances, transfers, approvals, name, symbol, decimals and total supply — and has no mint function, no owner and no upgrade path. Total supply is fixed at **990,000,000,000,000 BTT**. BitTorrent Chain does pay staking rewards, but they come out of a staking pool of BTT that already exists, about **23.38T BTT**, and that pool is already counted as circulating. Paying rewards out of it moves coins inside the market; it creates none.

Vesting unlocks are **0 BTT** for the last 90 days and **0 BTT** for the next 90. The BTT allocation was set in 2019: public sale 6%, private sale 2%, seed sale 9%, team 19%, TRON Foundation 20% and the TRON airdrop 10.1% have all been fully released — the monthly airdrop to TRX holders ended on Feb 11 2025. Three pools are still releasing: the BitTorrent user airdrop (10.0%), the BitTorrent ecosystem fund (19.9%) and partnerships (4.0%). They release once a year, in January, in ever smaller steps until January 2029. About **2.96T BTT** (0.30% of supply) is still held back. The next step, about **0.99T BTT** or 0.10% of supply, is due in January 2027 — just after the next 90-day window closes on Dec 28 2026.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are **0 BTT**. Large team-linked wallets did move this window — one founder-linked wallet sent **212B BTT** toward an exchange on Aug 19 2026, and a team wallet received **8.90T BTT** swept out of the old bridge on Jul 7 2026 — but every one of those coins was already counted as circulating, so none of it is new supply. Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is also **0 BTT**: there is no estate and no trustee, and the US case against the founder and the BitTorrent Foundation was settled in March 2026 with a cash fine, not coins.

## Buy pressure: where new BTT goes

The programmatic buyback is new. On Jul 6 2026 BitTorrent said it will spend **100%** of the revenue from its decentralized services — BTFS storage, faster downloads and the new BTTInferGrid AI compute network — to buy BTT on the market every quarter, and send the bought BTT to a burn address. The first batch was bought during the third quarter of 2026 and is due to be burned in mid-October 2026, with the amount, the share of supply and the transaction published. Until that burn happens, bought coins sit in a wallet that is part of the market, so the last 90 days book **0 BTT**. For the next 90 days we also book **0 BTT**, because BitTorrent has not published its revenue, a dollar figure or the buying wallet — we do not guess a size. When the October burn is shown on-chain, it enters this row at its real size.

The protocol fee burn is **0 BTT**. BTT has no fee burn: fees on BitTorrent Chain are paid to validators, and the token contract cannot destroy coins. The main burn address on TRON received nothing in the last 90 days; it holds about **7.9B BTT** from older burns. Foundation buying is **0 BTT** — no purchase outside the quarterly buyback was announced or seen. New long-term locks are **0 BTT**: about **2.66T BTT** more went into BitTorrent Chain staking, but staked BTT stays in the circulating count.

## Foundation and overhang

Four overhangs are tracked. First, the **2.96T BTT** not yet counted as circulating — the rest of the user airdrop, ecosystem and partnership pools — released once a year in January and checked at every rebuild. Second, a founder-linked wallet holding about **112.60T BTT**, which did not move in the window. Third, a second founder-linked wallet holding about **52.46T BTT**, which sold into an exchange route once in the window (212B on Aug 19 2026). Fourth, the team wallet holding the **8.90T BTT** swept from the bridge when BitTorrent closed it in June and July 2026, unmoved since. The wallet that holds each quarter’s buyback before the burn has not been named, so its size is unknown. The three wallets and the staking pool are all read on-chain every day; the unreleased pools are checked by hand every two weeks. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the foundation and unscheduled row at the next refresh — for the wallets already counted as circulating, it is recorded as a move inside the market.

## How BTT compares to other fixed-supply tokens

BTT sits in the same group as fixed-supply tokens that finished most of their unlocks years ago. Like a hard-capped chain after its last halving, BTT can never add a coin above its cap; unlike Bitcoin, it has no mining reward still paying out, so its sell side is not slowly shrinking — it is already at zero. That makes BTT flatter than any chain that pays validators in new coins: Ethereum, Solana or Cardano all add supply every day, while BTT adds none.

The difference from a clean fixed-supply chain is custody. A large share of BTT sits in a handful of founder-linked and team wallets, all counted as circulating. Their sales do not change the supply count, but they do change how many coins are actually for sale. That is closer to an exchange token or a foundation-heavy token than to a widely held proof-of-work coin.

On the buy side, BTT now joins the tokens that turn revenue into burns, like exchange tokens with quarterly burns. The difference is scale and proof: those programmes publish burn sizes tied to known revenue, while BTT’s first burn has no published size yet. Until the October report, BTT is a token with a burn promise, not a measured burn.

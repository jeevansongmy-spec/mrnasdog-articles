Today I sold **half of my LINK**, the coin of Chainlink. In my **Core altcoins** portfolio it went from **106.55** to **53.3 LINK**, and I moved that money into **Bitcoin**. LINK is about **$14.4** as I write. The reason is Chainlink's next token release, and Bitcoin.

Our LINK page, with the supply and the next releases: [mrnasdog.com/research/link](https://mrnasdog.com/research/link)

## A big LINK release is due in October

LINK has a fixed supply of 1 billion coins. About **748M** are in the market. The Chainlink team keeps the rest, about **252M LINK**, in 33 wallets, and releases about **7% of all LINK a year** in batches, roughly four times a year.

The autumn batch is the big one: **18.75M LINK**, about **$270M** at today's price. It came on Sep 15 in 2023, Sep 20 in 2024 and Oct 10 in 2025. This year it hasn't come yet, so it's probably due any day now. Then another **11.25M** usually comes around Dec 18. Together that's about **4% more LINK** in 90 days. Here is how it shows on our LINK page today:

![Our LINK page: autumn reserve release +18.75M LINK due now, December reserve release +11.25M LINK on Dec 18 2026](https://mrnasdog.com/analysis/link/upcoming-releases.webp)

*Figure 1. The next 90 days on our LINK page, Sep 30 2026.*

Where do these coins go? Most of each batch goes straight to an exchange. Last year's autumn batch went there **all at once, the same day**. These coins cost the team nothing, and coins sent to an exchange are most likely there to be sold.

| Autumn release | LINK | Where it went |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 15 2023 | 18.75M | 15.70M to an exchange, 3.05M to a team wallet |
| Sep 20 2024 | 18.75M | 18.125M to an exchange, 0.625M to a team wallet |
| Oct 10 2025 | 18.75M | All 18.75M to an exchange, the same day |
| Oct 2026 | 18.75M | Due now, not released yet |

*Figure 2. The autumn batch, from the team's wallets. Checked on the blockchain, Sep 29 2026.*

## What happened to LINK after last year's release?

The day before last year's release, LINK was **$21.99**. One week later it was down **24.5%**. After 90 days it was down **40%**. It kept falling for months and hit its lowest close, **$7.20**, on Jun 30 2026. That's **67% below** where it started.

![LINK price over one year, from about $22 in early October 2025 down to about $7 in July 2026, and back to $14.4 in September 2026](https://mrnasdog.com/analysis/link/price-1y-cmc.webp)

*Figure 3. LINK price, the last 12 months. Source: CoinMarketCap, Sep 30 2026. The drop on the left is Oct 10 2025.*

## Is it the release, or Bitcoin?

To be fair, the release was not the only reason for that fall. Oct 10 2025 was also a crash day for the whole market, and Bitcoin fell too. So I checked the same autumn release in the two years before:

| Autumn release | LINK, 90 days after | Bitcoin, 90 days after |
|---|---|---|
| Sep 15 2023 | +150% | +62% |
| Sep 20 2024 | +104% | +55% |
| Oct 10 2025 | −40% | −25% |

*Figure 4. Price change 90 days after each release, from the day before. Daily closing prices.*

In 2023 and 2024, LINK went **up** after the same release, by **150%** and **104%**, because Bitcoin was going up. In 2025, Bitcoin went down, and LINK fell about **twice as hard**.

So my read is roughly this: the release alone probably doesn't decide the price. Bitcoin does. If Bitcoin keeps rising, buyers can likely take 18.75M LINK, like in 2023 and 2024. If Bitcoin turns down, the team's free coins land on a weak market, and LINK could fall much harder, like last year. That's my worry, and it's why I moved the money into Bitcoin: last year, Bitcoin fell less.

## What could prove me wrong

If Bitcoin keeps going up, LINK could rise with it, like in 2023 and 2024, and I'd miss part of that move. Chainlink also buys LINK back every week and keeps it in its Reserve, which is a good sign for the long run. That's why I sold only half, not all of it.

## MrNasdog's Thought

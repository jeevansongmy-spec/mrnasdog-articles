# SEI Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

SEI supply is growing, and the next 90 days look the same. Two things add new SEI to the market: a fixed minting plan that pays stakers, which created **41.09M SEI** between Aug 8 and Nov 5 2026, and monthly team and private-sale unlocks, which released **293.33M SEI**. Nothing takes SEI back, so the Sei network's float grows a net **+4.97%** in 90 days and about **+4.96%** next, against **−0.03%** on other trackers whose circulating figure has not moved since April 2026.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 5 2026, SEI added **334.42M SEI** to a float of **6.73B SEI** and removed none, a net change of **+4.97%**. The next 90 days carry **334.13M SEI** of scheduled new supply, or **+4.96%**. The inflation monitor reads **−0.03%**, a gap of **5.00 points**, so the SEI coin page carries a warning note. The monitor's circulating figure has stayed at 6,733,333,333 SEI since at least Apr 21 2026, while the Sei chain kept minting staking pay and the team vesting contracts kept paying out every month. The gap is a stalled count, not a quiet chain, and our +4.97% stands. In one line: SEI is inflationary by schedule, with monthly unlocks doing most of the work.

## Sell pressure: where new SEI comes from

Protocol inflation is the first source. Sei's mint module follows a fixed ten-year plan that pays a set amount of new SEI to stakers each year, and the yearly amount stepped down from 180M to 165M SEI on Aug 15 2026. Across the window that plan created **41.09M SEI**. At the new rate of about 453,300 SEI a day, the next 90 days bring **40.80M SEI**. The plan ends in August 2033, with 757.38M SEI still to be created, and steps down again to 135M SEI a year on Aug 15 2027.

Vesting unlocks are the larger source. On the 15th of every month, two team vesting contracts on the Sei chain pay out 22.22M and 20M SEI, and private-sale investors receive 55.56M SEI. Those three payments came on Aug 15, Sep 15 and Oct 15 2026, for **293.33M SEI** in the window, and the same **97.78M SEI** is due on Nov 15 2026, Dec 15 2026 and Jan 15 2027. The team contracts still hold **902.86M SEI**, and private-sale investors are still owed **555.56M SEI**, which finishes on Aug 15 2027.

The Foundation and unscheduled unlock row is zero. About **1.05B SEI** sits outside the circulating count in Foundation and ecosystem-reserve wallets that are not publicly named, but no release date or size has been announced. The long-term locked or bankruptcy row is also zero: no court estate holds SEI waiting to be paid out.

## Buy pressure: where new SEI goes

Every buy row on the SEI ledger is zero. There is no programmatic buyback: nothing buys SEI back on the network's behalf, and no buyback has been proposed in Sei governance. There is no protocol fee burn either. Sei fees go to validators and stakers, and fewer than 1,100 SEI have been destroyed since launch in 2023. From Nov 4 to Nov 5 2026, supply rose by exactly the amount minted, to the last unit.

The Foundation buy row is zero because the Sei Foundation does not buy SEI back. Spot funds, the staked SEI fund filings and companies that hold SEI buy coins that already circulate, so they remove nothing from the count. The new long-term lock row is zero too: about **4.10B SEI** is staked, but staked SEI still counts as circulating. The team contracts stake their own locked coins, which were never counted, so that stake changes nothing either.

## Foundation and overhang

Three team-controlled piles of SEI sit outside the float. The first team vesting contract holds **222.86M SEI**, paid out at about 22.22M SEI a month until Aug 15 2027. The second holds **680M SEI**, paid out at 20M SEI a month until Aug 15 2029. Both are read on the Sei chain at every rebuild, and both send their payments to one team payout wallet, which held about 7.58M SEI on Nov 5 2026. The private-sale remainder of **555.56M SEI** is held in custody off chain and follows its published monthly schedule.

The fourth overhang is the largest unknown: about **1.05B SEI** of Foundation and ecosystem-reserve coins in wallets that are not publicly labelled. It has no schedule, so it books zero today. If the balance of any of these SEI overhangs falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlock row at the next refresh.

## How SEI compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

SEI sits in the group of young proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains whose supply is driven by monthly unlocks rather than by staking pay. Its staking inflation is small and falling: the Sei mint plan pays a fixed number of SEI per year that steps down every August and stops in 2033, against a planned total of 10B SEI. That is closer to a capped, shrinking schedule than to chains such as Ethereum or Cosmos Hub, whose validator pay has no end date and rises or falls with the amount staked.

Where SEI differs from Ethereum is the vesting side. Ethereum has no locked tranches left, and its fee burn takes back part of what it issues. SEI has no burn at all, and about 97.78M SEI of team and investor coins reaches the market every month until August 2027. In that respect SEI looks like other venture-funded Layer 1 chains with large insider allocations, where a monthly cliff calendar sets most of the new supply and staking pay is the smaller part.

The schedule does get lighter. After the private-sale tranche ends on Aug 15 2027 and the first team contract empties the same month, the monthly unlock drops from 97.78M SEI to 20M SEI. From that point the Sei mint plan and the second team contract, which ends in August 2029, are the only scheduled sources.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 15 2026:** the next monthly unlock of 97.78M SEI, made up of 42.22M SEI from the two team contracts and 55.56M SEI to private-sale investors.

**Dec 15 2026 and Jan 15 2027:** the same 97.78M SEI unlock again on each date, which brings the 90-day total to about 293.33M SEI.

**The Autobahn upgrade:** the on-chain text vote on SIP-6, a new block-building design for Sei, closes on Nov 5 2026 with about 2.38B SEI voting yes and none voting no so far. It changes speed, not supply, so it books zero.

**Staked SEI fund filings:** fund managers have filed for spot funds that would hold and stake SEI. Their buying would take coins that already circulate, so it would not change the supply count.

**The Foundation and ecosystem-reserve wallets:** about 1.05B SEI with no published schedule. Any announced grant program or treasury sale from this pile would add to the sell side.

## Summary

SEI supply grew **+4.97%** in the 90 days to Nov 5 2026 and is set to grow about **+4.96%** in the next 90 days. Most of that comes from monthly team and private-sale unlocks of 97.78M SEI, and the rest from the Sei mint plan, which now pays stakers about 453,300 SEI a day. SEI has no fee burn and no buyback, so nothing offsets the new supply. The main risk is the calendar itself, plus about 1.05B SEI of Foundation and reserve coins with no schedule; the relief comes after Aug 15 2027, when the monthly unlock falls to 20M SEI.

# SEI Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Our net reading for SEI is **+4.97%** over the last 90 days (Jul 29 to Oct 27 2026) and **+4.96%** for the next 90, measured against **6.73B SEI** of circulating supply. Other supply trackers read **−0.01%**, a gap of **4.98 points**, so the SEI coin page carries a warning note. The reason is simple: the circulating figure those trackers use has stayed at exactly 6,733,333,333 SEI every day since at least Jul 1 2026, while the Sei chain kept minting and the vesting schedules kept paying out. SEI is a coin with **supply growing on a fixed monthly calendar**, with nothing on the other side to take coins back.

## Sell pressure: where new SEI comes from

The largest source is vesting unlocks: **293.33M SEI** in 90 days. Every month on the 15th, Sei's two on-chain team vesting contracts release 22.22M and 20M SEI, and the private-sale investors receive 55.56M SEI, so each monthly SEI unlock adds about **97.78M SEI**. Three of these unlocks fell inside the window, on Aug 15, Sep 15 and Oct 15 2026, and we saw the team contracts pay out on Sep 16 and Oct 15 2026. Three more are due on Nov 15 2026, Dec 15 2026 and Jan 15 2027, so the vesting number for the next 90 days is the same. The investor unlocks end on Aug 15 2027, the larger team contract ends on Aug 15 2027, and the second team contract keeps paying 20M SEI a month until Aug 15 2029.

The second source is protocol inflation: **41.46M SEI** minted for stakers in the window. The Sei mint follows a fixed ten-year plan of 1.5B SEI that runs from Aug 15 2023 to Aug 14 2033 and pays out once a day. The plan year that began on Aug 15 2026 releases 165M SEI, down from 180M SEI the year before, so the Sei mint now pays about 453,297 SEI a day and the next 90 days should bring about **40.80M SEI**. The plan steps down again to 135M SEI on Aug 15 2027.

The Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row is zero: we found no public evidence of a Sei Foundation or ecosystem-reserve release in the window. The long-term locked or bankruptcy row is also zero, because no estate, court or exchange collapse holds SEI waiting to be released.

## Buy pressure: where new SEI goes

Nothing on the buy side removed SEI in the last 90 days. There is no programmatic buyback: no contract, treasury or governance plan buys SEI back, and none of the Sei governance votes in the window touched supply. There is no fee burn either: Sei transaction fees go to validators and stakers, and between Sep 25 and Oct 27 2026 total SEI supply rose by exactly the **14.51M SEI** the chain minted, to the last coin.

The Foundation buy row is zero because the Sei Foundation has announced no market buying. The staked SEI funds filed in the US would buy SEI that already trades, so they would not shrink the count. The new long-term lock row is zero as well: about **4.08B SEI** is staked, but staked SEI still counts as circulating, and the team vesting contracts stake their own locked coins while they wait to release them.

## Foundation and overhang

Three pools of team-controlled SEI sit outside the circulating count. The two team vesting contracts hold about **903M SEI** between them, about 223M SEI in the contract that finishes on Aug 15 2027 and about 680M SEI in the one that finishes on Aug 15 2029; both pay on a fixed calendar, and that calendar is already counted in the vesting row. The private-sale investors still have about **556M SEI** to receive, in ten monthly unlocks from Nov 15 2026 to Aug 15 2027.

The third pool is the open question: roughly **1.05B SEI** held by the Sei Foundation and the ecosystem reserve, for grants, incentives and airdrops, with no published release plan. The 761M SEI that the mint plan has still to create are protocol rewards, not a team wallet, and arrive on the schedule above. We read the two team contracts from the chain at every rebuild and walk the Foundation side by hand. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next refresh.

## How SEI compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

SEI belongs to the group of young proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains that launched with a large share of supply locked for the team and early investors and release it month by month. In that group the monthly unlock, not staking pay, is the main source of new coins, and SEI fits the pattern: about seven of every eight new SEI in the window came from vesting, and only one from the staking mint.

SEI differs from chains such as Ethereum, where new coins come only from staking pay and part of every fee is burned. Sei has no burn at all, so heavy use of the network does not slow SEI supply growth. SEI also differs from chains whose staking pay is a rate on the stake: the Sei mint is a fixed number of coins per day set years in advance, so it falls over time no matter how much SEI is staked.

The hard cap of 10B SEI is the limit on all of this. Once the investor unlocks end on Aug 15 2027, SEI supply growth should slow sharply, leaving the second team contract, the falling staking mint and any Foundation releases.

**Sei Foundation and ecosystem reserve:** any grant, incentive or airdrop paid from the roughly 1.05B SEI reserve would add to supply at the next refresh.

SEI supply is growing about **4.97%** every 90 days, and the next 90 days look the same. The Sei network adds about 97.78M SEI on the 15th of every month from team and investor unlocks, plus about 453,297 newly minted SEI a day for stakers, and nothing is burned or bought back. Other trackers show almost no change because their circulating figure for SEI has not moved since at least Jul 1 2026. The biggest risk is the steady monthly unlock until Aug 15 2027, and the ceiling is the hard cap of 10B SEI.

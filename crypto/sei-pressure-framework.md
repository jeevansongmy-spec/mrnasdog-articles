# SEI Inflation Analysis · August 2026 · Supply growing, projected to keep growing

Over the last 90 days Sei minted **43.9M SEI** under a fixed release calendar and released a further **266.7M SEI** through three monthly allocation cliffs, against buy pressure of exactly **zero** — Sei runs no buyback and no burn. The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads SEI at **+4.61%** net for the window and **+4.57%** forward; our independent supply monitor reads **+0.29%**, a gap of **4.32 percentage points** that traces to a float figure the monitor has been reading unchanged since **May 1 2026**. SEI is a **hard-capped chain in the middle of its vesting, with no offsetting mechanism of any kind**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 30 2026**, the Pressure Framework reads Sei at **+4.61%** net new supply — total sell pressure of **310.6M SEI** against buy pressure of **0 SEI**, divided by a circulating supply of **6,733.3M SEI**. Our independent supply monitor reads **+0.29%** for the same window, a gap of **4.32 percentage points**, which is far outside tolerance and raises a data-conflict flag on the SEI overview. The gap is not a disagreement about Sei; it is a disagreement about the float. The aggregated circulating figure the monitor divides by has held at **6,733,333,333 SEI** across every day-by-day reading from **May 1 2026** to **Aug 29 2026**, while the Sei chain itself minted a new instalment every single one of those days. The framework reads the chain; the monitor reads a number that has stopped moving. Forward the framework reads **+4.57%**, barely softer, because the only thing that changed is the mint. SEI in August 2026 is a **capped Layer-1 whose scheduled dilution is still running at roughly four and a half percent a quarter, with nothing on the other side of the ledger**.

## Sell pressure: where new SEI comes from

Sei has two supply taps and both are open. The first is the protocol mint. Sei is capped at **10 billion SEI**, and the chain reached **9,211.8M SEI** of that this session — the remainder is released by a token release schedule written into the mint module, not by an inflation rate. The schedule is a list of dated annual tranches, and it pays out one instalment per calendar day. The tranche covering **Aug 15 2025** to **Aug 14 2026** was **180M SEI**, or **494,505 SEI** a day; the tranche that opened on **Aug 15 2026** is **165M SEI**, or **453,296 SEI** a day. That step happened inside the window, so Sell #1 for the window is a blend of 75 days at the old rate and 15 days at the new one — **43.9M SEI** — while the forward column is re-based to the new rate alone at **40.8M SEI**. The mint module confirms itself: it reports **158,200,549 SEI** still to pay out of the 165M tranche, which is exactly 15 days of the new instalment already spent.

The second and much larger tap is Sell #2, the vesting unlocks. Sei's genesis allocation buckets — the team's 2 billion SEI and the early backers' 2 billion SEI — began releasing after a one-year cliff and pay out on the 15th of every month. The early-backer tranche runs 36 months and exhausts around **Aug 2027**; the team tranche runs 60 months and exhausts around **Aug 2029**. Together they release **88.9M SEI** a month to holders, and three of those cliffs fell inside the window — **Jun 15**, **Jul 15** and **Aug 15 2026** — for **266.7M SEI**. That single row is more than six times the size of the mint, and it is the whole story of SEI supply. Sei's genesis created no on-chain vesting accounts, so there is no escrow contract to read the realised flow from; the published allocation calendar governs, and it is corroborated by the two independent vesting durations and by an unlock tracker whose quoted monthly total decomposes to the same per-bucket arithmetic to the single token.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0** — no release from any identified project wallet was observed in the window, and a walk of the twenty largest holders on the Sei chain this session returned only staking module pools and exchange custodial wallets, with no address labelled as a project treasury. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0**: Sei has no bankruptcy estate, no trustee schedule, and no locked tranche outside the monthly allocation calendar already counted in Sell #2.

## Buy pressure: where new SEI goes

Nowhere. Every one of the four buy rows on the SEI ledger is **0**, and that is the most important structural fact about this token. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0** because Sei operates no buyback contract and has announced no repurchase programme — nothing routes network revenue back into the open market. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0**, and we checked it on both surfaces rather than one. The unspendable addresses on the Sei EVM layer hold **187 SEI** and **1,384 SEI** respectively — about eight dollars of movement, not a mechanism. And total supply on the chain sits within roughly **1,000 SEI** of what the release calendar alone predicts after three years, on a base of 9.2 billion, so nothing has been destroyed by a supply-reducing call either. Gas on Sei is paid out to stakers, not burned.

Buy #3, foundation buy, is **0** — no project entity has disclosed an open-market purchase of SEI, and there is no treasury programme that would generate one. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0** as well. Roughly **4.18B SEI** is bonded to validators, which is a large share of the float, but staking on a Cosmos chain is a 21-day withdrawal delay rather than a lock: the coins remain the owner's to move on the owner's timetable, and a standing staked balance is capacity, not a supply event. No new lockup contract was announced inside the window.

## Foundation and overhang

Sei's team-controlled overhang is large and it is enumerated by size rather than by address, because no Sei wallet is publicly labelled as a project treasury. Three items are tracked. First, the unreleased team tranche — roughly **1.2B SEI** still to release on the monthly calendar through **Aug 2029**. Second, the unreleased early-backer tranche — roughly **0.6B SEI** running through **Aug 2027**. Third, the ecosystem reserve, which is not static: it takes in a further **22.6M SEI** every month on the same monthly calendar, on top of the 88.9M that goes to the team and early backers. That reserve leg is deliberately not booked as sell pressure, because it is released into a project-controlled pool rather than to holders, and no outflow from it has been observed. Holding tokens is not the same as selling them. All three are re-read on every rebuild, and the trigger is simple: if any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

The scale of what remains is worth stating plainly. The Sei chain holds **9,211.8M SEI** against a circulating figure of **6,733.3M SEI**, and the cap is **10,000M SEI**. Roughly **2.48B SEI** — more than a third of the current float — exists on the chain but has not yet reached the market, and a further **788M SEI** has not been minted at all. The monthly calendar is what converts the first number into float, and it has three more years to run.

## How SEI compares to other capped Layer-1 chains

The useful comparison for Sei is not to uncapped continuous-emission Layer-1s but to the 2023 cohort of hard-capped, venture-backed chains that launched with a large locked allocation and a multi-year unlock calendar. Against a halving-model chain with a fixed cap, Sei looks very different: a halving chain's dilution is a single, decaying, fully public curve, and once the early years pass the schedule works in the holder's favour with no counterparty deciding anything. Sei has two curves running at once, and the larger of the two — the allocation calendar at **266.7M SEI** a quarter — is not a protocol emission at all. It is a contractual release to identified parties, which means its market impact depends on what those parties do, not on what the chain does.

Against an exchange token with a quarterly buyback, or an EIP-1559-style chain with a base-fee burn, the contrast is starker still, and it is the contrast that decides SEI's Inflation score. Those designs have a mechanism that converts network usage into supply removal, so activity and dilution pull against each other. Sei has no such mechanism at all: gas is redistributed to stakers rather than destroyed, and there is no buyback trust, no burn auction and no treasury purchase programme. However much the Sei EVM is used, none of that usage reduces SEI supply. The chain's headline strengths — sub-second finality, parallel execution, a single ledger shared between the Cosmos and EVM layers — are real engineering, but the Pressure Framework measures only supply, and on supply Sei is a one-directional ledger until **Aug 2027** at the earliest.

The one genuinely favourable structural feature Sei shares with the hard-cap cohort is the cap itself. The mint is not a rate that compounds on a growing base — it is a fixed list of dated tranches totalling **1.5B SEI** that steps down every August and ends. It has already fallen from **300M** a year at launch to **165M** now, and it drops to **135M** in **Aug 2027**. On a long enough horizon Sei's supply does converge. The problem in August 2026 is that the allocation calendar, not the mint, is doing the diluting.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The monthly allocation cliff on **Sep 15 2026** releases another **88.9M SEI** to the team and early-backer buckets, and the same event repeats on **Oct 15 2026** and **Nov 15 2026** — three firings inside the forward window, which is the entire 266.7M of Sell #2. Watch the ecosystem reserve alongside them: it accrues a further 22.6M a month and is not currently booked as sell pressure, so the first observed outflow from it would move a row that is presently zero. Watch for any governance proposal that touches the mint module's release schedule — the on-chain proposals passed inside this window were IBC parameters, a software upgrade and a consensus-timeout change, none of which affect supply, but the schedule lives in a module parameter that a vote could in principle amend. Watch for a first announcement of a fee burn or buyback, which would be the single largest possible change to this page and currently does not exist in any form. And note the next scheduled step in the mint itself is not until **Aug 15 2027**, when the annual tranche falls from 165M to 135M — nothing in the mint changes before then.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads SEI at **+4.61%** net new supply over the 90 days to **Aug 30 2026** and **+4.57%** forward, driven overwhelmingly by monthly allocation cliffs releasing **266.7M SEI** a quarter to the team and early backers rather than by the protocol mint's **43.9M**. The structural mechanism is a hard cap of **10 billion SEI** served by a dated token release schedule that steps down every August, sitting alongside a contractual vesting calendar that runs to **Aug 2029**. The key risk is that Sei has no offsetting mechanism whatsoever — no buyback, no fee burn, no project purchase — so network usage cannot reduce supply and the ledger is one-directional until the vesting calendar exhausts. The ceiling is real and the mint does end, but with roughly **2.48B SEI** already on the chain and not yet in the float and a further **788M** still to mint, the dilution that matters runs for another three years.

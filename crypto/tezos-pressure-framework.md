# XTZ Inflation Analysis · September 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

Tezos is a steadily inflationary chain. Over the last 90 days the Tezos protocol created **8.09M XTZ** as staking rewards, while storage burns, a trading-fee burn and lost rollup bonds destroyed **64,323 XTZ**, so XTZ supply grew **+0.73%** and is on track for about **+0.74%** in the next 90 days. There is no supply cap: the yearly issuance rate is set by adaptive issuance from the share of XTZ that is staked, and it stood at **3.03%** on Sep 29 2026.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Across the 90 days from Jul 1 2026 to Sep 29 2026, XTZ added **8.03M** coins to a circulating supply of **1.10B XTZ**, a net change of **+0.73%**. Our monitor, which reads circulating supply from market data, measured **+0.75%** over the same stretch, a gap of only **0.02 percentage points**, well inside the half-point tolerance, so no conflict flag is shown. The next 90 days project to **+0.74%**. Tezos is a chain whose supply grows at a slowing, stake-driven pace, with burns too small to change the direction.

## Sell pressure: where new XTZ comes from

Protocol inflation is the whole story. Tezos pays bakers and stakers in newly created XTZ for proposing blocks, attesting to them, confirming data on the data-availability layer and revealing randomness. Read on-chain at both ends of the window, these rewards came to **8,090,216 XTZ**, about 89,900 XTZ a day. The chain's own reward schedule allowed a maximum of about 8.33M XTZ; the gap is rewards that were never minted because some attestations and data checks were missed.

The rate is falling. Under adaptive issuance, the Tezos issuance rate drops as the staked share rises, and staked XTZ grew from 321.8M to 335.9M during the window. The yearly rate slid from **3.25%** to **3.03%**, and the reward per block fell from 6.86 XTZ to 6.30 XTZ. The Ushuaia protocol upgrade went live on Jun 30 2026, one day before the window, and changed no reward rule, so the forward figure keeps the trailing 8.09M; at today's rate it would be nearer 7.8M.

Vesting unlocks are zero. At launch on Jun 30 2018 the Tezos Foundation and the founding company each received about 76.3M XTZ that paid out monthly over four years; that schedule ended in 2022. The one unscheduled unlock is old fundraiser money: about **19.98M XTZ** from the 2017 sale has never been claimed, and it is the only XTZ not counted as circulating. One claim of **4,588 XTZ** came in on Jul 28 2026. With 11 claims worth 82,913 XTZ over the last 12 months, the next 90 days carry about **20.7K XTZ**. There is no bankruptcy estate or long-term lock releasing XTZ.

## Buy pressure: where new XTZ goes

Tezos has no buyback and no fee burn in the usual sense: transaction fees are paid to bakers. What it does have are two small protocol burns. First, every new byte of contract storage and every new account is paid for in XTZ that is destroyed: **22,011 XTZ** this window, about 245 a day. Second, the liquidity-baking exchange built into the protocol sends 0.1% of every trade to an address nobody controls: **12,313 XTZ**. Together the protocol fee burn removed **34,323 XTZ**, about one XTZ for every 236 created.

A third, one-off removal came from rollups. Operators of rollups on Tezos lock a 10,000 XTZ bond, and six bonds were lost in disputes between Aug 15 and Aug 18 2026. Half of each lost bond went to the winner and half was destroyed, taking **30,000 XTZ** out of supply. These losses are rare and unscheduled, so the forward window carries none.

There is no Foundation buy and no new long-term lock that leaves the float. Staking on Tezos is real and slashable, and 14.1M more XTZ was staked in the window, but staked XTZ still counts as circulating. A larger stake removes nothing directly; its effect is to lower the reward rate.

## Foundation and overhang

The Tezos Foundation is the one identified team holder. Its fourteen labelled baker and delegator wallets hold about **83.4M XTZ** and sent nothing out during the window; its own report valued its XTZ at about $76M on Dec 31 2025, roughly 146M XTZ at that day's price, which also counts loans and exchange liquidity. All of those coins are already inside the circulating count, so a Foundation sale would move XTZ within the market rather than add new supply. The second overhang is the 19.98M XTZ of unclaimed fundraiser money, which anyone holding the old keys can claim at any time. We read both on-chain at every rebuild. If either balance falls between checks, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next refresh.

## How XTZ compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1s

Among proof-of-stake chains, Tezos sits in the uncapped, reward-funded group. Like Ethereum, it pays validators in new coins and has no maximum supply. The difference is the brake: Ethereum burns part of every transaction fee, a burn that can matter when the chain is busy, while Tezos gives fees to bakers and only burns storage payments and a small trading slice, which together removed about 0.4% of what was created this window.

The closer cousin is a chain like Cosmos Hub, where the issuance rate also responds to how much of the supply is staked. Tezos uses the same idea in a smoother form: a rate that falls as the staked share rises toward a 50% target, plus a small term that drifts over time. Cardano, by contrast, pays rewards from a fixed reserve that drains toward a cap, so its new supply shrinks by schedule rather than by staking behaviour.

Against fixed-cap chains such as Bitcoin, the difference is structural. Tezos has no halving and no ceiling; its supply path depends on how much XTZ holders choose to stake. With the staked share near 30% and the target at 50%, there is still room for the rate to fall further if staking keeps growing.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The Tezos proposal period that opened on Sep 23 2026 closes on Oct 7 2026 with no protocol proposal injected yet; a next upgrade bringing liquid staking would likely raise the staked share and lower issuance. The staked share itself is the main dial: every step up cuts the reward rate. Fundraiser claims can arrive on any day and add to supply. Rollup disputes can destroy bonds again, as they did in mid-August. And the Foundation's next report should show whether its XTZ holding changed in the first half of 2026.

## Summary

XTZ supply grew **+0.73%** over the 90 days to Sep 29 2026 and is projected to grow about **+0.74%** over the next 90, in line with the monitor's **+0.75%**. Almost all new supply is the **8.09M XTZ** of staking rewards set by adaptive issuance, now running at about 3.03% a year and falling as more XTZ is staked. Burns removed only 64,323 XTZ, and the Tezos Foundation's 83.4M labelled XTZ is already in the float. Tezos has no supply cap, so the ceiling on dilution is the issuance rate itself.

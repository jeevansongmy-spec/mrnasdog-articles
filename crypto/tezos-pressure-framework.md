# XTZ Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the Tezos coin page for the short answer (should you buy XTZ?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/tezos](https://mrnasdog.com/research/tezos)

**XTZ** supply is growing, and the Pressure Framework expects it to keep growing at the same pace. Tezos has no supply cap: every block pays bakers and stakers in newly created XTZ, and the only things that take XTZ out are small burns. From Aug 9 to Nov 7 2026 the network created **7.95M XTZ**, old 2017 sale buyers claimed **39.00K XTZ**, and burns removed **68.35K XTZ**, for a net change of **+0.72%** in 90 days, in line with the **+0.71%** other trackers show.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 7 2026, XTZ supply grew **+0.72%**, and the next 90 days project **+0.72%** as well. Other supply trackers read **+0.71%** for the same window, a gap of **0.01 percentage points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. Almost all of the growth is staking pay: **7.95M XTZ** of new coins against **68.35K XTZ** burned, so the burns cancel less than one coin in a hundred. Tezos is an uncapped proof-of-stake chain that is inflationary by design, with a rate that slowly falls as more XTZ is staked.

## Sell pressure: where new XTZ comes from

The first and by far the largest source is protocol inflation. Tezos pays the bakers who make blocks, the bakers who sign them, and the stakers behind both, all in freshly created XTZ. From Aug 9 to Nov 7 2026 that pay came to **7.95M XTZ**, about **88,300 XTZ** a day. The Tezos rule, called adaptive issuance, lowers the pay when more XTZ is staked. Over these 90 days the stake grew from **320.5M** to **350.6M XTZ**, and the yearly rate slid from **3.29%** to **2.79%**. So the pay was about 95,100 XTZ a day in early August and about 83,900 XTZ a day by early November.

Vesting unlocks are **0**. The Tezos Foundation and founders' shares from the 2017 sale finished unlocking years ago, and no tracker lists a locked XTZ tranche waiting to open.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks come to **39.00K XTZ**, and none of it is Foundation money. About **19.94M XTZ** bought in the 2017 Tezos sale has still never been claimed by its buyers, and unclaimed XTZ does not count as circulating. Six buyers claimed theirs between Sep 30 and Nov 6 2026, the largest **24,900 XTZ** on Oct 22 2026. These claims have no schedule, but at least one has come in every quarter of the past year, about **30.19K XTZ** in a typical 90 days, which is what the next window carries.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is **0**. No court estate or bankruptcy pile of XTZ is waiting to be paid out, and no long lock is due to open before February 2027.

## Buy pressure: where new XTZ goes

A programmatic buyback is **0**. Nothing buys XTZ back on the network's behalf.

The protocol fee burn took **38.35K XTZ**. Tezos destroys XTZ when apps pay to store data on chain and when new accounts are created, which came to **28,285 XTZ** from Aug 9 to Nov 7 2026. The trading pool built into Tezos also burns a tiny cut of every trade by sending it to a dead address: **10,062 XTZ** in 7,732 sends. Together these burns cancel under **0.5%** of new staking pay.

A Foundation buy is **0**. The Tezos Foundation does not buy XTZ back, and funds or companies that buy XTZ take coins that already circulate.

A new long-term lock is **0**. Staked XTZ still counts as circulating, and leaving the stake takes only a few days, so the **350.6M XTZ** staked on Nov 6 2026 removes nothing from the count.

One extra row booked a one-off: lost rollup bonds, **30.00K XTZ**. Operators of rollups on Tezos post a 10,000 XTZ bond and lose it if they are caught posting a wrong result. Six bonds were lost between Aug 15 and Aug 18 2026; half of each went to the challenger and the other half was destroyed. Nothing like it is scheduled, so the next 90 days carry **0** here, and the burns shrink to **38.35K XTZ**.

## Foundation and overhang

The Tezos Foundation holds about **61.52M XTZ** in its 14 named wallets on chain, most of it in its four bakers. Two more wallets, funded with 15M XTZ each on Oct 7 2026, stake with Foundation bakers 1 and 2 and hold another **30.00M XTZ**, most likely Foundation money as well. The Foundation's latest report put its XTZ at USD 76 million on Dec 31 2025, about 146M XTZ at that day's price, including loans and exchange liquidity that do not sit in named wallets. All of this already counts as circulating, so a Foundation sale moves coins that already count and adds no new supply.

The one true overhang is the **19.94M XTZ** of unclaimed 2017 sale coins. It belongs to the original buyers, not to the team, and any of it can be claimed at any time. We read the Foundation wallets and the unclaimed pool on chain at every rebuild; if either falls between checks, the outflow enters the Foundation and unscheduled unlocks row at the next check.

## How XTZ compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

XTZ belongs with the uncapped proof-of-stake chains that pay stakers in new coins, like Ethereum, Cardano and Cosmos. Like Cosmos, Tezos ties its rate to how much is staked: when more XTZ is staked, the yearly rate falls, and the rule aims for about half of all XTZ staked. Ethereum also pays less per coin as the stake grows, but it adds a fee burn that can cancel a real share of new supply on busy days. On Tezos the burns are tied to storage and the built-in trading pool, and they cancel well under 1% of new pay.

Against capped chains such as Bitcoin, XTZ has no hard limit and no halving; its brake is the staking rule instead of a fixed calendar. Against chains with large team or investor unlocks, XTZ is simple: nothing vests, and the only coins entering the count from outside are slow claims from the 2017 sale. That leaves Tezos with a steady, low supply growth of about 0.7% a quarter, almost all of it paid to the people who stake.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The current Tezos proposal round runs from Nov 4 to Nov 18 2026, after three rounds since Sep 23 2026 with no proposal. A new upgrade, expected to bring XTZ liquid staking, would need several more voting rounds before it could go live, so it is unlikely to change pay before February 2027.

The share of XTZ staked keeps the yearly rate moving: it fell from 3.29% to 2.79% in the last 90 days. If the stake keeps climbing, the next 90 days will create fewer than 7.95M XTZ; if stakers leave, the rate rises again.

Claims of unclaimed 2017 sale coins: a large one, like the 24,900 XTZ on Oct 22 2026, adds straight to circulating supply. The pool still holds 19.94M XTZ.

The Tezos Foundation's next activity report, covering the first half of 2026, would show how much XTZ it still holds and whether it sold any.

## Summary

Tezos XTZ supply grew **+0.72%** in the 90 days to Nov 7 2026, and the Pressure Framework projects about the same for the next 90 days. Almost all new XTZ is staking pay, **7.95M XTZ**, while storage and trading burns plus six lost rollup bonds removed **68.35K XTZ**. XTZ has no supply cap and no vesting; its brake is a rule that cuts the yearly rate as more XTZ is staked. The main risk to the count is a large claim from the 19.94M XTZ of unclaimed 2017 sale coins.

# XTZ Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
➜ Start with the Tezos coin page for the short answer (should you buy XTZ?) and its price drivers: [mrnasdog.com/research/tezos](https://mrnasdog.com/research/tezos)

**XTZ** supply is growing at a slow, steady pace: about **+0.71%** in the 90 days from Aug 19 to Nov 16 2026, and about the same in the next 90 days. Tezos has no supply cap. It pays its bakers and stakers in new XTZ, **7.87M XTZ** in this window, plus **39.00K XTZ** claimed from the 2017 sale, against **38.84K XTZ** burned. Other trackers show **+0.71%** too.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 16 2026 the circulating supply of Tezos grew by a net **+0.71%**, and the next 90 days point to about **+0.71%** again. Other trackers read the same window at **+0.71%**, a difference of less than **0.01 percentage points**, so the two readings agree and the XTZ coin page carries no warning note. Nearly all of the new supply is staking pay, and the burns cancel only about half a percent of it. The cite-able label for XTZ is simple: **a low, self-adjusting staking inflation with no unlocks left and almost no burn**.

## Sell pressure: where new XTZ comes from

**Staking pay is the main source: 7.87M XTZ.** Every block, Tezos creates new XTZ and pays it to the bakers who produce and confirm blocks and to the people who stake with them. From Aug 19 to Nov 16 2026 that came to **7,869,809 XTZ**, about 87,400 XTZ a day. The yearly rate is not fixed: Tezos lowers it as more XTZ is staked and raises it as staking falls. In October 2026 the staked total rose from about 334M to about 350M XTZ, and the yearly rate slipped from about **3.1%** to about **2.8%**, so pay per day fell from about 89,000 to about 84,000 XTZ. The rate on Nov 17 2026 was **2.81%** a year.

**No XTZ vests.** The Tezos founders' and the Tezos Foundation's shares from the 2017 sale finished vesting years ago, and no XTZ unlock is scheduled for anyone.

**Old sale claims added 39.00K XTZ.** About **19.94M XTZ** bought in the 2017 Tezos sale has never been claimed by its buyers, so it does not count as circulating. It can be claimed at any time and has no deadline. Six claims between Sep 30 and Nov 6 2026 released **38,997 XTZ**; the largest was **24,900 XTZ** on Oct 22 2026. Claims came in every quarter of the past year, about **120,700 XTZ** in all, so the next 90 days carry about **30,200 XTZ** from this source. The Tezos Foundation's own sales and grants add nothing here, because its XTZ is already circulating.

**No locked or bankrupt pile is waiting.** No bankrupt firm or court holds XTZ for a later payout. Staked XTZ can be unstaked within days and already counts as circulating, so it is not a locked pile waiting to come out.

## Buy pressure: where new XTZ goes

**Nobody buys XTZ back.** No contract, company or treasury buys XTZ back. Transaction fees on Tezos go to the bakers as pay; they are not used to buy or burn XTZ.

**Burns removed 38.84K XTZ.** Tezos destroys a small fee each time a contract stores new data or a new account is created, which removed **28,383 XTZ** from Aug 19 to Nov 16 2026. The tzBTC/XTZ exchange built into the protocol also sends 0.1% of every trade to an address no one can spend from; that took out another **10,459 XTZ** across 8,237 transfers. Together the Tezos burns removed **38,842 XTZ**, about 0.5% of the new XTZ paid out in the same window. No baker was punished for double-signing in the window, so no XTZ was destroyed that way.

**The Foundation does not buy.** The Tezos Foundation pays grants and service providers partly in XTZ and has no plan to buy XTZ back. Exchange-traded products and funds trade XTZ that is already circulating.

**Staking locks nothing away.** About **349M XTZ**, close to a third of all XTZ, is staked, but staked XTZ still counts as circulating, so more staking takes nothing out of the supply. A built-in liquid staking token is planned for a later Tezos upgrade and has no date.

## Foundation and overhang

The **Tezos Foundation** is the one team-linked holder. In mid-November 2026 its 16 known wallets held about **91.9M XTZ**: about **33.7M XTZ** in its four baker wallets, about **28.2M XTZ** in its delegating wallets and **30.0M XTZ** in two staking wallets funded in late September and early October 2026. Several of the delegating wallets were emptied in the window and much of that XTZ moved into the two staking wallets. The Foundation's last report put its XTZ at **$76 million** on Dec 31 2025, about a seventh of its assets, with bitcoin the largest part. All of these coins already count as circulating.

The second pile is the **19.94M XTZ** still unclaimed from the 2017 sale. It belongs to the original buyers, not the team, and it enters the supply only as buyers claim it. Each claim adds that XTZ to the circulating supply on the day it is made. If the Foundation's wallets send XTZ to exchanges, nothing new is added, because those coins were already circulating.

## How XTZ compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Against **Ethereum**, Tezos shows the same broad shape, staking pay with no cap, but a weaker offset. Ethereum burns the base fee of every transaction, so in busy weeks its burn can cancel a real share of new ETH. Tezos sends transaction fees to bakers and burns only storage fees and one exchange's trade fee, so its burn stays near half a percent of new XTZ whatever the activity.

Against chains with fixed schedules such as **Cardano**, which pays out of a shrinking reserve, or **Solana**, whose inflation steps down every year, the Tezos rate follows staking instead of the calendar. When more XTZ is staked the rate falls, and when stakers leave it rises, within a floor of 0.25% and a ceiling of 10% a year. That makes XTZ inflation self-correcting but hard to forecast far ahead.

Against young proof-of-stake chains that still carry large investor and team cliffs, XTZ has none. The only unscheduled source is the 2017 sale's unclaimed coins, about **1.8%** of all XTZ, released in small amounts. For an XTZ holder the supply story is the staking rate, not an unlock calendar.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Nov 18 2026:** the current Tezos proposal period closes with only a long-running placeholder proposal on the ballot, so no protocol upgrade can start voting before the next period opens.

**Through Feb 15 2027:** the staked total, now about **349M XTZ**. If staking keeps rising, the **2.81%** yearly rate keeps falling and new XTZ slows; if stakers leave, the rate climbs back.

**Any day:** a large claim from the **19.94M XTZ** unclaimed 2017 pile. The biggest single claim of the past 90 days was 24,900 XTZ on Oct 22 2026.

**Early 2027:** the Tezos Foundation's report for the first half of 2026, which would show how much XTZ it still holds, and the next protocol proposal after Ushuaia, which may bring the built-in liquid staking token.

## Summary

XTZ supply grew by a net **+0.71%** over the 90 days to Nov 16 2026, and Tezos points to about the same over the next 90 days. Almost all of it is the **7.87M XTZ** paid to bakers and stakers under a rate that falls as more XTZ is staked, now **2.81%** a year. Tezos has no supply cap and no vesting left, and its burns of **38.84K XTZ** cancel only about half a percent of new coins. The main risk to the reading is a drop in staking, which would raise the rate, while the Tezos Foundation's roughly **91.9M XTZ** is already counted as circulating.

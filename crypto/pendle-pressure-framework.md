Pendle is a yield-tokenisation and fixed-income protocol on Ethereum whose PENDLE token did not mint or burn a single wei in the 90 days to **Aug 30 2026** — total supply read exactly **281,527,448.458531430459890761** at both ends of the window — and yet the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **1.73M PENDLE** of sell pressure against **zero** buy pressure, a net of **+1.00%** on a circulating base of **172.29M PENDLE**. Pendle dilutes without minting: the tokens come out of wallets the project already holds and out of a two-year vote-lock that is being retired, and the fee-funded buyback that would normally offset them delivers into a contract already counted as tradable supply. Our supply monitor reads **+0.70%** for the same window, a gap of **0.30 percentage points**, inside tolerance, so no monitor-gap flag ships. PENDLE is **a flat-supply token that is structurally inflationary on the active float**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

For the 90-day window ending **Aug 30 2026**, the MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads **PENDLE at +1.00% net**: sell pressure of **1.73M PENDLE** from the retiring vote-lock, the incentive reserve and the team multisig, against buy pressure of **0**, on a circulating base of **172,286,017 PENDLE**. The next 90 days read **+0.95%**, a touch softer only because the team wallet's roughly two-month drip fires once inside the next window rather than twice. Our supply monitor reads **+0.70%** for the same window on seven-day medians, a gap of **0.30 percentage points** — comfortably inside the half-point tolerance, so no flag is raised and no deep walk was triggered. The monitor is unusually noisy on Pendle, its last sixteen readings spanning **+0.37%** to **+1.25%**, which is why medians rather than single days were taken at each end. The right label for PENDLE is **a token with a frozen mint and an unfrozen float**.

## Sell pressure: where new PENDLE comes from

The first thing to settle about Pendle is that none of this is issuance. The PENDLE contract on Ethereum returned an identical **281,527,448.458531430459890761** at block **25,218,494** and at block **25,864,055**, so the entire ledger below is the movement of tokens that already existed across the line that separates counted supply from uncounted. Pendle's own documentation draws that line at five addresses; the market's classified circulating figure draws it at four of them, leaving the liquid staking contract inside the count. That distinction is not cosmetic — it decides five of the eight rows on this page.

Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0.60M PENDLE**. Pendle's incentive emissions are paid out of a governance reserve rather than minted, and that reserve made exactly two transfers in the window: **200,000** on **Jun 12 2026** and **400,000** on **Jul 20 2026**, with zero inbound. Both were traced downstream rather than assumed — one leg funded the gauge contract with **200,000**, the only inbound that contract received all quarter, and the other fed the rewards distributor with **230,000**. The reserve is being drawn well below Pendle's own documented terminal incentive rate of **1.9752%** a year, which would be roughly **1.39M** a quarter if it were actually minted. Nothing has left the reserve since **Jul 20 2026**.

Sell #4, long-term locked, is the largest row at **0.94M PENDLE**, and it is a one-way regime change rather than a schedule. Pendle retired its two-year vePENDLE vote-lock in favour of sPENDLE, a liquid staking token, pausing new locks on **Jan 29 2026**, and holders have been walking out ever since: the vote-lock contract's balance fell from **64.72M** to **63.77M** across this window. Because withdrawal is a choice rather than a date, the framework books what actually left, not what became eligible to leave — the undrawn **63.77M** is carried as an overhang instead. Sell #3, Foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0.19M**: the team multisig sent **120,000** on **Jun 25 2026** and **70,000** on **Aug 26 2026** to an onward wallet that empties the same day, a pattern running roughly every two months since late 2025. Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is **zero**, and permanently so in practice: Pendle's team, investor and advisor allocations finished vesting in **Sept 2024**, and an independent unlock tracker publishes Pendle as fully unlocked with no scheduled release ahead.

## Buy pressure: where new PENDLE goes

Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **zero**, and this is the call the whole PENDLE page turns on. Pendle routes **80%** of its yield and swap fees into buying PENDLE on the open market, harvesting fortnightly and spending through an hourly average price. The purchases are real, funded and verifiable: the buyback contract made seven settlements in this window — **218,235** on **Jun 5 2026**, **131,832** on **Jun 19 2026**, **239,840** on **Jul 3 2026**, **149,087** on **Jul 17 2026**, **144,388** on **Jul 31 2026**, **199,338** on **Aug 14 2026** and **94,065** on **Aug 28 2026**, totalling **1,176,785 PENDLE**. Every one of the seven went to the sPENDLE staking contract. That contract is inside the counted float, so a token bought off the float and delivered to the float removes nothing. Reading the buyback's size alone would have been the trap; reading its destination settles it.

Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **zero** for three independent reasons, and the arithmetic is worth spelling out because the surface reading points the other way. The sPENDLE contract's PENDLE balance rose from **34,207,803** to **35,395,694**, a gain of **1.19M** that looks like a large absorption. But **1,176,785** of that gain is the Buy #1 buyback delivery landing in the same contract, leaving about **11,000 PENDLE** of genuine net new staking — booking the balance change would have counted the buyback twice. Second, sPENDLE is not a long-term lock: exit takes **14 days**, or is instant for a **5%** fee, and **1.02M PENDLE** was already sitting in the withdrawal queue at window close. Third, the contract is inside the counted float, so nothing left the market in the first place. Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **zero**, verified on both surfaces: total supply is identical to the wei at both ends, and the dead address held an unchanged **0.10 PENDLE** throughout. Pendle's burn switch reads off, so the public burn call reverts. Buy #3, Foundation buy, is **zero** — not one of Pendle's reserve wallets received a single PENDLE in the window.

## Foundation and overhang

Five team-controlled overhangs are tracked for PENDLE, and the largest is not a wallet at all. The PENDLE token still carries a live liquidity-emission mint that creates one weekly tranche per elapsed week, callable by a single named recipient with no vote and no delay. Its counter has read week **204** since **Mar 2025** and read **204** at both ends of this window; the current week is **279**. Replaying the contract's own integer arithmetic over the **75** unclaimed weeks gives **9,064,052 PENDLE** that one uncontested transaction could create — **3.22%** of total supply, **5.26%** of the float. Nobody has called it, so the row is zero and the mint is carried as a watch line rather than a forecast, but it is by far the biggest thing on this page.

The wallet overhangs behind it are the retiring vePENDLE lock's undrawn **63.77M PENDLE**, which must all exit by **Jan 29 2028** and is draining at well under a million a quarter; the governance incentive reserve at **20.85M**, which released **0.60M** this window and nothing since **Jul 20 2026**; the Ecosystem Fund at **16.12M**, which had zero transfers of any size inside the window and whose last movement was **Apr 30 2026**; and the team multisig at **7.77M**, the one that drips. Together the four hold **108.51M PENDLE** outside the counted float, against **172.29M** inside it. If any of these balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh; the same applies to the buyback destination, whose balance falling would mean sPENDLE stakers unwinding rather than Pendle selling.

## How PENDLE compares to other DeFi governance tokens

The natural comparison set for PENDLE is DeFi governance tokens that pay liquidity incentives and share fees with lockers, and Pendle sits at an unusual point inside that group. The conventional design mints its incentives: the protocol issues new tokens on an emission curve, total supply visibly rises, and the framework reads the mint directly. Pendle does the opposite — its mint has been dormant for over a year while emissions are paid from a reserve of tokens minted long ago. On a total-supply chart Pendle looks like a hard-capped asset. On a float chart it does not, because the reserve release crosses exactly the same line that a mint would.

The lock mechanism diverges just as sharply. The classic vote-escrow model rewards multi-year commitment, and a growing lock balance is genuine float removal that the framework books as buy pressure. Pendle has walked away from that model, replacing a two-year vePENDLE lock with sPENDLE, exitable in fourteen days, and the migration is running the wrong way for supply: the old lock is releasing **0.94M** a quarter into the float while the new one, being inside the counted float already, absorbs nothing measurable. A protocol that swaps a hard lock for a soft one converts a structural float sink into a liquidity convenience, and this page is what that trade looks like on a supply ledger.

The buyback comparison is the third divergence. The strongest fee-share designs buy on the open market and burn what they buy, so every unit of revenue permanently retires float. Pendle buys with a very high share of fees — **80%** of yield and swap fees, well above the typical **10–20%** of fee-share models — but it distributes the purchases to sPENDLE stakers instead of retiring them, which recycles them into the float. On a supply ledger that is a round-trip worth zero, however large the headline number, and **1,176,785 PENDLE** is a large headline number. Against a peer whose emissions are minted but whose burn strikes the float, Pendle looks better on total supply and worse on the tradable float.

## Summary

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads Pendle at **+1.00%** net supply growth over the 90 days to **Aug 30 2026** and **+0.95%** over the next 90, against a monitor reading of **+0.70%**. The structural mechanism is dilution without issuance: PENDLE's total supply did not move a wei, but **1.73M PENDLE** crossed from project-held wallets and a retiring two-year vote-lock into the counted float, and Pendle's **1,176,785 PENDLE** of genuine fee-funded buying removed none of it because every bought token was delivered into a contract that is already counted as tradable. The key risk is the dormant mint: **9,064,052 PENDLE** — **5.26%** of the float — has accrued behind a function that one named wallet can call with no vote and no delay, and it has sat unclaimed for **75** weeks. There is no supply ceiling to fall back on; Pendle has no maximum supply, and its documented terminal incentive rate of **1.9752%** a year can mint indefinitely once anyone starts claiming it.

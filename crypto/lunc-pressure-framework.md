# LUNC Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

LUNC supply was roughly steady over the last 90 days: it fell a net **0.06%**, and we expect a fall of about **0.16%** over the next 90 days. Terra Classic mints no new LUNC, and a **1.5%** tax on on-chain transfers burned **7.99B LUNC** between Aug 9 and Nov 7 2026, while **6.35B LUNC** left the stake and became free to trade. Other trackers show **−0.06%** for the same 90 days, so the two readings agree.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 9 to Nov 7 2026, the LUNC that circulates fell by a net **0.06%**. Other trackers show **−0.06%** as well, a difference of less than **0.01** points, so the coin page carries no warning note. Over the next 90 days we expect circulating LUNC to fall by about **0.16%**, because the burn should keep its pace while the big wave of unstaking is not something we can count on again. In one line: Terra Classic is a zero-mint chain whose transfer tax shrinks supply slowly, and whose short-term count swings with staking.

## Sell pressure: where new LUNC comes from

Protocol inflation added nothing. The chain's inflation setting is zero, and the old swap that once turned USTC into freshly printed LUNC did not run a single time in the window. Staking rewards on Terra Classic are paid out of a reward pool and fees, which hold coins that already exist.

Vesting added nothing either. LUNC launched in 2019, and every launch allocation finished vesting long ago, so no locked tranche is waiting to open.

The community pool released **534.92M LUNC**. Terra Classic holders voted two payments out of it: **281.85M LUNC** on Sep 4 2026 to keep the IBC relayers running, and **253.07M LUNC** on Oct 15 2026 to pay the team that built the Hyperlane bridge. Over the past 12 months five such votes paid out **1.97B LUNC**, so we expect about **485.74M LUNC** more in the next 90 days.

The largest sell-side flow came from staking. Staked LUNC sits outside the circulating count, and between Aug 9 and Nov 7 2026 the stake shrank from **908.06B** to **901.71B LUNC**, freeing **6.35B LUNC**. Holders choose when to stake or unstake, with a 21-day wait, so we expect no set amount for the next 90 days. No court estate or bankruptcy pile is waiting to pay out LUNC.

## Buy pressure: where new LUNC goes

Nothing buys LUNC back on the chain's behalf, and no foundation buys it. Terraform Labs, the company that built the chain, is winding down and has no buying plan.

The transfer tax is the main way LUNC leaves supply. Every taxed transfer on the chain pays **1.5%**, up from 0.5% after a vote on Aug 2 2026, and 80% of that tax is destroyed. From Aug 9 to Nov 7 2026 the tax burned **7.99B LUNC**, close to **88.83M LUNC** a day. A single transfer of **28.92B LUNC** from a Binance wallet on Oct 23 2026 burned **347.04M LUNC** by itself. Trades on exchanges do not pay the tax, so the burn depends on how much LUNC moves on the chain.

LUNC has no long-term lock. Staking can be undone in 21 days, and the stake shrank rather than grew in this window.

Binance burned **692.31M LUNC** from its trading fees: **334.88M** on Sep 1 2026, **334.88M** again on Oct 1 2026, and a smaller **21.01M** the same day. The burn due on Nov 1 2026 never arrived, and Binance has not said why, so we count no Binance burns for the next 90 days until they restart.

The community pool took in **1.37B LUNC**. A tenth of every tax and half of the staking reward stream flow into it, and because the pool sits outside the circulating count, that intake works like a burn until holders vote to spend it. We expect about the same intake next. Finally, 45 holders sent **15.73M LUNC** of their own coins to the burn address, which we do not count ahead because it has no schedule.

## Foundation and overhang

Terra Classic has no foundation treasury. The largest pile the chain controls is the community pool, which holds **9.46B LUNC** outside the circulating count and pays out only after a holder vote. The stake itself, **901.71B LUNC**, also sits outside the count and returns to the market whenever holders unstake.

The reward pool that pays stakers holds **34.88B LUNC**, and the eight wallets left over from Terraform Labs hold **302.23M LUNC**; both already count as circulating, so a payout from either adds nothing new. A further **14.96B LUNC** is left out of the circulating count with no public owner, and it has barely moved since late August 2026. We check each of these balances at every rebuild; if the community pool or the stake falls before the next check, that outflow shows up on the sell side then.

## How LUNC compares to other proof-of-stake Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake chains pay their stakers in newly minted coins. Ethereum creates new ETH for validators every few minutes and burns part of each fee, and Cosmos Hub mints new ATOM to its stakers. Terra Classic does neither: its mint is switched off, so stakers are paid from existing coins, and supply can only go down.

The burn also works differently. BNB Chain and Ethereum burn a slice of the gas fee on every transaction, while Terra Classic taxes the value of each transfer. That makes LUNC's burn depend on how many coins move rather than how busy the chain is, and it explains why one large wallet move on Oct 23 2026 burned more than an average week of normal use.

The third difference is the exchange burn. Few chains rely on an outside company to shrink their supply, but Binance's monthly fee burns have been part of the LUNC story for years. With that burn now paused, the transfer tax carries almost all of the work.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

On Nov 10 2026 the vote on proposal 12236 ends; it would let one wallet upload new contract code for two Terra Classic apps and has no effect on supply.

On Dec 1 2026, and again on Jan 1 2027, Binance would normally burn its monthly LUNC fees; if those burns return, they add back to the buy side.

Any new community pool spending vote is worth watching, since a vote lasts seven days and a passed one releases coins at once; none is open as of Nov 7 2026.

The stake, now **901.71B LUNC**, is the swing factor: another wave of unstaking would push circulating LUNC up even while the tax keeps burning.

## Summary

LUNC supply was roughly steady over the last 90 days, down a net **0.06%**, and other trackers agree.

Terra Classic mints no new LUNC, and its **1.5%** transfer tax burned **7.99B LUNC** between Aug 9 and Nov 7 2026.

The burn was offset by **6.35B LUNC** leaving the stake and **534.92M LUNC** paid out of the community pool by vote.

With Binance's monthly burn paused since Nov 1 2026, we expect circulating LUNC to fall about **0.16%** over the next 90 days.

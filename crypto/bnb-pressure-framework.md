# BNB Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking

BNB supply is shrinking, and it should keep shrinking: no new BNB is ever created, while two burns keep destroying coins. Between Aug 1 and Oct 30 2026 nothing was added, **1.65M BNB** was burned and net supply fell **1.26%**, in line with the **−1.24%** other trackers show. The burns stop only when supply reaches **100M BNB**, about 31.5M below today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Oct 30 2026, BNB supply fell **1.26%**, from about 133.17M to **131.51M BNB**. Other supply trackers show **−1.24%** for the same stretch, a difference of **0.02 points**, so the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days, to Jan 28 2027, we expect another fall of about **1.19%**, because the next quarterly burn is due in mid-January 2027. In one line: BNB is deflationary by design, with a fixed-rule burn and no new coins at all.

## Sell pressure: where new BNB comes from

**Protocol inflation is zero.** BNB Smart Chain has no block reward. Validators and the people who stake with them are paid only from the gas fees that users pay, so running the chain never prints a single new BNB.

**Vesting unlocks are zero.** BNB launched in July 2017, and the shares set aside for the team and early backers were released years ago. Today every BNB that exists already counts as circulating, so there is no unlock calendar to wait for.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero.** The largest team-held pile is the wallet that pays for the quarterly burn, and the only coins that left it in these 90 days went straight to the burn address. Those coins were already counted, so nothing new reached the market.

**Long-term locks and bankruptcy releases are zero.** No court estate, frozen fund or long-term lock holds BNB that could be released into the market in the next 90 days.

## Buy pressure: where new BNB goes

**The quarterly Auto-Burn is the big one.** On Oct 19 2026, BNB Chain completed its 37th quarterly burn and destroyed **1,646,196 BNB**, worth about $1.2 billion at the time. The amount is set by two inputs: how many blocks the chain produced in the quarter and the quarter's median BNB price. A higher price means fewer coins are burned; a lower price means more. The 38th burn covers October to December 2026. At today's price of about $700 it would destroy roughly **1.56M BNB**, and it is expected in mid-January 2027.

**The real-time fee burn is small but never stops.** In every block, **10%** of the gas fees is destroyed, and that share did not change in these 90 days. Together with a few coins people sent to the burn address by hand, it removed **7,653 BNB** between Aug 1 and Oct 30 2026, about 85 a day. The pace slowed to about 61 a day after Oct 19 2026.

**Programmatic buybacks are zero.** Nothing is bought on the open market for the network; the quarterly burn uses BNB that was set aside in advance. **Foundation buying is zero** too: companies that hold BNB as a treasury, the largest with about 515K BNB at the end of July 2026, and the US spot BNB fund that opened on May 28 2026 buy coins that already circulate. **New long-term locks are zero**, because staked BNB still counts as circulating and can be unstaked.

## Foundation and overhang

The wallet that funds the quarterly burn held **5.25M BNB** on Aug 1 2026 and **3.61M BNB** on Oct 30 2026. The Oct 19 2026 burn was its only outflow, so at today's burn size it holds enough for roughly two more quarters. The old bridge hub, a system contract that pays out coins to people moving them over from the retired Beacon Chain, holds **25.97M BNB**; it paid out 4,246 BNB in these 90 days. Both piles already count as circulating, so neither adds new supply.

We read both balances again at every rebuild. If the burn wallet's balance falls for any reason other than a burn, that outflow is added to the sell side at the next check.

## How BNB compares to other exchange-backed chains

Most proof-of-stake Layer 1 coins pay validators in brand-new coins, so their supply grows every day and any fee burn only slows that growth. BNB is the opposite case. With no block reward at all, its only supply changes are burns, which is why its 90-day reading is negative even when the chain is quiet.

Among exchange-linked coins, the closest match is a quarterly burn tied to the parent exchange. Some of these coins buy their tokens back on the open market with a share of exchange profit, so the size of each burn depends on how well the business did. BNB's quarterly burn instead follows a fixed public rule based on price and block count, which makes the next burn easy to estimate in advance. Its total supply also has a clear floor of 100M BNB, while many other exchange coins have no stated end point.

Compared with Ethereum, which also burns part of every fee, BNB's real-time burn is much smaller, about 85 BNB a day against a supply of 131.51M. Almost all of BNB's shrinking comes from the quarterly burn, not from fees.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The 38th quarterly burn, expected in mid-January 2027.** January burns came on Jan 17 2024, Jan 23 2025 and Jan 15 2026. If the 38th burn slips past Jan 28 2027, the next-90-day reading would fall close to zero until it lands.

**The BNB price through Dec 31 2026.** The quarter's median price sets the size of the 38th burn: a higher price shrinks it, a lower price grows it.

**The Jenner upgrade on the main network.** It went live on the test network on Oct 29 2026 and adds a reserved lane for plain BNB payments. No main-network date is set yet, and it does not change the burn share or create new BNB.

**The stolen funds from the Oct 29 2026 Binance hack.** Any BNB the attacker took was already circulating, so selling it would not change the supply count, but it could weigh on the price.

## Summary

BNB supply fell **1.26%** in the 90 days to Oct 30 2026, because no new BNB is ever made and the Oct 19 2026 quarterly burn destroyed **1.65M BNB**. The real-time fee burn added another **7,653 BNB** of destruction, and nothing was unlocked or released. The next quarterly burn, about **1.56M BNB** at today's price, should cut supply by about another **1.19%** by Jan 28 2027, with the main risk being a burn date that slips past that day. The burns continue until supply reaches the 100M BNB floor.

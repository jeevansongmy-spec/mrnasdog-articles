# BNB Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply shrinking · projected to keep shrinking

**BNB** is deflationary: from Aug 28 to Nov 26 2026 the supply fell by **1.26%**, and the next 90 days should take off about **1.27%** more. No new BNB is ever minted, so the only movement is down, through two burns: the quarterly Auto-Burn, which destroyed **1,646,196 BNB** on Oct 19 2026, and the fee burn on BNB Smart Chain, which destroyed **6,492 BNB**. The supply started at 200M BNB and the burns stop once it reaches 100M BNB; it stands at **131.51M BNB** today.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to Nov 26 2026, BNB added **0 BNB** and destroyed **1.65M BNB**, a net change of **−1.26%** of the **131.51M BNB** in circulation. Other supply trackers show **−1.22%** for the same 90 days, a gap of only **0.03 points**, so the two readings agree and the BNB coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days, to Feb 24 2027, the 38th Auto-Burn of about **1.66M BNB** plus the fee burn put the projected change at **−1.27%**. In one line: BNB is a no-mint coin that shrinks by a fixed rule every quarter.

## Sell pressure: where new BNB comes from

**Protocol inflation is 0 BNB.** BNB Smart Chain has no block reward. The validators who make blocks, and the people who stake BNB with them, are paid only from the fees users spend on transactions, so running the chain never creates a single new BNB.

**Vesting unlocks are 0 BNB.** The team and early-backer shares from the 2017 launch were released long ago. Every BNB that exists today already counts as circulating, so there is no locked batch waiting for a date.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are 0 BNB.** The reserve wallet that pays for the Auto-Burn made one payment in the window, and that payment went straight to the burn address. Its coins already count as circulating, so even a sale from it would not add new BNB.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply is 0 BNB.** No court or bankrupt firm is holding BNB for a later payout. The old bridge wallet that pays out balances from the retired Beacon Chain paid about **5,666 BNB** to users in the window, but those coins were already counted as circulating.

## Buy pressure: where new BNB goes

**The quarterly Auto-Burn removed 1,646,196 BNB.** On Oct 19 2026 the 37th Auto-Burn sent **1,646,196 BNB** from the reserve wallet to a burn address, where no one can ever spend it. The size of each Auto-Burn is set by a published rule: it rises with the number of blocks in the quarter and falls as the BNB price rises, so a cheaper BNB means more BNB burned. The 36th Auto-Burn on Jul 15 2026 destroyed **1,615,828 BNB**, so the October burn was about 2% bigger.

**The protocol fee burn removed 6,492 BNB.** A tenth of the fees in every BNB Smart Chain block is destroyed the moment the block is made. Fees are tiny on BNB Smart Chain, so the fee burn is small next to the Auto-Burn, and it slowed through the window: about 100 BNB a day in early September and about 56 BNB a day in November. At that pace the fee burn adds about **6,492 BNB** again over the next 90 days.

**Programmatic buyback is 0 BNB.** BNB is not bought back on the open market; the Auto-Burn is paid from coins the reserve wallet already holds.

**Foundation buy is 0 BNB.** After the Oct 29 2026 hack, Binance bought tokens to replace what was stolen from its users, and a US fund holds BNB for its investors. Both trade coins already in circulation, so neither changes the BNB count.

**New long-term lock is 0 BNB.** Staked BNB still counts as circulating, so more staking does not shrink the supply. The planned second BNB Chain network will use BNB for fees and staking, but it has no launch date inside the next 90 days.

## Foundation and overhang

The one wallet that matters for BNB supply is the Auto-Burn reserve wallet. It held **5.25M BNB** at the start of the window and holds **3.61M BNB** today, after paying for the Oct 19 2026 burn. That is enough for about two more Auto-Burns at today's size. Anyone can watch it on-chain, and in the window it paid only the burn address. The old bridge wallet holds **25.97M BNB**, but that is money owed to users from the retired Beacon Chain, not a team reserve, and it shrinks only as users claim it. If the reserve wallet's balance falls between checks for any reason other than a burn, that outflow is added as new sell pressure at the next check.

## How BNB compares to other exchange-backed Layer 1 chains

Most proof-of-stake chains pay their validators in brand-new coins and use a fee burn to win some of it back. Ethereum is the clearest example: over roughly the same 90 days, Ethereum's validators earned about 267K new ETH while its fee burn destroyed only about 6.67K ETH, so ETH supply grew. BNB works the other way around. BNB Smart Chain pays validators from fees alone, so nothing is minted, and the burns are pure reduction.

Among coins tied to a big exchange, BNB's burn is unusual because it runs on a published rule rather than on company profits. Older exchange-coin burns, including BNB's own before 2021, depended on how much the exchange earned that quarter. OKB took a different path in 2025, burning most of its supply in one go and fixing the rest, which leaves it with no ongoing burn. BNB keeps a steady quarterly burn, sized by blocks and price, until the supply reaches 100M BNB.

The trade-off is that BNB's shrinking supply depends on a reserve wallet being topped up. The rule is public and the burns are easy to check on-chain, but the coins that feed each burn come from a wallet the BNB team refills, not from a rule written into the chain itself.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Dec 8 2026:** the Jenner upgrade goes live on BNB Smart Chain, adding a payment lane and a new token standard. It does not change the fee burn or create any BNB.

**Mid-January 2027:** the 38th quarterly Auto-Burn. The date is not set yet; the 2026 burns came between 15 and 24 days after each quarter ended. At today's price it would destroy about **1.66M BNB**; a lower BNB price before Dec 31 2026 would make it bigger, a higher one smaller.

**The reserve wallet:** after the January burn it would hold only about 1.95M BNB, so a top-up is likely in early 2027. A top-up moves coins already in circulation and adds nothing.

**Early 2027:** public testing of the second BNB Chain network, which will use BNB for fees, staking and governance. Its mainnet is aimed at the second half of 2027.

## Summary

BNB supply fell **1.26%** from Aug 28 to Nov 26 2026 and is projected to fall about **1.27%** more by Feb 24 2027. No new BNB is ever minted: BNB Smart Chain pays its validators from fees, and nothing is left to vest. The quarterly Auto-Burn does almost all the work, with **1,646,196 BNB** destroyed on Oct 19 2026 and about **1.66M BNB** expected in mid-January 2027, while the fee burn adds a few thousand BNB a quarter. The burns continue until the supply, **131.51M BNB** today, reaches 100M BNB.

## Should you buy Monero? The questions, answered

### Is it time to buy the Monero dip?

Supply and demand point to a mixed long-run signal for Monero (Oct 10 2026, score 6.5/10). Monero adds +0.21% new supply in 90 days, and demand reads moderate (3/5). Monero's price drivers: 1 ▲ price up, 0 ▼ price down.

### Why does Monero pay 0.6 XMR per block forever?

Monero's tail emission keeps miners paid after the main emission ended, at a fixed 0.6 XMR per block. That adds only +0.21% new supply in 90 days, and the rate keeps falling as the total grows. It seems to work: hashrate is 6.32 GH/s, close to the 6.36 record. Supply and demand still read mixed for Monero in the long run.

### Is Monero banned, and why do exchanges keep dropping XMR?

In most places Monero is still legal to hold, but big exchanges dropped it in 2024, and EU exchanges must stop offering it from Jul 10 2027. The reason is privacy: transfers can't be traced, which clashes with exchange rules. Demand reads moderate (3/5), probably partly because of this. That leaves Monero's long-run signal at mixed, by supply and demand.

### Could Monero ever hit $10,000?

Monero trades near $546 (Oct 10 2026). $10,000 a coin means a $188B market cap, 18 times today's $10.3B. Monero's supply also grows +0.84% in the next year, so every extra coin needs buyers too. Supply and demand still read mixed for Monero in the long run.

### Is it time to take profit on Monero?

We would read Monero differently if new supply jumps (today +0.21% in 90 days), or demand falls from moderate. Until then Monero's signal stays mixed. Mark this date for Monero: Jul 10 2027 — EU privacy-coin ban.

### Is Monero a good investment right now?

Monero scores 6.5/10 on our framework (Oct 10 2026): supply 3.5/5 (steady), demand 3/5. Over the next year Monero's supply changes +0.84%. The long-run signal is mixed.

Supply and demand favor Monero most when little new supply is coming, demand is strong and the price drivers point up. Right now Monero has +0.21% new supply in 90 days (steady), demand moderate (3/5), drivers 1 ▲ / 0 ▼.

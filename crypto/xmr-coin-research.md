## Should you buy Monero? The questions, answered

Supply and demand point to a mixed long-run signal for Monero (Oct 14 2026, score 6/10). Monero adds +0.21% new supply in 90 days, and demand reads moderate (3/5). Monero's price drivers: 1 ▲ price up, 0 ▼ price down.

Monero trades near $531 (Oct 14 2026). $10,000 a coin means a $188B market cap, 19 times today's $10B. Monero's supply also grows +0.84% in the next year, so every extra coin needs buyers too. Supply and demand still read mixed for Monero in the long run.

### Is it time to take profit on Monero?

We would read Monero differently if new supply jumps (today +0.21% in 90 days), or demand falls from moderate. Until then Monero's signal stays mixed. Mark this date for Monero: Jul 10 2027 — EU privacy-coin ban.

## What's new for members?

Thank you for being with us. We've built a home just for members, founding members and members alike. Tap your picture at the top of any page and choose **★ Member Home**. A gold ring around it means you're a member.

Inside, three things:

- **Ask a question.** Any investment question. We answer by email on AMA Day.
- **Book a one-to-one call with MrNasdog.** 20 minutes, on AMA Day.
- **Everything you get,** in one list, with simple on/off switches.

**AMA Day is one Sunday a month**, early in the month. The next ones are **Oct 4**, **Nov 1** and **Dec 6 2026**.

## How do I ask a question?

Write your question in the box and press **Send question**. It can be about any coin, or anything about investing. We'll answer by email on the next AMA Day.

![The Ask your question box on Member Home](https://mrnasdog.com/analysis/member-home/question.webp)
*Figure 1 — Ask your question, on Member Home.*

## How do I book a one-to-one call?

Want to talk it through? Press **Book a call**, pick a time and write your question, so MrNasdog can prepare. Times show in **your own time zone**.

You'll get an email with your own video link. It also shows at the top of Member Home, with **Join the call**, **Change time** and **Cancel**.

![The Next AMA Day card with the call times and the Book a call button](https://mrnasdog.com/analysis/member-home/ama.webp)
*Figure 2 — The next AMA Day, in your time zone.*

![The You're booked box with Join the call, Change time and Cancel](https://mrnasdog.com/analysis/member-home/booked.webp)
*Figure 3 — Once you book: join, change the time, or cancel.*

One call at a time. Booking opens right after the last AMA Day and closes 12 hours before the first call.

Portfolio alerts, member-only email, coin alerts, our personal portfolio and the full track record, all in one place. Tap a switch to turn an email on or off.

# GNO Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Mixed flows · supply roughly steady

<!-- main-page -->
The Gnosis coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/gno](https://mrnasdog.com/research/gno). Below: supply, line by line.

GNO supply was flat over the last 90 days and should stay flat over the next 90. The token on Ethereum was made once, **10M** GNO in 2017, and has no way to make more; after past burns and a pledged burn, the supply that counts is **3M**, of which **2.64M** GNO circulate. Between Jul 31 and Oct 29 2026, **0** GNO were created or released and **0** left the count, so the net change is **0%**, and other trackers show about **+0.04%**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

GNO's net supply change for the 90 days to Oct 29 2026 is **0%**, and the next 90 days to Jan 27 2027 also come out at **0%**. Other trackers show about **+0.04%**, a gap of **0.04 points**, and the coin page carries no warning note. The label is "mixed flows, supply roughly steady", but for GNO it is simpler than that: nothing was printed, nothing was unlocked and nothing was burned. Money did move. The DAO paid its stakers and bought GNO back, yet every one of those coins was already counted as circulating, so the count did not move.

## Sell pressure: where new GNO comes from

**Protocol inflation** is zero and cannot be anything else. The GNO token on Ethereum has no mint function, and the GNO on Gnosis Chain only exists when the same amount is locked in the bridge on Ethereum. Stakers on Gnosis Chain still get paid, but from coins that already exist: the DAO treasury refills the staking payout contract, most recently with **20,000 GNO** on Oct 27 2026. GnosisDAO itself says this staking pay takes value from non-stakers at roughly **2.3%** a year, yet it adds no new GNO.

**Vesting unlocks** are zero. Two vesting contracts from 2020 still hold GNO. The DAO's contract holds **3.85M GNO**, and a second contract, set up at the same time, holds **360,411 GNO**. Neither sent out a single coin between Jul 31 and Oct 29 2026.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks** are zero. The coins in the second contract have been fully unlocked since Nov 23 2025 and could be claimed on any day, but its owner last took coins out in May 2025 and has shared no plan to claim more. The DAO's own wallets moved GNO around, but those coins already counted as circulating.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy** is zero. No court case or bankruptcy estate holds GNO, and staked GNO is already counted as circulating.

## Buy pressure: where new GNO goes

**Programmatic buyback** books zero even though buying is real. The DAO's treasury manager bought about **7,800 GNO** for **$915K** between July and September 2026, and its buyer wallet has picked up **10,987 GNO** more since then. Those coins are kept in the DAO treasury, not burned. Because the treasury already counts as circulating, a coin moving from the market into the treasury does not shrink the count.

**Protocol fee burn** is zero. People pay for Gnosis Chain transactions in xDAI, not GNO, so using the chain burns no GNO, and the burn address did not grow in these 90 days.

**Foundation buy** is zero for this window. The DAO's one-time treasury exit closed on Jul 17 2026, two weeks before the window opened. In it, 92 holders handed in about **111K GNO** and **48K** staked GNO for a share of the treasury. Those coins went back to the treasury and were not burned.

**New long-term lock** is zero. Nothing new locks GNO away from the count, and staking, the one place GNO sits still, is due to end.

## Foundation and overhang

The biggest pile is the DAO's vesting contract with **3.85M GNO**. It is left out of the 3M supply because the DAO pledged in 2022 to cut supply to 3M and burned **3.15M** GNO from this same contract in January 2025. About **1.78M** of what is left has already vested and could be claimed by the DAO's old governance wallet. If those coins were ever claimed and not burned, they would be new supply, so this pot is the one to watch most closely.

The second vesting contract holds **360,411 GNO**, fully unlocked and unclaimed. It is the only GNO counted inside the 3M that does not yet circulate. If its owner claims it, circulating supply rises by up to about **13.7%** in one step.

The DAO treasury holds about **1.23M GNO** across its main wallets on Ethereum and Gnosis Chain, plus the **10,987 GNO** in its buyer wallet. These coins already circulate. They pay stakers, and they are where bought-back and handed-in GNO ends up.

## How GNO compares to other proof-of-stake chain tokens

Most proof-of-stake chains pay stakers in brand-new coins, so their supply grows every day. Ethereum adds new ETH to every block and burns part of the fees. GNO works the other way round: the supply is fixed, and staking pay comes out of the DAO treasury, so holders who do not stake pay for security through a smaller treasury rather than through more coins.

GNO is also unusual because its biggest supply risk is not a schedule but a promise. Many tokens unlock team and investor coins month after month. GNO's team coins finished vesting years ago. What remains is the DAO's pledge to burn its **3.85M** pot, and one owner's choice about when to claim its **360,411** GNO.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The move onto Ethereum.** GnosisDAO voted on Aug 19 2026 to turn Gnosis Chain from its own Layer 1 into a rollup that settles on Ethereum, with the start aimed at December 2026 or January 2027. Staking then ends and about **350K** staked GNO become free to move. Those coins already circulate, so the count does not change, but more GNO could reach the market at once.

**What GNO earns after staking.** The same proposal says a later vote will tie GNO to network income, through a fee share or a buyback. If a buyback burns GNO, that would be the first real buy pressure on the count.

**The DAO's burn pledge.** About **246K** more GNO vest in the DAO's contract every 90 days. A claim that is burned changes nothing; a claim that is kept would be new supply.

**The 360,411 unclaimed GNO.** A claim would show on-chain the same day and would add to circulating supply.

**Treasury buying and staking top-ups.** The DAO keeps buying GNO and refilling the staking payout contract. Both stay inside the circulating count unless the coins are burned.

## Summary

GNO supply was flat in the 90 days to Oct 29 2026, at **0%**, and should stay flat to Jan 27 2027.

No new GNO can be made, and Gnosis Chain stakers are paid from GNO the DAO already holds.

The DAO bought about **7,800 GNO** back in the third quarter, but it keeps them in the treasury, so the count does not fall.

The real supply risks are the DAO's **3.85M**-GNO vesting pot it has pledged to burn and the **360,411** unclaimed GNO in a second vesting contract.

## BNB supply questions, answered

As of Nov 17 2026, BNB supply moves −1.27% in 90 days and −5.08% in a year, so BNB is deflationary. Burns or buybacks take out more BNB than is created.

### What is the BNB inflation rate in 2026?

BNB supply shrinks −5.08% over the next 12 months and −1.27% over the next 90 days (checked Nov 17 2026).

### When is the next BNB token unlock?

No dated BNB unlock is scheduled in the next 90 days (checked Nov 17 2026).

### Is any BNB burned or bought back?

Yes. In our count, about 1.67M BNB is expected to leave the market over the next 90 days through burns, buybacks or locks (checked Nov 17 2026). How it works: The 37th quarterly burn destroyed 1.65M BNB on Oct 19 2026 and the fee burn 6,852 more; the 38th, about 1.66M BNB at today's price, is expected in mid-January 2027.

### How much BNB is in circulation?

BNB has about 132M coins in circulation as of Nov 17 2026; the next 90 days change that by −1.27% (−5.08% over a year).

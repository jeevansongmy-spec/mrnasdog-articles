## BNB supply questions, answered

### Is BNB inflationary or deflationary?

As of Nov 8 2026, BNB supply moves −1.19% in 90 days and −4.77% in a year, so BNB is deflationary. Burns or buybacks take out more BNB than is created.

### What is the BNB inflation rate in 2026?

BNB supply shrinks −4.77% over the next 12 months and −1.19% over the next 90 days (checked Nov 8 2026).

### When is the next BNB token unlock?

No dated BNB unlock is scheduled in the next 90 days (checked Nov 8 2026).

### Is any BNB burned or bought back?

Yes. In our count, about 1.57M BNB is expected to leave the market over the next 90 days through burns, buybacks or locks (checked Nov 8 2026). How it works: The 37th quarterly burn destroyed 1.65M BNB on Oct 19 2026 and the fee burn 7,185 more; the 38th, about 1.56M BNB, is expected in mid-January 2027.

### Where does new BNB supply come from?

Almost no new BNB reaches the market over the next 90 days (checked Nov 8 2026). No new BNB is made: validators are paid from fees only, nothing vests, and the 3.61M BNB set aside for burns is already counted.

### How much BNB is in circulation?

BNB has about 132M coins in circulation as of Nov 8 2026; the next 90 days change that by −1.19% (−4.77% over a year).

### Does BNB have more new supply than ETH, XRP or SOL?

Over the next 90 days (checked Nov 8 2026): BNB −1.19%, ETH +0.21%, XRP +1.42%, SOL +1.14%. BNB has the least new supply of these 4 coins.

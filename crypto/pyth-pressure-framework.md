Pyth Network is one of the very few tokens the Pressure Framework tracks where new supply is not merely unlikely but impossible. The PYTH mint on Solana reports **no mint authority** and **no freeze authority**, on a supply of **9,999,961,238 PYTH**, and neither can be restored by the Pyth DAO or by anyone else. The entire supply engine is a vesting calendar of four equal annual cliffs of **2,125,000,000 PYTH** — and for the first time since we began covering PYTH, no cliff falls inside either window. The third fired on **May 19 2026**, ten days before this 90-day window opens; the fourth and final one is **May 19 2027**. That leaves **267,500 PYTH** of DAO grant outflows on the sell side against **2,948,502 PYTH** of open-market buybacks funded by network revenue, for net **-0.03%** on **7,874,961,238 PYTH** circulating, with **-0.03%** projected forward. Our supply monitor reads **-0.05%**, a gap of **0.01 percentage points**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to **Aug 28 2026** the Pressure Framework reads Pyth Network at **-0.03%** net supply pressure: **0.27M PYTH** on the sell side against **2.95M PYTH** on the buy side. Forward it reads **-0.03%**, with **0 PYTH** of scheduled unlock against **2.02M PYTH** of already-approved buyback. Our supply monitor reads **-0.05%** for the same window, so the gap is **0.01 percentage points** and no data-conflict flag is raised. That agreement is itself the finding. The previous reading of PYTH carried a nine-point gap and a warning chip, because a single **2,125M PYTH** cliff sat inside the window and was being divided by two different supply bases. Nothing about the Pyth mechanism changed between then and now; the window moved past the cliff, both sides emptied, and the disagreement disappeared with it. PYTH is best labelled a **hard-capped oracle token in the quiet year between two cliffs**: nothing can be created, nothing is scheduled to unlock for eight months, and a small revenue-funded buyback is the only thing moving.

## Sell pressure: where new PYTH comes from

It does not come from issuance, and on Pyth Network that zero is read off the mint account rather than inferred from a flat chart. The PYTH mint **HZ1JovNiVvGrGNiiYvEozEVgZ58xaU3RKwX8eACQBCt3** returns a null mint authority and a null freeze authority. On Solana those nulls are permanent: there is no path, governance included, that restores the ability to create a PYTH. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is therefore **0 PYTH** and carries the permanent tag. It is zero twice over, because the reward stream that once paid PYTH to stakers is also switched off — the Pyth DAO set the oracle integrity staking reward rate to zero in **April 2026**, and the residual reward pool of **849,208 PYTH** was pulled back into the DAO treasury on **Jul 23 2026**. Oracle integrity staking and its slashing rules still run; the emission attached to them does not.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, is the row that normally carries the whole PYTH ledger, and it is **0 PYTH** in both columns. Pyth Network released **15%** of supply at launch and locked the remaining **85%** into four equal annual cliffs of **2,125,000,000 PYTH**. Three have fired, the most recent on **May 19 2026**. Ninety days back from **Aug 28 2026** is **May 30 2026**, so that cliff is ten days outside the trailing window, and the fourth and final cliff on **May 19 2027** is far outside the forward one. The framework never smooths a cliff into an average — a cliff inside the window counts in full and a cliff outside counts for nothing — so the row is genuinely empty rather than small. The chain confirms it independently: subtracting the classified float of **7,874,961,238 PYTH** from the on-chain supply leaves exactly **2,125,000,000 PYTH**, one cliff of headroom, and that float has not moved across the window.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is the only non-zero sell row on the page at **0.27M PYTH**. On **Jun 9 2026** the Pyth DAO treasury sent **267,500 PYTH** out in twelve grant transfers, and that is the sole outflow to third parties in the whole window — nothing left the treasury in July or August. Because the firings are irregular rather than scheduled, the forward column stays at zero. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0 PYTH**: Pyth Network has never been through a bankruptcy, so there is no estate, no trustee and no court-ordered distribution.

## Buy pressure: where new PYTH goes

Buy #1, the programmatic buyback, is the engine of this page at **2.95M PYTH**. The Pyth DAO deploys one third of its treasury balance every month to buy PYTH on the open market, funded by revenue from the network's data products, and two of those purchases fall inside the window: **1,803,873 PYTH** on **Jun 9 2026** and **1,144,629 PYTH** on **Jul 29 2026**. A third, **2,570,155 PYTH** on **May 13 2026**, is seventeen days too early to count. It matters which clock is used here: bought PYTH is credited back to the treasury weeks after the swap, so the credits landed on **Jun 2 2026**, **Jul 6 2026** and **Aug 3 2026**. Market absorption happens when the swap executes, so the framework books the purchase dates. The forward column of **2.02M PYTH** is built on an approved authorisation rather than an average: the round passed on **Aug 19 2026** funds roughly **0.67M PYTH**, and three monthly rounds land inside the next 90 days.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, is **0 PYTH**, and it was checked on both surfaces a burn can appear on rather than on total supply alone. The Solana incinerator address holds under **2 PYTH**, so nothing is being routed to a dead address; and the on-chain supply moved by about **7,000 PYTH** over eleven days, which is dust from closed token accounts rather than a mechanism. Two burn proposals were floated on the Pyth DAO forum in **June 2026** and neither was put to a vote. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **0 PYTH** because the DAO buyback is the only buyer and it is already counted in Buy #1. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0 PYTH**: staking is live but pays nothing, so it attracts no new deposits, and no vault or term lock was deployed in the window. The buyback is not double-counted here either, even though the PYTH it buys does stop trading.

## Foundation and overhang

Pyth Network's overhang is unusually well lit, because almost all of it is one on-chain account. The Pyth DAO treasury holds **37,836,259 PYTH** — this is where bought-back PYTH accumulates, and it is held rather than burned, so every buyback creates a matching overhang. Read at both ends of the window, that account went from **12,623,020 PYTH** to **37,836,259 PYTH**, a rise of **25.2M PYTH**, and only **5.5M** of that is bought PYTH being credited back. Another **0.85M** is the retired staking reward pool, and **19.1M** arrived in three monthly transfers from a team distribution account — revenue remitted in PYTH, which moves coins between team-controlled hands rather than off the market, and so is booked at zero. That distribution account still holds **15.4M PYTH** and has been sending roughly **7.7M** a month onward. The remaining watched balances are the council operations account at **326,687 PYTH**, the retired reward-pool custody at **84,834 PYTH**, and the **2,125,000,000 PYTH** still locked until **May 19 2027**. All of these are read from the chain at every rebuild, so if any of their balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How PYTH compares to other fixed-supply oracle and data tokens

Against the older oracle tokens, Pyth Network sits at the opposite end of the issuance question. The incumbent model funds node operators out of a reserve the sponsoring company controls, releasing tokens by decision rather than by calendar, which means its supply pressure is discretionary and hard to date. Pyth Network cannot do that: the mint authority is gone, and every remaining token is either already circulating or sitting behind a published cliff. The trade is transparency for lumpiness. An oracle that drips from a reserve looks calm month to month and never tells you when it will stop; Pyth Network looks calm for eleven months and then moves **2,125,000,000 PYTH** in a single day.

Against capped chains with a halving schedule, the comparison is about where the supply already sits. A halving-model coin issues forever but into a float that already holds nearly all of the supply, so its inflation is small and falls on a schedule nobody can alter. Pyth Network issues nothing at all, but **21.25%** of PYTH is still outside the float, arriving on a Pyth DAO calendar rather than through protocol code. Measured as supply reaching the market, which is what this framework measures, a zero-issuance token with one cliff left is quieter than a halving chain for eight months and much louder for one day.

Against exchange tokens with quarterly buybacks — the closest structural cousin, since both convert business revenue into supply removal — the difference is scale and disposal. Those programmes typically retire several percent of float a year and burn what they buy, so the removal is permanent. The PYTH Reserve removed about **0.037%** of float in 90 days and keeps what it buys inside the Pyth DAO treasury, where a future vote could redeploy it. That is a real buy row and it is what tips this page slightly negative, but it is not yet the kind of mechanism that can absorb a cliff. Scaling it is the stated plan: the Pyth Core upgrade on **Jul 31 2026** moved the network's price feeds to a paid model and routes that revenue to the treasury that funds the buyback.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First, the monthly buyback rounds themselves, roughly one a month through **Nov 2026**: the realised sequence has fallen from **2.57M PYTH** to **1.80M** to **1.14M**, and the round approved on **Aug 19 2026** is smaller again at about **0.67M**. Whether the paid-feed revenue reverses that slide is the single most informative number for this page. Second, the Pyth DAO votes on core deprecation and the upgraded core opened on **Aug 25 2026** and **Aug 26 2026**, which redirect where fee revenue lands and therefore how large the buyback can be. Third, the treasury balance of **37.8M PYTH**: it has only ever grown, and the first sustained fall would turn Sell #3 from a footnote into a real row. Fourth, the team distribution account holding **15.4M PYTH** — its monthly transfers have all gone to the treasury so far, and a transfer to a market venue instead would change the reading. Fifth, and beyond this window but decisive for the next one, the final cliff on **May 19 2027**, which frees **2,125,000,000 PYTH** — about **27%** of everything currently trading — in a single day.

## Summary

Pyth Network gives the cleanest possible answer to the issuance question — a null mint authority, a null freeze authority, and a staking reward rate that governance itself set to zero — and for this one window it gives the cleanest possible answer to the unlock question too, because the annual cliff fired ten days before the window opened and the next is eight months out. What is left is a small, honest ledger: **0.27M PYTH** of DAO grants against **2.95M PYTH** of revenue-funded buying, net **-0.03%**, with **-0.03%** projected forward and a supply monitor that agrees to within **0.01 percentage points**. The key risk is not inflation but concentration in time: **2,125,000,000 PYTH** lands on **May 19 2027** from a calendar no code can slow, and a buyback currently removing **0.037%** of float a quarter cannot meet it. The ceiling is **10,000,000,000 PYTH** and it is fixed forever, which makes PYTH a coin whose supply risk is entirely a question of when, never of how much.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Pyth Network is a price-data network: banks, trading firms and exchanges send in their prices, and apps on many blockchains read them. Its coin, PYTH, has a hard cap of **10B**, and every coin was created on launch day in November 2023. About **7.87B** PYTH is unlocked today. The rest, **2.13B**, is one locked batch that opens around May 20 2027. Between now and then nothing is scheduled to reach the market, so PYTH supply is flat: **0.00%** over the last 90 days and **0.00%** over the next 90. Other trackers read **+0.03%**, a tiny difference that is not new coins, so no warning note is needed. The label is mixed flows with supply roughly steady. The real event for PYTH holders is not in this window at all: it is the last unlock in May 2027.

## Sell pressure: where new PYTH comes from

**Protocol inflation: 0 PYTH.** The Pyth Network coin lives on Solana, and the switch that could make new coins is turned off for good. No reward, fee or upgrade can add a single PYTH to the 10B cap.

**Vesting unlocks: 0 PYTH.** At launch only 15% of PYTH could move. The other 85% was split into four equal batches of about 2.13B PYTH, opening 6, 18, 30 and 42 months after launch. The first three opened in May 2024, May 2025 and May 2026. The fourth and last opens around May 20 2027, which is after both the last 90 days and the next 90 days, so this line books nothing today.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks: 0 PYTH.** The Pyth DAO, the group of PYTH holders who vote on the network, spends from its treasury. On Nov 4 2026 it paid **4.68M PYTH** to its councils for their budgets and pay. Those coins were already unlocked and already counted, so moving them adds nothing new.

**Long-term locked or bankruptcy: 0 PYTH.** No court case or failed firm is sitting on PYTH that it must pay out later.

## Buy pressure: where new PYTH goes

**Programmatic buyback: 0 PYTH removed.** Pyth does buy its own coin. The DAO set up the PYTH Strategic Reserve in December 2025, and on Sep 24 2026 it voted to send all of its share of product income, such as the Pyth Pro data subscriptions, into PYTH every month without a new vote each time. From Aug 27 to Nov 25 2026 the council that does the buying picked up about **28.48M PYTH**: about 2.58M in September, 15.11M in October and 10.79M so far in November. The bought coins go back to the DAO treasury and are held, not burned. They were already counted as circulating before the purchase and they still are, so the count does not shrink. What the buying does change is who holds the coins: they leave traders and sit with the DAO, which may only sell them after a separate vote.

**Protocol fee burn: 0 PYTH.** Pyth has no burn. A few holders destroy small amounts by hand, about **41.46K PYTH** since launch in total, which is far too little to move the count.

**Foundation buy: 0 PYTH.** No foundation or company has said it will buy PYTH beyond the DAO reserve.

**New long-term lock: 0 PYTH.** PYTH staked for voting and the coins held back from publisher rewards for a year are still counted as circulating, so neither takes any PYTH out.

## Foundation and overhang

The Pyth DAO treasury holds **51.52M PYTH**, almost all of it bought back through the reserve. The council wallet that does the buying holds another **10.79M PYTH** from its November purchases, waiting to be sent to the treasury. Both wallets are public and anyone can watch them on-chain. The rules say this PYTH cannot be sold, lent or handed out without a new DAO vote, and no such vote is open today. The much bigger overhang is the last locked batch of **2.13B PYTH**, set to open around May 20 2027. That one batch is about a fifth of all PYTH, and it is the next real test for the coin.

## How PYTH compares to other oracle and data network coins

Data network coins tend to grow their supply in one of two ways. Some pay node operators in new coins every day, so supply creeps up all the time. Others, like PYTH, were fully created at launch and reach the market through locked batches. PYTH sits in the second group, which is why it shows long flat stretches broken by large steps once a year. The trade-off is plain: there is no steady drip to absorb, but each step is big, and the last one is 2.13B PYTH.

PYTH also differs in how it gives value back. Many networks burn part of their fees, which shrinks supply. Pyth turns its income into PYTH and keeps it in the DAO treasury instead. That takes coins out of trading hands but leaves them in the count, and it leaves the DAO with a growing pile it could one day vote to spend, lock or burn.

# CRV Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

<!-- main-page -->
The Curve DAO coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/crv](https://mrnasdog.com/research/crv). Below: supply, line by line.

**CRV** supply is growing: the circulating count rose about **2.45%** in the 90 days to Nov 5 2026, and the next 90 days point to about **1.96%**. Curve DAO pays new CRV every second to liquidity providers, and more CRV comes back to market as old veCRV locks end, while almost nothing is bought back or burned. Gross sell flow was **41.51M CRV** against **2.69M CRV** of buy flow; other trackers show **+2.63%**, and the hard cap is 3.03B CRV.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

From Aug 7 to Nov 5 2026 the circulating supply of Curve DAO's CRV token grew by a net **2.45%**, from about 1.55B to **1.58B CRV**. Other trackers put the same 90 days at **+2.63%**, a gap of **0.18 points**, and the coin page carries no warning note. For the next 90 days the reading is **+1.96%**: the yearly emission cut of Aug 12 2026 lowers new minting, and the one-off airdrop push of October is not counted again. The label for CRV is an emission-funded DEX token whose float grows steadily, with long locks as the only brake.

## Sell pressure: where new CRV comes from

The largest source is protocol inflation. Curve DAO releases CRV at a fixed rate per second toward the pools that veCRV holders vote for, and liquidity providers mint it when they claim. From Aug 7 to Nov 5 2026 that minting came to **24.29M CRV**. On Aug 12 2026 the sixth yearly epoch began and the rate fell by about 16%, from 3.66 to **3.08 CRV per second**, so the next 90 days print about **23.96M CRV**. The next cut is due on Aug 12 2027, outside the coming window.

The second source is vesting, and for CRV that means one old escrow. In 2020 Curve DAO set aside 151.5M CRV for the people who used Curve before the token existed, vesting over one year. That finished in August 2021, but the coins stay in the escrow until each owner claims them. In the window **5.57M CRV** was claimed, and **5.29M CRV** of that came on Oct 19 2026, when a single wallet claimed on behalf of 200 holders in one sweep. About **4.73M CRV** is still unclaimed. Ordinary claims run near 0.28M CRV per 90 days, and that is what the next window books.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks book zero. The only Curve DAO pot outside circulation is the community fund, which held 845.14K CRV at both ends of the window and paid nothing out. The Treasury and the wallets that fund it hold coins that already count as circulating, so their spending adds no new supply.

The fourth row is long-term locks. Holders lock CRV for up to four years to get veCRV, which carries votes and a share of fees. When a lock ends, the owner may take the coins back. From Aug 7 to Nov 5 2026, **11.65M CRV** came out of ended locks. Another 14.10M CRV sits in locks that have ended but not been withdrawn, and 6.88M CRV of locks end in the next 90 days, 5.44M CRV of them on Jan 7 2027. In the last window a bit under half of what could come out did, so the next 90 days book about **9.55M CRV**. There is no bankruptcy estate holding CRV.

## Buy pressure: where new CRV goes

Curve DAO runs no programmatic buyback. Trading and lending fees are paid to veCRV holders in crvUSD, Curve's own stablecoin, with fresh fees added most recently on Nov 5 2026, so fees never turn into CRV buying. There is no protocol fee burn either, and neither the DAO nor the core team bought CRV on the market in the window.

The one real absorber is the veCRV lock. Holders put **2.67M CRV** of new money into locks between Aug 7 and Nov 5 2026, taking it out of circulation for years. That is less than a quarter of the 11.65M CRV that came out of ended locks over the same days, so the locked pile shrank to **841.90M CRV**. Finally, **12.76K CRV** left supply for good: 12.68K CRV was sent by mistake to the CRV token's own address, where it is stuck, and 80.69 CRV was burned. Together the buy side came to **2.69M CRV**.

## Foundation and overhang

Curve DAO's community fund holds **845.14K CRV** outside circulation and pays only by DAO vote; it did not move in the window. The Curve Treasury went from zero to **5.73M CRV** after a multisig sent it 6.3M CRV on Aug 26 and Sep 3 2026; that multisig still holds **1.47M CRV**. On Sep 2 2026 the Treasury funded a one-year grant for a risk team with 568.18K CRV, of which **471.98K CRV** is still in the stream. All three already count as circulating, so spending from them adds nothing to the count.

The bigger overhangs belong to holders, not the team. The early-user escrow still holds 4.73M CRV that anyone owed coins can claim at any time, and 14.10M CRV sits in ended veCRV locks waiting to be withdrawn. Most of the locked pile is rolled forward by three large lockers that keep renewing to the four-year maximum: about 425.71M CRV, 119.15M CRV and 83.40M CRV, all ending in October 2030. Each of these balances is read on-chain at every rebuild; if one falls between refreshes, the outflow enters the sell rows at the next refresh.

## How CRV compares to other vote-escrow DEX tokens

CRV is the original vote-escrow token: locking for votes and fees was invented here, and many DEX tokens copied it. The design pays liquidity providers in new coins and asks holders to lock in return. Where a copy adds a buyback, a fee burn or emissions that shrink with demand, part of the new supply is soaked up by the protocol itself. CRV has none of these: its fees go out as crvUSD, so the only offset to minting is people choosing to lock.

Against fixed-supply DEX tokens, CRV still has a long tail to print. About 2.43B CRV exists today against a hard cap of 3.03B, and the yearly cut of about 16% slows the rate only gently. A token with no emissions and a fee burn can shrink; CRV, by design, keeps growing until well after 2030.

What sets CRV apart is how much of its supply sits in locks: about a third of all CRV. That keeps the circulating count far below the total, but it also means the lock calendar matters. A steady flow of locks ends every week, and when new locking slows, as it did this autumn, those coins become new supply on the market.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

The biggest dated item is **Jan 7 2027**, when about 5.44M CRV of veCRV locks end in a single week; whether those owners withdraw or relock decides most of the lock-ends reading. Watch the early-user escrow, which still holds 4.73M CRV after the Oct 19 2026 push: another batch claim would add to the sell side quickly. Watch the weekly gauge votes, since a new fund-raising gauge or killed gauges change where emissions go, though not how many are minted. And watch new locking: the 2.67M CRV locked in the last 90 days is the only buy flow, and any rise in it lowers the net reading.

## Summary

Curve DAO's CRV supply grew about **2.45%** in the 90 days to Nov 5 2026 and is set to grow about **1.96%** in the next 90 days. The growth comes from emissions paid to liquidity providers, coins leaving ended veCRV locks and claims from an old airdrop escrow. The only real offset is new locking, which took in just 2.67M CRV, because Curve pays its fees in crvUSD and buys back no CRV. CRV is capped at 3.03B coins, but about 600M are still to be minted, so the supply keeps growing for years.

# CRV Inflation Analysis · October 2026 · Supply growing · projected to keep growing

The Curve DAO coin page has the score, the price drivers and every question answered — [mrnasdog.com/research/crv](https://mrnasdog.com/research/crv). Below: supply, line by line.
**CRV supply is growing.** Over the 90 days to Oct 17 2026, Curve DAO paid out **25,437,980 CRV** of new liquidity rewards, **11,463,592 CRV** came out of ended veCRV locks and **918,360 CRV** of old 2020 rewards were claimed, while only **2,218,345 CRV** went into new locks. That is **+2.26%** net on a circulating supply of **1.57B CRV**, with about **+2.00%** expected in the next 90 days. CRV has a hard cap of 3.03B, but no buyback and no fee burn, so the cap is the only limit.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

The MrNasdog Pressure Framework reads CRV at **+2.26%** net supply over the last 90 days and about **+2.00%** over the next 90. Our monitor, which watches the circulating supply day by day, reads **+2.42%** for nearly the same window, a gap of **0.16 percentage points**. That is inside our 0.5-point tolerance, so no warning chip is shown: the two readings agree. Most of the small difference is the base, because the monitor divides by the supply of 90 days ago and we divide by today's. CRV is a coin that is **inflationary by design on the circulating float**: new coins flow out to liquidity providers every second, and locked coins come back as locks end.

## Sell pressure: where new CRV comes from

**Protocol inflation is the biggest source: 25,437,980 CRV in 90 days.** The CRV contract releases a fixed amount every second to the Curve pools and lending markets that CRV holders vote for, and the coins are created when liquidity providers claim them. The release rate drops by about 16% once a year. The latest cut came on Aug 12 2026, from 3.66 to 3.08 CRV a second, which is about 1.86M CRV a week. Because the cut landed inside the window, we project the next 90 days from the new rate: about **23.96M CRV**. The next cut is due in Aug 2027.

**Vesting unlocks added 918,360 CRV.** The team, investor and staff vesting for CRV ended years ago and those contracts are empty. One old contract still holds **10.29M CRV** that early liquidity providers earned in 2020 and never claimed; it is not counted as circulating until someone claims. This window one wallet claimed 706,795 CRV at once. We do not expect that again, so the next 90 days use the pace of the other claims, about **212K CRV**.

**Foundation and unscheduled unlocks are zero.** The DAO reserve contract holds 845,144 CRV and did not move. The DAO treasury and the multisigs around it already count as circulating, so their transfers move coins between holders and add no new supply.

**Long-term locks released 11,463,592 CRV.** CRV holders lock coins in veCRV for up to four years to get voting power and a share of Curve's fees, and locked CRV is not counted as circulating. When a lock ends, the owner can withdraw. About **14.08M CRV** has already finished its lock and not been withdrawn, and **7.11M CRV** more finishes in the next 90 days, including one large group of **5.41M CRV** on Jan 7 2027. At this window's pace of withdrawals, about **9.51M CRV** comes out next. There is no bankruptcy estate or trustee holding CRV.

## Buy pressure: where new CRV goes

**There is no programmatic buyback.** Curve pays its trading and lending fees to veCRV holders in crvUSD, its own stablecoin, and does not use them to buy CRV on the market. **There is no protocol fee burn either**, and nothing shows the DAO or the team buying CRV in this window.

**New veCRV locks took in 2,218,345 CRV.** That is the only real buyer of supply on the ledger, and it took in about one fifth of what ended locks gave back. Much of the locked pile is held by vaults that re-lock for the full four years over and over: **634.9M CRV** is set to end in Oct 2030, so that part is very unlikely to return soon. Finally, **12,750 CRV** left the market for good: 12,678 CRV sent by mistake to the CRV contract itself, where it can never move, and 72 CRV burned.

## Foundation and overhang

The largest pile outside the circulating count is the veCRV lock contract with **841.39M CRV**. It belongs to many users and vaults rather than to the team, but its ended-not-withdrawn part (about 14.08M CRV) is the closest thing CRV has to an unlock overhang. Next is the old 2020 rewards contract with **10.29M CRV** still unclaimed, and the DAO reserve contract with **845,144 CRV**. Inside the circulating count, the Curve DAO treasury went from zero to **5.73M CRV** after a multisig sent it 6.3M CRV, and it then paid 568,181 CRV into a one-year stream for a risk team approved by DAO vote; that multisig still holds 1.52M CRV. Earlier this year the DAO also approved CRV grants to Swiss Stake, the company behind Curve, and to a risk adviser; the DAO reserve paid nothing out in this window. We re-read every one of these balances on the chain at each rebuild. If the DAO reserve or the old rewards contract falls between rebuilds, that outflow enters the ledger as new supply at the next rebuild; treasury and multisig spending only moves coins that already circulate.

## How CRV compares to other DeFi exchange tokens

CRV is the original vote-escrow token. Holders lock CRV to steer where new emissions go, and protocols compete to win those votes, which is why so much CRV sits in long locks held by vaults. Other exchanges copied the veCRV model, but the basic trade-off is the same: emissions pay liquidity providers today, and the lock delays selling rather than destroying coins. When locks end, the coins come back.

Many exchange tokens now take the other road and use fees to buy back or burn their token, which takes coins out of the market for good. Curve sends its fees to lockers as crvUSD instead, so the fee income rewards holders without shrinking the supply. Next to fixed-supply tokens, CRV still has about **593M CRV** left to release before the 3.03B cap, at a rate that falls by about 16% each year.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**Jan 7 2027:** a group of veCRV locks holding about 5.41M CRV ends; whether the owners withdraw or re-lock moves the next window by up to a third of a percentage point.

**Lock flows week by week:** about 14.08M CRV has already finished its lock; if withdrawals speed up or new locks recover, the 9.51M CRV forward figure changes with them.

**DAO votes on emissions and grants:** new gauges, killed gauges and any vote to spend the DAO reserve or start a buyback would show up first in the weekly vote list.

**The next emission cut:** the rate stays at 3.08 CRV a second until Aug 2027, so emissions are steady through this window.

CRV supply grew **+2.26%** in the 90 days to Oct 17 2026 and is projected to grow about **+2.00%** in the next 90, in line with our monitor's +2.42%. The ceiling is the 3.03B CRV hard cap, and the yearly rate cut is the only thing that slows the flow.

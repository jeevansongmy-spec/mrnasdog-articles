# DASH Inflation Analysis · November 2026 · Mixed last 90D · projected to grow

Dash creates new coins in every block and destroys almost none, so its supply keeps rising, slowly and inside a hard cap of about **18.92M DASH**. Between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026 the network paid miners and masternodes **58,289 DASH**, the treasury paid out **18,913 DASH**, and **55,001 DASH** came back out of Dash Platform, while users moved **85,842 DASH** into Platform and about **31 DASH** of proposal fees were burned. Net, circulating DASH grew **+0.36%** in those 90 days, close to the **+0.36%** other trackers show, and we expect about **+0.70%** in the next 90 days, because one large deposit into Platform will not repeat on a schedule.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Dash's circulating supply rose from about **12.82M** to **12.86M DASH** between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026, a gain of **46,330 DASH** or **+0.36%**. Other trackers show **+0.36%** too, so the two readings agree within a hundredth of a point and the coin page carries no warning note. The last 90 days look calmer than the network really is: a single transfer of **43,998 DASH** into Dash Platform on Nov 22 2026 pulled those coins out of the circulating count just before the window closed. Without that transfer, and with no sign of another, the next 90 days point to about **+0.70%**. Our label: mixed last 90 days, projected to grow.

## Sell pressure: where new DASH comes from

The main source is the block reward. Dash runs on proof of work, and every block, about every two and a half minutes, creates **2.05 DASH**. Miners receive 20% and masternodes 37.5% right away. From Aug 26 to Nov 24 2026, across 49,337 blocks, that came to **58,289 DASH**. The reward is cut by one fourteenth about once a year; the last cut came on Aug 17 2026 and the next is due around Aug 2027, so the rate stays the same for the next 90 days.

Vesting unlocks add **0**. Dash launched in January 2014 with no presale and no team or investor allocation, so there is no locked batch waiting to be released.

Foundation and unscheduled unlocks add **0**. No foundation holds a locked store of DASH. Dash Core Group and the other teams that build Dash are paid from the treasury, and those coins are counted once, on the day the treasury creates them.

Long-term locked or bankruptcy supply adds **0**. No bankrupt firm or court is holding DASH for a later payout.

Treasury payouts added **18,913 DASH**. One fifth of every block reward is set aside for the treasury, but it only turns into real coins once a month, when the network pays the proposals that masternode owners voted for. The cap is **6,828 DASH** a month and any budget left unwon is simply never created. The payouts were **6,810 DASH** on Sep 21, **5,393 DASH** on Oct 21 and **6,710 DASH** on Nov 20 2026, and three more fall inside the next 90 days.

Platform withdrawals added **55,001 DASH**. Dash Platform is Dash's second chain, the one that runs usernames, apps and, since this year, shielded private payments. Coins held there sit outside the circulating count. Another 22.5% of every block reward goes straight into Platform as pay for the nodes that run it, and when those node runners or ordinary users take coins out, the coins re-enter circulation. Withdrawals happen every few hours, so the same pace is expected next.

## Buy pressure: where new DASH goes

Programmatic buybacks remove **0**. No contract, company or treasury buys DASH back, and transaction fees are paid to miners rather than destroyed.

The protocol fee burn removed about **31 DASH**. Filing a treasury proposal costs 1 DASH and that fee is destroyed; about 31 proposals were filed in the window. A proposal filed on Nov 16 2026 asked the treasury to burn 500 DASH, but it was losing its vote on Nov 24 2026.

Foundation buys remove **0**. No foundation, company or fund buys DASH on the network's behalf.

New long-term locks remove **0**. Masternode owners keep their stake in their own wallets, so it still counts as circulating.

Platform deposits removed **85,842 DASH**: the coins users moved into Dash Platform. Most days bring small deposits, but over half of the total came in one transfer of **43,998 DASH**, about $1.5 million, sent from 11 wallets on Nov 22 2026. Nobody has said who sent it or why. Because that transfer has no repeat schedule, the next 90 days count only the regular deposits, about **41,844 DASH**. That single change is why the projection is higher than the last 90 days.

## Foundation and overhang

Dash has no team, investor or foundation wallet waiting to unlock, and the treasury keeps no stockpile, because an unspent budget is never created. The one large balance outside the circulating count is Dash Platform itself, which held about **85.1K DASH** on Nov 24 2026, up from roughly **29.9K DASH** on Aug 26 2026. Those coins belong to Platform users and node runners, not to a team. They can come back at any time, within the network's limit on how much may leave Platform each day, and the v24 upgrade will raise that limit.

Masternode owners lock 1,000 DASH, or 4,000 DASH for the larger Platform nodes, to run a node. About 3,000 nodes were registered on Nov 24 2026. Those coins stay in their owners' own wallets and are already counted as circulating, so they do not form a separate overhang.

## How DASH compares to other proof-of-work payment coins

Bitcoin and Litecoin pay all new coins to miners and halve the reward every four years, so their supply growth falls in sudden steps. Dash cuts its reward by about 7% each year instead, a gentler slope, and splits each block between miners, masternodes, Platform node runners and a treasury that pays for development.

Monero has no cap at all and keeps a small fixed reward forever, while Dash stops near 18.92M DASH. Dash's treasury is the clearest difference: few proof-of-work coins let their node owners vote each month on how many new coins are created for builders, and that vote can leave part of the budget unminted.

Dash is also unusual in running a second chain whose coins sit outside circulation. That makes its circulating count move with Platform deposits and withdrawals, something Bitcoin, Litecoin and Monero do not have.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

**The v24 network upgrade, earliest Nov 28 2026.** Dash Core 24.0.3 came out on Nov 23 2026. The upgrade lets several people share one masternode and lets far more DASH leave Platform each day. On Nov 24 2026 only about 57% of recent blocks carried the miners' approval, short of the 80% needed, so the switch may come later.

**The 43,998 DASH in Platform.** If the sender of the Nov 22 2026 transfer takes those coins back out, they return to circulation and the 90-day growth figure rises.

**Treasury payouts on Dec 20 2026, Jan 20 2027 and Feb 19 2027.** Each can pay up to **6,828 DASH**. On Nov 24 2026 only 793 DASH of the December budget had enough votes, and voting runs until shortly before the payout. The biggest grant, **4,832 DASH** a month for Dash Core Group, ends on Dec 3 2026 and had not been renewed yet; if it is not, the December payout will be much smaller.

**The proposal to burn 500 DASH.** If masternode owners turn its vote around before the December payout, the treasury would create and then destroy those coins, a small but visible first.

## Summary

DASH supply grew **+0.36%** between Aug 26 and Nov 24 2026, in line with what other trackers show.

Miners and masternodes received **58,289 DASH**, the treasury created **18,913 DASH** for voted proposals, and Dash Platform paid out **55,001 DASH**, while users moved **85,842 DASH** into Platform.

A single **43,998 DASH** transfer into Platform on Nov 22 2026 held the last 90 days down; without a repeat, the next 90 days point to about **+0.70%**.

Dash has no vesting, no buyback and no burn worth counting, a hard cap near 18.92M DASH, and a reward that shrinks by about 7% a year.

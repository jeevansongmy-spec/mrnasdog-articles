Anyone confident about Bitcoin eventually thinks about leverage. Let's look at
what it actually costs, and then at a way around it.

## The old way: 3x leverage on Bitcoin

We buy Bitcoin at **$16,000** with **3x leverage**.

There is a line under us, near **$10,700**. **Drop below it and we are
liquidated** — the money is gone.

Bitcoin dips. It touches the line. We are out. Then Bitcoin runs to
**$120,000**.

![Bitcoin dips below the liquidation line soon after we buy with 3x, we are liquidated, and Bitcoin then climbs to 120,000 dollars without us](charts/liquidation.svg)

*Right about Bitcoin. The money was gone before it paid.*

Leverage does not punish us for being wrong. It punishes us for being early.

Two more bills run the whole time. **We pay interest to borrow**, every day,
even when Bitcoin does not move. And **we pay the shorts** — longs and shorts
must balance, almost everyone is long, so the crowded side pays the other side
every few hours.

## The new way: buy Strategy

**Strategy** (MSTR) is a company that hoards Bitcoin, and holds a very large
amount of it.

Because of that, its price follows Bitcoin. But it moves more — faster up, and
faster down.

**It is not real leverage.** No loan exists. Yet its history looks exactly like
leverage.

When Bitcoin was **$16,000**, Strategy was **$16**.

When Bitcoin reached **$120,000**, Strategy was **$394**.

Bitcoin: up about **7 times**. Strategy: up about **25 times**.

![From November 2022 to August 2025 Bitcoin rose about 7 times while Strategy stock rose about 25 times](charts/proof.svg)

*Same first day, same last day. About 3 times Bitcoin.*

About 3x, and it arrived with **no borrow fee** and **no fee to the shorts**.

Most importantly: **it can never be liquidated.** Not unlikely — impossible. A
stock has no liquidation line. Bitcoin could fall to $4,000 and the shares are
still sitting in our account. We decide when to sell.

## The two ways, side by side

| | 3x leverage on Bitcoin | Buying Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Fee to borrow | Every day | **None** |
| Fee to the shorts | Every few hours | **None** |
| Can we be liquidated? | Yes — money gone | **Never** |
| Moves more than Bitcoin? | Yes, exactly 3x | Yes, about 3x from its history |
| Paid to hold? | No | Possible — the preferred shares pay 8–10% |

The same shape of gain, without the part that ends the trade.

## Other coins have one too

Bitcoin is just the example.

**Bitmine** (BMNR) hoards Ethereum. **Forward Industries** (FWDI) hoards
Solana. More appear every year.

So the method generalises: **when we are confident about a coin, this kind of
stock can replace leverage on the coin.**

It is not risk-free. The stock swings harder than the coin, and there are
periods where the coin rises and the stock does not.

But next to real leverage — borrowing against our coins, or DeFi leverage — the
risk is far smaller, because of one thing that never changes:

**Nobody can close our position. Only we can.**

Let's keep researching, and let's keep improving.

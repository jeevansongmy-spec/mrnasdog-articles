Plasma has never minted a single XPL, and XPL is still one of the heaviest sellers the Pressure Framework tracks. All **10,000,000,000 XPL** were created at mainnet beta on **Sep 25 2025**; the 5% validator inflation written into Plasma's tokenomics has never been switched on, because external validators and stake delegation are not live. Every unit of supply pressure is therefore a vesting unlock. Over the 90 days to **Aug 22 2026** three monthly Ecosystem and Growth tranches of **88.9M XPL** released **266.7M XPL** against a buy side of exactly **zero** — no buyback, no staking lock, and a base-fee burn that destroyed about **0.05 XPL** in three months. The framework reads **+9.92%** net supply pressure on the **2,688,888,888 XPL** in circulation, and **+77.07%** forward, because the one-year team and investor cliff on **Sep 25 2026** frees **1,666.7M XPL** in a single day. Our supply monitor reads **+7.22%**, a gap of **2.70 percentage points**.

## The verdict, in one paragraph

Over the 90 days to **Aug 22 2026** the Pressure Framework reads Plasma at **+9.92%** net supply pressure: **266.7M XPL** of vesting unlocks on the sell side against **0 XPL** on the buy side. Forward it reads **+77.07%** — **2,072.2M XPL** of scheduled unlock against nothing at all. Our supply monitor reads **+7.22%** for the same window, so the gap is **2.70 percentage points** and the overview carries a data-conflict flag. That gap is an upstream counting error that has already been corrected: the classified supply series over-stepped by **41.7M XPL** a month on **Mar 25 2026**, **Apr 25 2026** and **May 25 2026** — exactly one month each of the 5% validator inflation that Plasma documents but has never activated — and the whole **125.0M XPL** accrual was reversed in one step on **Jun 11 2026**. The monitor's 90-days-ago snapshot still carries part of that accrual, so its window opens too high and counts two Plasma ecosystem tranches where three fired. XPL is best labelled a **zero-issuance chain with an unlock calendar bigger than its float**: nothing is being created, and the tradable supply is still on course to more than double inside a quarter.

## Sell pressure: where new XPL comes from

It does not come from issuance. Sell #1, protocol inflation, is **0 XPL**, and on Plasma that zero is measured rather than assumed. XPL is the native gas asset of the Plasma chain, so there is no token contract to audit — new XPL could only arrive through a block reward or a consensus-layer withdrawal, and neither exists. Every Plasma block read this session carries an empty withdrawals list and the empty-trie withdrawals root, and the Plasma execution layer identifies itself as a stock **reth** build with no custom minting path. The fee recipient address **0x000000000000000000000000000000000a11b005** went from **643,705.60 XPL** to **809,768.44 XPL** across the window — a gain of **166,062.83 XPL** in 90 days, roughly **1,845 XPL** a day, which is priority-fee revenue. A live 5% annual rate on 10 billion XPL would mint about **1,369,863 XPL** a day, more than seven hundred times as much. Plasma's own consensus documentation confirms it: proof of stake and committee formation are described as under active development, and the tokenomics page conditions all inflation on external validators and stake delegation going live.

Sell #2, vesting unlocks, carries the entire ledger at **266.7M XPL**. Plasma's Ecosystem and Growth allocation is 40% of supply, or **4,000M XPL**: **800M XPL** released at mainnet beta and the remaining **3,200M XPL** unlocks monthly pro-rata over three years, which is **88,888,889 XPL** on the 25th of each month through **Sep 25 2028**. Three of those tranches fall inside this window — **May 25 2026**, **Jun 25 2026** and **Jul 25 2026** — and the next lands **Aug 25 2026**, three days after the window closed. The arithmetic is not a model, it is an identity: **1,800M XPL** of launch float plus ten monthly tranches reproduces the circulating figure of **2,688,888,889 XPL** to the unit.

One in-window event that looks enormous adds nothing. The 12-month lockup on the American share of the Plasma public sale expired on **Jul 28 2026**, and several unlock trackers report it as **1 billion XPL** entering circulation. That figure cannot be right — the whole public sale allocation is **1,000M XPL** and the non-US portion was unlocked at launch — and the chain refutes it twice. Plasma's launch float of **1,800M XPL** is already **800M** of ecosystem plus the full **1,000M** public sale, and the classified supply series is flat at **2,688.9M XPL** from **Jul 26 2026** through **Aug 22 2026** with no step of any size on **Jul 28 2026**. This is a reclassification of already-counted supply: it freed trading, not supply, so the Pressure Framework books it at zero and names it in the row instead.

Sell #3, foundation and unscheduled unlocks, is **0 XPL** — but not for want of movement. The Plasma ecosystem wallet **0xF97Ba376d3Ecb36C4472B1D3c5aaF2D158c28558** fell from **2,834.5M XPL** to **2,400.0M XPL** over the window, a **434.5M XPL** outflow in seven transfers, and every one was traced to a destination that is still team-held: **250.0M XPL** to a fresh wallet that has not moved since receiving it, **150.0M XPL** through a Safe into Plasma's cross-chain lockbox, **25.0M XPL** into a protocol safe and **9.5M XPL** elsewhere. Not one unit reached an exchange, so nothing is booked. Sell #4, long-term locked or bankruptcy, is **0 XPL**: Plasma has never been through a bankruptcy, there is no estate and no trustee. Sell #5 is the one-year team and investor cliff, at **0 XPL** for the last 90 days and **1,805.6M XPL** for the next, and it is the single most important number on this page.

## Buy pressure: where new XPL goes

Nowhere. All four buy rows are **0 XPL**, which is unusual enough to be worth stating plainly rather than glossing. Buy #1, programmatic buyback, is **0 XPL**: Plasma has never announced, funded or executed a buyback, and neither team treasury wallet received a single inbound transfer over the window. Buy #3, foundation buy, is **0 XPL** for the same reason — every identified Plasma wallet only sent XPL out.

Buy #2, protocol fee burn, deserves the detail because Plasma genuinely does run an EIP-1559 base-fee burn and it genuinely does destroy almost nothing. Base fees were sampled at 41 evenly spaced blocks across the window: **8 wei** at the floor, **6,136 wei** at the peak, a mean of **346 wei**, on roughly **11M** gas per block at about one-second block times. Multiplied across the **7,771,876** blocks in the window that is a total burn of roughly **0.05 XPL** — five hundredths of a single coin in three months. This is not a broken read; it is the product working as designed, because Plasma sponsors gas for stablecoin transfers and the base fee therefore sits at the protocol floor almost permanently. A fee burn that scales with congestion cannot offset an unlock calendar on a chain built to have no congestion pricing. Buy #4, new long-term lock, is **0 XPL** because there is nothing to lock into: stake delegation is not live, so no XPL staking contract exists, and no vault or term deposit was deployed in the window. The one Plasma contract that does hold a large native balance is the LayerZero lock-and-mint adapter, and it removes nothing — it mints a matching XPL on the destination chain for every XPL it escrows, so cross-chain XPL is a mirror of that balance rather than a second supply.

## Foundation and overhang

Plasma's overhang is the story of the asset: **7,311.1M XPL** of the 10 billion sits outside the float, and every large holder was probed for code this session. None of them is a contract — `eth_getCode` returns empty for all of them — which means the Plasma unlock calendar is enforced by custody rather than by a vesting contract. There is no escrow that could hold the September cliff back. The wallets are: **0xCf54CB695A70f466fD5a271F85A7e268C2c97b25**, holding exactly **2,500,000,000 XPL** and untouched at both ends of the window — one whole 25% insider allocation; the ecosystem wallet **0xF97Ba376d3Ecb36C4472B1D3c5aaF2D158c28558** at **2,400.0M XPL**; the distribution wallet **0xA4E25178BBc0409944df09aaebE110ce858374a4** at **1,482.0M XPL**, down **48.5M XPL** across the window into three wallets that are all still holding; a dormant reserve **0x0E7B99b9B6B8D33FfC2A6aDbe54a3746e4E2A97A** at **555.6M XPL**, unchanged; a fresh wallet **0x9374437Baf4550929A5967D77D4fF979b88C970F** that went from **0** to **250.0M XPL** inside this window and has not moved since; a single-transaction wallet **0x7E52d521D24c04cB36C70130d92A2C1571E0b5a8** at **191.7M XPL**; and the protocol safe **0x000000000000000000000000000000000A11B004** at **5.3M XPL**. Not counted as overhang: the well-known exchange wallet holding **413.8M XPL**, which belongs to depositors, the cross-chain lockbox at **265.1M XPL**, which backs XPL already trading elsewhere, and Wrapped XPL, which is a user wrapper. All of these are read straight from the Plasma chain every rebuild, so if any of their balances falls between refreshes, the outflow enters Sell #3 at the next refresh.

## How XPL compares to other stablecoin-settlement chains

Against a halving-model chain with a hard cap, Plasma looks better on the surface and worse underneath. A capped proof-of-work chain issues new coins every block forever, but it issues them into a float that already contains almost the whole supply, so its inflation rate is small and falls on a fixed schedule nobody can change. Plasma issues nothing at all — genuinely zero, verified at the block level — but only **26.9%** of XPL is in the float, and the remaining **73.1%** arrives on a calendar set by the Plasma Foundation rather than by protocol code. Measured as supply reaching the market, which is what the Pressure Framework measures, a zero-issuance chain with a three-year unlock runway is far more inflationary than a chain that mints every ten minutes.

Against uncapped continuous-emission Layer 1s, Plasma is the same asset at a different point in its life. A mature proof-of-stake chain pays validators 3% to 5% a year and has long since finished its vesting, so its supply pressure is a smooth, forecastable trickle. Plasma has the 5% rate written down but not switched on, and its pressure arrives in monthly steps and one enormous cliff. Note which direction that resolves: when Plasma does activate external validators and stake delegation, XPL will acquire a second, permanent sell row of roughly **500M XPL** a year — and simultaneously acquire its first real buy row, because delegated staking is the only mechanism on the roadmap capable of locking float away.

Against exchange tokens with quarterly buybacks, the contrast is starkest and it is entirely about the buy side. Those tokens convert business revenue into supply removal, so the float shrinks when the product works. Plasma routes its product success into a base fee of eight wei, by design, because charging users for stablecoin transfers would defeat the point of the chain. That is a defensible product decision and a severe framework problem: it means Plasma has no revenue-linked absorption mechanism of any kind, and no announced plan to build one. Every fee-burning chain we track can flip deflationary in a busy month. Plasma cannot, at any volume.

## What to watch in the next 90 days

First and above everything, the one-year cliff on **Sep 25 2026**, when one-third of the **2,500M XPL** Plasma team allocation and one-third of the **2,500M XPL** investor allocation unlock together — **1,666.7M XPL**, about **62%** of everything currently trading, in a single day, out of custody wallets with no contract to slow them. Second, **Oct 25 2026**, when the two-year monthly tail on those same allocations begins at **138.9M XPL** a month on top of the ecosystem tranche. Third, the monthly Ecosystem and Growth tranches themselves on **Aug 25 2026**, **Sep 25 2026** and **Oct 25 2026**, at **88.9M XPL** each. Fourth, any announcement that external validators and stake delegation have gone live on Plasma: that switches Sell #1 from **0** to roughly **123M XPL** a quarter and simultaneously creates the first buy row XPL has ever had. Fifth, the fresh wallet **0x9374437Baf4550929A5967D77D4fF979b88C970F** holding **250.0M XPL** since this window — if it moves toward an exchange rather than toward another Plasma wallet, Sell #3 stops being zero.

## Summary

Plasma gives the cleanest possible answer to the issuance question — no block reward, no withdrawals, a stock execution layer, and a fee address that collected **166,063 XPL** of pure fee revenue in 90 days while a live 5% rate would have minted more than a hundred million. And it still reads at **+9.92%** over 90 days and **+77.07%** forward, because XPL's supply pressure is a vesting calendar with no counterweight: **266.7M XPL** of Ecosystem and Growth unlocks behind it, **2,072.2M XPL** ahead of it, and a buy side that is genuinely, mechanically zero. The key risk is the **Sep 25 2026** cliff, which frees **1,666.7M XPL** of team and investor supply into a float of **2,688.9M XPL** from wallets that are ordinary custody addresses rather than vesting contracts. There is no cap that helps here — the ceiling is **10,000,000,000 XPL** and the float is barely a quarter of the way to it.
